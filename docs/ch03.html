A recent graduate shares how she registered, scheduled, studied and survived the NCLEX. Her practical timeline and self-care habits illustrate the preparation plan described in the previous chapters.一位新畢業護理師分享她如何報名、預約、準備並通過 NCLEX。她的實務時間表與自我照顧習慣，說明了前幾章所述的備考計畫。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Why the author became a nurse成為護理師的動機

  • The author was inspired to serve her community after the 2017 Las Vegas tragedy and entered a 16-month nursing program.作者受 2017 年拉斯維加斯事件啟發，決心服務社區，並就讀 16 個月護理課程。
  • The NCLEX is a national licensure exam that tests critical thinking and nursing judgment.NCLEX 是測試批判性思考與護理判斷的全國執照考試。
p.40 · book 22

2Registration steps報名步驟

  • About 2 months before graduation she made an account with the state board, submitted the signed application, license and fingerprint fees.畢業前約 2 個月向州護理委員會建立帳號，提交簽名申請書與執照及指紋費用。
  • Start early; processing takes time. Download the Candidate Bulletin; steps vary by state.及早開始，處理需要時間；下載考生手冊，各州步驟不同。
  • After approval you may register with Pearson VUE, who sends the Authorization to Test (ATT) needed to schedule.核准後可向 Pearson VUE 註冊，並收到預約所需的考試授權書（ATT）。
p.40 · book 22

3Scheduling and building a study plan預約與擬定讀書計畫

  • Appointments may be scarce; she checked daily for cancellations and obtained an earlier date.考位可能稀少；她每天查詢取消名額，因此提早應試。
  • Use resources that worked in nursing school; start with an initial assessment exam to find weak areas.使用在校期間有效的資源；先做初始評量找出弱點。
  • Her plan: 3-4 hours daily for 100-150 practice questions, then the rest of the day for self-care.她的計畫：每天 3-4 小時做 100-150 題，其餘時間照顧自己。
p.40 · book 22p.41 · book 23

4Using rationales and the final week運用解析與最後一週

  • Read the rationale and strategy for every question, including those answered correctly, to confirm your reasoning and see why other options are wrong.每題都讀解析與策略（含答對題），確認思路並瞭解其他選項為何錯誤。
  • About a week before, take another assessment exam and focus on remaining weak concepts.約考前一週再做一次評量，專攻剩餘弱項。
  • The day before: no questions or content, relax, recheck the route, ID and ATT, sleep well.前一天：不做題、不讀內容，放鬆、再確認路線、證件與 ATT、好好睡覺。
p.41 · book 23

5Exam day and after考試日與考後

  • Have a light breakfast; at the center you get a locker, show ID and ATT, and are fingerprinted, palm-scanned and photographed.吃清淡早餐；到考場後使用置物櫃、出示證件與 ATT，並採集指紋、掌紋與照片。
  • Focus on one question at a time using your knowledge and test-taking strategies.一次專注一題，運用知識與應試策略。
  • Doubt after the exam is normal; she saw an unofficial early result as Pass after a couple of days.考後懷疑是正常的；她在數日後的早期查詢看到通過。
  • Believe in yourself and avoid being too hard on yourself.相信自己，別對自己太嚴苛。
p.41 · book 23

Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Apply to the state board early; you need the ATT before scheduling.及早向州護理委員會申請；預約前需有 ATT。
    p.40 · book 22
  2. Bring the ATT and valid ID to the testing center.攜帶 ATT 與有效證件到考場。
    p.41 · book 23
  3. Check for appointment cancellations if the date is far away.若考期太遠，可留意取消名額。
    p.40 · book 22
  4. Take an initial assessment to identify weak areas, then a second one about a week before.先做初始評量找弱項，考前約一週再做一次。
    p.41 · book 23
  5. Read rationales and strategies even for correct answers.答對的題也要讀解析與策略。
    p.41 · book 23
  6. Plan self-care after daily question goals (e.g., 100-150 questions).完成每日題量（如 100-150 題）後安排自我照顧。
    p.41 · book 23
  7. The day before the exam: no studying, rest and verify directions.考前一天：不讀書，休息並確認路線。
    p.41 · book 23
  8. Pearson VUE is the testing vendor that issues scheduling after state approval.Pearson VUE 為施測廠商，州核准後負責預約。
    p.40 · book 22
