Part XI · Respiratory Problems of the Adult Client成人呼吸問題

Chapter 52: Respiratory Medications第 52 章：呼吸系統藥物

📖 Source: PDF p.778–797 · printed book p.701–718.e2來源：PDF 第 778–797 頁 · 書本印刷頁 701–718.e2

This chapter covers inhalation devices and each respiratory drug class (bronchodilators, anticholinergics, corticosteroids, leukotriene modifiers, mast cell stabilizers, omalizumab, antihistamines, decongestants, expectorants/mucolytics, antitussives, opioid antagonists), then tuberculosis drugs and influenza/COVID-19 prevention and treatment. Focus on class action, key adverse effects, monitoring, and client teaching.本章涵蓋吸入裝置與各類呼吸系統藥物(支氣管擴張劑、抗膽鹼藥、類固醇、白三烯調節劑、肥大細胞穩定劑、omalizumab、抗組織胺、解鼻充血劑、化痰/黏液溶解劑、鎮咳劑、鴉片類拮抗劑),以及抗結核藥物和流感/COVID-19的預防與治療。重點為藥物分類的作用、主要不良反應、監測與衛教。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Inhalation devices and order of administration吸入裝置與給藥順序 📖 PDF p.778 (book p.701), PDF p.779 (book p.702), PDF p.780 (book p.703)

  • MDI uses a propellant to push out drug; a spacer improves lung delivery and reduces mouth/throat deposition.定量噴霧吸入器(MDI)以推進劑噴出藥物;使用間隔器可增加肺部沉積並減少口咽殘留。
  • DPI has no propellant but needs a strong, fast inhalation.乾粉吸入器(DPI)不含推進劑,但需要用力且快速吸氣。
  • Nebulizer turns liquid drug into a fine mist via mask or mouthpiece using air or oxygen under pressure.噴霧器以加壓空氣或氧氣將藥液變成細霧,經面罩或口含嘴給藥。
  • Give the bronchodilator first, corticosteroid second so airways are open for the steroid.先給支氣管擴張劑,再給類固醇,讓氣道先擴張使類固醇更有效。
  • Wait 5 minutes between two different inhaled drugs; wait 1-2 minutes between repeat puffs of the same drug.兩種不同吸入藥之間間隔5分鐘;同一藥物第二劑間隔1-2分鐘。
  • Technique: breathe out, activate while inhaling slowly about 5 seconds, hold breath 10-15 seconds; sip water before/after to reduce cough and bad taste.技巧:先吐氣,緩慢吸入並噴藥約5秒,閉氣10-15秒;吸入前後喝幾口水可減少咳嗽與口中異味。

Bronchodilators: beta2 agonists and methylxanthines支氣管擴張劑:β2受體促效劑與甲基黃嘌呤 📖 PDF p.778 (book p.701), PDF p.779 (book p.702), PDF p.780 (book p.703)

  • Beta2 agonists (albuterol, salmeterol, formoterol, terbutaline) relax bronchial smooth muscle; theophylline/aminophylline also stimulate CNS and cause diuresis.β2促效劑(albuterol、salmeterol、formoterol、terbutaline)放鬆支氣管平滑肌;theophylline/aminophylline另會刺激中樞神經並利尿。
  • Adverse effects: tachycardia, palpitations, dysrhythmias, tremor, nervousness, seizures, nausea, hyperglycemia, dry mouth, paradoxical bronchospasm.不良反應:心搏過速、心悸、心律不整、顫抖、神經質、癲癇、噁心、高血糖、口乾、反常性支氣管痙攣。
  • Caution: hypertension, diabetes, narrow-angle glaucoma, severe cardiac disease, hyperthyroidism, seizure disorder.需謹慎:高血壓、糖尿病、狹角性青光眼、嚴重心臟病、甲狀腺機能亢進、癲癇。
  • Therapeutic theophylline level 10-20 mcg/mL (55.5-111 mcmol/L); toxic above 20; early toxicity = restlessness, tremor, palpitations, tachycardia.Theophylline治療濃度10-20 mcg/mL(55.5-111 mcmol/L);超過20即可能中毒;早期徵象為躁動、顫抖、心悸、心搏過速。
  • Interactions: theophylline raises digoxin toxicity risk; beta blockers, cimetidine, erythromycin raise theophylline levels; barbiturates and carbamazepine lower them.交互作用:theophylline增加digoxin毒性風險;β阻斷劑、cimetidine、erythromycin使theophylline濃度升高;巴比妥類與carbamazepine使其降低。
  • Teach: do not crush enteric-coated/sustained-release forms, avoid caffeine (coffee, tea, cola, chocolate), take with/after meals, carry rescue inhaler, stop smoking, check pulse. IV theophylline via infusion pump, slowly.衛教:緩釋/腸溶錠不可磨碎、避免咖啡因(咖啡、茶、可樂、巧克力)、隨餐或餐後服用、隨身攜帶急救吸入器、戒菸、監測脈搏。IV theophylline須以輸液幫浦緩慢給予。

Anticholinergics, corticosteroids, leukotriene modifiers抗膽鹼藥、類固醇與白三烯調節劑 📖 PDF p.779 (book p.702), PDF p.780 (book p.703)

  • Inhaled anticholinergics (ipratropium, tiotropium, aclidinium, umeclidinium) block muscarinic receptors to prevent bronchoconstriction; used in COPD and exercise-induced bronchospasm.吸入型抗膽鹼藥(ipratropium、tiotropium、aclidinium、umeclidinium)阻斷蕈毒鹼受體以防支氣管收縮;用於COPD與運動誘發支氣管痙攣。
  • Dry mouth and pharyngeal irritation are common (sugarless candy helps); systemic effects are rare: blurred vision, glaucoma, urinary retention, constipation, tachycardia.常見口乾與咽部刺激(含無糖糖果可緩解);全身性作用少見:視力模糊、青光眼、尿滯留、便秘、心搏過速。
  • Some ipratropium products contain soy lecithin: avoid in peanut allergy.部分ipratropium產品含大豆卵磷脂,花生過敏者應避免。
  • Inhaled/oral glucocorticoids reduce airway inflammation and edema (see Chapter 48).吸入或口服糖皮質素可減輕氣道發炎與水腫(見第48章)。
  • Leukotriene modifiers (montelukast, zafirlukast, zileuton) are for chronic asthma prophylaxis, NOT acute attacks; monitor liver function; watch for depression, suicidal thinking, hallucinations.白三烯調節劑(montelukast、zafirlukast、zileuton)用於氣喘長期預防,不用於急性發作;需監測肝功能;注意憂鬱、自殺意念與幻覺。
  • Teach: take zafirlukast 1 hour before or 2 hours after meals; effect needs 48-72 hours of regular use; do not stop even when symptom-free.衛教:zafirlukast餐前1小時或餐後2小時服用;需規律使用48-72小時才見效;無症狀時也不可自行停藥。

Mast cell stabilizer and omalizumab肥大細胞穩定劑與omalizumab 📖 PDF p.780 (book p.703), PDF p.781 (book p.704)

  • Cromolyn sodium stabilizes mast cells to prevent mediator release; for allergic rhinitis, asthma, exercise-induced bronchospasm (prophylaxis).Cromolyn sodium穩定肥大細胞、防止介質釋放;用於過敏性鼻炎、氣喘及運動誘發支氣管痙攣的預防。
  • Adverse effects: cough, sneezing, nasal stinging, bronchospasm after inhalation, unpleasant taste. Do not stop abruptly (rebound asthma).不良反應:吸入後咳嗽、打噴嚏、鼻刺痛、支氣管痙攣、口中異味。不可突然停藥(反彈性氣喘發作)。
  • Omalizumab is an anti-IgE monoclonal antibody given subcutaneously every 2-4 weeks for allergic asthma; dose based on IgE level and weight.Omalizumab為抗IgE單株抗體,每2-4週皮下注射,用於過敏性氣喘;劑量依血清IgE與體重調整。
  • Risk of anaphylaxis: have emergency drugs available at first dose; avoid live vaccines; do not stop other asthma drugs; improvement is gradual.有過敏性休克風險:首次給藥需備妥急救藥物;避免活性疫苗;不可自行停其他氣喘藥;改善是漸進的。
  • Other adverse effects: injection site reactions, URI, sinusitis, headache, malignancy.其他不良反應:注射部位反應、上呼吸道感染、鼻竇炎、頭痛、惡性腫瘤。

Antihistamines, decongestants, expectorants, antitussives抗組織胺、解鼻充血劑、化痰藥與鎮咳劑 📖 PDF p.781 (book p.704), PDF p.782 (book p.705), PDF p.783 (book p.706)

  • H1 antihistamines (diphenhydramine, loratadine, cetirizine) block histamine; first-generation cause sedation, dry secretions, urinary retention, blurred vision; avoid with alcohol/CNS depressants; avoid diphenhydramine in narrow-angle glaucoma.H1抗組織胺(diphenhydramine、loratadine、cetirizine)阻斷組織胺;第一代易致嗜睡、分泌物變乾、尿滯留、視力模糊;避免與酒精/中樞抑制劑併用;狹角性青光眼避免diphenhydramine。
  • Antihistamines: use caution in COPD (drying); give IM deep in large muscle, never subcutaneous; motion sickness dose 30 min before event.抗組織胺:COPD者慎用(乾燥作用);肌肉注射須打在大肌肉,不可皮下注射;暈車藥於事前30分鐘服用。
  • Decongestants (pseudoephedrine, phenylephrine, oxymetazoline): cause hypertension, palpitations, insomnia, hyperglycemia; do not use longer than 48 hours (rebound congestion).解鼻充血劑(pseudoephedrine、phenylephrine、oxymetazoline):可致高血壓、心悸、失眠、高血糖;使用勿超過48小時(反彈性鼻塞)。
  • Guaifenesin (expectorant): take with a full glass of water; fluids up to 3000 mL/day unless contraindicated.Guaifenesin(化痰藥):以一整杯水服用;除非禁忌,每日液體攝取可達3000 mL。
  • Acetylcysteine (mucolytic): avoid in asthma (increases airway resistance); give bronchodilator 5 min before; do not mix in nebulizer; also antidote for acetaminophen overdose.Acetylcysteine(黏液溶解劑):氣喘者避免(增加氣道阻力);支氣管擴張劑先給,間隔5分鐘;噴霧時不與其他藥混合;亦為acetaminophen過量的解毒劑。
  • Antitussives (codeine, hydrocodone, benzonatate, dextromethorphan) suppress medullary cough center for dry irritating cough; avoid in head injury/post-craniotomy and with CNS depressants; avoid cough suppression in COPD.鎮咳劑(codeine、hydrocodone、benzonatate、dextromethorphan)抑制延腦咳嗽中樞,用於乾咳;頭部外傷/開顱術後及併用中樞抑制劑者避免;COPD者避免抑制咳嗽。
  • Call provider if cough lasts over 1 week or with fever or rash.咳嗽超過1週或伴隨發燒、皮疹應通知醫療人員。

Opioid antagonists鴉片類拮抗劑 📖 PDF p.783 (book p.706)

  • Naloxone reverses opioid-induced respiratory depression; do not use for non-opioid respiratory depression.Naloxone逆轉鴉片類引起的呼吸抑制;非鴉片類引起的呼吸抑制不使用。
  • Respiratory depression can recur if the opioid outlasts naloxone, so monitor closely and re-dose as needed.若鴉片類作用時間比naloxone長,呼吸抑制可能復發,需密切監測並視需要再給藥。
  • IV doses titrated every 2-5 minutes; keep oxygen and resuscitation equipment at bedside.IV劑量每2-5分鐘調整;床邊備氧氣與急救設備。
  • Adverse effects: nausea, vomiting, tremor, sweating, hypertension, tachycardia (withdrawal-type effects).不良反應:噁心、嘔吐、顫抖、出汗、高血壓、心搏過速(類戒斷反應)。

Tuberculosis therapy principles and first-line drugs結核病治療原則與第一線藥物 📖 PDF p.783 (book p.706), PDF p.784 (book p.707), PDF p.785 (book p.708), PDF p.786 (book p.709)

  • Multidrug therapy for active TB lasts 6-9 months (longer if immunosuppressed); transmission risk falls greatly after 2-3 weeks; sputum cultures usually negative by 3 months; exposed persons get isoniazid for 9-12 months.活動性結核採多重藥物治療6-9個月(免疫抑制者更久);治療2-3週後傳染力大幅降低;多數3個月後痰培養轉陰;暴露者以isoniazid預防9-12個月。
  • Nonadherence causes multidrug-resistant TB; use DOT/VDOT; take on an empty stomach (1 hour before/2 hours after meals), no antacids, no alcohol; all first-line drugs can harm the liver.不遵從服藥會產生多重抗藥性結核;可採直接觀察治療(DOT/VDOT);空腹服用(餐前1小時或餐後2小時),避免抗酸劑與酒精;所有第一線藥物皆可能傷肝。
  • First-line: isoniazid, rifampin, ethambutol, pyrazinamide.第一線藥物:isoniazid、rifampin、ethambutol、pyrazinamide。
  • Isoniazid: hepatitis (anorexia, dark urine, jaundice, fatigue), peripheral neuritis; give pyridoxine (B6); raises phenytoin levels; avoid tyramine foods (aged cheese, tuna); check baseline and ongoing liver enzymes.Isoniazid:肝炎(厭食、尿液變深、黃疸、疲倦)、周邊神經炎;給予pyridoxine(B6);使phenytoin濃度上升;避免含酪胺食物(陳年乳酪、鮪魚);需基線與持續追蹤肝酵素。
  • Rifampin: red-orange secretions (urine, sweat, tears, feces; stains soft contacts), hepatotoxicity; lowers effect of oral contraceptives, warfarin, oral hypoglycemics, digoxin, phenytoin, verapamil.Rifampin:體液呈橘紅色(尿、汗、淚、糞便;軟式隱形眼鏡會染色)、肝毒性;降低口服避孕藥、warfarin、口服降血糖藥、digoxin、phenytoin、verapamil的效果。
  • Ethambutol: optic neuritis (decreased acuity, red-green color confusion) - report immediately; raises uric acid; contraindicated under age 13 per book; give once daily with food.Ethambutol:視神經炎(視力下降、紅綠色辨識困難),須立即通報;升高尿酸;依本書不適用13歲以下兒童;每日一次隨餐服用。
  • Pyrazinamide: hepatotoxicity, raised uric acid (gout, joint pain), photosensitivity; can make diabetes harder to control.Pyrazinamide:肝毒性、尿酸升高(痛風、關節痛)、光敏感;可能使糖尿病更難控制。

Second-line TB drugs第二線結核藥物 📖 PDF p.786 (book p.709), PDF p.787 (book p.710), PDF p.788 (book p.711), PDF p.789 (book p.712)

  • Rifabutin: neutropenia, uveitis (eye pain, blurred vision), hepatitis, flu-like syndrome, red-orange secretions; use non-hormonal contraception; used for MAC in HIV.Rifabutin:嗜中性球低下、葡萄膜炎(眼痛、視力模糊)、肝炎、類流感症候群、橘紅色體液;應使用非荷爾蒙避孕;用於HIV患者的MAC感染。
  • Rifapentine: pulmonary TB only; hepatotoxicity, red-orange secretions, interacts with warfarin and oral contraceptives; baseline LFTs.Rifapentine:僅用於肺結核;肝毒性、橘紅色體液,與warfarin及口服避孕藥有交互作用;需基線肝功能。
  • Capreomycin (IM, MDR-TB): nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity, neuromuscular blockade; baseline audiometry; avoid with aminoglycosides/loop diuretics.Capreomycin(肌肉注射,用於MDR-TB):腎毒性、耳毒性、神經肌肉阻斷;需基線聽力檢查;避免與胺基糖苷類/袢利尿劑併用。
  • Aminoglycosides/fluoroquinolones (streptomycin, amikacin, levofloxacin): nephro-, oto-, neurotoxicity; audiometry every 1-2 months; hydrate; monitor BUN/creatinine, peak and trough.胺基糖苷類/氟喹諾酮類(streptomycin、amikacin、levofloxacin):腎、耳、神經毒性;每1-2個月聽力檢查;維持水分;監測BUN/肌酐及波峰/波谷濃度。
  • Ethionamide: GI upset, metallic taste, orthostatic hypotension, hepatotoxicity; give pyridoxine; watch glucose.Ethionamide:胃腸不適、金屬味、姿勢性低血壓、肝毒性;給予pyridoxine;注意血糖。
  • Aminosalicylic acid: GI irritation, crystalluria (push fluids), blood dyscrasias; discard if purplish-brown.Aminosalicylic acid:胃腸刺激、結晶尿(多喝水)、血液病變;若變紫褐色須丟棄。
  • Cycloserine: CNS toxicity, seizures, depression/suicidal thoughts; avoid alcohol; peak level 2 hours after dose 25-35 mcg/mL; weekly levels.Cycloserine:中樞神經毒性、癲癇、憂鬱/自殺意念;避免酒精;給藥後2小時峰值25-35 mcg/mL;每週監測濃度。
  • Streptomycin toxicity: nephro (urine output change), neuro (numbness, twitching), vestibular (vertigo), auditory (tinnitus, permanent hearing loss).Streptomycin毒性:腎(尿量改變)、神經(麻木、抽動)、前庭(眩暈)、聽覺(耳鳴、永久性聽損)。

Influenza, pneumococcal and COVID-19流感、肺炎鏈球菌與COVID-19 📖 PDF p.789 (book p.712), PDF p.790 (book p.713), PDF p.791 (book p.714)

  • Annual flu vaccine for all without contraindication; inactivated IM vaccine (deltoid) is safe in pregnancy and from 6 months; cannot cause influenza; antibodies take about 2 weeks.無禁忌者每年接種流感疫苗;不活化肌肉注射疫苗(三角肌)可用於孕婦及6個月以上嬰兒;不會引起流感;抗體約2週產生。
  • Live nasal vaccine: only healthy ages 2-49; not for pregnancy, long-term aspirin in children, asthma, diabetes, heart/kidney/lung disease; virus shed up to 2 days.活性鼻噴疫苗:僅限2-49歲健康者;孕婦、長期服用aspirin的兒童、氣喘、糖尿病、心/腎/肺疾病者不可用;病毒可排出約2天。
  • Inactivated vaccine contraindications: hypersensitivity, Guillain-Barre syndrome, febrile illness, under 6 months.不活化疫苗禁忌:過敏、格林-巴利症候群、發燒疾病、未滿6個月。
  • Antivirals (oseltamivir, zanamivir, peramivir, amantadine, rimantadine): start within 2 days of symptoms, finish the course; do not replace vaccination and may not stop transmission.抗病毒藥(oseltamivir、zanamivir、peramivir、amantadine、rimantadine):症狀出現2天內開始並完成療程;不能取代疫苗,也不一定能阻止傳染。
  • Amantadine/rimantadine: CNS effects, orthostatic hypotension; oseltamivir: insomnia, diarrhea; zanamivir: ENT infection, cough, bronchitis.Amantadine/rimantadine:中樞神經作用、姿勢性低血壓;oseltamivir:失眠、腹瀉;zanamivir:耳鼻喉感染、咳嗽、支氣管炎。
  • COVID-19: follow vaccination and boosters; consult the current NIH treatment guidelines for up-to-date therapy.COVID-19:依建議接種疫苗與追加劑;治療請隨時查閱最新NIH治療指引。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Bronchodilator before corticosteroid; wait 5 minutes between different inhaled drugs, 1-2 minutes between same-drug puffs.先支氣管擴張劑、後類固醇;不同吸入藥間隔5分鐘,同藥重複劑量間隔1-2分鐘。 📖 PDF p.778 (book p.701)
  2. Therapeutic theophylline 10-20 mcg/mL; >20 toxic (restlessness, tremor, tachycardia, palpitations); avoid caffeine.Theophylline治療濃度10-20 mcg/mL;>20中毒(躁動、顫抖、心搏過速、心悸);避免咖啡因。 📖 PDF p.779 (book p.702), PDF p.780 (book p.703)
  3. Theophylline + beta2 agonist together may cause dysrhythmias; theophylline raises digoxin toxicity risk.Theophylline併用β2促效劑可能導致心律不整;theophylline增加digoxin中毒風險。 📖 PDF p.778 (book p.701)
  4. Leukotriene modifiers prevent, they do not treat acute asthma; monitor liver function and mood/suicidal thoughts.白三烯調節劑為預防用藥,不治療急性氣喘;監測肝功能與情緒/自殺意念。 📖 PDF p.780 (book p.703)
  5. Cromolyn can cause bronchospasm; do not stop abruptly (rebound attack).Cromolyn可能引起支氣管痙攣;不可突然停藥(反彈性發作)。 📖 PDF p.780 (book p.703)
  6. Omalizumab: anaphylaxis risk (lip/tongue swelling, dyspnea) - have emergency meds ready at first dose; no live vaccines.Omalizumab:有過敏性休克風險(嘴唇/舌頭腫脹、呼吸困難),首次給藥備妥急救藥;避免活性疫苗。 📖 PDF p.780 (book p.703), PDF p.781 (book p.704)
  7. Decongestants max 48 hours because of rebound congestion; caution in hypertension, cardiac disease, diabetes, hyperthyroidism.解鼻充血劑最長使用48小時(反彈性鼻塞);高血壓、心臟病、糖尿病、甲狀腺亢進者慎用。 📖 PDF p.782 (book p.705)
  8. Acetylcysteine: avoid in asthma; also antidote for acetaminophen overdose.Acetylcysteine:氣喘者避免;亦為acetaminophen過量的解毒劑。 📖 PDF p.782 (book p.705)
  9. Naloxone: have resuscitation equipment ready; effect may wear off before the opioid, so respiratory depression can return.Naloxone:備妥急救設備;藥效可能比鴉片類先消退,呼吸抑制可能復發。 📖 PDF p.783 (book p.706)
  10. Isoniazid: hepatitis and peripheral neuritis; give pyridoxine (B6); report jaundice, dark urine, anorexia, fatigue; no alcohol; avoid tyramine foods.Isoniazid:肝炎與周邊神經炎;給予B6;通報黃疸、尿液變深、厭食、疲倦;禁酒;避免含酪胺食物。 📖 PDF p.784 (book p.707), PDF p.785 (book p.708)
  11. Rifampin turns urine, sweat, tears, feces red-orange (harmless, stains soft contacts); reduces oral contraceptive effectiveness.Rifampin使尿液、汗液、淚液、糞便呈橘紅色(無害,會染色軟式隱形眼鏡);降低口服避孕藥效果。 📖 PDF p.785 (book p.708)
  12. Ethambutol: report any visual change (red-green discrimination, decreased acuity) immediately - optic neuritis.Ethambutol:任何視覺改變(紅綠辨色困難、視力下降)須立即通報(視神經炎)。 📖 PDF p.785 (book p.708), PDF p.786 (book p.709)
  13. Streptomycin/aminoglycosides/capreomycin: ototoxicity, nephrotoxicity; baseline and repeated audiometry; report tinnitus, hearing loss, vertigo.Streptomycin/胺基糖苷類/capreomycin:耳毒性、腎毒性;基線及定期聽力檢查;通報耳鳴、聽力下降、眩暈。 📖 PDF p.787 (book p.710), PDF p.788 (book p.711), PDF p.789 (book p.712)
  14. Never skip TB doses: nonadherence creates multidrug-resistant TB; DOT improves adherence.結核藥物不可漏服:不遵從會產生多重抗藥性結核;DOT可提升遵從性。 📖 PDF p.783 (book p.706), PDF p.784 (book p.707)
  15. Influenza antivirals work best within 2 days of symptom onset and do not replace the vaccine.流感抗病毒藥在症狀出現2天內使用效果最佳,且不能取代疫苗。 📖 PDF p.790 (book p.713), PDF p.791 (book p.714)
  16. Live attenuated nasal flu vaccine is not for pregnancy or people with chronic lung/heart disease or asthma.活性減毒鼻噴流感疫苗不適用於孕婦或患有慢性肺/心臟病、氣喘者。 📖 PDF p.790 (book p.713)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. A client is prescribed albuterol and beclomethasone metered-dose inhalers at the same time. Which action by the nurse is correct?個案同時被開立albuterol與beclomethasone定量噴霧吸入器。護理師何種做法正確?

The bronchodilator opens airways first, improving steroid delivery. Different inhaled drugs are separated by 5 minutes.支氣管擴張劑先打開氣道,使類固醇更易到達;不同吸入藥間隔5分鐘。

2. A client taking theophylline reports restlessness, tremors and palpitations. The serum level is 24 mcg/mL. What does the nurse conclude and do first?服用theophylline的個案出現躁動、顫抖與心悸,血中濃度24 mcg/mL。護理師的判斷與首要處置為何?

Therapeutic range is 10-20 mcg/mL; above 20 is toxic and signs match early toxicity.治療範圍10-20 mcg/mL,超過20即中毒,症狀符合早期中毒。

These are signs of nonviral hepatitis; hold drug, notify provider, and check liver function tests.這些是非病毒性肝炎徵象;應停藥、通知醫師並檢查肝功能。
