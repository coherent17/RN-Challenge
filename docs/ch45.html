1Cancer basics, diagnosis and pain control癌症基礎、診斷與疼痛控制

  • Cancer is uncontrolled growth of cells that lost normal growth control; it can impair immunity, blood production, GI, motor/sensory and respiratory function.癌症是失去生長控制的細胞不受控增生，可損害免疫、造血、胃腸、運動感覺與呼吸功能。
  • Metastasis: local seeding, bloodborne (most common), lymphatic spread.轉移途徑：局部播種、血行性（最常見）、淋巴性。
  • Solid tumors arise in organs; hematological cancers (leukemia, lymphoma, myeloma) arise from blood-forming tissue.實體腫瘤源自器官；血液癌（白血病、淋巴瘤、多發性骨髓瘤）源自造血組織。
  • Grading (I-IV) describes cell differentiation under the microscope; staging (0-IV) describes size, spread and metastasis. Stage 0 = in situ, stage IV = distant metastasis.分級（I-IV）描述顯微鏡下細胞分化程度；分期（0-IV）描述大小、擴散與轉移。0期=原位癌，IV期=遠處轉移。
  • Risks: chemical, physical (radiation, UV), viral (EBV, HBV, HPV), H. pylori, tobacco, obesity, diet, genetics, age, immunosuppression.危險因子：化學物質、物理因素（輻射、紫外線）、病毒（EBV、HBV、HPV）、幽門螺旋桿菌、菸草、肥胖、飲食、遺傳、年齡、免疫抑制。
  • Early detection: mammography, Pap test, stool occult blood, colonoscopy, breast and testicular self-exam, skin checks, and the nine CAUTION-US warning signs.早期偵測：乳房攝影、子宮頸抹片、糞便潛血、大腸鏡、乳房與睪丸自我檢查、皮膚檢查及CAUTION警訊。
  • Biopsy is the definitive diagnosis; types are needle, incisional, excisional and staging. Frozen section gives results in minutes; permanent paraffin section takes about 24 hours but is clearer.切片為確診方法；類型有針吸、切開、全切除與分期切片。冷凍切片數分鐘出結果；石蠟永久切片約24小時但較清晰。
  • Other tests: CBC, CT/PET, MRI, tumor markers (CEA, alpha-fetoprotein), bone marrow exam, endoscopy; invasive procedures need informed consent.其他檢查：全血球計數、CT/PET、MRI、腫瘤標記（CEA、AFP）、骨髓檢查、內視鏡；侵入性檢查需知情同意。
  • Cancer pain arises from bone destruction, organ obstruction, nerve compression, infiltration, inflammation and anxiety.癌痛源於骨破壞、器官阻塞、神經壓迫、組織浸潤、發炎與焦慮。
  • Pain is what the client says it is. Most cancer pain is controlled with around-the-clock oral opioids; mild pain uses acetaminophen/NSAIDs; severe pain uses morphine, hydromorphone, methadone; neuropathic pain may need anticonvulsants or antidepressants.疼痛以病人主訴為準。多數癌痛可用口服鴉片類藥物定時給予控制；輕度用acetaminophen/NSAIDs；重度用morphine、hydromorphone、methadone；神經痛可用抗癲癇藥或抗憂鬱藥。
  • Use equianalgesic charts when changing routes; add relaxation, imagery, biofeedback, massage, heat/cold.更換給藥途徑時使用等效鎮痛換算表；加入放鬆、意象、生理回饋、按摩與冷熱敷。

2Surgery, chemotherapy and radiation手術、化療與放射治療

  • Surgery types: prophylactic, curative, control (debulking), palliative, reconstructive. Risks include pain, infection, bleeding, thromboembolism and body image grief.手術類型：預防性、根治性、控制性（減積）、緩和性、重建性。風險包括疼痛、感染、出血、血栓與身體意象失落。
  • Chemotherapy is systemic and harms rapidly dividing normal cells (skin, hair, GI lining, sperm, bone marrow); combinations are timed around the nadir. See Chapter 46.化療為全身性，傷害快速分裂的正常細胞（皮膚、毛髮、胃腸黏膜、精子、骨髓）；組合療程依最低點（nadir）安排。詳見第46章。
  • External beam radiation: client is not radioactive; teach skin care (no lotions or powders unless prescribed, mild soap, pat dry, avoid sun, do not wash off markings). Fatigue is the most common side effect.外部放射治療：病人不具放射性；皮膚護理：未經處方不塗乳液粉劑、溫和肥皂、輕拍擦乾、避免日曬、勿洗掉標記。疲倦為最常見副作用。
  • Brachytherapy: sealed source implants make the client radioactive but excreta are not; unsealed source (oral/IV) makes body fluids radioactive for about 48 hours.近接治療：密封式植入使病人具放射性，但排泄物不具放射性；非密封式（口服/靜脈）使體液具放射性約48小時。
  • Sealed implant precautions: private room, sign on door, 30 minutes per caregiver per shift, dosimeter badge, lead shielding, rotate staff, no pregnant staff, no children under 16 or pregnant visitors, visitors 6 feet away and 30 minutes/day, never touch a dislodged source (use long forceps into a lead container).密封植入防護：單人房、門上警示、每位照護者每班30分鐘、佩戴劑量計、鉛屏蔽、輪替人員、懷孕者不可照護、16歲以下兒童與孕婦不可探視、訪客距離6呎且每日30分鐘、掉落放射源不可徒手碰觸（以長夾放入鉛容器）。
  • After implant removal the client is not radioactive; cancer is not contagious.移除植入物後病人不再具放射性；癌症不會傳染。

3Hematopoietic stem cell transplantation造血幹細胞移植

  • BMT or PBSCT replaces marrow destroyed by high-dose chemotherapy or radiation; used for leukemia, lymphoma, myeloma and neuroblastoma.骨髓或周邊血幹細胞移植用以取代被高劑量化放療破壞的骨髓；用於白血病、淋巴瘤、骨髓瘤與神經母細胞瘤。
  • Donor types: autologous (own cells, most common, harvested in remission and frozen), allogeneic (sibling/unrelated), syngeneic (identical twin).捐贈類型：自體（最常見，緩解期採集冷凍）、異體（手足或非親屬）、同基因（同卵雙胞胎）。
  • Steps: harvest (apheresis 4-6 hours, or iliac crest aspiration), conditioning regimen, infusion through central line like a transfusion, then engraftment when WBC, RBC and platelets begin to rise (2-5 weeks).步驟：採集（血球分離4-6小時或髂骨抽取）、前處置（conditioning）、經中央靜脈如輸血般輸注、植入成功時白血球、紅血球、血小板開始回升（2-5週）。
  • Until engraftment the client has no natural immunity: infection, bleeding, neutropenia and thrombocytopenia are the main risks.植入前病人無自然免疫力：主要風險為感染、出血、嗜中性球與血小板低下。
  • Complications: failure to engraft, graft-versus-host disease (allogeneic: donor cells attack recipient), hepatic veno-occlusive disease (RUQ pain, jaundice, ascites, weight gain, hepatomegaly).併發症：植入失敗、移植物抗宿主病（異體：捐贈細胞攻擊受贈者）、肝靜脈阻塞症（右上腹痛、黃疸、腹水、體重增加、肝腫大）。

4Leukemia, Hodgkin's lymphoma and multiple myeloma白血病、何杰金氏淋巴瘤與多發性骨髓瘤

  • Leukemia is abnormal, immature leukocyte overproduction in marrow; ALL (children under 15), AML (15-39), CML (fourth decade), CLL (over 50).白血病為骨髓中不成熟白血球異常增生；ALL（15歲以下）、AML（15-39歲）、CML（30多歲，人生第四個十年）、CLL（50歲以上）。
  • Findings: anemia (pallor, fatigue, tachycardia), thrombocytopenia (petechiae, bleeding), fever, enlarged nodes/spleen/liver, bone pain; bone marrow biopsy shows blasts.表現：貧血（蒼白、疲倦、心搏過速）、血小板低下（瘀點、出血）、發燒、淋巴結脾肝腫大、骨痛；骨髓切片可見芽細胞。
  • Infection is a leading cause of death: protective isolation, private room (HEPA if possible), strict hand hygiene, no fresh flowers or plants, neutropenic diet without raw fruits and vegetables, no rectal temperatures or invasive procedures, no live vaccines.感染為主要死因：保護性隔離、單人房（可行時HEPA）、嚴格洗手、不放鮮花植物、嗜中性球低下飲食（不吃生鮮蔬果）、不測肛溫與避免侵入性處置、不打活性疫苗。
  • Bleeding: risk under 50,000/mm3, spontaneous under 20,000/mm3 (may need platelet transfusion); soft toothbrush, electric razor, stool softeners, avoid NSAIDs, no suppositories or enemas, pad side rails.出血：低於50,000/mm3有風險，低於20,000/mm3可能自發性出血（可能需輸血小板）；軟毛牙刷、電動刮鬍刀、軟便劑、避免NSAIDs、不用栓劑灌腸、床欄加墊。
  • Mucositis care (Box 45.8): soft brush, rinse, avoid alcohol or glycerin mouthwash, soft bland warm foods. Fatigue: small frequent high-calorie meals and rest.口腔黏膜炎照護（Box 45.8）：軟毛牙刷、漱口、避免含酒精或甘油漱口水、溫和軟質食物。疲倦：少量多餐高熱量並充分休息。
  • Chemotherapy phases: induction (remission), consolidation (cure), maintenance (stay in remission).化療階段：誘導（緩解）、鞏固（治癒）、維持（保持緩解）。
  • Hodgkin's: Reed-Sternberg cells in nodes; cervical nodes first, fever, night sweats, weight loss, fatigue, painless nodes; PET/CT stage; early stage treated with external radiation, advanced with multiagent chemotherapy; discuss sperm banking.何杰金氏：淋巴結出現Reed-Sternberg細胞；頸部淋巴結先受侵、發燒、盜汗、體重減輕、疲倦；以PET/CT分期；早期以外部放療，晚期多藥化療；討論精子保存。
  • Multiple myeloma: malignant plasma cells destroy bone; bone pain (ribs, spine, pelvis), pathological fractures, hypercalcemia, high uric acid, renal failure, recurrent infection, anemia.多發性骨髓瘤：惡性漿細胞破壞骨骼；肋骨脊椎骨盆疼痛、病理性骨折、高血鈣、高尿酸、腎衰竭、反覆感染、貧血。
  • Urine Bence Jones protein and bone marrow aspirate with immature plasma cells support diagnosis.尿液Bence Jones蛋白與骨髓抽吸出現不成熟漿細胞可輔助診斷。
  • Care: at least 2 L fluid per day, ambulation, handle gently to avoid fractures, IV fluids and diuretics for calcium, bisphosphonates, analgesics, monitor kidney function.照護：每日至少2 L水分、鼓勵活動、輕柔搬動防骨折、靜脈輸液與利尿劑降鈣、雙磷酸鹽、止痛、監測腎功能。

5Testicular, cervical, ovarian, endometrial cancer睪丸癌、子宮頸癌、卵巢癌與子宮內膜癌

  • Testicular cancer (ages 15-40; cryptorchidism risk): painless swelling, heavy 'dragging' feeling; do monthly testicular self-exam after a warm shower; unilateral orchiectomy and possibly retroperitoneal node dissection; offer sperm banking; ice pack first 48 hours.睪丸癌（15-40歲；隱睪症為危險因子）：無痛腫大、下墜感；溫水淋浴後每月自我檢查；單側睪丸切除與可能的腹膜後淋巴結清除；提供精子保存；術後前48小時冰敷。
  • Cervical cancer: HPV, smoking, early intercourse, multiple partners; painless postcoital bleeding and foul discharge; Pap test and HPV vaccine prevent; treatments include laser, cryosurgery (watery discharge several weeks, no tampons or intercourse), conization, hysterectomy and pelvic exenteration.子宮頸癌：HPV、吸菸、初次性行為早、多重性伴侶；無痛性性交後出血與惡臭分泌物；抹片與HPV疫苗可預防；治療包括雷射、冷凍（數週水樣分泌物，避免棉條與性行為）、錐狀切除、子宮切除與骨盆腔臟器切除。
  • After hysterectomy: monitor bleeding (more than one saturated pad per hour is excessive), SCDs, coughing and deep breathing, avoid lifting over 20 lb, no intercourse for 3-6 weeks. Pelvic exenteration: ileal conduit, colostomy, sexual counseling.子宮切除術後：監測出血（每小時浸濕超過一片護墊為過量）、使用間歇壓迫裝置、咳嗽深呼吸、避免提超過20磅、3-6週內不性行為。骨盆臟器切除：迴腸導管、結腸造口、性諮詢。
  • Ovarian cancer: often silent until advanced, bloating, urinary frequency, CA-125; surgery with debulking plus chemotherapy, intraperitoneal chemotherapy.卵巢癌：晚期前常無症狀，腹脹、頻尿、CA-125上升；手術減積加化療、腹腔內化療。
  • Endometrial cancer: postmenopausal abnormal bleeding; risk from estrogen, obesity, nulliparity, diabetes, hypertension; total hysterectomy with bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy; progesterone for estrogen-dependent tumors.子宮內膜癌：停經後異常出血；危險因子為雌激素、肥胖、未生育、糖尿病、高血壓；全子宮與雙側輸卵管卵巢切除；雌激素依賴型可用黃體素。

6Breast cancer and mastectomy care乳癌與乳房切除術照護

  • Risks: age over 65, family history, early menarche and late menopause, nulliparity, HRT, obesity. Findings: fixed, irregular, painless mass (upper outer quadrant), dimpling, peau d'orange, nipple retraction, axillary nodes.危險因子：年齡大於65歲、家族史、初經早停經晚、未生育、荷爾蒙治療、肥胖。表現：固定、不規則、無痛腫塊（外上象限）、凹陷、橘皮樣皮膚、乳頭內縮、腋下淋巴結腫。
  • Breast self-exam 7-10 days after menses (postmenopausal on a set day monthly); trastuzumab for HER-2 positive tumors.乳房自我檢查於月經後7-10天；停經者每月固定日期；HER-2陽性可用trastuzumab。
  • Procedures: lumpectomy, simple mastectomy, modified radical mastectomy (nodes removed).手術：腫瘤切除術、單純乳房切除術、改良式根治性乳房切除術（含淋巴結）。
  • Postoperative: semi-Fowler's, affected arm elevated above heart, manage Jackson-Pratt drain, deep breathing, monitor for lymphedema and fluid collection.術後：半坐臥、患側手臂高於心臟、管理Jackson-Pratt引流、深呼吸、監測淋巴水腫與積液。
  • No IVs, injections, BP or venipuncture in the affected arm; protect the arm, gloves for gardening, no tight jewelry, no heavy bag, medical alert bracelet.患側手臂不可靜脈注射、注射、量血壓或抽血；保護手臂、園藝戴手套、避免緊束飾物、不提重物、佩戴醫療警示手環。

7GI cancers: esophageal, gastric, pancreatic, colorectal消化道癌：食道、胃、胰臟、大腸

  • Esophageal: dysphagia, odynophagia; smoking, alcohol, reflux, Barrett's; watch aspiration and nutrition.食道癌：吞嚥困難、吞嚥痛；吸菸、酒精、逆流、Barrett食道；注意吸入與營養。
  • Gastric: H. pylori, smoked/salted foods; early indigestion, late weight loss, iron-deficiency anemia, epigastric mass; Billroth I and II or total gastrectomy; postoperative NG drainage bloody then brown then yellow; do not irrigate or remove NG tube; watch dumping, B12 deficiency.胃癌：幽門螺旋桿菌、燻製鹽漬食物；早期消化不良，晚期體重減輕、缺鐵性貧血、上腹腫塊；Billroth I與II或全胃切除；術後鼻胃管引流液先血性再褐色後黃色；勿自行沖洗或拔除；注意傾倒症候群與B12缺乏。
  • Pancreatic: jaundice, clay stools, dark urine, weight loss, glucose intolerance, poor prognosis; Whipple procedure; monitor blood glucose.胰臟癌：黃疸、陶土色便、深色尿、體重減輕、葡萄糖耐受不良、預後差；Whipple手術；監測血糖。
  • Colorectal: blood in stool most common; ascending tumor = diarrhea, descending = constipation or ribbon stool, rectal = alternating; complications: perforation, obstruction, abscess, hemorrhage.大腸直腸癌：糞便帶血最常見；升結腸=腹瀉，降結腸=便祕或細扁糞便，直腸=便祕腹瀉交替；併發症：穿孔、阻塞、膿瘍、出血。
  • Colostomy: stoma should be red or pink, pale means low Hgb/Hct, dark blue/purple/black means ischemia - call surgeon; ascending colostomy liquid stool, descending near-formed; empty pouch at one-third full. Ileostomy: dark green then yellow liquid stool, risk dehydration.結腸造口：造口應紅或粉紅，蒼白表示血紅素/血容比低，暗藍紫黑表示缺血須通知外科；升結腸造口為液狀便，降結腸接近成形；袋子1/3滿時倒空。迴腸造口：深綠後轉黃的液狀便，有脫水風險。

8Lung and laryngeal cancer肺癌與喉癌

  • Lung: smoking and pollutants; SCLC vs NSCLC; cough, hemoptysis, wheezing, hoarseness, weight loss, clubbing; diagnosed with imaging, bronchoscopy and sputum cytology.肺癌：吸菸與污染；小細胞肺癌與非小細胞肺癌；咳嗽、咳血、喘鳴、聲音沙啞、體重減輕、杵狀指；以影像、支氣管鏡與痰液細胞學診斷。
  • Airway is the priority; Fowler's position, oxygen, humidification, high-calorie diet; thoracentesis or pleurodesis for malignant effusion.氣道為優先；採Fowler's臥位、給氧、加濕、高熱量飲食；惡性肋膜積液可行胸腔穿刺或肋膜沾黏術。
  • Surgery: pneumonectomy, lobectomy, segmental resection. After pneumonectomy no chest tube drainage is usually used; fluid fills the space and consolidates, stabilizing the mediastinum. Avoid complete lateral turning per surgeon.手術：全肺、肺葉、肺節切除。全肺切除通常不放胸管引流，積液會填滿並固化，穩定縱膈；依醫囑避免完全側臥。
  • Laryngeal: smoking plus alcohol, persistent hoarseness, painless neck mass, dysphagia; total laryngectomy leaves a permanent stoma.喉癌：吸菸加飲酒、持續聲音沙啞、無痛頸部腫塊、吞嚥困難；全喉切除留下永久性氣切造口。
  • Post-laryngectomy: high Fowler's, suction, airway patency, neck hemorrhage and edema, tube feedings, stoma guard and shower shield, humidity, fluids 3000 mL, medical alert bracelet.喉切除術後：高坐臥、抽吸、維持氣道通暢、監測頸部出血與水腫、管灌飲食、造口護罩與淋浴護罩、增加環境濕度、每日液體3000 mL、佩戴醫療警示手環。
  • Speech options: esophageal speech, electrolarynx, tracheoesophageal fistula prosthesis.語言復健：食道發聲、電子喉、氣管食道瘻管發聲閥。
