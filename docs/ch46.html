Covers chemotherapy drug classes, their cell-cycle targets, signature toxicities, safe handling, extravasation and anaphylaxis management, plus colony-stimulating factors and anemia drugs. Focus on matching each drug to its special adverse effect and the monitoring it needs.涵蓋化療藥物分類、作用週期、特徵性毒性、安全處理、藥物外滲與過敏處置，以及群落刺激因子與貧血藥物。重點在將各藥物對應其特殊不良反應與監測項目。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Antineoplastic basics抗腫瘤藥物基礎

  • Chemotherapy kills or inhibits neoplastic cells for cure, longer survival, or fewer life-threatening complications; normal rapidly dividing cells are also hurt, which causes most side effects.化療殺死或抑制腫瘤細胞，目的為治癒、延長存活或減少危及生命的併發症；正常的快速分裂細胞也會受損，造成多數副作用。
  • Cell cycle phase-specific drugs act only in one phase; phase-nonspecific drugs act in any phase (Fig. 46.1).週期特異性藥物只在特定細胞週期作用；非特異性藥物在任何階段都有效（圖46.1）。
  • Drugs are usually combined, and may be paired with surgery or radiation; IV is the most common route, with oral, intra-arterial, isolated limb perfusion or intracavitary also used.通常合併多種藥物，並可與手術或放療並用；最常見為靜脈給藥，也可口服、動脈內、肢體隔離灌注或體腔內給藥。
  • Dosing is based on body surface area (BSA), so measure current height and weight before each dose.劑量依體表面積(BSA)計算，每次給藥前須測量當前身高與體重。
  • Chemotherapy causes rapid cell death with uric acid release; allopurinol may be given, especially in leukemia and lymphoma.化療造成細胞快速死亡並釋出尿酸；尤其白血病與淋巴瘤可給予allopurinol。

2Side effects and general nursing care副作用與一般護理

  • Common effects: anorexia, nausea/vomiting, mucositis, alopecia, diarrhea, anemia, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, infertility and neuropathy.常見副作用：厭食、噁心嘔吐、黏膜炎、落髮、腹瀉、貧血、嗜中性球低下、血小板低下、不孕與神經病變。
  • Monitor CBC, WBC, platelets, uric acid and electrolytes; give antiemetics hours before and 12-48 hours after chemotherapy; encourage at least 2000 mL fluid per day.監測全血球計數、白血球、血小板、尿酸與電解質；化療前數小時及後12-48小時給予止吐劑；每日至少攝取2000 mL液體。
  • Platelets below 50,000/mm3 raise bleeding risk; below 20,000/mm3 spontaneous bleeding may occur. Start bleeding precautions and avoid IM injections and venipunctures where possible.血小板低於50,000/mm3出血風險增加；低於20,000/mm3可能自發性出血。應啟動出血預防措施，盡量避免肌肉注射與靜脈穿刺。
  • ANC = WBC x % neutrophils; ANC below 1000 means neutropenia: hold the drug, notify the oncologist, and start neutropenic precautions (example: ANC 900).ANC=白血球數x嗜中性球百分比；ANC低於1000為嗜中性球低下：暫停給藥、通知腫瘤科醫師並啟動嗜中性球低下防護（例如ANC 900）。
  • Teach: avoid crowds and sick contacts, soft toothbrush, electric razor, no aspirin, no live vaccines, consult the provider before any vaccination, oral hygiene and mouth rinses.衛教：避免人群與感染者、使用軟毛牙刷與電動刮鬍刀、避免阿斯匹靈、不可施打活性疫苗、接種前先詢問醫師、加強口腔衛生。
  • Psychosocial: discuss hair loss and wigs before treatment, hair regrows months after the last dose; teratogenic so contraception is needed; infertility may be permanent, so offer sperm banking or egg preservation.心理社會：治療前討論落髮與假髮，髮量於末次治療後數月長回；具致畸性需避孕；不孕可能不可逆，可提供精子或卵子保存。

3Safe handling, extravasation and anaphylaxis安全處理、藥物外滲與過敏性反應

  • Prepare IV chemotherapy in a biological safety cabinet; wear gloves, gown, eye protection and mask; pregnant nurses should avoid preparing or giving chemotherapy; discard in biohazard containers.在生物安全櫃中調配靜脈化療藥；穿戴手套、隔離衣、護目與口罩；懷孕護理師應避免調配或給藥；廢棄物丟入生物危害容器。
  • Vesicants go through a central line when possible; with a peripheral line, check blood return first.起疱劑盡可能經中央靜脈導管給予；若用周邊靜脈須先確認有回血。
  • Extravasation (pain, redness, swelling, slowed infusion): stop infusion, notify the provider, expect heat or ice depending on the drug and possibly an antidote injected into the site. Never restart in the same vein or speed the flow.藥物外滲（疼痛、紅腫、滴速變慢）：停止輸注、通知醫師，依藥物使用冷敷或熱敷，可能需於局部注射解毒劑；不可在同一條靜脈重新建立或加快滴速。
  • Anaphylaxis signs: dyspnea, chest tightness, urticaria, tachycardia, hypotension, anxiety, cyanosis. Stop the drug, call rapid response, give oxygen, keep IV open with saline, prepare epinephrine and intubation.過敏性休克徵象：呼吸困難、胸悶、蕁麻疹、心搏過速、低血壓、焦慮、發紺。立即停藥、呼叫快速反應小組、給氧、以生理食鹽水維持靜脈通路、備epinephrine與插管。
  • Prevent anaphylaxis: allergy history, test dose if prescribed, stay with the client, have emergency equipment ready.預防：詢問過敏史、依醫囑做測試劑量、全程陪伴病人、備妥急救設備。

4Alkylating agents and antitumor antibiotics烷化劑與抗腫瘤抗生素

  • Alkylating agents (nitrogen mustards, nitrosoureas, platinum compounds) break the DNA helix; they are cell cycle nonspecific.烷化劑（氮芥類、亞硝基脲類、鉑類）破壞DNA雙股螺旋，屬非週期特異性。
  • Busulfan, chlorambucil and mechlorethamine raise uric acid; cisplatin causes ototoxicity, tinnitus, nephrotoxicity, hypokalemia, hypocalcemia and hypomagnesemia.Busulfan、chlorambucil、mechlorethamine使尿酸升高；cisplatin造成耳毒性、耳鳴、腎毒性、低鉀、低鈣與低鎂。
  • Cyclophosphamide and ifosfamide cause hemorrhagic cystitis: drink 2-3 L/day; mesna protects against ifosfamide cystitis; oral cyclophosphamide is taken without food.Cyclophosphamide與ifosfamide造成出血性膀胱炎：每日飲水2-3 L；mesna可預防ifosfamide膀胱炎；口服cyclophosphamide宜空腹。
  • Antitumor antibiotics (bleomycin, daunorubicin, doxorubicin, idarubicin) interfere with DNA/RNA synthesis and are nonspecific.抗腫瘤抗生素（bleomycin、daunorubicin、doxorubicin、idarubicin）干擾DNA/RNA合成，屬非特異性。
  • Daunorubicin/doxorubicin/idarubicin: cardiotoxicity, heart failure (crackles, edema, weight gain), ECG changes; dexrazoxane is cardioprotective. Bleomycin: pulmonary toxicity and fibrosis, so follow PFTs and lung sounds.Daunorubicin/doxorubicin/idarubicin：心毒性、心衰竭（囉音、水腫、體重增加）、心電圖變化；dexrazoxane具心臟保護作用。Bleomycin：肺毒性與肺纖維化，需追蹤肺功能與呼吸音。

5Antimetabolites, mitotic inhibitors, topoisomerase inhibitors抗代謝藥、有絲分裂抑制劑、拓樸異構酶抑制劑

  • Antimetabolites act in the S phase: cytarabine, fluorouracil, mercaptopurine and methotrexate; bone marrow depression, stomatitis, diarrhea, alopecia.抗代謝藥作用於S期：cytarabine、fluorouracil、mercaptopurine、methotrexate；骨髓抑制、口腔炎、腹瀉、落髮。
  • Fluorouracil: cerebellar dysfunction (dizziness, weakness, ataxia) and photosensitivity; methotrexate: photosensitivity, hepatotoxicity; give leucovorin rescue with high-dose methotrexate.Fluorouracil：小腦功能異常（頭暈、無力、共濟失調）與光敏感；methotrexate：光敏感、肝毒性；高劑量methotrexate須給leucovorin解救。
  • Vinca alkaloids (vincristine, vinblastine, vinorelbine) act in the M phase: peripheral neuropathy, ptosis, hoarseness, constipation and paralytic ileus; give by minibag, not IV push.長春花生物鹼（vincristine、vinblastine、vinorelbine）作用於M期：周邊神經病變、眼瞼下垂、聲音沙啞、便祕與麻痺性腸阻塞；以小袋稀釋輸注，不可靜脈推注。
  • Taxanes (paclitaxel, docetaxel) are also mitotic inhibitors and can trigger hypersensitivity reactions.紫杉醇類（paclitaxel、docetaxel）亦為有絲分裂抑制劑，可能引發過敏反應。
  • Topoisomerase inhibitors (etoposide, irinotecan, topotecan) act in G2 and S phases: myelosuppression, diarrhea, orthostatic hypotension, hypersensitivity; infuse etoposide slowly over 30-60 minutes.拓樸異構酶抑制劑（etoposide、irinotecan、topotecan）作用於G2與S期：骨髓抑制、腹瀉、姿勢性低血壓、過敏；etoposide須緩慢輸注30-60分鐘。

6Hormonal agents and enzymes荷爾蒙藥物與酵素

  • Hormonal agents block or change hormones in hormone-sensitive tumors: estrogens, antiestrogens (tamoxifen, anastrozole, letrozole), antiandrogens, progestins (megestrol), leuprolide, mitotane, asparaginase.荷爾蒙藥物阻斷或改變荷爾蒙敏感腫瘤的激素環境：雌激素、抗雌激素（tamoxifen、anastrozole、letrozole）、抗雄激素、黃體素（megestrol）、leuprolide、mitotane、asparaginase。
  • Effects: nausea, leukopenia, hot flashes, weight gain, edema, hypertension, thromboembolism, sex-characteristic changes (gynecomastia, masculinizing effects).影響：噁心、白血球低下、熱潮紅、體重增加、水腫、高血壓、血栓栓塞、性徵改變（男性女乳症、女性男性化）。
  • Tamoxifen: hypercalcemia, high cholesterol and triglycerides, venous thromboembolism risk; used to treat and prevent breast cancer. Monitor calcium.Tamoxifen：高血鈣、膽固醇與三酸甘油酯升高、靜脈血栓風險；用於治療及預防乳癌。需監測血鈣。
  • Asparaginase impairs pancreatic function: contraindicated with pancreatitis history; watch nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain. Megestrol: caution with venous thromboembolism history.Asparaginase損害胰臟功能：有胰臟炎病史者禁用；注意噁心、嘔吐、腹痛。Megestrol：有靜脈血栓病史者需謹慎。

7Immunotherapy, targeted therapy, other agents免疫療法、標靶治療與其他藥物

  • Immunomodulators (interleukins, interferons) stimulate the immune system to destroy cancer cells.免疫調節劑（介白素、干擾素）刺激免疫系統辨識並消滅癌細胞。
  • Colony-stimulating factors speed marrow recovery: filgrastim and pegfilgrastim (G-CSF), sargramostim (GM-CSF), epoetin and darbepoetin (erythropoietin).群落刺激因子加速骨髓恢復：filgrastim、pegfilgrastim（G-CSF）、sargramostim（GM-CSF）、epoetin與darbepoetin（紅血球生成素）。
  • Targeted therapy uses monoclonal antibodies (rituximab, trastuzumab, bevacizumab, cetuximab) and small molecule inhibitors (imatinib, erlotinib); monoclonal antibodies can cause allergic reactions.標靶治療包括單株抗體（rituximab、trastuzumab、bevacizumab、cetuximab）與小分子抑制劑（imatinib、erlotinib）；單株抗體可能引起過敏反應。
  • Other agents: altretamine (ovarian cancer), denileukin diftitox and bexarotene (cutaneous T-cell lymphoma), pegaspargase (ALL when asparaginase cannot be used).其他藥物：altretamine（卵巢癌）、denileukin diftitox與bexarotene（皮膚T細胞淋巴瘤）、pegaspargase（無法使用asparaginase的急性淋巴性白血病）。

8Medications for anemia貧血治療藥物

  • Iron-deficiency anemia: iron. Vitamin B12 deficiency: B12. Folate deficiency: folate.缺鐵性貧血：補鐵；維生素B12缺乏：補B12；葉酸缺乏：補葉酸。
  • Acute blood-loss anemia: transfusion of packed red cells, platelets or fresh frozen plasma depending on cause.急性失血性貧血：依原因輸注濃縮紅血球、血小板或新鮮冷凍血漿。
  • Anemia of chronic disease: iron and erythropoietic, leukopoietic and thrombopoietic growth factors.慢性病貧血：鐵劑與紅血球、白血球、血小板生成因子。

Antimetabolites, mitotic inhibitors, topoisomerase inhibitors抗代謝藥、有絲分裂抑制劑、拓樸異構酶抑制劑
  • Antimetabolites act in the S phase: cytarabine, fluorouracil, mercaptopurine and methotrexate; bone marrow depression, stomatitis, diarrhea, alopecia.抗代謝藥作用於S期：cytarabine、fluorouracil、mercaptopurine、methotrexate；骨髓抑制、口腔炎、腹瀉、落髮。
  • Fluorouracil: cerebellar dysfunction (dizziness, weakness, ataxia) and photosensitivity; methotrexate: photosensitivity, hepatotoxicity; give leucovorin rescue with high-dose methotrexate.Fluorouracil：小腦功能異常（頭暈、無力、共濟失調）與光敏感；methotrexate：光敏感、肝毒性；高劑量methotrexate須給leucovorin解救。
  • Vinca alkaloids (vincristine, vinblastine, vinorelbine) act in the M phase: peripheral neuropathy, ptosis, hoarseness, constipation and paralytic ileus; give by minibag, not IV push.長春花生物鹼（vincristine、vinblastine、vinorelbine）作用於M期：周邊神經病變、眼瞼下垂、聲音沙啞、便祕與麻痺性腸阻塞；以小袋稀釋輸注，不可靜脈推注。
  • Taxanes (paclitaxel, docetaxel) are also mitotic inhibitors and can trigger hypersensitivity reactions.紫杉醇類（paclitaxel、docetaxel）亦為有絲分裂抑制劑，可能引發過敏反應。
  • Topoisomerase inhibitors (etoposide, irinotecan, topotecan) act in G2 and S phases: myelosuppression, diarrhea, orthostatic hypotension, hypersensitivity; infuse etoposide slowly over 30-60 minutes.拓樸異構酶抑制劑（etoposide、irinotecan、topotecan）作用於G2與S期：骨髓抑制、腹瀉、姿勢性低血壓、過敏；etoposide須緩慢輸注30-60分鐘。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Hormonal agents and enzymes荷爾蒙藥物與酵素
  • Hormonal agents block or change hormones in hormone-sensitive tumors: estrogens, antiestrogens (tamoxifen, anastrozole, letrozole), antiandrogens, progestins (megestrol), leuprolide, mitotane, asparaginase.荷爾蒙藥物阻斷或改變荷爾蒙敏感腫瘤的激素環境：雌激素、抗雌激素（tamoxifen、anastrozole、letrozole）、抗雄激素、黃體素（megestrol）、leuprolide、mitotane、asparaginase。
  • Effects: nausea, leukopenia, hot flashes, weight gain, edema, hypertension, thromboembolism, sex-characteristic changes (gynecomastia, masculinizing effects).影響：噁心、白血球低下、熱潮紅、體重增加、水腫、高血壓、血栓栓塞、性徵改變（男性女乳症、女性男性化）。
  • Tamoxifen: hypercalcemia, high cholesterol and triglycerides, venous thromboembolism risk; used to treat and prevent breast cancer. Monitor calcium.Tamoxifen：高血鈣、膽固醇與三酸甘油酯升高、靜脈血栓風險；用於治療及預防乳癌。需監測血鈣。
  • Asparaginase impairs pancreatic function: contraindicated with pancreatitis history; watch nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain. Megestrol: caution with venous thromboembolism history.Asparaginase損害胰臟功能：有胰臟炎病史者禁用；注意噁心、嘔吐、腹痛。Megestrol：有靜脈血栓病史者需謹慎。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Immunotherapy, targeted therapy, other agents免疫療法、標靶治療與其他藥物
  • Immunomodulators (interleukins, interferons) stimulate the immune system to destroy cancer cells.免疫調節劑（介白素、干擾素）刺激免疫系統辨識並消滅癌細胞。
  • Colony-stimulating factors speed marrow recovery: filgrastim and pegfilgrastim (G-CSF), sargramostim (GM-CSF), epoetin and darbepoetin (erythropoietin).群落刺激因子加速骨髓恢復：filgrastim、pegfilgrastim（G-CSF）、sargramostim（GM-CSF）、epoetin與darbepoetin（紅血球生成素）。
  • Targeted therapy uses monoclonal antibodies (rituximab, trastuzumab, bevacizumab, cetuximab) and small molecule inhibitors (imatinib, erlotinib); monoclonal antibodies can cause allergic reactions.標靶治療包括單株抗體（rituximab、trastuzumab、bevacizumab、cetuximab）與小分子抑制劑（imatinib、erlotinib）；單株抗體可能引起過敏反應。
  • Other agents: altretamine (ovarian cancer), denileukin diftitox and bexarotene (cutaneous T-cell lymphoma), pegaspargase (ALL when asparaginase cannot be used).其他藥物：altretamine（卵巢癌）、denileukin diftitox與bexarotene（皮膚T細胞淋巴瘤）、pegaspargase（無法使用asparaginase的急性淋巴性白血病）。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Medications for anemia貧血治療藥物
  • Iron-deficiency anemia: iron. Vitamin B12 deficiency: B12. Folate deficiency: folate.缺鐵性貧血：補鐵；維生素B12缺乏：補B12；葉酸缺乏：補葉酸。
  • Acute blood-loss anemia: transfusion of packed red cells, platelets or fresh frozen plasma depending on cause.急性失血性貧血：依原因輸注濃縮紅血球、血小板或新鮮冷凍血漿。
  • Anemia of chronic disease: iron and erythropoietic, leukopoietic and thrombopoietic growth factors.慢性病貧血：鐵劑與紅血球、白血球、血小板生成因子。

Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Phase-specific drugs: S = antimetabolites; M = mitotic inhibitors (vinca, taxanes); G2/S = topoisomerase inhibitors. Nonspecific = Alkylating, Antitumor antibiotics, Hormonal (AAH).週期特異性：S期=抗代謝藥；M期=有絲分裂抑制劑（長春花、紫杉類）；G2/S期=拓樸異構酶抑制劑。非特異性=烷化劑、抗腫瘤抗生素、荷爾蒙（AAH）。

1A client is scheduled for chemotherapy. The morning CBC shows WBC 3000/mm3 with 25% neutrophils. What should the nurse do?病人預定化療，晨間血液檢查白血球3000/mm3，嗜中性球25%。護理師應如何處理？
