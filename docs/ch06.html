This chapter covers ethical principles and dilemmas, regulation of nursing practice, liability, consents, HIPAA confidentiality, occurrence reports, documentation, advance directives and mandatory reporting. These topics are heavily tested under Management of Care.本章涵蓋倫理原則與兩難、護理執業規範、法律責任、同意書、HIPAA 保密、事件報告、文件記錄、預立醫囑與強制通報。這些內容常見於「照護管理」考題。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Ethics, principles and advocacy倫理、原則與倡議

  • Ethics distinguishes right from wrong using a body of knowledge; morals reflect customs or beliefs; values influence decisions; values clarification analyzes your own values.倫理依知識體系分辨對錯；道德反映習俗或信念；價值觀影響決策；價值澄清是分析自身價值觀。
  • Principles: autonomy, nonmaleficence (do no harm), beneficence (do good; paternalism is undesirable), justice, veracity (truth), fidelity (keep promises).原則：自主、不傷害、行善（家長式作風不可取）、公正、誠實、忠誠。
  • Ethical codes (ICN, ANA) are not legally binding but boards of nursing can discipline unprofessional conduct.倫理守則（ICN、ANA）無法律效力，但護理委員會可懲處不專業行為。
  • An ethical dilemma is a conflict between two or more ethical principles with no clearly correct choice; use ethical reasoning (gather data, examine values, state the problem, consider options, negotiate, evaluate).倫理兩難為兩項以上倫理原則衝突、無明確正解；運用倫理推理（蒐集資料、檢視價值、說明問題、考慮選項、協商、評值）。
  • An advocate speaks for the client and supports the client's decision even if it conflicts with the nurse's own; ethics committees offer interprofessional consultation.倡議者為病人發聲並支持其決定（即使與自己意見不同）；倫理委員會提供跨專業諮詢。

2Regulation of nursing practice護理執業規範

  • The Nurse Practice Act (state statute) sets education requirements, scope of practice, licensure and grounds for discipline; nurses must know their state's act.護理執業法（州法）規範教育要求、執業範圍、執照與懲處依據；護理師須熟悉所在州法規。
  • Standards of care define what clients can expect; failing both standards and agency policy makes the nurse clearly liable. Respondeat superior makes the employer liable for employees' negligence within employment.照護標準界定病人可期待的照護；同時違反標準與機構政策則責任明確。僱主責任原則使僱主需為員工執行職務中的過失負責。
  • Policies are not laws but courts generally rule against nurses who violate them.政策不是法律，但法院通常判違反政策的護理師敗訴。
  • Understaffing: walking out can be abandonment; report short staffing to administration.人力不足：擅離可能構成遺棄；應向行政單位報告人力不足。
  • Floating is acceptable; refusal is allowed only with union contract protection or proven lack of knowledge. Tell the supervisor your limits, request a resource nurse and accept patients matching your experience.調派支援可接受；僅在工會合約保障或能證明缺乏相關知識時方可拒絕。向主管說明限制、要求資源護理師並接受符合經驗的病人。
  • Discipline reasons: unprofessional conduct, confidentiality breach, insufficient judgment, abuse, unsafe delegation, inaccurate or falsified records, leaving an assignment without notice.懲處原因：不專業行為、違反保密、判斷不足、虐待病人、不安全委派、記錄不實或偽造、未通知即離開工作。

3Legal liability, negligence and malpractice法律責任、過失與醫療疏失

  • Negligence is conduct below the standard of care (acts of commission or omission); malpractice is professional negligence.過失是低於照護標準的行為（作為或不作為）；醫療疏失是專業過失。
  • Four elements of liability: duty, breach of duty, proximate cause, damage/injury.責任四要件：義務、違反義務、因果關係、損害。
  • Negligence examples: medication or IV errors, falls, burns, retained surgical items, failure to monitor, report or delegate properly, incomplete handoff.過失例子：給藥或輸液錯誤、跌倒、燙傷、手術遺留異物、未監測/通報/適當委派、交班不完整。
  • Own liability insurance gives the nurse an attorney whose only interest is the nurse; Good Samaritan laws protect those giving reasonable, uncompensated emergency care.自行投保責任險可有只為護理師利益的律師；好撒馬利亞人法保護提供合理且無償緊急救助者。
  • Controlled substances: locked, signed out, inventory counted.管制藥品：上鎖、簽領、清點庫存。
  • Collective bargaining negotiates wages and conditions; strikes pose a moral dilemma for nurses.集體協商談判薪資與勞動條件；罷工對護理師是道德困境。

4Legal risk areas法律風險領域

  • Assault: threat causing fear of harmful contact; battery: touching without consent.傷害威脅：使人害怕有害接觸；傷害行為：未經同意的觸碰。
  • Invasion of privacy: violating confidentiality; false imprisonment: preventing a client from leaving or unjustified restraints; a competent client may sign an AMA form.侵犯隱私：違反保密；非法拘禁：不當阻止離院或無正當臨床需求使用約束；有行為能力者可簽自動出院（AMA）。
  • Defamation: libel (written) or slander (spoken); fraud: deliberate deception for gain.毀謗：書面（誹謗）或口頭（誹謗/口誹）；詐欺：蓄意欺騙以牟利。
  • Exception: acutely disturbed mental health clients who pose risk may need least-restrictive interventions first, then restraint if behavior demands.例外：急性精神困擾且有危險者，先採最低限制措施，必要時才約束。

5Patient rights, organ donation and consents病人權利、器官捐贈與同意書

  • The Patient Care Partnership (formerly Bill of Rights) from the AHA must be posted; related laws include ADA, ACA, Mental Health Systems Act.美國醫院協會（AHA）的病人照護夥伴關係（原病人權利法案）須張貼；相關法律含 ADA、ACA、心理健康系統法。
  • Organ donation: adults 18+ may declare intent; heart, lungs, liver require a ventilated brain-dead donor; request usually by trained provider; no delay in funeral or cost to family.器官捐贈：18 歲以上成人可表達意願；心、肺、肝需來自使用呼吸器的腦死者；通常由受訓人員提出請求；不延誤喪禮且家屬無費用。
  • Informed consent is the provider's responsibility (risks, benefits, alternatives, consequences of refusal); the nurse witnesses the signature.知情同意由醫師負責說明（風險、益處、替代方案與拒絕後果）；護理師見證簽名。
  • Consent must be voluntary, witnessed, signed by a competent adult; not valid if sedated, unconscious, intoxicated or demented without a legal representative. Use a professional interpreter when needed.同意須自願、有見證、由有行為能力的成人簽署；鎮靜、昏迷、醉酒或失智而無法定代理人時無效。需要時使用專業口譯。
  • Emergencies waive consent; clients may refuse information or withdraw consent anytime; if a client changes their mind before surgery, hold prep and notify the surgeon.緊急情況可免同意；病人可拒絕資訊或隨時撤回同意；術前病人改變心意，應暫停術前準備並通知外科醫師。
  • Minors need parent/guardian consent except in emergencies, emancipated minors, or services like STI, HIV, birth control, pregnancy, substance abuse and psychiatric care in many states.未成年人需父母或監護人同意，除非緊急、已解除監護的未成年人，或多州允許自行同意的服務（性傳染病、HIV、避孕、懷孕、物質濫用、精神科）。

6HIPAA and confidentialityHIPAA 與保密

  • HIPAA protects personal health information (PHI); clients may inspect, request amendment, request disclosure list, restrict use and file complaints.HIPAA 保護個人健康資訊（PHI）；病人可查閱、要求修正、索取揭露清單、限制使用並提出申訴。
  • PHI may be used for treatment, payment and operations, emergencies, public health, legal proceedings and with signed authorization.PHI 可用於治療、付款與營運、緊急狀況、公共衛生、法律程序，及經簽署授權。
  • Only staff involved in care may access records; share information only on a need-to-know basis in private areas.僅參與照護者可取得記錄；僅在有必要時於私密場所分享。
  • Privacy violations include posting on social media, photographs, open curtains, overheard interviews and unauthorized record access; never share passwords.侵犯隱私包括社群媒體貼文、拍照、未拉簾、談話被聽到、未授權查閱記錄；絕不分享密碼。

7Legal safeguards, orders and documentation法律保障、醫囑與文件記錄

  • Occurrence (incident) reports: factual, not copied or mentioned in the chart; chart the event itself; reassess the client; notify the provider.事件報告：客觀陳述，不影印也不在病歷提及；病歷中記錄事件本身；再評估病人；通知醫師。
  • Carry out prescriptions unless inappropriate or inaccurate; if unclear or unusually high, do not give it and contact the prescriber, then the supervisor.執行醫囑，除非不適當或不正確；若不清楚或劑量異常，不給藥並聯絡開醫囑者，再通報主管。
  • Telephone orders: identify client, date/time, read back, write 't.o.' with prescriber's name and sign; prescriber countersigns.電話醫囑：確認病人、日期時間、覆誦、註明「t.o.」與醫師姓名並簽名；醫師事後簽署。
  • Documentation: objective, factual, timely, black ink, sign each entry, no erasing, no blank spaces, no judgmental terms, no unacceptable abbreviations, correct errors per policy.文件記錄：客觀、事實、即時、黑筆、每筆簽名、不擦拭、不留空白、不用評斷性字眼與禁用縮寫、依政策更正錯誤。
  • Document teaching: what was taught, understanding, who was present.記錄衛教：教了什麼、理解程度、在場者。

8Advance directives and reporting duties預立醫囑與通報責任

  • Patient Self-Determination Act: ask about advance directives on admission and document them.病人自主權法：入院時詢問預立醫囑並記錄。
  • Two types: instructional directive (living will) and durable power of attorney for health care (proxy).兩類：指示型預立醫囑（生前遺囑）與醫療持續委任書（代理人）。
  • DNR requires informed consent and clear definition; without a DNR order, make every effort to resuscitate; review regularly.DNR 需知情同意且定義清楚；無 DNR 醫囑時須盡力急救；定期檢討。
  • Some agencies bar nurses from witnessing legal documents; if allowed, document who was present and the client's conduct.部分機構禁止護理師見證法律文件；若允許，須記錄在場者與病人表現。
  • Mandatory reports: certain communicable diseases, animal bites, suicides, child/elder abuse, domestic violence, gunshot or stab wounds, assaults, homicides.強制通報：特定傳染病、動物咬傷、自殺、兒童/老人虐待、家暴、槍傷或刀傷、攻擊、殺人。
  • Suspected impaired coworker: report confidentially to the supervisor; client safety first. OSHA protects workers who report unsafe conditions; sexual harassment is prohibited.懷疑同事受物質影響：向主管保密通報，病人安全優先。OSHA 保護通報不安全條件者；性騷擾受法律禁止。

  • Occurrence (incident) reports: factual, not copied or mentioned in the chart; chart the event itself; reassess the client; notify the provider.事件報告：客觀陳述，不影印也不在病歷提及；病歷中記錄事件本身；再評估病人；通知醫師。

  • Telephone orders: identify client, date/time, read back, write 't.o.' with prescriber's name and sign; prescriber countersigns.電話醫囑：確認病人、日期時間、覆誦、註明「t.o.」與醫師姓名並簽名；醫師事後簽署。
  • Documentation: objective, factual, timely, black ink, sign each entry, no erasing, no blank spaces, no judgmental terms, no unacceptable abbreviations, correct errors per policy.文件記錄：客觀、事實、即時、黑筆、每筆簽名、不擦拭、不留空白、不用評斷性字眼與禁用縮寫、依政策更正錯誤。
  • Document teaching: what was taught, understanding, who was present.記錄衛教：教了什麼、理解程度、在場者。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Advance directives and reporting duties預立醫囑與通報責任
  • Patient Self-Determination Act: ask about advance directives on admission and document them.病人自主權法：入院時詢問預立醫囑並記錄。
  • Two types: instructional directive (living will) and durable power of attorney for health care (proxy).兩類：指示型預立醫囑（生前遺囑）與醫療持續委任書（代理人）。
  • DNR requires informed consent and clear definition; without a DNR order, make every effort to resuscitate; review regularly.DNR 需知情同意且定義清楚；無 DNR 醫囑時須盡力急救；定期檢討。
  • Some agencies bar nurses from witnessing legal documents; if allowed, document who was present and the client's conduct.部分機構禁止護理師見證法律文件；若允許，須記錄在場者與病人表現。
  • Mandatory reports: certain communicable diseases, animal bites, suicides, child/elder abuse, domestic violence, gunshot or stab wounds, assaults, homicides.強制通報：特定傳染病、動物咬傷、自殺、兒童/老人虐待、家暴、槍傷或刀傷、攻擊、殺人。
  • Suspected impaired coworker: report confidentially to the supervisor; client safety first. OSHA protects workers who report unsafe conditions; sexual harassment is prohibited.懷疑同事受物質影響：向主管保密通報，病人安全優先。OSHA 保護通報不安全條件者；性騷擾受法律禁止。

Reports contain observed facts, not interpretations.報告只記錄觀察到的事實，不含推測。
