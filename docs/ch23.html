This chapter covers high-risk pregnancy conditions: bleeding disorders (abortion, ectopic, molar pregnancy, previa, abruption, DIC), medical disorders (cardiac disease, diabetes, hypertensive disorders, infections), and TORCH/STI infections. Know the key assessment differences and priority nursing actions, especially magnesium sulfate safety and previa versus abruption.本章涵蓋高危險妊娠：出血性問題（流產、子宮外孕、葡萄胎、前置胎盤、胎盤早期剝離、DIC）、內科疾病（心臟病、糖尿病、高血壓疾患、感染）與 TORCH/性傳染病。須熟記關鍵評估差異與優先處置，特別是硫酸鎂安全與前置胎盤對胎盤早剝。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Bleeding, Abortion and Ectopic Pregnancy出血、流產與子宮外孕

  • Implantation bleeding occurs 10-14 days after conception, is light, lasts 1-2 days, and needs no treatment.著床出血發生於受孕後 10-14 天，量少、持續 1-2 天，無需治療。
  • Abortion is loss before 20 weeks. Types: threatened (bleeding and cramping, cervix closed), inevitable (cervix dilating), incomplete (some tissue retained), complete, missed (fetal death retained), habitual (3 or more).流產為 20 週前終止妊娠。類型：先兆流產（出血痙攣、子宮頸未開）、不可避免流產（子宮頸開始擴張）、不完全流產（部分組織殘留）、完全流產、過期流產（胎死留於子宮）、習慣性流產（連續 3 次以上）。
  • Care: monitor vital signs, count and weigh pads (1 g = 1 mL), save tissue, IV fluids, D&C for incomplete abortion, RhoGAM if Rh-negative, emotional support.照護：監測生命徵象、計算並秤重護墊（1 g＝1 mL）、保留排出組織、靜脈輸液、不完全流產做刮除術、Rh 陰性給 RhoGAM、情緒支持。
  • Ectopic pregnancy: usually ampulla of tube; missed period, abdominal pain, dark red/brown spotting; rupture brings severe pain, shoulder pain and shock.子宮外孕：多在輸卵管壺腹；停經、腹痛、暗紅或棕色出血；破裂時劇痛、肩痛與休克。
  • Ectopic management: methotrexate (folic acid antagonist) for early cases, or laparotomy/tube removal or repair; RhoGAM if Rh-negative.子宮外孕處置：早期用 methotrexate（葉酸拮抗劑），或剖腹手術切除/修補輸卵管；Rh 陰性給 RhoGAM。
  • Incompetent cervix: painless dilation at 4th-5th month, treated with cerclage at 10-14 weeks; remove at 37 weeks.子宮頸閉鎖不全：第 4-5 個月無痛性擴張，10-14 週行子宮頸環紮，37 週拆除。

2Hydatidiform Mole and Hyperemesis Gravidarum葡萄胎與妊娠劇吐

  • Mole: abnormal trophoblast, grape-like cluster; no fetal heart tones, hyperemesis, bleeding, early preeclampsia signs before 20 weeks, uterus larger than dates, hCG over 100,000 mIU/mL, snowstorm ultrasound.葡萄胎：滋養層異常、葡萄串狀；無胎心、嚴重孕吐、出血、20 週前出現子癇前症徵象、子宮大於週數、hCG 超過 100,000 mIU/mL、超音波呈暴風雪樣。
  • Treat with suction evacuation; follow hCG every 1-2 weeks until normal, then monthly for 1 year; avoid pregnancy during follow-up (risk of choriocarcinoma).以抽吸術清除；每 1-2 週追蹤 hCG 至正常，之後每 1-2 個月追蹤一年；追蹤期避孕（可能轉為絨毛膜癌）。
  • Hyperemesis: intractable vomiting in first trimester with weight loss, dehydration, electrolyte imbalance and ketonuria; give IV fluids, antiemetics, small low-fat carbohydrate meals, fluids between meals.妊娠劇吐：第一孕期持續嘔吐，伴體重減輕、脫水、電解質失衡與酮尿；給靜脈輸液、止吐藥、低脂碳水少量餐、兩餐間喝水。

3Cardiac Disease, Chorioamnionitis, Diabetes心臟病、絨毛膜羊膜炎與糖尿病

  • Blood volume peaks at 32-34 weeks; watch for cardiac decompensation: cough, dyspnea, fatigue, palpitations, edema, chest pain. Limit activity, low-sodium diet, avoid excess weight gain.血容積於 32-34 週達高峰；注意心臟代償不全：咳嗽、呼吸困難、疲倦、心悸、水腫、胸痛。限制活動、低鈉飲食、避免體重過度增加。
  • Cardiac labor care: side-lying with head and shoulders raised, oxygen, early pain control, controlled pushing, cardiac and fetal monitoring.心臟病產婦產程：側臥並抬高頭肩、給氧、早期止痛、控制用力、心臟與胎兒監測。
  • Chorioamnionitis: infection of amniotic cavity after prolonged rupture; uterine tenderness, fever, maternal/fetal tachycardia, foul fluid, leukocytosis; give antibiotics after cultures.絨毛膜羊膜炎：破水過久後羊膜腔感染；子宮壓痛、發燒、母胎心搏過速、羊水惡臭、白血球增加；培養後給抗生素。
  • Diabetes: glucose crosses the placenta, insulin does not; insulin needs fall in first trimester, rise in 2nd/3rd, and drop sharply after placenta delivery.糖尿病：葡萄糖可通過胎盤，胰島素不行；第一孕期胰島素需求下降，二、三孕期上升，胎盤娩出後驟降。
  • Infant of diabetic mother: macrosomia, hypoglycemia, hyperbilirubinemia, RDS, hypocalcemia, anomalies.糖尿病母親的新生兒：巨嬰、低血糖、高膽紅素、呼吸窘迫、低血鈣、先天異常。
  • GDM: screen 24-28 weeks (1-hr GCT 140 or more, then 3-hr OGTT); diet, exercise, glucose checks 4x daily; targets fasting under 95, 1-hr under 130-140, 2-hr under 120 mg/dL.妊娠糖尿病：24-28 週篩檢（1 小時 GCT ≥140 再做 3 小時 OGTT）；飲食、運動、每日 4 次血糖監測；目標空腹小於 95、餐後 1 小時小於 130-140、2 小時小於 120 mg/dL。
  • Labor: continuous fetal monitoring, regulate insulin and IV glucose. Postpartum: watch for hypoglycemia as insulin need falls.產程：持續胎心監測，調整胰島素與靜脈葡萄糖。產後：胰島素需求下降，注意低血糖。

4DIC, Hematoma and Hepatitis BDIC、血腫與 B 型肝炎

  • DIC: clotting cascade activated, consuming platelets and factors; bleeding, petechiae, purpura, shock; low fibrinogen/platelets, high PT/PTT and fibrin degradation products; keep urine at least 30 mL/hr.DIC：凝血串聯被活化，耗盡血小板與凝血因子；出血、瘀點、紫斑、休克；纖維蛋白原與血小板降低、PT/PTT 與纖維蛋白降解物升高；尿量至少 30 mL/hr。
  • Predisposing: abruptio placentae, amniotic fluid embolism, gestational hypertension, HELLP, fetal demise, sepsis, severe hemorrhage.誘發因素：胎盤早剝、羊水栓塞、妊娠高血壓、HELLP、胎死腹中、敗血症、嚴重出血。
  • Vulvar hematoma: severe perineal pain, pressure, swelling, inability to void, falling Hgb/Hct; apply ice, analgesics, catheterize if needed, antibiotics.外陰血腫：劇烈會陰疼痛、壓迫感、腫脹、無法排尿、Hgb/Hct 下降；冰敷、止痛、必要時導尿、抗生素。
  • Hepatitis B: after birth wash blood off the newborn first, then give HBIG and hepatitis B vaccine (doses at birth, 1 and 6 months); breastfeeding allowed after newborn treated.B 肝：新生兒出生後先清除血液，再給 HBIG 與 B 肝疫苗（出生、1 個月、6 個月）；治療後可哺乳。

5HIV/AIDSHIV/AIDS

  • HAART (3 drugs) lowers viral load; zidovudine orally after 12 weeks, IV in labor (1 hr before vaginal birth, 3 hr before cesarean if RNA 400 or more or unknown), and syrup to newborn at 2 hr then q12h for 6 weeks.HAART（三合一）降低病毒量；12 週後口服 zidovudine，產程靜脈注射（陰道生產前 1 小時、剖腹前 3 小時，若 RNA ≥400 或不明），新生兒出生 2 小時後起每 12 小時口服糖漿共 6 週。
  • Vaginal birth acceptable if viral load under 1000 copies/mL; otherwise cesarean.病毒量小於 1000 copies/mL 可陰道生產；否則剖腹。
  • Single reactive ELISA is not diagnostic; confirm with Western blot or IFA.單次 ELISA 反應不能確診；需 Western blot 或 IFA 確認。
  • Avoid amniocentesis, scalp electrodes, episiotomy, oxytocin; remove newborn blood promptly; breastfeeding generally restricted.避免羊膜穿刺、胎兒頭皮電極、會陰切開、催產素；盡快清除新生兒身上血液；通常限制哺乳。

6Hypertensive Disorders of Pregnancy妊娠高血壓疾患

  • Categories: chronic hypertension (before 20 wk), gestational hypertension (after 20 wk), preeclampsia, chronic hypertension with superimposed preeclampsia; eclampsia means seizures.分類：慢性高血壓（20 週前）、妊娠高血壓（20 週後）、子癇前症、慢性高血壓合併子癇前症；子癇指抽搐。
  • Preeclampsia BP 140/90 or higher twice 4 hr apart after 20 weeks; severe features: 160/110 or higher, platelets under 100,000, doubled liver enzymes, creatinine over 1.0, pulmonary edema, headache, visual changes, RUQ pain, oliguria under 500 mL/day.子癇前症：20 週後相隔 4 小時兩次血壓 ≥140/90；嚴重特徵：≥160/110、血小板小於 100,000、肝酵素加倍、肌酐酸大於 1.0、肺水腫、頭痛、視覺障礙、右上腹痛、尿量小於 500 mL/天。
  • Proteinuria is no longer a reliable diagnostic criterion. HELLP: hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes, low platelets; an emergency.蛋白尿不再是可靠診斷標準。HELLP：溶血、肝酵素升高、血小板低下；屬急症。
  • Care: side-lying rest, BP and weight monitoring, kick counts, urine output at least 30 mL/hr, check reflexes and clonus, lower BP gradually, corticosteroids for fetal lungs, delivery planning.照護：側臥休息、監測血壓與體重、數胎動、尿量至少 30 mL/hr、檢查反射與陣攣、血壓緩慢下降、給予類固醇促進胎肺成熟、準備分娩。
  • Magnesium sulfate via controlled pump to prevent seizures (continue 24-48 hr postpartum). Toxicity: flushing, sweating, hypotension, absent reflexes, low urine output, respiratory depression; antidote calcium gluconate.硫酸鎂以控制輸液幫浦給予預防抽搐（產後持續 24-48 小時）。中毒徵象：潮紅、出汗、低血壓、反射消失、尿量減少、呼吸抑制；解毒劑為葡萄糖酸鈣。
  • Eclampsia seizure: stay, call for help, side-lying, airway, oxygen 8-10 L/min, monitor FHR, give seizure medication, suction after.子癇抽搐：留在床邊並求援、側臥、維持呼吸道、給氧 8-10 L/min、監測胎心、給予抗痙攣藥、抽搐後抽吸。

7TORCH and Other InfectionsTORCH 與其他感染

  • Toxoplasmosis: raw meat and cat litter; teach avoidance. Rubella: teratogenic in first trimester (eyes, heart, ears, brain); vaccinate postpartum if titer under 1:8.弓形蟲：生肉與貓砂；教導避免。德國麻疹：第一孕期致畸（眼、心、耳、腦）；抗體價小於 1:8 者產後接種。
  • CMV: low birth weight, hepatosplenomegaly, jaundice, hearing loss, seizures; contact precautions. HSV: no vaginal exams with active lesions; cesarean if vaginal lesions; acyclovir/valacyclovir suppression late pregnancy.CMV：低出生體重、肝脾腫大、黃疸、聽力喪失、抽搐；接觸隔離。HSV：有活動性病灶不做陰道檢查；陰道有病灶時剖腹；孕晚期用 acyclovir/valacyclovir 抑制。
  • GBS: vaginal/rectal culture at 35-37 weeks; IV antibiotics in labor to prevent early-onset newborn sepsis.GBS：35-37 週陰道/直腸培養；產程給靜脈抗生素預防新生兒早發型敗血症。
  • COVID-19: pregnant people have higher risk of severe illness and preterm birth. Zika: microcephaly risk; supportive care, avoid aspirin/NSAIDs.COVID-19：孕婦重症與早產風險較高。Zika：小頭症風險；支持性療法，避免阿斯匹靈與 NSAIDs。

8Multiple Gestation and Placental Abnormalities多胞胎與胎盤異常

  • Multiples: large-for-dates uterus, more than one FHR, excessive weight gain; risks include anemia, preterm labor, IUGR, hypertension, polyhydramnios, postpartum hemorrhage; give oxytocics after birth.多胞胎：子宮大於週數、聽到多個胎心、體重增加過多；風險包括貧血、早產、胎兒生長受限、高血壓、羊水過多、產後出血；產後給子宮收縮劑。
  • Placenta accreta, increta, percreta: abnormal adherence; hemorrhage after birth, possible hysterectomy.植入性胎盤（accreta、increta、percreta）：胎盤異常附著；產後出血，可能需子宮切除。
  • Previa: painless bright red bleeding, soft relaxed nontender uterus; NO vaginal exams; types total, partial, marginal; total needs cesarean.前置胎盤：無痛性鮮紅色出血、子宮柔軟放鬆無壓痛；禁做陰道檢查；分完全、部分、邊緣型；完全型需剖腹。
  • Abruptio: dark red bleeding (may be concealed), painful rigid tender uterus, fetal distress, shock, DIC risk; rapid delivery.胎盤早期剝離：暗紅色出血（可為隱性）、子宮疼痛僵硬壓痛、胎兒窘迫、休克、DIC 風險；儘速分娩。

9STIs, TB, UTI, Obesity and Other Complications性傳染病、結核、泌尿道感染、肥胖與其他

  • Chlamydia: often asymptomatic, neonatal conjunctivitis/pneumonia; gonorrhea: ophthalmia neonatorum; treat partner; rescreen near term.披衣菌：常無症狀，新生兒結膜炎/肺炎；淋病：新生兒眼炎；伴侶需治療；近足月再篩檢。
  • Syphilis: VDRL/RPR at first visit and again at 36 weeks; crosses placenta after month 4; test for HIV too.梅毒：首次產檢與 36 週做 VDRL/RPR；4 個月後通過胎盤；亦需驗 HIV。
  • HPV warts: treatment usually delayed until after birth; cesarean only if obstructing. Trichomoniasis and bacterial vaginosis: metronidazole. Candidiasis: topical antifungal; avoid oral fluconazole.HPV 生殖器疣：通常延至產後治療；僅阻塞產道才剖腹。滴蟲與細菌性陰道炎：metronidazole。念珠菌：局部抗黴菌劑；避免口服 fluconazole。
  • TB: chest X-ray only after 20 weeks with shield; isoniazid, pyrazinamide, rifampin (add ethambutol if resistance) with pyridoxine; breastfeed only if noninfectious.結核：20 週後才拍胸部 X 光並護腹；isoniazid、pyrazinamide、rifampin（可能抗藥加 ethambutol）並併用維生素 B6；僅無傳染性時可哺乳。
  • UTI: screen at first visit or 12-16 weeks; pyelonephritis needs hospitalization, risks preterm labor.泌尿道感染：首次產檢或 12-16 週篩檢；腎盂腎炎需住院，有早產風險。
  • Obesity: GDM, hypertension, preeclampsia, VTE, cesarean, postpartum hemorrhage, endometritis; use SCDs/stockings, early ambulation, incision care.肥胖：妊娠糖尿病、高血壓、子癇前症、靜脈栓塞、剖腹、產後出血、子宮內膜炎；使用 SCD/彈性襪、早期下床、傷口照護。
  • Iron-deficiency anemia: CBC at first visit, 24-28 and 36 weeks; iron between meals. Asthma meds are generally safe; maintain action plan.缺鐵性貧血：首次產檢、24-28 週、36 週做 CBC；鐵劑於兩餐間服用。氣喘藥物一般安全；維持氣喘行動計畫。

  • Cardiac labor care: side-lying with head and shoulders raised, oxygen, early pain control, controlled pushing, cardiac and fetal monitoring.心臟病產婦產程：側臥並抬高頭肩、給氧、早期止痛、控制用力、心臟與胎兒監測。
  • Chorioamnionitis: infection of amniotic cavity after prolonged rupture; uterine tenderness, fever, maternal/fetal tachycardia, foul fluid, leukocytosis; give antibiotics after cultures.絨毛膜羊膜炎：破水過久後羊膜腔感染；子宮壓痛、發燒、母胎心搏過速、羊水惡臭、白血球增加；培養後給抗生素。
  • Diabetes: glucose crosses the placenta, insulin does not; insulin needs fall in first trimester, rise in 2nd/3rd, and drop sharply after placenta delivery.糖尿病：葡萄糖可通過胎盤，胰島素不行；第一孕期胰島素需求下降，二、三孕期上升，胎盤娩出後驟降。
  • Infant of diabetic mother: macrosomia, hypoglycemia, hyperbilirubinemia, RDS, hypocalcemia, anomalies.糖尿病母親的新生兒：巨嬰、低血糖、高膽紅素、呼吸窘迫、低血鈣、先天異常。
  • GDM: screen 24-28 weeks (1-hr GCT 140 or more, then 3-hr OGTT); diet, exercise, glucose checks 4x daily; targets fasting under 95, 1-hr under 130-140, 2-hr under 120 mg/dL.妊娠糖尿病：24-28 週篩檢（1 小時 GCT ≥140 再做 3 小時 OGTT）；飲食、運動、每日 4 次血糖監測；目標空腹小於 95、餐後 1 小時小於 130-140、2 小時小於 120 mg/dL。
  • Labor: continuous fetal monitoring, regulate insulin and IV glucose. Postpartum: watch for hypoglycemia as insulin need falls.產程：持續胎心監測，調整胰島素與靜脈葡萄糖。產後：胰島素需求下降，注意低血糖。
Topic 4 · tap to flip主題 4 · 點擊翻面
DIC, Hematoma and Hepatitis BDIC、血腫與 B 型肝炎
  • DIC: clotting cascade activated, consuming platelets and factors; bleeding, petechiae, purpura, shock; low fibrinogen/platelets, high PT/PTT and fibrin degradation products; keep urine at least 30 mL/hr.DIC：凝血串聯被活化，耗盡血小板與凝血因子；出血、瘀點、紫斑、休克；纖維蛋白原與血小板降低、PT/PTT 與纖維蛋白降解物升高；尿量至少 30 mL/hr。
  • Predisposing: abruptio placentae, amniotic fluid embolism, gestational hypertension, HELLP, fetal demise, sepsis, severe hemorrhage.誘發因素：胎盤早剝、羊水栓塞、妊娠高血壓、HELLP、胎死腹中、敗血症、嚴重出血。
  • Vulvar hematoma: severe perineal pain, pressure, swelling, inability to void, falling Hgb/Hct; apply ice, analgesics, catheterize if needed, antibiotics.外陰血腫：劇烈會陰疼痛、壓迫感、腫脹、無法排尿、Hgb/Hct 下降；冰敷、止痛、必要時導尿、抗生素。
  • Hepatitis B: after birth wash blood off the newborn first, then give HBIG and hepatitis B vaccine (doses at birth, 1 and 6 months); breastfeeding allowed after newborn treated.B 肝：新生兒出生後先清除血液，再給 HBIG 與 B 肝疫苗（出生、1 個月、6 個月）；治療後可哺乳。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
HIV/AIDSHIV/AIDS
  • HAART (3 drugs) lowers viral load; zidovudine orally after 12 weeks, IV in labor (1 hr before vaginal birth, 3 hr before cesarean if RNA 400 or more or unknown), and syrup to newborn at 2 hr then q12h for 6 weeks.HAART（三合一）降低病毒量；12 週後口服 zidovudine，產程靜脈注射（陰道生產前 1 小時、剖腹前 3 小時，若 RNA ≥400 或不明），新生兒出生 2 小時後起每 12 小時口服糖漿共 6 週。
  • Vaginal birth acceptable if viral load under 1000 copies/mL; otherwise cesarean.病毒量小於 1000 copies/mL 可陰道生產；否則剖腹。
  • Single reactive ELISA is not diagnostic; confirm with Western blot or IFA.單次 ELISA 反應不能確診；需 Western blot 或 IFA 確認。
  • Avoid amniocentesis, scalp electrodes, episiotomy, oxytocin; remove newborn blood promptly; breastfeeding generally restricted.避免羊膜穿刺、胎兒頭皮電極、會陰切開、催產素；盡快清除新生兒身上血液；通常限制哺乳。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Hypertensive Disorders of Pregnancy妊娠高血壓疾患
  • Categories: chronic hypertension (before 20 wk), gestational hypertension (after 20 wk), preeclampsia, chronic hypertension with superimposed preeclampsia; eclampsia means seizures.分類：慢性高血壓（20 週前）、妊娠高血壓（20 週後）、子癇前症、慢性高血壓合併子癇前症；子癇指抽搐。
  • Preeclampsia BP 140/90 or higher twice 4 hr apart after 20 weeks; severe features: 160/110 or higher, platelets under 100,000, doubled liver enzymes, creatinine over 1.0, pulmonary edema, headache, visual changes, RUQ pain, oliguria under 500 mL/day.子癇前症：20 週後相隔 4 小時兩次血壓 ≥140/90；嚴重特徵：≥160/110、血小板小於 100,000、肝酵素加倍、肌酐酸大於 1.0、肺水腫、頭痛、視覺障礙、右上腹痛、尿量小於 500 mL/天。
  • Proteinuria is no longer a reliable diagnostic criterion. HELLP: hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes, low platelets; an emergency.蛋白尿不再是可靠診斷標準。HELLP：溶血、肝酵素升高、血小板低下；屬急症。
  • Care: side-lying rest, BP and weight monitoring, kick counts, urine output at least 30 mL/hr, check reflexes and clonus, lower BP gradually, corticosteroids for fetal lungs, delivery planning.照護：側臥休息、監測血壓與體重、數胎動、尿量至少 30 mL/hr、檢查反射與陣攣、血壓緩慢下降、給予類固醇促進胎肺成熟、準備分娩。
  • Magnesium sulfate via controlled pump to prevent seizures (continue 24-48 hr postpartum). Toxicity: flushing, sweating, hypotension, absent reflexes, low urine output, respiratory depression; antidote calcium gluconate.硫酸鎂以控制輸液幫浦給予預防抽搐（產後持續 24-48 小時）。中毒徵象：潮紅、出汗、低血壓、反射消失、尿量減少、呼吸抑制；解毒劑為葡萄糖酸鈣。
  • Eclampsia seizure: stay, call for help, side-lying, airway, oxygen 8-10 L/min, monitor FHR, give seizure medication, suction after.子癇抽搐：留在床邊並求援、側臥、維持呼吸道、給氧 8-10 L/min、監測胎心、給予抗痙攣藥、抽搐後抽吸。

TORCH and Other InfectionsTORCH 與其他感染
  • Toxoplasmosis: raw meat and cat litter; teach avoidance. Rubella: teratogenic in first trimester (eyes, heart, ears, brain); vaccinate postpartum if titer under 1:8.弓形蟲：生肉與貓砂；教導避免。德國麻疹：第一孕期致畸（眼、心、耳、腦）；抗體價小於 1:8 者產後接種。
  • CMV: low birth weight, hepatosplenomegaly, jaundice, hearing loss, seizures; contact precautions. HSV: no vaginal exams with active lesions; cesarean if vaginal lesions; acyclovir/valacyclovir suppression late pregnancy.CMV：低出生體重、肝脾腫大、黃疸、聽力喪失、抽搐；接觸隔離。HSV：有活動性病灶不做陰道檢查；陰道有病灶時剖腹；孕晚期用 acyclovir/valacyclovir 抑制。
  • GBS: vaginal/rectal culture at 35-37 weeks; IV antibiotics in labor to prevent early-onset newborn sepsis.GBS：35-37 週陰道/直腸培養；產程給靜脈抗生素預防新生兒早發型敗血症。
  • COVID-19: pregnant people have higher risk of severe illness and preterm birth. Zika: microcephaly risk; supportive care, avoid aspirin/NSAIDs.COVID-19：孕婦重症與早產風險較高。Zika：小頭症風險；支持性療法，避免阿斯匹靈與 NSAIDs。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Multiple Gestation and Placental Abnormalities多胞胎與胎盤異常
  • Multiples: large-for-dates uterus, more than one FHR, excessive weight gain; risks include anemia, preterm labor, IUGR, hypertension, polyhydramnios, postpartum hemorrhage; give oxytocics after birth.多胞胎：子宮大於週數、聽到多個胎心、體重增加過多；風險包括貧血、早產、胎兒生長受限、高血壓、羊水過多、產後出血；產後給子宮收縮劑。
  • Placenta accreta, increta, percreta: abnormal adherence; hemorrhage after birth, possible hysterectomy.植入性胎盤（accreta、increta、percreta）：胎盤異常附著；產後出血，可能需子宮切除。
  • Previa: painless bright red bleeding, soft relaxed nontender uterus; NO vaginal exams; types total, partial, marginal; total needs cesarean.前置胎盤：無痛性鮮紅色出血、子宮柔軟放鬆無壓痛；禁做陰道檢查；分完全、部分、邊緣型；完全型需剖腹。
  • Abruptio: dark red bleeding (may be concealed), painful rigid tender uterus, fetal distress, shock, DIC risk; rapid delivery.胎盤早期剝離：暗紅色出血（可為隱性）、子宮疼痛僵硬壓痛、胎兒窘迫、休克、DIC 風險；儘速分娩。

STIs, TB, UTI, Obesity and Other Complications性傳染病、結核、泌尿道感染、肥胖與其他
  • Chlamydia: often asymptomatic, neonatal conjunctivitis/pneumonia; gonorrhea: ophthalmia neonatorum; treat partner; rescreen near term.披衣菌：常無症狀，新生兒結膜炎/肺炎；淋病：新生兒眼炎；伴侶需治療；近足月再篩檢。
  • Syphilis: VDRL/RPR at first visit and again at 36 weeks; crosses placenta after month 4; test for HIV too.梅毒：首次產檢與 36 週做 VDRL/RPR；4 個月後通過胎盤；亦需驗 HIV。
  • HPV warts: treatment usually delayed until after birth; cesarean only if obstructing. Trichomoniasis and bacterial vaginosis: metronidazole. Candidiasis: topical antifungal; avoid oral fluconazole.HPV 生殖器疣：通常延至產後治療；僅阻塞產道才剖腹。滴蟲與細菌性陰道炎：metronidazole。念珠菌：局部抗黴菌劑；避免口服 fluconazole。
  • TB: chest X-ray only after 20 weeks with shield; isoniazid, pyrazinamide, rifampin (add ethambutol if resistance) with pyridoxine; breastfeed only if noninfectious.結核：20 週後才拍胸部 X 光並護腹；isoniazid、pyrazinamide、rifampin（可能抗藥加 ethambutol）並併用維生素 B6；僅無傳染性時可哺乳。
  • UTI: screen at first visit or 12-16 weeks; pyelonephritis needs hospitalization, risks preterm labor.泌尿道感染：首次產檢或 12-16 週篩檢；腎盂腎炎需住院，有早產風險。
  • Obesity: GDM, hypertension, preeclampsia, VTE, cesarean, postpartum hemorrhage, endometritis; use SCDs/stockings, early ambulation, incision care.肥胖：妊娠糖尿病、高血壓、子癇前症、靜脈栓塞、剖腹、產後出血、子宮內膜炎；使用 SCD/彈性襪、早期下床、傷口照護。
  • Iron-deficiency anemia: CBC at first visit, 24-28 and 36 weeks; iron between meals. Asthma meds are generally safe; maintain action plan.缺鐵性貧血：首次產檢、24-28 週、36 週做 CBC；鐵劑於兩餐間服用。氣喘藥物一般安全；維持氣喘行動計畫。
