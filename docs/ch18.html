This chapter summarizes four major developmental theories tested on NCLEX: Erikson (psychosocial), Piaget (cognitive), Kohlberg (moral) and Freud (psychosexual). Match each age group to its stage, task and appropriate nursing approach, and recognize when a finding is developmentally abnormal.本章整理 NCLEX 常考的四大發展理論：艾瑞克森（心理社會）、皮亞傑（認知）、柯爾伯格（道德）與佛洛伊德（心理性慾）。須將各年齡層對應其階段、任務與適當護理方式，並能辨識發展異常。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Erikson: Psychosocial Development艾瑞克森：心理社會發展

  • Eight stages from birth to death; each presents a crisis that must be resolved for emotional progress, though outcomes can be revised by later experience.從出生到死亡共八個階段；每階段有一個危機，須解決才能情緒成長，但結果可被日後經驗改變。
  • Infancy (birth-18 mo): trust vs mistrust; attachment to caregiver. Respond to infant cues, hold and comfort.嬰兒期（出生-18個月）：信任對不信任；與主要照顧者依附。回應嬰兒的訊號、擁抱與安撫。
  • Early childhood (18 mo-3 yr): autonomy vs shame and doubt; allow self-feeding, dressing and safe choices; set limits for tantrums with simple explanations.幼兒期（18個月-3歲）：自主對羞愧懷疑；讓孩子自行進食、穿衣與在安全下做選擇；對發脾氣設限並簡單說明。
  • Late childhood (3-6 yr): initiative vs guilt; purposeful play, medical-equipment play.學齡前期（3-6歲）：主動對罪惡感；有目的的遊戲、使用醫療器材玩具。
  • School age (6-12 yr): industry vs inferiority; learning and social skills; peers become important; continue schoolwork in hospital.學齡期（6-12歲）：勤奮對自卑；學習與社交技能；同儕變重要；住院時持續課業。
  • Adolescence (12-20 yr): identity vs role confusion; allow choices and private interviews without parents.青春期（12-20歲）：自我認同對角色混淆；讓其參與決定並可在無父母在場下會談。
  • Early adulthood (20-35): intimacy vs isolation. Middle adulthood (35-65): generativity vs stagnation. Later adulthood (65+): integrity vs despair; listen to reminiscence.成年早期（20-35歲）：親密對孤立。成年中期（35-65歲）：生產對停滯。老年期（65歲以上）：完整對絕望；傾聽其回顧人生。

2Piaget: Cognitive Development皮亞傑：認知發展

  • Schema is a mental framework; assimilation fits new information into existing schemas; accommodation changes the schema to fit new information.基模是思考架構；同化是將新資訊納入既有基模；調適是改變基模以容納新資訊。
  • Sensorimotor (birth-2 yr): learns through senses and movement; object permanence develops (does not know hidden objects exist at first).感覺動作期（出生-2歲）：透過感覺與動作學習；發展物體恆存概念（起初不知被遮物體仍存在）。
  • Preoperational (2-7 yr): prelogical, egocentric, needs concrete examples, thinks in past/present/future.前運思期（2-7歲）：前邏輯思考、自我中心、需要具體例子，開始有過去、現在、未來概念。
  • Concrete operational (7-11 yr): classifies, orders, sorts; logical about concrete problems; less egocentric.具體運思期（7-11歲）：分類、排序、歸納；對具體問題有邏輯；較不自我中心。
  • Formal operational (11 yr to adulthood): abstract and logical thinking like an adult.形式運思期（11歲至成年）：如成人般抽象與邏輯思考。
  • A 6-year-old who lacks object permanence is abnormal: report to the pediatrician.6歲兒童缺乏物體恆存屬異常：應通報小兒科醫師。

3Kohlberg: Moral Development柯爾伯格：道德發展

  • Moral development is sequential; stages cannot be skipped; it follows cognitive development; not everyone reaches stages 5-6.道德發展依序進行，不可跳階；隨認知發展而進展；並非人人達到第5-6階段。
  • Preconventional: stage 1 punishment-obedience (2-4 yr), stage 2 instrumental relativist, 'what is in it for me' (4-7 yr). Motivated by punishment and reward.前習俗層次：第1階段懲罰與服從（2-4歲），第2階段工具相對（「我有什麼好處」）（4-7歲）。動機來自懲罰與獎賞。
  • Conventional: stage 3 good boy/nice girl (7-10 yr) to please others; stage 4 law and order (10-12 yr).習俗層次：第3階段乖男孩／好女孩（7-10歲），為取悅他人；第4階段法律與秩序（10-12歲）。
  • Postconventional (about age 13+): stage 5 social contract; stage 6 universal ethical principles; internal conscience.後習俗層次（約13歲後）：第5階段社會契約；第6階段普遍倫理原則；內在良知。
  • Toddler discipline: simple explanations, praise good behavior, distraction; avoid physical punishment or withdrawing love.幼兒管教：簡單說明、稱讚良好行為、轉移注意；避免體罰或收回關愛。

4Freud: Levels of Awareness and Personality佛洛伊德：意識層次與人格結構

  • Unconscious: illogical, pleasure principle, repressed material. Preconscious: recallable. Conscious: logical, reality principle.潛意識：不合邏輯、享樂原則、壓抑的內容。前意識：可回憶。意識：合邏輯、現實原則。
  • Id: drives, pleasure principle, present at birth, impulsive. Ego: reality testing and problem solving, mediates between id and world, starts about 4-5 months. Superego: conscience and ego ideal, develops in phallic stage (3-6 yr).本我：驅力、享樂原則、出生即有、衝動。自我：現實檢驗與解決問題、調節本我與外界，約4-5個月開始形成。超我：良知與自我理想，於性器期（3-6歲）發展。
  • Defense mechanisms are mostly unconscious (except suppression) and distort reality to reduce anxiety; needed for survival but extreme use hinders adjustment.防衛機轉多為無意識（壓抑除外），扭曲現實以減少焦慮；為生存所必需，但過度使用妨礙適應。

5Freud: Psychosexual Stages佛洛伊德：心理性慾期

  • Oral (birth-1 yr): gratification through mouth; trust begins; ego emerges.口腔期（出生-1歲）：以口腔獲得滿足；信任開始；自我萌芽。
  • Anal (1-3 yr): toilet training and control of body; learning to delay gratification.肛門期（1-3歲）：如廁訓練與身體控制；學習延遲滿足。
  • Phallic (3-6 yr): genital focus, Oedipus complex, identification with same-sex parent; superego emerges.性器期（3-6歲）：注意生殖器、戀母情結、認同同性父母；超我出現。
  • Latency (6-12 yr): sexual urges quiet; energy goes to school, friends and skills.潛伏期（6-12歲）：性衝動沉寂；精力投入學業、友誼與技能。
  • Genital (12+): puberty, mature sexual and emotional relationships, life goals and identity.兩性期／生殖期（12歲以上）：青春期、成熟的性與情感關係、人生目標與認同。
  • Each stage has a conflict to resolve; early experiences shape adult personality.每階段有需解決的衝突；早期經驗形塑成人人格。

1According to Kohlberg, what motivates good and bad actions in a child at the preconventional level?依柯爾伯格理論，前習俗層次的兒童以何種因素決定行為好壞？

At the preconventional level morality is motivated by punishment and reward.前習俗層次的道德以懲罰與獎賞為動機。

2Using Erikson's theory, what should the nurse teach new parents about their newborn?依艾瑞克森理論，護理師應教導新手父母如何對待新生兒？

4Which characteristic relates to Freud's anal stage?哪項特徵屬於佛洛伊德的肛門期？

Anal stage (1-3 yr) centers on control of elimination. Oral = mouth; latency = repression; phallic = identification.肛門期（1-3歲）以排泄控制為核心；口腔期為口腔、潛伏期為壓抑、性器期為認同。

5Which behavior is characteristic of Piaget's formal operations stage?哪種行為是皮亞傑形式運思期的特徵？
