This chapter covers how and when to measure temperature, pulse, respirations, blood pressure, oxygen saturation and pain, followed by pain-management interventions and drugs. It then lists high-yield laboratory reference intervals (electrolytes, coagulation studies, CBC, lipids, glucose/HbA1c, renal tests, WBC) with causes of abnormal results and nursing implications.本章說明體溫、脈搏、呼吸、血壓、血氧飽和度與疼痛的測量時機與方法,以及疼痛處置與藥物;接著列出常考的檢驗參考值(電解質、凝血功能、全血球計數、血脂、血糖/HbA1c、腎功能、白血球)及異常原因與護理意義。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Vital signs: principles and timing生命徵象:原則與測量時機

  • Vital signs are temperature, pulse, respirations, BP, pulse oximetry and pain; the first set is the baseline.生命徵象包括體溫、脈搏、呼吸、血壓、血氧飽和度與疼痛;第一次測量作為基準值。
  • Assistive personnel may measure routine vital signs, but the nurse interprets findings and reports abnormal values to the provider.輔助人員可測量常規生命徵象,但解讀結果並通報異常的責任在護理師。
  • Measure on admission, during assessment, before/after invasive or surgical procedures, with drugs affecting heart/lungs/temperature, before/during/after transfusion, with any change in condition, and every 2-4 hours with fever or infection.測量時機:入院、評估時、侵入性檢查或手術前後、使用影響心肺或體溫藥物時、輸血前中後、病情改變時,以及發燒或感染時每 2-4 小時。
  • Postoperative rising heart rate with falling BP and SpO2 suggests bleeding.術後心跳上升、血壓與血氧下降提示出血。

2Temperature體溫

  • Normal range about 97.0-99.0 F (36.1-37.2 C); many settings define fever as 100.4 F (38 C) or higher.正常範圍約 97.0-99.0 F(36.1-37.2 C);許多機構以 100.4 F(38 C)以上定義為發燒。
  • Rectal readings run about 1 F higher; tympanic and axillary about 1 F lower than oral. Temperature is lowest on waking and peaks late afternoon/evening.肛溫約高 1 F;耳溫與腋溫約低 1 F。體溫起床時最低,傍晚到晚上最高。
  • Oral: wait 15-30 min after hot/cold food, smoking or gum; avoid with nasal congestion. Rectal: left side-lying, insert about 1.5 in (3.8 cm), 0.5 in in infants.口溫:進食冷熱食物、吸菸或嚼口香糖後等 15-30 分鐘;鼻塞者避免。肛溫:左側臥,插入約 1.5 吋(3.8 公分),嬰兒 0.5 吋。
  • No rectal temperatures in cardiac clients, rectal surgery, diarrhea, fecal impaction or bleeding risk. Axillary is least accurate. Temporal probe may go behind the earlobe if diaphoretic.心臟病患者、肛門手術後、腹瀉、糞便嵌塞或有出血風險者不量肛溫。腋溫最不準確。出汗時顳動脈探頭可置於耳垂後。
  • Conversion: F to C = (F - 32) x 5/9; C to F = C x 9/5 + 32.換算:攝氏 = (華氏 - 32) x 5/9;華氏 = 攝氏 x 9/5 + 32。
  • Inability to obtain a temperature should not be ignored; it may be hypothermia in infants or older adults.量不到體溫不可忽視,在嬰兒或老年人可能是低體溫。

3Pulse and respirations脈搏與呼吸

  • Adult heart rate 60-100 bpm; assess rate, rhythm, strength and equality. Pulse grade: 4+ bounding, 3+ full, 2+ normal, 1+ weak, 0 absent.成人心率 60-100 次/分;評估速率、節律、強度與對稱性。脈搏分級:4+ 洪大、3+ 飽滿、2+ 正常、1+ 微弱、0 無。
  • Heart rate rises with exercise, pain, fever, emotions, stimulants, hypovolemia and hemorrhage; it slows with age and with depressant/cardiac drugs.運動、疼痛、發燒、情緒、興奮劑、血容不足與出血會使心率上升;年齡增加及抑制劑/心臟藥物使心率減慢。
  • Apical pulse (left midclavicular 5th intercostal space) is counted for 1 full minute for irregular pulse, cardiac disease, before digoxin or beta blockers, and in children under 2.心尖脈(左鎖骨中線第五肋間)須測滿 1 分鐘,適用於不規則脈搏、心臟疾病、給予 digoxin 或乙型阻斷劑前,及 2 歲以下兒童。
  • Pulse deficit = radial rate lower than apical rate; indicates poor perfusion/dysrhythmia; notify the provider.脈搏短絀 = 橈動脈脈率低於心尖脈率,表示周邊灌流不良或心律不整,須通報醫師。
  • Adult respiratory rate 12-20/min; count for 30 sec x 2 unless ill or irregular (then 1 full minute); assess rate, depth, pattern and sounds.成人呼吸 12-20 次/分;測 30 秒乘 2,若病重或不規則則測滿 1 分鐘;評估速率、深度、型態與呼吸音。
  • High CO2/low O2 raise the rate; head injury/raised ICP and opioids depress breathing.CO2 升高或 O2 降低使呼吸加快;頭部外傷/顱內壓上升及鴉片類藥物抑制呼吸。

4Blood pressure and pulse oximetry血壓與血氧飽和度

  • Adult categories: normal below 120/80; elevated 120-129 and under 80; stage 1 130-139 or 80-89; stage 2 at least 140 or 90; crisis over 180 and/or over 120.成人分類:正常低於 120/80;偏高 120-129 且舒張壓小於 80;第 1 期 130-139 或 80-89;第 2 期 140 以上或 90 以上;高血壓危象收縮壓大於 180 和/或舒張壓大於 120。
  • Orthostatic vitals: BP and pulse supine, sitting, standing, 1-3 minutes after each change.姿位性生命徵象:分別於平躺、坐、站姿測血壓與脈搏,每次改變姿勢後 1-3 分鐘測量。
  • Avoid cuffs on arms with IV, AV fistula/shunt, breast/axillary surgery or trauma. No smoking or exercise 30 min before; rest 5 min seated with feet flat.避免在有靜脈輸液、動靜脈瘻管/分流、乳房/腋下手術或外傷的手臂上量血壓。測前 30 分鐘勿吸菸或運動;坐姿雙腳平放休息 5 分鐘。
  • Too-small cuff gives a falsely high reading; too-large gives a falsely low reading. Systolic is Korotkoff phase 1; diastolic is phase 5.壓脈帶太小使讀數偏高,太大使讀數偏低。收縮壓為 Korotkoff 第 1 期,舒張壓為第 5 期。
  • SpO2 normal 95-100%; below 90% requires notifying the provider (except some chronic conditions). Nail polish, motion, hypotension, anemia and poor perfusion distort readings; do not use a cold extremity.SpO2 正常 95-100%;低於 90% 須通知醫師(部分慢性病患除外)。指甲油、移動、低血壓、貧血與灌流不良會影響讀數;勿選用冰冷的肢體。

5Pain assessment and nonpharmacological care疼痛評估與非藥物處置

  • Pain types: acute, chronic noncancer, chronic episodic (migraine, sickle cell crisis), cancer, idiopathic.疼痛類型:急性、慢性非癌症、慢性間歇性(偏頭痛、鐮刀型紅血球危象)、癌症、特發性。
  • Pain is subjective; assess timing, location, severity, quality, aggravating and relieving factors. Use a 0-10 scale, or FACES/FLACC for children and nonverbal clients.疼痛為主觀感受;評估時間、位置、強度、性質、加重與緩解因素。使用 0-10 量表,兒童與無法言語者用 FACES/FLACC。
  • Older adults may show pain as sleep disturbance, gait change, withdrawal or depression; consider culture and beliefs.老年人疼痛可能表現為睡眠障礙、步態改變、社交減少或憂鬱;須考量文化與信仰。
  • Nonpharmacological: heat/cold (15-30 min with a barrier), massage, TENS, binders/elevation, distraction, relaxation, guided imagery, music, acupuncture, biofeedback.非藥物措施:冷熱敷(隔層布、15-30 分鐘)、按摩、TENS、束帶/抬高、分散注意力、放鬆、引導想像、音樂、針灸、生理回饋。
  • Ask about herbal remedies because of drug interactions.須詢問是否使用草藥,以免與藥物交互作用。

6Pharmacological pain management疼痛的藥物處置

  • NSAIDs/aspirin: gastric irritation, bleeding, sodium and water retention, tinnitus; take with food; avoid in ulcer disease; potentiate anticoagulants.NSAIDs/阿斯匹靈:胃刺激、出血、鈉水滯留、耳鳴;隨餐服用;潰瘍者避免;會加強抗凝劑作用。
  • Ibuprofen can cause hypoglycemia with oral antidiabetics and toxicity with calcium channel blockers.ibuprofen 與口服降血糖藥併用可能低血糖,與鈣離子阻斷劑併用有中毒風險。
  • Acetaminophen: main risk is hepatotoxicity; self-treatment no more than 10 days in adults (5 in children); antidote acetylcysteine.acetaminophen:主要風險為肝毒性;成人自行用藥不超過 10 天(兒童 5 天);解毒劑為 acetylcysteine。
  • Opioids: give 30-60 min before painful activity; hold and call if RR below 12 or bradycardia; check BP, sedation, bowel/bladder; keep naloxone, O2 and resuscitation equipment; use an infusion pump for continuous or PCA IV.鴉片類:於疼痛活動前 30-60 分鐘給予;呼吸低於 12 次/分或心搏過緩時暫停並通報;監測血壓、鎮靜程度、排便與排尿;備妥 naloxone、氧氣與急救設備;持續或 PCA 靜脈輸注須用輸液幫浦。
  • Adjuvants (amitriptyline, venlafaxine, gabapentin) help neuropathic pain.輔助藥物(amitriptyline、venlafaxine、gabapentin)有助於神經病變性疼痛。

7Lab values: electrolytes and coagulation studies檢驗值:電解質與凝血功能

  • Sodium 135-145 mEq/L; potassium 3.5-5.0 mEq/L. Prolonged tourniquet or hemolysis can falsely raise potassium; do not draw from an arm with an infusion.鈉 135-145 mEq/L;鉀 3.5-5.0 mEq/L。止血帶綁太久或溶血會使鉀假性升高;勿由有輸液的手臂抽血。
  • aPTT normal 30-40 sec; therapeutic heparin is 1.5-2.5 times normal (about 45-100 sec). Start bleeding precautions if over 100 sec; draw 1 hr before the next intermittent dose.aPTT 正常 30-40 秒;使用肝素治療目標為正常的 1.5-2.5 倍(約 45-100 秒)。超過 100 秒啟動出血防範;間歇給藥者於下次劑量前 1 小時抽血。
  • PT 11-12.5 sec; INR 0.81-1.20; warfarin keeps PT at 1.5-2 times control. PT over 25 sec or INR over 3.0 on warfarin means bleeding precautions.PT 11-12.5 秒;INR 0.81-1.20;warfarin 使 PT 維持在對照值 1.5-2 倍。服用 warfarin 者 PT 超過 25 秒或 INR 超過 3.0 須啟動出血防範。
  • High vitamin K intake (green leafy vegetables) shortens PT and lowers warfarin effect.攝取大量維生素 K(深綠色葉菜)會縮短 PT、降低 warfarin 效果。
  • Platelets 150,000-400,000/mm3; low with chemotherapy, DIC, leukemia, hemorrhage; apply pressure to puncture sites 3-5 min.血小板 150,000-400,000/mm3;化療、DIC、白血病、出血時降低;穿刺處加壓 3-5 分鐘。

8Lab values: CBC, lipids, glucose and renal tests檢驗值:血球、血脂、血糖與腎功能

  • Hemoglobin: male 14-18, female 12-16 g/dL. Hematocrit: male 42-52%, female 37-47%. Low in anemia and hemorrhage; hematocrit rises with dehydration and falls with overhydration.血紅素:男 14-18、女 12-16 g/dL。血比容:男 42-52%、女 37-47%。貧血與出血時降低;脫水使血比容上升,水過多則下降。
  • Lipids (fast 12-14 hr): total cholesterol under 200, LDL under 100, HDL over 60 mg/dL; triglycerides male 40-160, female 35-135 mg/dL.血脂(空腹 12-14 小時):總膽固醇低於 200、LDL 低於 100、HDL 高於 60 mg/dL;三酸甘油酯男 40-160、女 35-135 mg/dL。
  • Fasting glucose 70-99 mg/dL (fast 8-12 hr; hold morning insulin/oral agents until drawn). HbA1c reflects 3-4 months: normal under 5.7%, prediabetes 5.7-6.4%, diabetes 6.5% or more.空腹血糖 70-99 mg/dL(禁食 8-12 小時;抽血前暫停早晨胰島素/口服藥)。HbA1c 反映 3-4 個月血糖控制:正常低於 5.7%,糖尿病前期 5.7-6.4%,糖尿病 6.5% 以上。
  • Creatinine: male 0.6-1.2, female 0.5-1.1 mg/dL (specific for renal function). BUN 10-20 mg/dL; raised by dehydration, GI bleeding, renal disease.肌酸酐:男 0.6-1.2、女 0.5-1.1 mg/dL(腎功能的專一指標)。BUN 10-20 mg/dL;脫水、消化道出血、腎病時升高。
  • WBC 5,000-10,000/mm3; left shift (more immature neutrophils) suggests bacterial infection. Monitor WBC/differential in chemotherapy clients for neutropenia and infection.白血球 5,000-10,000/mm3;左移(未成熟嗜中性球增加)提示細菌感染。化療患者須監測白血球及分類以防嗜中性球低下與感染。

Pain assessment and nonpharmacological care疼痛評估與非藥物處置
  • Pain types: acute, chronic noncancer, chronic episodic (migraine, sickle cell crisis), cancer, idiopathic.疼痛類型:急性、慢性非癌症、慢性間歇性(偏頭痛、鐮刀型紅血球危象)、癌症、特發性。
  • Pain is subjective; assess timing, location, severity, quality, aggravating and relieving factors. Use a 0-10 scale, or FACES/FLACC for children and nonverbal clients.疼痛為主觀感受;評估時間、位置、強度、性質、加重與緩解因素。使用 0-10 量表,兒童與無法言語者用 FACES/FLACC。
  • Older adults may show pain as sleep disturbance, gait change, withdrawal or depression; consider culture and beliefs.老年人疼痛可能表現為睡眠障礙、步態改變、社交減少或憂鬱;須考量文化與信仰。
  • Nonpharmacological: heat/cold (15-30 min with a barrier), massage, TENS, binders/elevation, distraction, relaxation, guided imagery, music, acupuncture, biofeedback.非藥物措施:冷熱敷(隔層布、15-30 分鐘)、按摩、TENS、束帶/抬高、分散注意力、放鬆、引導想像、音樂、針灸、生理回饋。
  • Ask about herbal remedies because of drug interactions.須詢問是否使用草藥,以免與藥物交互作用。

Pharmacological pain management疼痛的藥物處置
  • NSAIDs/aspirin: gastric irritation, bleeding, sodium and water retention, tinnitus; take with food; avoid in ulcer disease; potentiate anticoagulants.NSAIDs/阿斯匹靈:胃刺激、出血、鈉水滯留、耳鳴;隨餐服用;潰瘍者避免;會加強抗凝劑作用。
  • Ibuprofen can cause hypoglycemia with oral antidiabetics and toxicity with calcium channel blockers.ibuprofen 與口服降血糖藥併用可能低血糖,與鈣離子阻斷劑併用有中毒風險。
  • Acetaminophen: main risk is hepatotoxicity; self-treatment no more than 10 days in adults (5 in children); antidote acetylcysteine.acetaminophen:主要風險為肝毒性;成人自行用藥不超過 10 天(兒童 5 天);解毒劑為 acetylcysteine。
  • Opioids: give 30-60 min before painful activity; hold and call if RR below 12 or bradycardia; check BP, sedation, bowel/bladder; keep naloxone, O2 and resuscitation equipment; use an infusion pump for continuous or PCA IV.鴉片類:於疼痛活動前 30-60 分鐘給予;呼吸低於 12 次/分或心搏過緩時暫停並通報;監測血壓、鎮靜程度、排便與排尿;備妥 naloxone、氧氣與急救設備;持續或 PCA 靜脈輸注須用輸液幫浦。
  • Adjuvants (amitriptyline, venlafaxine, gabapentin) help neuropathic pain.輔助藥物(amitriptyline、venlafaxine、gabapentin)有助於神經病變性疼痛。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Lab values: electrolytes and coagulation studies檢驗值:電解質與凝血功能
  • Sodium 135-145 mEq/L; potassium 3.5-5.0 mEq/L. Prolonged tourniquet or hemolysis can falsely raise potassium; do not draw from an arm with an infusion.鈉 135-145 mEq/L;鉀 3.5-5.0 mEq/L。止血帶綁太久或溶血會使鉀假性升高;勿由有輸液的手臂抽血。
  • aPTT normal 30-40 sec; therapeutic heparin is 1.5-2.5 times normal (about 45-100 sec). Start bleeding precautions if over 100 sec; draw 1 hr before the next intermittent dose.aPTT 正常 30-40 秒;使用肝素治療目標為正常的 1.5-2.5 倍(約 45-100 秒)。超過 100 秒啟動出血防範;間歇給藥者於下次劑量前 1 小時抽血。
  • PT 11-12.5 sec; INR 0.81-1.20; warfarin keeps PT at 1.5-2 times control. PT over 25 sec or INR over 3.0 on warfarin means bleeding precautions.PT 11-12.5 秒;INR 0.81-1.20;warfarin 使 PT 維持在對照值 1.5-2 倍。服用 warfarin 者 PT 超過 25 秒或 INR 超過 3.0 須啟動出血防範。
  • High vitamin K intake (green leafy vegetables) shortens PT and lowers warfarin effect.攝取大量維生素 K(深綠色葉菜)會縮短 PT、降低 warfarin 效果。
  • Platelets 150,000-400,000/mm3; low with chemotherapy, DIC, leukemia, hemorrhage; apply pressure to puncture sites 3-5 min.血小板 150,000-400,000/mm3;化療、DIC、白血病、出血時降低;穿刺處加壓 3-5 分鐘。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Lab values: CBC, lipids, glucose and renal tests檢驗值:血球、血脂、血糖與腎功能
  • Hemoglobin: male 14-18, female 12-16 g/dL. Hematocrit: male 42-52%, female 37-47%. Low in anemia and hemorrhage; hematocrit rises with dehydration and falls with overhydration.血紅素:男 14-18、女 12-16 g/dL。血比容:男 42-52%、女 37-47%。貧血與出血時降低;脫水使血比容上升,水過多則下降。
  • Lipids (fast 12-14 hr): total cholesterol under 200, LDL under 100, HDL over 60 mg/dL; triglycerides male 40-160, female 35-135 mg/dL.血脂(空腹 12-14 小時):總膽固醇低於 200、LDL 低於 100、HDL 高於 60 mg/dL;三酸甘油酯男 40-160、女 35-135 mg/dL。
  • Fasting glucose 70-99 mg/dL (fast 8-12 hr; hold morning insulin/oral agents until drawn). HbA1c reflects 3-4 months: normal under 5.7%, prediabetes 5.7-6.4%, diabetes 6.5% or more.空腹血糖 70-99 mg/dL(禁食 8-12 小時;抽血前暫停早晨胰島素/口服藥)。HbA1c 反映 3-4 個月血糖控制:正常低於 5.7%,糖尿病前期 5.7-6.4%,糖尿病 6.5% 以上。
  • Creatinine: male 0.6-1.2, female 0.5-1.1 mg/dL (specific for renal function). BUN 10-20 mg/dL; raised by dehydration, GI bleeding, renal disease.肌酸酐:男 0.6-1.2、女 0.5-1.1 mg/dL(腎功能的專一指標)。BUN 10-20 mg/dL;脫水、消化道出血、腎病時升高。
  • WBC 5,000-10,000/mm3; left shift (more immature neutrophils) suggests bacterial infection. Monitor WBC/differential in chemotherapy clients for neutropenia and infection.白血球 5,000-10,000/mm3;左移(未成熟嗜中性球增加)提示細菌感染。化療患者須監測白血球及分類以防嗜中性球低下與感染。
