Summary重點摘要Test-critical考試關鍵Mnemonics記憶口訣

Pediatric GI disorders from vomiting/diarrhea to congenital anomalies (cleft lip/palate, TEF, pyloric stenosis, Hirschsprung, imperforate anus, abdominal wall defects), intussusception, appendicitis, celiac disease, hepatitis, poisonings (lead, acetaminophen, aspirin, corrosives) and parasites.小兒腸胃疾病：嘔吐與腹瀉、先天異常（唇顎裂、食道閉鎖與氣管食道瘻管、幽門狹窄、巨結腸症、肛門閉鎖、腹壁缺損）、腸套疊、闌尾炎、乳糜瀉、肝炎、中毒（鉛、乙醯胺酚、阿斯匹靈、腐蝕劑）與寄生蟲。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Vomiting and diarrhea嘔吐與腹瀉

  • Vomiting risks: dehydration, electrolyte loss, metabolic alkalosis, aspiration. Position on side, monitor I&O, small frequent oral rehydration, antiemetics as ordered.嘔吐風險：脫水、電解質流失、代謝性鹼中毒、吸入。側臥、監測輸出入、少量頻繁口服補液、依醫囑給止吐藥。
  • Projectile vomiting suggests pyloric stenosis or increased ICP; report blood in vomitus, forceful vomiting, abdominal pain.噴射狀嘔吐提示幽門狹窄或顱內壓升高；嘔血、劇烈嘔吐、腹痛須通報。
  • Diarrhea risks: dehydration and metabolic acidosis, especially under 5 years; rotavirus is severe at 3-24 months and nosocomial.腹瀉風險：脫水與代謝性酸中毒，尤其 5 歲以下；輪狀病毒在 3–24 個月最嚴重且可院內感染。
  • Enteric isolation, handwashing, skin care; ORS (Pedialyte) for mild-moderate; avoid carbonated drinks and apple juice; NPO plus IV for severe; give IV potassium only if voiding.腸道隔離、洗手、皮膚護理；輕中度用口服補液鹽（Pedialyte）；避免碳酸飲料與蘋果汁；重度禁食加靜脈輸液；有排尿才給靜脈鉀。

2Cleft lip and palate唇裂與顎裂

  • Failure of tissues to fuse; folic acid before pregnancy lowers risk. Lip repair at 3-6 months; palate repair around 1 year. Risk of otitis media and hearing loss.組織未融合；孕前補葉酸可降低風險。唇裂 3–6 個月修補；顎裂約 1 歲修補。易中耳炎與聽力損失。
  • Feeding: upright, formula to side/back of mouth, small amounts, burp often, special nipples; ESSR (enlarge nipple, stimulate suck, swallow, rest).餵食：直立、奶流向口腔側後方、少量、常拍嗝、特殊奶嘴；ESSR（放大奶嘴、刺激吸吮、吞嚥、休息）。
  • After lip repair: protect suture line, keep clean, back-lying, no prone or on repair side, elbow restraints removed every 1-2 hours.唇裂術後：保護縫線、保持清潔乾燥、仰臥、避免趴或壓術側、肘部約束每 1–2 小時鬆開。
  • After palate repair: nothing hard in mouth (no suction, straws, spoons, pacifiers, tongue depressor, thermometer); watch airway.顎裂術後：口中不放硬物（不抽吸、吸管、湯匙、奶嘴、壓舌板、體溫計）；注意呼吸道。

3Esophageal atresia/TEF and GER食道閉鎖/氣管食道瘻管與胃食道逆流

  • EA/TEF signs: frothy saliva, drooling, the 3 Cs (coughing, choking, cyanosis) with feeding, abdominal distention, respiratory distress.食道閉鎖/瘻管徵象：口鼻泡沫唾液、流口水、餵食時 3C（咳、嗆、發紺）、腹脹、呼吸窘迫。
  • Preop: NPO, upright at least 30 degrees, suction blind pouch, IV fluids, antibiotics. Postop: watch for anastomotic leak, esophagogram before feeds; G-tube feeds around day 5-7.術前：禁食、上半身抬高至少 30 度、抽吸盲袋、靜脈輸液、抗生素。術後：留意吻合處滲漏、進食前做食道攝影；約第 5–7 天經胃造瘻餵食。
  • GER: regurgitation, poor weight gain, irritability, respiratory problems; complications include esophagitis, strictures, FTT, aspiration pneumonia.GER：溢奶、體重增加差、易怒、呼吸問題；併發症有食道炎、狹窄、生長遲滯、吸入性肺炎。
  • Upright 20-30 minutes after feeds (not infant seat); small frequent feedings, thicken with rice cereal, burp often; supine for sleep; PPIs/H2 blockers.餵後直立 20–30 分鐘（避免半臥嬰兒座椅）；少量多餐、可加米糊增稠、常拍嗝；睡眠仰臥；使用 PPI/H2 阻斷劑。
  • Avoid fatty foods, chocolate, tomato, carbonated and citrus drinks, spicy foods, vigorous play after eating.避免油膩、巧克力、番茄、碳酸與柑橘飲料、辛辣食物、餐後劇烈活動。

4Pyloric stenosis, lactose intolerance, celiac disease幽門狹窄、乳糖不耐、乳糜瀉

  • Pyloric stenosis: first weeks of life; projectile non-bilious vomiting after feeds, hungry, visible peristaltic waves, olive-shaped mass; dehydration and metabolic alkalosis.幽門狹窄：出生數週內；餵食後噴射狀非膽汁嘔吐、仍飢餓、可見蠕動波、橄欖狀腫塊；脫水與代謝性鹼中毒。
  • Pyloromyotomy: correct fluids/electrolytes first, NPO, NG decompression; postop small frequent feeds advanced gradually.幽門肌切開術：先矯正水與電解質、禁食、NG 減壓；術後少量頻繁餵食逐步增加。
  • Lactose intolerance: lactase deficiency; bloating, cramps, diarrhea, flatus after dairy; use enzyme tablets, soy formula, hard cheese/yogurt; supplement calcium and vitamin D.乳糖不耐：缺乏乳糖酶；進食乳製品後腹脹、絞痛、腹瀉、排氣；使用酵素錠、豆基配方、硬起司/優格；補充鈣與維生素 D。
  • Celiac disease: gluten (wheat, barley, rye, oats) damages villi; steatorrhea, distention, muscle wasting, anemia; onset 1-5 years.乳糜瀉：麩質（小麥、大麥、裸麥、燕麥）損傷絨毛；脂肪便、腹脹、肌肉萎縮、貧血；1–5 歲發病。
  • Lifelong gluten-free diet (corn, rice, millet allowed) plus iron, folic acid, vitamins A, D, E, K; celiac crisis from fasting, infection or gluten: profuse diarrhea, dehydration, acidosis.終身無麩質飲食（可吃玉米、米、小米）並補充鐵、葉酸、維生素 A、D、E、K；乳糜瀉危象由禁食、感染或麩質誘發：大量水瀉、脫水、酸中毒。

5Appendicitis闌尾炎

  • Pain starts periumbilical then localizes to RLQ at McBurney point; rebound tenderness, rigidity, low-grade fever, anorexia/nausea/vomiting after pain, elevated WBC.疼痛起於臍周後移至右下腹 McBurney 點；反彈痛、僵硬、低燒、疼痛後才厭食噁心嘔吐、白血球升高。
  • Sudden relief of pain followed by worsening suggests perforation and peritonitis (fever, distention, tachycardia, pallor).疼痛突然緩解後又加劇提示穿孔與腹膜炎（發燒、腹脹、心跳快、蒼白）。
  • Preop: NPO, IV fluids, antibiotics, position of comfort, ice if prescribed; NO heat, laxatives or enemas.術前：禁食、靜脈輸液、抗生素、舒適姿勢、依醫囑冰敷；禁熱敷、瀉劑、灌腸。
  • Postop: monitor temperature, incision, drain; right side-lying or low semi-Fowler with legs flexed; NG suction; advance diet when bowel sounds return.術後：監測體溫、傷口、引流；右側臥或低半坐臥並屈膝；NG 抽吸；腸音恢復後漸進飲食。

6Hirschsprung, intussusception, abdominal wall defects, hernia巨結腸症、腸套疊、腹壁缺損、疝氣

  • Hirschsprung: absent ganglion cells; newborn fails to pass meconium, abdominal distention, bile vomiting; later ribbon-like foul stools; enterocolitis is the serious complication (fever, explosive diarrhea). Biopsy diagnoses.巨結腸症：缺乏神經節細胞；新生兒胎便未排、腹脹、膽汁嘔吐；之後絲帶狀惡臭便；腸炎為嚴重併發症（發燒、爆發性水瀉）。切片診斷。
  • Hirschsprung care: no rectal temperatures, measure girth, staged surgery with temporary colostomy; stoma should be red and moist.巨結腸症照護：不量肛溫、量腹圍、分期手術含暫時性結腸造口；造口應紅潤濕潤。
  • Intussusception: colicky pain with knees drawn up, currant-jelly stool, sausage-shaped mass; hydrostatic (air/fluid) reduction if no perforation or shock; normal brown stool means reduction.腸套疊：陣發性絞痛屈膝、果醬樣血便、香腸狀腫塊；無穿孔休克可行水（空氣）壓灌腸整復；排出正常棕色便代表已復位。
  • Omphalocele: covered sac, saline gauze plus plastic wrap, monitor temperature. Gastroschisis: no membrane, lateral to cord, loosely cover with saline pads, surgery within hours, prolonged ileus.臍膨出：有囊膜，生理食鹽水紗布加塑膠膜、監測體溫。腹裂：無膜、位於臍側，生理食鹽水墊鬆覆蓋、數小時內手術、常有長期腸阻塞。
  • Incarcerated hernia (irritable, tender, nonreducible, distended) is an emergency. Hydrocele post-op: ice, scrotal support, no tub bath, no straddle toys 2-4 weeks.嵌頓疝氣（易怒、壓痛、無法復位、腹脹）屬緊急。陰囊水腫術後：冰敷、陰囊托、避免盆浴、2–4 週不騎跨玩具。

7Constipation, IBS, imperforate anus便秘、腸躁症、肛門閉鎖

  • Constipation/encopresis: fiber and fluids, less milk/sugar, enemas to clear impaction (watch hypernatremia, hyperphosphatemia), toilet sitting 5-10 minutes after meals, treatment 3-6 months.便秘/大便失禁：高纖高水分、減少牛奶與糖、灌腸清除糞塊（留意高血鈉、高血磷）、餐後如廁坐 5–10 分鐘、治療 3–6 個月。
  • IBS: diffuse pain, alternating constipation/diarrhea with mucus; reassure, moderate fiber low fat, anticholinergics.腸躁症：瀰漫性腹痛、便祕腹瀉交替含黏液；安撫、中等纖維低脂飲食、抗膽鹼藥。
  • Imperforate anus: no meconium, absent anus, anal membrane or fistula; stool in urine/vagina indicates fistula.肛門閉鎖：無胎便、無肛門、肛膜或瘻管；尿或陰道有糞便代表瘻管。
  • Postop: side-lying prone with hips elevated, or legs suspended; no rectal temperatures; anal dilation only with provider-supplied dilator, 1-2 cm, water-soluble lubricant.術後：側俯臥抬高臀部，或腿懸吊成 90 度；不量肛溫；僅用醫師提供之擴張器、插入 1–2 公分、水溶性潤滑劑。

8Hepatitis肝炎

  • HAV: fecal-oral; vaccine 2 doses from 12 months; enteric precautions at least 1 week after jaundice; may return to school 1 week after jaundice onset.A 肝：糞口傳染；12 個月起接種 2 劑疫苗；黃疸後至少 1 週腸道隔離；黃疸起 1 週後可返校。
  • HBV: mostly perinatal; vaccine before discharge then at 1 and 6 months; HBIG for newborns of HBsAg-positive mothers; can cause cirrhosis and liver cancer.B 肝：多為周產期傳染；出院前接種，之後 1 與 6 個月；母親 HBsAg 陽性新生兒給 HBIG；可致肝硬化與肝癌。
  • HCV parenteral and often chronic; HDV only with HBV; HEV uncommon, no chronic state.C 肝經血液傳染且常慢性化；D 肝僅與 B 肝併發；E 肝罕見且不慢性化。
  • Phases: anicteric prodrome 5-7 days (anorexia, malaise, RUQ pain); icteric phase (jaundice, dark urine, pale stools, pruritus).分期：無黃疸前驅期 5–7 天（厭食、倦怠、右上腹痛）；黃疸期（黃疸、深色尿、灰白便、搔癢）。
  • Handwashing, standard and enteric precautions, bleach 1/4 cup per gallon for diaper surfaces, low-fat diet, caution with medications.洗手、標準與腸道隔離、尿布區以 1/4 杯漂白水兌 1 加侖水消毒、低脂飲食、用藥需謹慎。

9Poisonings and lead中毒與鉛中毒

  • Poisoning first steps: ABCs, stop exposure, identify agent/amount, prevent absorption, call Poison Control.中毒首要：ABC、終止暴露、辨識毒物與量、阻止吸收、聯絡毒物中心。
  • Lead: hand-to-mouth from paint chips/dust/pottery; CNS most affected; screen at 1-2 years; levels under 5 mcg/dL rescreen yearly, 20-44 treatment, 45-69 within 48 hr, 70 or more immediate.鉛：經手口接觸漆片/灰塵/陶器；中樞神經最受影響；1–2 歲篩檢；<5 mcg/dL 每年複查，20–44 治療，45–69 於 48 小時內，≥70 立即處理。
  • Chelation: calcium disodium EDTA, succimer (oral), BAL (peanut oil: contraindicated with peanut allergy, G6PD deficiency, and iron). Ensure urine output first; monitor kidneys.螯合治療：calcium disodium EDTA、succimer（口服）、BAL（花生油製劑：花生過敏、G6PD 缺乏、併用鐵劑者禁用）。先確認尿量；監測腎功能。
  • Acetaminophen toxic at 150 mg/kg or more; stages: GI symptoms, latent 24-36 h, hepatic injury; antidote N-acetylcysteine; charcoal if under 4 hours; do not use charcoal with NAC.乙醯胺酚毒性劑量 ≥150 mg/kg；階段：腸胃症狀、24–36 小時潛伏期、肝損傷；解毒劑 N-acetylcysteine；<4 小時可給活性碳；活性碳不與 NAC 併用。
  • Aspirin: tinnitus, hyperpnea, fever, metabolic acidosis; charcoal, IV fluids, sodium bicarbonate, cooling, dialysis if needed.阿斯匹靈：耳鳴、過度換氣、發燒、代謝性酸中毒；活性碳、靜脈輸液、碳酸氫鈉、降溫、必要時透析。
  • Corrosives: dilute with water/milk (up to 4 oz), do NOT induce vomiting or neutralize.腐蝕劑：以水/牛奶稀釋（最多 4 盎司）、不可催吐、不可中和。

10Intestinal parasites腸道寄生蟲

  • Giardiasis: protozoa in daycare; diarrhea, steatorrhea, cramps, FTT; 3 or more stool samples.梨形鞭毛蟲症：日托常見；腹瀉、脂肪便、絞痛、生長遲滯；需 ≥3 次糞便檢體。
  • Pinworms: intense perianal itching, restless sleep; tape test in morning; flashlight inspection 2-3 hours after sleep.蟯蟲：肛門劇癢、睡眠不安；晨間透明膠帶採檢；入睡後 2–3 小時以手電筒檢查。
  • Treat all family members; repeat medication in 2 weeks; hot-water laundry and meticulous handwashing.全家同時治療；2 週後重複給藥；熱水洗衣並勤洗手。

Topic 4 · tap to flip主題 4 · 點擊翻面
Pyloric stenosis, lactose intolerance, celiac disease幽門狹窄、乳糖不耐、乳糜瀉
  • Pyloric stenosis: first weeks of life; projectile non-bilious vomiting after feeds, hungry, visible peristaltic waves, olive-shaped mass; dehydration and metabolic alkalosis.幽門狹窄：出生數週內；餵食後噴射狀非膽汁嘔吐、仍飢餓、可見蠕動波、橄欖狀腫塊；脫水與代謝性鹼中毒。
  • Pyloromyotomy: correct fluids/electrolytes first, NPO, NG decompression; postop small frequent feeds advanced gradually.幽門肌切開術：先矯正水與電解質、禁食、NG 減壓；術後少量頻繁餵食逐步增加。
  • Lactose intolerance: lactase deficiency; bloating, cramps, diarrhea, flatus after dairy; use enzyme tablets, soy formula, hard cheese/yogurt; supplement calcium and vitamin D.乳糖不耐：缺乏乳糖酶；進食乳製品後腹脹、絞痛、腹瀉、排氣；使用酵素錠、豆基配方、硬起司/優格；補充鈣與維生素 D。
  • Celiac disease: gluten (wheat, barley, rye, oats) damages villi; steatorrhea, distention, muscle wasting, anemia; onset 1-5 years.乳糜瀉：麩質（小麥、大麥、裸麥、燕麥）損傷絨毛；脂肪便、腹脹、肌肉萎縮、貧血；1–5 歲發病。
  • Lifelong gluten-free diet (corn, rice, millet allowed) plus iron, folic acid, vitamins A, D, E, K; celiac crisis from fasting, infection or gluten: profuse diarrhea, dehydration, acidosis.終身無麩質飲食（可吃玉米、米、小米）並補充鐵、葉酸、維生素 A、D、E、K；乳糜瀉危象由禁食、感染或麩質誘發：大量水瀉、脫水、酸中毒。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Appendicitis闌尾炎
  • Pain starts periumbilical then localizes to RLQ at McBurney point; rebound tenderness, rigidity, low-grade fever, anorexia/nausea/vomiting after pain, elevated WBC.疼痛起於臍周後移至右下腹 McBurney 點；反彈痛、僵硬、低燒、疼痛後才厭食噁心嘔吐、白血球升高。
  • Sudden relief of pain followed by worsening suggests perforation and peritonitis (fever, distention, tachycardia, pallor).疼痛突然緩解後又加劇提示穿孔與腹膜炎（發燒、腹脹、心跳快、蒼白）。
  • Preop: NPO, IV fluids, antibiotics, position of comfort, ice if prescribed; NO heat, laxatives or enemas.術前：禁食、靜脈輸液、抗生素、舒適姿勢、依醫囑冰敷；禁熱敷、瀉劑、灌腸。
  • Postop: monitor temperature, incision, drain; right side-lying or low semi-Fowler with legs flexed; NG suction; advance diet when bowel sounds return.術後：監測體溫、傷口、引流；右側臥或低半坐臥並屈膝；NG 抽吸；腸音恢復後漸進飲食。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Hirschsprung, intussusception, abdominal wall defects, hernia巨結腸症、腸套疊、腹壁缺損、疝氣
  • Hirschsprung: absent ganglion cells; newborn fails to pass meconium, abdominal distention, bile vomiting; later ribbon-like foul stools; enterocolitis is the serious complication (fever, explosive diarrhea). Biopsy diagnoses.巨結腸症：缺乏神經節細胞；新生兒胎便未排、腹脹、膽汁嘔吐；之後絲帶狀惡臭便；腸炎為嚴重併發症（發燒、爆發性水瀉）。切片診斷。
  • Hirschsprung care: no rectal temperatures, measure girth, staged surgery with temporary colostomy; stoma should be red and moist.巨結腸症照護：不量肛溫、量腹圍、分期手術含暫時性結腸造口；造口應紅潤濕潤。
  • Intussusception: colicky pain with knees drawn up, currant-jelly stool, sausage-shaped mass; hydrostatic (air/fluid) reduction if no perforation or shock; normal brown stool means reduction.腸套疊：陣發性絞痛屈膝、果醬樣血便、香腸狀腫塊；無穿孔休克可行水（空氣）壓灌腸整復；排出正常棕色便代表已復位。
  • Omphalocele: covered sac, saline gauze plus plastic wrap, monitor temperature. Gastroschisis: no membrane, lateral to cord, loosely cover with saline pads, surgery within hours, prolonged ileus.臍膨出：有囊膜，生理食鹽水紗布加塑膠膜、監測體溫。腹裂：無膜、位於臍側，生理食鹽水墊鬆覆蓋、數小時內手術、常有長期腸阻塞。
  • Incarcerated hernia (irritable, tender, nonreducible, distended) is an emergency. Hydrocele post-op: ice, scrotal support, no tub bath, no straddle toys 2-4 weeks.嵌頓疝氣（易怒、壓痛、無法復位、腹脹）屬緊急。陰囊水腫術後：冰敷、陰囊托、避免盆浴、2–4 週不騎跨玩具。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Constipation, IBS, imperforate anus便秘、腸躁症、肛門閉鎖
  • Constipation/encopresis: fiber and fluids, less milk/sugar, enemas to clear impaction (watch hypernatremia, hyperphosphatemia), toilet sitting 5-10 minutes after meals, treatment 3-6 months.便秘/大便失禁：高纖高水分、減少牛奶與糖、灌腸清除糞塊（留意高血鈉、高血磷）、餐後如廁坐 5–10 分鐘、治療 3–6 個月。
  • IBS: diffuse pain, alternating constipation/diarrhea with mucus; reassure, moderate fiber low fat, anticholinergics.腸躁症：瀰漫性腹痛、便祕腹瀉交替含黏液；安撫、中等纖維低脂飲食、抗膽鹼藥。
  • Imperforate anus: no meconium, absent anus, anal membrane or fistula; stool in urine/vagina indicates fistula.肛門閉鎖：無胎便、無肛門、肛膜或瘻管；尿或陰道有糞便代表瘻管。
  • Postop: side-lying prone with hips elevated, or legs suspended; no rectal temperatures; anal dilation only with provider-supplied dilator, 1-2 cm, water-soluble lubricant.術後：側俯臥抬高臀部，或腿懸吊成 90 度；不量肛溫；僅用醫師提供之擴張器、插入 1–2 公分、水溶性潤滑劑。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Hepatitis肝炎
  • HAV: fecal-oral; vaccine 2 doses from 12 months; enteric precautions at least 1 week after jaundice; may return to school 1 week after jaundice onset.A 肝：糞口傳染；12 個月起接種 2 劑疫苗；黃疸後至少 1 週腸道隔離；黃疸起 1 週後可返校。
  • HBV: mostly perinatal; vaccine before discharge then at 1 and 6 months; HBIG for newborns of HBsAg-positive mothers; can cause cirrhosis and liver cancer.B 肝：多為周產期傳染；出院前接種，之後 1 與 6 個月；母親 HBsAg 陽性新生兒給 HBIG；可致肝硬化與肝癌。
  • HCV parenteral and often chronic; HDV only with HBV; HEV uncommon, no chronic state.C 肝經血液傳染且常慢性化；D 肝僅與 B 肝併發；E 肝罕見且不慢性化。
  • Phases: anicteric prodrome 5-7 days (anorexia, malaise, RUQ pain); icteric phase (jaundice, dark urine, pale stools, pruritus).分期：無黃疸前驅期 5–7 天（厭食、倦怠、右上腹痛）；黃疸期（黃疸、深色尿、灰白便、搔癢）。
  • Handwashing, standard and enteric precautions, bleach 1/4 cup per gallon for diaper surfaces, low-fat diet, caution with medications.洗手、標準與腸道隔離、尿布區以 1/4 杯漂白水兌 1 加侖水消毒、低脂飲食、用藥需謹慎。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
Poisonings and lead中毒與鉛中毒
  • Poisoning first steps: ABCs, stop exposure, identify agent/amount, prevent absorption, call Poison Control.中毒首要：ABC、終止暴露、辨識毒物與量、阻止吸收、聯絡毒物中心。
  • Lead: hand-to-mouth from paint chips/dust/pottery; CNS most affected; screen at 1-2 years; levels under 5 mcg/dL rescreen yearly, 20-44 treatment, 45-69 within 48 hr, 70 or more immediate.鉛：經手口接觸漆片/灰塵/陶器；中樞神經最受影響；1–2 歲篩檢；<5 mcg/dL 每年複查，20–44 治療，45–69 於 48 小時內，≥70 立即處理。
  • Chelation: calcium disodium EDTA, succimer (oral), BAL (peanut oil: contraindicated with peanut allergy, G6PD deficiency, and iron). Ensure urine output first; monitor kidneys.螯合治療：calcium disodium EDTA、succimer（口服）、BAL（花生油製劑：花生過敏、G6PD 缺乏、併用鐵劑者禁用）。先確認尿量；監測腎功能。
  • Acetaminophen toxic at 150 mg/kg or more; stages: GI symptoms, latent 24-36 h, hepatic injury; antidote N-acetylcysteine; charcoal if under 4 hours; do not use charcoal with NAC.乙醯胺酚毒性劑量 ≥150 mg/kg；階段：腸胃症狀、24–36 小時潛伏期、肝損傷；解毒劑 N-acetylcysteine；<4 小時可給活性碳；活性碳不與 NAC 併用。
  • Aspirin: tinnitus, hyperpnea, fever, metabolic acidosis; charcoal, IV fluids, sodium bicarbonate, cooling, dialysis if needed.阿斯匹靈：耳鳴、過度換氣、發燒、代謝性酸中毒；活性碳、靜脈輸液、碳酸氫鈉、降溫、必要時透析。
  • Corrosives: dilute with water/milk (up to 4 oz), do NOT induce vomiting or neutralize.腐蝕劑：以水/牛奶稀釋（最多 4 盎司）、不可催吐、不可中和。
Topic 10 · tap to flip主題 10 · 點擊翻面
Intestinal parasites腸道寄生蟲
  • Giardiasis: protozoa in daycare; diarrhea, steatorrhea, cramps, FTT; 3 or more stool samples.梨形鞭毛蟲症：日托常見；腹瀉、脂肪便、絞痛、生長遲滯；需 ≥3 次糞便檢體。
  • Pinworms: intense perianal itching, restless sleep; tape test in morning; flashlight inspection 2-3 hours after sleep.蟯蟲：肛門劇癢、睡眠不安；晨間透明膠帶採檢；入睡後 2–3 小時以手電筒檢查。
  • Treat all family members; repeat medication in 2 weeks; hot-water laundry and meticulous handwashing.全家同時治療；2 週後重複給藥；熱水洗衣並勤洗手。
