This chapter reviews cardiac anatomy and conduction, diagnostic tests and procedures, cardiac surgery, dysrhythmias with defibrillation/cardioversion/pacemakers, coronary artery disease, angina, MI, heart failure, inflammatory heart disease, valvular disease, cardiomyopathy, vascular and arterial disorders, aneurysms, and hypertension. Priorities: recognize life-threatening rhythms, perfusion problems, and post-procedure complications.本章涵蓋心臟解剖與傳導系統、診斷檢查與處置、心臟手術、心律不整(含去顫/電擊/節律器)、冠狀動脈疾病、心絞痛、心肌梗塞、心衰竭、心臟發炎性疾病、瓣膜疾病、心肌病變、血管與動脈疾病、動脈瘤及高血壓。重點為辨識致命性心律、灌流問題及術後併發症。

1Anatomy and physiology解剖與生理

  • Heart wall: epicardium (outer), myocardium (muscle), endocardium (inner lining). Pericardial space holds 5-20 mL fluid.心壁分三層:心外膜、心肌層、心內膜。心包腔內含5-20 mL液體。
  • Valves: tricuspid and mitral (AV valves) close at start of ventricular contraction; pulmonic and aortic (semilunar) open during contraction.瓣膜:三尖瓣與二尖瓣(房室瓣)於心室收縮開始時關閉;肺動脈瓣與主動脈瓣(半月瓣)於收縮時開啟。
  • Conduction: SA node 60-100/min, AV node 40-60/min as backup, Purkinje fibers 20-40/min.傳導:竇房結60-100次/分;房室結備援40-60次/分;浦金氏纖維20-40次/分。
  • Coronary arteries: RCA supplies SA/AV nodes and inferior wall; LAD supplies anterior wall, septum and apex; circumflex supplies lateral and posterior wall.冠狀動脈:右冠狀動脈供應竇房結/房室結與下壁;左前降支供應前壁、中隔與心尖;迴旋支供應側壁與後壁。
  • S1 = AV valves closing (loudest at apex); S2 = semilunar valves closing; S3 suggests heart failure/regurgitation (normal under 30); S4 suggests stiff ventricle.S1為房室瓣關閉(心尖最清楚);S2為半月瓣關閉;S3提示心衰竭/逆流(30歲以下可正常);S4提示心室僵硬。
  • Sympathetic (norepinephrine) raises HR, contractility and vasoconstriction; parasympathetic (acetylcholine) lowers HR. Baroreceptors in aortic arch and carotid sinuses regulate BP.交感神經(norepinephrine)使心率、收縮力與血管收縮增加;副交感(acetylcholine)使心率下降。壓力感受器位於主動脈弓與頸動脈竇,調節血壓。
  • Renin-angiotensin-aldosterone: renin makes angiotensin I, converted to II in lungs; AII causes vasoconstriction and aldosterone-driven Na/water retention.腎素-血管收縮素-醛固酮系統:腎素產生血管收縮素I,於肺轉成II;AII引起血管收縮與醛固酮造成鈉水滯留。

2Laboratory diagnostic tests實驗室檢查

  • Troponin I rises within 3 hours and stays elevated 7-10 days (normal <0.35 ng/mL; troponin T <0.1). High-sensitivity troponin detects injury in 60-90 minutes.Troponin I於3小時內上升,持續7-10天(正常<0.35 ng/mL;troponin T<0.1)。高敏感度troponin可在60-90分鐘偵測損傷。
  • CK-MB (2-6 ng/mL) rises within hours, peaks at 18 hours; myoglobin rises in 2 hours, declines by 7 hours but is not cardiac specific.CK-MB(2-6 ng/mL)數小時內上升,18小時達高峰;肌紅素2小時上升、7小時後下降,但非心臟特異。
  • BNP normally <100 ng/mL; higher means more severe heart failure. hsCRP <1 mg/L low risk, >3 mg/L high risk.BNP正常<100 ng/mL;越高代表心衰竭越嚴重。hsCRP<1 mg/L低風險,>3 mg/L高風險。
  • Hypokalemia: flat/inverted T, U wave, ST depression, ventricular dysrhythmias, digoxin toxicity risk. Hyperkalemia: tall peaked T, wide QRS, flat P, asystole.低血鉀:T波平坦/倒置、U波、ST下降、心室心律不整、增加digoxin中毒風險。高血鉀:高尖T波、QRS變寬、P波消失、心搏停止。
  • Hypocalcemia prolongs ST/QT; hypercalcemia shortens ST, AV block; low magnesium causes VT/VF; high Mg gives weakness, hypotension, bradycardia.低血鈣使ST/QT延長;高血鈣使ST縮短、房室傳導阻滯;低血鎂可致VT/VF;高血鎂致無力、低血壓、心搏過緩。
  • WBC rises after MI; BUN rises in HF/cardiogenic shock; coagulation factors rise after MI; elevated Lp(a) and homocysteine raise CVD risk.MI後白血球上升;心衰竭/心因性休克時BUN上升;MI後凝血因子上升;Lp(a)與同半胱胺酸升高增加心血管風險。

3Diagnostic procedures, cardiac catheterization and revascularization診斷處置、心導管與血運重建

  • ECG basics: PR 0.12-0.20 s, QRS 0.04-0.10 s, QT 0.32-0.40 s; each small box 0.04 s, large 0.20 s. Holter records 24+ hours.心電圖基礎:PR 0.12-0.20秒、QRS 0.04-0.10秒、QT 0.32-0.40秒;小格0.04秒、大格0.20秒。Holter監測24小時以上。
  • Stress test: avoid smoking, alcohol, caffeine; beta/calcium blockers usually held that day, theophylline 12 hours before; dipyridamole/dobutamine if cannot exercise.運動負荷測試:避免吸菸、酒精、咖啡因;當天通常停用β阻斷劑/鈣通道阻斷劑,theophylline於前12小時停用;無法運動者用dipyridamole/dobutamine。
  • MRI: screen for pacemaker/metal. TEE is invasive (prepare like endoscopy).MRI:檢查有無節律器/金屬。經食道超音波(TEE)為侵入性,準備類似內視鏡。
  • Cardiac cath pre-procedure: consent, check iodine/seafood allergy, NPO (solids 6-8 h, liquids 4 h), baseline pulses and weight; hold metformin 24 hours before and until renal function reviewed (about 48 hours after).心導管術前:同意書、確認碘/海鮮過敏、禁食(固體6-8小時、液體4小時)、記錄基線脈搏與體重;metformin術前24小時停用,待腎功能確認後(約術後48小時)恢復。
  • Post-cath: vitals and rhythm q30 min x 2 hours; check distal pulses, color, warmth; cool/pale limb or lost pulse = emergency (clot). Bleeding: manual pressure. Extremity straight 4-6 h, bed rest 6-12 h, HOB no more than 15 degrees if no closure device; push fluids to flush dye.心導管術後:前2小時每30分鐘監測生命徵象與心律;檢查遠端脈搏、顏色、溫度;肢體冰冷蒼白或脈搏消失為急症(血栓)。出血時手動加壓。無縫合裝置時肢體伸直4-6小時、臥床6-12小時,床頭≤15度;鼓勵補充水分以排除顯影劑。
  • PTCA balloon dilates artery; complications: dissection, rupture, embolization, spasm, MI, reocclusion. Stents need antiplatelets (clopidogrel + aspirin) for months; IV nitroglycerin may prevent spasm.PTCA以球囊擴張血管;併發症:剝離、破裂、栓塞、痙攣、心肌梗塞、再阻塞。支架術後需抗血小板藥(clopidogrel+aspirin)數月;IV nitroglycerin可預防痙攣。
  • Other options: laser angioplasty, atherectomy, transmyocardial revascularization, IVUS, CAC score >300 high risk.其他:雷射血管成形術、粥狀斑塊切除術、經心肌雷射血管重建術、血管內超音波;冠狀動脈鈣化分數>300為高風險。

4CABG and heart transplant冠狀動脈繞道手術與心臟移植

  • CABG uses saphenous vein or internal mammary artery; MIDCAB for LAD lesions needs no sternotomy or bypass pump.冠狀動脈繞道術使用隱靜脈或內乳動脈;MIDCAB用於左前降支病灶,不需胸骨切開或體外循環。
  • Pre-op: teach splinting, coughing, incentive spirometer, tubes/ventilator; diuretics stopped 2-3 days, digoxin 12 hours, aspirin/anticoagulants 1 week before.術前:教導按壓傷口、咳嗽、誘發性肺量計、管路/呼吸器;利尿劑術前2-3天、digoxin術前12小時、aspirin/抗凝血劑術前1週停用。
  • Post-op: chest tube drainage over 100-150 mL/hr reported; keep K+ 4-5 mEq/L; fluids 1500-2000 mL; rewarm no faster than 1.8 degrees per hour; ground equipment for epicardial wires (microshock).術後:胸管引流量>100-150 mL/小時需通報;維持鉀4-5 mEq/L;液體限制1500-2000 mL;回溫速度每小時不超過1.8度;心外膜導線設備須接地(防微電擊)。
  • Tamponade signs: sudden stop of heavy mediastinal drainage, JVD with clear lungs, equalized pressures, pulsus paradoxus. Anginal pain suggests graft failure.心包填塞徵象:大量縱隔引流突然停止、頸靜脈怒張但肺部清晰、壓力趨於相等、奇脈。心絞痛樣疼痛提示移植血管失敗。
  • Recovery: stop activity if BP drops 10-20 mm Hg or pulse rises over 10; sternum heals 6-8 weeks; avoid pushing/pulling 6 weeks; infection if WBC/fever persists after 3-4 days.恢復:血壓下降10-20 mm Hg或脈搏增加>10次應停止活動;胸骨約6-8週癒合;6週內避免推拉;3-4天後仍發燒/白血球高提示感染。
  • Heart transplant: lifelong immunosuppression, infection risk, denervated heart (no angina, resting rate about 100, slow response to exercise), two P waves on ECG, rejection signs: hypotension, dysrhythmias, fatigue; biopsies for surveillance.心臟移植:終身免疫抑制、感染風險、心臟去神經(無心絞痛、靜止心率約100、運動反應慢)、心電圖有兩種P波、排斥徵象:低血壓、心律不整、疲倦;定期心內膜切片。

5Cardiac dysrhythmias心律不整

  • Estimate rate with 6-second strip x 10. Normal sinus rhythm: regular, 60-100, normal PR and QRS.以6秒心電圖條帶QRS數x10估算心率。正常竇性心律:規則、60-100次/分、PR與QRS正常。
  • Sinus bradycardia (<60): treat if symptomatic with oxygen, atropine (avoid extra doses), then transcutaneous pacing; hold causative drugs.竇性心搏過緩(<60):有症狀時給氧、atropine(避免多餘劑量),再用經皮節律器;暫停可疑藥物。
  • Sinus tachycardia (100-180): find and treat the cause.竇性心搏過速(100-180):找出並治療原因。
  • Atrial fibrillation: 350-600 chaotic atrial impulses, no P waves, fibrillatory waves, thrombus/stroke risk; give anticoagulants, rate control, cardioversion (anticoagulate 4-6 weeks and TEE first, hold digoxin 48 h).心房顫動:每分鐘350-600次紊亂心房衝動、無P波、有顫動波、有血栓/中風風險;給予抗凝血劑、控制心室率、電擊(術前抗凝4-6週並做TEE,停digoxin 48小時)。
  • PVCs: check O2 sat and K+; notify provider for chest pain, increased frequency, multifocal, R-on-T, or runs of VT. Bigeminy, trigeminy, couplets described.PVC:檢查血氧與鉀;若胸痛、頻率增加、多源性、R-on-T或連續VT需通知醫師。依序有二聯、三聯、成對等型態。
  • VT (140-250/min): stable = oxygen plus antidysrhythmics; unstable with pulse = synchronized cardioversion; pulseless = defibrillation and CPR.VT(140-250次/分):穩定時給氧與抗心律不整藥;不穩定但有脈搏=同步電擊;無脈搏=去顫與CPR。
  • VF: no cardiac output, unconscious, no pulse; fatal in 3-5 minutes; CPR then immediate defibrillation, 2 minutes CPR between shocks.VF:無心輸出量、意識喪失、無脈搏;3-5分鐘內致命;CPR並立即去顫,每次電擊間CPR 2分鐘。
  • Sudden loss of complexes: assess the client first (asystole vs lead displacement) before touching equipment.心電圖波形突然消失:先評估個案(心搏停止或電極脫落),再處理儀器。

6Dysrhythmia management: cardioversion, defibrillation, AICD, pacemakers心律不整處置:電擊、去顫、AICD與節律器

  • Vagal maneuvers (carotid massage, Valsalva) for supraventricular tachycardia; need monitor, defibrillator ready, record strips.迷走神經刺激(頸動脈按摩、Valsalva)用於上心室心搏過速;需心電監測、備妥去顫器並記錄心電圖。
  • Cardioversion is synchronized (to R wave) with lower energy; sedate, consent if elective; turn off oxygen; clear everyone; after: airway first, then vitals, rhythm, chest burns.電擊(同步於R波)使用較低能量;給予鎮靜、擇期需同意書;關閉氧氣;確認無人接觸;術後先確保呼吸道,再評估生命徵象、心律與胸部灼傷。
  • Defibrillation is asynchronous for pulseless VT/VF: 120-200 J biphasic or 360 J monophasic; resume CPR immediately; confirm rhythm after placing pads; do not delay for drugs.去顫為非同步,用於無脈搏VT/VF:雙相120-200 J或單相360 J;立即恢復CPR;貼電擊板後先確認心律;不因給藥而延遲。
  • Pad placement: right of sternum 3rd ICS and left midaxillary 5th ICS; avoid breast tissue, medication patches, implanted devices; AED: stop CPR during analysis, shocks only for pulseless VT/VF.電擊板位置:胸骨右側第3肋間與左腋中線第5肋間;避開乳房組織、藥物貼片與植入裝置;AED:分析時停止CPR,僅對無脈搏VT/VF放電。
  • AICD delivers 25-30 J up to 4 times; client sits or lies down after a shock, keeps a log, avoids strong magnetic fields, contact sports, MRI/diathermy; carry ID card.AICD放電25-30 J、最多4次;被電擊後應坐下或躺下、記錄日誌、避免強磁場、接觸性運動、MRI/透熱療法;隨身攜帶識別卡。
  • Pacemakers: demand (senses, paces if rate too low) vs fixed rate; spike before P = atrial pacing, before QRS = ventricular; capture = spike followed by depolarization.節律器:需求型(感知後才起搏)與固定頻率型;P波前尖峰=心房起搏、QRS前尖峰=心室起搏;有後續去極化即為起搏成功(capture)。
  • Transcutaneous pacing: do not take pulse/BP on left side; lack of capture = check skin contact then raise current. Temporary wires: insulate, ground equipment to prevent microshock.經皮起搏:勿測左側脈搏/血壓;失去capture時先檢查皮膚接觸,再提高電流。暫時性導線:絕緣、設備接地以防微電擊。
  • Permanent pacemaker: limit operative arm movement, report fever/swelling, dizziness, edema; battery 10 years (lithium); use cell phone on opposite side; avoid strong electromagnetic fields.永久性節律器:限制術側手臂活動、通報發燒/腫脹、頭暈、水腫;鋰電池約10年;手機用對側耳朵;避免強電磁場。

7Coronary artery disease and angina冠狀動脈疾病與心絞痛

  • CAD = atherosclerosis narrowing coronaries; significant when left main lumen reduced 50% or a major branch 75%.CAD為粥狀硬化使冠狀動脈狹窄;左主幹管徑減少50%或主要分支減少75%即有意義。
  • Ischemia: ST depression/T inversion; infarction: ST elevation, then T inversion and Q wave. Manage risk factors, diet low in calories, sodium, cholesterol and fat.缺血:ST下降/T波倒置;梗塞:ST上升,繼而T波倒置與Q波。控制危險因子,低熱量、低鈉、低膽固醇、低脂飲食。
  • Angina is O2 supply-demand imbalance. Stable: exertion, relieved by rest/NTG. Unstable: unpredictable, >15 min, escalating, may not respond to NTG. Variant (Prinzmetal): coronary spasm at rest, ST elevation.心絞痛為氧供需失衡。穩定型:運動誘發、休息/NTG可緩解。不穩定型:不可預測、>15分鐘、逐漸加重、NTG可能無效。變異型(Prinzmetal):冠狀動脈痙攣、休息時發作、ST上升。
  • Angina pain: substernal, squeezing, radiates to arm/jaw/back, usually <5 min (up to 15-20), relieved by rest or NTG; troponin normal.心絞痛疼痛:胸骨下壓榨感、放射至手臂/下顎/背部,通常<5分鐘(最長15-20分鐘),休息或NTG緩解;troponin正常。
  • Acute angina: stop activity, oxygen, monitor, NTG, semi-Fowler, 12-lead ECG, IV access; at home call EMS if NTG fails (aspirin may be chewed).急性心絞痛:停止活動、給氧、監測、NTG、半坐臥、12導程心電圖、建立靜脈管路;居家NTG無效應叫救護車(可嚼服aspirin)。
  • Medications: nitrates, calcium channel blockers (variant angina; avoid grapefruit juice), beta blockers, statins, antiplatelets.藥物:硝酸鹽、鈣通道阻斷劑(變異型心絞痛;避免葡萄柚汁)、β阻斷劑、statin、抗血小板藥。

8Myocardial infarction心肌梗塞

  • Evolves over hours; blue swollen area by 6 hours, gray with yellow streaks at 48 hours, granulation 8-10 days, scar 2-3 months. LAD = anterior/septal; circumflex = lateral/posterior; RCA = inferior.梗塞數小時內進展;6小時呈藍色腫脹、48小時灰色帶黃紋、8-10天形成肉芽組織、2-3個月形成疤痕。LAD=前壁/中隔;迴旋支=側壁/後壁;RCA=下壁。
  • Pain: crushing substernal, radiates to jaw/back/left arm, lasts 30+ minutes, not relieved by rest or NTG (opioids needed); nausea, diaphoresis, dyspnea, doom; women/older adults may present atypically (fatigue, dyspnea, confusion).疼痛:胸骨下壓榨痛、放射至下顎/背/左臂、持續30分鐘以上、休息或NTG無法緩解(需鴉片類);噁心、冒汗、呼吸困難、瀕死感;女性/老年人可能症狀不典型(疲倦、呼吸困難、意識混亂)。
  • Labs: troponin 3 h-7-10 days; CK-MB peaks 18 h, normal in 48-72 h; myoglobin 2 h; WBC up day 2. ECG: STEMI, T inversion, Q waves persist.檢驗:troponin 3小時起、持續7-10天;CK-MB 18小時達高峰、48-72小時恢復;肌紅素2小時;白血球第2天上升。心電圖:STEMI、T波倒置、Q波持續存在。
  • Acute care: morphine is priority for pain (reduces O2 demand), oxygen, NTG, monitor, semi-Fowler, IV, 12-lead ECG, beta blockers, thrombolytics within 6 hours if no emergent cath/PTCA.急性期:疼痛處置首選morphine(降低耗氧)、給氧、NTG、監測、半坐臥、靜脈管路、12導程心電圖、β阻斷劑,若無緊急心導管/PTCA則6小時內給血栓溶解劑。
  • If SBP <100 or 25 mm Hg below prior, lower HOB and notify provider. Watch for poor output (cool, clammy, weak pulses), HF, dysrhythmias (PVCs, tachycardia in first hours).若收縮壓<100或比先前低25 mm Hg,降低床頭並通知醫師。注意輸出不足(冰冷濕黏、脈搏弱)、心衰竭、心律不整(最初數小時PVC、心搏過速)。

Cardiac dysrhythmias心律不整
  • Estimate rate with 6-second strip x 10. Normal sinus rhythm: regular, 60-100, normal PR and QRS.以6秒心電圖條帶QRS數x10估算心率。正常竇性心律:規則、60-100次/分、PR與QRS正常。
  • Sinus bradycardia (<60): treat if symptomatic with oxygen, atropine (avoid extra doses), then transcutaneous pacing; hold causative drugs.竇性心搏過緩(<60):有症狀時給氧、atropine(避免多餘劑量),再用經皮節律器;暫停可疑藥物。
  • Sinus tachycardia (100-180): find and treat the cause.竇性心搏過速(100-180):找出並治療原因。
  • Atrial fibrillation: 350-600 chaotic atrial impulses, no P waves, fibrillatory waves, thrombus/stroke risk; give anticoagulants, rate control, cardioversion (anticoagulate 4-6 weeks and TEE first, hold digoxin 48 h).心房顫動:每分鐘350-600次紊亂心房衝動、無P波、有顫動波、有血栓/中風風險;給予抗凝血劑、控制心室率、電擊(術前抗凝4-6週並做TEE,停digoxin 48小時)。
  • PVCs: check O2 sat and K+; notify provider for chest pain, increased frequency, multifocal, R-on-T, or runs of VT. Bigeminy, trigeminy, couplets described.PVC:檢查血氧與鉀;若胸痛、頻率增加、多源性、R-on-T或連續VT需通知醫師。依序有二聯、三聯、成對等型態。
  • VT (140-250/min): stable = oxygen plus antidysrhythmics; unstable with pulse = synchronized cardioversion; pulseless = defibrillation and CPR.VT(140-250次/分):穩定時給氧與抗心律不整藥;不穩定但有脈搏=同步電擊;無脈搏=去顫與CPR。
  • VF: no cardiac output, unconscious, no pulse; fatal in 3-5 minutes; CPR then immediate defibrillation, 2 minutes CPR between shocks.VF:無心輸出量、意識喪失、無脈搏;3-5分鐘內致命;CPR並立即去顫,每次電擊間CPR 2分鐘。
  • Sudden loss of complexes: assess the client first (asystole vs lead displacement) before touching equipment.心電圖波形突然消失:先評估個案(心搏停止或電極脫落),再處理儀器。

Dysrhythmia management: cardioversion, defibrillation, AICD, pacemakers心律不整處置:電擊、去顫、AICD與節律器
  • Vagal maneuvers (carotid massage, Valsalva) for supraventricular tachycardia; need monitor, defibrillator ready, record strips.迷走神經刺激(頸動脈按摩、Valsalva)用於上心室心搏過速;需心電監測、備妥去顫器並記錄心電圖。
  • Cardioversion is synchronized (to R wave) with lower energy; sedate, consent if elective; turn off oxygen; clear everyone; after: airway first, then vitals, rhythm, chest burns.電擊(同步於R波)使用較低能量;給予鎮靜、擇期需同意書;關閉氧氣;確認無人接觸;術後先確保呼吸道,再評估生命徵象、心律與胸部灼傷。
  • Defibrillation is asynchronous for pulseless VT/VF: 120-200 J biphasic or 360 J monophasic; resume CPR immediately; confirm rhythm after placing pads; do not delay for drugs.去顫為非同步,用於無脈搏VT/VF:雙相120-200 J或單相360 J;立即恢復CPR;貼電擊板後先確認心律;不因給藥而延遲。
  • Pad placement: right of sternum 3rd ICS and left midaxillary 5th ICS; avoid breast tissue, medication patches, implanted devices; AED: stop CPR during analysis, shocks only for pulseless VT/VF.電擊板位置:胸骨右側第3肋間與左腋中線第5肋間;避開乳房組織、藥物貼片與植入裝置;AED:分析時停止CPR,僅對無脈搏VT/VF放電。
  • AICD delivers 25-30 J up to 4 times; client sits or lies down after a shock, keeps a log, avoids strong magnetic fields, contact sports, MRI/diathermy; carry ID card.AICD放電25-30 J、最多4次;被電擊後應坐下或躺下、記錄日誌、避免強磁場、接觸性運動、MRI/透熱療法;隨身攜帶識別卡。
  • Pacemakers: demand (senses, paces if rate too low) vs fixed rate; spike before P = atrial pacing, before QRS = ventricular; capture = spike followed by depolarization.節律器:需求型(感知後才起搏)與固定頻率型;P波前尖峰=心房起搏、QRS前尖峰=心室起搏;有後續去極化即為起搏成功(capture)。
  • Transcutaneous pacing: do not take pulse/BP on left side; lack of capture = check skin contact then raise current. Temporary wires: insulate, ground equipment to prevent microshock.經皮起搏:勿測左側脈搏/血壓;失去capture時先檢查皮膚接觸,再提高電流。暫時性導線:絕緣、設備接地以防微電擊。
  • Permanent pacemaker: limit operative arm movement, report fever/swelling, dizziness, edema; battery 10 years (lithium); use cell phone on opposite side; avoid strong electromagnetic fields.永久性節律器:限制術側手臂活動、通報發燒/腫脹、頭暈、水腫;鋰電池約10年;手機用對側耳朵;避免強電磁場。

Coronary artery disease and angina冠狀動脈疾病與心絞痛
  • CAD = atherosclerosis narrowing coronaries; significant when left main lumen reduced 50% or a major branch 75%.CAD為粥狀硬化使冠狀動脈狹窄;左主幹管徑減少50%或主要分支減少75%即有意義。
  • Ischemia: ST depression/T inversion; infarction: ST elevation, then T inversion and Q wave. Manage risk factors, diet low in calories, sodium, cholesterol and fat.缺血:ST下降/T波倒置;梗塞:ST上升,繼而T波倒置與Q波。控制危險因子,低熱量、低鈉、低膽固醇、低脂飲食。
  • Angina is O2 supply-demand imbalance. Stable: exertion, relieved by rest/NTG. Unstable: unpredictable, >15 min, escalating, may not respond to NTG. Variant (Prinzmetal): coronary spasm at rest, ST elevation.心絞痛為氧供需失衡。穩定型:運動誘發、休息/NTG可緩解。不穩定型:不可預測、>15分鐘、逐漸加重、NTG可能無效。變異型(Prinzmetal):冠狀動脈痙攣、休息時發作、ST上升。
  • Angina pain: substernal, squeezing, radiates to arm/jaw/back, usually <5 min (up to 15-20), relieved by rest or NTG; troponin normal.心絞痛疼痛:胸骨下壓榨感、放射至手臂/下顎/背部,通常<5分鐘(最長15-20分鐘),休息或NTG緩解;troponin正常。

  • Medications: nitrates, calcium channel blockers (variant angina; avoid grapefruit juice), beta blockers, statins, antiplatelets.藥物:硝酸鹽、鈣通道阻斷劑(變異型心絞痛;避免葡萄柚汁)、β阻斷劑、statin、抗血小板藥。

Myocardial infarction心肌梗塞
  • Evolves over hours; blue swollen area by 6 hours, gray with yellow streaks at 48 hours, granulation 8-10 days, scar 2-3 months. LAD = anterior/septal; circumflex = lateral/posterior; RCA = inferior.梗塞數小時內進展;6小時呈藍色腫脹、48小時灰色帶黃紋、8-10天形成肉芽組織、2-3個月形成疤痕。LAD=前壁/中隔;迴旋支=側壁/後壁;RCA=下壁。
  • Pain: crushing substernal, radiates to jaw/back/left arm, lasts 30+ minutes, not relieved by rest or NTG (opioids needed); nausea, diaphoresis, dyspnea, doom; women/older adults may present atypically (fatigue, dyspnea, confusion).疼痛:胸骨下壓榨痛、放射至下顎/背/左臂、持續30分鐘以上、休息或NTG無法緩解(需鴉片類);噁心、冒汗、呼吸困難、瀕死感;女性/老年人可能症狀不典型(疲倦、呼吸困難、意識混亂)。
  • Labs: troponin 3 h-7-10 days; CK-MB peaks 18 h, normal in 48-72 h; myoglobin 2 h; WBC up day 2. ECG: STEMI, T inversion, Q waves persist.檢驗:troponin 3小時起、持續7-10天;CK-MB 18小時達高峰、48-72小時恢復;肌紅素2小時;白血球第2天上升。心電圖:STEMI、T波倒置、Q波持續存在。
  • Acute care: morphine is priority for pain (reduces O2 demand), oxygen, NTG, monitor, semi-Fowler, IV, 12-lead ECG, beta blockers, thrombolytics within 6 hours if no emergent cath/PTCA.急性期:疼痛處置首選morphine(降低耗氧)、給氧、NTG、監測、半坐臥、靜脈管路、12導程心電圖、β阻斷劑,若無緊急心導管/PTCA則6小時內給血栓溶解劑。
  • If SBP <100 or 25 mm Hg below prior, lower HOB and notify provider. Watch for poor output (cool, clammy, weak pulses), HF, dysrhythmias (PVCs, tachycardia in first hours).若收縮壓<100或比先前低25 mm Hg,降低床頭並通知醫師。注意輸出不足(冰冷濕黏、脈搏弱)、心衰竭、心律不整(最初數小時PVC、心搏過速)。

  • Left HF: pulmonary congestion - dyspnea, crackles, cough, PND, nocturia. Right HF: systemic - dependent edema, JVD, hepatomegaly, abdominal distention, weight gain.左心衰竭:肺部鬱血-呼吸困難、囉音、咳嗽、夜間陣發性呼吸困難、夜尿。右心衰竭:全身性-下垂性水腫、頸靜脈怒張、肝腫大、腹脹、體重增加。

  • Embolectomy: baseline pulses, bed cradle, extremity slightly dependent; watch reperfusion compartment swelling (pain on passive movement, tense muscle, numbness).取栓術:基線脈搏、床架、肢體略下垂;注意再灌流造成肌肉腫脹(被動活動疼痛、肌肉緊繃、麻木)。

  • BP categories: normal <120/<80; elevated 120-129/<80; stage 1 130-139 or 80-89; stage 2 >=140 or >=90. Higher value classifies.血壓分類:正常<120/<80;偏高120-129/<80;第1期130-139或80-89;第2期>=140或>=90。取較高者分類。
  • Lifestyle: sodium about 2 g/day, weight loss, exercise (no isometrics/heavy lifting), limit alcohol/caffeine, stop smoking; drugs lifelong, never stop for side effects without calling.生活型態:鈉約2 g/天、減重、運動(避免等長/重量訓練)、限制酒精/咖啡因、戒菸;藥物需終身服用,有副作用先聯絡醫療人員,勿自行停藥。
