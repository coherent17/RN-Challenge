This chapter covers medications used in pregnancy, labor, postpartum and the newborn: tocolytics, magnesium sulfate, antenatal corticosteroids, labor analgesics, prostaglandins, oxytocin, postpartum hemorrhage drugs, Rho(D) immune globulin, rubella vaccine, surfactant, newborn eye prophylaxis, vitamin K, hepatitis B vaccine, contraceptives and fertility drugs. The key items are safety checks (respirations, DTRs, urine output, blood pressure) and antidotes.本章涵蓋孕期、生產、產後與新生兒用藥：安胎藥、硫酸鎂、產前類固醇、產程止痛藥、前列腺素、催產素、產後出血用藥、Rho(D)免疫球蛋白、德國麻疹疫苗、表面張力素、新生兒眼部預防、維生素K、B型肝炎疫苗、避孕藥與助孕藥。重點在給藥前安全評估（呼吸、深肌腱反射、尿量、血壓）與解毒劑。

1Tocolytics overview安胎藥總論

  • FDA's PLLR replaced pregnancy letter categories (A, B, C, D, X) with narrative risk-benefit information.FDA的PLLR取代了孕期字母分級（A、B、C、D、X），改以文字敘述風險與效益。
  • Tocolytics relax the uterus to delay preterm birth. Maternal contraindications: severe preeclampsia/eclampsia, active bleeding, intrauterine infection, cardiac disease, abruption, uncontrolled diabetes.安胎藥使子宮放鬆以延遲早產。母體禁忌：重度子癇前症／子癇、活動性出血、子宮內感染、心臟病、胎盤早剝、血糖控制不良。
  • Fetal contraindications: >37 weeks, dilation >4 cm, fetal demise, lethal anomaly, chorioamnionitis, acute distress, chronic IUGR.胎兒禁忌：大於37週、擴張大於4公分、胎死腹中、致死性異常、絨毛膜羊膜炎、急性窘迫、慢性生長遲滯。
  • Care: side-lying, frequent maternal/fetal/labor monitoring, adverse effects, daily weight, I&O, psychosocial support.護理：側臥、頻繁監測母體／胎兒／產程、副作用、每日體重、輸出入量、心理支持。
  • Indomethacin (NSAID): not >2-3 days, avoid >32 weeks (ductus arteriosus closure), oligohydramnios, NEC/IVH risk. Nifedipine (calcium channel blocker): tachycardia, hypotension, headache, flushing; avoid with magnesium sulfate.Indomethacin（NSAID）：不超過2至3天，32週後避免（動脈導管早閉）、羊水過少、增加NEC／腦室內出血風險。Nifedipine（鈣離子阻斷劑）：心搏過速、低血壓、頭痛、臉潮紅；避免與硫酸鎂併用。

2Magnesium sulfate硫酸鎂

  • CNS depressant and smooth muscle relaxant; used to prevent/control seizures in preeclampsia/eclampsia and sometimes for preterm labor. Antidote: calcium gluconate.中樞神經抑制劑與平滑肌鬆弛劑；用於預防及控制子癇前症／子癇抽搐，有時用於早產。解毒劑：葡萄糖酸鈣。
  • Toxicity: respiratory depression (RR <12), loss of DTRs, hypotension, flushing, extreme weakness, urine output <25-30 mL/hr (<100 mL/4 hr), pulmonary edema, heart block, cardiac arrest.中毒徵象：呼吸抑制（呼吸小於12次/分）、深肌腱反射消失、低血壓、臉潮紅、極度無力、尿量小於25至30 mL/小時（4小時小於100 mL）、肺水腫、心臟傳導阻滯、心跳停止。
  • Therapeutic level as tocolytic 4-7.5 mEq/L (2-3.75 mmol/L); check levels every 6 hr; give by IV pump only; check patellar reflex and RR before each parenteral dose; keep calcium gluconate and suction/oxygen at bedside.作為安胎藥的治療濃度4至7.5 mEq/L（2至3.75 mmol/L）；每6小時檢測；僅用靜脈輸液幫浦給藥；每次注射前檢查膝反射與呼吸；床邊備葡萄糖酸鈣、抽吸與氧氣。
  • Contraindicated in heart block, myocardial damage, kidney failure; newborn may show hypotonia and sleepiness; if used for preeclampsia it continues 12-24 hr postpartum.禁用於心臟傳導阻滯、心肌損傷、腎衰竭；新生兒可能肌張力低、嗜睡；用於子癇前症時產後持續12至24小時。
  • DTR grading 0-4: 2 is expected; 0 none, 1 diminished, 3 brisk, 4 clonus.深肌腱反射分級0至4：2為預期正常；0無反應、1減弱、3較活躍、4陣攣。

3Antenatal corticosteroids and analgesics產前類固醇與止痛藥

  • Betamethasone/dexamethasone speed fetal lung maturity (surfactant) for preterm labor at 28-32 weeks if labor can be delayed 48 hr; deep IM; monitor infection, WBC, glucose (hyperglycemia in diabetics), pulmonary edema.Betamethasone／dexamethasone促進胎兒肺成熟（表面張力素）；用於28至32週早產且可延遲48小時者；深部肌肉注射；監測感染、白血球、血糖（糖尿病者血糖升高）、肺水腫。
  • Opioid analgesics in labor: withhold and call provider if RR <=12; monitor FHR and contractions, hypotension (keep recumbent with hip wedge), bladder; keep naloxone ready (may precipitate withdrawal in dependent clients). Newborn withdrawal: irritability, tremors, seizures.產程鴉片類止痛藥：呼吸小於等於12次/分暫停給藥並通知醫師；監測胎心與宮縮、低血壓（採側臥並以楔形枕墊髖）、膀胱；備妥naloxone（對依賴者可誘發戒斷）。新生兒戒斷：易怒、顫抖、抽搐。

4Prostaglandins and oxytocin前列腺素與催產素

  • Prostaglandins ripen cervix when Bishop score <=4 (dinoprostone PGE2 gel/insert; misoprostol PGE1). Effects: GI upset, fever, tachysystole (>=12 contractions in 20 min), hyperstimulation, meconium. Client voids first, lies supine with lateral tilt or side-lying 30-60 min (gel) to 2 hr (insert). Stop when Bishop >=8 or 3+ contractions in 10 min; oxytocin can start 6-12 hr later.前列腺素用於Bishop評分小於等於4時軟化子宮頸（dinoprostone PGE2凝膠／栓劑；misoprostol PGE1）。副作用：腸胃不適、發燒、過度收縮（20分鐘內大於等於12次）、過度刺激、胎便。先排尿，採仰臥並側傾或側臥：凝膠30至60分鐘、栓劑至2小時。Bishop達8以上或10分鐘內達3次以上宮縮即停；催產素可於6至12小時後開始。
  • Prostaglandin contraindications include prior cesarean or uterine surgery, fetal malpresentation, nonreassuring FHR, previa, significant CPD, regular contractions.前列腺素禁忌：曾剖腹產或子宮手術、胎位不正、胎心不安心、前置胎盤、明顯頭骨盆不對稱、已有規律宮縮。
  • Oxytocin: increases force, frequency, duration of contractions; milk let-down; induces or augments labor, controls postpartum bleeding. Give IV by pump, piggyback at port nearest the insertion, never leave client unattended. Monitor vitals, contractions, FHR every 15 min. Risks: hypertonic contractions, uterine rupture, water intoxication, postpartum atony. Contraindicated with inability to deliver vaginally, hypertonic contractions, active genital herpes.催產素：增強宮縮強度、頻率與持續時間；促進泌乳排出；引產或加強宮縮、控制產後出血。以靜脈幫浦給藥，接在最靠近穿刺處的接口，不可離開產婦。每15分鐘監測生命徵象、宮縮與胎心。風險：高張性宮縮、子宮破裂、水中毒、產後子宮收縮不良。禁忌：無法經陰道生產、高張性宮縮、活動性生殖器疱疹。
  • If hyperstimulation or nonreassuring FHR: stop oxytocin, side-lying, increase plain IV fluid, oxygen by face mask; have magnesium sulfate available to relax uterus.若過度刺激或胎心不安心：停催產素、側臥、增加無藥輸液速度、面罩給氧；備妥硫酸鎂以放鬆子宮。
  • Dose example: oxytocin 2 mU/min from 20 U in 1000 mL = 0.1 mL/min = 6 mL/hr (convert mU to U first).劑量範例：催產素2 mU/分，配置為20 U加於1000 mL，等於0.1 mL/分＝6 mL/小時（先將mU換算為U）。

5Postpartum hemorrhage drugs產後出血用藥

  • Methylergonovine (ergot alkaloid): firm tetanic uterine contraction, vasoconstriction. Give only after placenta delivered. Check BP first; withhold and notify if BP raised. Contraindicated in hypertension, preeclampsia, cardiovascular and peripheral vascular disease. Adverse: nausea, cramping, bradycardia, severe hypertension, MI.Methylergonovine（麥角生物鹼）：使子宮強直收縮、血管收縮。僅於胎盤娩出後使用。給藥前先量血壓；血壓高則暫停並通知醫師。禁用於高血壓、子癇前症、心血管與周邊血管疾病。副作用：噁心、絞痛、心搏過緩、嚴重高血壓、心肌梗塞。
  • Carboprost (PGF2 alpha): contracts uterus; adverse headache, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, fever, tachycardia, hypertension; contraindicated in asthma.Carboprost（PGF2α）：使子宮收縮；副作用頭痛、噁心嘔吐、腹瀉、發燒、心搏過速、高血壓；氣喘者禁用。
  • Oxytocin is the first-line uterotonic.催產素為首選子宮收縮劑。

6Rho(D) immune globulin and rubella vaccineRho(D)免疫球蛋白與德國麻疹疫苗

  • Rho(D) IG: Rh-negative, antibody-negative client; IM at 28 weeks and within 72 hr after delivery of an Rh-positive baby; also after amniocentesis, CVS, abortion, trauma, bleeding. Never IV, not for the newborn, no benefit if already sensitized (positive titer).Rho(D)免疫球蛋白：用於Rh陰性且抗體陰性者；28週肌肉注射，分娩Rh陽性嬰兒後72小時內再注射；羊膜穿刺、絨毛取樣、流產、外傷、出血後亦需給予。不可靜脈注射、不給新生兒、已致敏（抗體滴度陽性）者無效。
  • Rubella vaccine: subcutaneous before discharge if titer <1:8; check egg allergy and immunocompromised household members; avoid pregnancy 1-3 months.德國麻疹疫苗：滴度小於1:8者出院前皮下注射；評估蛋過敏與家人免疫功能低下；1至3個月內避免懷孕。

7Newborn medications新生兒藥物

  • Surfactant: intratracheal for RDS; watch bradycardia and desaturation; no suctioning for 2 hours after.表面張力素：氣管內給藥治療RDS；注意心搏過緩與血氧下降；給藥後2小時內避免抽吸。
  • Erythromycin eye ointment: legally required; prevents gonorrhea and chlamydia ophthalmia; clean eyes first, do not flush; may delay up to 1 hr for bonding.紅黴素眼藥膏：法律規定；預防淋病與披衣菌所致眼炎；先清潔眼睛、給藥後不沖洗；為親子依附可延後最多1小時。
  • Phytonadione (vitamin K): protect from light; IM into the vastus lateralis; newborns deficient for first 5-8 days; rare hyperbilirubinemia; monitor bruising and cord bleeding.維生素K（phytonadione）：避光；肌肉注射於股外側肌；新生兒前5至8天缺乏；少見高膽紅素血症；監測瘀青與臍帶出血。
  • Hepatitis B vaccine IM in vastus lateralis before discharge with parental consent; if mother HBsAg-positive, add hepatitis B immune globulin within 12 hr of birth.B型肝炎疫苗於出院前取得同意後肌肉注射股外側肌；母親HBsAg陽性者，出生12小時內加打B型肝炎免疫球蛋白。

8Contraceptives and fertility medications避孕藥與助孕藥

  • Combined estrogen-progestin suppress ovulation and thicken cervical mucus; usually 21 days on, 7 off; full effect up to 1 week.雌激素合併黃體素複方抑制排卵並使子宮頸黏液變稠；通常服21天、停7天；可能需1週才完全有效。
  • Risk: smoking, obesity, hypertension. Contraindicated: hypertension, thromboembolism, CVA/CAD, estrogen-dependent cancer, pregnancy. Antibiotics may reduce effectiveness; report thromboembolic signs; diabetics monitor glucose.風險：吸菸、肥胖、高血壓。禁忌：高血壓、血栓栓塞、腦中風／冠心病、雌激素依賴性癌症、懷孕。抗生素可能降低效果；回報血栓徵象；糖尿病者監測血糖。
  • Patch: 3 weeks on, 1 week off, new site weekly; off <24 hr reapply/replace, >24 hr start new cycle. Ring: 3 weeks in, 1 out; discard in foil, not toilet. Use backup 2 months after stopping before conceiving.貼片：貼3週、停1週，每週換新位置；脫落小於24小時可重貼或換新，超過24小時需開始新週期。陰道環：置入3週、取出1週；以鋁箔包好丟棄，不可丟馬桶。停藥後為排出荷爾蒙，需再避孕2個月才受孕。
  • Fertility drugs (clomiphene, menotropins, FSH analogs, hCG): risks multiple births, ovarian hyperstimulation (abdominal pain, distention, ascites, pleural effusion); teach timing of intercourse and follow-up.助孕藥（clomiphene、menotropins、FSH類似物、hCG）：風險為多胞胎、卵巢過度刺激（腹痛、腹脹、腹水、肋膜積水）；衛教行房時機與追蹤。

Contraindicated with inability to deliver vaginally, hypertonic contractions, active genital herpes.催產素：增強宮縮強度、頻率與持續時間；促進泌乳排出；引產或加強宮縮、控制產後出血。以靜脈幫浦給藥，接在最靠近穿刺處的接口，不可離開產婦。每15分鐘監測生命徵象、宮縮與胎心。風險：高張性宮縮、子宮破裂、水中毒、產後子宮收縮不良。禁忌：無法經陰道生產、高張性宮縮、活動性生殖器疱疹。
  • If hyperstimulation or nonreassuring FHR: stop oxytocin, side-lying, increase plain IV fluid, oxygen by face mask; have magnesium sulfate available to relax uterus.若過度刺激或胎心不安心：停催產素、側臥、增加無藥輸液速度、面罩給氧；備妥硫酸鎂以放鬆子宮。
  • Dose example: oxytocin 2 mU/min from 20 U in 1000 mL = 0.1 mL/min = 6 mL/hr (convert mU to U first).劑量範例：催產素2 mU/分，配置為20 U加於1000 mL，等於0.1 mL/分＝6 mL/小時（先將mU換算為U）。

Postpartum hemorrhage drugs產後出血用藥
  • Methylergonovine (ergot alkaloid): firm tetanic uterine contraction, vasoconstriction. Give only after placenta delivered. Check BP first; withhold and notify if BP raised. Contraindicated in hypertension, preeclampsia, cardiovascular and peripheral vascular disease. Adverse: nausea, cramping, bradycardia, severe hypertension, MI.Methylergonovine（麥角生物鹼）：使子宮強直收縮、血管收縮。僅於胎盤娩出後使用。給藥前先量血壓；血壓高則暫停並通知醫師。禁用於高血壓、子癇前症、心血管與周邊血管疾病。副作用：噁心、絞痛、心搏過緩、嚴重高血壓、心肌梗塞。
  • Carboprost (PGF2 alpha): contracts uterus; adverse headache, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, fever, tachycardia, hypertension; contraindicated in asthma.Carboprost（PGF2α）：使子宮收縮；副作用頭痛、噁心嘔吐、腹瀉、發燒、心搏過速、高血壓；氣喘者禁用。
  • Oxytocin is the first-line uterotonic.催產素為首選子宮收縮劑。

Rho(D) immune globulin and rubella vaccineRho(D)免疫球蛋白與德國麻疹疫苗
  • Rho(D) IG: Rh-negative, antibody-negative client; IM at 28 weeks and within 72 hr after delivery of an Rh-positive baby; also after amniocentesis, CVS, abortion, trauma, bleeding. Never IV, not for the newborn, no benefit if already sensitized (positive titer).Rho(D)免疫球蛋白：用於Rh陰性且抗體陰性者；28週肌肉注射，分娩Rh陽性嬰兒後72小時內再注射；羊膜穿刺、絨毛取樣、流產、外傷、出血後亦需給予。不可靜脈注射、不給新生兒、已致敏（抗體滴度陽性）者無效。
  • Rubella vaccine: subcutaneous before discharge if titer <1:8; check egg allergy and immunocompromised household members; avoid pregnancy 1-3 months.德國麻疹疫苗：滴度小於1:8者出院前皮下注射；評估蛋過敏與家人免疫功能低下；1至3個月內避免懷孕。

Newborn medications新生兒藥物
  • Surfactant: intratracheal for RDS; watch bradycardia and desaturation; no suctioning for 2 hours after.表面張力素：氣管內給藥治療RDS；注意心搏過緩與血氧下降；給藥後2小時內避免抽吸。

Contraceptives and fertility medications避孕藥與助孕藥
  • Combined estrogen-progestin suppress ovulation and thicken cervical mucus; usually 21 days on, 7 off; full effect up to 1 week.雌激素合併黃體素複方抑制排卵並使子宮頸黏液變稠；通常服21天、停7天；可能需1週才完全有效。
  • Risk: smoking, obesity, hypertension. Contraindicated: hypertension, thromboembolism, CVA/CAD, estrogen-dependent cancer, pregnancy. Antibiotics may reduce effectiveness; report thromboembolic signs; diabetics monitor glucose.風險：吸菸、肥胖、高血壓。禁忌：高血壓、血栓栓塞、腦中風／冠心病、雌激素依賴性癌症、懷孕。抗生素可能降低效果；回報血栓徵象；糖尿病者監測血糖。
  • Patch: 3 weeks on, 1 week off, new site weekly; off <24 hr reapply/replace, >24 hr start new cycle. Ring: 3 weeks in, 1 out; discard in foil, not toilet. Use backup 2 months after stopping before conceiving.貼片：貼3週、停1週，每週換新位置；脫落小於24小時可重貼或換新，超過24小時需開始新週期。陰道環：置入3週、取出1週；以鋁箔包好丟棄，不可丟馬桶。停藥後為排出荷爾蒙，需再避孕2個月才受孕。
  • Fertility drugs (clomiphene, menotropins, FSH analogs, hCG): risks multiple births, ovarian hyperstimulation (abdominal pain, distention, ascites, pleural effusion); teach timing of intercourse and follow-up.助孕藥（clomiphene、menotropins、FSH類似物、hCG）：風險為多胞胎、卵巢過度刺激（腹痛、腹脹、腹水、肋膜積水）；衛教行房時機與追蹤。
