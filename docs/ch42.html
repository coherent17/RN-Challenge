Chapter 42: Pediatric Medication Administration and Calculations第 42 章：兒童給藥與劑量計算

📖 Source: PDF p.578–585 · printed book p.520–526.e1來源：PDF 第 578–585 頁 · 書本印刷頁 520–526.e1

Safe medication administration in children: oral, subcutaneous/IM and IV routes, equipment such as burettes and syringe pumps, weight-based and BSA-based dose calculations, safe-dose checks, and age-appropriate approaches to give medications.兒童安全給藥：口服、皮下/肌肉與靜脈途徑、燒瓶（burette）與注射幫浦等設備、依體重與體表面積計算劑量、安全劑量檢核，以及依年齡的給藥技巧。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Oral medications口服藥物 📖 PDF p.578 (book p.520)

  • Most pediatric oral drugs are liquids, suspensions or chewables; measure with an oral syringe or suitable device chosen for developmental age.兒童口服藥多為液劑、懸液或咀嚼錠；依發展年齡以口服針筒或適當器具測量。
  • Shake suspensions well and give immediately after measuring so no drug settles out.懸液需充分搖勻，量好後立即給藥，避免藥物沉澱造成劑量不足。
  • Give upright with head elevated to prevent aspiration; hold small child sideways on lap, syringe toward the side/back of the cheek, slowly.採直立、頭抬高以防吸入；小兒側抱於腿上，針筒置於頰側後方，緩慢給予。
  • Check the mouth after tablets; enteric-coated, timed-release tablets and capsules must not be crushed.給錠劑後檢查口腔；腸溶錠、緩釋錠與膠囊不可壓碎。
  • Newborns and infants have immature liver and kidneys, so drug metabolism and elimination are delayed.新生兒與嬰兒肝腎功能未成熟，藥物代謝與排除延遲。

Medication guidelines for children兒童給藥原則 📖 PDF p.579 (book p.521)

  • Use two identifiers (name, MRN, birth date); ask parents about allergies and successful methods.使用兩項辨識（姓名、病歷號、生日）；詢問家長過敏史與成功的給藥方式。
  • Do not hide medication in food, milk or formula (may cause refusal and incomplete dose).不要將藥混入食物、牛奶或配方奶（可能導致拒食且劑量不足）。
  • For bad taste: pinch nose and drink through a straw; offer juice or a frozen juice bar afterward.味道不佳時：捏鼻並用吸管喝；服藥後可給果汁或冰棒。
  • Record the most successful method on the care plan.將最成功的給藥方式記錄於護理計畫。

Subcutaneous and intramuscular injections皮下與肌肉注射 📖 PDF p.578 (book p.520), PDF p.579 (book p.521)

  • Subcutaneous: insulin and some vaccines; sites include lateral upper arm, abdomen, anterior thigh.皮下注射：胰島素與部分疫苗；部位為上臂外側、腹部、大腿前側。
  • Infants: vastus lateralis is the preferred IM site; infant deltoid is not recommended.嬰兒：肌肉注射首選股外側肌；嬰兒不建議用三角肌。
  • IM volumes: vastus lateralis and ventrogluteal 0.5 mL infant, 0.5-2 mL child; deltoid 0.5-1 mL child.肌肉注射量：股外側肌與腹臀肌嬰兒0.5 mL、兒童0.5-2 mL；三角肌兒童0.5-1 mL。
  • Needle 22-25 gauge, about 5/8 to 1 inch; estimate length as half of the pinched muscle; small doses measured to nearest hundredth in a tuberculin syringe.針頭22-25號，約5/8至1吋；長度約為捏起肌肉厚度的一半；小劑量用結核菌素針筒測至百分位。
  • Place an adhesive bandage afterward.注射後貼上膠布。

Intravenous medications靜脈給藥 📖 PDF p.579 (book p.521), PDF p.580 (book p.522)

  • IV drugs are diluted; assess site for inflammation, infiltration or extravasation before, during and after.靜脈藥物需稀釋；給藥前、中、後評估注射部位有無發炎、滲漏或外滲。
  • Flush with normal saline (3-20 mL by policy) before and after, at the same rate as the drug; include flush volume in intake.給藥前後用生理食鹽水沖洗（依規定3-20 mL），速度與藥物相同；沖洗量計入輸入量。
  • Always use pumps/controllers; burette sets are microdrip (60 gtt/mL), 100-150 mL capacity, in 1-mL increments; label clearly.一律使用幫浦/控制器；burette為微滴型（60 gtt/mL）、容量100-150 mL、刻度1 mL；需清楚標示。
  • Syringe pump connects via Y-connector and delivers over the prescribed time.注射幫浦經Y型接頭連接，於指定時間內輸注。
  • Track 24-hour fluid intake including flushes to prevent overhydration.追蹤24小時輸液量（含沖洗量）以防水分過量。

Dose calculation by weight and BSA依體重與體表面積計算劑量 📖 PDF p.580 (book p.522), PDF p.581 (book p.523)

  • lb to kg: divide by 2.2; kg to lb: multiply by 2.2 (nearest tenth).磅轉公斤：除以2.2；公斤轉磅：乘以2.2（取至小數點後一位）。
  • Steps: convert weight, compute total daily dose (mg/kg/day x kg), divide by number of doses, compare with safe range.步驟：換算體重、計算每日總量（mg/kg/day x kg）、除以劑數、與安全範圍比較。
  • Example: ceftriaxone 500 mg for a 15 lb (6.8 kg) child; safe 50-75 mg/kg = 340-510 mg, so safe. Ampicillin 850 mg for 36 lb (16.4 kg) at 50 mg/kg = 820 mg, so unsafe.例：15磅（6.8 kg）孩童ceftriaxone 500 mg；安全範圍50-75 mg/kg＝340-510 mg，故安全。36磅（16.4 kg）ampicillin 850 mg，50 mg/kg＝820 mg，故不安全。
  • BSA dosing: dose per m2 x BSA (4 mg/m2 x 1.1 m2 = 4.4 mg); child dose from adult dose = (child BSA / 1.73) x adult dose.體表面積劑量：每m2劑量 x BSA（4 mg/m2 x 1.1 m2＝4.4 mg）；由成人劑量換算＝（兒童BSA/1.73）x 成人劑量。
  • If weight is unavailable in an emergency use a length-based tape (Broselow).緊急且無體重時使用依身長估算的Broselow色碼尺。

Developmental considerations發展階段考量 📖 PDF p.581 (book p.523), PDF p.582 (book p.524)

  • Infants: perform quickly, then hold and comfort; pacifier allowed.嬰兒：快速執行，之後擁抱安撫；可用奶嘴。
  • Toddlers and preschoolers: brief concrete explanation then perform; accept reasonable aggression; comfort afterward.幼兒與學齡前：簡短具體說明後立即執行；合理接受攻擊行為；事後安撫。
  • School-age: explain, offer some control, therapeutic play, set limits (crying ok, biting not).學齡兒童：說明並給予部分控制權、治療性遊戲、設定界線（可哭不可咬人）。
  • Adolescents: explain, allow control, encourage participation and self-expression.青少年：說明、給予控制權、鼓勵參與與自我表達。
  • Ask parents whether they want to stay present; plan comfort measures before and after.詢問家長是否願意在場；事前事後規劃安撫措施。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Never mix medication into food, milk or formula.不可將藥物混入食物、牛奶或配方奶。 📖 PDF p.579 (book p.521)
  2. Suspensions: mix well and administer immediately after measuring.懸液：搖勻並於量好後立即給藥。 📖 PDF p.578 (book p.520)
  3. Do not crush enteric-coated or timed-release tablets or capsules.不可壓碎腸溶錠、緩釋錠或膠囊。 📖 PDF p.578 (book p.520)
  4. Vastus lateralis is the best IM site for infants (deltoid not recommended).嬰兒最佳肌肉注射部位為股外側肌（不建議三角肌）。 📖 PDF p.579 (book p.521)
  5. IM volumes: infant 0.5 mL; child 0.5-2 mL (deltoid 0.5-1 mL).肌肉注射量：嬰兒0.5 mL；兒童0.5-2 mL（三角肌0.5-1 mL）。 📖 PDF p.579 (book p.521)
  6. Two identifiers before every administration.每次給藥前使用兩項辨識。 📖 PDF p.579 (book p.521)
  7. lb to kg: divide by 2.2.磅轉公斤：除以2.2。 📖 PDF p.581 (book p.523)
  8. Always check the calculated dose against the safe mg/kg range before giving.給藥前一律以安全mg/kg範圍檢核計算劑量。 📖 PDF p.581 (book p.523)
  9. Include IV flush volumes (3-20 mL) in intake; use pumps for all IV fluids and intermittent medications.靜脈沖洗量（3-20 mL）計入輸入量；所有靜脈輸液與間歇藥物使用幫浦。 📖 PDF p.580 (book p.522)
  10. Burette sets are microdrip, 60 gtt/mL.Burette為微滴型，60 gtt/mL。 📖 PDF p.580 (book p.522)
  11. Assess IV site for infiltration/extravasation before, during, after each IV medication.每次靜脈給藥前、中、後評估有無滲漏/外滲。 📖 PDF p.579 (book p.521)
  12. Infants have delayed drug metabolism/elimination due to immature liver and kidneys.嬰兒肝腎未成熟，藥物代謝與排除延遲。 📖 PDF p.578 (book p.520)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Dose formula: Desired / Available x Volume (D/H x Q).劑量公式：所需劑量/現有劑量 x 容量（D/H x Q）。 📖 PDF p.583 (book p.525)
  • Pounds to kilograms: 'divide by 2.2'; kilograms to pounds: 'multiply by 2.2' (kilo is bigger number of pounds).磅轉公斤「除2.2」；公斤轉磅「乘2.2」（同重量用磅的數字較大）。 📖 PDF p.581 (book p.523)

2. Ampicillin 125 mg IV is prescribed. After reconstitution the label states 1 g per 7.4 mL. How many mL does the nurse draw up?醫囑ampicillin 125 mg IV。復溶後標示每7.4 mL含1 g。護理師應抽取多少mL？

125 mg / 1000 mg x 7.4 mL = 0.925 mL.125 mg / 1000 mg x 7.4 mL = 0.925 mL。

3. Digoxin maintenance is 8 mcg/kg/day in two divided doses for a 7.2 kg infant. How many mcg per dose?7.2 kg嬰兒毛地黃維持量8 mcg/kg/day，分兩次。每次多少mcg？

8 x 7.2 = 57.6 mcg/day; divided by 2 = 28.8 mcg per dose. 57.6 is the daily total.8 x 7.2＝57.6 mcg/天；除以2＝每次28.8 mcg。57.6為每日總量。

4. A child weighs 33 lb. Gentamicin is prescribed at 2.5 mg/kg per dose. What dose does the nurse calculate (nearest tenth)?孩童體重33磅。醫囑gentamicin 2.5 mg/kg/次。護理師計算之劑量（取至小數第一位）？

33 / 2.2 = 15 kg; 15 x 2.5 = 37.5 mg. Mistakenly multiplying pounds by 2.5 gives 82.5.33/2.2＝15 kg；15 x 2.5＝37.5 mg。誤用磅數乘2.5會得82.5。
