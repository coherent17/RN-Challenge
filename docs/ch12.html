Chapter 12: Health and Physical Assessment of the Adult Client第 12 章：成人健康評估與身體評估

📖 Source: PDF p.168–191 · printed book p.143–165.e1來源：PDF 第 168–191 頁 · 書本印刷頁 143–165.e1

This chapter covers the interview setting, health history, mental status, the four assessment techniques, and a system-by-system physical assessment (skin through rectum), with normal findings, key abnormal findings, tests such as Snellen, Romberg, Brudzinski and Kernig, cranial nerves, and client teaching. It closes with documentation principles.本章涵蓋會談環境、健康史、心智狀態、四種評估技術,以及由皮膚到直腸的各系統身體評估,包括正常與重要異常發現、Snellen、Romberg、Brudzinski、Kernig 等檢查、腦神經與衛教,最後說明記錄原則。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Interview, setting and SOAP notes會談、環境與 SOAP 記錄 📖 PDF p.168 (book p.143), PDF p.169 (book p.144), PDF p.170 (book p.145), PDF p.171 (book p.146)

  • Ensure privacy, comfort, good lighting and no interruptions; sit at eye level without barriers such as a desk; use open-ended questions and therapeutic communication.確保隱私與舒適、光線充足、避免打擾;坐在與患者視線同高處、避免桌子等阻隔;使用開放式問句與治療性溝通。
  • Consider culture, language (use a professional interpreter, not family), values, eye contact and touch.考量文化、語言(使用專業口譯人員,不要由家屬代譯)、價值觀、眼神接觸與觸碰。
  • Assessment types: complete (baseline), focused (a limited problem), episodic/follow-up (progress), emergency (rapid, during lifesaving care).評估類型:完整(基準資料)、聚焦(特定問題)、階段性/追蹤(評估進展)、緊急(搶救中快速收集資料)。
  • SOAP = Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan. History of present illness uses OLDCARTS (onset, location, duration, character, aggravating, relieving, timing, severity).SOAP = 主觀、客觀、評估、計畫。現病史以 OLDCARTS 分析(開始、位置、持續、性質、加重、緩解、時間、嚴重度)。
  • Health history: general state of health, chief complaint (client's own words), family and social history, and domestic violence screening done privately.健康史:整體健康狀態、主訴(記錄患者原話)、家族史與社會史,以及在私下進行的家庭暴力篩檢。

Mental status and assessment techniques心智狀態與評估技術 📖 PDF p.168 (book p.143), PDF p.169 (book p.144), PDF p.172 (book p.147)

  • Mental status: appearance, behavior and level of consciousness, facial expression, speech, and cognitive function (orientation, attention, recent and remote memory, new learning, judgment, thought processes).心智狀態:外觀、行為與意識程度、表情、言語,以及認知功能(定向感、注意力、近期與遠期記憶、新學習、判斷力、思考過程)。
  • Order of techniques: inspection, palpation, percussion, auscultation; for the abdomen use inspection, auscultation, percussion, palpation.技術順序:視診、觸診、扣診、聽診;腹部則為視診、聽診、扣診、觸診。
  • Palpation: warm hands, light then deep, tender areas last. Percussion notes: resonance, hyperresonance, tympany, dullness, flatness.觸診:手保持溫暖、先輕後深、疼痛處最後。扣診音:清音、過度清音、鼓音、濁音、平音。
  • Vital signs, height, weight and nutritional status are part of the physical exam.生命徵象、身高、體重與營養狀態屬於身體評估的一部分。

Integumentary system皮膚系統 📖 PDF p.169 (book p.144), PDF p.170 (book p.145), PDF p.172 (book p.147)

  • Assess color, temperature, moisture, turgor, texture, lesions, edema, hair and nails; use a Wood's light for some lesions.評估顏色、溫度、濕度、彈性、質地、病灶、水腫、毛髮與指甲;部分病灶可用伍氏燈。
  • In dark skin: check lips/tongue (gray = cyanosis), nail beds/palms/soles (blue), conjunctivae for pallor, oral mucosa and sclera near the iris for jaundice.深色皮膚:看嘴唇/舌頭(灰色=發紺)、甲床/手掌/腳底(藍色)、結膜是否蒼白、口腔黏膜與靠近虹膜的鞏膜是否黃疸。
  • Capillary refill returns within 3 seconds when perfusion is normal. Poor turgor suggests severe dehydration or weight loss.灌流正常時微血管再充填在 3 秒內。皮膚彈性差提示嚴重脫水或體重過度減輕。
  • Pitting edema scale: 1+ slight, 2+ about 4 mm (10-15 s), 3+ deep (over a minute), 4+ very deep (2-5 min) with gross distortion.凹陷性水腫分級:1+ 輕微、2+ 約 4 mm(10-15 秒)、3+ 深(超過 1 分鐘)、4+ 非常深(2-5 分鐘)且肢體明顯變形。
  • Teach monthly skin self-exam with ABCDE: asymmetry, border, color, diameter over 6 mm, evolving; avoid sun exposure.教導每月皮膚自我檢查 ABCDE:不對稱、邊緣不規則、顏色不均、直徑大於 6 mm、變化中;避免曝曬。

Head, neck and lymph nodes頭、頸與淋巴結 📖 PDF p.172 (book p.147), PDF p.173 (book p.148)

  • Inspect/palpate skull and face, temporal arteries, frontal and maxillary sinuses; TMJ movement tests cranial nerve V.視診/觸診頭骨與臉部、顳動脈、額竇與上頜竇;顳顎關節活動測試第 V 對腦神經。
  • Test cranial nerve XI by chin and shoulder-shrug against resistance. The trachea should be midline.以抵抗阻力轉頭與聳肩測試第 XI 對腦神經。氣管應位於中線。
  • Thyroid: inspect while the client swallows, palpate anterior-posterior; a normal thyroid is usually not palpable; auscultate for a bruit if enlarged.甲狀腺:吞嚥時視診、由前後方觸診;正常甲狀腺通常摸不到;若腫大需聽診是否有血管雜音。
  • Palpate lymph nodes gently with circular finger pads, both sides, from preauricular down to supraclavicular; note size, shape, mobility, consistency, tenderness.以指腹輕柔畫圈觸診淋巴結,雙側比較,由耳前往下到鎖骨上;記錄大小、形狀、活動度、硬度與壓痛。
  • Never move the neck if a neck injury is suspected.懷疑頸部受傷時,絕不可活動頸部。

Eyes眼睛 📖 PDF p.173 (book p.148), PDF p.174 (book p.149), PDF p.175 (book p.150)

  • Inspect eyebrows, lashes, lids (ptosis), globes (exophthalmos/enophthalmos), conjunctiva (clear), sclera (white), cornea, iris.檢查眉毛、睫毛、眼瞼(下垂)、眼球(突眼/眼球內陷)、結膜(清澈)、鞏膜(白色)、角膜、虹膜。
  • Snellen chart: 20 feet, one eye at a time, keep distance glasses on; normal 20/20; tests cranial nerve II. Near vision normal 14/14. Confrontation tests peripheral vision.Snellen 視力表:距離 20 呎、單眼測試、保留遠視眼鏡;正常 20/20;測試第 II 對腦神經。近視力正常 14/14。對面法測周邊視野。
  • Corneal light reflex checks eye alignment; cover/uncover detects slight deviation; six cardinal positions test cranial nerves III, IV, VI and look for nystagmus.角膜光反射檢查眼軸平行;遮蓋/去遮蓋測微小斜視;六個基本注視方向測試第 III、IV、VI 對腦神經並觀察眼震。
  • Ishihara chart tests red-green color vision. PERRLA: pupils equal, round, reactive to light (direct and consensual) and accommodation.Ishihara 圖測紅綠色覺。PERRLA:瞳孔等大、圓形、對光(直接與間接)及調節有反應。
  • Ophthalmoscopy in a darkened room: red reflex; absent reflex may mean lens opacity; optic disc creamy yellow-orange to pink with distinct margins. Teach regular eye exams.眼底鏡檢查在暗室進行:觀察紅反射,缺失可能表示水晶體混濁;視神經盤為乳黃橘至粉紅色且邊緣清晰。教導定期眼科檢查。

Ears, nose, mouth and throat耳、鼻、口與喉 📖 PDF p.175 (book p.150), PDF p.176 (book p.151), PDF p.177 (book p.152)

  • Conductive hearing loss: obstruction to sound transmission. Sensorineural: cochlea, cranial nerve VIII or brain defect. Mixed: both.傳導性聽損:聲波傳導受阻。感覺神經性聽損:耳蝸、第 VIII 對腦神經或腦部缺損。混合性:兩者並存。
  • Whisper test (1-2 ft, one ear at a time) and ticking-watch test screen hearing; Weber/Rinne tuning-fork tests are less sensitive.耳語測試(1-2 呎、一次一耳)與滴答錶測試用於篩檢聽力;Weber/Rinne 音叉測試敏感度較低。
  • Otoscope: check canal for foreign body, never insert blindly; in adults pull the pinna up and back; normal drum is pearly gray with cone of light at 5 o'clock (right) and 7 o'clock (left). Clean ears with a washcloth, never cotton swabs.耳鏡:先檢查耳道有無異物,絕不盲目插入;成人將耳廓向上向後拉;正常鼓膜為珍珠灰,光錐在右耳 5 點、左耳 7 點。以濕毛巾清潔,勿用棉花棒。
  • Vestibular tests: Romberg (positive = marked sway), past pointing, gaze nystagmus, Dix-Hallpike.前庭檢查:Romberg(明顯搖晃為陽性)、指向偏差、凝視眼震、Dix-Hallpike。
  • Nose/sinuses: check patency, septum, sinus tenderness. Mouth/throat: lips, teeth, gums, tongue, buccal mucosa, palates, uvula (rises midline with 'ahh', CN X), tonsil grade 0-4+, tongue protrusion midline (CN XII), gag reflex (CN IX).鼻/竇:檢查通暢度、鼻中隔、竇壓痛。口/喉:嘴唇、牙齒、牙齦、舌、頰黏膜、顎、懸雍垂(發「啊」時居中上抬,第 X 對)、扁桃腺分級 0-4+、伸舌居中(第 XII 對)、作嘔反射(第 IX 對)。

Lungs肺部 📖 PDF p.177 (book p.152), PDF p.178 (book p.153), PDF p.179 (book p.154)

  • Record smoking in pack-years (packs per day x years). Screen for infectious respiratory illness (fever, cough, dyspnea, loss of taste/smell, exposure).吸菸史以包年記錄(每日包數 x 年數)。篩檢感染性呼吸道疾病(發燒、咳嗽、呼吸困難、味覺/嗅覺喪失、接觸史)。
  • Palpate chest excursion (symmetrical, thumbs separate about 2 inches/5 cm) and tactile fremitus ('ninety-nine').觸診胸廓擴張(對稱,雙拇指分開約 2 吋/5 公分)與觸覺震顫(說「九十九」)。
  • Percussion: resonance is normal; hyperresonance means excess air; dullness means density. Auscultate with the diaphragm, side to side, apices down; avoid breast tissue.扣診:清音為正常;過度清音表示空氣過多;濁音表示組織密度增加。以膜面聽診、左右對比、由肺尖往下;避開乳房組織。
  • Normal breath sounds: vesicular, bronchovesicular, bronchial (over trachea). Adventitious: fine crackles (end inspiration), coarse crackles, wheeze (high-pitched, asthma), rhonchi (low snoring), pleural friction rub.正常呼吸音:肺泡音、支氣管肺泡音、支氣管音(氣管上方)。異常音:細囉音(吸氣末)、粗囉音、哮鳴音(高音調、氣喘)、鼾音(低音調)、胸膜摩擦音。
  • Voice sounds (bronchophony, egophony, whispered pectoriloquy) are tested when pathology is suspected; teach smoking cessation and pneumonia/influenza vaccines.懷疑病變時測聲音傳導(支氣管語音、羊音、耳語胸音);教導戒菸與肺炎/流感疫苗。

Heart and peripheral vascular system心臟與周邊血管 📖 PDF p.179 (book p.154), PDF p.180 (book p.155), PDF p.181 (book p.156)

  • Apical impulse at the 4th-5th intercostal space, medial to the midclavicular line. Heart areas: aortic 2nd RICS, pulmonic 2nd LICS, Erb's point 3rd LICS, tricuspid 4th LICS, mitral 5th LMCL.心尖搏動位於第 4-5 肋間、鎖骨中線內側。聽診區:主動脈瓣右第 2 肋間、肺動脈瓣左第 2 肋間、Erb 點左第 3 肋間、三尖瓣左第 4 肋間、二尖瓣左鎖骨中線第 5 肋間。
  • S1 'lub' and S2 'dub' are normal; a murmur is a blowing or swooshing sound. Grades I-VI: grade IV and above have a palpable thrill.S1「lub」與 S2「dub」為正常;心雜音是吹風或嘶嘶聲。分級 I-VI:IV 級以上可摸到震顫。
  • Palpate only one carotid at a time. A carotid bruit signals turbulence or blockage: notify the provider and monitor neurological status.一次只觸診一側頸動脈。頸動脈雜音表示血流擾動或阻塞:通報醫師並監測神經功能。
  • Assess pulses for equality and force (0-4+), calf circumference and pretibial edema; use Doppler if pulse not palpable.評估脈搏對稱性與強度(0-4+)、小腿圍與脛前水腫;摸不到脈搏時用都卜勒。

Breasts and abdomen乳房與腹部 📖 PDF p.180 (book p.155), PDF p.181 (book p.156), PDF p.182 (book p.157)

  • Breast inspection with arms raised, hands on hips, and leaning forward; palpate supine with arm behind head and small pillow under shoulder, using pads of three fingers; one breast is often larger; axillary nodes are normally not palpable.乳房視診採舉手過頭、雙手叉腰、前傾姿勢;觸診平躺、同側手置頭後、肩下墊小枕,以三指指腹檢查;兩側常不一樣大;腋下淋巴結正常摸不到。
  • Teach breast self-exam and report lumps immediately.教導乳房自我檢查,發現腫塊須立即通報。
  • Abdomen: empty bladder, warm hands, painful areas last; order is inspection, auscultation, percussion, palpation.腹部:先排尿、手保持溫暖、疼痛處最後;順序為視診、聽診、扣診、觸診。
  • Bowel sounds: high-pitched gurgles 5-30 per minute, start in RLQ; listen 5 minutes before calling them absent. Tympany predominates; dullness over liver/spleen; costovertebral tenderness suggests kidney problem.腸蠕動音:高音調咕嚕聲每分鐘 5-30 次,從右下腹開始;判定消失前須聽 5 分鐘。以鼓音為主;肝脾上方為濁音;肋脊角壓痛提示腎臟問題。
  • Light palpation about 1 cm then deep 5-8 cm; the aortic pulsation expands laterally with an aneurysm.淺觸診約 1 公分、深觸診 5-8 公分;若有動脈瘤,主動脈搏動會向兩側擴張。

Musculoskeletal and neurological systems肌肉骨骼與神經系統 📖 PDF p.182 (book p.157), PDF p.183 (book p.158), PDF p.184 (book p.159), PDF p.185 (book p.160), PDF p.186 (book p.161)

  • Inspect gait, posture and spinal curves (lordosis, kyphosis, scoliosis); active and passive ROM; muscle strength 0-5 (5 = full range against gravity and full resistance).檢查步態、姿勢與脊椎彎曲(脊柱前凸、後凸、側彎);主動與被動關節活動度;肌力 0-5 級(5 級=可抗重力與完全阻力)。
  • Neuro exam: level of consciousness (Glasgow Coma Scale), pupils, motor, cerebellar (tandem gait, Romberg), coordination (rapid alternating movements, finger-to-nose, heel-to-shin), sensory (pain, light touch, position, stereognosis, graphesthesia).神經檢查:意識程度(格拉斯哥昏迷指數)、瞳孔、運動、小腦功能(直線行走、Romberg)、協調(快速輪替動作、指鼻、跟脛測試)、感覺(痛覺、輕觸、位置覺、實體覺、書寫覺)。
  • Cranial nerves: I smell, II vision, III/IV/VI eye movement, V face sensation and chewing, VII face movement and taste, VIII hearing and balance, IX/X swallowing and gag, XI shoulder shrug, XII tongue.腦神經:I 嗅覺、II 視覺、III/IV/VI 眼球運動、V 臉部感覺與咀嚼、VII 臉部運動與味覺、VIII 聽覺與平衡、IX/X 吞嚥與作嘔、XI 聳肩、XII 舌頭。
  • Deep tendon reflexes 0-4+ (2+ normal). Babinski's sign (great toe dorsiflexion with toe fanning) is abnormal over age 2 and indicates CNS disease.深部肌腱反射 0-4+(2+ 正常)。Babinski 徵象(大拇趾背屈、其餘腳趾散開)在 2 歲以上為異常,提示中樞神經疾病。
  • Meningeal irritation: Brudzinski (neck flexion makes hips and knees flex) and Kernig (pain when the flexed leg is extended).腦膜刺激徵象:Brudzinski(屈頸時髖膝不自主彎曲)與 Kernig(彎曲的腿伸直時疼痛)。
  • Teaching: calcium and vitamin D foods, avoid joint strain, keep normal weight; wear helmets and seat belts.衛教:攝取含鈣與維生素 D 食物、避免關節勞損、維持正常體重;戴安全帽與繫安全帶。

Genitalia, rectum and documentation生殖器、直腸與記錄 📖 PDF p.186 (book p.161), PDF p.187 (book p.162)

  • Female exam: calm approach, empty bladder, lithotomy position; the nurse prepares and assists. Speculum exam permits Pap/HPV screening; bimanual exam checks uterus and adnexa. Check for cystocele and rectocele.女性檢查:態度平穩、先排尿、截石位;護理師負責準備與協助。窺陰器檢查可做 Pap/HPV 篩檢;雙合診檢查子宮與附屬器。注意膀胱膨出與直腸膨出。
  • Male exam: inspect penis and scrotum (left testicle normally hangs lower), check inguinal canal for hernia with cough. Teach testicular self-exam on the same day monthly, after a warm bath or shower.男性檢查:檢查陰莖與陰囊(左側睪丸通常較低)、咳嗽時檢查腹股溝管有無疝氣。教導每月固定日期在溫水沐浴後做睪丸自我檢查。
  • Rectal exam: lithotomy or bent-forward (or left lateral) position; assess sphincter tone, masses and prostate (firm without nodules; hard nodules suggest cancer, boggy or tender suggests infection). Teach high-fiber, low-fat diet and screening.直腸檢查:截石位或前彎(或左側臥)姿勢;評估括約肌張力、腫塊與前列腺(堅實無結節;硬結提示癌症,鬆軟或壓痛提示感染)。教導高纖低脂飲食與篩檢。
  • Teach STI prevention and that partners need evaluation.教導預防性傳染病,並告知伴侶須接受檢查。
  • Documentation is a legal record: accurate, concise, complete, legible, objective; chart as soon as possible after assessment and protect confidentiality.記錄為法律文件:須準確、簡潔、完整、清晰、客觀;評估後儘快記錄並保護隱私。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Use a professional interpreter, not family or staff, for clients who need language services.需要語言協助的患者,應使用專業口譯人員,而非家屬或一般職員。 📖 PDF p.168 (book p.143), PDF p.188 (book p.163), PDF p.189 (book p.164)
  2. Order of assessment: inspection, palpation, percussion, auscultation; abdomen: inspection, auscultation, percussion, palpation.評估順序:視診、觸診、扣診、聽診;腹部:視診、聽診、扣診、觸診。 📖 PDF p.169 (book p.144), PDF p.182 (book p.157)
  3. Focused assessment targets the presenting problem (e.g., cough: lungs, temperature, respirations); complete assessment is the baseline.聚焦評估針對主訴(如咳嗽:肺部、體溫、呼吸);完整評估為基準資料。 📖 PDF p.169 (book p.144), PDF p.189 (book p.164), PDF p.190 (book p.165)

  5. Never perform neck movement if a neck injury is suspected.懷疑頸部受傷時,絕不可活動頸部。 📖 PDF p.173 (book p.148)

Snellen 20/20 tests cranial nerve II; six cardinal positions test III, IV, VI (extraocular muscle weakness); PERRLA documents normal pupils.Snellen 20/20 測第 II 對腦神經;六個基本注視方向測第 III、IV、VI 對(眼外肌無力);PERRLA 記錄正常瞳孔。 📖 PDF p.173 (book p.148), PDF p.174 (book p.149), PDF p.188 (book p.163), PDF p.189 (book p.164)
  7. Never insert an otoscope blindly; in adults pull the pinna up and back; never use cotton-tipped applicators in the ear.絕不盲目插入耳鏡;成人將耳廓向上向後拉;耳道勿使用棉花棒。 📖 PDF p.175 (book p.150), PDF p.176 (book p.151)
  8. Conductive hearing loss = physical obstruction to sound transmission; sensorineural = cochlea/CN VIII/brain.傳導性聽損=聲波傳導受阻;感覺神經性=耳蝸/第 VIII 對腦神經/大腦。 📖 PDF p.175 (book p.150), PDF p.188 (book p.163), PDF p.189 (book p.164)
  9. Positive Romberg = significant sway with feet together and eyes closed (balance/vestibular/cerebellar problem).Romberg 陽性=雙腳併攏閉眼時明顯搖晃(平衡/前庭/小腦問題)。 📖 PDF p.176 (book p.151), PDF p.183 (book p.158), PDF p.189 (book p.164)
  10. Wheezes (asthma, narrowed airways); fine crackles at end of inspiration (HF, pneumonia); rhonchi clear with coughing; pleural friction rub is not cleared by cough.哮鳴音(氣喘、氣道狹窄);吸氣末細囉音(心衰竭、肺炎);鼾音咳嗽後可能消失;胸膜摩擦音咳嗽無法消除。 📖 PDF p.179 (book p.154), PDF p.190 (book p.165)
  11. Carotid bruit: notify the provider and monitor neurological status; palpate carotids one at a time.頸動脈雜音:通報醫師並監測神經狀態;頸動脈一次只觸診一側。 📖 PDF p.180 (book p.155), PDF p.181 (book p.156)
  12. Absent bowel sounds can only be declared after listening 5 minutes; normal is 5-30/min.須聽診 5 分鐘才能判定腸蠕動音消失;正常為每分鐘 5-30 次。 📖 PDF p.182 (book p.157)
  13. Babinski's sign is abnormal after age 2 (CNS disease); positive Brudzinski/Kernig indicates meningeal irritation (meningitis).Babinski 徵象在 2 歲後為異常(中樞神經疾病);Brudzinski/Kernig 陽性表示腦膜刺激(腦膜炎)。 📖 PDF p.185 (book p.160), PDF p.186 (book p.161), PDF p.190 (book p.165)
  14. Cheyne-Stokes respirations: rhythmic breathing with periods of apnea.Cheyne-Stokes 呼吸:有規律的呼吸伴隨呼吸暫停期。 📖 PDF p.188 (book p.163), PDF p.189 (book p.164)
  15. Testicular self-exam: same day each month, after a warm shower or bath; breast self-exam and report lumps immediately.睪丸自我檢查:每月同一天、溫水沐浴後;乳房自我檢查,發現腫塊立即通報。 📖 PDF p.181 (book p.156), PDF p.187 (book p.162), PDF p.190 (book p.165)

1. A client who does not speak English requests an interpreter at triage. What is the best action?不會說英語的患者在急診檢傷時要求口譯。最佳作法為何?

A professional interpreter ensures accuracy and confidentiality. Family or non-clinical staff may paraphrase and breach privacy.專業口譯確保準確與隱私;家屬或非醫療人員可能轉述不實並洩漏隱私。

2. The nurse documents a positive Romberg test. Which observation supports this?護理師記錄 Romberg 測試陽性。下列哪項觀察支持此判斷?

Option 1 is nystagmus, option 2 Babinski, option 4 past pointing (vestibular).選項 1 為眼震、選項 2 為 Babinski 徵象、選項 4 為指向偏差(前庭功能)。

3. A client with suspected meningitis flexes the hips and knees when the nurse flexes the client's neck and reports spinal pain. This is which finding?疑似腦膜炎患者在護理師屈曲其頸部時,髖與膝不自主彎曲並訴脊椎疼痛。這是什麼徵象?

Brudzinski's sign is flexion of the hip and knee in response to neck flexion; Kernig's is pain on leg extension from a flexed hip and knee.Brudzinski 徵象是屈頸時髖膝彎曲;Kernig 徵象是髖膝彎曲後伸直腿時疼痛。

4. A client with asthma in respiratory distress is auscultated. Which adventitious sound is expected?氣喘發作呼吸窘迫的患者聽診,預期有哪種異常呼吸音?

Bronchoconstriction narrows airways and produces high-pitched musical wheezes. Stridor signals upper-airway obstruction.支氣管收縮使氣道狹窄,產生高音調哮鳴音;喘鳴提示上呼吸道阻塞。

5. A clinic client complains of cold symptoms, cough and chest congestion. What does the focused assessment include? Select all that apply.門診患者主訴感冒症狀、咳嗽與胸悶。聚焦評估應包括哪些?(複選) (Select all that apply)（複選）
