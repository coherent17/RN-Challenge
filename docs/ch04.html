Summary重點摘要Test-critical考試關鍵Mnemonics記憶口訣

This chapter gives a toolkit for answering NCLEX questions: read the question carefully, note strategic words and the subject, prioritize with ABCs, Maslow and the nursing process, apply NCJMM skills, and eliminate poor options. It also covers delegation, available-resources thinking and pharmacology tips.本章提供作答工具：仔細讀題、注意策略性字詞與主題，以 ABC、馬斯洛與護理過程排定優先順序、運用 NCJMM 技能並刪去不佳選項。另含委派、資源假設與藥理應試技巧。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Clinical judgment and key strategies臨床判斷與關鍵策略

  • Combine the NCJMM cognitive skills with other test-taking strategies.將 NCJMM 認知技能與其他應試策略合併運用。
  • Read every word, identify the subject, who the client is, strategic words, and whether the query is positive or negative.逐字閱讀，找出主題、題目中的病人是誰、策略性字詞，以及題目是正向或負向。
  • Use process of elimination; reread the question before the final choice.運用刪去法；選定前再讀一次題目。
  • Avoid 'What if...' reading into traditional items, but for NGN items consider existing and potential concerns.傳統題避免『如果……』過度臆測；NGN 題則需考量現存與潛在問題。
p.42 · book 24p.43 · book 25

2Avoiding reading into the question; ingredients of a question避免過度解讀；題目的組成

  • Base the answer only on the data given; look for abnormalities, the client of the question, and assume all needed resources are at the bedside.只依題目資料作答；找出異常、確認題目中的病人，並假設床邊具備所有所需資源。
  • Ingredients: event (the situation), event query (the question stem) and options.題目組成：事件（情境）、事件詢問（題幹）與選項。
  • Multiple response: each option is true or false; choose all true ones. Ordered response: prioritize actions.多重選擇：每個選項為真或假，選出所有真者；排序題：依優先順序排列。
  • Chart/exhibit items contain relevant data and distractors; fill-in-the-blank requires rechecking calculations with the on-screen calculator.圖表題含相關資料與干擾資訊；填空題需用螢幕計算機再次檢查計算。
  • NGN trend example: for a client with new type 2 diabetes, rising pulse, rising respirations, high glucose, falling potassium and falling urine output are worsening findings to follow up.NGN 趨勢範例：新診斷第 2 型糖尿病病人，脈搏上升、呼吸上升、血糖偏高、鉀下降與尿量減少為惡化而需追蹤的發現。

3Strategic words, subject, positive and negative event queries策略性字詞、主題、正負向詢問

  • Prioritizing words: best, early or late, essential, first, highest priority, immediate, initial, most, next, priority, primary.需排序的字詞：最佳、早期或晚期、必要、首先、最優先、立即、起始、最、接下來、優先、主要。
  • Assessment words (assess, check, determine, monitor, obtain information) often point to assessing first.評估字詞（評估、檢查、確定、監測、蒐集資料）常指向先評估。
  • A positive query asks for a correct item; a negative query (need for further teaching/follow-up) asks for an incorrect one.正向詢問要選正確項；負向詢問（需再教導/追蹤）要選不正確項。
  • Identify the subject; focus on it to rule out options that are true but irrelevant.辨識題目主題，排除正確但無關的選項。
p.43 · book 25p.46 · book 28p.47 · book 29

4Prioritizing: ABCs, CAB, Maslow優先順序：ABC、CAB、馬斯洛

  • ABCs: airway, then breathing, then circulation; for CPR use CAB (compressions, airway, breathing).ABC：先氣道、再呼吸、再循環；CPR 使用 CAB（按壓、氣道、呼吸）。
  • Maslow: physiological, safety and security, love and belonging, self-esteem, self-actualization; if no physiological option exists, choose safety.馬斯洛：生理、安全與保障、愛與歸屬、自尊、自我實現；若無生理選項，選安全。
  • When prioritizing, all options may be correct; choose the order or the most urgent.排序時所有選項可能都正確；需選最緊急或排出順序。
  • Actual problems take priority over potential problems in most situations.多數情況下，現存問題優先於潛在問題。
p.47 · book 29p.48 · book 30p.50 · book 32

5Nursing process and NCJMM in questions題目中的護理過程與 NCJMM

  • Assessment/recognize cues is first: choose the option collecting data when asked for first or initial action, unless it is an emergency.評估/辨識線索為第一步：問『首先』時選蒐集資料的選項，緊急情況除外。
  • Analysis: analyze cues and prioritize hypotheses; these are the hardest items and require interpreting data (e.g., ABG: pH 7.45, PCO2 30, HCO3 22 = compensated respiratory alkalosis).分析：分析線索並排序假設；最困難，需解讀資料（例：動脈血氣 pH 7.45、PCO2 30、HCO3 22 = 代償性呼吸性鹼中毒）。
  • Planning/generate solutions: set priorities and goals; implementation/take action: choose nursing actions, not medical actions.計畫/產生方案：設定優先順序與目標；執行/採取行動：選護理行動而非醫療行動。
  • Evaluation/evaluate outcomes: compare observed outcomes with expected; negative queries are common.評值/評值結果：比較實際與預期結果；常出現負向詢問。
  • If an abnormality exists, further assessment or action is needed; do not choose just continue monitoring or documenting.若有異常，需進一步評估或行動；不要選僅持續監測或記錄。
  • Choose options that ensure client safety.選擇確保病人安全的選項。

6Client Needs categories and strategies病人需求類別與策略

  • Physiological Integrity: Maslow, ABCs, nursing process.生理完整性：馬斯洛、ABC、護理過程。
  • Safe and Effective Care Environment: safety, handwashing, call system, side rails, asepsis, precautions, triage, emergency planning.安全有效照護環境：安全、洗手、呼叫系統、床欄、無菌、隔離措施、檢傷、緊急應變。
  • Health Promotion and Maintenance: teaching-learning theory, client readiness first; negative queries are frequent.健康促進與維護：教與學理論，先確認病人準備度；常出現負向詢問。
  • Psychosocial Integrity: therapeutic communication focused on the client's feelings; avoid 'why' questions, false reassurance and blunt replies.心理社會整合：以病人感受為主的治療性溝通；避免『為什麼』、虛假保證與生硬回應。
p.51 · book 33p.52 · book 34p.53 · book 35

7Eliminating options: alike, closed-ended words, umbrella刪去選項：相似、封閉字詞、傘狀選項

  • Comparable or alike options can usually be eliminated.相似的選項通常可刪除。
  • Closed-ended words (all, always, every, must, none, never, only) usually make an option wrong; open-ended words (may, usually, generally) are more likely right.封閉字詞（所有、總是、每個、必須、沒有、絕不、只）通常使選項錯誤；開放字詞（可能、通常、一般）較可能正確。
  • The umbrella option is broad and includes the concepts of other options; it is often correct (e.g., contact precautions includes gown and gloves).傘狀選項涵蓋其他選項概念，常為正確答案（如接觸隔離包含隔離衣與手套）。
p.53 · book 35

8Delegation, resources and pharmacology委派、資源與藥理

  • AP: noninvasive care (skin care, ROM, ambulation, hygiene). LPN/LVN: AP tasks plus focused assessments, dressings, suctioning, catheterization, oral/SC/IM medications; RN: comprehensive assessment, teaching initiation, IV medications, supervision.助理（AP）：非侵入性照護（皮膚、關節活動、行走、衛生）。LPN/LVN：AP 工作加聚焦評估、換藥、抽吸、導尿、口服/皮下/肌肉藥物；RN：完整評估、起始衛教、靜脈給藥、督導。
  • Use national standards and the Nurse Practice Act; assume ideal conditions with all equipment at the bedside (e.g., chest tube dislodged: cover the site with a sterile dressing).依全國標準與護理執業法；假設理想狀況且設備齊全（例：胸管脫出，用無菌敷料覆蓋傷口）。
  • Pharmacology: identify drug class from name stems (-lol beta blocker, -pril ACE inhibitor), know the question focus (intended, side, adverse, toxic effect).藥理：由藥名字尾辨識類別（-lol 乙型阻斷劑、-pril ACE 抑制劑），確認題目焦點（預期、副作用、不良反應、毒性）。
  • Pyramid points: no antacids with meds; do not crush enteric-coated/sustained-release; never change doses or stop meds abruptly; avoid OTC/herbals unless approved; avoid alcohol; do not give unclear or abnormal orders.重點：服藥時不併用制酸劑；腸溶/緩釋劑不可壓碎；不可自行調整或突然停藥；非經核准避免 OTC/草藥；避免酒精；醫囑不清或劑量異常不給藥。

  1. Airway is always first (ABCs); in CPR use CAB.氣道永遠第一（ABC）；CPR 用 CAB。

  2. Physiological needs come first (Maslow); if none, choose safety.生理需求優先（馬斯洛）；若無，選安全。
    p.47 · book 29p.48 · book 30
  3. Assessment is the first step of the nursing process unless the scenario is an emergency.護理過程第一步為評估，除非是緊急情況。
    p.48 · book 30
  4. Base answers only on the data given; avoid the 'What if' syndrome on traditional items.只依題目資料作答；傳統題避免『如果』症候群。
    p.43 · book 25
  5. Negative query (need for further teaching/follow-up) asks for the incorrect statement.負向詢問（需再教導/追蹤）要選不正確的陳述。
    p.47 · book 29
  6. Eliminate options with closed-ended words (always, never, only, all, must).刪除含封閉字詞（總是、絕不、只、所有、必須）的選項。
    p.53 · book 35
  7. Digoxin toxicity: GI upset, neurologic and visual changes, bradycardia; call provider if pulse < 60; keep potassium normal (3.5-5.0 mEq/L).毛地黃中毒：腸胃不適、神經與視覺改變、心搏過緩；脈搏低於 60 要通報；鉀需維持正常（3.5-5.0 mEq/L）。
    p.47 · book 29p.51 · book 33
  8. Morphine suppresses cough and respiration: priority is coughing and deep breathing (airway/breathing).嗎啡抑制咳嗽與呼吸：優先鼓勵咳嗽與深呼吸（氣道/呼吸）。
    p.48 · book 30
  9. Cast with pain on passive movement: assess for paresthesia/paralysis (compartment syndrome) first.石膏處被動活動疼痛：先評估感覺異常/麻痺（腔室症候群）。
    p.49 · book 31
  10. Nitroglycerin: unrelieved chest pain after one SL tablet, give another (up to 3 tablets 5 minutes apart) after checking blood pressure.硝化甘油：舌下一顆後胸痛未緩解，可再給（每 5 分鐘最多 3 顆），並先確認血壓。
    p.50 · book 32
  11. Early hypovolemic shock sign is increased pulse (and restlessness); increased respiratory depth comes later.低血容性休克早期徵象為脈搏加快（與不安）；呼吸加深較晚出現。
    p.46 · book 28

  13. First-dose ACE inhibitor (-pril): monitor blood pressure (first-dose hypotension).ACE 抑制劑（-pril）首劑：監測血壓（首劑低血壓）。
