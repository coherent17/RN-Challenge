Pediatric cardiovascular nursing: hyperlipidemia, heart failure (HF) and digoxin/diuretic care, congenital heart defects grouped by blood-flow effect, cardiac catheterization and surgery care, rheumatic fever, and Kawasaki disease.兒童心血管護理：高血脂、心衰竭（HF）與毛地黃/利尿劑照護、依血流影響分類的先天性心臟缺損、心導管與心臟手術護理、風濕熱與川崎病。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Hyperlipidemia in children兒童高血脂

  • Ages 2-19: total cholesterol <170 mg/dL acceptable, 170-199 borderline, >=200 high.2-19歲：總膽固醇 <170 mg/dL 為可接受，170-199 為臨界，≥200 為偏高。
  • LDL <110 acceptable, 110-129 borderline, >=130 high; HDL >45 acceptable, <40 low.LDL <110 可接受，110-129 臨界，≥130 偏高；HDL >45 可接受，<40 偏低。
  • Triglycerides: <100 mg/dL (age <=9) or <130 mg/dL (age >=10) is acceptable.三酸甘油酯：9歲以下 <100 mg/dL，10歲以上 <130 mg/dL 為可接受。
  • Childhood hyperlipidemia tends to persist and raises later cardiac event risk; any elevation warrants cardiology referral.兒童期高血脂常持續至成年並增加心臟事件風險；任何異常升高皆應轉介心臟科。
  • Treatment centers on diet, physical activity and behavior change.治療以飲食、身體活動與行為改變為主。

2Heart failure: assessment心衰竭：評估

  • HF = heart cannot meet the body's metabolic/oxygen needs; in children usually from congenital defects causing volume or pressure overload; both sides typically fail together.HF指心臟無法供應身體代謝與氧需求；兒童多因先天缺損造成容積或壓力負荷過重，通常左右心同時衰竭。
  • Early signs: tachycardia (even at rest), tachypnea, profuse scalp sweating in infants, fatigue, irritability, sudden weight gain, respiratory distress.早期徵象：休息時心跳過速、呼吸過速、嬰兒頭皮大量出汗、疲倦易怒、體重突然增加、呼吸窘迫。
  • Left-sided: crackles, cough, dyspnea, grunting, nasal flaring, retractions, orthopnea. Right-sided: hepatosplenomegaly, JVD, peripheral/periorbital edema, ascites, oliguria, weight gain.左心衰：囉音、咳嗽、呼吸困難、呻吟、鼻翼搧動、肋間凹陷。右心衰：肝脾腫大、頸靜脈怒張、周邊/眼周水腫、腹水、少尿、體重增加。

3Heart failure: nursing care and medications心衰竭：護理與藥物

  • Count apical pulse and respirations for a full minute; strict I&O (weigh diapers); daily weight, same scale/time; 0.5 kg (1 lb) gain in a day suggests fluid retention.心尖脈與呼吸需測滿1分鐘；嚴格I&O（秤尿布）；每日同一體重計、同一時間量體重，一天增加0.5 kg代表液體滯留。
  • Semi-Fowler position, cool humidified oxygen, neutral thermal environment, cluster care for rest, small frequent feedings.半坐臥、冷濕化氧氣、維持中性溫度、集中護理讓其休息、少量多餐。
  • Digoxin: hold if apical rate <90-110 (infants/young children) or <70 (older children); therapeutic level 0.8-2 ng/mL; toxicity signs are anorexia, poor feeding, N/V, bradycardia, dysrhythmias.毛地黃：心尖脈嬰幼兒 <90-110、較大兒童 <70 時暫停給藥；治療濃度0.8-2 ng/mL；中毒徵象為厭食、拒食、噁心嘔吐、心搏過緩、心律不整。
  • Furosemide causes hypokalemia (K+ <3.5), which potentiates digoxin toxicity; give potassium only if indicated and renal function is adequate.furosemide可致低血鉀（K+ <3.5），會加重毛地黃中毒；需有適應症且腎功能良好才補鉀。
  • ACE inhibitors: watch for hypotension, renal dysfunction, cough.ACE抑制劑：注意低血壓、腎功能異常、咳嗽。
  • Parents learn digoxin home care and CPR.需教導家長在家給予毛地黃及CPR。

4Defects with increased pulmonary blood flow肺血流增加之缺損

  • Left-to-right shunts (ASD, VSD, PDA, AV canal): blood moves from high-pressure left side to right; signs of HF.左向右分流（ASD、VSD、PDA、房室管缺損）：血液由高壓左側流向右側，出現HF徵象。
  • ASD: opening between atria; may be asymptomatic; closed by catheter or open repair, usually before school age.ASD：心房間開口；可無症狀；可經心導管或開心手術修補，多在學齡前完成。
  • AV canal defect: incomplete endocardial cushion fusion; most common defect in Down syndrome.房室管缺損：心內膜墊融合不全；唐氏症最常見之心臟缺損。
  • PDA: machinery-like murmur, widened pulse pressure, bounding pulses; indomethacin closes it in premature infants; prostaglandin E keeps it open in ductal-dependent lesions.PDA：機械樣雜音、脈壓增寬、脈搏洪大；indomethacin可使早產兒導管關閉；導管依賴型缺損用前列腺素E維持導管開放。
  • VSD: many small/moderate defects close spontaneously in the first year; HF common.VSD：許多小至中型缺損在第一年自行閉合；常見HF。

5Obstructive defects阻塞性缺損

  • Aortic stenosis: narrowed aortic valve; LV hypertrophy; children have activity intolerance, chest pain, dizziness; treated with balloon dilation or valvotomy.主動脈瓣狹窄：瓣膜狹窄、左心室肥大；兒童活動耐受不良、胸痛、頭暈；以氣球擴張或瓣膜切開術治療。
  • Coarctation of the aorta: BP higher in arms than legs, bounding arm pulses, weak/absent femoral pulses, cool legs; headache, epistaxis, dizziness.主動脈縮窄：上肢血壓高於下肢、上肢脈搏洪大、股動脈脈搏弱或消失、下肢冰冷；頭痛、流鼻血、頭暈。
  • Coarctation repair is outside the heart: thoracotomy, no cardiopulmonary bypass.縮窄修補位於心臟外：開胸，不需體外循環。
  • Pulmonary stenosis: right ventricular hypertrophy, decreased pulmonary flow; severe cases cyanotic in newborns; treated by dilation or valvotomy.肺動脈瓣狹窄：右心室肥大、肺血流減少；嚴重者新生兒發紺；以擴張或瓣膜切開治療。

6Defects with decreased pulmonary blood flow肺血流減少之缺損

  • Right-to-left shunts cause hypoxemia and cyanosis.右向左分流造成低血氧與發紺。
  • Tetralogy of Fallot: VSD, pulmonary stenosis, overriding aorta, RV hypertrophy.法洛氏四重症：VSD、肺動脈狹窄、主動脈騎跨、右心室肥大。
  • Tet spells occur with crying, feeding, defecating; older children squat and develop clubbing and poor growth.哭泣、餵食、排便時可出現缺氧發作（tet spell）；較大兒童會蹲踞、杵狀指、生長遲緩。
  • Management: palliative shunt in infants who cannot have primary repair; complete repair in the first year via sternotomy and bypass.處置：無法一次修補的嬰兒先做姑息性分流；第一年內以胸骨切開與體外循環完全修補。
  • Tricuspid atresia: no RA-to-RV connection; blood passes through ASD/PFO and VSD; cyanosis, clubbing.三尖瓣閉鎖：右心房與右心室無相通；血液經ASD/PFO與VSD；發紺、杵狀指。
p.524 · book 471

7Mixed defects混合性缺損

  • Hypoplastic left heart: hypoplastic LV and aortic atresia; deteriorates as the ductus closes; needs prostaglandin E1 and ventilation before surgery.左心發育不全：左心室發育不良與主動脈閉鎖；導管關閉後急速惡化；術前需前列腺素E1與呼吸器。
  • Transposition of great arteries: aorta from RV, pulmonary artery from LV; severe cyanosis if little mixing; PGE1 and balloon atrial septostomy; arterial switch repair.大動脈轉位：主動脈出自右心室、肺動脈出自左心室；混合少則重度發紺；使用PGE1與氣球心房中膈造口；動脈調換術修補。
  • Total anomalous pulmonary venous connection: pulmonary veins fail to reach LA; rapid decline with obstruction; repaired in early infancy.全肺靜脈異位連接：肺靜脈未接左心房；阻塞時快速惡化；嬰兒早期修補。
  • Truncus arteriosus: single vessel overrides both ventricles; HF and variable cyanosis; repair in first months.永存動脈幹：單一血管跨接兩心室；HF與不等程度發紺；數月內修補。
  • General defect care: monitor respiratory status, reverse Trendelenburg for work of breathing, watch for hypercyanotic spells, daily weight, high-calorie nutrition.缺損一般照護：監測呼吸、以反Trendelenburg減少呼吸功、注意缺氧發作、每日體重、高熱量營養。

8Cardiac catheterization and cardiac surgery心導管檢查與心臟手術

  • Pre-procedure: accurate height/weight, iodine allergy, signs of infection, mark and record distal pulses, baseline O2 saturation.術前：測準確身高體重、碘過敏史、感染徵象、標記並記錄遠端脈搏、基礎血氧。
  • Post-procedure: monitor 4 hours; check pulses, color and warmth below site (coolness = arterial obstruction); keep leg flat 4-6 h (venous) or 6-8 h (arterial).術後：監測4小時；檢查穿刺處以下脈搏、顏色與溫度（變冷＝動脈阻塞）；靜脈入路平躺4-6小時、動脈入路6-8小時。
  • If bleeding: apply continuous direct pressure at or just above the site and report.若出血：於穿刺處或其上方持續直接加壓並通報。
  • Cardiac surgery postop: watch temperature and sepsis, aseptic technique, pain control, rest, early parent contact.心臟手術後：監測體溫與敗血症、無菌技術、止痛、休息、盡早親子接觸。
  • Home care after surgery: avoid crowds 2 weeks, no falls risk activities 2-4 weeks, no lotions/powders on incision, no new foods for infants, no PE for 2 months, dental visits with antibiotic notice.術後居家：2週避免人群、2-4週避免易跌倒活動、傷口勿擦乳液或粉、嬰兒不加新食物、2個月不上體育課、看牙需告知心臟病史。

9Rheumatic fever風濕熱

  • Autoimmune inflammation appearing 2-6 weeks after untreated/partly treated group A beta-hemolytic strep pharyngitis; mitral valve most affected.未治療或不完全治療之A群乙型溶血性鏈球菌咽炎後2-6週發生的自體免疫發炎；最常侵犯二尖瓣。
  • Jones: major = carditis, polyarthritis, chorea, erythema marginatum, subcutaneous nodules; minor = fever, arthralgia, raised ESR/CRP, prolonged PR. Need 2 major or 1 major + 2 minor plus evidence of strep (ASO titer).Jones準則：主要＝心臟炎、多發性關節炎、舞蹈症、環形紅斑、皮下結節；次要＝發燒、關節痛、ESR/CRP升高、PR間期延長。需2主要或1主要+2次要，加上鏈球菌感染證據（ASO）。
  • Care: bed rest in acute phase, antibiotics, anti-inflammatories after diagnosis confirmed, seizure precautions for chorea, long-term antibiotic prophylaxis.照護：急性期臥床、抗生素、確診後才給抗發炎藥、舞蹈症時癲癇防護、長期抗生素預防。

10Kawasaki disease川崎病

  • Acute systemic vasculitis of unknown cause; coronary aneurysm is the major complication.原因不明的急性全身性血管炎；主要併發症為冠狀動脈瘤。
  • Acute: fever, conjunctival hyperemia, cracked red lips/strawberry tongue, swollen hands/feet with red palms/soles, rash, cervical lymphadenopathy, irritability.急性期：發燒、結膜充血、嘴唇乾裂紅腫/草莓舌、手足腫脹與掌蹠紅斑、皮疹、頸部淋巴結腫大、易怒。
  • Subacute: peeling fingertips/toes, joint pain, thrombocytosis. Convalescent: looks normal, labs still abnormal.亞急性期：指趾脫皮、關節痛、血小板增多。恢復期：外觀正常，檢驗仍異常。
  • Treatment: high-dose IVIG (blood product) plus aspirin; delay MMR/varicella vaccines 11 months after IVIG; no aspirin if exposed to chickenpox or flu.治療：高劑量IVIG（血液製品）加阿斯匹靈；IVIG後11個月內延後MMR/水痘疫苗；接觸水痘或流感時避免阿斯匹靈。

  • Transposition of great arteries: aorta from RV, pulmonary artery from LV; severe cyanosis if little mixing; PGE1 and balloon atrial septostomy; arterial switch repair.大動脈轉位：主動脈出自右心室、肺動脈出自左心室；混合少則重度發紺；使用PGE1與氣球心房中膈造口；動脈調換術修補。
  • Total anomalous pulmonary venous connection: pulmonary veins fail to reach LA; rapid decline with obstruction; repaired in early infancy.全肺靜脈異位連接：肺靜脈未接左心房；阻塞時快速惡化；嬰兒早期修補。
  • Truncus arteriosus: single vessel overrides both ventricles; HF and variable cyanosis; repair in first months.永存動脈幹：單一血管跨接兩心室；HF與不等程度發紺；數月內修補。
  • General defect care: monitor respiratory status, reverse Trendelenburg for work of breathing, watch for hypercyanotic spells, daily weight, high-calorie nutrition.缺損一般照護：監測呼吸、以反Trendelenburg減少呼吸功、注意缺氧發作、每日體重、高熱量營養。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Cardiac catheterization and cardiac surgery心導管檢查與心臟手術
  • Pre-procedure: accurate height/weight, iodine allergy, signs of infection, mark and record distal pulses, baseline O2 saturation.術前：測準確身高體重、碘過敏史、感染徵象、標記並記錄遠端脈搏、基礎血氧。
  • Post-procedure: monitor 4 hours; check pulses, color and warmth below site (coolness = arterial obstruction); keep leg flat 4-6 h (venous) or 6-8 h (arterial).術後：監測4小時；檢查穿刺處以下脈搏、顏色與溫度（變冷＝動脈阻塞）；靜脈入路平躺4-6小時、動脈入路6-8小時。
  • If bleeding: apply continuous direct pressure at or just above the site and report.若出血：於穿刺處或其上方持續直接加壓並通報。
  • Cardiac surgery postop: watch temperature and sepsis, aseptic technique, pain control, rest, early parent contact.心臟手術後：監測體溫與敗血症、無菌技術、止痛、休息、盡早親子接觸。
  • Home care after surgery: avoid crowds 2 weeks, no falls risk activities 2-4 weeks, no lotions/powders on incision, no new foods for infants, no PE for 2 months, dental visits with antibiotic notice.術後居家：2週避免人群、2-4週避免易跌倒活動、傷口勿擦乳液或粉、嬰兒不加新食物、2個月不上體育課、看牙需告知心臟病史。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
Rheumatic fever風濕熱
  • Autoimmune inflammation appearing 2-6 weeks after untreated/partly treated group A beta-hemolytic strep pharyngitis; mitral valve most affected.未治療或不完全治療之A群乙型溶血性鏈球菌咽炎後2-6週發生的自體免疫發炎；最常侵犯二尖瓣。
  • Jones: major = carditis, polyarthritis, chorea, erythema marginatum, subcutaneous nodules; minor = fever, arthralgia, raised ESR/CRP, prolonged PR. Need 2 major or 1 major + 2 minor plus evidence of strep (ASO titer).Jones準則：主要＝心臟炎、多發性關節炎、舞蹈症、環形紅斑、皮下結節；次要＝發燒、關節痛、ESR/CRP升高、PR間期延長。需2主要或1主要+2次要，加上鏈球菌感染證據（ASO）。
  • Care: bed rest in acute phase, antibiotics, anti-inflammatories after diagnosis confirmed, seizure precautions for chorea, long-term antibiotic prophylaxis.照護：急性期臥床、抗生素、確診後才給抗發炎藥、舞蹈症時癲癇防護、長期抗生素預防。
Topic 10 · tap to flip主題 10 · 點擊翻面
Kawasaki disease川崎病
  • Acute systemic vasculitis of unknown cause; coronary aneurysm is the major complication.原因不明的急性全身性血管炎；主要併發症為冠狀動脈瘤。
  • Acute: fever, conjunctival hyperemia, cracked red lips/strawberry tongue, swollen hands/feet with red palms/soles, rash, cervical lymphadenopathy, irritability.急性期：發燒、結膜充血、嘴唇乾裂紅腫/草莓舌、手足腫脹與掌蹠紅斑、皮疹、頸部淋巴結腫大、易怒。
  • Subacute: peeling fingertips/toes, joint pain, thrombocytosis. Convalescent: looks normal, labs still abnormal.亞急性期：指趾脫皮、關節痛、血小板增多。恢復期：外觀正常，檢驗仍異常。
  • Treatment: high-dose IVIG (blood product) plus aspirin; delay MMR/varicella vaccines 11 months after IVIG; no aspirin if exposed to chickenpox or flu.治療：高劑量IVIG（血液製品）加阿斯匹靈；IVIG後11個月內延後MMR/水痘疫苗；接觸水痘或流感時避免阿斯匹靈。

Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Early HF signs in infants: tachycardia, tachypnea, scalp sweating, fatigue, sudden weight gain.嬰兒HF早期徵象：心跳過速、呼吸過速、頭皮出汗、疲倦、體重突增。
    p.520 · book 467
  2. Hold digoxin if apical pulse <90-110 (infant/young child) or <70 (older child); therapeutic 0.8-2 ng/mL.心尖脈嬰幼兒 <90-110、較大兒童 <70 時暫停毛地黃；治療濃度0.8-2 ng/mL。
    p.521 · book 468
  3. Hypokalemia from furosemide increases digoxin toxicity risk.furosemide造成的低血鉀增加毛地黃中毒風險。
    p.521 · book 468
  4. Do not repeat a digoxin dose if the child vomits; do not double missed doses; give 1 h before or 2 h after feeds; never mix in food.嘔吐後勿補給毛地黃；漏服勿加倍；餵食前1小時或後2小時給藥；勿混入食物。
    p.522 · book 469
  5. Weigh diapers to measure infant urine output accurately.以秤尿布方式準確測量嬰兒尿量。
    p.520 · book 467p.521 · book 468
  6. Tet spell: calm infant, knee-chest position, 100% O2, morphine, IV fluids, notify provider.缺氧發作：安撫、膝胸臥位、100%氧氣、嗎啡、靜脈輸液、通知醫師。
    p.524 · book 471
  7. Coarctation: higher BP in arms than legs, weak femoral pulses.主動脈縮窄：上肢血壓高於下肢、股動脈脈搏弱。
    p.523 · book 470
  8. PDA: machinery murmur, bounding pulses, wide pulse pressure.PDA：機械樣雜音、脈搏洪大、脈壓增寬。
    p.523 · book 470
  9. AV canal defect is the most common cardiac defect in Down syndrome.房室管缺損為唐氏症最常見心臟缺損。
    p.522 · book 469
  10. Ductal-dependent lesions (hypoplastic left heart, TGA) need prostaglandin E1 to keep the ductus open.導管依賴型缺損（左心發育不全、大動脈轉位）需前列腺素E1維持導管開放。
    p.525 · book 472
  11. After cardiac catheterization: coolness or absent pulses distal to the site = arterial obstruction; bleeding = direct pressure and call.心導管後：穿刺遠端冰冷或脈搏消失＝動脈阻塞；出血＝直接加壓並通報。
    p.526 · book 473
  12. After cardiac surgery: no lotions/powders on incision, avoid crowds 2 weeks.心臟手術後：傷口不擦乳液/粉、2週避免人群。
    p.527 · book 474
  13. Rheumatic fever: ask about sore throat/fever in prior 2 months; ASO titer confirms strep; Jones criteria; do not give salicylates before diagnosis (masks arthritis).風濕熱：詢問前2個月咽痛/發燒史；ASO確認鏈球菌；Jones準則；確診前不給水楊酸（會掩蓋關節炎）。
    p.527 · book 474
  14. Kawasaki acute stage: conjunctival hyperemia, strawberry tongue, swollen red hands/feet; desquamation comes later (subacute).川崎病急性期：結膜充血、草莓舌、手足紅腫；脫皮出現較晚（亞急性）。
    p.528 · book 475
  15. Kawasaki: IVIG + aspirin; delay MMR/varicella 11 months after IVIG.川崎病：IVIG+阿斯匹靈；IVIG後延後MMR/水痘疫苗11個月。
    p.528 · book 475
