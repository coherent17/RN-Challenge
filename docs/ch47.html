1Endocrine anatomy and physiology內分泌解剖與生理

  • Endocrine glands: hypothalamus, pituitary, thyroid, parathyroids, adrenals, pancreas, ovaries, testes; they regulate metabolism, stress response, growth, reproduction, fluid and electrolyte balance.內分泌腺體：下視丘、腦下垂體、甲狀腺、副甲狀腺、腎上腺、胰臟、卵巢、睪丸；調控代謝、壓力反應、生長、生殖與水電解質平衡。
  • Pituitary is the master gland: anterior lobe makes ACTH, TSH, FSH, LH, GH, prolactin, MSH; posterior lobe stores oxytocin and ADH (vasopressin) made by the hypothalamus.腦下垂體為主控腺體：前葉分泌ACTH、TSH、FSH、LH、GH、泌乳素、MSH；後葉儲存下視丘製造的oxytocin與ADH（vasopressin）。
  • Adrenal cortex: glucocorticoids (cortisol) and mineralocorticoids (aldosterone: sodium retention, potassium excretion); medulla: epinephrine and norepinephrine.腎上腺皮質：糖皮質素（cortisol）與礦物皮質素（aldosterone：留鈉排鉀）；髓質：epinephrine與norepinephrine。
  • Thyroid makes T3, T4 and calcitonin; parathyroid makes PTH (calcium and phosphorus); pancreas makes insulin and glucagon.甲狀腺分泌T3、T4與降鈣素；副甲狀腺分泌PTH（鈣磷代謝）；胰臟分泌胰島素與升糖素。
  • Negative feedback: rising target-gland hormone suppresses releasing hormones.負回饋：標的腺體荷爾蒙上升會抑制其釋放荷爾蒙。

2Diagnostic tests診斷檢查

  • Stimulation tests check for hypofunction (hormone fails to rise); suppression tests check for hyperfunction (hormone fails to fall). Overnight dexamethasone suppression helps identify Cushing's.刺激試驗檢查功能低下（荷爾蒙未上升）；抑制試驗檢查功能亢進（荷爾蒙未下降）。夜間dexamethasone抑制試驗用於辨別庫欣氏症。
  • Radioactive iodine uptake: about 3-19% at 2-4 hours, 11-30% at 24 hours; high in hyperthyroidism; contraindicated in pregnancy.放射性碘攝取：2-4小時約3-19%、24小時約11-30%；甲亢時升高；懷孕禁用。
  • Total T4 5-12 mcg/dL; free T4 0.8-2.8 ng/dL; TSH 0.5-5.0 mIU/L: high TSH = primary hypothyroidism; low TSH = hyperthyroidism or secondary hypothyroidism.總T4 5-12 mcg/dL；游離T4 0.8-2.8 ng/dL；TSH 0.5-5.0 mIU/L：TSH高=原發性甲低；TSH低=甲亢或次發性甲低。
  • Thyroid scan: avoid iodine-containing meds and contrast; NPO after midnight; contraindicated in pregnancy; fine needle aspiration for cytology.甲狀腺掃描：避免含碘藥物與顯影劑；午夜後禁食；懷孕禁用；細針抽吸做細胞學檢查。
  • HbA1c reflects glucose control over about 90 days, no fasting needed, normal under 6% in adults without diabetes.HbA1c反映約90天血糖控制，無需空腹，無糖尿病成人正常小於6%。
  • 24-hour urine VMA, metanephrines and catecholamines for pheochromocytoma.24小時尿液VMA、甲氧基腎上腺素與兒茶酚胺用於診斷嗜鉻細胞瘤。

3Pituitary problems腦下垂體疾病

  • Hypopituitarism: low GH, gonadotropins, TSH, ACTH or ADH from tumor, trauma, stroke; fatigue, low BP, infertility, headache and visual defects with tumors; replace deficient hormones.腦下垂體功能低下：腫瘤、外傷、中風造成GH、性腺激素、TSH、ACTH或ADH不足；疲倦、低血壓、不孕，腫瘤可致頭痛與視野缺損；補充缺乏荷爾蒙。
  • Acromegaly (excess GH in adults): large hands/feet, jaw protrusion, joint pain, oily skin, sweating, hypertension, heart disease, sleep apnea, hyperglycemia, colon polyps.肢端肥大症（成人GH過多）：手腳增大、下顎突出、關節痛、油性皮膚、多汗、高血壓、心臟病、睡眠呼吸中止、高血糖、大腸息肉。
  • Hypophysectomy: transsphenoidal approach preferred; complications CSF leak, infection, diabetes insipidus, hypopituitarism.腦下垂體切除：偏好經蝶竇途徑；併發症為腦脊髓液漏、感染、尿崩症、腦下垂體功能低下。
  • Postop: HOB elevated, avoid sneezing, coughing, blowing nose, straining; no toothbrush early; report clear nasal drainage (test for glucose), monitor urine output; lifelong ADH, cortisol, thyroid replacement if entire gland removed.術後：抬高床頭，避免打噴嚏、咳嗽、擤鼻、用力；早期不刷牙；通報清澈鼻液（檢測葡萄糖）、監測尿量；全切除需終身補充ADH、cortisol、甲狀腺素。

4Diabetes insipidus and SIADH尿崩症與SIADH

  • Diabetes insipidus: too little ADH (central) or kidney non-response (nephrogenic); large volumes of dilute urine, polydipsia, dehydration, low specific gravity, hypotension, tachycardia.尿崩症：ADH不足（中樞性）或腎臟無反應（腎因性）；大量稀釋尿、多渴、脫水、尿比重低、低血壓、心搏過速。
  • DI care: replace fluids (hypotonic IV), strict I&O, weight, serum osmolality, desmopressin or vasopressin (watch for water intoxication), medical alert bracelet.尿崩症照護：補充水分（低張靜脈輸液）、嚴格記錄出入量、體重、血清滲透壓、desmopressin或vasopressin（注意水中毒）、佩戴醫療警示手環。
  • SIADH: excess ADH causes water retention, dilutional hyponatremia, concentrated scant urine, weight gain without edema, confusion, seizures.SIADH：ADH過多造成水滯留、稀釋性低血鈉、尿少濃縮、無水腫體重增加、意識混亂、抽搐。
  • SIADH care: fluid restriction (800-1000 mL/day if chronic), seizure precautions, HOB no more than 10 degrees, daily weight, hypertonic or normal saline, loop diuretic only if sodium at least 125 mEq/L, vasopressin antagonists.SIADH照護：限水（慢性800-1000 mL/日）、抽搐防護、床頭最高抬高10度、每日體重、高張或生理食鹽水、鈉至少125 mEq/L才用袢利尿劑、vasopressin拮抗劑。

5Adrenal problems: Addison's, Cushing's, Conn's腎上腺疾病：艾迪森氏、庫欣氏、康氏症候群

  • Addison's disease: cortical hypofunction (autoimmune): weakness, weight loss, hypotension, hypoglycemia, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, bronze hyperpigmentation; lifelong glucocorticoid (and often mineralocorticoid) replacement.艾迪森氏病：腎上腺皮質功能低下（自體免疫）：無力、體重減輕、低血壓、低血糖、低血鈉、高血鉀、皮膚古銅色；需終身糖皮質素（常加礦物皮質素）補充。
  • Addisonian crisis: triggered by stress, infection, trauma, surgery, abrupt steroid stop; severe headache, abdominal/back pain, confusion, severe hypotension, shock; give IV hydrocortisone and fluids, bed rest, monitor Na, K, glucose.艾迪森氏危象：由壓力、感染、外傷、手術、驟停類固醇誘發；劇烈頭痛、腹痛背痛、意識混亂、嚴重低血壓、休克；給予靜脈hydrocortisone與輸液、臥床休息、監測鈉鉀血糖。
  • Cushing's: excess cortisol; moon face, buffalo hump, truncal obesity with thin limbs, striae, bruising, hypertension, hyperglycemia, hypernatremia, hypokalemia, infection risk. Cushing's disease = pituitary ACTH source; syndrome includes exogenous steroids.庫欣氏：皮質醇過多；月亮臉、水牛肩、軀幹肥胖四肢細、紫紋、易瘀青、高血壓、高血糖、高血鈉、低血鉀、易感染。庫欣氏病=腦下垂體ACTH來源；症候群含外源性類固醇。
  • Cushing's care: monitor BP, glucose, I&O, weight; protect from infection and thrombi; hypophysectomy or adrenalectomy; stress-dose steroids after surgery.庫欣氏照護：監測血壓、血糖、出入量、體重；預防感染與血栓；腦下垂體或腎上腺切除；術後壓力情況增加類固醇。
  • Conn's (primary hyperaldosteronism): sodium and water retention, potassium loss: hypertension, hypokalemia, alkalosis, weakness, dysrhythmias, tetany; give potassium-sparing diuretics/aldosterone antagonists; adrenalectomy.康氏症（原發性醛固酮過多）：留鈉水、失鉀：高血壓、低血鉀、鹼中毒、無力、心律不整、手足抽搐；給保鉀利尿劑/醛固酮拮抗劑；腎上腺切除。

6Pheochromocytoma and adrenalectomy嗜鉻細胞瘤與腎上腺切除

  • Catecholamine-secreting adrenal medulla tumor: paroxysmal hypertension, severe headache, palpitations, diaphoresis, flushing, tremor, hyperglycemia.分泌兒茶酚胺的腎上腺髓質腫瘤：陣發性高血壓、劇烈頭痛、心悸、盜汗、臉潮紅、顫抖、高血糖。
  • Avoid triggers: abdominal palpation or pressure, smoking, caffeine, sudden position changes; quiet environment; alpha-blockers started 7-10 days before beta-blockers.避免誘因：腹部觸診或壓迫、吸菸、咖啡因、突然變換姿勢；安靜環境；alpha阻斷劑須比beta阻斷劑早7-10天開始。
  • Hypertensive crisis: semi-Fowler's, notify PHCP, oxygen, IV access with slow saline, IV antihypertensives.高血壓危象：半坐臥、通知醫師、給氧、緩慢輸注生理食鹽水保持靜脈通路、靜脈降壓藥。
  • Adrenalectomy: bilateral = lifelong glucocorticoid and mineralocorticoid; unilateral = temporary replacement; watch hemorrhage, shock, hypotension, urine output under 30 mL/hr, paralytic ileus, adrenal insufficiency.腎上腺切除：雙側=終身糖皮質素與礦物皮質素補充；單側=暫時補充；注意出血、休克、低血壓、尿量低於30 mL/小時、麻痺性腸阻塞、腎上腺功能不足。

7Thyroid problems甲狀腺疾病

  • Hypothyroidism: low T4, high TSH (primary): fatigue, cold intolerance, weight gain, bradycardia, constipation, dry skin, myxedema; levothyroxine; avoid sedatives and opioids; warmth; watch overdose (tachycardia, chest pain, nervousness).甲狀腺功能低下：T4低、TSH高（原發性）：疲倦、怕冷、體重增加、心搏過緩、便祕、皮膚乾燥、黏液水腫；levothyroxine；避免鎮靜劑與鴉片類；保暖；注意過量（心搏過速、胸痛、緊張）。
  • Myxedema coma: hypotension, bradycardia, hypothermia, hyponatremia, hypoglycemia, respiratory failure; secure airway, IV levothyroxine, IV fluids and glucose, corticosteroids, warm gently.黏液水腫昏迷：低血壓、心搏過緩、體溫過低、低血鈉、低血糖、呼吸衰竭；確保氣道、靜脈levothyroxine、輸液與葡萄糖、皮質類固醇、緩慢保暖。
  • Hyperthyroidism (Graves' disease): T3/T4 high, TSH low; nervousness, tremor, heat intolerance, weight loss, diarrhea, tachycardia, exophthalmos, goiter.甲狀腺功能亢進（葛瑞夫茲氏病）：T3/T4高、TSH低；緊張、顫抖、怕熱、體重減輕、腹瀉、心搏過速、突眼、甲狀腺腫。
  • Treatment: methimazole or propylthiouracil, iodides, propranolol, radioactive iodine, subtotal thyroidectomy; cool quiet environment, high-calorie diet; exophthalmos: HOB up, artificial tears, dark glasses, tape lids at night.治療：methimazole或propylthiouracil、碘劑、propranolol、放射性碘、甲狀腺次全切除；涼爽安靜環境、高熱量飲食；突眼：抬高床頭、人工淚液、深色眼鏡、夜間貼合眼瞼。
  • Thyroid storm: fever, tachycardia, systolic hypertension, agitation, delirium; give antithyroid drugs, iodides, propranolol, glucocorticoids, cooling blanket, acetaminophen (not aspirin).甲狀腺風暴：發燒、心搏過速、收縮壓高、躁動、譫妄；給抗甲狀腺藥、碘劑、propranolol、糖皮質素、降溫毯、acetaminophen（避免阿斯匹靈）。
  • Thyroidectomy postop: semi-Fowler's, support neck, tracheostomy set and suction at bedside, monitor hoarseness, laryngeal nerve damage, bleeding behind the neck, hypocalcemia with tetany (Chvostek, Trousseau), calcium gluconate ready.甲狀腺切除術後：半坐臥、支托頸部、床邊備氣切包與抽吸、監測聲音沙啞、喉返神經損傷、頸後出血、低血鈣手足抽搐（Chvostek、Trousseau徵象）、備calcium gluconate。

8Parathyroid problems副甲狀腺疾病

  • Hypoparathyroidism (often after thyroidectomy): hypocalcemia, hyperphosphatemia, tingling, cramps, positive Chvostek/Trousseau, laryngospasm, seizures.副甲狀腺功能低下（常見於甲狀腺切除後）：低血鈣、高血磷、麻刺感、抽筋、Chvostek/Trousseau陽性、喉痙攣、抽搐。
  • Care: IV calcium gluconate, seizure precautions, tracheostomy set, high-calcium low-phosphorus diet, calcium and vitamin D, phosphate binders.照護：靜脈calcium gluconate、抽搐防護、備氣切包、高鈣低磷飲食、鈣與維生素D、磷結合劑。
  • Hyperparathyroidism: hypercalcemia, low phosphorus, bone pain and fractures, renal stones, constipation, weakness, dysrhythmias.副甲狀腺功能亢進：高血鈣、低血磷、骨痛與骨折、腎結石、便祕、無力、心律不整。
  • Care: fluids, furosemide, IV saline, calcitonin, bisphosphonates, move gently, parathyroidectomy; postop watch hypocalcemic crisis, hoarseness.照護：補水、furosemide、靜脈生理食鹽水、降鈣素、雙磷酸鹽、輕柔移動、副甲狀腺切除；術後注意低血鈣危象與聲音沙啞。

9Diabetes mellitus overview, diet, exercise糖尿病概論、飲食與運動

  • Type 1: nearly absolute insulin deficiency from beta cell destruction (ketosis-prone); type 2: insulin resistance or relative deficiency. Metabolic syndrome: abdominal obesity, hyperglycemia, hypertension, high triglycerides, low HDL.第1型：胰島β細胞破壞造成幾近絕對胰島素缺乏（易酮症）；第2型：胰島素阻抗或相對不足。代謝症候群：腹部肥胖、高血糖、高血壓、高三酸甘油酯、低HDL。
  • Classic signs: polyuria, polydipsia, polyphagia, blurred vision, slow healing, infections; weight loss common in type 1.典型症狀：多尿、多渴、多食、視力模糊、傷口癒合慢、感染；體重減輕多見於第1型。
  • Diet: consistent timing and amounts, carbohydrate counting for intensive insulin therapy.飲食：進食時間與份量一致，強化胰島素治療可用碳水化合物計算。
  • Exercise lowers glucose; check glucose before, during, after; do not exercise if glucose above 250 mg/dL with ketones (type 1); avoid injecting insulin into the limb about to be exercised.運動降血糖；運動前中後監測血糖；血糖大於250 mg/dL且有酮體（第1型）時不運動；避免將胰島素注射於即將運動的肢體。
  • Urine glucose is not reliable for monitoring; test urine ketones during illness or glucose over 250 mg/dL on two checks in type 1.尿糖不可靠，不用於監測；生病或第1型連續兩次血糖大於250 mg/dL時檢測尿酮。

10Insulin therapy and phenomena胰島素治療與現象

  • Insulin treats type 1 and some type 2; never withhold during illness or stress. Know peak times for hypoglycemia risk. Only regular insulin (and rapid-acting) may be given IV.胰島素用於第1型及部分第2型；生病或壓力時不可停用。需了解高峰時間以預防低血糖。僅短效（regular）及速效胰島素可靜脈給予。
  • Local allergy at the site; lipodystrophy from repeated site use: rotate within one anatomical area, sites 1.5 inches apart, not the same site for 2-3 weeks.局部過敏；重複注射同一處造成脂肪營養不良：在同一解剖區輪替，間隔1.5吋，2-3週內不重複同一處。
  • Dawn phenomenon: morning hyperglycemia from GH and cortisol; adjust insulin dose or timing.黎明現象：因生長激素與皮質醇造成晨間高血糖；調整胰島素劑量或時間。
  • Somogyi effect: nighttime hypoglycemia (2-3 a.m.) triggers counterregulatory hormones and morning hyperglycemia; night sweats, headaches, nightmares; decrease insulin or add bedtime snack.蘇木吉效應：夜間2-3點低血糖引發反調節荷爾蒙造成晨間高血糖；盜汗、頭痛、惡夢；減少胰島素或睡前加點心。
  • Insulin pump: continuous basal rate plus meal bolus; rapid or regular insulin; change needle/catheter every 2-3 days; skin sensor monitors glucose. Pancreas transplant is done in limited clients, often with kidney.胰島素幫浦：持續基礎量加餐前大量；速效或短效胰島素；每2-3天更換針頭/導管；皮膚感測器監測血糖。胰臟移植用於少數病人，常與腎臟移植同時。

11Hypoglycemia, DKA and HHS低血糖、酮酸中毒與高滲透壓高血糖狀態

  • Hypoglycemia: glucose under 70 mg/dL; mild (sweating, tremor, tachycardia, hunger), moderate (confusion, slurred speech, irritability), severe (unarousable, seizures). Beta-blockers, older age and frequent episodes cause hypoglycemia unawareness.低血糖：血糖低於70 mg/dL；輕度（出汗、顫抖、心搏過速、飢餓）、中度（混亂、口齒不清、易怒）、重度（無法喚醒、抽搐）。Beta阻斷劑、高齡與頻繁發作可造成低血糖無感。
  • 15/15 rule: give 15 g fast carbohydrate (1/2 cup juice, glucose gel), recheck in 15 minutes, repeat; if altered consciousness give glucagon IM/SC or 25-50 mL of 50% dextrose IV; follow with a complex carbohydrate and protein snack. Never give oral food to a semiconscious client.15/15原則：給15 g速效碳水化合物（1/2杯果汁、葡萄糖膠），15分鐘後複測並重複；意識改變時給glucagon肌注/皮下或靜脈50%葡萄糖25-50 mL；之後給複合碳水加蛋白質點心。意識不清者不可口服進食。

Insulin therapy and phenomena胰島素治療與現象
  • Insulin treats type 1 and some type 2; never withhold during illness or stress. Know peak times for hypoglycemia risk. Only regular insulin (and rapid-acting) may be given IV.胰島素用於第1型及部分第2型；生病或壓力時不可停用。需了解高峰時間以預防低血糖。僅短效（regular）及速效胰島素可靜脈給予。
  • Local allergy at the site; lipodystrophy from repeated site use: rotate within one anatomical area, sites 1.5 inches apart, not the same site for 2-3 weeks.局部過敏；重複注射同一處造成脂肪營養不良：在同一解剖區輪替，間隔1.5吋，2-3週內不重複同一處。
  • Dawn phenomenon: morning hyperglycemia from GH and cortisol; adjust insulin dose or timing.黎明現象：因生長激素與皮質醇造成晨間高血糖；調整胰島素劑量或時間。
  • Somogyi effect: nighttime hypoglycemia (2-3 a.m.) triggers counterregulatory hormones and morning hyperglycemia; night sweats, headaches, nightmares; decrease insulin or add bedtime snack.蘇木吉效應：夜間2-3點低血糖引發反調節荷爾蒙造成晨間高血糖；盜汗、頭痛、惡夢；減少胰島素或睡前加點心。
  • Insulin pump: continuous basal rate plus meal bolus; rapid or regular insulin; change needle/catheter every 2-3 days; skin sensor monitors glucose. Pancreas transplant is done in limited clients, often with kidney.胰島素幫浦：持續基礎量加餐前大量；速效或短效胰島素；每2-3天更換針頭/導管；皮膚感測器監測血糖。胰臟移植用於少數病人，常與腎臟移植同時。

Hypoglycemia, DKA and HHS低血糖、酮酸中毒與高滲透壓高血糖狀態
  • Hypoglycemia: glucose under 70 mg/dL; mild (sweating, tremor, tachycardia, hunger), moderate (confusion, slurred speech, irritability), severe (unarousable, seizures). Beta-blockers, older age and frequent episodes cause hypoglycemia unawareness.低血糖：血糖低於70 mg/dL；輕度（出汗、顫抖、心搏過速、飢餓）、中度（混亂、口齒不清、易怒）、重度（無法喚醒、抽搐）。Beta阻斷劑、高齡與頻繁發作可造成低血糖無感。
  • 15/15 rule: give 15 g fast carbohydrate (1/2 cup juice, glucose gel), recheck in 15 minutes, repeat; if altered consciousness give glucagon IM/SC or 25-50 mL of 50% dextrose IV; follow with a complex carbohydrate and protein snack. Never give oral food to a semiconscious client.15/15原則：給15 g速效碳水化合物（1/2杯果汁、葡萄糖膠），15分鐘後複測並重複；意識改變時給glucagon肌注/皮下或靜脈50%葡萄糖25-50 mL；之後給複合碳水加蛋白質點心。意識不清者不可口服進食。
  • DKA (type 1): hyperglycemia, ketosis, acidosis, dehydration; Kussmaul respirations, fruity breath, abdominal pain; glucose over 300, pH under 7.35, HCO3 under 15, ketones positive; sudden onset.DKA（第1型）：高血糖、酮症、酸中毒、脫水；Kussmaul呼吸、水果味呼氣、腹痛；血糖大於300、pH低於7.35、HCO3低於15、酮體陽性；起病急。
  • DKA treatment: fluids first (0.9% or 0.45% saline), then IV regular insulin by pump, replace potassium once urine output adequate (K falls quickly), add dextrose when glucose 250-300 mg/dL, cardiac monitoring, watch for cerebral edema if glucose drops too fast.DKA治療：先補液（0.9%或0.45%食鹽水），再以幫浦靜脈給regular insulin，尿量足夠後補鉀（鉀快速下降），血糖降至250-300 mg/dL時加葡萄糖，心電圖監測，血糖降太快注意腦水腫。
  • HHS (type 2, older): glucose over 800 mg/dL, osmolarity over 350, no ketosis or acidosis, gradual onset, severe dehydration and neurological changes; treat with careful fluids (heart failure risk), electrolytes, insulin.HHS（第2型、老年）：血糖大於800 mg/dL、滲透壓大於350、無酮症或酸中毒、逐漸發作、嚴重脫水與神經症狀；謹慎補液（心衰風險）、電解質、胰島素。
  • Sick-day rules: continue insulin, check glucose and ketones every 3-4 hours, sip fluids every 30-60 minutes, call PHCP if glucose above 250-300, ketonuria over 24 hours, unable to eat or drink for 4 hours.生病期間守則：持續使用胰島素、每3-4小時測血糖與尿酮、每30-60分鐘小口喝水、血糖大於250-300、酮尿超過24小時、4小時無法進食飲水須通知醫師。
