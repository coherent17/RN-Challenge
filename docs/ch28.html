This chapter covers immediate newborn care and Apgar scoring, the head-to-toe newborn exam, body system adaptations (thermoregulation, jaundice, vitamin K, cord and circumcision care), reflexes, safety and parent teaching, and common neonatal problems: preterm/postterm, SGA/LGA, respiratory conditions, NEC, hyperbilirubinemia, Rh disease, sepsis, infections, substance exposure, maternal diabetes, hypoglycemia, infant choking and CPR.本章涵蓋新生兒立即照護與Apgar評分、新生兒全身評估、各系統適應（體溫調節、黃疸、維生素K、臍帶與包皮環切照護）、反射、安全與父母衛教，以及常見新生兒問題：早產／過期產、SGA/LGA、呼吸問題、壞死性腸炎、高膽紅素血症、Rh疾病、敗血症、感染、物質暴露、母親糖尿病、低血糖、嬰兒哽塞與CPR。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Initial care and Apgar scoring初期照護與Apgar評分

  • Dry and stimulate; suction mouth first then nose; skin-to-skin, warm blankets, hat, radiant warmer; wear gloves until the first bath; matching ID bracelets, footprints.擦乾並刺激；先吸口再吸鼻；肌膚接觸、保暖毯、帽子、輻射保暖台；初次沐浴前戴手套；配戴相符手環、採足印。
  • Apgar scored at 1 and 5 minutes on heart rate, respiratory effort, muscle tone, reflex irritability, color (0-2 each). 8-10 support only; 4-7 stimulate, oxygen, rescore; 0-3 full resuscitation.於1及5分鐘評估心率、呼吸、肌張力、反射、膚色（各0至2分）。8至10分僅需支持；4至7分刺激、給氧、再評分；0至3分需完整復甦。
  • Transition period (first 6-8 hours): first reactivity, decreased responsiveness, second reactivity.轉換期（前6至8小時）：第一反應期、反應遲鈍期、第二反應期。

2Vital signs, measurements and head/face exam生命徵象、測量與頭面部評估

  • HR 110-160 (apical, 1 full min; 90-110 asleep, up to 180 crying); RR 30-60; axillary temp 97.7-<99.5 F (36.5-<37.5 C); BP 80-90/40-50 only if cardiac concern.心率110至160次/分（心尖脈量1分鐘；睡眠90至110、哭泣可達180）；呼吸30至60次/分；腋溫36.5至未滿37.5°C（97.7至<99.5°F）；僅疑心臟問題時測血壓80至90/40至50。
  • Length 18-22 in (45-55 cm), weight 2500-4000 g, head circumference 33-35 cm.身長45至55公分、體重2500至4000公克、頭圍33至35公分。
  • Molding resolves in about 72 hr. Caput succedaneum crosses suture lines, resolves in days; cephalhematoma does not cross sutures, resolves in weeks (up to 6).產瘤變形約72小時消退。產瘤跨越縫合線，數日消退；頭血腫不跨縫合線，需數週（至6週）吸收。
  • Anterior fontanel closes 12-18 months; posterior by 2-3 months. Eyes cross occasionally, red reflex present. Low-set ears suggest syndromes. Nose breathers; flaring means distress. Epstein pearls normal; thrush does not wipe off.前囟門12至18個月閉合；後囟門2至3個月內閉合。眼睛偶爾斜視、有紅色反射。耳位低提示症候群。新生兒用鼻呼吸；鼻翼搧動代表窘迫。上顎珍珠腫正常；鵝口瘡擦不掉。

3Skin, abdomen, genitals, spine, extremities皮膚、腹部、生殖器、脊柱與四肢

  • Acrocyanosis normal early; central cyanosis is not. Vernix, lanugo, milia, Mongolian spots, stork bites normal. Harlequin sign may point to cardiac shunting or sepsis.初期手腳發紺屬正常；中心性發紺不正常。胎脂、胎毛、粟粒疹、蒙古斑、鸛咬痕屬正常。小丑症狀可能代表心臟分流或敗血症。
  • Cord has 2 arteries and 1 vein; a 2-vessel cord is linked to IUGR and anomalies. Cord clamp stays at least 24 hr; first meconium within 24 hr; first void within 24 hr.臍帶有2條動脈1條靜脈；兩條血管者與子宮內生長遲滯及異常有關。臍夾至少保留24小時；24小時內排胎便與首次排尿。
  • Port-wine stain stays; strawberry mark fades by 7-9 yr; stork bites fade by 2 yr; Mongolian spots fade in 1-2 yr.葡萄酒斑不會消退；草莓狀血管瘤7至9歲消退；鸛咬痕2歲前消退；蒙古斑1至2年內消退。
  • Check clavicle fractures, hip dysplasia (Ortolani/Barlow), spinal dimples or hair tufts, symmetric movements. Jittery tremors may signal hypoglycemia, hypocalcemia, or drug withdrawal.檢查鎖骨骨折、髖關節發育不良（Ortolani／Barlow）、脊柱凹陷或毛髮叢、動作對稱性。顫抖可能代表低血糖、低血鈣或藥物戒斷。

4Body systems: respiratory, hepatic, renal, immune身體系統：呼吸、肝臟、腎臟、免疫

  • Respiratory distress: grunting, flaring, retractions, cyanosis, apnea over 15 sec. Bulb syringe: compress before insertion.呼吸窘迫：呻吟、鼻翼搧動、肋間凹陷、發紺、呼吸中止超過15秒。吸球使用前先擠壓。
  • Physiologic jaundice appears after 24 hr (term) and peaks around day 5 (indirect bili 6-7 mg/dL); jaundice in the first 24 hr is pathologic. Prevent chilling; feed early.生理性黃疸於24小時後出現（足月），約第5天最高（間接膽紅素6至7 mg/dL）；24小時內出現黃疸為病理性。避免受寒、儘早餵食。
  • Vitamin K (phytonadione) 0.5-1 mg IM into the vastus lateralis prevents hemorrhagic disease because gut flora is absent.維生素K（phytonadione）0.5至1 mg肌肉注射於股外側肌，因腸道菌叢尚未建立，可預防出血性疾病。
  • Weight loss 5-10% in week 1, regain by 10-14 days; 6 wet diapers by day 4 (1 g = 1 mL).第1週體重下降5至10%，10至14天恢復；第4天起每日6片濕尿布（1 g = 1 mL）。
  • IgG crosses placenta, IgA via colostrum; raised IgM suggests intrauterine infection. Eye prophylaxis (erythromycin 0.5%) within 1 hr to prevent ophthalmia neonatorum (gonorrhea).IgG經胎盤傳遞、IgA來自初乳；IgM升高提示子宮內感染。1小時內給予紅黴素0.5%眼藥膏預防新生兒眼炎（淋病）。
  • Cord care: keep dry and clean, fold diaper below cord, sponge baths until it falls off (10-14 days); moist, oozing, reddened base means infection. Circumcision: petroleum gauze, loose diaper, must void within 24 hr, yellow exudate normal; some blood tinge normal.臍帶照護：保持乾燥清潔、尿布折在臍帶下方、掉落前（10至14天）以擦澡；潮濕、滲液、基部發紅提示感染。包皮環切：凡士林紗布、尿布寬鬆、24小時內須排尿；黃色滲出物屬正常；少許血跡正常。

5GI, neurologic and thermal regulation; reflexes腸胃、神經與體溫調節；反射

  • Stomach <10 mL at birth, about 90 mL by day 10. Breastfeed every 2-3 hr, formula every 3-4 hr; burp; position right side after feeding but supine for sleep (SIDS prevention). Meconium first 24 hr, transitional stool, then seedy yellow (breast) or pale yellow-brown (formula).出生時胃容量少於10 mL，約第10天達90 mL。母乳每2至3小時、配方奶每3至4小時；拍嗝；餵後右側臥，但睡覺一律仰臥（預防SIDS）。依序為胎便（24小時內）、過渡便、黃色顆粒便（母乳）或淺黃至淺褐便（配方奶）。
  • PKU screening after 24 hr of formula/human milk.PKU篩檢於攝入配方奶或母乳24小時後進行。
  • Newborns do not shiver; use brown fat. Heat loss: evaporation (dry), radiation (away from cold walls), convection (no drafts), conduction (warm surfaces). Cold stress leads to hypoxia, hypoglycemia and acidosis. Axillary temp q30 min for 2 hr, q4h to 24 hr.新生兒不會顫抖，靠棕色脂肪產熱。散熱方式：蒸發（擦乾）、輻射（遠離冰冷牆面）、對流（避免風）、傳導（暖墊）。受寒會導致缺氧、低血糖與酸中毒。腋溫前2小時每30分鐘一次，至24小時每4小時。
  • Reflexes: rooting/sucking (3-4 mo), tonic neck (3-4 mo), palmar grasp (3-4 mo), plantar grasp (8 mo), Moro (gone by 6 mo; persistence abnormal), startle (4 mo), Babinski (1 yr), stepping (3-4 mo), crawling (6 wk). Absence of normal reflexes needs neurologic evaluation.反射：尋乳／吸吮（3至4個月）、頸張力（3至4個月）、手掌抓握（3至4個月）、足底抓握（8個月）、擁抱反射Moro（6個月消失；持續為異常）、驚嚇（4個月）、巴賓斯基（1歲）、踏步（3至4個月）、爬行（6週）。正常反射缺失需神經評估。

6Newborn safety and parent teaching新生兒安全與父母衛教

  • Match ID bands, transport infant in bassinet (never carry), verify staff badges, crib away from door, never leave infant unattended or alone on elevated surface.核對手環、以嬰兒車運送（不可抱著走）、確認工作人員識別證、嬰兒床遠離門口、不可獨留嬰兒或放在高處。
  • Formula or human milk is sufficient for 4-6 months; do not microwave bottles. Bathe in warm room, clean to dirty, eyes inner to outer canthus. Dress newborn in one more layer than parents.配方奶或母乳足以供應4至6個月；勿用微波爐加熱奶瓶。在溫暖房間沐浴，由乾淨到髒、眼睛由內眥往外。穿著比父母多一層。
  • Uncircumcised: never forcibly retract foreskin; separation completes by 3-6 years.未割包皮：切勿強行翻開包皮；3至6歲自然分離。

7Gestational age variations and respiratory problems胎齡差異與呼吸問題

  • Preterm (<37 wk): apnea, hypothermia, poor suck, thin skin, lanugo; keep in warmer, monitor VS q2-4h, girth, weight. Postterm (>42 wk): hypoglycemia, dry cracked skin, long nails, meconium staining.早產（<37週）：呼吸中止、低體溫、吸吮差、皮膚薄、胎毛多；置於保暖設備、每2至4小時測生命徵象、腹圍、體重。過期產（>42週）：低血糖、皮膚乾裂、指甲長、胎便染色。
  • SGA (<=10th percentile) and LGA (>=90th): both at risk for hypoglycemia; SGA also polycythemia, LGA birth trauma.SGA（小於等於第10百分位）與LGA（大於等於第90百分位）：皆有低血糖風險；SGA另有紅血球增多症，LGA有產傷風險。
  • RDS: surfactant deficiency; grunting, flaring, seesaw breathing; surfactant therapy, lowest effective oxygen, eye exam for ROP; side/back position, neck slightly extended.呼吸窘迫症候群：缺乏表面張力素；呻吟、鼻翼搧動、蹺蹺板式呼吸；給予表面張力素、使用最低有效氧濃度、檢查視網膜病變；側臥或仰臥、頸部微伸。
  • Meconium aspiration: term/postterm, yellow-green staining; suction if depressed. BPD: oxygen-dependent >28 days. TTN: retained lung fluid, resolves in 24-48 hr.胎便吸入：足月／過期產、黃綠染色；若活力差需氣管內抽吸。支氣管肺發育不良：依賴氧氣超過28天。暫時性呼吸急促：肺液吸收不全，24至48小時消退。

8IVH, ROP, NEC腦室內出血、早產兒視網膜病變、壞死性腸炎

  • IVH: prematurity, RDS; absent Moro, lethargy, apnea, shrill cry, seizures; midline head, elevate 30 degrees.腦室內出血：早產、RDS；莫羅反射減弱或消失、嗜睡、呼吸中止、尖叫、抽搐；頭保持中線、抬高30度。
  • ROP: prematurity and prolonged oxygen; leukocoria; laser treatment.早產兒視網膜病變：早產與長期用氧；白瞳症；雷射治療。
  • NEC: day 4-10, preterm; girth increase, absent bowel sounds, bile-stained vomit, bloody stool. No rectal temps; hold feeds, OG decompression, IV antibiotics and fluids.壞死性腸炎：第4至10天、早產兒；腹圍增加、腸音減弱或消失、膽汁性嘔吐、血便。不測肛溫；禁食、胃管減壓、靜脈抗生素與輸液。

9Hyperbilirubinemia, Rh disease, sepsis, infections高膽紅素血症、Rh疾病、敗血症與感染

  • Evaluate bilirubin >12 mg/dL in term infant; kernicterus is the danger. Jaundice spreads head to toe; check in natural light with blanching. Early frequent feeding (every 2-4 hr; continue breastfeeding).足月兒膽紅素大於12 mg/dL需評估；怕核黃疸。黃疸由頭向腳擴散；於自然光下按壓皮膚檢查。早期頻繁餵食（每2至4小時；持續哺乳）。
  • Phototherapy: expose skin, cover genitals, eye shields (remove each shift), photometer, extra fluids, skin temp, loose green stools, bronze baby syndrome, reposition q2h, turn lights off before drawing bilirubin, watch for rebound.光照療法：暴露皮膚、遮蓋生殖器、戴眼罩（每班移除檢查）、光度計、增加水分、監測皮膚溫度、綠色稀便、青銅寶寶症候群、每2小時翻身、抽血前關燈、注意反彈性升高。
  • Erythroblastosis fetalis: Rh-negative mother, Rh-positive fetus; jaundice before 24 hr, anemia, edema; RhoGAM within 72 hr; exchange transfusion.胎兒母細胞增多症：Rh陰性母親、Rh陽性胎兒；24小時內黃疸、貧血、水腫；72小時內給RhoGAM；換血治療。
  • Sepsis (e.g., group B strep): pallor, temperature instability, poor feeding, apnea, distention; cultures, antibiotics, warmth.敗血症（如B群鏈球菌）：蒼白、體溫不穩、吸吮差、呼吸中止、腹脹；做培養、給抗生素、保暖。
  • Congenital syphilis: hepatosplenomegaly, palmar rash, snuffles; penicillin; contact precautions; gloves until 24 hr of antibiotics.先天性梅毒：肝脾腫大、手掌皮疹、鼻塞（snuffles）；青黴素；接觸隔離；抗生素24小時內戴手套。

10Substance exposure, HIV, maternal diabetes, hypoglycemia, hypothyroidism物質暴露、HIV、母親糖尿病、低血糖、甲狀腺低下

  • Addicted newborn: irritable, tremors, hypertonic, high-pitched cry, sneezing, poor feeding, fist-sucking, seizures. Swaddle, dim quiet room, small frequent feeds, seizure precautions.藥物成癮新生兒：易怒、顫抖、肌張力高、高頻哭聲、打噴嚏、吸吮差、吸拳頭、抽搐。包裹、昏暗安靜環境、少量多餐、癲癇防護。
  • FASD: short palpebral fissures, thin upper lip, flat philtrum and midface, abnormal palmar creases, heart defects; priority is feeding and weight gain monitoring, low stimulation.胎兒酒精譜系障礙：眼裂短、上唇薄、人中平坦、中臉扁平、手掌紋異常、心臟缺損；優先監測餵食與體重，減少刺激。
  • HIV-exposed newborn: standard precautions, no breastfeeding/chest-feeding, clean skin before injections, no circumcision until status known, PJP prophylaxis, antiretrovirals 6 weeks, delay live vaccines.HIV暴露新生兒：標準防護、不哺乳、注射前清潔皮膚、確定感染狀態前不做包皮環切、預防肺囊蟲肺炎、抗反轉錄病毒藥物6週、延後活性疫苗。
  • Infant of diabetic mother: large, puffy, hypoglycemia, hyperbilirubinemia, RDS, hypocalcemia, birth trauma. Early feeding, glucose monitoring.糖尿病母親的新生兒：體型大、浮腫、低血糖、高膽紅素血症、RDS、低血鈣、產傷。儘早餵食並監測血糖。
  • Hypoglycemia <45 mg/dL (normal 45-60 day 1; 50-90 after): jitteriness, high-pitched cry, apnea, seizures; early feeds or IV glucose.低血糖小於45 mg/dL（第1天正常45至60；之後50至90）：顫抖、高頻哭聲、呼吸中止、抽搐；儘早餵食或靜脈給葡萄糖。
  • Hypothyroidism: thick protruding tongue, hoarse cry, low tone, abdominal distention; thyroid replacement.甲狀腺低下：舌大突出、哭聲沙啞、肌張力低、腹脹；甲狀腺素補充。

11Infant choking and CPR嬰兒哽塞與CPR

  • Mild obstruction: good air exchange, forceful cough; do not interfere. Severe: weak/no cough, high-pitched or no sound, cyanosis, cannot cry.輕度阻塞：氣體交換良好、有力咳嗽；不要干預。重度：咳嗽無力或無聲、高頻或無聲音、發紺、無法哭。
  • Severe: 5 back slaps (head lower than chest, supported on forearm), then 5 chest thrusts (lower half of sternum); repeat; no blind finger sweeps; if unresponsive call for help and start CPR.重度：5次背部拍擊（頭低於胸，以前臂支撐），再5次胸部推擠（胸骨下半）；重複進行；不可盲目手指清除；若無反應則求救並開始CPR。
  • Infant pulse check: brachial artery; infant = 1 year or younger.嬰兒脈搏檢查：肱動脈；嬰兒指1歲或以下。

  • HR 110-160 (apical, 1 full min; 90-110 asleep, up to 180 crying); RR 30-60; axillary temp 97.7-<99.5 F (36.5-<37.5 C); BP 80-90/40-50 only if cardiac concern.心率110至160次/分（心尖脈量1分鐘；睡眠90至110、哭泣可達180）；呼吸30至60次/分；腋溫36.5至未滿37.5°C（97.7至<99.5°F）；僅疑心臟問題時測血壓80至90/40至50。
  • Length 18-22 in (45-55 cm), weight 2500-4000 g, head circumference 33-35 cm.身長45至55公分、體重2500至4000公克、頭圍33至35公分。
  • Molding resolves in about 72 hr. Caput succedaneum crosses suture lines, resolves in days; cephalhematoma does not cross sutures, resolves in weeks (up to 6).產瘤變形約72小時消退。產瘤跨越縫合線，數日消退；頭血腫不跨縫合線，需數週（至6週）吸收。
  • Anterior fontanel closes 12-18 months; posterior by 2-3 months. Eyes cross occasionally, red reflex present. Low-set ears suggest syndromes. Nose breathers; flaring means distress. Epstein pearls normal; thrush does not wipe off.前囟門12至18個月閉合；後囟門2至3個月內閉合。眼睛偶爾斜視、有紅色反射。耳位低提示症候群。新生兒用鼻呼吸；鼻翼搧動代表窘迫。上顎珍珠腫正常；鵝口瘡擦不掉。
Topic 3 · tap to flip主題 3 · 點擊翻面
Skin, abdomen, genitals, spine, extremities皮膚、腹部、生殖器、脊柱與四肢
  • Acrocyanosis normal early; central cyanosis is not. Vernix, lanugo, milia, Mongolian spots, stork bites normal. Harlequin sign may point to cardiac shunting or sepsis.初期手腳發紺屬正常；中心性發紺不正常。胎脂、胎毛、粟粒疹、蒙古斑、鸛咬痕屬正常。小丑症狀可能代表心臟分流或敗血症。
  • Cord has 2 arteries and 1 vein; a 2-vessel cord is linked to IUGR and anomalies. Cord clamp stays at least 24 hr; first meconium within 24 hr; first void within 24 hr.臍帶有2條動脈1條靜脈；兩條血管者與子宮內生長遲滯及異常有關。臍夾至少保留24小時；24小時內排胎便與首次排尿。
  • Port-wine stain stays; strawberry mark fades by 7-9 yr; stork bites fade by 2 yr; Mongolian spots fade in 1-2 yr.葡萄酒斑不會消退；草莓狀血管瘤7至9歲消退；鸛咬痕2歲前消退；蒙古斑1至2年內消退。
  • Check clavicle fractures, hip dysplasia (Ortolani/Barlow), spinal dimples or hair tufts, symmetric movements. Jittery tremors may signal hypoglycemia, hypocalcemia, or drug withdrawal.檢查鎖骨骨折、髖關節發育不良（Ortolani／Barlow）、脊柱凹陷或毛髮叢、動作對稱性。顫抖可能代表低血糖、低血鈣或藥物戒斷。
Topic 4 · tap to flip主題 4 · 點擊翻面
Body systems: respiratory, hepatic, renal, immune身體系統：呼吸、肝臟、腎臟、免疫
  • Respiratory distress: grunting, flaring, retractions, cyanosis, apnea over 15 sec. Bulb syringe: compress before insertion.呼吸窘迫：呻吟、鼻翼搧動、肋間凹陷、發紺、呼吸中止超過15秒。吸球使用前先擠壓。
  • Physiologic jaundice appears after 24 hr (term) and peaks around day 5 (indirect bili 6-7 mg/dL); jaundice in the first 24 hr is pathologic. Prevent chilling; feed early.生理性黃疸於24小時後出現（足月），約第5天最高（間接膽紅素6至7 mg/dL）；24小時內出現黃疸為病理性。避免受寒、儘早餵食。
  • Vitamin K (phytonadione) 0.5-1 mg IM into the vastus lateralis prevents hemorrhagic disease because gut flora is absent.維生素K（phytonadione）0.5至1 mg肌肉注射於股外側肌，因腸道菌叢尚未建立，可預防出血性疾病。
  • Weight loss 5-10% in week 1, regain by 10-14 days; 6 wet diapers by day 4 (1 g = 1 mL).第1週體重下降5至10%，10至14天恢復；第4天起每日6片濕尿布（1 g = 1 mL）。
  • IgG crosses placenta, IgA via colostrum; raised IgM suggests intrauterine infection. Eye prophylaxis (erythromycin 0.5%) within 1 hr to prevent ophthalmia neonatorum (gonorrhea).IgG經胎盤傳遞、IgA來自初乳；IgM升高提示子宮內感染。1小時內給予紅黴素0.5%眼藥膏預防新生兒眼炎（淋病）。
  • Cord care: keep dry and clean, fold diaper below cord, sponge baths until it falls off (10-14 days); moist, oozing, reddened base means infection. Circumcision: petroleum gauze, loose diaper, must void within 24 hr, yellow exudate normal; some blood tinge normal.臍帶照護：保持乾燥清潔、尿布折在臍帶下方、掉落前（10至14天）以擦澡；潮濕、滲液、基部發紅提示感染。包皮環切：凡士林紗布、尿布寬鬆、24小時內須排尿；黃色滲出物屬正常；少許血跡正常。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
GI, neurologic and thermal regulation; reflexes腸胃、神經與體溫調節；反射
  • Stomach <10 mL at birth, about 90 mL by day 10. Breastfeed every 2-3 hr, formula every 3-4 hr; burp; position right side after feeding but supine for sleep (SIDS prevention). Meconium first 24 hr, transitional stool, then seedy yellow (breast) or pale yellow-brown (formula).出生時胃容量少於10 mL，約第10天達90 mL。母乳每2至3小時、配方奶每3至4小時；拍嗝；餵後右側臥，但睡覺一律仰臥（預防SIDS）。依序為胎便（24小時內）、過渡便、黃色顆粒便（母乳）或淺黃至淺褐便（配方奶）。
  • PKU screening after 24 hr of formula/human milk.PKU篩檢於攝入配方奶或母乳24小時後進行。
  • Newborns do not shiver; use brown fat. Heat loss: evaporation (dry), radiation (away from cold walls), convection (no drafts), conduction (warm surfaces). Cold stress leads to hypoxia, hypoglycemia and acidosis. Axillary temp q30 min for 2 hr, q4h to 24 hr.新生兒不會顫抖，靠棕色脂肪產熱。散熱方式：蒸發（擦乾）、輻射（遠離冰冷牆面）、對流（避免風）、傳導（暖墊）。受寒會導致缺氧、低血糖與酸中毒。腋溫前2小時每30分鐘一次，至24小時每4小時。
  • Reflexes: rooting/sucking (3-4 mo), tonic neck (3-4 mo), palmar grasp (3-4 mo), plantar grasp (8 mo), Moro (gone by 6 mo; persistence abnormal), startle (4 mo), Babinski (1 yr), stepping (3-4 mo), crawling (6 wk). Absence of normal reflexes needs neurologic evaluation.反射：尋乳／吸吮（3至4個月）、頸張力（3至4個月）、手掌抓握（3至4個月）、足底抓握（8個月）、擁抱反射Moro（6個月消失；持續為異常）、驚嚇（4個月）、巴賓斯基（1歲）、踏步（3至4個月）、爬行（6週）。正常反射缺失需神經評估。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Newborn safety and parent teaching新生兒安全與父母衛教
  • Match ID bands, transport infant in bassinet (never carry), verify staff badges, crib away from door, never leave infant unattended or alone on elevated surface.核對手環、以嬰兒車運送（不可抱著走）、確認工作人員識別證、嬰兒床遠離門口、不可獨留嬰兒或放在高處。
  • Formula or human milk is sufficient for 4-6 months; do not microwave bottles. Bathe in warm room, clean to dirty, eyes inner to outer canthus. Dress newborn in one more layer than parents.配方奶或母乳足以供應4至6個月；勿用微波爐加熱奶瓶。在溫暖房間沐浴，由乾淨到髒、眼睛由內眥往外。穿著比父母多一層。
  • Uncircumcised: never forcibly retract foreskin; separation completes by 3-6 years.未割包皮：切勿強行翻開包皮；3至6歲自然分離。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Gestational age variations and respiratory problems胎齡差異與呼吸問題
  • Preterm (<37 wk): apnea, hypothermia, poor suck, thin skin, lanugo; keep in warmer, monitor VS q2-4h, girth, weight. Postterm (>42 wk): hypoglycemia, dry cracked skin, long nails, meconium staining.早產（<37週）：呼吸中止、低體溫、吸吮差、皮膚薄、胎毛多；置於保暖設備、每2至4小時測生命徵象、腹圍、體重。過期產（>42週）：低血糖、皮膚乾裂、指甲長、胎便染色。
  • SGA (<=10th percentile) and LGA (>=90th): both at risk for hypoglycemia; SGA also polycythemia, LGA birth trauma.SGA（小於等於第10百分位）與LGA（大於等於第90百分位）：皆有低血糖風險；SGA另有紅血球增多症，LGA有產傷風險。
  • RDS: surfactant deficiency; grunting, flaring, seesaw breathing; surfactant therapy, lowest effective oxygen, eye exam for ROP; side/back position, neck slightly extended.呼吸窘迫症候群：缺乏表面張力素；呻吟、鼻翼搧動、蹺蹺板式呼吸；給予表面張力素、使用最低有效氧濃度、檢查視網膜病變；側臥或仰臥、頸部微伸。
  • Meconium aspiration: term/postterm, yellow-green staining; suction if depressed. BPD: oxygen-dependent >28 days. TTN: retained lung fluid, resolves in 24-48 hr.胎便吸入：足月／過期產、黃綠染色；若活力差需氣管內抽吸。支氣管肺發育不良：依賴氧氣超過28天。暫時性呼吸急促：肺液吸收不全，24至48小時消退。

IVH, ROP, NEC腦室內出血、早產兒視網膜病變、壞死性腸炎
  • IVH: prematurity, RDS; absent Moro, lethargy, apnea, shrill cry, seizures; midline head, elevate 30 degrees.腦室內出血：早產、RDS；莫羅反射減弱或消失、嗜睡、呼吸中止、尖叫、抽搐；頭保持中線、抬高30度。
  • ROP: prematurity and prolonged oxygen; leukocoria; laser treatment.早產兒視網膜病變：早產與長期用氧；白瞳症；雷射治療。
  • NEC: day 4-10, preterm; girth increase, absent bowel sounds, bile-stained vomit, bloody stool. No rectal temps; hold feeds, OG decompression, IV antibiotics and fluids.壞死性腸炎：第4至10天、早產兒；腹圍增加、腸音減弱或消失、膽汁性嘔吐、血便。不測肛溫；禁食、胃管減壓、靜脈抗生素與輸液。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
Hyperbilirubinemia, Rh disease, sepsis, infections高膽紅素血症、Rh疾病、敗血症與感染
  • Evaluate bilirubin >12 mg/dL in term infant; kernicterus is the danger. Jaundice spreads head to toe; check in natural light with blanching. Early frequent feeding (every 2-4 hr; continue breastfeeding).足月兒膽紅素大於12 mg/dL需評估；怕核黃疸。黃疸由頭向腳擴散；於自然光下按壓皮膚檢查。早期頻繁餵食（每2至4小時；持續哺乳）。
  • Phototherapy: expose skin, cover genitals, eye shields (remove each shift), photometer, extra fluids, skin temp, loose green stools, bronze baby syndrome, reposition q2h, turn lights off before drawing bilirubin, watch for rebound.光照療法：暴露皮膚、遮蓋生殖器、戴眼罩（每班移除檢查）、光度計、增加水分、監測皮膚溫度、綠色稀便、青銅寶寶症候群、每2小時翻身、抽血前關燈、注意反彈性升高。
  • Erythroblastosis fetalis: Rh-negative mother, Rh-positive fetus; jaundice before 24 hr, anemia, edema; RhoGAM within 72 hr; exchange transfusion.胎兒母細胞增多症：Rh陰性母親、Rh陽性胎兒；24小時內黃疸、貧血、水腫；72小時內給RhoGAM；換血治療。
  • Sepsis (e.g., group B strep): pallor, temperature instability, poor feeding, apnea, distention; cultures, antibiotics, warmth.敗血症（如B群鏈球菌）：蒼白、體溫不穩、吸吮差、呼吸中止、腹脹；做培養、給抗生素、保暖。
  • Congenital syphilis: hepatosplenomegaly, palmar rash, snuffles; penicillin; contact precautions; gloves until 24 hr of antibiotics.先天性梅毒：肝脾腫大、手掌皮疹、鼻塞（snuffles）；青黴素；接觸隔離；抗生素24小時內戴手套。
Topic 10 · tap to flip主題 10 · 點擊翻面
Substance exposure, HIV, maternal diabetes, hypoglycemia, hypothyroidism物質暴露、HIV、母親糖尿病、低血糖、甲狀腺低下
  • Addicted newborn: irritable, tremors, hypertonic, high-pitched cry, sneezing, poor feeding, fist-sucking, seizures. Swaddle, dim quiet room, small frequent feeds, seizure precautions.藥物成癮新生兒：易怒、顫抖、肌張力高、高頻哭聲、打噴嚏、吸吮差、吸拳頭、抽搐。包裹、昏暗安靜環境、少量多餐、癲癇防護。
  • FASD: short palpebral fissures, thin upper lip, flat philtrum and midface, abnormal palmar creases, heart defects; priority is feeding and weight gain monitoring, low stimulation.胎兒酒精譜系障礙：眼裂短、上唇薄、人中平坦、中臉扁平、手掌紋異常、心臟缺損；優先監測餵食與體重，減少刺激。
  • HIV-exposed newborn: standard precautions, no breastfeeding/chest-feeding, clean skin before injections, no circumcision until status known, PJP prophylaxis, antiretrovirals 6 weeks, delay live vaccines.HIV暴露新生兒：標準防護、不哺乳、注射前清潔皮膚、確定感染狀態前不做包皮環切、預防肺囊蟲肺炎、抗反轉錄病毒藥物6週、延後活性疫苗。
  • Infant of diabetic mother: large, puffy, hypoglycemia, hyperbilirubinemia, RDS, hypocalcemia, birth trauma. Early feeding, glucose monitoring.糖尿病母親的新生兒：體型大、浮腫、低血糖、高膽紅素血症、RDS、低血鈣、產傷。儘早餵食並監測血糖。
  • Hypoglycemia <45 mg/dL (normal 45-60 day 1; 50-90 after): jitteriness, high-pitched cry, apnea, seizures; early feeds or IV glucose.低血糖小於45 mg/dL（第1天正常45至60；之後50至90）：顫抖、高頻哭聲、呼吸中止、抽搐；儘早餵食或靜脈給葡萄糖。
  • Hypothyroidism: thick protruding tongue, hoarse cry, low tone, abdominal distention; thyroid replacement.甲狀腺低下：舌大突出、哭聲沙啞、肌張力低、腹脹；甲狀腺素補充。
Topic 11 · tap to flip主題 11 · 點擊翻面
Infant choking and CPR嬰兒哽塞與CPR
  • Mild obstruction: good air exchange, forceful cough; do not interfere. Severe: weak/no cough, high-pitched or no sound, cyanosis, cannot cry.輕度阻塞：氣體交換良好、有力咳嗽；不要干預。重度：咳嗽無力或無聲、高頻或無聲音、發紺、無法哭。
  • Severe: 5 back slaps (head lower than chest, supported on forearm), then 5 chest thrusts (lower half of sternum); repeat; no blind finger sweeps; if unresponsive call for help and start CPR.重度：5次背部拍擊（頭低於胸，以前臂支撐），再5次胸部推擠（胸骨下半）；重複進行；不可盲目手指清除；若無反應則求救並開始CPR。
  • Infant pulse check: brachial artery; infant = 1 year or younger.嬰兒脈搏檢查：肱動脈；嬰兒指1歲或以下。

1Which nursing action best prevents heat loss by evaporation in a newborn immediately after birth?新生兒剛出生時，哪項護理行動最能預防蒸發散熱？

Evaporation occurs from wet skin; drying removes moisture. The other options address conduction, convection and radiation.蒸發來自濕潤皮膚，擦乾可去除水分；其他選項分別針對傳導、對流與輻射。

Moistness, discharge and redness are cord infection signs that need provider evaluation.潮濕、分泌物與發紅為臍帶感染徵象，需醫師評估。

8A parent asks why the newborn needs a vitamin K injection. Which is the best response?父母詢問新生兒為何需要打維生素K。最佳回答為何？

The sterile newborn gut cannot yet make vitamin K needed for clotting factors II, VII, IX, X.新生兒腸道無菌，尚無法合成製造凝血因子II、VII、IX、X所需的維生素K。
