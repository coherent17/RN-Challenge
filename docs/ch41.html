Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Transmission precautions and HIV/AIDS basics傳染防護與HIV/AIDS基礎 📖 PDF p.564 (book p.507), PDF p.565 (book p.508)

  • Standard precautions for all children; add airborne, droplet or contact precautions by disease (e.g., influenza plus wound infection needs both).所有兒童採標準防護；依疾病加上空氣、飛沫或接觸防護（如流感併傷口感染則需多重）。
  • HIV infects CD4+ T cells, causing progressive immunodeficiency and opportunistic infections.HIV感染CD4+ T細胞，造成進行性免疫缺損與伺機性感染。
  • Spread by blood, semen, vaginal secretions and human milk; vertical (perinatal) or horizontal (sexual, parenteral).經血液、精液、陰道分泌物與母乳傳播；垂直（周產期）或水平（性接觸、血液）。
  • Most common opportunistic infection in children: Pneumocystis jiroveci pneumonia, often at 3-6 months; cough is a common sign.兒童最常見的伺機性感染：肺囊蟲肺炎（P. jiroveci），常見於3-6個月；咳嗽為常見徵象。
  • Findings: failure to thrive, chronic diarrhea/cough, oral candidiasis, lymphadenopathy, hepatosplenomegaly, parotitis, developmental delay or regression.表現：生長遲滯、慢性腹瀉/咳嗽、口腔念珠菌感染、淋巴結腫大、肝脾腫大、腮腺炎、發展遲緩或退化。

HIV testing, treatment and immunizationHIV檢測、治療與疫苗 📖 PDF p.565 (book p.508), PDF p.566 (book p.509)

  • Under 18 months: use PCR (proviral DNA) or p24 antigen, accurate at 1-4 months; ELISA/Western blot only from 18 months because maternal antibodies cross the placenta.18個月以下：用PCR（前病毒DNA）或p24抗原，1-4個月最準；ELISA/Western blot需18個月後才用，因母體抗體會經胎盤傳給嬰兒。
  • CD4+ counts are age-adjusted; severe suppression <15% or <750/uL (<12 mo), <500 (1-5 y), <200 (6-12 y).CD4+數值需依年齡校正；嚴重抑制為<15%或<750/uL（<12個月）、<500（1-5歲）、<200（6-12歲）。
  • Goals: slow viral replication with combination antiretrovirals, prevent/treat opportunistic infections, nutrition, symptom control; PJP prophylaxis especially in first year of life.目標：以複合抗反轉錄病毒藥物減緩病毒複製、預防/治療伺機性感染、營養、症狀控制；尤其出生第一年給予PJP預防。
  • Immunize all HIV-exposed/infected children; if symptomatic or severely immunosuppressed use only inactivated vaccines (IM influenza yearly, inactivated polio); no measles, varicella or rotavirus vaccine; give VZIG after varicella exposure and immunoglobulin after measles exposure.所有HIV暴露/感染兒童皆應接種疫苗；有症狀或重度免疫抑制時僅用不活化疫苗（每年IM流感、不活化小兒麻痺）；不給麻疹、水痘、輪狀病毒疫苗；水痘暴露後給VZIG，麻疹暴露後給免疫球蛋白。

HIV home care and adolescent educationHIV居家照護與青少年衛教 📖 PDF p.566 (book p.509)

  • Frequent handwashing, gloves with body fluids, avoid contagious contacts, keep child home when sick, keep immunizations current.勤洗手、接觸體液戴手套、避免接觸傳染性病人、生病在家休養、疫苗按時接種。
  • Report fever, malaise, weight loss, vomiting, diarrhea, oral lesions; monitor weight; high-calorie, high-protein diet; no raw meat/fish or unwashed produce if immunosuppressed.發燒、倦怠、體重減輕、嘔吐、腹瀉、口腔病灶需通報；監測體重；高熱量高蛋白飲食；免疫低下時避免生肉/魚與未洗蔬果。
  • Dishwasher for utensils; discard unused food/formula after 24 h; fold soiled diapers inward into covered bin; clean spills with bleach 1:10 (bleach:water as 10 water to 1 bleach).餐具用洗碗機清洗；未食用食物/配方奶24小時後丟棄；髒尿布向內摺並放入有蓋垃圾桶；體液外溢以漂白水（水:漂白水=10:1）清理。
  • Adolescents: teach transmission, abstinence, condoms, risk reduction and support resources.青少年：教導傳染途徑、禁慾、保險套、降低風險與支持資源。

Measles, roseola, rubella, mumps麻疹、玫瑰疹、德國麻疹、腮腺炎 📖 PDF p.566 (book p.509), PDF p.567 (book p.510), PDF p.568 (book p.511)

  • Measles (rubeola): the 3 Cs (coryza, cough, conjunctivitis), Koplik spots (blue-white spots with red base on buccal mucosa), rash from face downward; airborne + contact precautions, rest, cool mist, dim lights, vitamin A.麻疹：3C（鼻炎、咳嗽、結膜炎）、Koplik斑（頰黏膜藍白點、紅底）、紅疹由臉向下蔓延；空氣+接觸防護、休息、冷霧、調暗燈光、補充維生素A。
  • Roseola (HHV-6): high fever 3-5 days in a well-looking child, then rose-pink rash after fever breaks; febrile seizures possible; supportive care.玫瑰疹（HHV-6）：外觀良好的孩子高燒3-5天，退燒後出現玫瑰色疹；可能熱痙攣；支持性照護。
  • Rubella: low fever, face-to-body pink maculopapular rash in 1-3 days, soft palate petechiae; airborne/droplet/contact precautions; keep away from pregnant persons.德國麻疹：低燒、1-3天內由臉蔓延全身的粉紅斑丘疹、軟顎瘀點；空氣/飛沫/接觸防護；遠離孕婦。
  • Mumps: parotid swelling, jaw/ear pain with chewing, orchitis, deafness, aseptic meningitis; droplet + contact precautions, soft foods, rest, scrotal support; lethargy/vomiting means evaluate for meningitis.腮腺炎：腮腺腫脹、咀嚼時下顎/耳痛、睪丸炎、耳聾、無菌性腦膜炎；飛沫+接觸防護、軟食、休息、陰囊托高；嗜睡/嘔吐需評估腦膜炎。

Varicella, pertussis, diphtheria, polio水痘、百日咳、白喉、小兒麻痺 📖 PDF p.568 (book p.511), PDF p.569 (book p.512)

  • Varicella: macules to vesicles to crusts, trunk and scalp first; contagious 1-2 days before rash until all crusted (about 6 days); airborne, droplet and contact precautions; VZIG/IVIG for high-risk exposed children.水痘：斑疹→水泡→結痂，先出現於軀幹與頭皮；出疹前1-2天至全部結痂（約6天）具傳染力；空氣、飛沫與接觸防護；高風險暴露兒童給VZIG/IVIG。
  • Pertussis: cough with loud whoop, cyanosis, tongue protrusion; droplet + contact precautions, antibiotics, avoid cough triggers, hydration, suction/oxygen; contagious in catarrhal stage; infants lack maternal immunity, so Tdap for postpartum parent and close contacts.百日咳：咳嗽伴吸氣哮鳴、發紺、吐舌；飛沫+接觸防護、抗生素、避免誘發咳嗽因素、補水、吸痰/氧氣；卡他期傳染力最強；嬰兒無母體免疫，產後父母與密切接觸者接種Tdap。
  • Diphtheria: sore throat, foul nasal discharge, dense pseudomembrane, bull neck; strict isolation, antitoxin after horse-serum sensitivity test, antibiotics, suction, trach care.白喉：喉嚨痛、惡臭鼻分泌物、厚偽膜、牛頸；嚴格隔離、先做馬血清過敏測試再給抗毒素、抗生素、吸痰、氣切護理。
  • Polio: enterovirus, fecal-oral; fever, stiffness, possible paralysis; enteric and contact precautions, watch respiratory paralysis, PT.小兒麻痺：腸病毒、糞口傳染；發燒、僵硬、可能麻痺；腸道與接觸防護、注意呼吸麻痺、物理治療。

Scarlet fever, fifth disease, mononucleosis, RMSF猩紅熱、第五病、單核球增多症、落磯山斑疹熱 📖 PDF p.569 (book p.512), PDF p.570 (book p.513), PDF p.571 (book p.514)

  • Scarlet fever (group A strep): sandpaper rash sparing face, Pastia lines, strawberry tongue, desquamation of palms/soles; contact + droplet precautions until 24 h of antibiotics.猩紅熱（A群鏈球菌）：砂紙樣疹、臉部不受影響、Pastia線、草莓舌、掌蹠脫皮；接觸+飛沫防護至抗生素24小時後。
  • Fifth disease (parvovirus B19): slapped-cheek rash then lacy rash on extremities; supportive; pregnant contacts should call their provider.第五病（微小病毒B19）：拍打臉頰樣紅斑，之後四肢網狀疹；支持性照護；孕婦接觸者應通知醫師。
  • Mononucleosis (EBV): fever, sore throat, lymphadenopathy, hepatosplenomegaly; teach splenic rupture signs (LUQ/left shoulder pain, abdominal pain).傳染性單核球增多症（EBV）：發燒、喉痛、淋巴結腫、肝脾腫大；教導脾破裂徵象（左上腹/左肩/腹痛）。
  • Rocky Mountain spotted fever (tick-borne Rickettsia): fever, headache, rash on wrists/ankles spreading to palms/soles; antibiotics; prevention box on tick bites.落磯山斑疹熱（蜱傳立克次體）：發燒、頭痛、手腕腳踝紅疹並擴及掌蹠；抗生素；蜱咬預防。
  • Tick removal: tweezers at skin, pull straight up steadily, save tick, wash hands; wear light long clothing, DEET/permethrin with caution.移除蜱：鑷子貼近皮膚、穩定向上直拉、保留蜱、洗手；穿淺色長袖長褲、謹慎使用DEET/permethrin。

CA-MRSA, influenza and COVID-19社區型MRSA、流感與COVID-19 📖 PDF p.571 (book p.514), PDF p.572 (book p.515)

  • CA-MRSA: infection in a healthy person without recent health-care exposure; spread by contact and contaminated items; red, warm, painful, pus-draining skin lesion; can progress to sepsis.CA-MRSA：無近期醫療接觸的健康者感染；經接觸與污染物品傳播；皮膚紅腫熱痛流膿；可進展為敗血症。
  • Prevention: handwashing, no sharing personal items, clean shared equipment, cover wounds; care: assess, drainage and cultures, antibiotics.預防：洗手、不共用個人物品、清潔共用器材、覆蓋傷口；處置：評估、引流與培養、抗生素。
  • Influenza: sudden high fever, aches, cough; annual vaccine, hand hygiene, keep home until fever-free 24 h without antipyretics; antivirals, fluids, rest.流感：突發高燒、痠痛、咳嗽；每年疫苗、手部衛生、無退燒藥下退燒24小時後才返校；抗病毒藥、補水、休息。
  • COVID-19: droplet/airborne + contact precautions; fever and cough common; seek care for breathing difficulty, cyanosis, confusion, inability to keep fluids; watch for pediatric inflammatory multisystem syndrome (Kawasaki-like).COVID-19：飛沫/空氣+接觸防護；常見發燒咳嗽；呼吸困難、發紺、意識混亂、無法進水需就醫；注意兒童多系統發炎症候群（類川崎病）。
  • Immunization schedules follow Chapter 19 and CDC guidance.疫苗時程參照第19章與CDC指引。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. P. jiroveci pneumonia is the most common opportunistic infection in HIV-infected children (peak 3-6 months); cough is a key sign.肺囊蟲肺炎為HIV感染兒童最常見伺機性感染（高峰3-6個月）；咳嗽為關鍵徵象。 📖 PDF p.564 (book p.507)
  2. Infants <18 months: PCR or p24 antigen, not ELISA (maternal antibodies cross placenta).18個月以下嬰兒：使用PCR或p24抗原，而非ELISA（母體抗體經胎盤）。 📖 PDF p.565 (book p.508)
  3. Symptomatic/immunosuppressed HIV child: no live vaccines (measles, varicella, rotavirus); IM inactivated influenza yearly.有症狀/免疫抑制之HIV兒童：不給活性疫苗（麻疹、水痘、輪狀病毒）；每年IM不活化流感疫苗。 📖 PDF p.566 (book p.509)
  4. HIV-infected child with fever must stay home; clean body fluid spills with bleach (alcohol ineffective).HIV感染兒童發燒須留在家中；體液外溢用漂白水清理（酒精無效）。 📖 PDF p.566 (book p.509)
  5. Measles: Koplik spots, 3 Cs, airborne + contact precautions, vitamin A.麻疹：Koplik斑、3C、空氣+接觸防護、維生素A。 📖 PDF p.566 (book p.509)
  6. Roseola: high fever first, rash appears after fever resolves.玫瑰疹：先高燒，退燒後才出疹。 📖 PDF p.567 (book p.510)
  7. Rubella: isolate from pregnant persons.德國麻疹：需與孕婦隔離。 📖 PDF p.567 (book p.510)
  8. Mumps with lethargy or vomiting: suspect aseptic meningitis, child needs to be seen; orchitis supported with snug underwear.腮腺炎合併嗜睡或嘔吐：懷疑無菌性腦膜炎，需就診；睪丸炎以合身內褲托高。 📖 PDF p.567 (book p.510), PDF p.568 (book p.511)
  9. Varicella: airborne, droplet and contact precautions; contagious until all lesions crusted; VZIG for susceptible immunocompromised exposures.水痘：空氣、飛沫、接觸防護；全部結痂前具傳染力；易感免疫低下者暴露後給VZIG。 📖 PDF p.568 (book p.511)
  10. Pertussis: droplet precautions during catarrhal stage; no isolation needed in convalescence; Tdap for caregivers.百日咳：卡他期飛沫防護；恢復期不需隔離；照顧者接種Tdap。 📖 PDF p.568 (book p.511), PDF p.569 (book p.512)
  11. Diphtheria antitoxin requires horse-serum sensitivity testing first; pseudomembrane threatens airway.白喉抗毒素需先做馬血清過敏測試；偽膜威脅呼吸道。 📖 PDF p.569 (book p.512)
  12. Scarlet fever: sandpaper rash, strawberry tongue, Pastia lines; precautions until 24 h of antibiotics.猩紅熱：砂紙疹、草莓舌、Pastia線；抗生素24小時內仍需防護。 📖 PDF p.569 (book p.512)
  13. Fifth disease: slapped-cheek rash; pregnant contacts notify provider.第五病：拍臉頰疹；孕婦接觸者通知醫師。 📖 PDF p.570 (book p.513)
  14. Mononucleosis: avoid contact sports; report LUQ or left-shoulder pain (splenic rupture).單核球增多症：避免接觸性運動；左上腹或左肩痛需通報（脾破裂）。 📖 PDF p.570 (book p.513)
  15. RMSF: rash on wrists/ankles and palms/soles after tick bite; remove tick with tweezers straight up.落磯山斑疹熱：蜱咬後手腕腳踝及掌蹠出疹；用鑷子垂直向上移除蜱。 📖 PDF p.570 (book p.513), PDF p.571 (book p.514)
  16. Influenza: return to school after 24 h fever-free without antipyretics.流感：無退燒藥下退燒24小時後才可返校。 📖 PDF p.571 (book p.514), PDF p.572 (book p.515)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Measles '3 Cs + K': Coryza, Cough, Conjunctivitis, Koplik spots.麻疹「3C+K」：鼻炎、咳嗽、結膜炎、Koplik斑。 📖 PDF p.566 (book p.509)
  • Rash direction: measles and rubella start on the face and go down; scarlet fever spares the face; RMSF starts on wrists/ankles.疹子方向：麻疹與德國麻疹由臉往下；猩紅熱臉部不受影響；RMSF由手腕腳踝開始。 📖 PDF p.566 (book p.509), PDF p.567 (book p.510), PDF p.569 (book p.512), PDF p.570 (book p.513)
  • Roseola: 'Rash Only after Fever' (rose rash after fever ends).玫瑰疹：「燒退才疹」。 📖 PDF p.567 (book p.510)

1. An infant of an HIV-positive parent is monitored. Which sign suggests the most common opportunistic infection?HIV陽性父母的嬰兒接受監測。哪項徵象提示最常見的伺機性感染？

Pneumocystis jiroveci pneumonia is most common, around 3-6 months, and presents with cough and respiratory signs.肺囊蟲肺炎最常見，約3-6個月，表現為咳嗽與呼吸症狀。

2. Which test is most appropriate to diagnose HIV in a 2-month-old born to an HIV-positive parent?HIV陽性父母所生2個月嬰兒，最適合哪項診斷檢查？

Maternal antibodies make antibody tests unreliable before 18 months; PCR or p24 antigen is accurate in infants.母體抗體使18個月前抗體檢測不可靠；PCR或p24抗原適用於嬰兒。
