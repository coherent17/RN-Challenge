Pediatric airway and lung disorders: epiglottitis, croup, bronchitis, bronchiolitis/RSV, pneumonia, asthma, cystic fibrosis, SIDS, foreign body aspiration and tuberculosis. Airway protection, recognizing emergencies and correct positioning/medication teaching dominate the testing.小兒呼吸道與肺部疾病：會厭炎、哮吼、支氣管炎、細支氣管炎/RSV、肺炎、氣喘、囊性纖維化、嬰兒猝死症、異物吸入與結核病。重點為保護呼吸道、辨識緊急狀況，以及正確擺位與用藥衛教。

1Epiglottitis會厭炎

  • Bacterial (usually H. influenzae type b), age 2-8, abrupt onset, winter; emergency that can obstruct the airway completely. Hib vaccine lowers risk.細菌性（多為 Hib），2–8 歲，突發、冬季；可迅速完全阻塞呼吸道的急症。Hib 疫苗可降低風險。
  • Classic signs: tripod position, drooling, stridor, muffled voice, high fever, dysphagia, agitation, no spontaneous cough, cherry-red swollen epiglottis.典型徵象：三腳架姿勢、流口水、喘鳴、聲音悶啞、高燒、吞嚥困難、躁動、無自發性咳嗽、櫻桃紅腫脹會厭。
  • Do NOT examine the throat, culture, take oral temperature, or agitate the child until the airway is secured (risk of spasm and total occlusion).在呼吸道確保前，不可檢視咽喉、採喉頭培養、量口溫或使孩童躁動（可能痙攣致完全阻塞）。
  • Keep child with parent, never leave unattended; humidified oxygen, pulse oximetry, lateral neck film, ready intubation/tracheostomy equipment.與家長在一起，不可獨留；給濕化氧氣、監測血氧、頸部側位 X 光、備妥插管/氣切設備。

2Croup (laryngotracheobronchitis)哮吼（喉氣管支氣管炎）

  • Viral (parainfluenza 2 and 3, RSV, influenza), under 5 years, gradual onset after URI.病毒性（副流感 2、3 型、RSV、流感），5 歲以下，上呼吸道感染後逐漸發生。
  • Stages (Box 36.1): I low fever, hoarseness, seal-bark cough, inspiratory stridor; II continuous stridor, retractions; III restlessness, pallor, tachypnea, hypoxia; IV cyanosis, apnea.分期（框 36.1）：I 低燒、聲音沙啞、犬吠樣咳嗽、吸氣性喘鳴；II 持續喘鳴、凹陷；III 躁動、蒼白、呼吸急促、缺氧；IV 發紺、呼吸暫停。
  • Cool mist/humidified air, HOB elevated, fluids, corticosteroids, nebulized racemic epinephrine, heliox; antibiotics only if bacterial; no cough/cold medicines.涼霧/濕化空氣、抬高床頭、補水、類固醇、racemic epinephrine 噴霧、氦氧混合氣；細菌感染才用抗生素；避免咳嗽與感冒藥。
  • Home: cool vaporizer, cool night air, or open freezer air.居家：冷霧機、夜間冷空氣或冷凍庫開門冷空氣。
  • Bronchitis: usually viral; dry hacking cough worse at night becoming productive in 2-3 days; supportive care, cool humidified air, fluids.支氣管炎：多為病毒性；夜間加劇的乾咳，2–3 天後轉為有痰；支持療法、涼濕空氣、補水。

3Bronchiolitis and RSV細支氣管炎與 RSV

  • RSV is the common cause of bronchiolitis; spread by droplet and contact; fall to spring; worst under 6 months; high-risk: chronic disease, immunocompromised.RSV 為常見病因；經飛沫與接觸傳播；秋至春季；6 個月以下最嚴重；高危險：慢性病、免疫低下。
  • Signs: rhinorrhea, cough, wheezing, fever; progression to air hunger, retractions, cyanosis; severe: rate over 70/min, apnea, decreased breath sounds, listlessness.徵象：流鼻水、咳嗽、喘鳴、發燒；進展至空氣飢餓、凹陷、發紺；嚴重：呼吸 >70/分、呼吸暫停、呼吸音減弱、嗜睡。
  • Single room, contact + droplet + standard precautions; don't assign nurse to other high-risk children.單人房、接觸+飛沫+標準防護；照護者勿同時照顧其他高危兒。
  • Position 30-40 degrees with neck slightly extended; humidified O2, pulse oximetry, fluids, suction before feeding; palivizumab for high-risk infants.擺位抬高 30–40 度、頸稍伸展；濕化氧氣、血氧監測、補水、餵食前抽吸；高危嬰兒給 palivizumab。
  • No honey cough remedies under 1 year (botulism); cough suppressants used cautiously.1 歲以下不可用含蜂蜜止咳品（肉毒桿菌）；止咳藥謹慎使用。
  • Table 36.1: cannula 22-40% (can eat/talk), mask higher, tent 30-50% (access poor), hood/face tent up to 100%.表 36.1：鼻導管 22–40%（可進食說話）、面罩較高、氧氣帳 30–50%（接近困難）、氧罩/面罩最高 100%。

4Pneumonia肺炎

  • Viral most common; mycoplasma/chlamydia (atypical) ages 5-12 fall-winter; bacterial often needs hospitalization; aspiration pneumonia from food/secretions; pneumococcal vaccine prevents.病毒性最常見；黴漿菌/披衣菌（非典型）5–12 歲、秋冬；細菌性常需住院；吸入性肺炎由食物/分泌物引起；肺炎鏈球菌疫苗可預防。
  • Bacterial: abrupt fever, infant irritability/poor feeding/cyanosis; older child chest pain, headache; diminished breath sounds, crackles.細菌性：突發發燒；嬰兒易怒、餵食差、發紺；較大兒童胸痛、頭痛；呼吸音減弱、囉音。
  • Blood cultures then antibiotics (IV in hospital), O2 and pulse oximetry, cool humidified air, suction, CPT every 4 hours, bed rest, lie on affected side, fluids carefully.先抽血培養再給抗生素（住院多靜脈）、給氧與血氧監測、涼濕空氣、抽吸、每 4 小時胸腔物理治療、臥床休息、患側臥、小心補水。
  • Monitor temperature (febrile seizures); isolation for pneumococcal/staph; chest tube for empyema; thoracentesis.監測體溫（熱性痙攣）；肺炎鏈球菌/葡萄球菌需隔離；膿胸放胸管；胸腔穿刺。
  • Viral/atypical pneumonia: symptomatic care, recovery 7-10 days for atypical.病毒性/非典型肺炎：症狀治療，非典型約 7–10 天恢復。

5Asthma氣喘

  • Chronic airway inflammation with bronchospasm, mucus, edema; early and late reactions; triggers in Box 36.4 (allergens, smoke, cold air, exercise, infection, emotions, aspirin/NSAIDs, beta blockers, sulfites).氣道慢性發炎，支氣管痙攣、黏液、水腫；有立即與延遲反應；誘發因子見框 36.4（過敏原、菸、冷空氣、運動、感染、情緒、阿斯匹靈/NSAID、乙型阻斷劑、亞硫酸鹽）。
  • Attack: wheeze, cough, dyspnea, retractions, prolonged expiration, short phrases, tripod posture; SILENT CHEST and ineffective cough mean severe obstruction; status asthmaticus unresponsive to beta-agonists is an emergency.發作：喘鳴、咳嗽、呼吸困難、凹陷、呼氣延長、短句說話、三腳架姿勢；「無聲胸」與無效咳嗽代表嚴重阻塞；對乙型作用劑無反應的重積性氣喘為急症。
  • Severity (Table 36.2): mild intermittent, mild persistent, moderate persistent, severe persistent by symptom frequency and FEV1 (over 80%, over 80%, 60-80%, 60% or less).嚴重度（表 36.2）：輕度間歇、輕度持續、中度持續、重度持續；依症狀頻率與 FEV1（>80%、>80%、60–80%、≤60%）。
  • Quick-relief: SABA, anticholinergics, systemic steroids. Controllers: inhaled steroids, LABA (never alone), leukotriene modifiers, cromolyn, monoclonal antibody.速效藥：SABA、抗膽鹼、全身性類固醇。控制藥：吸入類固醇、LABA（不可單用）、白三烯調節劑、cromolyn、單株抗體。
  • Use a spacer with inhaled steroids, rinse mouth (thrush); monitor growth; peak flow personal best; decreased PEF warns of exacerbation; CPT not during acute attack.吸入類固醇使用吸藥輔助器並漱口（預防鵝口瘡）；監測生長；峰值流速個人最佳值；PEF 下降警示惡化；急性發作時不做胸腔物理治療。
  • Acute ED care: humidified O2, rescue meds, IV access, corticosteroids, chest x-ray, ABG as needed.急診處置：濕化氧氣、速效藥、建立靜脈、類固醇、胸部 X 光、必要時動脈血氣。
  • Home: remove allergens, avoid cold air and infections, annual flu vaccine from 6 months, inform school.居家：移除過敏原、避免冷空氣與感染、6 個月起每年流感疫苗、通知學校。

6Cystic fibrosis囊性纖維化

  • Autosomal recessive exocrine gland disorder: thick mucus obstructs lungs, pancreas, intestines, reproductive tract; high sweat sodium and chloride; CFTR modulators exist.體染色體隱性外分泌腺疾病：濃稠黏液阻塞肺、胰、腸、生殖道；汗液鈉氯升高；有 CFTR 調節藥。
  • Sweat chloride: under 40 mEq/L normal; over 60 positive; 40-60 repeat (over 40 diagnostic under 3 months). Also newborn screening (IRT), 72-hour stool fat.汗液氯：<40 mEq/L 正常；>60 陽性；40–60 需重測（3 個月以下 >40 即診斷）。另有新生兒篩檢（IRT）、72 小時糞便脂肪。
  • Lungs: chronic infection, wheeze, clubbing, barrel chest, pneumonia, pulmonary hypertension/cor pulmonale, pneumothorax, hemoptysis. GI: meconium ileus (earliest), frothy foul stools, DIOS, FTT, low vitamins A D E K, rectal prolapse, diabetes risk.肺部：慢性感染、喘鳴、杵狀指、桶狀胸、肺炎、肺高壓/肺心症、氣胸、咳血。腸胃：胎便性腸阻塞（最早）、泡沫惡臭便、遠端腸阻塞症候群、生長遲滯、維生素 A D E K 缺乏、直腸脫垂、糖尿病風險。
  • Skin tastes salty; risk of dehydration in heat; delayed puberty, infertility.皮膚嚐起來鹹；高溫易脫水；青春期延遲、不孕。
  • Respiratory care: CPT on waking and evening (not right after meals), bronchodilator before CPT, huffing, Flutter, vest, exercise, antibiotics; careful with oxygen (CO2 retention).呼吸照護：起床與晚間胸腔物理治療（餐後不做）、治療前給支氣管擴張劑、哈氣技巧、Flutter、背心、運動、抗生素；給氧小心（CO2 滯留）。
  • GI care: high calorie/protein diet, extra vitamins, pancreatic enzymes within 30 minutes of meals and snacks (not NPO), do not crush enteric-coated capsules; extra salt in heat.腸胃照護：高熱量高蛋白飲食、補充維生素、餐點與點心 30 分鐘內給胰臟酵素（禁食不給）、腸溶膠囊不可壓碎；高溫時多補鹽。

7SIDS嬰兒猝死症

  • Unexplained death of infant under 1 year, peak 2-4 months, winter, higher in males; lower with breastfeeding and pacifier use.1 歲以下嬰兒不明原因死亡，高峰 2–4 個月、冬季、男嬰較多；哺乳與使用奶嘴者較低。
  • Risks: prone sleep, soft bedding, sofa sleeping, overheating, co-sleeping, maternal smoking/substance use, tobacco smoke exposure.危險因子：趴睡、柔軟寢具、睡沙發、過熱、同床、母親吸菸/物質濫用、二手菸。
  • Prevention: supine for sleep, firm mattress, no toys or loose items in crib; tummy time while awake and varied head position to prevent plagiocephaly.預防：仰睡、硬床墊、嬰兒床不放玩具或鬆軟物；清醒時趴玩並變換頭位以預防斜頭畸形。
  • Support grieving parents.支持悲傷的父母。

8Foreign body aspiration and tuberculosis異物吸入與結核病

  • Round foods (hot dogs, grapes, peanuts, popcorn, candy) are common; signs choking, cough, stridor, wheeze, cyanosis; abdominal thrusts per AHA guidelines; endoscopic removal then humidified air.圓形食物（熱狗、葡萄、花生、爆米花、糖果）常見；徵象嗆咳、喘鳴、發紺；依 AHA 指引腹部推擠；內視鏡取出後給濕化空氣。
  • Prevention: keep small objects/balloons away, avoid small round foods; teach caregivers emergency measures.預防：小物件/氣球放遠、避免小而圓的食物；教導照顧者緊急處理。
  • TB: droplet transmission, mainly from household contact; Mantoux positive 2-12 weeks after infection, does not confirm active disease; sputum or gastric washings culture is definitive; don't test with measles vaccine.結核：飛沫傳染，多來自家人接觸；感染後 2–12 週 Mantoux 陽性，不代表活動性；痰或胃洗液培養為確診；不與麻疹疫苗同時測試。
  • Latent TB: isoniazid 9 months (12 if HIV). Active: isoniazid + rifampin + pyrazinamide 2 months then isoniazid + rifampin 4 months; DOT; orange-red body fluids; N95 and respiratory isolation.潛伏結核：isoniazid 9 個月（HIV 為 12 個月）。活動性：isoniazid + rifampin + pyrazinamide 2 個月，再 isoniazid + rifampin 4 個月；直接觀察治療；體液橘紅色；N95 與呼吸道隔離。

  • Chronic airway inflammation with bronchospasm, mucus, edema; early and late reactions; triggers in Box 36.4 (allergens, smoke, cold air, exercise, infection, emotions, aspirin/NSAIDs, beta blockers, sulfites).氣道慢性發炎，支氣管痙攣、黏液、水腫；有立即與延遲反應；誘發因子見框 36.4（過敏原、菸、冷空氣、運動、感染、情緒、阿斯匹靈/NSAID、乙型阻斷劑、亞硫酸鹽）。
  • Attack: wheeze, cough, dyspnea, retractions, prolonged expiration, short phrases, tripod posture; SILENT CHEST and ineffective cough mean severe obstruction; status asthmaticus unresponsive to beta-agonists is an emergency.發作：喘鳴、咳嗽、呼吸困難、凹陷、呼氣延長、短句說話、三腳架姿勢；「無聲胸」與無效咳嗽代表嚴重阻塞；對乙型作用劑無反應的重積性氣喘為急症。
  • Severity (Table 36.2): mild intermittent, mild persistent, moderate persistent, severe persistent by symptom frequency and FEV1 (over 80%, over 80%, 60-80%, 60% or less).嚴重度（表 36.2）：輕度間歇、輕度持續、中度持續、重度持續；依症狀頻率與 FEV1（>80%、>80%、60–80%、≤60%）。
  • Quick-relief: SABA, anticholinergics, systemic steroids. Controllers: inhaled steroids, LABA (never alone), leukotriene modifiers, cromolyn, monoclonal antibody.速效藥：SABA、抗膽鹼、全身性類固醇。控制藥：吸入類固醇、LABA（不可單用）、白三烯調節劑、cromolyn、單株抗體。
  • Use a spacer with inhaled steroids, rinse mouth (thrush); monitor growth; peak flow personal best; decreased PEF warns of exacerbation; CPT not during acute attack.吸入類固醇使用吸藥輔助器並漱口（預防鵝口瘡）；監測生長；峰值流速個人最佳值；PEF 下降警示惡化；急性發作時不做胸腔物理治療。
  • Acute ED care: humidified O2, rescue meds, IV access, corticosteroids, chest x-ray, ABG as needed.急診處置：濕化氧氣、速效藥、建立靜脈、類固醇、胸部 X 光、必要時動脈血氣。
  • Home: remove allergens, avoid cold air and infections, annual flu vaccine from 6 months, inform school.居家：移除過敏原、避免冷空氣與感染、6 個月起每年流感疫苗、通知學校。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Cystic fibrosis囊性纖維化
  • Autosomal recessive exocrine gland disorder: thick mucus obstructs lungs, pancreas, intestines, reproductive tract; high sweat sodium and chloride; CFTR modulators exist.體染色體隱性外分泌腺疾病：濃稠黏液阻塞肺、胰、腸、生殖道；汗液鈉氯升高；有 CFTR 調節藥。
  • Sweat chloride: under 40 mEq/L normal; over 60 positive; 40-60 repeat (over 40 diagnostic under 3 months). Also newborn screening (IRT), 72-hour stool fat.汗液氯：<40 mEq/L 正常；>60 陽性；40–60 需重測（3 個月以下 >40 即診斷）。另有新生兒篩檢（IRT）、72 小時糞便脂肪。
  • Lungs: chronic infection, wheeze, clubbing, barrel chest, pneumonia, pulmonary hypertension/cor pulmonale, pneumothorax, hemoptysis. GI: meconium ileus (earliest), frothy foul stools, DIOS, FTT, low vitamins A D E K, rectal prolapse, diabetes risk.肺部：慢性感染、喘鳴、杵狀指、桶狀胸、肺炎、肺高壓/肺心症、氣胸、咳血。腸胃：胎便性腸阻塞（最早）、泡沫惡臭便、遠端腸阻塞症候群、生長遲滯、維生素 A D E K 缺乏、直腸脫垂、糖尿病風險。
  • Skin tastes salty; risk of dehydration in heat; delayed puberty, infertility.皮膚嚐起來鹹；高溫易脫水；青春期延遲、不孕。
  • Respiratory care: CPT on waking and evening (not right after meals), bronchodilator before CPT, huffing, Flutter, vest, exercise, antibiotics; careful with oxygen (CO2 retention).呼吸照護：起床與晚間胸腔物理治療（餐後不做）、治療前給支氣管擴張劑、哈氣技巧、Flutter、背心、運動、抗生素；給氧小心（CO2 滯留）。
  • GI care: high calorie/protein diet, extra vitamins, pancreatic enzymes within 30 minutes of meals and snacks (not NPO), do not crush enteric-coated capsules; extra salt in heat.腸胃照護：高熱量高蛋白飲食、補充維生素、餐點與點心 30 分鐘內給胰臟酵素（禁食不給）、腸溶膠囊不可壓碎；高溫時多補鹽。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
SIDS嬰兒猝死症
  • Unexplained death of infant under 1 year, peak 2-4 months, winter, higher in males; lower with breastfeeding and pacifier use.1 歲以下嬰兒不明原因死亡，高峰 2–4 個月、冬季、男嬰較多；哺乳與使用奶嘴者較低。
  • Risks: prone sleep, soft bedding, sofa sleeping, overheating, co-sleeping, maternal smoking/substance use, tobacco smoke exposure.危險因子：趴睡、柔軟寢具、睡沙發、過熱、同床、母親吸菸/物質濫用、二手菸。
  • Prevention: supine for sleep, firm mattress, no toys or loose items in crib; tummy time while awake and varied head position to prevent plagiocephaly.預防：仰睡、硬床墊、嬰兒床不放玩具或鬆軟物；清醒時趴玩並變換頭位以預防斜頭畸形。
  • Support grieving parents.支持悲傷的父母。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Foreign body aspiration and tuberculosis異物吸入與結核病
  • Round foods (hot dogs, grapes, peanuts, popcorn, candy) are common; signs choking, cough, stridor, wheeze, cyanosis; abdominal thrusts per AHA guidelines; endoscopic removal then humidified air.圓形食物（熱狗、葡萄、花生、爆米花、糖果）常見；徵象嗆咳、喘鳴、發紺；依 AHA 指引腹部推擠；內視鏡取出後給濕化空氣。
  • Prevention: keep small objects/balloons away, avoid small round foods; teach caregivers emergency measures.預防：小物件/氣球放遠、避免小而圓的食物；教導照顧者緊急處理。
  • TB: droplet transmission, mainly from household contact; Mantoux positive 2-12 weeks after infection, does not confirm active disease; sputum or gastric washings culture is definitive; don't test with measles vaccine.結核：飛沫傳染，多來自家人接觸；感染後 2–12 週 Mantoux 陽性，不代表活動性；痰或胃洗液培養為確診；不與麻疹疫苗同時測試。
  • Latent TB: isoniazid 9 months (12 if HIV). Active: isoniazid + rifampin + pyrazinamide 2 months then isoniazid + rifampin 4 months; DOT; orange-red body fluids; N95 and respiratory isolation.潛伏結核：isoniazid 9 個月（HIV 為 12 個月）。活動性：isoniazid + rifampin + pyrazinamide 2 個月，再 isoniazid + rifampin 4 個月；直接觀察治療；體液橘紅色；N95 與呼吸道隔離。
