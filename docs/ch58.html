Summary重點摘要Test-critical考試關鍵Mnemonics記憶口訣

Covers correct instillation of eye and ear medications and the drug classes used: mydriatics/cycloplegics, anti-infectives, anti-inflammatories, topical anesthetics, lubricants, glaucoma drugs, macular degeneration drugs, otic preparations and ototoxic drugs. Know each class's action, adverse effects, contraindications and the key safety teaching.涵蓋眼藥與耳藥的正確給藥方式，以及散瞳/睫狀肌麻痺劑、抗感染藥、消炎藥、局部麻醉劑、潤滑劑、青光眼藥物、黃斑部病變藥物、耳用製劑與耳毒性藥物。需熟記各類作用、不良反應、禁忌與重要衛教。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Ophthalmic administration眼用藥物給藥

  • Wash hands, wear gloves, place drops in the lower conjunctival sac (never on the cornea), do not touch the dropper to the eye; separate bottle per client.洗手、戴手套，將藥液滴入下結膜囊(不可直接滴在角膜)，滴管不可碰到眼睛；每位病人專用藥瓶。
  • After instilling, press the inner canthus/nasolacrimal duct 30-60 seconds and close eye gently (do not squeeze) to reduce systemic absorption.滴藥後壓住內眼角/鼻淚管30-60秒並輕閉眼(勿用力擠眼)，以減少全身吸收。
  • Order: drops before ointment; glucocorticoid before other drugs; wait 3-5 min between drops of the same drug and 5-10 min between different drugs.順序：先滴眼藥水再擠藥膏；類固醇先於其他藥物；同藥多滴間隔3-5分鐘，不同藥物間隔5-10分鐘。
  • Ointment: thin ribbon along lower conjunctival sac from inner to outer canthus, tube not touching eye; vision blurs, best at bedtime.藥膏：沿下結膜囊由內眥向外眥擠一細條，管口不碰眼睛；會暫時視力模糊，最好睡前使用。
  • Monitor pulse and BP with ophthalmic beta blockers; mydriatic/cycloplegic use means no driving; sunglasses for photophobia; atropine drops in infants may cause abdominal distention.使用眼用β阻斷劑需監測脈搏與血壓；散瞳/睫狀肌麻痺後不可開車；畏光戴太陽眼鏡；嬰兒使用atropine眼藥可能造成腹脹。
  • Older client with tremor: involve caregiver, home care nurse or adaptive bottle-positioning device. Missed dose: take when remembered unless next dose due in 1-2 h.手抖的老年人：請照顧者、居家護理師協助或使用瓶口定位輔具。漏用：想起即用，除非1-2小時內需下一次用藥。

2Mydriatics, cycloplegics and anticholinergics散瞳劑、睫狀肌麻痺劑與抗膽鹼藥

  • Action: dilate the pupil (mydriasis) and paralyze the ciliary muscle (cycloplegia) for exams and surgery. Examples: atropine, cyclopentolate, homatropine, phenylephrine, scopolamine, tropicamide.作用：散瞳並麻痺睫狀肌，用於檢查與手術。例：atropine、cyclopentolate、homatropine、phenylephrine、scopolamine、tropicamide。
  • Side/adverse effects: tachycardia, photophobia, conjunctivitis, dermatitis, raised BP; a temporary burning on instillation is also expected. Atropine toxicity: dry mouth, blurred vision, fever, tachycardia, urinary retention, constipation, confusion, hallucinations, worsening glaucoma.副作用/不良反應：心搏過速、畏光、結膜炎、皮膚炎、血壓上升；滴藥時也可能有暫時性灼熱感。Atropine中毒：口乾、視力模糊、發燒、心搏過速、尿滯留、便秘、混亂、幻覺、青光眼惡化。
  • Contraindicated in glaucoma, cardiac dysrhythmias, or cerebral atherosclerosis (risk of raised IOP/cardiac strain); use with caution in older clients and those with prostatic hypertrophy, diabetes, or parkinsonism.青光眼、心律不整或腦動脈硬化病人禁用(升高眼壓/心臟負擔風險)；老年人、攝護腺肥大、糖尿病或帕金森氏症者須小心使用。
  • Teach: no driving or hazardous activity for 24 h, sunglasses, report eye pain, vision loss, flushing, difficulty breathing.衛教：24小時內不可開車或從事危險活動、戴太陽眼鏡、眼痛、視力喪失、潮紅或呼吸困難需通報。

3Anti-infective, anti-inflammatory, anesthetic and lubricant eye drugs抗感染、消炎、麻醉與潤滑眼藥

  • Anti-infectives (bacitracin, erythromycin, gentamicin, tobramycin, moxifloxacin, natamycin antifungal, ganciclovir antiviral, sulfacetamide): superinfection, local irritation; complete the course, clean exudate first, hand hygiene.抗感染藥(bacitracin、erythromycin、gentamicin、tobramycin、moxifloxacin、抗黴菌natamycin、抗病毒ganciclovir、sulfacetamide)：重複感染、局部刺激；需完成療程、先清除分泌物、勤洗手。
  • Anti-inflammatories: corticosteroids (dexamethasone, prednisolone), NSAIDs (ketorolac, diclofenac, nepafenac), mast cell stabilizers (cromolyn, olopatadine). Steroid effects: cataracts, raised IOP/glaucoma, delayed healing, masks infection; not for abrasions or wounds.消炎藥：皮質類固醇(dexamethasone、prednisolone)、NSAIDs(ketorolac、diclofenac、nepafenac)、肥大細胞穩定劑(cromolyn、olopatadine)。類固醇副作用：白內障、眼壓升高/青光眼、延遲癒合、掩蓋感染；不可用於角膜擦傷或傷口。
  • Topical anesthetics (tetracaine): stinging, loss of corneal blink reflex; never for home or self use; do not rub eye; patch until reflex returns.局部麻醉劑(tetracaine)：刺痛、角膜眨眼反射消失；不可居家或自行使用；勿揉眼；以眼罩保護至反射恢復。
  • Lubricants (carboxymethylcellulose, hydroxypropyl methylcellulose, polyvinyl alcohol, petrolatum ointment): for dry eye, unconscious or anesthetized clients; may burn on instillation; watch for preservative allergy.潤滑劑(carboxymethylcellulose、hydroxypropyl methylcellulose、polyvinyl alcohol、凡士林藥膏)：用於乾眼、昏迷或麻醉病人；滴入時可能灼熱；留意防腐劑過敏。

4Glaucoma: miotics and beta blockers青光眼：縮瞳劑與β阻斷劑

  • Miotics/cholinergic agonists (pilocarpine, carbachol, echothiophate): constrict pupil, contract ciliary muscle, increase aqueous outflow. Side effects: myopia, headache, eye pain, poor vision in dim light.縮瞳劑/膽鹼促效劑(pilocarpine、carbachol、echothiophate)：縮瞳、睫狀肌收縮、增加房水排出。副作用：近視、頭痛、眼痛、暗處視力差。
  • Systemic cholinergic effects: flushing, sweating, salivation, diarrhea, urinary frequency, bronchospasm; toxicity: bradycardia, hypotension, syncope, seizures. Antidote: atropine sulfate.全身膽鹼效應：潮紅、流汗、流涎、腹瀉、頻尿、支氣管痙攣；中毒：心搏過緩、低血壓、昏厥、癲癇。解毒劑：atropine sulfate。
  • Contraindicated with retinal detachment, iris-lens adhesions, inflammation; caution with asthma, bradycardia, ulcers, parkinsonism.視網膜剝離、虹膜與水晶體沾黏、發炎時禁用；氣喘、心搏過緩、潰瘍、帕金森氏症者小心。
  • Beta blockers (timolol, betaxolol, carteolol, levobunolol, metipranolol): reduce aqueous production without changing pupil size.β阻斷劑(timolol、betaxolol、carteolol、levobunolol、metipranolol)：減少房水生成，不影響瞳孔大小。
  • Beta blocker risks: bradycardia, hypotension, bronchospasm; contraindicated in asthma/COPD; hold and call if pulse <=60 or systolic BP <90; may mask hypoglycemia in diabetics.β阻斷劑風險：心搏過緩、低血壓、支氣管痙攣；氣喘/COPD禁用；脈搏≦60或收縮壓<90應暫停並通報；糖尿病人可能掩蓋低血糖。
  • Do not stop glaucoma drugs abruptly; change positions slowly.不可突然停用青光眼藥物；改變姿勢要緩慢。

5Glaucoma: carbonic anhydrase inhibitors, osmotics, other agents青光眼：碳酸酐酶抑制劑、滲透劑與其他藥物

  • Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors (dorzolamide, brinzolamide; oral acetazolamide) decrease aqueous humor formation; contraindicated in sulfonamide allergy; caution in severe renal/liver disease.碳酸酐酶抑制劑(dorzolamide、brinzolamide；口服acetazolamide)減少房水生成；磺胺過敏禁用；嚴重腎/肝病者小心。
  • Adverse effects: paresthesias, GI upset, polyuria, hypokalemia, renal calculi, photosensitivity, lethargy, depression. Monitor K+, I&O, weight; increase fluids; no contact lenses during or within 15 minutes of drops.不良反應：感覺異常、腸胃不適、多尿、低血鉀、腎結石、光敏感、嗜睡、憂鬱。監測鉀、輸出入量、體重；增加水分；滴藥期間及滴後15分鐘內勿戴隱形眼鏡。
  • Ocusert: pilocarpine wafer replaced every 7 days, stored in refrigerator, check presence at bedtime and on arising; may leak, migrate or be lost.Ocusert：pilocarpine藥片每7天更換、冷藏保存、睡前與起床時檢查是否在位；可能漏藥、移位或遺失。
  • Osmotic agents (mannitol): emergency IOP reduction and peri-operative; headache, N/V, dehydration, disorientation, electrolyte imbalance; monitor I&O, weight, orientation.滲透劑(mannitol)：緊急降眼壓及手術前後使用；頭痛、噁心嘔吐、脫水、定向力障礙、電解質失衡；監測輸出入量、體重與定向力。
  • Other glaucoma agents in Box 58.5: prostaglandin analogs (latanoprost, bimatoprost, travoprost), alpha-2 agonists (brimonidine, apraclonidine), rho kinase inhibitor (netarsudil).方塊58.5其他青光眼藥物：前列腺素類似物(latanoprost、bimatoprost、travoprost)、α2促效劑(brimonidine、apraclonidine)、Rho激酶抑制劑(netarsudil)。

6Macular degeneration medications黃斑部病變藥物

  • Wet (neovascular) ARMD is faster and treated with anti-VEGF agents (ranibizumab, bevacizumab, aflibercept, pegaptanib) and verteporfin photodynamic therapy; dry ARMD is more common and slow.濕性(新生血管)黃斑部病變進展較快，使用抗VEGF藥物(ranibizumab、bevacizumab、aflibercept、pegaptanib)與verteporfin光動力療法；乾性較常見且進展慢。
  • Adverse effects: endophthalmitis, blurred vision, cataract, corneal edema, conjunctival hemorrhage, raised IOP, reduced acuity.不良反應：眼內炎、視力模糊、白內障、角膜水腫、結膜出血、眼壓升高、視力下降。
  • Teach administration and when to notify the provider (pain, vision loss, redness).教導用藥方式及何時通報(疼痛、視力喪失、紅腫)。

7Otic administration and ototoxic drugs耳用給藥與耳毒性藥物

  • Adults: pull pinna up and back; children under 3 y: down and back (older children up and back). Tilt the head away from the affected ear, instill the drops, then gently rock the head back and forth about 5 times to help the medication travel down the canal.成人：耳廓向上向後拉；3歲以下兒童：向下向後拉(年齡較大兒童則向上向後拉)。頭偏向健側後滴入藥液，再輕輕前後搖動頭部約5次，幫助藥液流到耳道深處。
  • Irrigation requires a prescription and visible tympanic membrane; warm to 37 C (98.6 F); aim at canal wall; client ear up with emesis basin and fall precautions; never irrigate if perforation suspected.沖洗需有醫囑且可見鼓膜；加溫至37°C(98.6°F)；朝耳道壁沖；病人患耳朝上，備嘔吐盆與防跌；懷疑穿孔時禁止沖洗。
  • Ototoxic drugs: aminoglycosides (gentamicin, amikacin, streptomycin, tobramycin, neomycin), vancomycin, loop diuretics (furosemide, ethacrynic acid), cisplatin, aspirin, NSAIDs, quinine, sildenafil.耳毒性藥物：胺基糖苷類(gentamicin、amikacin、streptomycin、tobramycin、neomycin)、vancomycin、袢利尿劑(furosemide、ethacrynic acid)、cisplatin、阿斯匹靈、NSAIDs、quinine、sildenafil。
  • Anti-infective ear drops (ofloxacin, ciprofloxacin, gentamicin otic, acetic acid): overgrowth of resistant organisms; keep ears dry; finish course; report dizziness, fever, sore throat.抗感染耳藥(ofloxacin、ciprofloxacin、gentamicin耳劑、acetic acid)：抗藥菌過度生長；保持耳朵乾燥；完成療程；頭暈、發燒、喉嚨痛需通報。

8Antihistamines, decongestants and ceruminolytics抗組織胺、解充血劑與耳垢軟化劑

  • Antihistamines/decongestants reduce mucosal edema to open the eustachian tube; drowsiness, blurred vision, dry mouth; hypertensive clients check with provider first.抗組織胺/解充血劑減輕黏膜水腫以打開耳咽管；嗜睡、視力模糊、口乾；高血壓病人需先詢問醫師。
  • Carbamide peroxide emulsifies cerumen; irritation, redness, swelling; insert cotton plug after drops; irrigate gently with warm water 30 minutes later; mineral oil for chronic impaction.Carbamide peroxide乳化耳垢；刺激、紅腫；滴藥後塞棉球；30分鐘後以溫水輕柔沖洗；慢性耳垢可用礦物油。
  • Do not exceed prescribed frequency; avoid touching ear with dropper; notify provider if redness, pain, or swelling persists.勿超過處方頻率；滴管勿碰耳朵；紅腫疼痛持續需通報。

Glaucoma: miotics and beta blockers青光眼：縮瞳劑與β阻斷劑
  • Miotics/cholinergic agonists (pilocarpine, carbachol, echothiophate): constrict pupil, contract ciliary muscle, increase aqueous outflow. Side effects: myopia, headache, eye pain, poor vision in dim light.縮瞳劑/膽鹼促效劑(pilocarpine、carbachol、echothiophate)：縮瞳、睫狀肌收縮、增加房水排出。副作用：近視、頭痛、眼痛、暗處視力差。
  • Systemic cholinergic effects: flushing, sweating, salivation, diarrhea, urinary frequency, bronchospasm; toxicity: bradycardia, hypotension, syncope, seizures. Antidote: atropine sulfate.全身膽鹼效應：潮紅、流汗、流涎、腹瀉、頻尿、支氣管痙攣；中毒：心搏過緩、低血壓、昏厥、癲癇。解毒劑：atropine sulfate。
  • Contraindicated with retinal detachment, iris-lens adhesions, inflammation; caution with asthma, bradycardia, ulcers, parkinsonism.視網膜剝離、虹膜與水晶體沾黏、發炎時禁用；氣喘、心搏過緩、潰瘍、帕金森氏症者小心。
  • Beta blockers (timolol, betaxolol, carteolol, levobunolol, metipranolol): reduce aqueous production without changing pupil size.β阻斷劑(timolol、betaxolol、carteolol、levobunolol、metipranolol)：減少房水生成，不影響瞳孔大小。
  • Beta blocker risks: bradycardia, hypotension, bronchospasm; contraindicated in asthma/COPD; hold and call if pulse <=60 or systolic BP <90; may mask hypoglycemia in diabetics.β阻斷劑風險：心搏過緩、低血壓、支氣管痙攣；氣喘/COPD禁用；脈搏≦60或收縮壓<90應暫停並通報；糖尿病人可能掩蓋低血糖。
  • Do not stop glaucoma drugs abruptly; change positions slowly.不可突然停用青光眼藥物；改變姿勢要緩慢。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Glaucoma: carbonic anhydrase inhibitors, osmotics, other agents青光眼：碳酸酐酶抑制劑、滲透劑與其他藥物
  • Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors (dorzolamide, brinzolamide; oral acetazolamide) decrease aqueous humor formation; contraindicated in sulfonamide allergy; caution in severe renal/liver disease.碳酸酐酶抑制劑(dorzolamide、brinzolamide；口服acetazolamide)減少房水生成；磺胺過敏禁用；嚴重腎/肝病者小心。
  • Adverse effects: paresthesias, GI upset, polyuria, hypokalemia, renal calculi, photosensitivity, lethargy, depression. Monitor K+, I&O, weight; increase fluids; no contact lenses during or within 15 minutes of drops.不良反應：感覺異常、腸胃不適、多尿、低血鉀、腎結石、光敏感、嗜睡、憂鬱。監測鉀、輸出入量、體重；增加水分；滴藥期間及滴後15分鐘內勿戴隱形眼鏡。
  • Ocusert: pilocarpine wafer replaced every 7 days, stored in refrigerator, check presence at bedtime and on arising; may leak, migrate or be lost.Ocusert：pilocarpine藥片每7天更換、冷藏保存、睡前與起床時檢查是否在位；可能漏藥、移位或遺失。
  • Osmotic agents (mannitol): emergency IOP reduction and peri-operative; headache, N/V, dehydration, disorientation, electrolyte imbalance; monitor I&O, weight, orientation.滲透劑(mannitol)：緊急降眼壓及手術前後使用；頭痛、噁心嘔吐、脫水、定向力障礙、電解質失衡；監測輸出入量、體重與定向力。
  • Other glaucoma agents in Box 58.5: prostaglandin analogs (latanoprost, bimatoprost, travoprost), alpha-2 agonists (brimonidine, apraclonidine), rho kinase inhibitor (netarsudil).方塊58.5其他青光眼藥物：前列腺素類似物(latanoprost、bimatoprost、travoprost)、α2促效劑(brimonidine、apraclonidine)、Rho激酶抑制劑(netarsudil)。

Macular degeneration medications黃斑部病變藥物
  • Wet (neovascular) ARMD is faster and treated with anti-VEGF agents (ranibizumab, bevacizumab, aflibercept, pegaptanib) and verteporfin photodynamic therapy; dry ARMD is more common and slow.濕性(新生血管)黃斑部病變進展較快，使用抗VEGF藥物(ranibizumab、bevacizumab、aflibercept、pegaptanib)與verteporfin光動力療法；乾性較常見且進展慢。
  • Adverse effects: endophthalmitis, blurred vision, cataract, corneal edema, conjunctival hemorrhage, raised IOP, reduced acuity.不良反應：眼內炎、視力模糊、白內障、角膜水腫、結膜出血、眼壓升高、視力下降。
  • Teach administration and when to notify the provider (pain, vision loss, redness).教導用藥方式及何時通報(疼痛、視力喪失、紅腫)。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Otic administration and ototoxic drugs耳用給藥與耳毒性藥物
  • Adults: pull pinna up and back; children under 3 y: down and back (older children up and back). Tilt the head away from the affected ear, instill the drops, then gently rock the head back and forth about 5 times to help the medication travel down the canal.成人：耳廓向上向後拉；3歲以下兒童：向下向後拉(年齡較大兒童則向上向後拉)。頭偏向健側後滴入藥液，再輕輕前後搖動頭部約5次，幫助藥液流到耳道深處。
  • Irrigation requires a prescription and visible tympanic membrane; warm to 37 C (98.6 F); aim at canal wall; client ear up with emesis basin and fall precautions; never irrigate if perforation suspected.沖洗需有醫囑且可見鼓膜；加溫至37°C(98.6°F)；朝耳道壁沖；病人患耳朝上，備嘔吐盆與防跌；懷疑穿孔時禁止沖洗。
  • Ototoxic drugs: aminoglycosides (gentamicin, amikacin, streptomycin, tobramycin, neomycin), vancomycin, loop diuretics (furosemide, ethacrynic acid), cisplatin, aspirin, NSAIDs, quinine, sildenafil.耳毒性藥物：胺基糖苷類(gentamicin、amikacin、streptomycin、tobramycin、neomycin)、vancomycin、袢利尿劑(furosemide、ethacrynic acid)、cisplatin、阿斯匹靈、NSAIDs、quinine、sildenafil。
  • Anti-infective ear drops (ofloxacin, ciprofloxacin, gentamicin otic, acetic acid): overgrowth of resistant organisms; keep ears dry; finish course; report dizziness, fever, sore throat.抗感染耳藥(ofloxacin、ciprofloxacin、gentamicin耳劑、acetic acid)：抗藥菌過度生長；保持耳朵乾燥；完成療程；頭暈、發燒、喉嚨痛需通報。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Antihistamines, decongestants and ceruminolytics抗組織胺、解充血劑與耳垢軟化劑
  • Antihistamines/decongestants reduce mucosal edema to open the eustachian tube; drowsiness, blurred vision, dry mouth; hypertensive clients check with provider first.抗組織胺/解充血劑減輕黏膜水腫以打開耳咽管；嗜睡、視力模糊、口乾；高血壓病人需先詢問醫師。
  • Carbamide peroxide emulsifies cerumen; irritation, redness, swelling; insert cotton plug after drops; irrigate gently with warm water 30 minutes later; mineral oil for chronic impaction.Carbamide peroxide乳化耳垢；刺激、紅腫；滴藥後塞棉球；30分鐘後以溫水輕柔沖洗；慢性耳垢可用礦物油。
  • Do not exceed prescribed frequency; avoid touching ear with dropper; notify provider if redness, pain, or swelling persists.勿超過處方頻率；滴管勿碰耳朵；紅腫疼痛持續需通報。

  1. Instill eye drops in the lower conjunctival sac, never on the cornea; press nasolacrimal duct 30-60 s to limit systemic absorption.眼藥水滴在下結膜囊，不可滴在角膜；壓迫鼻淚管30-60秒以減少全身吸收。

  2. Give eye drops before ointment; separate different eye drugs by 5-10 minutes; glucocorticoid first.先滴眼藥水再擠藥膏；不同眼藥間隔5-10分鐘；類固醇先給。

  3. Mydriatics/cycloplegics are contraindicated in glaucoma (raise IOP); no driving 24 h.散瞳/睫狀肌麻痺劑青光眼禁用(升高眼壓)；24小時內不可開車。

  5. Ophthalmic beta blockers: check pulse and BP; hold if pulse <=60 or SBP <90; contraindicated in asthma/COPD.眼用β阻斷劑：測脈搏與血壓；脈搏≦60或收縮壓<90暫停；氣喘/COPD禁用。

    p.934 · book 845p.935 · book 846
  8. Topical anesthetics are never for home use; protect the anesthetized eye until the blink reflex returns.局部麻醉劑不可居家使用；麻醉後眼睛需保護至眨眼反射恢復。
