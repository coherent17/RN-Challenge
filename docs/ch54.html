Organized by drug class: anticoagulants, thrombolytics, antiplatelets, inotropes, digoxin, diuretics, antihypertensives (alpha blockers, central agents, ACE inhibitors/ARBs, beta blockers, calcium channel blockers, vasodilators), nitrates, antidysrhythmics, adrenergic agonists, and lipid-lowering drugs. For each class know the action, key adverse effects, monitoring parameter, antidote, and teaching.依藥物分類整理:抗凝血劑、血栓溶解劑、抗血小板藥、強心劑、digoxin、利尿劑、降血壓藥(α阻斷劑、中樞作用劑、ACE抑制劑/ARB、β阻斷劑、鈣離子通道阻斷劑、血管擴張劑)、硝酸鹽、抗心律不整藥、腎上腺素促效劑及降血脂藥。每類需掌握作用、主要不良反應、監測指標、解毒劑與衛教。

1Anticoagulants抗凝血劑

  • Prevent clot formation/extension; used in MI, unstable angina, AF, DVT, PE, mechanical valves. Contraindicated with active bleeding (except DIC), bleeding disorders, ulcers, severe liver/kidney disease, hemorrhagic brain injury.預防血栓形成與延伸;用於心肌梗塞、不穩定型心絞痛、心房顫動、DVT、肺栓塞、機械瓣膜。活動性出血(DIC除外)、出血性疾病、潰瘍、嚴重肝腎疾病、出血性腦傷為禁忌。
  • Heparin blocks thrombin conversion of fibrinogen to fibrin; does not dissolve existing clots. Normal aPTT 30-40 s; therapeutic 1.5-2.5 x normal; check every 4-6 hours initially. Antidote: protamine sulfate.Heparin阻止凝血酶將纖維蛋白原轉為纖維蛋白;不會溶解已有血栓。aPTT正常30-40秒,治療目標為正常值1.5-2.5倍;起始時每4-6小時監測。解毒劑:protamine sulfate。
  • Subcutaneous heparin: abdomen, 5/8-inch needle, 90 degrees, do not aspirate or rub. Continuous IV via infusion pump. Monitor platelets (thrombocytopenia).皮下注射heparin:腹部、5/8吋針頭、90度角,不回抽、不按揉。持續靜脈滴注須用輸液幫浦。監測血小板(血小板低下)。
  • Enoxaparin (low-molecular-weight heparin): give to recumbent client in abdominal wall, do not expel air bubble or aspirate; anti-Xa therapeutic 0.5-1.2 IU/mL; antidote protamine. Rivaroxaban is oral once daily.Enoxaparin(低分子量肝素):個案平躺、注射於腹壁,不排除預充針筒氣泡、不回抽;抗Xa治療範圍0.5-1.2 IU/mL;解毒劑protamine。Rivaroxaban為口服每日一次。
  • Warfarin antagonizes vitamin K (factors II, VII, IX, X); monitored by PT (normal 11-12.5 s) and INR (normal 0.81-1.2). Goal INR 2-3 standard, 3-4.5 high-dose. Antidote: phytonadione (vitamin K). Heparin bridge until INR therapeutic.Warfarin拮抗維生素K(因子II、VII、IX、X);以PT(正常11-12.5秒)與INR(正常0.81-1.2)監測。標準目標INR 2-3,高強度3-4.5。解毒劑:phytonadione(維生素K)。INR達標前以heparin銜接。
  • Dabigatran: direct thrombin inhibitor for nonvalvular AF, fixed twice-daily dose, no routine blood monitoring.Dabigatran:直接凝血酶抑制劑,用於非瓣膜性心房顫動,固定每日兩次,無需常規抽血監測。
  • Avoid with anticoagulants: NSAIDs, salicylates, ginkgo/ginseng, corticosteroids, macrolides, fluoroquinolones, phenytoin, sulfonamides, cimetidine, allopurinol, oral hypoglycemics; keep vitamin K intake consistent (green leafy vegetables).與抗凝血劑併用應避免:NSAIDs、水楊酸類、銀杏/人蔘、類固醇、巨環類、氟喹諾酮、phenytoin、磺胺類、cimetidine、allopurinol、口服降血糖藥;維生素K攝取量需穩定(深綠色蔬菜)。

2Thrombolytics and antiplatelets血栓溶解劑與抗血小板藥

  • Thrombolytics (alteplase, reteplase, tenecteplase) activate plasminogen to plasmin; used early in MI (within 4-6 hours of onset), arterial/venous thrombosis, PE, ischemic stroke, occluded catheters.血栓溶解劑(alteplase、reteplase、tenecteplase)活化纖溶酶原成纖溶酶;用於MI早期(發作4-6小時內)、動靜脈血栓、肺栓塞、缺血性中風、導管阻塞。
  • Contraindications: active bleeding, prior hemorrhagic stroke, intracranial problems/surgery within 2 months, major surgery within 10 days, uncontrolled hypertension, prolonged CPR.禁忌:活動性出血、曾有出血性中風、顱內問題/2個月內顱內或脊椎手術、10天內大手術、血壓失控、近期長時間CPR。
  • Main risk is bleeding; also dysrhythmias and allergy. Monitor aPTT, PT, fibrinogen, Hct, platelets, neuro status; avoid injections/venipuncture; direct pressure 20-30 min; handle gently. Antidote: aminocaproic acid.主要風險為出血,另有心律不整與過敏。監測aPTT、PT、纖維蛋白原、血比容、血小板與神經狀態;避免注射/抽血;穿刺處直接加壓20-30分鐘;輕柔搬動。解毒劑:aminocaproic acid。
  • Antiplatelets (aspirin, clopidogrel, ticagrelor, cilostazol, dipyridamole, abciximab, eptifibatide, tirofiban) inhibit aggregation; used after MI, stents, revascularization, stroke. Adverse: bruising, GI bleeding, tarry stools, hematuria.抗血小板藥(aspirin、clopidogrel、ticagrelor、cilostazol、dipyridamole、abciximab、eptifibatide、tirofiban)抑制血小板聚集;用於MI後、支架、血管重建、中風。不良反應:瘀斑、消化道出血、黑便、血尿。

3Inotropes and digoxin強心劑與digoxin

  • IV inotropes (dobutamine, dopamine, milrinone) for short-term advanced heart failure; use infusion pump and continuous ECG; stop if BP drops or dysrhythmias occur; watch potassium, liver enzymes, platelets.靜脈強心劑(dobutamine、dopamine、milrinone)用於晚期心衰竭短期處置;使用輸液幫浦與持續心電圖;血壓下降或出現心律不整時停止;注意鉀、肝酵素、血小板。
  • Digoxin: positive inotrope, negative chronotrope and dromotrope (slows AV conduction); second-line for HF, used for AF/flutter rate control; contraindicated in ventricular dysrhythmias and 2nd/3rd-degree block.Digoxin:正性肌力、負性變時與負性傳導作用(減慢房室傳導);為心衰竭第二線用藥,用於心房顫動/撲動心室率控制;心室心律不整與二、三度房室傳導阻滯禁用。
  • Therapeutic level 0.5-2.0 ng/mL (0.63-2.56 nmol/L); low end preferred. Toxicity: anorexia, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea first, then bradycardia/dysrhythmias and visual changes (blurred, yellow-green vision, halos).治療濃度0.5-2.0 ng/mL(0.63-2.56 nmol/L),偏低端較佳。中毒:先出現食慾不振、噁心、嘔吐、腹瀉,後出現心搏過緩/心律不整與視覺異常(模糊、黃綠視、光暈)。
  • Risk factors: hypokalemia (K below 3.5), hypomagnesemia, hypercalcemia, hypothyroidism, renal disease, older age, K-wasting diuretics, corticosteroids. Antidote: digoxin immune Fab.風險因素:低血鉀(<3.5)、低血鎂、高血鈣、甲狀腺機能低下、腎病、高齡、排鉀利尿劑、類固醇。解毒劑:digoxin immune Fab。
  • Check apical pulse for a full minute; hold and call if below 60/min. Teach pulse taking (report <60 or >100), eat potassium-rich foods, avoid OTC drugs.測心尖脈搏滿一分鐘;低於60次/分應暫停並通知醫師。教導自測脈搏(<60或>100通報)、攝取高鉀食物、避免自行服用成藥。

4Diuretics利尿劑

  • Thiazides (hydrochlorothiazide, chlorthalidone, metolazone): act on distal tubule; for hypertension and edema; need normal renal function. Adverse: hypokalemia, hyponatremia, hypercalcemia, hyperglycemia, hyperuricemia, photosensitivity; sulfa-based.Thiazide類(hydrochlorothiazide、chlorthalidone、metolazone):作用於遠曲小管;用於高血壓與水腫;腎功能須正常。不良反應:低血鉀、低血鈉、高血鈣、高血糖、高尿酸、光敏感;含磺胺結構。
  • Thiazide teaching: take in morning, rise slowly, sunscreen, potassium-rich foods, watch digoxin/lithium toxicity.Thiazide衛教:早晨服用、緩慢起身、防曬、攝取高鉀食物、注意digoxin/lithium毒性。
  • Loop diuretics (furosemide, bumetanide, torsemide): potent, act on loop of Henle; for pulmonary edema, HF, hypertension. Adverse: hypokalemia, hyponatremia, hypocalcemia, hypomagnesemia, ototoxicity, hyperuricemia. Give IV furosemide slowly over 1-2 minutes.袢利尿劑(furosemide、bumetanide、torsemide):作用強,作用於亨利氏環;用於肺水腫、心衰竭、高血壓。不良反應:低血鉀、低血鈉、低血鈣、低血鎂、耳毒性、高尿酸。IV furosemide須於1-2分鐘緩慢推注。
  • Potassium-sparing diuretics (spironolactone, eplerenone, amiloride, triamterene): primary concern is hyperkalemia (K above 5.0; peaked T waves). Avoid salt substitutes, K supplements; caution with ACE inhibitors.保鉀利尿劑(spironolactone、eplerenone、amiloride、triamterene):主要顧慮為高血鉀(K>5.0;T波高尖)。避免鹽代用品與鉀補充劑;與ACE抑制劑併用需謹慎。

5Alpha blockers, central sympatholytics, ACE inhibitors, ARBsα阻斷劑、中樞交感神經抑制劑、ACE抑制劑與ARB

  • Peripheral alpha blockers (doxazosin, prazosin, terazosin): vasodilation; adverse orthostatic hypotension, reflex tachycardia, fluid retention. Rise slowly, limit salt.周邊α阻斷劑(doxazosin、prazosin、terazosin):血管擴張;不良反應為姿勢性低血壓、反射性心搏過速、水分滯留。緩慢起身、限鹽。
  • Central agents (clonidine, methyldopa, guanfacine): adverse dry mouth, drowsiness, bradycardia, depression, edema; never stop abruptly (rebound hypertension); monitor liver function.中樞作用劑(clonidine、methyldopa、guanfacine):不良反應為口乾、嗜睡、心搏過緩、憂鬱、水腫;不可突然停藥(反彈性高血壓);監測肝功能。
  • ACE inhibitors (-pril) block angiotensin I to II; ARBs (-sartan) block AII receptors. Used for hypertension, HF, post-MI protection.ACE抑制劑(-pril)阻斷血管收縮素I轉換為II;ARB(-sartan)阻斷血管收縮素II受體。用於高血壓、心衰竭、MI後心臟保護。
  • Adverse: persistent dry cough (ACE inhibitors), hyperkalemia, hypotension, taste change; captopril may cause bruising/neutropenia and is taken 20-60 minutes before meals.不良反應:持續乾咳(ACE抑制劑)、高血鉀、低血壓、味覺改變;captopril可能引起瘀斑/嗜中性球低下,須餐前20-60分鐘服用。
  • Avoid potassium supplements and K-sparing diuretics; monitor BUN, creatinine, K+; do not stop abruptly; report cough that persists.避免鉀補充劑與保鉀利尿劑;監測BUN、肌酐、鉀;不可突然停藥;持續咳嗽應通報。

6Nitrates (antianginals)硝酸鹽類(抗心絞痛藥)

  • Vasodilate, lowering preload and afterload and myocardial oxygen demand. Contraindicated with significant hypotension, increased ICP, severe anemia, and erectile dysfunction drugs (PDE5 inhibitors).血管擴張,降低前負荷、後負荷與心肌耗氧。顯著低血壓、顱內壓升高、嚴重貧血及使用勃起功能障礙藥物(PDE5抑制劑)者禁用。
  • Adverse: headache (use acetaminophen), orthostatic hypotension, dizziness, flushing, reflex tachycardia.不良反應:頭痛(可用acetaminophen)、姿勢性低血壓、頭暈、臉潮紅、反射性心搏過速。
  • Sublingual: sit down, dissolve under tongue, do not swallow; at home 1 tablet and call EMS if pain not relieved; in hospital 1 tablet every 5 minutes up to 3 doses, check BP before each, notify provider if pain persists.舌下含片:坐下、含於舌下溶解、不吞服;居家1錠,疼痛未緩解立即叫救護車;住院每5分鐘1錠,最多3錠,每次給藥前測血壓,仍疼痛通知醫師。
  • Store in dark tightly closed bottle; tablets can expire within 6 months of opening; a burning/stinging sensation suggests potency.存放於深色緊閉瓶中;開瓶後約6個月內可能失效;刺痛或灼熱感表示藥效仍在。
  • Patch: hairless site, rotate daily, remove after 12-14 hours for a 10-12 hour nitrate-free period (prevents tolerance); not on chest where defibrillator pads go; wear gloves for ointment/patch. Do not crush sustained-release forms; avoid abrupt withdrawal.貼片:無毛部位、每日輪換,12-14小時後移除,保留10-12小時無藥期(防止耐受);不貼於除顫電擊板位置的胸部;塗抹藥膏/貼片時戴手套。緩釋劑型不可磨碎;避免突然停藥。

7Beta blockers, calcium channel blockers, vasodilatorsβ阻斷劑、鈣離子通道阻斷劑與血管擴張劑

  • Beta blockers (-olol): lower HR, BP, contractility; nonselective (propranolol, carvedilol, labetalol, nadolol) vs cardioselective (metoprolol, atenolol, bisoprolol, esmolol). Contraindicated in asthma, bradycardia, decompensated HF; caution in diabetes (mask hypoglycemia).β阻斷劑(-olol):降低心率、血壓與收縮力;非選擇性(propranolol、carvedilol、labetalol、nadolol)與心臟選擇性(metoprolol、atenolol、bisoprolol、esmolol)。氣喘、心搏過緩、失代償心衰竭禁用;糖尿病慎用(掩蓋低血糖)。
  • Beta blocker care: hold if pulse/BP outside parameters; report wheezing, dizziness; do not stop abruptly (rebound tachycardia, angina); avoid cold/decongestant OTCs.β阻斷劑護理:脈搏/血壓超出參數時暫停給藥;通報喘鳴、頭暈;不可突然停藥(反彈性心搏過速、心絞痛);避免感冒藥/解鼻充血劑。
  • Calcium channel blockers (amlodipine, nifedipine, diltiazem, verapamil): vasodilate and reduce contractility; adverse bradycardia, hypotension, peripheral edema, constipation, headache, flushing. Caution in HF, AV block; do not crush sustained-release.鈣離子通道阻斷劑(amlodipine、nifedipine、diltiazem、verapamil):血管擴張並降低收縮力;不良反應:心搏過緩、低血壓、周邊水腫、便祕、頭痛、臉潮紅。心衰竭、房室傳導阻滯慎用;緩釋劑不可磨碎。
  • Peripheral vasodilators (pentoxifylline, CCBs, alpha blockers) for vasospasm like Raynaud's; response may take up to 3 months; no smoking, avoid alcohol and aspirin unless approved.周邊血管擴張劑(pentoxifylline、鈣通道阻斷劑、α阻斷劑)用於血管痙攣如雷諾氏症;療效可能需3個月;不可吸菸,避免酒精,未經許可不用aspirin。
  • Direct arteriolar vasodilators (hydralazine, nitroprusside, diazoxide, fenoldopam): for severe hypertension/emergencies; hypotension, reflex tachycardia, edema.直接作用小動脈血管擴張劑(hydralazine、nitroprusside、diazoxide、fenoldopam):用於嚴重高血壓/高血壓急症;低血壓、反射性心搏過速、水腫。
  • Nitroprusside: risk of cyanide/thiocyanate toxicity; protect from light with dark bag; discard if red, green or blue. Nesiritide (BNP) for decompensated HF: hypotension; monitor BP, rhythm, urine output, weight.Nitroprusside:有氰化物/硫氰酸鹽中毒風險;以深色袋避光;若呈紅、綠、藍色須丟棄。Nesiritide(BNP)用於失代償心衰竭:低血壓;監測血壓、心律、尿量、體重。

8Antidysrhythmics and adrenergic agonists抗心律不整藥與腎上腺素促效劑

  • Class I sodium channel blockers: IA (procainamide, quinidine, disopyramide), IB (lidocaine, mexiletine, phenytoin), IC (flecainide, propafenone); adverse hypotension, HF, proarrhythmia, GI upset.第I類鈉離子通道阻斷劑:IA(procainamide、quinidine、disopyramide)、IB(lidocaine、mexiletine、phenytoin)、IC(flecainide、propafenone);不良反應:低血壓、心衰竭、致心律不整、胃腸不適。
  • Class II beta blockers; Class III potassium channel blockers (amiodarone, dofetilide, ibutilide, sotalol) prolong QT, risk torsades de pointes; Class IV calcium channel blockers (verapamil, diltiazem). Others: adenosine, digoxin.第II類為β阻斷劑;第III類鉀離子通道阻斷劑(amiodarone、dofetilide、ibutilide、sotalol)延長QT間期,有torsades de pointes風險;第IV類為鈣離子通道阻斷劑(verapamil、diltiazem)。其他:adenosine、digoxin。
  • Amiodarone: pulmonary fibrosis, photosensitivity, blue-gray skin, corneal deposits, hypothyroidism, neuropathy; monitor ECG, lungs, thyroid; use sunscreen.Amiodarone:肺纖維化、光敏感、皮膚藍灰色、角膜沉積、甲狀腺機能低下、神經病變;監測心電圖、肺部、甲狀腺;使用防曬。
  • General care: continuous cardiac monitoring, IV via infusion pump, check lidocaine vial is not the local-anesthetic (epinephrine/preservative) form, give oral antidysrhythmics without food (mexiletine may be with food), rise slowly, limit fluid/salt, fiber for constipation.一般護理:持續心臟監測、IV使用輸液幫浦、確認lidocaine非含epinephrine/防腐劑的局部麻醉劑型、口服抗心律不整藥不與食物併服(mexiletine可隨餐)、緩慢起身、限水限鹽、增加纖維預防便祕。
  • Adrenergic agonists: dobutamine (beta; HF, cardiac output), dopamine (alpha/beta; BP and renal flow), epinephrine (cardiac arrest, anaphylaxis, asthma), norepinephrine (vasoconstriction in shock/hypotension). Adverse dysrhythmias, tachycardia, angina; give via large vein; monitor ECG, urine output.腎上腺素促效劑:dobutamine(β;心衰竭、增加心輸出量)、dopamine(α/β;升血壓與腎血流)、epinephrine(心跳停止、過敏性休克、氣喘)、norepinephrine(休克/低血壓時血管收縮)。不良反應:心律不整、心搏過速、心絞痛;經大靜脈給予;監測心電圖與尿量。

9Antilipemic medications降血脂藥

  • Bile sequestrants (cholestyramine, colestipol, colesevelam): bind bile acids; constipation, bloating; mix gritty powder in juice/water, take with plenty of fluid; may raise triglycerides.膽酸結合劑(cholestyramine、colestipol、colesevelam):結合膽酸;便祕、腹脹;粉末需以果汁/水充分混合,並多喝水;可能升高三酸甘油酯。
  • Statins (atorvastatin, simvastatin, lovastatin, rosuvastatin, pravastatin): monitor liver enzymes; report unexplained muscle pain immediately (myopathy/rhabdomyolysis); annual eye exam; avoid with gemfibrozil.Statin類(atorvastatin、simvastatin、lovastatin、rosuvastatin、pravastatin):監測肝酵素;不明原因肌肉痛須立即通報(肌病變/橫紋肌溶解);每年眼科檢查;避免與gemfibrozil併用。
