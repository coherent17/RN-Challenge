This chapter reviews anxiety and related disorders (GAD, panic, PTSD, phobia, OCD, somatic and dissociative disorders), mood disorders (bipolar, depression, ECT), schizophrenia, personality disorders, and dementia/Alzheimer disease. Priorities are safety, therapeutic communication, reality-based interaction, and recognizing key signs of each disorder.本章涵蓋焦慮及相關疾患（GAD、恐慌、PTSD、畏懼症、強迫症、身體症狀與解離疾患）、情感性疾患（雙相、憂鬱、電痙攣治療）、思覺失調症、人格疾患，以及失智症/阿茲海默症。重點為安全、治療性溝通、以現實為基礎的互動，以及辨識各疾患的關鍵表現。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Anxiety: levels and general nursing care焦慮：程度與一般護理

  • Mild: heightened perception, motivates learning. Moderate: narrowed focus, selective inattention, can still learn; tachycardia, sweating, mild tremor.輕度：感知增強，促進學習。中度：注意力縮窄、選擇性忽略，仍可學習；心跳加快、流汗、輕微顫抖。
  • Severe: very narrow field, scattered details, no learning or problem-solving; sympathetic symptoms, hyperventilation; needs direction.重度：視野極窄、專注於零碎細節，無法學習或解決問題；交感症狀、換氣過度；需要引導。
  • Panic: terror, disorganization, distorted reality, cannot communicate; prolonged panic can cause exhaustion.恐慌：恐懼、思緒紊亂、扭曲現實、無法溝通；持續太久可致衰竭。
  • First action: decrease stimuli and provide a calm, quiet environment; recognize anxiety, establish trust, protect the client, do not criticize coping, do not force anxiety-provoking situations.首要措施：減少刺激並提供平靜安靜環境；辨識焦慮、建立信任、保護客戶、不批評因應方式、不強迫面對引發焦慮的情境。
  • Mild-moderate: identify source, talk, problem-solve, gross motor exercise. Severe-panic: stay with client, calm low-pitched voice, short simple statements, walk with the client, medications as prescribed.輕中度：找出來源、談論感受、解決問題、大肌肉運動。重度至恐慌：陪伴、平穩低沉聲音、簡短語句、陪同走動、依醫囑給藥。

2GAD and panic attacks廣泛性焦慮與恐慌發作

  • GAD: excessive worry on most days for 6 months or more with 3+ symptoms (restless, easily tired, poor concentration, irritable, muscle tension, sleep problems), not due to substances or medical cause (Box 66.1).GAD：多數日子過度擔憂達 6 個月以上，並有 3 項以上症狀（坐立不安、易疲倦、注意力差、易怒、肌肉緊繃、睡眠問題），且非物質或醫療因素所致（方框 66.1）。
  • Panic attack: sudden intense dread lasting about 5-30 minutes with choking sensation, pounding heart, chest pain, dizziness, paresthesias, fear of dying.恐慌發作：突發強烈恐懼約 5–30 分鐘，伴窒息感、心悸、胸痛、頭暈、麻木、瀕死恐懼。
  • Stay with the client, treat physical symptoms, use cognitive restructuring to replace distorted thoughts, give antianxiety drugs if prescribed.陪伴客戶、處理身體症狀、用認知重建取代扭曲想法、依醫囑給抗焦慮藥。

3PTSD, trauma-informed care and moral injury創傷後壓力症、創傷知情照護與道德傷害

  • PTSD follows trauma (disaster, combat, assault, abuse): re-experiencing (flashbacks, nightmares), avoidance, hyperarousal (startle, hypervigilance, irritability), guilt, detachment.PTSD 發生於創傷後（災難、戰爭、暴力、虐待）：再經歷（閃回、夢魘）、迴避、過度警覺（驚嚇反應、警戒、易怒）、罪惡感、疏離。
  • During a flashback: ensure safety, stay calm, reassure that the client is safe, do not startle. Monitor suicide risk, teach stress management, include family and support groups.閃回時：確保安全、保持冷靜、告知客戶安全、避免驚嚇。監測自殺風險、教導壓力管理、納入家屬與支持團體。
  • Evidence-based treatments: medication, CBT, prolonged exposure, EMDR.實證治療：藥物、認知行為治療、延長暴露治療、眼動減敏與歷程更新（EMDR）。
  • Trauma-informed care asks 'what happened to you?' rather than 'what is wrong with you?'; avoid restraints/coercion, collaborate, peer support, empowerment, cultural respect.創傷知情照護問的是「你經歷了什麼？」而非「你哪裡不對勁？」；避免約束與強制，強調合作、同儕支持、賦權與文化尊重。
  • Moral injury: distress from acts that violate one's moral beliefs (guilt, shame, disgust, anger, inability to self-forgive); not currently a mental disorder; fear-based hyperarousal is not characteristic. Assess depression and self-harm first; use nonjudgmental approach, group therapy, cognitive processing therapy.道德傷害：因違背自身道德信念的行為而痛苦（罪惡感、羞愧、厭惡、憤怒、無法自我原諒）；目前不屬精神疾病；恐懼性過度警覺不是其特徵。優先評估憂鬱與自傷；採不批判態度、團體治療、認知處理治療。

4Phobia, OCD, somatic and dissociative disorders畏懼症、強迫症、身體症狀與解離疾患

  • Specific phobia: irrational fear with avoidance; stay with the client, never force contact; teach relaxation; gradual desensitization (Box 66.2).特定畏懼症：非理性恐懼並迴避；陪伴、絕不強迫接觸；教導放鬆；漸進式減敏（方框 66.2）。
  • OCD: obsessions (intrusive thoughts) and compulsions (rituals) that reduce anxiety. Do not interrupt rituals unless unsafe; allow time, set limits, schedule distractions, written contract, reinforce non-ritual behavior (Box 66.3).強迫症：強迫思考（侵入性想法）與強迫行為（儀式）以降低焦慮。除非危險否則不打斷儀式；給予時間、設限、安排分散活動、書面契約、強化非儀式行為（方框 66.3）。
  • Related: hoarding, skin-picking, trichotillomania.相關疾患：囤積症、摳皮症、拔毛症。
  • Somatic symptom disorder: physical complaints without findings, anxiety redirected into body, secondary gain. Allow a limited time to discuss complaints, do not reinforce them, assess any new complaint, explore anxiety.身體症狀疾患：無器質證據的身體不適，焦慮轉移至身體，有次級獲益。限定時間討論不適、不強化症狀、評估任何新主訴、探索焦慮。
  • Conversion disorder: sudden neurological deficit (blindness, paralysis, mutism) without organic cause, 'la belle indifference'; symptoms reduce anxiety; rule out physical causes first.轉化症：突發神經功能缺損（失明、癱瘓、失語）但無器質原因，常有「泰然漠視」；症狀可減輕焦慮；須先排除生理原因。
  • Dissociative disorders: DID (host and alters, sudden switching), dissociative amnesia (localized, selective, generalized), depersonalization/derealization (reality testing intact). Orient, build trust, simple routines, let client set pace.解離疾患：DID（主人格與次人格，突然轉換）、解離性失憶（局部、選擇性、廣泛性）、人格解體/現實解體（現實感仍完整）。給予定向、建立信任、簡單作息、依客戶步調進行。

5Bipolar disorder and mania雙相疾患與躁症

  • Types: bipolar I (mania to depression), bipolar II (hypomania plus severe depression), cyclothymic, mixed features.類型：雙相 I 型（躁症到憂鬱）、II 型（輕躁加重度憂鬱）、循環性情感疾患、混合特徵。
  • Lithium: toxic, needs regular serum level monitoring; keep sodium intake steady and fluids 2-3 L/day. Atypical antipsychotics also used; avoid antianxiety agents if substance abuse history.鋰鹽：有毒性，需定期監測血中濃度；維持穩定鈉攝取與每日 2–3 公升水分。亦使用非典型抗精神病藥；有物質濫用史者避免抗焦慮藥。
  • Mania signs: flight of ideas, pressured speech, grandiosity, little sleep, excessive spending, distractibility, euphoria/irritability (Box 66.5).躁症表現：意念飛躍、言語急迫、誇大、睡眠極少、過度消費、分心、欣快或易怒（方框 66.5）。
  • Interventions: safety first, low stimulation, private room, short calm interactions, do not argue, redirect grandiosity, high-calorie finger foods, rest, solitary or gross-motor non-competitive activities, set limits.處置：安全為先、低刺激、單人房、簡短平穩互動、不爭辯、轉移誇大想法、高熱量手指食物、休息、獨自或非競爭性大肌肉活動、設限。
  • Manipulation: clear consistent limits, nonpunitive follow-through, avoid power struggles. Aggression: de-escalation, respect space, calm assertive tone (Box 66.6).操弄行為：清楚一致的限制、非懲罰性執行、避免權力鬥爭。攻擊行為：降級技巧、尊重個人空間、平穩而果斷的語氣（方框 66.6）。

6Depression and ECT憂鬱症與電痙攣治療

  • Major depression: depressed mood or anhedonia plus changes in appetite/weight, sleep, energy, concentration, worthlessness/guilt, thoughts of death for most days; rule out hypothyroidism and substances.重度憂鬱：多數日子出現情緒低落或失去興趣，加上食慾/體重、睡眠、精力、專注力改變、無價值感/罪惡感、死亡念頭；須排除甲狀腺低下與物質。
  • Ask directly about self-harm. Suicide plan: one-to-one supervision, remove harmful objects. If an antidepressant begins to lift energy, watch closely - the client may now have energy to act.直接詢問自傷念頭。有自殺計畫：一對一監視、移除危險物品。抗憂鬱藥開始增加精力時要密切觀察——客戶可能有力氣執行自殺。
  • Depression care: structured, achievable, simple activities, begin one-to-one then small groups, stay with client at meals, monitor hygiene, sleep, nutrition (Box 66.7).憂鬱症照護：結構化、可達成的簡單活動，先一對一再進入小團體，用餐陪伴，監測衛生、睡眠與營養（方框 66.7）。
  • ECT: for severe/psychotic depression, suicidal, catatonia, treatment-resistant mania; course of 6-12 treatments every 2-5 days. Needs informed consent, NPO (after midnight or at least 4 h), void, remove dentures/hairpins/lenses.ECT：用於重度/精神病性憂鬱、自殺、僵直症、難治性躁症；療程 6–12 次、每 2–5 天一次。需知情同意、禁食（午夜後或至少 4 小時）、排尿、取下假牙/髮夾/隱形眼鏡。
  • During ECT: anesthetic and muscle relaxant, oxygen, bite guard, seizure 15-70 seconds. After: recovery area with suction/O2, orient the client, expect confusion and short-term memory loss. Risk clients: recent MI, stroke, intracranial mass.ECT 進行中：麻醉與肌肉鬆弛劑、給氧、咬合器、痙攣 15–70 秒。術後：於備有抽吸與氧氣的恢復區，持續定向，預期意識混亂與短期記憶喪失。高風險：近期心肌梗塞、中風、顱內腫塊。

7Schizophrenia思覺失調症

  • Symptom domains: positive (hallucinations, delusions, disorganized speech, bizarre behavior), negative (blunted affect, anhedonia, avolition, alogia), cognitive, mood (Fig. 66.1).症狀向度：陽性（幻覺、妄想、語言紊亂、怪異行為）、陰性（情感鈍化、失樂症、動機缺乏、言語貧乏）、認知、情緒（圖 66.1）。
  • Motor: catatonic posturing/excitement, waxy flexibility, echopraxia. Thought: loose associations, thought blocking, neologisms, word salad, echolalia, clang association (Boxes 66.9, 66.10, 66.13).動作：僵直姿勢/興奮、蠟樣屈曲、仿作。思考：聯想鬆散、思考中斷、新語症、字詞沙拉、仿說、音韻聯想（方框 66.9、66.10、66.13）。
  • Delusions: reference, persecution, grandeur, somatic. Do not argue or try to convince; express feelings; reality-based topics; validate real parts (Box 66.11).妄想：關係、被害、誇大、身體妄想。不爭辯或說服；表達感受；談現實話題；肯定真實部分（方框 66.11）。
  • Hallucinations: ask directly, do not treat as real or deny the experience, reduce stimuli, concrete activity, avoid touch; safety first - check for command hallucinations (Box 66.12).幻覺：直接詢問、不當作真實也不否定經驗、降低刺激、安排具體活動、避免碰觸；安全為先——確認是否有命令式幻覺（方框 66.12）。
  • Care: safe, simple, structured routine; brief frequent contact; do not whisper; tell client when leaving; initially no choices; monitor suicide risk; withdrawn/catatonic clients: sit quietly with open-ended questions (Box 66.14).照護：安全、簡單、有結構的作息；簡短頻繁接觸；不要耳語；離開時告知；初期不提供選擇；監測自殺風險；退縮/僵直者：安靜陪坐並使用開放式問句（方框 66.14）。

8Personality disorders人格疾患

  • Inflexible maladaptive patterns, reality testing usually intact but poor insight; impulsivity, acting out, impaired judgment and relationships.僵化不適應的模式，現實感通常完整但缺乏病識感；衝動、行動化、判斷與人際受損。
  • Cluster A (odd): schizoid, schizotypal, paranoid. Paranoid: do not whisper or laugh in front of client; be honest, brief frequent contact, keep commitments (Box 66.15).A 群（怪異）：類分裂型、思覺失調型、猜疑型。猜疑型：勿在客戶面前耳語或笑；誠實、簡短頻繁接觸、遵守承諾（方框 66.15）。
  • Cluster B (dramatic): histrionic, narcissistic, antisocial (no remorse, manipulative), borderline (unstable relationships, self-harm, splitting, fear of abandonment).B 群（戲劇化）：做作型、自戀型、反社會型（無悔意、操弄）、邊緣型（關係不穩、自傷、分裂、害怕被遺棄）。
  • Cluster C (anxious): obsessive-compulsive personality, avoidant (fear of rejection, social isolation), dependent.C 群（焦慮）：強迫型人格、逃避型（怕被拒絕、社交孤立）、依賴型。
  • Care: safety, consistent team response, limits, written behavioral contract, identify splitting, encourage talking not acting out, praise nonmanipulative behavior.照護：安全、團隊一致回應、設限、書面行為契約、辨識分裂、鼓勵說出而非行動化、讚美非操弄行為。

9Dementia and Alzheimer disease失智症與阿茲海默症

  • Dementia: progressive intellectual decline with memory loss, poor judgment, self-care deficit; rule out reversible physiological causes first. Alzheimer is the most common type, irreversible.失智症：進行性智能退化，記憶喪失、判斷力差、自我照顧缺損；須先排除可逆的生理原因。阿茲海默症最常見且不可逆。
  • Stages: mild (forgetfulness), moderate (confusion), moderate-severe (ambulatory dementia), late (end stage). Aphasia, agnosia, apraxia, amnesia (Box 66.16).分期：輕度（健忘）、中度（意識混亂）、中重度（能行走的失智）、末期。失語、失認、失用、失憶（方框 66.16）。
  • Safety is the priority: clutter-free environment, close supervision, secured doors, ID bracelet, lower water-heater temperature, remove throw rugs.安全為首要：整潔無雜物、密切監督、鎖好門、識別手環、調低熱水溫度、移除地毯。
  • Sundowning: evening worsening. Use indirect light, consistent routine, afternoon rest, avoid hypnotics, allow safe wandering.日落症候群：傍晚症狀加重。使用間接光源、固定作息、午後休息、避免安眠藥、允許在安全處走動。
  • Communication: face the client, short simple sentences, one question or direction at a time, repeat without rephrasing, allow time, don't argue; support caregivers.溝通：面對客戶、短而簡單的句子、一次一個問題或指令、重複但不改述、給時間、不爭辯；支持照顧者。
  • Neurodevelopmental disorders (autism, ADHD) are covered in Chapter 39.神經發展疾患（自閉症、ADHD）請見第 39 章。

  • During a flashback: ensure safety, stay calm, reassure that the client is safe, do not startle. Monitor suicide risk, teach stress management, include family and support groups.閃回時：確保安全、保持冷靜、告知客戶安全、避免驚嚇。監測自殺風險、教導壓力管理、納入家屬與支持團體。
  • Evidence-based treatments: medication, CBT, prolonged exposure, EMDR.實證治療：藥物、認知行為治療、延長暴露治療、眼動減敏與歷程更新（EMDR）。
  • Trauma-informed care asks 'what happened to you?' rather than 'what is wrong with you?'; avoid restraints/coercion, collaborate, peer support, empowerment, cultural respect.創傷知情照護問的是「你經歷了什麼？」而非「你哪裡不對勁？」；避免約束與強制，強調合作、同儕支持、賦權與文化尊重。
  • Moral injury: distress from acts that violate one's moral beliefs (guilt, shame, disgust, anger, inability to self-forgive); not currently a mental disorder; fear-based hyperarousal is not characteristic. Assess depression and self-harm first; use nonjudgmental approach, group therapy, cognitive processing therapy.道德傷害：因違背自身道德信念的行為而痛苦（罪惡感、羞愧、厭惡、憤怒、無法自我原諒）；目前不屬精神疾病；恐懼性過度警覺不是其特徵。優先評估憂鬱與自傷；採不批判態度、團體治療、認知處理治療。
Topic 4 · tap to flip主題 4 · 點擊翻面
Phobia, OCD, somatic and dissociative disorders畏懼症、強迫症、身體症狀與解離疾患
  • Specific phobia: irrational fear with avoidance; stay with the client, never force contact; teach relaxation; gradual desensitization (Box 66.2).特定畏懼症：非理性恐懼並迴避；陪伴、絕不強迫接觸；教導放鬆；漸進式減敏（方框 66.2）。
  • OCD: obsessions (intrusive thoughts) and compulsions (rituals) that reduce anxiety. Do not interrupt rituals unless unsafe; allow time, set limits, schedule distractions, written contract, reinforce non-ritual behavior (Box 66.3).強迫症：強迫思考（侵入性想法）與強迫行為（儀式）以降低焦慮。除非危險否則不打斷儀式；給予時間、設限、安排分散活動、書面契約、強化非儀式行為（方框 66.3）。
  • Related: hoarding, skin-picking, trichotillomania.相關疾患：囤積症、摳皮症、拔毛症。
  • Somatic symptom disorder: physical complaints without findings, anxiety redirected into body, secondary gain. Allow a limited time to discuss complaints, do not reinforce them, assess any new complaint, explore anxiety.身體症狀疾患：無器質證據的身體不適，焦慮轉移至身體，有次級獲益。限定時間討論不適、不強化症狀、評估任何新主訴、探索焦慮。
  • Conversion disorder: sudden neurological deficit (blindness, paralysis, mutism) without organic cause, 'la belle indifference'; symptoms reduce anxiety; rule out physical causes first.轉化症：突發神經功能缺損（失明、癱瘓、失語）但無器質原因，常有「泰然漠視」；症狀可減輕焦慮；須先排除生理原因。
  • Dissociative disorders: DID (host and alters, sudden switching), dissociative amnesia (localized, selective, generalized), depersonalization/derealization (reality testing intact). Orient, build trust, simple routines, let client set pace.解離疾患：DID（主人格與次人格，突然轉換）、解離性失憶（局部、選擇性、廣泛性）、人格解體/現實解體（現實感仍完整）。給予定向、建立信任、簡單作息、依客戶步調進行。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Bipolar disorder and mania雙相疾患與躁症
  • Types: bipolar I (mania to depression), bipolar II (hypomania plus severe depression), cyclothymic, mixed features.類型：雙相 I 型（躁症到憂鬱）、II 型（輕躁加重度憂鬱）、循環性情感疾患、混合特徵。
  • Lithium: toxic, needs regular serum level monitoring; keep sodium intake steady and fluids 2-3 L/day. Atypical antipsychotics also used; avoid antianxiety agents if substance abuse history.鋰鹽：有毒性，需定期監測血中濃度；維持穩定鈉攝取與每日 2–3 公升水分。亦使用非典型抗精神病藥；有物質濫用史者避免抗焦慮藥。
  • Mania signs: flight of ideas, pressured speech, grandiosity, little sleep, excessive spending, distractibility, euphoria/irritability (Box 66.5).躁症表現：意念飛躍、言語急迫、誇大、睡眠極少、過度消費、分心、欣快或易怒（方框 66.5）。
  • Interventions: safety first, low stimulation, private room, short calm interactions, do not argue, redirect grandiosity, high-calorie finger foods, rest, solitary or gross-motor non-competitive activities, set limits.處置：安全為先、低刺激、單人房、簡短平穩互動、不爭辯、轉移誇大想法、高熱量手指食物、休息、獨自或非競爭性大肌肉活動、設限。
  • Manipulation: clear consistent limits, nonpunitive follow-through, avoid power struggles. Aggression: de-escalation, respect space, calm assertive tone (Box 66.6).操弄行為：清楚一致的限制、非懲罰性執行、避免權力鬥爭。攻擊行為：降級技巧、尊重個人空間、平穩而果斷的語氣（方框 66.6）。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Depression and ECT憂鬱症與電痙攣治療
  • Major depression: depressed mood or anhedonia plus changes in appetite/weight, sleep, energy, concentration, worthlessness/guilt, thoughts of death for most days; rule out hypothyroidism and substances.重度憂鬱：多數日子出現情緒低落或失去興趣，加上食慾/體重、睡眠、精力、專注力改變、無價值感/罪惡感、死亡念頭；須排除甲狀腺低下與物質。
  • Ask directly about self-harm. Suicide plan: one-to-one supervision, remove harmful objects. If an antidepressant begins to lift energy, watch closely - the client may now have energy to act.直接詢問自傷念頭。有自殺計畫：一對一監視、移除危險物品。抗憂鬱藥開始增加精力時要密切觀察——客戶可能有力氣執行自殺。
  • Depression care: structured, achievable, simple activities, begin one-to-one then small groups, stay with client at meals, monitor hygiene, sleep, nutrition (Box 66.7).憂鬱症照護：結構化、可達成的簡單活動，先一對一再進入小團體，用餐陪伴，監測衛生、睡眠與營養（方框 66.7）。
  • ECT: for severe/psychotic depression, suicidal, catatonia, treatment-resistant mania; course of 6-12 treatments every 2-5 days. Needs informed consent, NPO (after midnight or at least 4 h), void, remove dentures/hairpins/lenses.ECT：用於重度/精神病性憂鬱、自殺、僵直症、難治性躁症；療程 6–12 次、每 2–5 天一次。需知情同意、禁食（午夜後或至少 4 小時）、排尿、取下假牙/髮夾/隱形眼鏡。
  • During ECT: anesthetic and muscle relaxant, oxygen, bite guard, seizure 15-70 seconds. After: recovery area with suction/O2, orient the client, expect confusion and short-term memory loss. Risk clients: recent MI, stroke, intracranial mass.ECT 進行中：麻醉與肌肉鬆弛劑、給氧、咬合器、痙攣 15–70 秒。術後：於備有抽吸與氧氣的恢復區，持續定向，預期意識混亂與短期記憶喪失。高風險：近期心肌梗塞、中風、顱內腫塊。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Schizophrenia思覺失調症
  • Symptom domains: positive (hallucinations, delusions, disorganized speech, bizarre behavior), negative (blunted affect, anhedonia, avolition, alogia), cognitive, mood (Fig. 66.1).症狀向度：陽性（幻覺、妄想、語言紊亂、怪異行為）、陰性（情感鈍化、失樂症、動機缺乏、言語貧乏）、認知、情緒（圖 66.1）。
  • Motor: catatonic posturing/excitement, waxy flexibility, echopraxia. Thought: loose associations, thought blocking, neologisms, word salad, echolalia, clang association (Boxes 66.9, 66.10, 66.13).動作：僵直姿勢/興奮、蠟樣屈曲、仿作。思考：聯想鬆散、思考中斷、新語症、字詞沙拉、仿說、音韻聯想（方框 66.9、66.10、66.13）。
  • Delusions: reference, persecution, grandeur, somatic. Do not argue or try to convince; express feelings; reality-based topics; validate real parts (Box 66.11).妄想：關係、被害、誇大、身體妄想。不爭辯或說服；表達感受；談現實話題；肯定真實部分（方框 66.11）。
  • Hallucinations: ask directly, do not treat as real or deny the experience, reduce stimuli, concrete activity, avoid touch; safety first - check for command hallucinations (Box 66.12).幻覺：直接詢問、不當作真實也不否定經驗、降低刺激、安排具體活動、避免碰觸；安全為先——確認是否有命令式幻覺（方框 66.12）。
  • Care: safe, simple, structured routine; brief frequent contact; do not whisper; tell client when leaving; initially no choices; monitor suicide risk; withdrawn/catatonic clients: sit quietly with open-ended questions (Box 66.14).照護：安全、簡單、有結構的作息；簡短頻繁接觸；不要耳語；離開時告知；初期不提供選擇；監測自殺風險；退縮/僵直者：安靜陪坐並使用開放式問句（方框 66.14）。

Personality disorders人格疾患
  • Inflexible maladaptive patterns, reality testing usually intact but poor insight; impulsivity, acting out, impaired judgment and relationships.僵化不適應的模式，現實感通常完整但缺乏病識感；衝動、行動化、判斷與人際受損。
  • Cluster A (odd): schizoid, schizotypal, paranoid. Paranoid: do not whisper or laugh in front of client; be honest, brief frequent contact, keep commitments (Box 66.15).A 群（怪異）：類分裂型、思覺失調型、猜疑型。猜疑型：勿在客戶面前耳語或笑；誠實、簡短頻繁接觸、遵守承諾（方框 66.15）。
  • Cluster B (dramatic): histrionic, narcissistic, antisocial (no remorse, manipulative), borderline (unstable relationships, self-harm, splitting, fear of abandonment).B 群（戲劇化）：做作型、自戀型、反社會型（無悔意、操弄）、邊緣型（關係不穩、自傷、分裂、害怕被遺棄）。
  • Cluster C (anxious): obsessive-compulsive personality, avoidant (fear of rejection, social isolation), dependent.C 群（焦慮）：強迫型人格、逃避型（怕被拒絕、社交孤立）、依賴型。
  • Care: safety, consistent team response, limits, written behavioral contract, identify splitting, encourage talking not acting out, praise nonmanipulative behavior.照護：安全、團隊一致回應、設限、書面行為契約、辨識分裂、鼓勵說出而非行動化、讚美非操弄行為。

Dementia and Alzheimer disease失智症與阿茲海默症
  • Dementia: progressive intellectual decline with memory loss, poor judgment, self-care deficit; rule out reversible physiological causes first. Alzheimer is the most common type, irreversible.失智症：進行性智能退化，記憶喪失、判斷力差、自我照顧缺損；須先排除可逆的生理原因。阿茲海默症最常見且不可逆。
  • Stages: mild (forgetfulness), moderate (confusion), moderate-severe (ambulatory dementia), late (end stage). Aphasia, agnosia, apraxia, amnesia (Box 66.16).分期：輕度（健忘）、中度（意識混亂）、中重度（能行走的失智）、末期。失語、失認、失用、失憶（方框 66.16）。
  • Safety is the priority: clutter-free environment, close supervision, secured doors, ID bracelet, lower water-heater temperature, remove throw rugs.安全為首要：整潔無雜物、密切監督、鎖好門、識別手環、調低熱水溫度、移除地毯。
  • Sundowning: evening worsening. Use indirect light, consistent routine, afternoon rest, avoid hypnotics, allow safe wandering.日落症候群：傍晚症狀加重。使用間接光源、固定作息、午後休息、避免安眠藥、允許在安全處走動。
  • Communication: face the client, short simple sentences, one question or direction at a time, repeat without rephrasing, allow time, don't argue; support caregivers.溝通：面對客戶、短而簡單的句子、一次一個問題或指令、重複但不改述、給時間、不爭辯；支持照顧者。
  • Neurodevelopmental disorders (autism, ADHD) are covered in Chapter 39.神經發展疾患（自閉症、ADHD）請見第 39 章。
