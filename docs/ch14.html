Chapter 14: Medication Administration and Intravenous Therapies第 14 章：給藥與靜脈治療

📖 Source: PDF p.205–217 · printed book p.178–188.e2來源：PDF 第 205–217 頁 · 書本印刷頁 178–188.e2

This chapter reviews medication measurement systems, unit conversions, reading labels and prescriptions, oral and parenteral administration, syringes and injection sites, and dosage and IV flow-rate calculations. NCLEX expects you to calculate drops per minute, mL per hour and unit-per-hour infusions accurately and to question unsafe doses.本章複習藥物計量系統、單位換算、藥物標籤與處方判讀、口服與注射給藥、針筒與注射部位，以及劑量與靜脈輸液速率計算。NCLEX 要求能精確計算每分鐘滴數、每小時毫升數與每小時單位數的輸注，並能質疑不安全的劑量。

Medication Administration Overview and Rights給藥概述與給藥權利 📖 PDF p.205 (book p.178), PDF p.206 (book p.179)

  • Assess the order, reconcile with current medications, ask about allergies and herbal use, and review vital signs, labs and interactions before giving a drug.給藥前評估醫囑、與現用藥物核對（藥物整合）、詢問過敏與草藥使用，並檢視生命徵象、檢驗值與交互作用。
  • Rights include right prescription, medication, dose, client, route, time, reason, education, assessment, refusal, approach, evaluation and documentation.給藥權利包括正確的處方、藥物、劑量、個案、途徑、時間、原因、衛教、評估、拒絕權、技術方式、評值及記錄。
  • Teach the client the drug name, purpose, action and adverse effects; address cultural, religious or other concerns.向個案說明藥名、目的、作用與不良反應，並處理文化、宗教等顧慮。
  • Document administration and the client's response. If an order is unclear or inconsistent, contact the prescriber immediately.記錄給藥及個案反應。若醫囑不清或前後不一致，須立即聯絡開立者確認。
  • A nurse who gives a wrong dose is legally responsible regardless of the source of the error.不論錯誤來源為何，護理師給錯劑量須負法律責任。

Measurement Systems and Conversions計量系統與換算 📖 PDF p.205 (book p.178), PDF p.206 (book p.179), PDF p.207 (book p.180)

  • Metric basics: meter (length), liter (volume), gram (mass). It is the most used system in practice.公制基本單位：公尺（長度）、公升（容積）、公克（質量），是臨床最常用系統。
  • 1 g = 1000 mg; 1 mg = 1000 mcg; 1 kg = 2.2 lb; 1 mL = 0.001 L.1 g = 1000 mg；1 mg = 1000 mcg；1 kg = 2.2 lb；1 mL = 0.001 L。
  • Larger to smaller unit: multiply by 1000 (move decimal 3 places right). Smaller to larger: divide by 1000 (3 places left).大單位換小單位：乘以1000（小數點右移3位）；小單位換大單位：除以1000（左移3位）。
  • Household measures (drop, teaspoon, tablespoon, ounce, cup) and apothecary units (grain, dram, minim) are older; NCLEX will not ask apothecary-to-metric conversion, though 1 gr = 60 mg appears in an example.家用單位（滴、茶匙、湯匙、盎司、杯）與藥衡制（grain、dram、minim）較舊；NCLEX 不考藥衡制換算公制，但書中範例用 1 gr = 60 mg。
  • mEq expresses electrolyte amounts; units (insulin, heparin, penicillin) measure drug activity rather than weight.mEq 表示電解質量；單位（胰島素、肝素、青黴素）以藥效而非重量計量。
  • Conversion is the first step in dosage calculation; labels show the generic name, so learn generic names.換算是劑量計算的第一步；藥標一定有學名，考試以學名為主。

Oral Medications and Prescriptions口服藥物與處方 📖 PDF p.206 (book p.179), PDF p.207 (book p.180)

  • A complete prescription has client name, date/time, drug, dose, route, frequency/time and prescriber signature; use only Joint Commission-approved abbreviations.完整處方包括個案姓名、日期時間、藥名、劑量、途徑、頻率/時間及開立者簽名；僅用聯合委員會核可的縮寫。
  • Scored tablets may be split; enteric-coated and sustained-release products delay absorption and must not be crushed.有刻痕的錠劑可依規定分割；腸溶錠與緩釋劑延遲吸收，不可壓碎。
  • Medicine cup holds 30 mL (1 oz); pour at eye level on a flat surface. Volumes under 5 mL are measured in a syringe (no needle).藥杯容量30 mL（1盎司）；置於平面並平視刻度倒藥。小於5 mL以去針頭的針筒抽取。
  • Use a calibrated oral syringe for children.兒童使用有刻度的口服針筒。

Parenteral Medications, Syringes and Needles注射藥物、針筒與針頭 📖 PDF p.207 (book p.180), PDF p.208 (book p.181), PDF p.209 (book p.182)

  • Parenteral routes: IV, IM, subcutaneous, intradermal. Angles: intradermal 5-15 degrees, subcutaneous 45 or 90 degrees, IM 90 degrees.注射途徑：靜脈、肌肉、皮下、皮內。角度：皮內5-15度、皮下45或90度、肌肉90度。
  • Draw to the top ring of the plunger (closest to the needle). Standard 3-mL syringe is calibrated in 0.1 mL; 5-mL in 0.2 mL; tuberculin 1-mL in 0.01 mL.抽藥時以活塞最靠針頭端的環對齊刻度。標準3 mL針筒刻度0.1 mL；5 mL為0.2 mL；結核菌素1 mL為0.01 mL。
  • Maximum volumes: IM 3 mL per site (2 mL deltoid); subcutaneous 0.5-1.5 mL for adults. Question larger volumes.最大注射量：肌肉每處3 mL（三角肌2 mL）；成人皮下0.5-1.5 mL。超量須再確認。
  • Subcutaneous: 25-gauge 5/8-inch needle at 45 degrees or 1/2-inch at 90 degrees. IM needle length depends on weight and fat; larger gauge for viscous drugs. Z-track uses a deep muscle such as ventrogluteal.皮下：25號、5/8吋針以45度，或1/2吋針以90度。肌肉針長依體重與脂肪厚度而定，黏稠藥物用較粗針；Z字法選深層肌肉如腹臀肌。
  • Insulin only in insulin syringes (100 units = 1 mL). Mixing: draw clear regular first, then NPH (RN). Pens and pumps are alternatives.胰島素僅用胰島素針筒（100單位 = 1 mL）。混合時先抽透明的 Regular，再抽 NPH（RN）。另有胰島素筆與幫浦。
  • Prefilled cartridges are single use; discard extra before injecting a partial dose. Engage safety needle device right after injection.預充式卡匣單次使用；給部分劑量時先丟棄多餘藥量。注射後立即啟動安全針具。
  • Powdered drugs are reconstituted with sterile diluent per label; the final volume exceeds the diluent volume; label multidose vials with date, time, initials and expiration.粉劑依標示以無菌稀釋液調製；最終體積大於稀釋液體積；多劑量瓶須標註日期時間、簽名縮寫及有效期。

Dosage Calculation Methods劑量計算方法 📖 PDF p.207 (book p.180), PDF p.209 (book p.182), PDF p.210 (book p.183)

  • Ratio and proportion: H : V :: D : X. Standard formula: (Desired / Available) x Quantity.比例法：H : V :: D : X。標準公式：（所需量／現有量）× 單位量。
  • Dimensional analysis: start with the desired unit, set conversion fractions so unwanted units cancel.單位分析法：先確定所求單位，排列換算分數使不需要的單位相消。
  • Check that everything is in the same unit first; more than 3 tablets or an oddly large injection volume should prompt verification.先確認單位一致；超過3錠或注射量異常過大時，須再確認。
  • Round at the end; adult injections to the nearest 0.1 mL; small or pediatric doses may be rounded down to 0.01 mL per policy; follow the rounding instruction in the question.計算完最後再四捨五入；成人注射至0.1 mL；微量或兒童劑量依規定可捨至0.01 mL；依題目指示進位。
  • Percent solution = g per 100 mL (10% calcium gluconate = 10 g/100 mL); ratio solution = g per total mL (epinephrine 1:1000 = 1 g/1000 mL).百分比溶液＝每100 mL的公克數（10%葡萄糖酸鈣＝10 g/100 mL）；比例溶液＝每總毫升的公克數（腎上腺素1:1000＝1 g/1000 mL）。

IV Flow Rates and Infusion Safety靜脈輸液速率與安全 📖 PDF p.209 (book p.182), PDF p.210 (book p.183), PDF p.211 (book p.184)

  • Drops/min = (total volume x drop factor) / time in minutes. mL/hr = total mL / hours. Infusion time = total volume / mL per hr.每分鐘滴數＝（總量 × 滴系數）／分鐘數；mL/hr＝總毫升／小時數；輸注時間＝總量／每小時毫升。
  • Macrodrip sets give 10-20 gtt/mL; microdrip about 60 gtt/mL (ICU, pediatrics). The calibration is printed on the tubing package.大滴管每 mL 10-20滴；微滴管約60滴/mL（加護與兒科）。滴系數印在輸液管包裝上。
  • Monitor flow rate often even with a pump. Never speed up a late infusion without PHCP approval; watch for crackles and increased respirations (fluid overload), especially in older, cardiac, renal, pulmonary or neurologic clients.即使使用幫浦也須經常檢查速率。輸液落後時未經醫師同意不可加快；觀察呼吸變快與肺部囉音（容積過量），尤其是老年及心、肺、腎、神經疾病個案。
  • IV fluids count as intake.靜脈輸液須計入輸入量。
  • Unit-per-hour infusions (heparin, regular insulin): step 1 units per mL = units in bag / mL in bag; step 2 mL/hr = dose per hr / units per mL.每小時單位數輸注（肝素、Regular 胰島素）：步驟1 每 mL 單位數＝袋內單位／袋內毫升；步驟2 mL/hr＝每小時劑量／每 mL 單位數。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Conversion comes first: g to mg multiply by 1000; mcg to mg divide by 1000 (150 mcg = 0.15 mg).先換算：g 轉 mg 乘1000；mcg 轉 mg 除以1000（150 mcg = 0.15 mg）。 📖 PDF p.206 (book p.179), PDF p.213 (book p.186)
  2. Drops/min = volume x drop factor / minutes; round to a whole number (drops cannot be fractional).滴/分＝總量 × 滴系數／分鐘；四捨五入至整數。 📖 PDF p.210 (book p.183), PDF p.212 (book p.185)
  3. Heparin/insulin drip: units per mL = units / mL in bag; mL/hr = ordered units per hr / units per mL.肝素／胰島素點滴：每 mL 單位＝單位／袋容量；mL/hr＝醫囑單位數／每 mL 單位。 📖 PDF p.211 (book p.184)
  4. Never speed up a behind-schedule IV without PHCP approval; assess for fluid overload when rate increases.IV 落後時未經醫師同意不可加速；速率增加時評估容積過量。 📖 PDF p.209 (book p.182), PDF p.210 (book p.183)
  5. Question any oral dose needing more than 3 tablets, or excessive injection volume (IM over 3 mL, 2 mL deltoid; subcutaneous over 1.5 mL).口服需超過3錠或注射量過大（肌肉超過3 mL、三角肌超過2 mL、皮下超過1.5 mL）須質疑。 📖 PDF p.208 (book p.181), PDF p.209 (book p.182)
  6. Do not crush enteric-coated or sustained-release medications.腸溶或緩釋劑不可壓碎。 📖 PDF p.207 (book p.180)
  7. Insulin is measured only in an insulin syringe; mix regular before NPH (RN).胰島素只用胰島素針筒；先抽 Regular 再抽 NPH（RN）。 📖 PDF p.209 (book p.182)
  8. Intradermal angle 5-15 degrees with a tuberculin syringe; IM 90 degrees; subcutaneous 45 or 90 degrees.皮內5-15度用結核菌素針筒；肌肉90度；皮下45或90度。 📖 PDF p.208 (book p.181)
  9. Verify unclear or inconsistent orders with the prescriber immediately.醫囑不清或不一致時，立即向開立者確認。 📖 PDF p.206 (book p.179)
  10. Reconstituted volume exceeds diluent volume; label multidose vials with date, time, initials, expiration.調製後體積大於稀釋液體積；多劑量瓶標註日期、時間、簽名、效期。 📖 PDF p.210 (book p.183)
  11. Draw the syringe to the top ring of the plunger closest to the needle.抽取針筒時以最靠針頭端的活塞環對齊刻度。 📖 PDF p.208 (book p.181)
  12. Medicine cup is 30 mL; use syringe for volumes under 5 mL, and for children.藥杯30 mL；小於5 mL及兒童劑量用針筒。 📖 PDF p.207 (book p.180)
  13. Percent solution is g/100 mL; 1:1000 epinephrine is 1 g/1000 mL (1 mg/mL).百分比溶液為 g/100 mL；1:1000 腎上腺素為 1 g/1000 mL（1 mg/mL）。 📖 PDF p.209 (book p.182)
  14. NCLEX uses generic drug names; learn generics.NCLEX 使用藥物學名，須熟記。 📖 PDF p.206 (book p.179)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. Order: 1000 mL NS over 8 hours. Drop factor 15 gtt/mL. How many drops per minute (nearest whole number)?醫囑：1000 mL 生理食鹽水於8小時輸完，滴系數15 gtt/mL。每分鐘幾滴（取整數）？

1000 x 15 / 480 min = 31.25, rounded to 31 gtt/min. 125 is the mL/hr rate.1000 × 15 / 480 = 31.25，取整為31滴/分。125為 mL/hr。

📖 PDF p.210 (book p.183), PDF p.213 (book p.186)

2. Order: heparin 1300 units/hr. Bag: 20,000 units in 250 mL D5W. At what mL/hr does the nurse set the pump (nearest whole number)?醫囑：肝素1300單位/小時。藥袋：250 mL D5W 含20,000單位。幫浦應設定幾 mL/hr（取整數）？

20,000/250 = 80 units/mL; 1300/80 = 16.25, so 16 mL/hr.20,000/250 = 80單位/mL；1300/80 = 16.25，故為16 mL/hr。

150 mcg = 0.15 mg; 0.15/0.1 = 1.5 tablets.150 mcg = 0.15 mg；0.15/0.1 = 1.5錠。

5. An IV infusion is 2 hours behind schedule. What is the nurse's best action?靜脈輸液已落後2小時。護理師最適當的處置為何？

7. Regular insulin 8 units/hr IV is ordered. The bag contains 100 units in 100 mL NS. What mL/hr is set?醫囑 Regular 胰島素8單位/小時靜脈輸注。藥袋為100 mL 生理食鹽水含100單位。應設定幾 mL/hr？
