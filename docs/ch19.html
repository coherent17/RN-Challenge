This chapter covers how hospitalization affects children at each age, communication and safety by developmental stage, well-child care, the childhood and adult immunization schedules, expected growth milestones from infancy through middle adulthood, gender identity, and end-of-life care. Age-appropriate play, safety teaching, vaccine contraindications and vital sign norms are heavily tested.本章涵蓋各年齡層兒童住院反應、依發展階段的溝通與安全、兒童健康檢查、兒童與成人疫苗時程、嬰兒至中年的生長發展里程碑、性別認同與臨終照護。適齡遊戲、安全衛教、疫苗禁忌與生命徵象正常值為常考重點。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Hospitalized Infant, Toddler, Preschooler住院的嬰兒、幼兒與學齡前兒童

  • Toddler separation anxiety has three phases: protest (crying, searching, aggression), despair (withdrawn, loses new skills), detachment (seems adjusted but is resignation; the most serious phase).幼兒分離焦慮三階段：抗議（哭鬧、尋找、攻擊）、絕望（退縮、喪失新技能）、疏離（看似適應，實為放棄；最嚴重）。
  • Toddlers fear pain and loss of control; they regress, show tantrums and ritual disruption. Offer choices, keep home routines, allow comfort objects, play therapy, accept regression.幼兒害怕疼痛與失控；會退化、發脾氣。提供選擇、維持家中作息、容許安慰物、遊戲治療、接納退化。
  • Infants: therapeutic touch, soft voice, pacifier for sucking even if NPO, contrasting colors and textures for stimulation.嬰兒：治療性碰觸、輕柔聲音、即使禁食也提供奶嘴吸吮、對比色與質感刺激。
  • Preschoolers: separation anxiety is less obvious; fear mutilation and invasive procedures and think illness is punishment for their thoughts. Encourage rooming-in or a favorite toy, simple explanations, play, independence, same-age peers.學齡前：分離焦慮較不明顯；害怕身體受損與侵入性處置，認為生病是自己想法或行為造成的。鼓勵陪住或帶心愛玩具、簡單說明、遊戲、獨立、同齡玩伴。

2Hospitalized School-Age Child and Adolescent住院的學齡兒童與青少年

  • School-age: worry about missing school and being forgotten by friends; fear injury, disability, death and genital exams; protest-despair-detachment seldom occur.學齡兒童：擔心缺課與被朋友遺忘；害怕受傷、失能、死亡與生殖器檢查；少出現抗議-絕望-疏離。
  • Interventions: choices, involvement in care, body diagrams for explanations, privacy, schoolwork, contact with friends, accept regression but encourage independence.措施：提供選擇、參與照護、用人體圖說明、保護隱私、學業安排、與朋友聯繫、接納退化並鼓勵獨立。
  • Adolescents: ambivalent about parents, upset at being excluded by friends, fear being different, guard sexual-development exams; may seem unafraid.青少年：對父母態度矛盾、被朋友排除會難過、害怕與眾不同、對性發展檢查防衛；外表可能裝作不怕。
  • Support: privacy, own clothes and grooming, peer contact, schooling, future planning, positive coping.支持：隱私、穿自己的衣服與整理儀容、同儕聯繫、教育、未來規劃、正向因應。

3Communication, Car Safety and Preventive Care溝通、汽車安全與預防保健

  • Infant: slow, calm approach, soft voice, respond to cries. Toddler: short concrete words, take words literally, prepare immediately before procedures, use play and let them handle equipment.嬰兒：緩慢溫和接近、輕柔聲音、回應哭聲。幼兒：簡短具體用詞、字面理解、操作前才準備、用遊戲並讓其摸器材。
  • Preschooler: simple sentences, offer choices, play and equipment handling. School-age: clear explanation, photos/dolls/medical play. Adolescent: privacy, avoid judgmental, too abstract or prying talk.學齡前：簡單句子、給選擇、遊戲與接觸器材。學齡兒童：清楚說明、照片/娃娃/醫療遊戲。青少年：保護隱私，避免批判、過度抽象或追問。
  • Back seat is safest for all children; follow manufacturer seat guidance; an 8-year-old 4 ft tall still needs a booster seat.所有兒童坐後座最安全；依製造商指引使用安全座椅；8 歲、身高 4 英尺（約 122 公分）仍需增高墊。
  • Well-child visits follow AAP/Bright Futures (screenings include development, autism, vision, hearing, lead, anemia, depression).依 AAP/Bright Futures 進行兒童健康檢查（篩檢包括發展、自閉症、視力、聽力、鉛、貧血、憂鬱）。

4Immunizations: Principles and Childhood Schedule疫苗：原則與兒童時程

  • Missed doses are given without restarting the series; vaccines can be given simultaneously; preterm infants get full doses at chronological age.漏打只需補打不需重來；可同時接種；早產兒依實足年齡給足量。
  • Contraindications: anaphylaxis to prior dose or component; live vaccines not for severe immunodeficiency, pregnancy or gelatin sensitivity; caution with moderate/severe acute illness.禁忌：先前劑量或成分過敏性休克；活性疫苗不用於嚴重免疫缺損、孕婦或明膠過敏者；中重度急性病需小心。
  • Administration: separate syringes and sites; IM in vastus lateralis (deltoid from 36 months); IM needle 1 inch 23-25 g, subcutaneous 5/8 inch 25 g; observe 15-30 minutes; report adverse events.給藥：分開針筒與部位；肌肉注射於股外側肌（36 個月起可用三角肌）；IM 針 1 英寸 23-25 號、皮下針 5/8 英寸 25 號；觀察 15-30 分鐘；通報不良反應。
  • Schedule: birth HepB; 2, 4, 6 mo DTaP, Hib, IPV, PCV, RV; 12-15 mo MMR, varicella, HepA, PCV, Hib; 4-6 yr DTaP, IPV, MMR, varicella; 11-12 yr Tdap, MCV4, HPV. Flu yearly from 6 months.時程：出生 HepB；2、4、6 個月 DTaP、Hib、IPV、PCV、RV；12-15 個月 MMR、水痘、A 肝、PCV、Hib；4-6 歲 DTaP、IPV、MMR、水痘；11-12 歲 Tdap、MCV4、HPV。6 個月起每年流感。
  • Mother HBsAg positive: infant gets HepB vaccine and HBIG within 12 hours of birth.母親 HBsAg 陽性：嬰兒 12 小時內接種 HepB 疫苗並給 HBIG。
  • Rotavirus is oral; MMR, varicella, IPV given subcutaneously; delay MMR 3-6 months after immunoglobulin; avoid aspirin after varicella vaccine (Reye syndrome).輪狀病毒為口服；MMR、水痘、IPV 皮下注射；注射免疫球蛋白後 MMR 延後 3-6 個月；水痘疫苗後避免阿斯匹靈（Reye 症候群）。

5Other Vaccines, Adult Immunizations and Reactions其他疫苗、成人疫苗與反應

  • MCV4 at 11-12 yr with booster at 16; contraindicated with history of Guillain-Barre syndrome. HPV 3 doses over 6 months if started at 11-12 yr; avoid in pregnancy.MCV4 於 11-12 歲接種、16 歲追加；有格林巴利症候群病史者禁用。HPV 若 11-12 歲開始需 3 劑（6 個月內）；孕期避免。
  • Adults: yearly flu, Tdap then Td every 10 years, Tdap each pregnancy, zoster recombinant 2 doses at 50+, pneumococcal at 65+.成人：每年流感、Tdap 後每 10 年 Td、每次懷孕打 Tdap、50 歲以上帶狀疱疹重組疫苗 2 劑、65 歲以上肺炎鏈球菌。
  • Local reactions: tenderness, redness, low fever; cool compresses first 24 hours; acetaminophen or ibuprofen every 4-6 hr.局部反應：觸痛、紅腫、低燒；前 24 小時冷敷；每 4-6 小時給乙醯胺酚或布洛芬。
  • Anaphylaxis: secure airway, epinephrine, fluids, vasopressors, CPR if needed.過敏性休克：確保呼吸道、給 epinephrine、輸液、升壓劑，必要時 CPR。
  • COVID-19 vaccine: rare myocarditis/pericarditis; report chest pain, dyspnea, palpitations.COVID-19 疫苗：罕見心肌炎/心包炎；胸痛、呼吸困難、心悸須通報。

6Infant Development嬰兒發展

  • Birth weight doubles at 5-6 months and triples at 12 months; length rises 50% by 1 year; head circumference 33-35 cm at birth.體重 5-6 個月加倍、12 個月三倍；1 歲身長增加 50%；出生頭圍 33-35 公分。
  • Posterior fontanel closes by 4 months; anterior by 18 months; soft and flat is normal; first teeth (lower central incisors) at 6-10 months.後囟門 4 個月前閉合；前囟門 18 個月前閉合；軟而平為正常；6-10 個月長出下中門牙。
  • Vital signs: newborn HR 120-160, RR 30-60; 1-year-old HR 90-130, RR 20-40, BP 90/56.生命徵象：新生兒心跳 120-160、呼吸 30-60；1 歲心跳 90-130、呼吸 20-40、血壓 90/56。
  • Nutrition: breast milk preferred for 6 months; vitamin D 400 IU for breastfed infants; iron stores depleted by 4 months; solids at 5-6 months one at a time every 4-5 days (iron-fortified rice cereal first); no cow's milk before 12 months, no honey under 12 months, no microwaving bottles, avoid choking foods.營養：6 個月內以母乳為佳；純母乳嬰兒補充維生素 D 400 IU；4 個月鐵質儲存耗盡；5-6 個月起一次一種副食品、間隔 4-5 天（先吃強化鐵米糊）；12 個月前不喝牛奶、不吃蜂蜜、不微波奶瓶、避免易噎食物。
  • Milestones: social smile 2-3 mo, rolls over 4-5 mo, sits with support and stranger fear 6-7 mo, sits unsupported and crawls 8-9 mo, walks alone 14-15 mo.里程碑：2-3 個月社交性微笑、4-5 個月翻身、6-7 個月扶坐與怕生、8-9 個月獨坐與爬行、14-15 個月獨自走路。
  • Safety: baby-proof, never leave on bed or in bath, no small toys, cords away, poison control number, never shake an infant.安全：居家防護、不讓嬰兒獨自在床上或浴缸、避免小零件玩具、繩索遠離、備毒物中心電話、絕不搖晃嬰兒。

7Toddler and Preschooler Development幼兒與學齡前兒童發展

  • Toddler: gains about 3 in/yr, 22-27 lb at 2 yr, lordosis (potbelly) normal, anterior fontanel closes 12-18 months, one nap, vital signs HR 80-120, RR 20-30.幼兒：每年長高約 7.5 公分、2 歲體重 10-12 公斤、腹部凸出（腰椎前凸）正常、前囟門 12-18 個月閉合、一次午睡、心跳 80-120、呼吸 20-30。
  • Toddler diet: 2-3 servings milk daily (24-30 oz), small meals, physiological anorexia normal, no bottle to bed, avoid choking foods, no food as reward.幼兒飲食：每天 2-3 份奶（700-800 mL）、少量多餐、生理性厭食正常、不含奶瓶入睡、避免易噎食物、不以食物當獎懲。
  • Toddler skills: runs by 2, hops on one foot by 3; about 300 words at 2; parallel play; toilet readiness signs (dry 2 hr, sits 5-10 min); bowel control precedes bladder.幼兒技能：2 歲會跑、3 歲單腳跳；2 歲約 300 字；平行遊戲；如廁準備徵象（乾燥 2 小時、可坐 5-10 分鐘）；先控制排便再控制排尿。
  • Toddler safety: back stove burners, pot handles inward, gates, window locks, never alone near water, lock poisons.幼兒安全：用後排爐頭、鍋柄朝內、安全門、窗鎖、水邊不離人、毒物上鎖。
  • Preschooler: 5 lb and 2.5-3 in per year, 12 hr sleep, alternates feet on stairs, cooperative and imaginative play, imaginary friends, magical thinking; teach fire, gun, stranger and 911 safety; guns locked unloaded.學齡前：每年增加 2.25 公斤與 6.5-7.5 公分、睡 12 小時、可雙腳交替上樓、合作性與想像遊戲、假想玩伴、魔幻思考；教導火、槍、陌生人與 119/911 安全；槍械退彈上鎖。
  • Preschooler vital signs HR 70-110, RR 16-22; vocabulary 900 words at 3 yr, 2100 at 5 yr.學齡前生命徵象心跳 70-110、呼吸 16-22；3 歲詞彙約 900 字、5 歲約 2100 字。

8School-Age, Adolescent and Adult Development學齡、青少年與成人發展

  • School-age: 2 in/yr, 4.5-6.5 lb/yr, permanent teeth from age 6, 10-12 hr sleep, competitive rule-based play, vital signs HR 60-100, RR 18-20; helmets, traffic and water safety.學齡：每年 5 公分、2-3 公斤、6 歲起恆齒、睡 10-12 小時、有規則的競爭遊戲、心跳 60-100、呼吸 18-20；戴安全帽、交通與水上安全。
  • Adolescent: puberty, menses about 2.5 years after puberty onset, risk-taking; teach about substances, seat belts, diving feet-first, STIs, internet safety; vitals HR 55-90, RR 12-20.青少年：青春期、初經約在青春期開始後 2.5 年、冒險行為；教導物質濫用、安全帶、腳先入水、性傳染病、網路安全；心跳 55-90、呼吸 12-20。
  • Early adulthood: separation from family, career/marriage decisions, ignores symptoms, STI risk. Middle adulthood: menopause/climacteric, generativity, empty nest, body image concerns.成年早期：脫離原生家庭、職涯婚姻決定、忽略症狀、性傳染病風險。成年中期：更年期、生產力（generativity）、空巢、身體形象。
  • Gender identity is personal sense of gender; gender expression is outward expression; sexual orientation is attraction. Support children with unconditional acceptance, watch for depression, prepare for bullying.性別認同為個人對自身性別的感受；性別表達為對外表現；性傾向為吸引對象。以無條件接納支持孩子、留意憂鬱、預備霸凌因應。

9End-of-Life Care臨終照護

  • Care follows client wishes: advance directives, organ donation, withholding or withdrawing treatment, CPR. Palliative/hospice care focuses on comfort not cure.依個案意願：預立醫囑、器官捐贈、不給予或撤除治療、CPR。安寧緩和照護著重舒適而非治癒。
  • Near-death signs: slowed metabolism, blurred vision, hearing lost last, noisy respirations (death rattle), Cheyne-Stokes, slow HR and falling BP, cool mottled extremities, less urine, constipation, Kennedy terminal ulcer on sacrum.臨終徵象：代謝減慢、視力模糊、聽力最後消失、痰鳴（死前嘎嘎聲）、陳施氏呼吸、心跳慢血壓降、四肢冰冷斑駁、尿量減少、便秘、薦骨肯尼迪終末潰瘍。
  • Comfort care: never delay pain medication; elevate head of bed and oxygen for dyspnea; oral care, ice chips, do not force food; do not restrain restless client.舒適照護：不延誤止痛；呼吸困難時抬高床頭並給氧；口腔照護、冰塊、不強迫進食；躁動者不約束。
  • Address fears: pain, loneliness, being meaningless; allow family presence, touch if culturally acceptable, life review.處理恐懼：疼痛、孤獨、無意義感；容許家屬陪伴、文化允許時碰觸、生命回顧。
  • Respect culture and religion; ask the family about preferences. Postmortem care: dignity, check organ donor status, close eyes, replace dentures, wash, remove tubes, allow viewing; preserve evidence for medical examiner cases.尊重文化與宗教，詢問家屬偏好。遺體護理：維護尊嚴、確認器官捐贈、闔眼、裝回假牙、清潔、拔除管路、讓家屬瞻仰；需法醫案件須保留證據。

Immunizations: Principles and Childhood Schedule疫苗：原則與兒童時程
  • Missed doses are given without restarting the series; vaccines can be given simultaneously; preterm infants get full doses at chronological age.漏打只需補打不需重來；可同時接種；早產兒依實足年齡給足量。
  • Contraindications: anaphylaxis to prior dose or component; live vaccines not for severe immunodeficiency, pregnancy or gelatin sensitivity; caution with moderate/severe acute illness.禁忌：先前劑量或成分過敏性休克；活性疫苗不用於嚴重免疫缺損、孕婦或明膠過敏者；中重度急性病需小心。
  • Administration: separate syringes and sites; IM in vastus lateralis (deltoid from 36 months); IM needle 1 inch 23-25 g, subcutaneous 5/8 inch 25 g; observe 15-30 minutes; report adverse events.給藥：分開針筒與部位；肌肉注射於股外側肌（36 個月起可用三角肌）；IM 針 1 英寸 23-25 號、皮下針 5/8 英寸 25 號；觀察 15-30 分鐘；通報不良反應。
  • Schedule: birth HepB; 2, 4, 6 mo DTaP, Hib, IPV, PCV, RV; 12-15 mo MMR, varicella, HepA, PCV, Hib; 4-6 yr DTaP, IPV, MMR, varicella; 11-12 yr Tdap, MCV4, HPV. Flu yearly from 6 months.時程：出生 HepB；2、4、6 個月 DTaP、Hib、IPV、PCV、RV；12-15 個月 MMR、水痘、A 肝、PCV、Hib；4-6 歲 DTaP、IPV、MMR、水痘；11-12 歲 Tdap、MCV4、HPV。6 個月起每年流感。
  • Mother HBsAg positive: infant gets HepB vaccine and HBIG within 12 hours of birth.母親 HBsAg 陽性：嬰兒 12 小時內接種 HepB 疫苗並給 HBIG。
  • Rotavirus is oral; MMR, varicella, IPV given subcutaneously; delay MMR 3-6 months after immunoglobulin; avoid aspirin after varicella vaccine (Reye syndrome).輪狀病毒為口服；MMR、水痘、IPV 皮下注射；注射免疫球蛋白後 MMR 延後 3-6 個月；水痘疫苗後避免阿斯匹靈（Reye 症候群）。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Other Vaccines, Adult Immunizations and Reactions其他疫苗、成人疫苗與反應
  • MCV4 at 11-12 yr with booster at 16; contraindicated with history of Guillain-Barre syndrome. HPV 3 doses over 6 months if started at 11-12 yr; avoid in pregnancy.MCV4 於 11-12 歲接種、16 歲追加；有格林巴利症候群病史者禁用。HPV 若 11-12 歲開始需 3 劑（6 個月內）；孕期避免。
  • Adults: yearly flu, Tdap then Td every 10 years, Tdap each pregnancy, zoster recombinant 2 doses at 50+, pneumococcal at 65+.成人：每年流感、Tdap 後每 10 年 Td、每次懷孕打 Tdap、50 歲以上帶狀疱疹重組疫苗 2 劑、65 歲以上肺炎鏈球菌。
  • Local reactions: tenderness, redness, low fever; cool compresses first 24 hours; acetaminophen or ibuprofen every 4-6 hr.局部反應：觸痛、紅腫、低燒；前 24 小時冷敷；每 4-6 小時給乙醯胺酚或布洛芬。
  • Anaphylaxis: secure airway, epinephrine, fluids, vasopressors, CPR if needed.過敏性休克：確保呼吸道、給 epinephrine、輸液、升壓劑，必要時 CPR。
  • COVID-19 vaccine: rare myocarditis/pericarditis; report chest pain, dyspnea, palpitations.COVID-19 疫苗：罕見心肌炎/心包炎；胸痛、呼吸困難、心悸須通報。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Infant Development嬰兒發展
  • Birth weight doubles at 5-6 months and triples at 12 months; length rises 50% by 1 year; head circumference 33-35 cm at birth.體重 5-6 個月加倍、12 個月三倍；1 歲身長增加 50%；出生頭圍 33-35 公分。
  • Posterior fontanel closes by 4 months; anterior by 18 months; soft and flat is normal; first teeth (lower central incisors) at 6-10 months.後囟門 4 個月前閉合；前囟門 18 個月前閉合；軟而平為正常；6-10 個月長出下中門牙。
  • Vital signs: newborn HR 120-160, RR 30-60; 1-year-old HR 90-130, RR 20-40, BP 90/56.生命徵象：新生兒心跳 120-160、呼吸 30-60；1 歲心跳 90-130、呼吸 20-40、血壓 90/56。
  • Nutrition: breast milk preferred for 6 months; vitamin D 400 IU for breastfed infants; iron stores depleted by 4 months; solids at 5-6 months one at a time every 4-5 days (iron-fortified rice cereal first); no cow's milk before 12 months, no honey under 12 months, no microwaving bottles, avoid choking foods.營養：6 個月內以母乳為佳；純母乳嬰兒補充維生素 D 400 IU；4 個月鐵質儲存耗盡；5-6 個月起一次一種副食品、間隔 4-5 天（先吃強化鐵米糊）；12 個月前不喝牛奶、不吃蜂蜜、不微波奶瓶、避免易噎食物。
  • Milestones: social smile 2-3 mo, rolls over 4-5 mo, sits with support and stranger fear 6-7 mo, sits unsupported and crawls 8-9 mo, walks alone 14-15 mo.里程碑：2-3 個月社交性微笑、4-5 個月翻身、6-7 個月扶坐與怕生、8-9 個月獨坐與爬行、14-15 個月獨自走路。
  • Safety: baby-proof, never leave on bed or in bath, no small toys, cords away, poison control number, never shake an infant.安全：居家防護、不讓嬰兒獨自在床上或浴缸、避免小零件玩具、繩索遠離、備毒物中心電話、絕不搖晃嬰兒。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Toddler and Preschooler Development幼兒與學齡前兒童發展
  • Toddler: gains about 3 in/yr, 22-27 lb at 2 yr, lordosis (potbelly) normal, anterior fontanel closes 12-18 months, one nap, vital signs HR 80-120, RR 20-30.幼兒：每年長高約 7.5 公分、2 歲體重 10-12 公斤、腹部凸出（腰椎前凸）正常、前囟門 12-18 個月閉合、一次午睡、心跳 80-120、呼吸 20-30。
  • Toddler diet: 2-3 servings milk daily (24-30 oz), small meals, physiological anorexia normal, no bottle to bed, avoid choking foods, no food as reward.幼兒飲食：每天 2-3 份奶（700-800 mL）、少量多餐、生理性厭食正常、不含奶瓶入睡、避免易噎食物、不以食物當獎懲。
  • Toddler skills: runs by 2, hops on one foot by 3; about 300 words at 2; parallel play; toilet readiness signs (dry 2 hr, sits 5-10 min); bowel control precedes bladder.幼兒技能：2 歲會跑、3 歲單腳跳；2 歲約 300 字；平行遊戲；如廁準備徵象（乾燥 2 小時、可坐 5-10 分鐘）；先控制排便再控制排尿。
  • Toddler safety: back stove burners, pot handles inward, gates, window locks, never alone near water, lock poisons.幼兒安全：用後排爐頭、鍋柄朝內、安全門、窗鎖、水邊不離人、毒物上鎖。
  • Preschooler: 5 lb and 2.5-3 in per year, 12 hr sleep, alternates feet on stairs, cooperative and imaginative play, imaginary friends, magical thinking; teach fire, gun, stranger and 911 safety; guns locked unloaded.學齡前：每年增加 2.25 公斤與 6.5-7.5 公分、睡 12 小時、可雙腳交替上樓、合作性與想像遊戲、假想玩伴、魔幻思考；教導火、槍、陌生人與 119/911 安全；槍械退彈上鎖。
  • Preschooler vital signs HR 70-110, RR 16-22; vocabulary 900 words at 3 yr, 2100 at 5 yr.學齡前生命徵象心跳 70-110、呼吸 16-22；3 歲詞彙約 900 字、5 歲約 2100 字。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
School-Age, Adolescent and Adult Development學齡、青少年與成人發展
  • School-age: 2 in/yr, 4.5-6.5 lb/yr, permanent teeth from age 6, 10-12 hr sleep, competitive rule-based play, vital signs HR 60-100, RR 18-20; helmets, traffic and water safety.學齡：每年 5 公分、2-3 公斤、6 歲起恆齒、睡 10-12 小時、有規則的競爭遊戲、心跳 60-100、呼吸 18-20；戴安全帽、交通與水上安全。
  • Adolescent: puberty, menses about 2.5 years after puberty onset, risk-taking; teach about substances, seat belts, diving feet-first, STIs, internet safety; vitals HR 55-90, RR 12-20.青少年：青春期、初經約在青春期開始後 2.5 年、冒險行為；教導物質濫用、安全帶、腳先入水、性傳染病、網路安全；心跳 55-90、呼吸 12-20。
  • Early adulthood: separation from family, career/marriage decisions, ignores symptoms, STI risk. Middle adulthood: menopause/climacteric, generativity, empty nest, body image concerns.成年早期：脫離原生家庭、職涯婚姻決定、忽略症狀、性傳染病風險。成年中期：更年期、生產力（generativity）、空巢、身體形象。
  • Gender identity is personal sense of gender; gender expression is outward expression; sexual orientation is attraction. Support children with unconditional acceptance, watch for depression, prepare for bullying.性別認同為個人對自身性別的感受；性別表達為對外表現；性傾向為吸引對象。以無條件接納支持孩子、留意憂鬱、預備霸凌因應。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
End-of-Life Care臨終照護
  • Care follows client wishes: advance directives, organ donation, withholding or withdrawing treatment, CPR. Palliative/hospice care focuses on comfort not cure.依個案意願：預立醫囑、器官捐贈、不給予或撤除治療、CPR。安寧緩和照護著重舒適而非治癒。
  • Near-death signs: slowed metabolism, blurred vision, hearing lost last, noisy respirations (death rattle), Cheyne-Stokes, slow HR and falling BP, cool mottled extremities, less urine, constipation, Kennedy terminal ulcer on sacrum.臨終徵象：代謝減慢、視力模糊、聽力最後消失、痰鳴（死前嘎嘎聲）、陳施氏呼吸、心跳慢血壓降、四肢冰冷斑駁、尿量減少、便秘、薦骨肯尼迪終末潰瘍。
  • Comfort care: never delay pain medication; elevate head of bed and oxygen for dyspnea; oral care, ice chips, do not force food; do not restrain restless client.舒適照護：不延誤止痛；呼吸困難時抬高床頭並給氧；口腔照護、冰塊、不強迫進食；躁動者不約束。
  • Address fears: pain, loneliness, being meaningless; allow family presence, touch if culturally acceptable, life review.處理恐懼：疼痛、孤獨、無意義感；容許家屬陪伴、文化允許時碰觸、生命回顧。
  • Respect culture and religion; ask the family about preferences. Postmortem care: dignity, check organ donor status, close eyes, replace dentures, wash, remove tubes, allow viewing; preserve evidence for medical examiner cases.尊重文化與宗教，詢問家屬偏好。遺體護理：維護尊嚴、確認器官捐贈、闔眼、裝回假牙、清潔、拔除管路、讓家屬瞻仰；需法醫案件須保留證據。
