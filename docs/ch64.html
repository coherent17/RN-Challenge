Summary重點摘要Test-critical考試關鍵Mnemonics記憶口訣

This chapter groups the drugs used for HIV/AIDS, transplant and autoimmune immunosuppression, and bacterial/fungal/viral infections. For each class, know the action, signature adverse effects, the lab or sign to monitor, and the teaching points. Many NCLEX items simply ask 'which adverse effect goes with which drug class'.本章依藥物類別整理 HIV/AIDS 用藥、移植與自體免疫疾病的免疫抑制劑，以及抗細菌、抗黴菌、抗病毒藥物。每一類都要記住作用、代表性不良反應、需監測的檢驗或徵象，以及衛教重點。NCLEX 常考「哪個不良反應對應哪一類藥」。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1HIV/AIDS drugs: overview and HAARTHIV/AIDS 藥物：總覽與 HAART

  • Drug classes: NRTIs, NNRTIs, protease inhibitors (PIs), integrase inhibitors, fusion inhibitor (enfuvirtide) and the CCR5 antagonist maraviroc.藥物類別：核苷(酸)類逆轉錄酶抑制劑 NRTI、非核苷類 NNRTI、蛋白酶抑制劑 PI、整合酶抑制劑、融合抑制劑 enfuvirtide，以及 CCR5 拮抗劑 maraviroc。
  • NRTIs and NNRTIs block reverse transcriptase; PIs block the enzyme protease; fusion inhibitors block HIV binding/entry into cells; maraviroc binds CCR5 and blocks viral entry; integrase inhibitors stop viral replication.NRTI 與 NNRTI 抑制逆轉錄酶；PI 抑制蛋白酶；融合抑制劑阻止 HIV 與細胞結合；maraviroc 結合 CCR5 阻斷病毒進入；整合酶抑制劑阻止病毒複製。
  • Standard therapy is 3-4 drugs together (HAART). It is not a cure, but it delays or reverses immune loss and prolongs life.標準治療是同時使用 3–4 種藥物（HAART）。它不能治癒，但可延緩或逆轉免疫功能喪失並延長壽命。
  • Other drugs treat complications and opportunistic infections; the client with HIV/AIDS is at high risk of opportunistic infection.其他藥物用於治療併發症與伺機性感染；HIV/AIDS 客戶有高度伺機性感染風險。

2NRTIs (nucleoside/nucleotide RT inhibitors)NRTI 類藥物

  • Abacavir: nausea; watch for hypersensitivity (fever, rash, N/V/D, malaise, sore throat, cough, dyspnea).Abacavir：噁心；監測過敏反應（發燒、皮疹、噁心嘔吐腹瀉、倦怠、喉痛、咳嗽、呼吸困難）。
  • Didanosine: peripheral neuropathy, hepatotoxicity, pancreatitis (rising serum amylase may mean drug must be stopped).Didanosine：周邊神經病變、肝毒性、胰臟炎（血清澱粉酶上升可能需停藥）。
  • Stavudine: peripheral neuropathy and pancreatitis; check gait and paresthesias.Stavudine：周邊神經病變與胰臟炎；評估步態與感覺異常。
  • Zidovudine and lamivudine/zidovudine: anemia, neutropenia/leukopenia, myopathy; combinations may add lactic acidosis with severe hepatomegaly.Zidovudine 及 lamivudine/zidovudine：貧血、嗜中性球/白血球減少、肌病變；複方可能併有乳酸中毒與嚴重肝腫大。
  • Emtricitabine: headache, diarrhea, nausea, rash, palm/sole hyperpigmentation, lactic acidosis. Lamivudine: nausea, nasal congestion. Tenofovir: nausea and vomiting.Emtricitabine：頭痛、腹瀉、噁心、皮疹、手掌腳底色素沉著、乳酸中毒。Lamivudine：噁心、鼻塞。Tenofovir：噁心嘔吐。

3NNRTIs, protease inhibitors, integrase, fusion, CCR5NNRTI、PI、整合酶、融合與 CCR5 抑制劑

  • NNRTIs: rash is the common theme; nevirapine can cause Stevens-Johnson syndrome and hepatitis; efavirenz causes dizziness, confusion, abnormal dreams; etravirine adds hypertension and neuropathy.NNRTI：共通特徵是皮疹；nevirapine 可致 Stevens-Johnson 症候群與肝炎；efavirenz 造成頭暈、意識混亂、怪夢；etravirine 另有高血壓與神經病變。
  • Protease inhibitors (-navir): GI upset, altered taste, circumoral paresthesia, hyperglycemia, hyperlipidemia, fat redistribution, liver effects.蛋白酶抑制劑（-navir）：腸胃不適、味覺改變、口周感覺異常、高血糖、高血脂、脂肪重新分佈、肝功能異常。
  • Specific PIs: indinavir causes kidney stones/nephritis and hyperbilirubinemia; tipranavir is hepatotoxic; saquinavir causes photosensitivity; atazanavir raises bleeding risk in hemophilia.特定 PI：indinavir 造成腎結石/腎炎與高膽紅素血症；tipranavir 有肝毒性；saquinavir 造成光敏感；atazanavir 使血友病患出血風險增加。
  • Integrase inhibitors (-gravir): nausea, diarrhea, headache, fatigue; dolutegravir: hypersensitivity and liver problems.整合酶抑制劑（-gravir）：噁心、腹瀉、頭痛、疲倦；dolutegravir 可致過敏與肝問題。
  • Enfuvirtide (fusion inhibitor, injected): injection-site reactions, fatigue, insomnia, peripheral neuropathy. Maraviroc: cough, fever, rash, upper respiratory infection, liver injury, cardiovascular events.Enfuvirtide（注射）：注射部位反應、疲倦、失眠、周邊神經病變。Maraviroc：咳嗽、發燒、皮疹、上呼吸道感染、肝損傷、心血管事件。

4Treatment of opportunistic infections伺機性感染用藥

  • Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia and toxoplasma encephalitis: sulfamethoxazole/trimethoprim (also pentamidine, atovaquone).肺囊蟲肺炎與弓形蟲腦炎：sulfamethoxazole/trimethoprim（亦可用 pentamidine、atovaquone）。
  • Antifungals (amphotericin B, fluconazole, itraconazole, ketoconazole, voriconazole): candidiasis and cryptococcal meningitis.抗黴菌藥（amphotericin B、fluconazole、itraconazole、ketoconazole、voriconazole）：念珠菌感染與隱球菌腦膜炎。
  • Antivirals (acyclovir, ganciclovir, foscarnet, valacyclovir): CMV retinitis, herpes simplex, varicella-zoster.抗病毒藥（acyclovir、ganciclovir、foscarnet、valacyclovir）：CMV 視網膜炎、單純疱疹、水痘帶狀疱疹。
  • Fever during pentamidine therapy in AIDS suggests a new infection because the drug can cause leukopenia, thrombocytopenia, and anemia.AIDS 客戶使用 pentamidine 時發燒，應考慮新的感染，因藥物可造成白血球、血小板減少與貧血。

5Immunosuppressants免疫抑制劑

  • Uses: prevent organ/tissue rejection after transplant and treat autoimmune disease (e.g., lupus). Classes: calcineurin inhibitors, cytotoxic drugs, antibodies, mTOR/other drugs, glucocorticoids.用途：預防移植排斥並治療自體免疫疾病（如狼瘡）。類別：calcineurin 抑制劑、細胞毒性藥、抗體、mTOR/其他、糖皮質激素。
  • Cyclosporine: nephrotoxicity, infection, hypertension, hirsutism. Tacrolimus: nephrotoxicity, neurotoxicity, hyperkalemia, hyperglycemia, hypertension, gum hyperplasia.Cyclosporine：腎毒性、感染、高血壓、多毛。Tacrolimus：腎毒性、神經毒性、高血鉀、高血糖、高血壓、牙齦增生。
  • Azathioprine: neutropenia, thrombocytopenia (renal transplant). Cyclophosphamide: neutropenia, hemorrhagic cystitis. Methotrexate: hepatic fibrosis/cirrhosis, bone marrow suppression, stomatitis, renal damage.Azathioprine：嗜中性球與血小板減少（腎移植）。Cyclophosphamide：嗜中性球減少、出血性膀胱炎。Methotrexate：肝纖維化/肝硬化、骨髓抑制、口腔潰瘍、腎損傷。
  • Mycophenolate: diarrhea, vomiting, neutropenia, sepsis, lymphoma risk. Basiliximab: severe acute hypersensitivity, including anaphylaxis. Antithymocyte/lymphocyte immune globulin and muromonab-CD3: fever, chills, leukopenia, skin reactions, and anaphylactoid reactions.Mycophenolate：腹瀉、嘔吐、嗜中性球減少、敗血症、淋巴瘤風險。Basiliximab：嚴重急性過敏反應，包括過敏性休克。抗胸腺細胞/淋巴細胞球蛋白與 muromonab-CD3：發燒、畏寒、白血球減少、皮膚反應，以及類過敏反應。
  • Sirolimus: infection, elevated cholesterol/triglycerides, renal injury, rash, anemia, thrombocytopenia, hypokalemia. Everolimus: mouth sores, edema, infection.Sirolimus：感染、膽固醇與三酸甘油酯上升、腎損傷、皮疹、貧血、血小板減少、低血鉀。Everolimus：口腔潰瘍、水腫、感染。
  • Monitor all immunosuppressed clients closely for signs of infection.所有免疫抑制客戶都要密切監測感染徵象。

6Antimicrobials: classes and adverse effects抗微生物藥：類別與不良反應

  • Aminoglycosides (-mycin/-micin, e.g., gentamicin, amikacin): ototoxicity, nephrotoxicity, confusion, GI irritation, palpitations, BP changes, hypersensitivity. Report hearing loss at once.胺基糖苷類（如 gentamicin、amikacin）：耳毒性、腎毒性、意識混亂、腸胃刺激、心悸、血壓變化、過敏。聽力減退須立即通報。
  • Cephalosporins (cef-/ceph-): GI upset, pseudomembranous colitis, nephrotoxicity, superinfection. Fluoroquinolones (-floxacin): CNS effects, photosensitivity, tendinopathy, GI effects.頭孢菌素類：腸胃不適、偽膜性結腸炎、腎毒性、二重感染。氟喹諾酮類（-floxacin）：中樞神經效應、光敏感、肌腱病變、腸胃效應。
  • Macrolides (-thromycin) and lincosamides (clindamycin): GI effects, pseudomembranous colitis, superinfection.大環內酯類（-thromycin）與林可醯胺類（clindamycin）：腸胃效應、偽膜性結腸炎、二重感染。
  • Penicillins: hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis, GI effects, sore mouth, furry tongue, superinfection. Sulfonamides: nephrotoxicity, hepatotoxicity, bone marrow suppression, rash/photosensitivity, neurologic symptoms.青黴素類：過敏/過敏性休克、腸胃效應、口腔痛、毛舌、二重感染。磺胺類：腎毒性、肝毒性、骨髓抑制、皮疹/光敏感、神經症狀。
  • Tetracyclines (-cycline): tooth staining and bone damage, photosensitivity, hepatotoxicity, GI effects. Aztreonam: GI, hepatotoxicity, allergy.四環黴素類（-cycline）：牙齒染色與骨骼傷害、光敏感、肝毒性、腸胃效應。Aztreonam：腸胃、肝毒性、過敏。
  • Antifungals: GI effects, headache, rash, anemia, hepatotoxicity, hearing loss, peripheral neuritis. Ketoconazole: give with food, avoid alcohol, antacids 2 h apart, avoid sun, monitor liver function.抗黴菌藥：腸胃效應、頭痛、皮疹、貧血、肝毒性、聽力喪失、周邊神經炎。Ketoconazole：隨餐服用、避免酒精、制酸劑間隔 2 小時、避免日曬、監測肝功能。

7Nursing considerations for antimicrobials抗微生物藥護理考量

  • Assess for allergies before the first dose and review baseline labs; monitor labs during therapy.首次給藥前評估過敏史並檢視基準檢驗值；治療期間持續監測。
  • Report adverse effects to the primary health care provider; monitor intake and output and encourage fluids unless contraindicated.通報不良反應給醫師；監測輸入輸出量，除非禁忌否則鼓勵飲水。
  • Institute safety precautions for CNS effects; teach how to take the drug and to finish the full course.因可能有中樞神經效應，採取安全措施；教導用藥方式並強調完成整個療程。
  • Severe diarrhea on a cephalosporin: contact precautions, monitor fluid/electrolytes, oral fluids (avoid caffeine, sugar, fatty/spicy food), ask the provider about probiotics and antidiarrheals.使用頭孢菌素後嚴重腹瀉：接觸隔離、監測體液電解質、口服補液（避免咖啡因、高糖、油膩辛辣）、詢問醫師是否使用益生菌與止瀉藥。
  • Immunizations are covered in Chapter 19.疫苗接種請見第 19 章。

  • Sirolimus: infection, elevated cholesterol/triglycerides, renal injury, rash, anemia, thrombocytopenia, hypokalemia. Everolimus: mouth sores, edema, infection.Sirolimus：感染、膽固醇與三酸甘油酯上升、腎損傷、皮疹、貧血、血小板減少、低血鉀。Everolimus：口腔潰瘍、水腫、感染。
  • Monitor all immunosuppressed clients closely for signs of infection.所有免疫抑制客戶都要密切監測感染徵象。

Antimicrobials: classes and adverse effects抗微生物藥：類別與不良反應
  • Aminoglycosides (-mycin/-micin, e.g., gentamicin, amikacin): ototoxicity, nephrotoxicity, confusion, GI irritation, palpitations, BP changes, hypersensitivity. Report hearing loss at once.胺基糖苷類（如 gentamicin、amikacin）：耳毒性、腎毒性、意識混亂、腸胃刺激、心悸、血壓變化、過敏。聽力減退須立即通報。
  • Cephalosporins (cef-/ceph-): GI upset, pseudomembranous colitis, nephrotoxicity, superinfection. Fluoroquinolones (-floxacin): CNS effects, photosensitivity, tendinopathy, GI effects.頭孢菌素類：腸胃不適、偽膜性結腸炎、腎毒性、二重感染。氟喹諾酮類（-floxacin）：中樞神經效應、光敏感、肌腱病變、腸胃效應。
  • Macrolides (-thromycin) and lincosamides (clindamycin): GI effects, pseudomembranous colitis, superinfection.大環內酯類（-thromycin）與林可醯胺類（clindamycin）：腸胃效應、偽膜性結腸炎、二重感染。
  • Penicillins: hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis, GI effects, sore mouth, furry tongue, superinfection. Sulfonamides: nephrotoxicity, hepatotoxicity, bone marrow suppression, rash/photosensitivity, neurologic symptoms.青黴素類：過敏/過敏性休克、腸胃效應、口腔痛、毛舌、二重感染。磺胺類：腎毒性、肝毒性、骨髓抑制、皮疹/光敏感、神經症狀。
  • Tetracyclines (-cycline): tooth staining and bone damage, photosensitivity, hepatotoxicity, GI effects. Aztreonam: GI, hepatotoxicity, allergy.四環黴素類（-cycline）：牙齒染色與骨骼傷害、光敏感、肝毒性、腸胃效應。Aztreonam：腸胃、肝毒性、過敏。
  • Antifungals: GI effects, headache, rash, anemia, hepatotoxicity, hearing loss, peripheral neuritis. Ketoconazole: give with food, avoid alcohol, antacids 2 h apart, avoid sun, monitor liver function.抗黴菌藥：腸胃效應、頭痛、皮疹、貧血、肝毒性、聽力喪失、周邊神經炎。Ketoconazole：隨餐服用、避免酒精、制酸劑間隔 2 小時、避免日曬、監測肝功能。

Nursing considerations for antimicrobials抗微生物藥護理考量
  • Assess for allergies before the first dose and review baseline labs; monitor labs during therapy.首次給藥前評估過敏史並檢視基準檢驗值；治療期間持續監測。
  • Report adverse effects to the primary health care provider; monitor intake and output and encourage fluids unless contraindicated.通報不良反應給醫師；監測輸入輸出量，除非禁忌否則鼓勵飲水。
  • Institute safety precautions for CNS effects; teach how to take the drug and to finish the full course.因可能有中樞神經效應，採取安全措施；教導用藥方式並強調完成整個療程。
  • Severe diarrhea on a cephalosporin: contact precautions, monitor fluid/electrolytes, oral fluids (avoid caffeine, sugar, fatty/spicy food), ask the provider about probiotics and antidiarrheals.使用頭孢菌素後嚴重腹瀉：接觸隔離、監測體液電解質、口服補液（避免咖啡因、高糖、油膩辛辣）、詢問醫師是否使用益生菌與止瀉藥。
  • Immunizations are covered in Chapter 19.疫苗接種請見第 19 章。
