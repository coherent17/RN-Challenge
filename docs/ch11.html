This chapter reviews macronutrients, vitamins, minerals and water, markers of malnutrition, healthy-eating guidance, nutrition across the life span, and each therapeutic diet (liquid, soft, fiber, cardiac, renal, protein-, sodium- and potassium-modified, and others), plus vegetarian diets and enteral feeding. Expect NCLEX items asking which foods a client should choose or avoid.本章複習巨量營養素、維生素、礦物質與水分、營養不良指標、健康飲食原則、各生命階段營養,以及各類治療性飲食(流質、軟質、纖維、心臟、腎臟、蛋白質/鈉/鉀調整等)、素食與管灌飲食。NCLEX 常考「患者應選擇或避免哪些食物」。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Nutrients: carbohydrates, fats, proteins營養素:醣類、脂肪、蛋白質

  • Carbohydrates (4 kcal/g) are the preferred fuel for brain and muscle; sources include milk, grains, fruits and vegetables.醣類(4 kcal/g)是腦與肌肉偏好的能量來源;來源包括奶類、穀類、水果與蔬菜。
  • Fats (9 kcal/g) store energy, protect organs, keep body warm and carry fat-soluble vitamins; deficiency of essential fatty acids causes cold intolerance, skin lesions, infection risk and amenorrhea.脂肪(9 kcal/g)儲存能量、保護器官、維持體溫並協助吸收脂溶性維生素;必需脂肪酸缺乏會導致怕冷、皮膚病變、易感染與閉經。
  • Proteins (4 kcal/g) build and repair tissue, balance fluids and acid-base, make antibodies, enzymes and hormones.蛋白質(4 kcal/g)建構與修復組織、調節體液與酸鹼、製造抗體、酵素與荷爾蒙。
  • Complete proteins contain all essential amino acids; inadequate protein causes protein-energy malnutrition and wasting.完全蛋白質含所有必需胺基酸;蛋白質不足會造成蛋白質熱量營養不良與肌肉脂肪消耗。
  • Pregnancy, lactation, infancy, childhood and adolescence have special needs; aging also changes needs.懷孕、哺乳、嬰兒期、兒童期與青春期有特殊需求;老化也會改變營養需求。

2Vitamins, minerals and water維生素、礦物質與水

  • Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) are stored and can reach toxic levels; water-soluble (B vitamins, C) are excreted in urine.脂溶性維生素(A、D、E、K)會儲存,過量可中毒;水溶性維生素(B 群、C)隨尿液排出。
  • Vitamin sources: B12 in meat/liver; folic acid in leafy greens and liver; C in citrus, tomatoes, broccoli; K in green leafy and cruciferous vegetables; thiamine in pork, legumes, nuts, whole grains.維生素來源:B12 在肉類/肝臟;葉酸在綠葉蔬菜與肝臟;C 在柑橘、番茄、花椰菜;K 在綠葉與十字花科蔬菜;硫胺素(B1)在豬肉、豆類、堅果、全穀。
  • Mineral sources: calcium (dairy, tofu, greens); iron (red meat, liver, dried beans/fruits, fortified cereals); potassium (bananas, citrus, potatoes, melon, meats); magnesium (greens, nuts, legumes); sodium (table salt, processed foods).礦物質來源:鈣(乳製品、豆腐、綠葉蔬菜);鐵(紅肉、肝臟、乾豆/乾果、強化穀片);鉀(香蕉、柑橘、馬鈴薯、哈密瓜、肉類);鎂(綠葉蔬菜、堅果、豆類);鈉(食鹽、加工食品)。
  • Water is 60-70% of body weight; survival without it is limited to a few days.水佔體重 60-70%;缺水僅能存活數天。

3Malnutrition markers and healthy eating營養不良指標與健康飲食

  • Short-term protein status: retinol-binding protein, prealbumin, transferrin, creatinine, BUN. Long-term protein status: serum albumin. Nitrogen balance uses urinary urea.短期蛋白質狀態:視黃醇結合蛋白、前白蛋白、運鐵蛋白、肌酸酐、BUN。長期蛋白質狀態:血清白蛋白。氮平衡以尿中尿素評估。
  • CBC with indices helps separate anemias from nutritional deficiencies.全血球計數與紅血球指數有助區分貧血與營養缺乏。
  • Always assess ability to eat and swallow and support independence in eating.務必評估進食與吞嚥能力,並盡量促進自行進食。
  • MyPlate/Dietary Guidelines teaching: half the plate fruits and vegetables, at least half of grains whole, low-fat milk, varied proteins, less sodium/saturated fat/added sugar, water instead of sugary drinks.MyPlate/飲食指南教導:半盤水果與蔬菜、至少一半穀類為全穀、低脂奶、多樣蛋白質、減少鈉/飽和脂肪/添加糖、以水取代含糖飲料。
  • Consider culture, spiritual beliefs and personal choices when planning diets.規劃飲食時須考量文化、宗教與個人選擇。

4Nutrition across the life span生命各階段的營養

  • Infants: breast/chest-feeding exclusively about 6 months; add new foods one at a time, 4-7 days apart; no cow's milk before 1 year; no honey or corn syrup before 1 year (botulism).嬰兒:約 6 個月純母乳;新食物一次一種、間隔 4-7 天;1 歲前不喝牛奶;1 歲前不吃蜂蜜或玉米糖漿(肉毒桿菌風險)。
  • Toddlers: more than 24 oz milk/day risks milk anemia; avoid choking hazards (hot dogs, grapes, nuts, popcorn, candy, raw vegetables); finger foods build independence.幼兒:每日牛奶超過 24 盎司有「牛奶貧血」風險;避免噎塞食物(熱狗、葡萄、堅果、爆米花、糖果、生菜);手抓食物可培養獨立。
  • School-age: watch high fat/sugar/salt snacks and childhood obesity; adolescents need more protein, calcium and iron and are at risk for eating disorders.學齡兒童:留意高脂/糖/鹽零食與肥胖;青少年需要更多蛋白質、鈣與鐵,且是飲食障礙好發期。
  • Pregnancy: protein 60 g/day, more calcium, iron and folic acid. Lactation: about 500 extra kcal/day, plenty of fluids, avoid alcohol and caffeine.懷孕:蛋白質每日 60 公克,增加鈣、鐵與葉酸。哺乳:每日約多 500 大卡、足量水分,避免酒精與咖啡因。
  • Older adults: lower energy needs, reduced taste, smell and thirst, dental changes; ensure calcium and vitamin D; discourage grapefruit with many medications.老年人:能量需求下降、味覺嗅覺與口渴感減退、牙齒變化;確保鈣與維生素 D;許多藥物不宜與葡萄柚併用。

5Liquid and texture-modified diets流質與質地調整飲食

  • Clear liquid: water, broth, clear juice, gelatin, ice pops, carbonated drinks, tea/coffee; short-term only; deficient in calories and nutrients. Dairy and opaque juices are not clear; avoid red/purple dye before colon exams.清流質:水、清湯、澄清果汁、果凍、冰棒、碳酸飲料、茶/咖啡;僅短期使用,熱量與營養不足。乳製品與不透明果汁不算清流質;腸道檢查前避免紅/紫色染料。
  • Weight change of 1 kg equals about 1 L of fluid gained or lost; monitor I&O and weight on liquid diets.體重變化 1 公斤約等於 1 公升水分增減;流質飲食期間須監測輸出入量與體重。
  • Full liquid adds milk, ice cream, pudding, custard, strained soups and cereals; use supplements if used more than 3 days.全流質加入牛奶、冰淇淋、布丁、卡士達、過濾湯品與精製穀粥;使用超過 3 天需營養補充品。
  • Mechanical soft: pureed, ground or chopped foods for chewing difficulty or dysphagia; avoid nuts, dried and raw fruits/vegetables, fried and tough meats.機械軟質:將食物打泥、絞碎或切碎,適用咀嚼困難或吞嚥困難;避免堅果、乾果、生蔬果、油炸與粗硬肉類。
  • Soft diet: for mouth sores, oral surgery, jaw fracture, stroke; serve cooler foods for mouth sores; avoid nuts/seeds, raw produce, fried foods and whole grains; no straw for dysphagia.軟質飲食:適用口腔潰瘍、口腔手術、下顎骨折、中風;口腔潰瘍給較涼的食物;避免堅果/種子、生蔬果、油炸與全穀類;吞嚥困難者不可使用吸管。

6Fiber and cardiac-related diets纖維與心臟相關飲食

  • Low-fiber (low-residue) for IBD, partial obstruction, gastroenteritis, diarrhea: white bread, refined cereals, white rice, peeled potatoes; avoid raw fruits (except bananas), nuts, seeds, whole grains.低纖維(低渣)飲食適用發炎性腸道疾病、部分腸阻塞、腸胃炎、腹瀉:白麵包、精製穀片、白飯、去皮馬鈴薯;避免生水果(香蕉除外)、堅果、種子、全穀。
  • High-fiber 20-35 g/day for constipation, IBS and asymptomatic diverticular disease; increase gradually with plenty of fluids; limit gas-forming foods.高纖維每日 20-35 公克,適用便秘、大腸激躁症與無症狀憩室疾病;須漸進增加並大量喝水;限制易產氣食物。
  • Cardiac/DASH diet: restrict sodium, cholesterol and fats; fruits, vegetables, whole grains, low-fat dairy, fish, poultry, nuts.心臟/DASH 飲食:限制鈉、膽固醇與脂肪;多吃蔬果、全穀、低脂乳品、魚、禽肉、堅果。
  • Fat-restricted diet for malabsorption, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, GERD; fecal fat above 6-8 g/day indicates malabsorption.限脂飲食適用吸收不良、胰臟炎、膽囊疾病、胃食道逆流;糞便脂肪每日超過 6-8 公克表示脂肪吸收不良。
  • High-calorie, high-protein diet for burns, cancer, HIV, COPD, wound healing; use calorie counts.高熱量高蛋白飲食適用燒傷、癌症、HIV、COPD、傷口癒合;可做熱量計算。

7Diabetic, sodium, protein and renal diets糖尿病、鈉、蛋白質與腎臟飲食

  • Carbohydrate-consistent diet (carb counting) for diabetes; Diabetes Plate: half nonstarchy vegetables, quarter lean protein, quarter complex carbohydrate, water.固定醣類飲食(醣類計算)適用糖尿病;糖尿病餐盤法:一半非澱粉蔬菜、四分之一瘦蛋白質、四分之一複合醣類,飲水。
  • Sodium restriction for hypertension, heart failure, renal, liver disease: under 2,300 mg/day, ideally 1,500. Avoid processed, canned, smoked, pickled foods and effervescent medicines; salt substitutes contain potassium and are not for renal disease.限鈉適用高血壓、心衰竭、腎臟與肝臟疾病:每日低於 2,300 mg,理想 1,500 mg。避免加工、罐頭、燻製、醃漬食品與發泡錠藥物;代鹽含鉀,腎病患者不可用。
  • Protein restriction (40-60 g/day) for renal disease and end-stage liver disease: use high-biological-value protein and enough calories so protein is not burned for energy.限制蛋白質(每日 40-60 公克)適用腎病與末期肝病:選擇高生物價蛋白質並確保足夠熱量,避免蛋白質被當作能量。
  • Renal diet controls protein, sodium, phosphorus, calcium, potassium and fluids; peritoneal dialysis is less restrictive than hemodialysis. Weigh daily as the priority indicator of fluid status.腎臟飲食控制蛋白質、鈉、磷、鈣、鉀與水分;腹膜透析比血液透析寬鬆。每日量體重是評估水分狀態的優先指標。
  • Thirst relief with fluid restriction: sugar-free gum or hard candy, frozen fluids, lemon in water, cold mouth rinse.限水時緩解口渴:無糖口香糖或硬糖、冰凍液體、水中加檸檬、冷漱口水。

8Potassium, calcium, purine and iron diets鉀、鈣、普林與高鐵飲食

  • Low-potassium diet for hyperkalemia (renal failure, Addison's, ACE inhibitors, K+-sparing diuretics): applesauce, green beans, cabbage, lettuce, grapes, blueberries.低鉀飲食適用高血鉀(腎衰竭、愛迪生氏症、ACE 抑制劑、保鉀利尿劑):蘋果泥、四季豆、高麗菜、萵苣、葡萄、藍莓。
  • High-potassium diet for hypokalemia (diarrhea, vomiting, K+-depleting diuretics, Cushing's, steroids).高鉀飲食適用低血鉀(腹瀉、嘔吐、排鉀利尿劑、庫欣氏症、類固醇)。
  • High-calcium diet for bone growth and osteoporosis prevention; lactose-intolerant clients need nondairy calcium sources.高鈣飲食用於骨骼生長與預防骨質疏鬆;乳糖不耐者需攝取非乳製品鈣來源。
  • Low-purine diet for gout, kidney stones, high uric acid: avoid anchovies, herring, mackerel, sardines, scallops, organ meats, gravies, game.低普林飲食適用痛風、腎結石、高尿酸:避免鯷魚、鯡魚、鯖魚、沙丁魚、干貝、內臟、肉汁、野味。
  • High-iron diet for anemia (organ meats, meat, egg yolk, whole wheat, dark leafy greens, dried fruit, legumes); take vitamin C with iron to improve absorption.高鐵飲食適用貧血(內臟、肉類、蛋黃、全麥、深綠葉菜、乾果、豆類);鐵與維生素 C 併用可增加吸收。

9Vegetarian diets and enteral nutrition素食與管灌營養

  • Vegan: no animal products; lacto-vegetarian adds dairy; lacto-ovo adds dairy and eggs; ovo-vegetarian adds eggs only.純素:不含任何動物性產品;乳素加乳製品;蛋奶素加乳製品與蛋;蛋素僅加蛋。
  • Possible deficiencies: energy, protein, vitamin B12, zinc, iron, calcium, omega-3, vitamin D; B12 is most likely lacking in vegans.可能缺乏:熱量、蛋白質、維生素 B12、鋅、鐵、鈣、omega-3、維生素 D;純素者最容易缺乏 B12。
  • Teach complementary proteins across the day; eat iron plus vitamin C together; soy protein is comparable to animal protein.教導一天內搭配互補蛋白質;鐵與維生素 C 同餐;大豆蛋白品質與動物性蛋白相當。
  • BMI = weight (kg) / height (m) squared.BMI = 體重(公斤)/ 身高(公尺)平方。
  • Enteral nutrition: for a working GI tract when oral intake is inadequate (dysphagia, burns, trauma, organ failure, severe malnutrition); use lactose-free formula for lactose intolerance.管灌營養:適用腸胃道功能正常但口服攝取不足(吞嚥困難、燒傷、創傷、器官衰竭、嚴重營養不良);乳糖不耐者用無乳糖配方。

  • Toddlers: more than 24 oz milk/day risks milk anemia; avoid choking hazards (hot dogs, grapes, nuts, popcorn, candy, raw vegetables); finger foods build independence.幼兒:每日牛奶超過 24 盎司有「牛奶貧血」風險;避免噎塞食物(熱狗、葡萄、堅果、爆米花、糖果、生菜);手抓食物可培養獨立。
  • School-age: watch high fat/sugar/salt snacks and childhood obesity; adolescents need more protein, calcium and iron and are at risk for eating disorders.學齡兒童:留意高脂/糖/鹽零食與肥胖;青少年需要更多蛋白質、鈣與鐵,且是飲食障礙好發期。
  • Pregnancy: protein 60 g/day, more calcium, iron and folic acid. Lactation: about 500 extra kcal/day, plenty of fluids, avoid alcohol and caffeine.懷孕:蛋白質每日 60 公克,增加鈣、鐵與葉酸。哺乳:每日約多 500 大卡、足量水分,避免酒精與咖啡因。
  • Older adults: lower energy needs, reduced taste, smell and thirst, dental changes; ensure calcium and vitamin D; discourage grapefruit with many medications.老年人:能量需求下降、味覺嗅覺與口渴感減退、牙齒變化;確保鈣與維生素 D;許多藥物不宜與葡萄柚併用。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Liquid and texture-modified diets流質與質地調整飲食
  • Clear liquid: water, broth, clear juice, gelatin, ice pops, carbonated drinks, tea/coffee; short-term only; deficient in calories and nutrients. Dairy and opaque juices are not clear; avoid red/purple dye before colon exams.清流質:水、清湯、澄清果汁、果凍、冰棒、碳酸飲料、茶/咖啡;僅短期使用,熱量與營養不足。乳製品與不透明果汁不算清流質;腸道檢查前避免紅/紫色染料。
  • Weight change of 1 kg equals about 1 L of fluid gained or lost; monitor I&O and weight on liquid diets.體重變化 1 公斤約等於 1 公升水分增減;流質飲食期間須監測輸出入量與體重。
  • Full liquid adds milk, ice cream, pudding, custard, strained soups and cereals; use supplements if used more than 3 days.全流質加入牛奶、冰淇淋、布丁、卡士達、過濾湯品與精製穀粥;使用超過 3 天需營養補充品。
  • Mechanical soft: pureed, ground or chopped foods for chewing difficulty or dysphagia; avoid nuts, dried and raw fruits/vegetables, fried and tough meats.機械軟質:將食物打泥、絞碎或切碎,適用咀嚼困難或吞嚥困難;避免堅果、乾果、生蔬果、油炸與粗硬肉類。
  • Soft diet: for mouth sores, oral surgery, jaw fracture, stroke; serve cooler foods for mouth sores; avoid nuts/seeds, raw produce, fried foods and whole grains; no straw for dysphagia.軟質飲食:適用口腔潰瘍、口腔手術、下顎骨折、中風;口腔潰瘍給較涼的食物;避免堅果/種子、生蔬果、油炸與全穀類;吞嚥困難者不可使用吸管。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Fiber and cardiac-related diets纖維與心臟相關飲食
  • Low-fiber (low-residue) for IBD, partial obstruction, gastroenteritis, diarrhea: white bread, refined cereals, white rice, peeled potatoes; avoid raw fruits (except bananas), nuts, seeds, whole grains.低纖維(低渣)飲食適用發炎性腸道疾病、部分腸阻塞、腸胃炎、腹瀉:白麵包、精製穀片、白飯、去皮馬鈴薯;避免生水果(香蕉除外)、堅果、種子、全穀。
  • High-fiber 20-35 g/day for constipation, IBS and asymptomatic diverticular disease; increase gradually with plenty of fluids; limit gas-forming foods.高纖維每日 20-35 公克,適用便秘、大腸激躁症與無症狀憩室疾病;須漸進增加並大量喝水;限制易產氣食物。
  • Cardiac/DASH diet: restrict sodium, cholesterol and fats; fruits, vegetables, whole grains, low-fat dairy, fish, poultry, nuts.心臟/DASH 飲食:限制鈉、膽固醇與脂肪;多吃蔬果、全穀、低脂乳品、魚、禽肉、堅果。
  • Fat-restricted diet for malabsorption, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, GERD; fecal fat above 6-8 g/day indicates malabsorption.限脂飲食適用吸收不良、胰臟炎、膽囊疾病、胃食道逆流;糞便脂肪每日超過 6-8 公克表示脂肪吸收不良。
  • High-calorie, high-protein diet for burns, cancer, HIV, COPD, wound healing; use calorie counts.高熱量高蛋白飲食適用燒傷、癌症、HIV、COPD、傷口癒合;可做熱量計算。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Diabetic, sodium, protein and renal diets糖尿病、鈉、蛋白質與腎臟飲食
  • Carbohydrate-consistent diet (carb counting) for diabetes; Diabetes Plate: half nonstarchy vegetables, quarter lean protein, quarter complex carbohydrate, water.固定醣類飲食(醣類計算)適用糖尿病;糖尿病餐盤法:一半非澱粉蔬菜、四分之一瘦蛋白質、四分之一複合醣類,飲水。
  • Sodium restriction for hypertension, heart failure, renal, liver disease: under 2,300 mg/day, ideally 1,500. Avoid processed, canned, smoked, pickled foods and effervescent medicines; salt substitutes contain potassium and are not for renal disease.限鈉適用高血壓、心衰竭、腎臟與肝臟疾病:每日低於 2,300 mg,理想 1,500 mg。避免加工、罐頭、燻製、醃漬食品與發泡錠藥物;代鹽含鉀,腎病患者不可用。
  • Protein restriction (40-60 g/day) for renal disease and end-stage liver disease: use high-biological-value protein and enough calories so protein is not burned for energy.限制蛋白質(每日 40-60 公克)適用腎病與末期肝病:選擇高生物價蛋白質並確保足夠熱量,避免蛋白質被當作能量。
  • Renal diet controls protein, sodium, phosphorus, calcium, potassium and fluids; peritoneal dialysis is less restrictive than hemodialysis. Weigh daily as the priority indicator of fluid status.腎臟飲食控制蛋白質、鈉、磷、鈣、鉀與水分;腹膜透析比血液透析寬鬆。每日量體重是評估水分狀態的優先指標。
  • Thirst relief with fluid restriction: sugar-free gum or hard candy, frozen fluids, lemon in water, cold mouth rinse.限水時緩解口渴:無糖口香糖或硬糖、冰凍液體、水中加檸檬、冷漱口水。

Potassium, calcium, purine and iron diets鉀、鈣、普林與高鐵飲食
  • Low-potassium diet for hyperkalemia (renal failure, Addison's, ACE inhibitors, K+-sparing diuretics): applesauce, green beans, cabbage, lettuce, grapes, blueberries.低鉀飲食適用高血鉀(腎衰竭、愛迪生氏症、ACE 抑制劑、保鉀利尿劑):蘋果泥、四季豆、高麗菜、萵苣、葡萄、藍莓。
  • High-potassium diet for hypokalemia (diarrhea, vomiting, K+-depleting diuretics, Cushing's, steroids).高鉀飲食適用低血鉀(腹瀉、嘔吐、排鉀利尿劑、庫欣氏症、類固醇)。
  • High-calcium diet for bone growth and osteoporosis prevention; lactose-intolerant clients need nondairy calcium sources.高鈣飲食用於骨骼生長與預防骨質疏鬆;乳糖不耐者需攝取非乳製品鈣來源。
  • Low-purine diet for gout, kidney stones, high uric acid: avoid anchovies, herring, mackerel, sardines, scallops, organ meats, gravies, game.低普林飲食適用痛風、腎結石、高尿酸:避免鯷魚、鯡魚、鯖魚、沙丁魚、干貝、內臟、肉汁、野味。
  • High-iron diet for anemia (organ meats, meat, egg yolk, whole wheat, dark leafy greens, dried fruit, legumes); take vitamin C with iron to improve absorption.高鐵飲食適用貧血(內臟、肉類、蛋黃、全麥、深綠葉菜、乾果、豆類);鐵與維生素 C 併用可增加吸收。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
Vegetarian diets and enteral nutrition素食與管灌營養
  • Vegan: no animal products; lacto-vegetarian adds dairy; lacto-ovo adds dairy and eggs; ovo-vegetarian adds eggs only.純素:不含任何動物性產品;乳素加乳製品;蛋奶素加乳製品與蛋;蛋素僅加蛋。
  • Possible deficiencies: energy, protein, vitamin B12, zinc, iron, calcium, omega-3, vitamin D; B12 is most likely lacking in vegans.可能缺乏:熱量、蛋白質、維生素 B12、鋅、鐵、鈣、omega-3、維生素 D;純素者最容易缺乏 B12。
  • Teach complementary proteins across the day; eat iron plus vitamin C together; soy protein is comparable to animal protein.教導一天內搭配互補蛋白質;鐵與維生素 C 同餐;大豆蛋白品質與動物性蛋白相當。
  • BMI = weight (kg) / height (m) squared.BMI = 體重(公斤)/ 身高(公尺)平方。
  • Enteral nutrition: for a working GI tract when oral intake is inadequate (dysphagia, burns, trauma, organ failure, severe malnutrition); use lactose-free formula for lactose intolerance.管灌營養:適用腸胃道功能正常但口服攝取不足(吞嚥困難、燒傷、創傷、器官衰竭、嚴重營養不良);乳糖不耐者用無乳糖配方。
