Chapter 5: Population Health Nursing第 5 章：族群健康護理

📖 Source: PDF p.58–71 · printed book p.40–52.e1來源：PDF 第 58–71 頁 · 書本印刷頁 40–52.e1

This chapter reviews vulnerable and at-risk populations and how disparities, social determinants and culture shape care. It teaches a nonjudgmental, individualized assessment approach, key health risks for each group and nursing considerations such as interpreter use, teach-back, safety and referrals.本章回顧弱勢與高風險族群，以及健康不平等、社會決定因素與文化如何影響照護。強調非批判、個別化的評估，並說明各族群主要健康風險與護理考量（如口譯、回述、安全與轉介）。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Health care disparities and nursing assessment健康照護不平等與護理評估 📖 PDF p.58 (book p.40), PDF p.59 (book p.41), PDF p.60 (book p.42), PDF p.61 (book p.43)

  • Health care disparities are differences in care offered to different groups; vulnerable groups include minorities, the uninsured, those in poverty or homeless, people with chronic illness or disability, immigrants, refugees, limited-English speakers, the incarcerated and LGBTQIA people.健康照護不平等是不同群體獲得照護的差異；弱勢族群包括少數族群、無保險者、貧窮或無家者、慢性病或身心障礙者、移民、難民、英語能力有限者、受監禁者與 LGBTQIA。
  • Leading U.S. chronic problems: heart disease, cancer, lung disease, stroke, Alzheimer's, diabetes, kidney disease; risk factors include tobacco, poor nutrition, obesity, inactivity and excess alcohol.美國主要慢性病：心臟病、癌症、肺病、中風、阿茲海默症、糖尿病、腎臟病；危險因子包括吸菸、營養不良、肥胖、缺乏運動與過量飲酒。
  • Use a nonjudgmental, nonthreatening approach; address reported symptoms first to encourage adherence and follow-up.採非批判、非威脅的態度；先處理主訴症狀以促進遵從與回診。
  • The needs assessment tool asks about language, interpreter need, culture, diet, living situation, support, finances, insurance, safety/abuse, substances, advance directive, immunizations and mental health.需求評估工具詢問語言、是否需口譯、文化、飲食、居住、支持系統、經濟、保險、安全/虐待、物質使用、預立醫囑、疫苗與心理健康。
  • Be self-aware of your own values and biases; avoid stereotyping since every client is an individual.覺察自身價值觀與偏見；避免刻板印象，每位病人都是個體。

COVID-19 and minority groupsCOVID-19 與少數族群 📖 PDF p.61 (book p.43), PDF p.62 (book p.44)

  • COVID-19 is a highly contagious respiratory illness (SARS-CoV) that can affect many body systems; high risk includes older adults, chronic illness, cancer, immunocompromise and minority groups.COVID-19 為高傳染性呼吸道疾病（SARS-CoV），可影響多個系統；高風險族群含年長者、慢性病、癌症、免疫低下者與少數族群。
  • Emergency warning signs: trouble breathing, persistent chest pain or pressure, new confusion, inability to stay awake, bluish lips or face.緊急警訊：呼吸困難、持續胸痛或壓迫感、新發意識混亂、無法保持清醒、嘴唇或臉部發紫。
  • Prevention: handwashing, mask and eye protection, distancing, avoiding crowds and sick contacts, covering coughs, avoiding touching the face, quarantine.預防：洗手、口罩與護目、保持距離、避免人群與病人、遮掩咳嗽、避免觸摸臉部、自我隔離。
  • Minority groups face higher disparities; examples: obesity/diabetes in Hispanic/Latinx; smoking, diabetes, hepatitis B, TB, SIDS in Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander; diabetes, heart disease, accidents, suicide in Native American/Alaska Native; hypertension, heart disease, asthma in African Americans; cancer, TB, hepatitis in Asian Americans.少數族群不平等更明顯：拉丁裔肥胖/糖尿病；夏威夷原住民與太平洋島民吸菸、糖尿病、B 肝、結核、SIDS；美洲原住民/阿拉斯加原住民糖尿病、心臟病、意外、自殺；非裔高血壓、心臟病、氣喘；亞裔癌症、結核、肝炎。
  • Do not use family or friends as interpreters; use designated interpreters. Use cultural responsiveness and teach-back (return demonstration).不可請家人朋友擔任口譯，應使用指定口譯員。展現文化回應能力並使用回述（回覆示教）。

LGBTQIA individualsLGBTQIA 族群 📖 PDF p.62 (book p.44), PDF p.63 (book p.45)

  • Fear of stigma reduces access to care; transgender people are less likely to complete screenings such as mammograms, cervical and prostate screening.害怕汙名化降低就醫；跨性別者較少完成乳房攝影、子宮頸與攝護腺等篩檢。
  • Concerns: STIs, depression and suicide, bullying and abuse of youth, rejection by family, barriers to child-rearing.關注議題：性傳染病、憂鬱與自殺、青少年霸凌與虐待、家人排斥、育兒障礙。
  • Create a welcoming environment, use the client's identified name and pronouns, use inclusive forms and policies, take a careful sexual history.營造友善環境，使用病人認同的姓名與代名詞，採用包容的表單與政策，詳細詢問性史。
  • Consider nPEP after possible HIV exposure; monitor transgender hormone therapy (e.g., polycythemia with testosterone); keep age-appropriate screening for retained organs/breast tissue.可能暴露於 HIV 後考慮 nPEP；追蹤跨性別荷爾蒙治療（如睪固酮造成紅血球增多症）；對保留的器官/乳房組織持續做適齡篩檢。

Homeless, socioeconomically disadvantaged and uninsured無家者、社經弱勢與無保險者 📖 PDF p.63 (book p.45), PDF p.64 (book p.46)

  • Homelessness is linked to early death, malnutrition, STIs, TB and pneumonia, wounds, mental illness and substance abuse; children have asthma, lead poisoning and ear infections.無家者與早逝、營養不良、性傳染病、結核與肺炎、傷口、精神疾病及物質濫用相關；兒童常有氣喘、鉛中毒與中耳炎。
  • Address the client's stated symptom first (e.g., pain with numbness in both feet), assess wounds, establish an emergency contact and follow-up, and connect to resources.先處理病人主訴（例：雙腳疼痛麻木），評估傷口，建立緊急聯絡人與追蹤，並連結資源。
  • Social determinants (education, income, family and social support, community safety) are as important as lifestyle and access to care.社會決定因素（教育、收入、家庭與社會支持、社區安全）與生活型態及就醫可近性同樣重要。
  • Use social work and refer the uninsured to Medicaid or financial services; lack of insurance leads to missed preventive care.運用社工，並轉介無保險者申請 Medicaid 或財務服務；缺乏保險導致預防照護缺漏。

Intellectual disability, abuse and neglect, single parents, foster children智能障礙、虐待與忽略、單親與寄養兒童 📖 PDF p.64 (book p.46), PDF p.65 (book p.47)

  • Intellectually disabled clients may present atypically; history taking takes more time with questions asked in different ways; assess choking/aspiration risk; behavior change may signal illness.智能障礙病人症狀可能不典型；問診需花更多時間並以不同方式提問；評估噎嗆/吸入風險；行為改變可能是疾病訊號。
  • Abuse victims: bruises (eyes, neck), sprains/fractures, fatigue, PTSD, depression; safety is the priority; nurses are mandated reporters; photograph injuries when needed.受虐者：瘀傷（眼周、頸部）、扭傷/骨折、疲倦、創傷後壓力症、憂鬱；安全為優先；護理師為強制通報者；必要時拍攝傷處。
  • Single parents need a therapeutic relationship and community resources (child care, food security, immunizations); preventive screenings matter.單親需要治療性關係與社區資源（托育、糧食、疫苗）；預防篩檢很重要。
  • Foster children often have unmet physical, mental, behavioral and dental needs; include social workers and community resources.寄養兒童常有未滿足的身體、心理、行為與牙科需求；需納入社工與社區資源。

Mental health, older adults, veterans, prisoners精神疾病、老年人、退伍軍人、受刑人 📖 PDF p.65 (book p.47), PDF p.66 (book p.48)

  • Mental illness: psychotropic weight gain and metabolic syndrome, diabetes risk, STIs, xerostomia, poor access; screen regularly.精神疾病：精神科藥物導致體重增加與代謝症候群、糖尿病風險、性傳染病、口乾、就醫困難；需定期篩檢。
  • Older adults: neglect is the most common elder abuse; report suspected abuse; common problems include dementia, falls, depression, osteoporosis.老年人：忽略是最常見的老人虐待；疑似虐待須通報；常見問題包括失智、跌倒、憂鬱、骨質疏鬆。
  • Veterans: PTSD, moral injury, TBI, substance use and suicide risk, amputations, chemical exposure; treat mental health to reduce suicide risk.退伍軍人：創傷後壓力症、道德創傷、腦外傷、物質使用與自殺風險、截肢、化學暴露；治療心理健康以降低自殺風險。
  • Prisoners: TB, STIs and infectious disease risk, asthma, diabetes, hypertension, mental illness; facility is usually the only care provider; screening must be thorough and regular.受刑人：結核、性傳染病與傳染病風險、氣喘、糖尿病、高血壓、精神疾病；矯正機構通常是唯一照護提供者；篩檢須完整且定期。

Immigrants, refugees and chronic illness移民、難民與慢性病 📖 PDF p.66 (book p.48), PDF p.67 (book p.49)

  • Immigrants face language, culture, housing, employment and access barriers; infections include TB, hepatitis, STIs and vaccine-preventable diseases; services must be offered regardless of insurance; provide vaccinations.移民面臨語言、文化、住房、就業與就醫障礙；感染包括結核、肝炎、性傳染病與疫苗可預防疾病；不論保險均應提供服務並施打疫苗。
  • Refugees: mental health problems from war, violence and rape are a priority concern.難民：因戰爭、暴力與性侵造成的心理健康問題是優先關注。
  • Chronic illness is the leading cause of death and disability; modifiable factors (diet, inactivity, tobacco) vs nonmodifiable (age, genetics).慢性病為死亡與失能首要原因；可改變因素（飲食、缺乏運動、吸菸）與不可改變因素（年齡、基因）。
  • Manage the 'big picture', not one illness; use interprofessional collaboration, follow-up (home health) and include the client and supporters in decisions.管理整體狀況而非單一疾病；跨專業合作、持續追蹤（居家照護），並讓病人與支持者參與決策。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Teach-back (return explanation/demonstration) is the most reliable way to confirm understanding.回述/回覆示教是確認理解最可靠的方式。 📖 PDF p.62 (book p.44), PDF p.68 (book p.50)
  2. Never use family or friends as interpreters; use agency-designated interpreters.絕不使用家人或朋友當口譯，應使用機構指定口譯員。 📖 PDF p.62 (book p.44)
  3. Address transgender clients by their identified name and pronouns.以跨性別者認同的姓名與代名詞稱呼。 📖 PDF p.62 (book p.44), PDF p.68 (book p.50)
  4. With homeless clients, address the client's reported symptom first to improve return and adherence.對無家者先處理其主訴，以提高回診與遵從。 📖 PDF p.63 (book p.45), PDF p.68 (book p.50)
  5. Abuse red flags: bruises around eyes, red-purple marks on neck, sprained wrists, PTSD, chronic fatigue.受虐警訊：眼周瘀傷、頸部紅紫痕、手腕扭傷、創傷後壓力、慢性疲倦。 📖 PDF p.65 (book p.47), PDF p.69 (book p.51)
  6. Nurses are mandated reporters of abuse and neglect; safety is the priority.護理師是虐待與忽略的強制通報者；安全為優先。 📖 PDF p.65 (book p.47)
  7. Neglect is the most common type of elder abuse.忽略是最常見的老人虐待類型。 📖 PDF p.66 (book p.48)
  8. Veterans: PTSD and suicide risk are the priority concerns.退伍軍人：創傷後壓力症與自殺風險為優先關注。 📖 PDF p.66 (book p.48), PDF p.69 (book p.51)
  9. Refugees: mental health care is the priority need.難民：心理健康照護為優先需求。 📖 PDF p.67 (book p.49), PDF p.69 (book p.51)
  10. Prison environments predispose to TB, STIs and other communicable diseases.監獄環境易導致結核、性傳染病與其他傳染病。 📖 PDF p.66 (book p.48), PDF p.69 (book p.51)
  11. Intellectually disabled clients: allow extra time for history, assess for choking, and watch behavior as a symptom.智能障礙者：病史採集多給時間、評估噎嗆、並將行為改變視為症狀。 📖 PDF p.64 (book p.46), PDF p.69 (book p.51)
  12. Chronic illness care: look at the big picture, collaborate across providers, involve client and supporters.慢性病照護：看整體、跨專業合作、讓病人與支持者參與。 📖 PDF p.67 (book p.49), PDF p.69 (book p.51), PDF p.70 (book p.52)
  13. COVID-19 emergency signs: dyspnea, chest pain, new confusion, cyanosis.COVID-19 緊急警訊：呼吸困難、胸痛、新發混亂、發紺。 📖 PDF p.61 (book p.43)
  14. Social determinants of health matter as much as lifestyle and access to care.社會決定因素與生活型態及就醫可近性同樣重要。 📖 PDF p.64 (book p.46)

1. Which teaching method best confirms a client's understanding when teaching members of a specific population?針對特定族群衛教時，哪種方法最能確認病人理解？

Return explanation and demonstration verify comprehension; other methods are supplementary.回述與回覆示教可驗證理解；其他方法為輔助。

Respect for identified name and pronouns builds rapport.尊重認同的姓名與代名詞可建立關係。
