Musculoskeletal anatomy and bone healing, diagnostic tests, soft tissue injuries, fractures with casts, traction and fixation, fracture complications, assistive devices, hip and knee surgery, dislocation, disc herniation, amputation, rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, osteoporosis and gout. Priorities are neurovascular checks, safe mobility and recognition of emergencies such as compartment syndrome and fat embolism.涵蓋肌肉骨骼解剖與骨癒合、診斷檢查、軟組織損傷、骨折與石膏、牽引與固定、骨折併發症、輔具、髖/膝手術、脫位、椎間盤突出、截肢、類風濕性關節炎、退化性關節炎、骨質疏鬆與痛風。重點為神經血管評估、安全活動，以及辨識腔室症候群與脂肪栓塞等急症。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Anatomy, bone healing and risk factors解剖、骨癒合與危險因子

  • Bones: spongy bone handles multidirectional force; compact bone resists longitudinal force; length growth stops at ages 18-25; bone resorption speeds with aging.骨骼：海綿骨承受多方向力量；緻密骨抗縱向力；長度生長在18-25歲停止；老化後骨質吸收加速。
  • Joints: synarthrosis (fixed), amphiarthrosis (slightly movable), diarthrosis (synovial, free movement); synovial fluid lubricates and cushions.關節：不動關節、微動關節、滑動關節（可自由活動）；滑液潤滑與緩衝。
  • Muscle contraction needs ATP and calcium; acetylcholine from the motor end plate starts it and acetylcholinesterase breaks it down.肌肉收縮需要ATP與鈣；運動終板釋放乙醯膽鹼啟動，乙醯膽鹼酯酶分解之。
  • Bone healing stages: hematoma, granulation tissue, callus formation, ossification/remodeling, healing complete.骨癒合階段：血腫、肉芽組織、骨痂形成、骨化/重塑、癒合完成。
  • Risk factors: falls, calcium deficiency, postmenopause, obesity, medications, hyperuricemia, infection, autoimmune and neoplastic disease.危險因子：跌倒、缺鈣、停經後、肥胖、藥物、高尿酸、感染、自體免疫與腫瘤疾病。

2Diagnostic tests診斷檢查

  • X-ray/MRI: support the injured limb above and below the joint, give analgesia first, remove metal, screen for pregnancy; no pacemaker or magnetic implants for MRI.X光/MRI：支托受傷肢體上下關節、事先給止痛、移除金屬、詢問懷孕；MRI不可有心律調節器或磁性植入物。
  • Arthrocentesis: consent, compression bandage, ice, rest joint 8-24 h; report fever or swelling; steroid injection may raise glucose in diabetics.關節穿刺：同意書、壓縮繃帶、冰敷、關節休息8-24小時；發燒或腫脹要通報；類固醇注射可能使糖尿病人血糖升高。
  • Arthroscopy: fast 8-12 h, consent, neurovascular checks, compression bandage 2-4 days, elevate and ice; report fever, redness, drainage >3 days.關節鏡：禁食8-12小時、同意書、神經血管評估、壓縮繃帶2-4天、抬高與冰敷；發燒、紅腫、引流超過3天要通報。
  • DXA measures bone density (minimal radiation); bone scan uses IV radioisotope, drink 32 oz fluid, void before scanning; no special precautions afterward.DXA測骨密度（輻射極少）；骨掃描靜脈注射放射性同位素，需喝32盎司水、掃描前排尿；之後無特殊防護。
  • Biopsy: monitor bleeding, hematoma, infection, elevate site 24 h. EMG: needle insertion is uncomfortable; no stimulants or sedatives 24 h before.切片：監測出血、血腫、感染、抬高部位24小時。肌電圖：針刺不適；檢查前24小時避免興奮劑或鎮靜劑。

3Soft tissue injuries軟組織損傷

  • Strain = overstretched muscle/tendon; sprain = stretched or torn ligament (twisting). Manage with RICE: rest, ice, compression, elevation.拉傷＝肌肉/肌腱過度拉扯；扭傷＝韌帶拉傷或撕裂（扭轉）。以RICE處置：休息、冰敷、加壓、抬高。
  • Moderate sprains may need casting; severe ligament damage needs surgery.中度扭傷可能需石膏；嚴重韌帶損傷需手術。
  • Rotator cuff tear: shoulder pain and inability to hold arm abducted (drop-arm); NSAIDs, PT, sling, ice/heat; surgery if complete tear.旋轉肌袖撕裂：肩痛且無法維持手臂外展（落臂試驗）；NSAID、物理治療、吊帶、冷熱敷；完全撕裂需手術。

4Fractures, reduction, fixation, traction and casts骨折、復位、固定、牽引與石膏

  • Findings: pain, deformity, crepitus, swelling, loss of function, spasm, neurovascular compromise. Immobilize first and check neurovascular status; cover an open fracture with a sterile dressing and splint.表現：疼痛、變形、骨擦音、腫脹、功能喪失、痙攣、神經血管受損。先固定並評估神經血管；開放性骨折以無菌敷料覆蓋並夾板固定。
  • Closed reduction is manual; open reduction (ORIF) is surgical with plates, screws, pins or rods. External fixators need pin care and stability checks.閉鎖性復位為手法；開放性復位（ORIF）為手術並用鋼板、螺釘、鋼釘或髓內釘。外固定器需針孔護理與穩定性檢查。
  • Traction: weights hang freely, never touch the floor; do not remove weights without order; ropes move freely in pulleys; keep alignment.牽引：重量自由懸吊、不碰地；未經醫囑勿移除重量；繩索在滑輪中順暢；維持對位。
  • Skeletal traction 25-40 lb with pin site care; skin traction (Buck's) not more than 8-10 lb, foot of bed raised; balanced suspension keeps ~20 degree thigh angle.骨骼牽引25-40磅並做針孔護理；皮膚牽引（Buck氏）不超過8-10磅、抬高床尾；平衡懸吊維持大腿約20度。
  • Plaster cast dries 24-72 h (synthetic ~20 min); handle with palms; cool hair dryer only; keep dry; elevate; turn every 1-2 h; do not insert objects.石膏：乾燥需24-72小時（合成約20分鐘）；以手掌托持；僅用冷風吹風機；保持乾燥；抬高；每1-2小時翻動；勿塞入物品。
  • Watch for circulation problems (pain, swelling, discoloration, tingling, coolness, weak pulse) and infection (fever, hot spots, odor).注意循環障礙（疼痛、腫脹、變色、刺痛、冰冷、脈搏弱）與感染（發燒、熱點、異味）。

5Complications of fractures骨折併發症

  • Fat embolism: restlessness, sudden dyspnea, chest pain, cough, hypoxemia, crackles, confusion; give oxygen, notify PHCP, prepare for ventilation.脂肪栓塞：躁動、突發呼吸困難、胸痛、咳嗽、低血氧、囉音、意識混亂；給氧、通知醫師、準備呼吸器。
  • Acute compartment syndrome: pain unrelieved or worse, pain on passive movement, paresthesia, pallor; pulselessness is late; irreversible damage after 4-6 h; loosen dressings/bivalve cast, fasciotomy.急性腔室症候群：疼痛無法緩解或加劇、被動活動疼痛、感覺異常、蒼白；無脈為晚期；4-6小時後可能不可逆；鬆開敷料/剖開石膏、筋膜切開術。
  • Osteomyelitis: fever >101 F, local pain, leukocytosis, high ESR; long-term IV antibiotics, surgery if resistant.骨髓炎：發燒>101°F、局部疼痛、白血球與ESR升高；長期靜脈抗生素、抗藥者需手術。
  • Avascular necrosis: blood supply interrupted causing bone death; report pain/numbness.缺血性壞死：血供中斷導致骨壞死；疼痛/麻木需通報。

6Crutches, canes and walkers拐杖、手杖與助行器

  • Crutches fit: 2-3 finger widths below axilla, elbows flexed 20-30 degrees; never bear weight on axillae (brachial plexus injury); crutches placed 6-10 in diagonally forward; stand on affected side when assisting.拐杖合適度：腋下留2-3指寬、肘彎20-30度；腋下勿承重（臂神經叢損傷）；拐杖置於斜前6-10吋；協助時站在患側。
  • Gaits: 2-point (partial weight, bilateral prostheses), 3-point (no/partial weight on one leg), 4-point (weight bearing both), swing-to/swing-through (strong arms).步態：兩點（部分承重）、三點（患肢不/部分承重）、四點（雙腳可承重）、擺至/擺過（手臂有力）。
  • Stairs: up with the good leg first (up with the good); down with crutches and the affected leg first (down with the bad).樓梯：上樓先出健腿；下樓先出拐杖與患腿。
  • Cane in hand on the unaffected side, handle at greater trochanter, 4-6 in from the foot; walker - all four points flat before weight bearing.手杖拿在健側手、握把與大轉子同高、距足側4-6吋；助行器－四腳著地後再承重。

7Hip fracture, knee replacement, dislocation髖骨折、膝關節置換與脫位

  • Intracapsular hip fracture: poor blood supply, treated with replacement or ORIF; extracapsular: traction then ORIF/nail.囊內髖骨折：血供差，以置換或ORIF處理；囊外：牽引後ORIF/髓內釘。
  • Post-hip surgery: avoid hip flexion >90 degrees, adduction/crossing legs, internal rotation; avoid low chairs; keep leg aligned; monitor delirium, DVT, infection and neurovascular status.髖部術後：避免髖屈曲>90度、內收/翹腳、內旋；避免低椅；維持肢體排列；監測譫妄、深部靜脈栓塞、感染與神經血管。
  • Total knee replacement: CPM as prescribed, analgesia before CPM, weight bearing with device as tolerated, avoid dangling leg; tranexamic acid reduces blood loss.全膝關節置換：依醫囑使用CPM、CPM前給止痛、使用輔具依耐受承重、避免下垂；tranexamic acid減少失血。
  • Dislocation: asymmetry, pain, swelling; risks neurovascular compromise and avascular necrosis; reduce promptly then immobilize.脫位：不對稱、疼痛、腫脹；風險為神經血管受損與缺血性壞死；及早復位後固定。

8Disc herniation and disc surgery椎間盤突出與手術

  • Cervical herniation (C5-C6, C6-C7): pain to shoulder/arm, paresthesia; collar, heat/ice, bed rest, avoid neck flexion/rotation.頸椎突出（C5-C6、C6-C7）：疼痛放射至肩臂、感覺異常；頸圈、冷熱敷、臥床、避免頸屈曲/旋轉。
  • Lumbar herniation (L4-L5, L5-S1): back pain radiating down leg (sciatica), worse with bending, lifting, coughing; sleep on side with pillow between flexed knees; neurological deterioration or bowel/bladder dysfunction needs urgent care.腰椎突出（L4-L5、L5-S1）：下背痛放射至腿（坐骨神經痛）、彎腰、提重物、咳嗽加劇；側臥並屈膝間夾枕；神經惡化或大小便功能異常需緊急處理。
  • After cervical surgery: watch breathing, hoarseness (laryngeal nerve), hematoma; after lumbar surgery: sensation/movement of legs, urinary retention, ileus, CSF leak (clear drainage, glucose-positive), logroll.頸椎術後：觀察呼吸、聲音沙啞（喉返神經）、血腫；腰椎術後：下肢感覺活動、尿滯留、腸阻塞、腦脊髓液漏（清澈、葡萄糖陽性）、軸線翻身。

9Amputation截肢

  • Complications: hemorrhage, infection, phantom pain, flexion contractures; diabetics prone to wound separation and infection.併發症：出血、感染、幻肢痛、屈曲攣縮；糖尿病人易傷口裂開與感染。
  • Never place a pillow under the residual limb (risk of hip flexion contracture); for the first 24 h elevate the foot of the bed, not a pillow, to reduce edema, then keep the bed flat; prone positioning as prescribed after 24-48 h; wrap with elastic bandage or shrinker, and rewrap immediately if it slips.殘肢下勿墊枕頭（避免髖屈曲攣縮）；最初24小時應抬高床尾而非用枕頭以減少腫脹，之後維持床平；24-48小時後依醫囑採俯臥；以彈性繃帶或殘肢套包紮，鬆脫時立即重新包紮。
  • Above-knee: prevent external rotation with trochanter roll; below-knee: do not let limb hang, avoid long sitting.膝上截肢：以轉子捲防外旋；膝下截肢：殘肢勿下垂、避免久坐。
  • Traumatic amputation: call 911, direct pressure, elevate, wrap part in a sealed bag placed in ice water (not directly on ice).外傷性截肢：呼叫119、直接加壓、抬高、離斷部位裝密封袋置於冰水中（不直接接觸冰）。

10Rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, osteoporosis, gout類風濕性關節炎、退化性關節炎、骨質疏鬆與痛風

  • RA: systemic autoimmune inflammation, morning stiffness >30 min, symmetric joint deformity, elevated ESR, positive rheumatoid factor; NSAIDs, DMARDs, glucocorticoids; balance rest and activity, heat/cold, joint protection.類風濕性關節炎：全身性自體免疫發炎、晨僵>30分鐘、對稱性關節變形、ESR升高、類風濕因子陽性；NSAID、DMARD、類固醇；休息與活動平衡、冷熱敷、保護關節。
  • OA: cartilage loss in weight-bearing joints; pain worse after activity, relieved by rest; Heberden and Bouchard nodes; acetaminophen first, weight control; joint replacement when other measures fail.退化性關節炎：負重關節軟骨流失；活動後痛加劇、休息緩解；Heberden與Bouchard結節；首選acetaminophen、控制體重；其他方法無效時行關節置換。
  • Osteoporosis: demineralization, silent until fracture; kyphosis, height loss; calcium, vitamin D, protein; weight-bearing exercise; avoid alcohol/smoking/coffee; fall prevention.骨質疏鬆：骨質流失、骨折前無症狀；駝背、身高變矮；補充鈣、維生素D、蛋白質；負重運動；避免酒精/吸菸/咖啡；預防跌倒。
  • Gout: uric acid crystals, acute great-toe pain, tophi, renal stones; low-purine diet (avoid organ meats, wine, aged cheese), fluids 2000 mL/day, avoid alcohol and starvation diets, uric acid normal 2.7-8.5 mg/dL.痛風：尿酸結晶、大拇趾急性劇痛、痛風石、腎結石；低普林飲食（避免內臟、酒、陳年起司）、水分2000 mL/日、避免酒精與飢餓飲食，尿酸正常2.7-8.5 mg/dL。

  • Skeletal traction 25-40 lb with pin site care; skin traction (Buck's) not more than 8-10 lb, foot of bed raised; balanced suspension keeps ~20 degree thigh angle.骨骼牽引25-40磅並做針孔護理；皮膚牽引（Buck氏）不超過8-10磅、抬高床尾；平衡懸吊維持大腿約20度。
  • Plaster cast dries 24-72 h (synthetic ~20 min); handle with palms; cool hair dryer only; keep dry; elevate; turn every 1-2 h; do not insert objects.石膏：乾燥需24-72小時（合成約20分鐘）；以手掌托持；僅用冷風吹風機；保持乾燥；抬高；每1-2小時翻動；勿塞入物品。
  • Watch for circulation problems (pain, swelling, discoloration, tingling, coolness, weak pulse) and infection (fever, hot spots, odor).注意循環障礙（疼痛、腫脹、變色、刺痛、冰冷、脈搏弱）與感染（發燒、熱點、異味）。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Complications of fractures骨折併發症
  • Fat embolism: restlessness, sudden dyspnea, chest pain, cough, hypoxemia, crackles, confusion; give oxygen, notify PHCP, prepare for ventilation.脂肪栓塞：躁動、突發呼吸困難、胸痛、咳嗽、低血氧、囉音、意識混亂；給氧、通知醫師、準備呼吸器。
  • Acute compartment syndrome: pain unrelieved or worse, pain on passive movement, paresthesia, pallor; pulselessness is late; irreversible damage after 4-6 h; loosen dressings/bivalve cast, fasciotomy.急性腔室症候群：疼痛無法緩解或加劇、被動活動疼痛、感覺異常、蒼白；無脈為晚期；4-6小時後可能不可逆；鬆開敷料/剖開石膏、筋膜切開術。
  • Osteomyelitis: fever >101 F, local pain, leukocytosis, high ESR; long-term IV antibiotics, surgery if resistant.骨髓炎：發燒>101°F、局部疼痛、白血球與ESR升高；長期靜脈抗生素、抗藥者需手術。
  • Avascular necrosis: blood supply interrupted causing bone death; report pain/numbness.缺血性壞死：血供中斷導致骨壞死；疼痛/麻木需通報。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Crutches, canes and walkers拐杖、手杖與助行器
  • Crutches fit: 2-3 finger widths below axilla, elbows flexed 20-30 degrees; never bear weight on axillae (brachial plexus injury); crutches placed 6-10 in diagonally forward; stand on affected side when assisting.拐杖合適度：腋下留2-3指寬、肘彎20-30度；腋下勿承重（臂神經叢損傷）；拐杖置於斜前6-10吋；協助時站在患側。
  • Gaits: 2-point (partial weight, bilateral prostheses), 3-point (no/partial weight on one leg), 4-point (weight bearing both), swing-to/swing-through (strong arms).步態：兩點（部分承重）、三點（患肢不/部分承重）、四點（雙腳可承重）、擺至/擺過（手臂有力）。
  • Stairs: up with the good leg first (up with the good); down with crutches and the affected leg first (down with the bad).樓梯：上樓先出健腿；下樓先出拐杖與患腿。
  • Cane in hand on the unaffected side, handle at greater trochanter, 4-6 in from the foot; walker - all four points flat before weight bearing.手杖拿在健側手、握把與大轉子同高、距足側4-6吋；助行器－四腳著地後再承重。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Hip fracture, knee replacement, dislocation髖骨折、膝關節置換與脫位
  • Intracapsular hip fracture: poor blood supply, treated with replacement or ORIF; extracapsular: traction then ORIF/nail.囊內髖骨折：血供差，以置換或ORIF處理；囊外：牽引後ORIF/髓內釘。
  • Post-hip surgery: avoid hip flexion >90 degrees, adduction/crossing legs, internal rotation; avoid low chairs; keep leg aligned; monitor delirium, DVT, infection and neurovascular status.髖部術後：避免髖屈曲>90度、內收/翹腳、內旋；避免低椅；維持肢體排列；監測譫妄、深部靜脈栓塞、感染與神經血管。
  • Total knee replacement: CPM as prescribed, analgesia before CPM, weight bearing with device as tolerated, avoid dangling leg; tranexamic acid reduces blood loss.全膝關節置換：依醫囑使用CPM、CPM前給止痛、使用輔具依耐受承重、避免下垂；tranexamic acid減少失血。
  • Dislocation: asymmetry, pain, swelling; risks neurovascular compromise and avascular necrosis; reduce promptly then immobilize.脫位：不對稱、疼痛、腫脹；風險為神經血管受損與缺血性壞死；及早復位後固定。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Disc herniation and disc surgery椎間盤突出與手術
  • Cervical herniation (C5-C6, C6-C7): pain to shoulder/arm, paresthesia; collar, heat/ice, bed rest, avoid neck flexion/rotation.頸椎突出（C5-C6、C6-C7）：疼痛放射至肩臂、感覺異常；頸圈、冷熱敷、臥床、避免頸屈曲/旋轉。
  • Lumbar herniation (L4-L5, L5-S1): back pain radiating down leg (sciatica), worse with bending, lifting, coughing; sleep on side with pillow between flexed knees; neurological deterioration or bowel/bladder dysfunction needs urgent care.腰椎突出（L4-L5、L5-S1）：下背痛放射至腿（坐骨神經痛）、彎腰、提重物、咳嗽加劇；側臥並屈膝間夾枕；神經惡化或大小便功能異常需緊急處理。
  • After cervical surgery: watch breathing, hoarseness (laryngeal nerve), hematoma; after lumbar surgery: sensation/movement of legs, urinary retention, ileus, CSF leak (clear drainage, glucose-positive), logroll.頸椎術後：觀察呼吸、聲音沙啞（喉返神經）、血腫；腰椎術後：下肢感覺活動、尿滯留、腸阻塞、腦脊髓液漏（清澈、葡萄糖陽性）、軸線翻身。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
Amputation截肢
  • Complications: hemorrhage, infection, phantom pain, flexion contractures; diabetics prone to wound separation and infection.併發症：出血、感染、幻肢痛、屈曲攣縮；糖尿病人易傷口裂開與感染。
  • Never place a pillow under the residual limb (risk of hip flexion contracture); for the first 24 h elevate the foot of the bed, not a pillow, to reduce edema, then keep the bed flat; prone positioning as prescribed after 24-48 h; wrap with elastic bandage or shrinker, and rewrap immediately if it slips.殘肢下勿墊枕頭（避免髖屈曲攣縮）；最初24小時應抬高床尾而非用枕頭以減少腫脹，之後維持床平；24-48小時後依醫囑採俯臥；以彈性繃帶或殘肢套包紮，鬆脫時立即重新包紮。
  • Above-knee: prevent external rotation with trochanter roll; below-knee: do not let limb hang, avoid long sitting.膝上截肢：以轉子捲防外旋；膝下截肢：殘肢勿下垂、避免久坐。
  • Traumatic amputation: call 911, direct pressure, elevate, wrap part in a sealed bag placed in ice water (not directly on ice).外傷性截肢：呼叫119、直接加壓、抬高、離斷部位裝密封袋置於冰水中（不直接接觸冰）。
Topic 10 · tap to flip主題 10 · 點擊翻面
Rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, osteoporosis, gout類風濕性關節炎、退化性關節炎、骨質疏鬆與痛風
  • RA: systemic autoimmune inflammation, morning stiffness >30 min, symmetric joint deformity, elevated ESR, positive rheumatoid factor; NSAIDs, DMARDs, glucocorticoids; balance rest and activity, heat/cold, joint protection.類風濕性關節炎：全身性自體免疫發炎、晨僵>30分鐘、對稱性關節變形、ESR升高、類風濕因子陽性；NSAID、DMARD、類固醇；休息與活動平衡、冷熱敷、保護關節。
  • OA: cartilage loss in weight-bearing joints; pain worse after activity, relieved by rest; Heberden and Bouchard nodes; acetaminophen first, weight control; joint replacement when other measures fail.退化性關節炎：負重關節軟骨流失；活動後痛加劇、休息緩解；Heberden與Bouchard結節；首選acetaminophen、控制體重；其他方法無效時行關節置換。
  • Osteoporosis: demineralization, silent until fracture; kyphosis, height loss; calcium, vitamin D, protein; weight-bearing exercise; avoid alcohol/smoking/coffee; fall prevention.骨質疏鬆：骨質流失、骨折前無症狀；駝背、身高變矮；補充鈣、維生素D、蛋白質；負重運動；避免酒精/吸菸/咖啡；預防跌倒。
  • Gout: uric acid crystals, acute great-toe pain, tophi, renal stones; low-purine diet (avoid organ meats, wine, aged cheese), fluids 2000 mL/day, avoid alcohol and starvation diets, uric acid normal 2.7-8.5 mg/dL.痛風：尿酸結晶、大拇趾急性劇痛、痛風石、腎結石；低普林飲食（避免內臟、酒、陳年起司）、水分2000 mL/日、避免酒精與飢餓飲食，尿酸正常2.7-8.5 mg/dL。
