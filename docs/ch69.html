This chapter reviews the major psychotropic drug classes by action, adverse effects, antidote or monitoring, and nursing teaching: antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs, TCAs, MAOIs), mood stabilizers (lithium), anxiolytics, sedative-hypnotics, antipsychotics, drugs for alcohol and opioid use disorders, ADHD stimulants, and Alzheimer drugs. Expect NCLEX items on lithium levels, tyramine and hypertensive crisis, serotonin syndrome, extrapyramidal effects, NMS, and safe tapering.本章依藥物類別整理精神科藥物的作用、重要不良反應、解毒劑或監測項目及護理衛教：抗憂鬱劑（SSRI、SNRI、TCA、MAOI）、情緒穩定劑（鋰鹽）、抗焦慮藥、鎮靜安眠藥、抗精神病藥、酒精與鴉片類使用障礙用藥、ADHD 刺激劑及阿茲海默症用藥。NCLEX 常考鋰鹽濃度、酪胺與高血壓危象、血清素症候群、錐體外症狀、NMS 及安全減藥。

1SSRIs and SNRIs選擇性血清素再吸收抑制劑（SSRI）與 SNRI

  • Action: block serotonin reuptake; first-line for depression with anxiety or agitation. Examples: citalopram, escitalopram, fluoxetine, paroxetine, sertraline. SNRIs (venlafaxine, duloxetine) also block norepinephrine.作用：抑制血清素再吸收，為合併焦慮或躁動之憂鬱症的首選。例：citalopram、escitalopram、fluoxetine、paroxetine、sertraline；SNRI（venlafaxine、duloxetine）另可抑制正腎上腺素再吸收。
  • Fewer anticholinergic effects than TCAs. Common effects: insomnia or somnolence, nervousness, tremor, weight change, decreased libido, BP changes, dry mouth.抗膽鹼作用比 TCA 少。常見副作用：失眠或嗜睡、神經質、顫抖、體重改變、性慾下降、血壓變化、口乾。
  • Serotonin syndrome (excess serotonin, especially SSRI + MAOI or OTC cold medicines/St. John's wort): restlessness, tachycardia, fever, high BP, delirium, myoclonus, rigidity, seizures, abdominal pain/diarrhea.血清素症候群（SSRI 合併 MAOI、感冒成藥或聖約翰草）：躁動、心搏過速、發燒、高血壓、譫妄、肌躍症、僵硬、抽搐、腹痛腹瀉。
  • Teaching: give with food or a snack; takes 2-4 weeks for full effect; avoid alcohol; do not stop abruptly (discontinuation syndrome: GI upset, movement disorders, insomnia, sensory disturbance); ibuprofen/NSAIDs raise upper GI bleeding risk.衛教：隨餐或點心服用；2–4 週才達完整療效；避免酒精；不可驟停（停藥症候群：腸胃不適、動作異常、失眠、感覺異常）；併用 ibuprofen／NSAID 會增加上消化道出血風險。
  • Monitor suicidality closely, especially when mood and energy begin to improve; monitor WBC/neutrophils, liver and renal function with long-term use; interacts with digoxin and warfarin; extra caution in children, adolescents, older adults, and pregnancy.密切監測自殺風險，特別是情緒與精力開始改善時；長期使用需監測白血球／嗜中性球及肝腎功能；可與 digoxin、warfarin 交互作用；兒童、青少年、老年人及孕婦需特別謹慎。

2Tricyclic Antidepressants (TCAs)三環抗憂鬱劑（TCA）

  • Action: block norepinephrine and serotonin reuptake. Examples: amitriptyline, imipramine, nortriptyline, doxepin; clomipramine is used for OCD.作用：抑制正腎上腺素與血清素再吸收。例：amitriptyline、imipramine、nortriptyline、doxepin；clomipramine 用於強迫症。
  • Anticholinergic effects: dry mouth, blurred vision, constipation, urinary retention; also orthostatic hypotension, tachycardia/dysrhythmias, sedation, lowered seizure threshold.抗膽鹼作用：口乾、視力模糊、便秘、尿滯留；另有姿勢性低血壓、心搏過速／心律不整、鎮靜、降低癲癇閾值。
  • Cardiac toxicity: obtain ECG before and periodically during therapy. Overdose is life-threatening: airway, oxygen, cardiac monitoring, ECG, activated charcoal/lavage if within 2 hours, IV fluids.心臟毒性：治療前及期間定期做心電圖。過量可致命：維持呼吸道、給氧、心電監視、心電圖、2 小時內可做洗胃／活性碳、靜脈輸液。
  • Give the whole daily dose at bedtime (sedating). Teach slow position changes, high-fiber diet and fluids, oral hygiene/hard candy for dry mouth, no alcohol/OTC cold meds, taper gradually.每日全量於睡前服用（有鎮靜作用）。衛教緩慢改變姿勢、高纖與多喝水、口腔衛生及硬糖緩解口乾、避免酒精與非處方感冒藥、需逐步減藥。
  • Concurrent MAOI can cause hypertensive crisis; alcohol/antihistamines add CNS depression; may reduce antihypertensive effect. Therapeutic effect takes 2-4 weeks. Watch for suicide risk as energy returns.併用 MAOI 可致高血壓危象；酒精與抗組織胺加重中樞抑制；可能降低降血壓藥效果。療效需 2–4 週。精力恢復時留意自殺風險。

3Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors (MAOIs)單胺氧化酶抑制劑（MAOI）

  • Action: inhibit MAO, raising dopamine, norepinephrine, serotonin and tyramine levels. Examples: isocarboxazid, phenelzine, selegiline, tranylcypromine. Reserved for depression not responding to other therapy.作用：抑制 MAO，使多巴胺、正腎上腺素、血清素及酪胺升高。例：isocarboxazid、phenelzine、selegiline、tranylcypromine。用於其他治療無效的憂鬱症。
  • Hypertensive crisis: severe hypertension, occipital headache, neck stiffness, N/V, sweating, fever, dilated pupils, palpitations, chest pain. Antidote: IV phentolamine.高血壓危象：嚴重高血壓、後枕部頭痛、頸部僵硬、噁心嘔吐、冒汗、發燒、瞳孔放大、心悸、胸痛。解毒劑：靜脈注射 phentolamine。
  • Avoid tyramine-rich foods: aged cheese, wine/beer, sausage/salami, sauerkraut, soy sauce, avocado, banana, figs, raisins, yogurt, sour cream, liver, caffeine/chocolate. Avoid amphetamines, decongestants, other antidepressants, levodopa, meperidine/opioids.避免高酪胺食物：陳年乳酪、紅酒／啤酒、香腸／臘腸、酸菜、醬油、酪梨、香蕉、無花果、葡萄乾、優格、酸奶油、肝臟、咖啡因／巧克力。避免安非他命、鼻充血劑、其他抗憂鬱劑、levodopa、meperidine／鴉片類。
  • Teaching: BP monitoring; report headache, neck stiffness or palpitations immediately; change positions slowly; carry a MedicAlert card; take in the morning (insomnia); missed dose may be taken within 3 hours; benefit may need up to 3 weeks; taper gradually.衛教：監測血壓；頭痛、頸部僵硬或心悸立即通報；緩慢改變姿勢；隨身攜帶醫療警示卡；早上服用（避免失眠）；漏服 3 小時內可補服；療效最多需 3 週；需逐步減藥。

4Atypical Antidepressants and Other Mood Stabilizers非典型抗憂鬱劑與其他情緒穩定劑

  • Atypical antidepressants listed: bupropion, mirtazapine, trazodone, nefazodone, vortioxetine, brexanolone. Bupropion lowers seizure threshold; seizures signal excess dose.非典型抗憂鬱劑：bupropion、mirtazapine、trazodone、nefazodone、vortioxetine、brexanolone。bupropion 降低癲癇閾值，出現抽搐表示劑量過高。
  • Other mood stabilizers: anticonvulsants (valproate, carbamazepine, lamotrigine, oxcarbazepine, gabapentin) and atypical antipsychotics (olanzapine, quetiapine, risperidone, aripiprazole, ziprasidone, etc.).其他情緒穩定劑：抗癲癇藥（valproate、carbamazepine、lamotrigine、oxcarbazepine、gabapentin）及非典型抗精神病藥（olanzapine、quetiapine、risperidone、aripiprazole、ziprasidone 等）。

5Lithium鋰鹽

  • Therapeutic serum level 0.6-1.2 mEq/L; margin to toxicity is narrow. Draw level in the morning 12 hours after the last dose; check frequently at start, then every 1-2 months or when behavior changes.治療血中濃度 0.6–1.2 mEq/L，與中毒濃度很接近。於末次給藥後 12 小時的早晨抽血；起始時頻繁檢測，之後每 1–2 個月或行為改變時檢測。
  • Levels rise with low sodium intake, dehydration, sweating, vomiting/diarrhea, diuretics, NSAIDs, fluoxetine. Never give diuretics with lithium. Keep fluids 6-8 glasses/day and normal salt intake.低鈉攝取、脫水、大量流汗、嘔吐腹瀉、利尿劑、NSAID、fluoxetine 會使濃度上升。鋰鹽期間不可給利尿劑。每日飲水 6–8 杯並維持正常鹽分攝取。
  • Expected effects: polyuria, polydipsia, fine hand tremor, nausea, soft stools, weight gain, hypothyroidism/goiter. Baseline ECG, renal and thyroid tests needed. Crosses the placenta (fetal toxicity).預期副作用：多尿、多渴、手部細微顫抖、噁心、軟便、體重增加、甲狀腺低下／甲狀腺腫。治療前需基礎心電圖、腎功能及甲狀腺檢查。可通過胎盤（胎兒毒性）。
  • Toxicity begins at about 1.5 mEq/L. Mild (1.5): lethargy, coarse tremor, mild ataxia. Moderate (1.5-2.0): vomiting, severe diarrhea, slurred speech, tinnitus, blurred vision. Severe (>2.0): nystagmus, fasciculations, hyperreflexia, oliguria, seizures, coma.約 1.5 mEq/L 開始中毒。輕度（1.5）：倦怠、粗顫、輕度共濟失調。中度（1.5–2.0）：嘔吐、嚴重腹瀉、言語不清、耳鳴、視力模糊。重度（>2.0）：眼球震顫、肌束顫動、反射亢進、少尿、抽搐、昏迷。
  • Toxicity actions: withhold lithium, notify provider, monitor VS, LOC and ECG, obtain lithium, electrolytes, BUN/creatinine, CBC; seizure and suicide precautions. Missed dose: take within 2 hours, otherwise skip. Do not stop abruptly.中毒處置：停藥並通知醫師，監測生命徵象、意識與心電圖，抽鋰濃度、電解質、BUN／肌酸酐、全血球計數；採取抽搐與自殺防範。漏服 2 小時內可補服，否則跳過。不可驟停。

6Benzodiazepines, Buspirone, Barbiturates and Sedative-Hypnotics苯二氮平類、buspirone、巴比妥類與鎮靜安眠藥

  • Benzodiazepines (alprazolam, lorazepam, diazepam, clonazepam, midazolam...) enhance GABA: anxiolytic, sedative, muscle relaxant, anticonvulsant. Contraindicated in acute narrow-angle glaucoma; cautious use in older adults and liver impairment; short-term use.苯二氮平類（alprazolam、lorazepam、diazepam、clonazepam、midazolam 等）增強 GABA：抗焦慮、鎮靜、肌肉鬆弛、抗痙攣。急性窄角青光眼禁用；老年人與肝功能不全者慎用；宜短期使用。
  • Adverse effects: sedation, dizziness, hypotension, amnesia, slurred speech, paradoxical excitement (older adults). Antidote for overdose: flumazenil IV (acts within about 5 minutes; may cause agitation or seizures).不良反應：鎮靜、頭暈、低血壓、失憶、言語不清、老年人矛盾性興奮。過量解毒劑：flumazenil 靜脈注射（約 5 分鐘起效）。
  • Never stop abruptly (seizures). Taper over 2-6 weeks; withdrawal: restlessness, irritability, insomnia, tremor, cramps, sweating, vomiting, seizures. Avoid alcohol; fall precautions.不可驟停（抽搐）。應在 2–6 週內逐步減量；戒斷症狀：不安、易怒、失眠、顫抖、痙攣、冒汗、嘔吐、抽搐。避免酒精；防跌。
  • Buspirone is a non-benzodiazepine anxiolytic; effective when anxiety signs such as rapid heartbeat and tension are absent after several weeks; no sedation or dependence.buspirone 為非苯二氮平類抗焦慮藥，數週後心跳加速、緊張等焦慮表現消失代表有效；無明顯鎮靜與依賴。
  • Barbiturates (phenobarbital, pentobarbital) and hypnotics (zolpidem, eszopiclone, zaleplon, ramelteon, chloral hydrate): depress the CNS via GABA. Overdose: respiratory depression, hypotension, shock, coma. Withdrawal within 24 hours in dependent clients: tremor, delirium, seizures. Avoid alcohol; take 30 minutes before bed; morning hangover possible.巴比妥類（phenobarbital、pentobarbital）及安眠藥（zolpidem、eszopiclone、zaleplon、ramelteon、chloral hydrate）經 GABA 抑制中樞。過量：呼吸抑制、低血壓、休克、昏迷。依賴者 24 小時內可出現戒斷：顫抖、譫妄、抽搐。避免酒精；睡前 30 分鐘服用；隔日可能宿醉感。

7Antipsychotics and Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome抗精神病藥與抗精神病藥惡性症候群（NMS）

  • Block dopamine receptors. Typical (haloperidol, chlorpromazine, fluphenazine): better for positive symptoms (hallucinations, delusions, aggression). Atypical (risperidone, olanzapine, clozapine, quetiapine, aripiprazole): also help negative symptoms (apathy, avolition, alogia).阻斷多巴胺受體。典型藥（haloperidol、chlorpromazine、fluphenazine）對陽性症狀（幻覺、妄想、攻擊）較有效；非典型藥（risperidone、olanzapine、clozapine、quetiapine、aripiprazole）對陰性症狀（冷漠、無意志、言語貧乏）亦有效。
  • Extrapyramidal effects: parkinsonism (tremor, rigidity, shuffling gait, drooling), dystonia (oculogyric crisis, grimacing, torso twisting), akathisia (restlessness), tardive dyskinesia (tongue protrusion, chewing, involuntary movements).錐體外症狀：類帕金森氏症（顫抖、僵硬、拖曳步態、流口水）、急性肌張力不全（眼動危象、扮鬼臉、軀幹扭曲）、靜坐不能（坐立不安）、遲發性運動障礙（吐舌、咀嚼、不自主運動）。
  • Other effects: anticholinergic effects, orthostatic hypotension, photosensitivity, weight gain and hyperglycemia, agranulocytosis (sore throat, fever, malaise; clozapine: stop if WBC < 3000/mm3), jaundice, impaired temperature regulation, gynecomastia/lactation.其他：抗膽鹼作用、姿勢性低血壓、光敏感、體重增加與高血糖、顆粒性白血球缺乏（喉嚨痛、發燒、不適；clozapine 白血球 < 3000/mm3 須停藥）、黃疸、體溫調節受損、男性女乳症／泌乳。
  • Teaching: onset in 7-10 days, full effect 3-6 weeks; sunscreen and protective clothing; rise slowly; no alcohol; dilute liquid concentrate in juice, avoid skin contact; urine may turn pink to red-brown; taper gradually; long-acting injectables help adherence; monitor glucose and urine output.衛教：7–10 天見初效、3–6 週完整療效；使用防曬與防護衣物；緩慢改變姿勢；避免酒精；濃縮液以果汁稀釋並避免接觸皮膚；尿液可能變粉紅至紅褐色；需逐步減藥；長效針劑可提升服藥順從性；監測血糖與尿量。
  • NMS (rare, life-threatening; usually with first-generation drugs, at start, dose increase or switch): hyperpyrexia, severe muscle rigidity, autonomic instability (labile BP, tachycardia, diaphoresis, drooling), altered LOC, dysphagia, elevated WBC, CPK and LFTs.NMS（罕見、危及生命；多見於第一代藥物，開始、增量或換藥時）：高熱、嚴重肌肉僵硬、自律神經不穩（血壓波動、心搏過速、冒汗、流口水）、意識改變、吞嚥困難、白血球、CPK 及肝功能升高。
  • NMS actions: stop the drug, notify provider, VS and LOC monitoring, seizure precautions, antipyretics and cooling blanket, IV fluids/electrolytes; watch for DVT and rhabdomyolysis.NMS 處置：停藥、通知醫師、監測生命徵象與意識、抽搐防範、退燒藥及冷卻毯、靜脈輸液與電解質；注意深部靜脈栓塞與橫紋肌溶解。

8Alcohol Withdrawal and Alcohol Use Disorder Drugs酒精戒斷與酒精使用障礙用藥

  • Benzodiazepines (chlordiazepoxide, diazepam, lorazepam, oxazepam) are the most effective for withdrawal: stabilize vital signs and reduce seizure and delirium tremens risk; long half-life agents give best protection.苯二氮平類（chlordiazepoxide、diazepam、lorazepam、oxazepam）為戒斷首選：穩定生命徵象、降低抽搐與震顫譫妄風險；長半衰期藥物保護最佳。
  • Atenolol/propranolol and clonidine reduce autonomic symptoms but only as adjuncts to benzodiazepines.atenolol／propranolol 與 clonidine 可減少自律神經症狀，但只能作為苯二氮平的輔助。
  • Thiamine (B1) IM then oral to prevent Wernicke's encephalopathy; also folic acid, multivitamin, magnesium if low.先肌肉注射再口服 thiamine（B1）預防 Wernicke 腦病變；另補葉酸、綜合維他命，鎂低時補鎂。
  • Maintaining abstinence (only after withdrawal is over): naltrexone blocks the pleasurable effect of alcohol; acamprosate reduces discomfort of abstinence; disulfiram causes a severe reaction if alcohol is ingested.維持戒酒（須戒斷結束後才用）：naltrexone 阻斷飲酒愉悅感；acamprosate 減輕戒酒不適；disulfiram 一旦飲酒會引起嚴重反應。
  • Disulfiram reaction: flushing, throbbing headache, nausea/vomiting, sweating, hypotension, tachycardia; begins within minutes, lasts 30 min-2 hr. Abstain at least 12 hours before first dose; avoid alcohol in cough medicines, mouthwash, aftershave, vinegar, rubbing alcohol; effect can persist several days after stopping.disulfiram 反應：臉潮紅、搏動性頭痛、噁心嘔吐、冒汗、低血壓、心搏過速；數分鐘內出現，持續 30 分鐘至 2 小時。首次用藥前至少戒酒 12 小時；避免含酒精之咳嗽藥、漱口水、刮鬍水、醋、消毒酒精；停藥後數天仍可能反應。

9Opioid Use Disorder Drugs鴉片類使用障礙用藥

  • Methadone: full opioid agonist replacing the abused opioid; prevents withdrawal; given only under close supervision (overdose risk: bradypnea, cyanosis, stupor, coma).methadone：完全鴉片類促效劑，取代濫用的鴉片；預防戒斷；須嚴密監督使用（過量風險：呼吸過慢、發紺、木僵、昏迷）。
  • Buprenorphine: partial agonist-antagonist for maintenance or detox; can be prescribed in general medical settings; low overdose and abuse potential but suppresses cravings.buprenorphine：部分促效／拮抗劑，用於維持治療或戒毒；可於一般醫療場所開立；濫用與過量風險低但可抑制渴求。
  • Naltrexone: opioid antagonist used after detoxification to block euphoria and discourage relapse.naltrexone：鴉片類拮抗劑，戒毒後使用，阻斷欣快感以防復發。
  • Clonidine decreases autonomic withdrawal symptoms (fatigue, dizziness, dry mouth).clonidine 減輕自律神經戒斷症狀（疲倦、頭暈、口乾）。

10ADHD Stimulants and Alzheimer DrugsADHD 刺激劑與阿茲海默症藥物

  • ADHD CNS stimulants (methylphenidate, dexmethylphenidate, amphetamine salts, lisdexamfetamine; also atomoxetine, guanfacine) calm children and lengthen attention span. Effects: tachycardia, high BP, anorexia and weight loss, insomnia, agitation.ADHD 中樞刺激劑（methylphenidate、dexmethylphenidate、安非他命鹽類、lisdexamfetamine；另有 atomoxetine、guanfacine）使兒童穩定並延長專注。副作用：心搏過速、高血壓、食慾不振與體重下降、失眠、躁動。
  • Nursing: baseline ECG, BP, height and weight; last dose at least 6 hours before bedtime (14 hours for extended release); avoid OTC drugs; drug holidays may be used; benefit takes weeks.護理：基礎心電圖、血壓、身高體重；最後一劑至少於睡前 6 小時（長效劑型 14 小時）；避免非處方藥；可安排藥物假期；療效需數週。
  • Cholinesterase inhibitors for mild to moderate Alzheimer disease: donepezil (bradycardia), galantamine (bronchoconstriction; caution in asthma/COPD), rivastigmine (give with food; caution in ulcer, bradycardia, sick sinus, urinary obstruction). Common: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, anorexia, weight loss.輕中度阿茲海默症之膽鹼酯酶抑制劑：donepezil（心搏過緩）、galantamine（支氣管收縮；氣喘／COPD 慎用）、rivastigmine（隨餐服用；潰瘍、心搏過緩、病竇症候群、尿路阻塞者慎用）。常見：噁心、嘔吐、腹瀉、食慾不振、體重下降。
  • Memantine: NMDA antagonist for moderate to severe disease; dizziness, headache, confusion, constipation; avoid with amantadine or ketamine; urine-alkalinizing drugs (sodium bicarbonate) and renal impairment raise levels.memantine：NMDA 受體拮抗劑，用於中重度；副作用頭暈、頭痛、混亂、便秘；避免與 amantadine 或 ketamine 併用；鹼化尿液藥物（碳酸氫鈉）及腎功能不全會使濃度上升。

SSRIs and SNRIs選擇性血清素再吸收抑制劑（SSRI）與 SNRI
  • Action: block serotonin reuptake; first-line for depression with anxiety or agitation. Examples: citalopram, escitalopram, fluoxetine, paroxetine, sertraline. SNRIs (venlafaxine, duloxetine) also block norepinephrine.作用：抑制血清素再吸收，為合併焦慮或躁動之憂鬱症的首選。例：citalopram、escitalopram、fluoxetine、paroxetine、sertraline；SNRI（venlafaxine、duloxetine）另可抑制正腎上腺素再吸收。
  • Fewer anticholinergic effects than TCAs. Common effects: insomnia or somnolence, nervousness, tremor, weight change, decreased libido, BP changes, dry mouth.抗膽鹼作用比 TCA 少。常見副作用：失眠或嗜睡、神經質、顫抖、體重改變、性慾下降、血壓變化、口乾。
  • Serotonin syndrome (excess serotonin, especially SSRI + MAOI or OTC cold medicines/St. John's wort): restlessness, tachycardia, fever, high BP, delirium, myoclonus, rigidity, seizures, abdominal pain/diarrhea.血清素症候群（SSRI 合併 MAOI、感冒成藥或聖約翰草）：躁動、心搏過速、發燒、高血壓、譫妄、肌躍症、僵硬、抽搐、腹痛腹瀉。

Tricyclic Antidepressants (TCAs)三環抗憂鬱劑（TCA）
  • Action: block norepinephrine and serotonin reuptake. Examples: amitriptyline, imipramine, nortriptyline, doxepin; clomipramine is used for OCD.作用：抑制正腎上腺素與血清素再吸收。例：amitriptyline、imipramine、nortriptyline、doxepin；clomipramine 用於強迫症。

Lithium鋰鹽
  • Therapeutic serum level 0.6-1.2 mEq/L; margin to toxicity is narrow. Draw level in the morning 12 hours after the last dose; check frequently at start, then every 1-2 months or when behavior changes.治療血中濃度 0.6–1.2 mEq/L，與中毒濃度很接近。於末次給藥後 12 小時的早晨抽血；起始時頻繁檢測，之後每 1–2 個月或行為改變時檢測。
  • Levels rise with low sodium intake, dehydration, sweating, vomiting/diarrhea, diuretics, NSAIDs, fluoxetine. Never give diuretics with lithium. Keep fluids 6-8 glasses/day and normal salt intake.低鈉攝取、脫水、大量流汗、嘔吐腹瀉、利尿劑、NSAID、fluoxetine 會使濃度上升。鋰鹽期間不可給利尿劑。每日飲水 6–8 杯並維持正常鹽分攝取。
  • Expected effects: polyuria, polydipsia, fine hand tremor, nausea, soft stools, weight gain, hypothyroidism/goiter. Baseline ECG, renal and thyroid tests needed. Crosses the placenta (fetal toxicity).預期副作用：多尿、多渴、手部細微顫抖、噁心、軟便、體重增加、甲狀腺低下／甲狀腺腫。治療前需基礎心電圖、腎功能及甲狀腺檢查。可通過胎盤（胎兒毒性）。
  • Toxicity begins at about 1.5 mEq/L. Mild (1.5): lethargy, coarse tremor, mild ataxia. Moderate (1.5-2.0): vomiting, severe diarrhea, slurred speech, tinnitus, blurred vision. Severe (>2.0): nystagmus, fasciculations, hyperreflexia, oliguria, seizures, coma.約 1.5 mEq/L 開始中毒。輕度（1.5）：倦怠、粗顫、輕度共濟失調。中度（1.5–2.0）：嘔吐、嚴重腹瀉、言語不清、耳鳴、視力模糊。重度（>2.0）：眼球震顫、肌束顫動、反射亢進、少尿、抽搐、昏迷。
  • Toxicity actions: withhold lithium, notify provider, monitor VS, LOC and ECG, obtain lithium, electrolytes, BUN/creatinine, CBC; seizure and suicide precautions. Missed dose: take within 2 hours, otherwise skip. Do not stop abruptly.中毒處置：停藥並通知醫師，監測生命徵象、意識與心電圖，抽鋰濃度、電解質、BUN／肌酸酐、全血球計數；採取抽搐與自殺防範。漏服 2 小時內可補服，否則跳過。不可驟停。

  • Benzodiazepines (alprazolam, lorazepam, diazepam, clonazepam, midazolam...) enhance GABA: anxiolytic, sedative, muscle relaxant, anticonvulsant. Contraindicated in acute narrow-angle glaucoma; cautious use in older adults and liver impairment; short-term use.苯二氮平類（alprazolam、lorazepam、diazepam、clonazepam、midazolam 等）增強 GABA：抗焦慮、鎮靜、肌肉鬆弛、抗痙攣。急性窄角青光眼禁用；老年人與肝功能不全者慎用；宜短期使用。
  • Adverse effects: sedation, dizziness, hypotension, amnesia, slurred speech, paradoxical excitement (older adults). Antidote for overdose: flumazenil IV (acts within about 5 minutes; may cause agitation or seizures).不良反應：鎮靜、頭暈、低血壓、失憶、言語不清、老年人矛盾性興奮。過量解毒劑：flumazenil 靜脈注射（約 5 分鐘起效）。

  • Buspirone is a non-benzodiazepine anxiolytic; effective when anxiety signs such as rapid heartbeat and tension are absent after several weeks; no sedation or dependence.buspirone 為非苯二氮平類抗焦慮藥，數週後心跳加速、緊張等焦慮表現消失代表有效；無明顯鎮靜與依賴。
  • Barbiturates (phenobarbital, pentobarbital) and hypnotics (zolpidem, eszopiclone, zaleplon, ramelteon, chloral hydrate): depress the CNS via GABA. Overdose: respiratory depression, hypotension, shock, coma. Withdrawal within 24 hours in dependent clients: tremor, delirium, seizures. Avoid alcohol; take 30 minutes before bed; morning hangover possible.巴比妥類（phenobarbital、pentobarbital）及安眠藥（zolpidem、eszopiclone、zaleplon、ramelteon、chloral hydrate）經 GABA 抑制中樞。過量：呼吸抑制、低血壓、休克、昏迷。依賴者 24 小時內可出現戒斷：顫抖、譫妄、抽搐。避免酒精；睡前 30 分鐘服用；隔日可能宿醉感。

Antipsychotics and Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome抗精神病藥與抗精神病藥惡性症候群（NMS）
  • Block dopamine receptors. Typical (haloperidol, chlorpromazine, fluphenazine): better for positive symptoms (hallucinations, delusions, aggression). Atypical (risperidone, olanzapine, clozapine, quetiapine, aripiprazole): also help negative symptoms (apathy, avolition, alogia).阻斷多巴胺受體。典型藥（haloperidol、chlorpromazine、fluphenazine）對陽性症狀（幻覺、妄想、攻擊）較有效；非典型藥（risperidone、olanzapine、clozapine、quetiapine、aripiprazole）對陰性症狀（冷漠、無意志、言語貧乏）亦有效。
  • Extrapyramidal effects: parkinsonism (tremor, rigidity, shuffling gait, drooling), dystonia (oculogyric crisis, grimacing, torso twisting), akathisia (restlessness), tardive dyskinesia (tongue protrusion, chewing, involuntary movements).錐體外症狀：類帕金森氏症（顫抖、僵硬、拖曳步態、流口水）、急性肌張力不全（眼動危象、扮鬼臉、軀幹扭曲）、靜坐不能（坐立不安）、遲發性運動障礙（吐舌、咀嚼、不自主運動）。
  • Other effects: anticholinergic effects, orthostatic hypotension, photosensitivity, weight gain and hyperglycemia, agranulocytosis (sore throat, fever, malaise; clozapine: stop if WBC < 3000/mm3), jaundice, impaired temperature regulation, gynecomastia/lactation.其他：抗膽鹼作用、姿勢性低血壓、光敏感、體重增加與高血糖、顆粒性白血球缺乏（喉嚨痛、發燒、不適；clozapine 白血球 < 3000/mm3 須停藥）、黃疸、體溫調節受損、男性女乳症／泌乳。
  • Teaching: onset in 7-10 days, full effect 3-6 weeks; sunscreen and protective clothing; rise slowly; no alcohol; dilute liquid concentrate in juice, avoid skin contact; urine may turn pink to red-brown; taper gradually; long-acting injectables help adherence; monitor glucose and urine output.衛教：7–10 天見初效、3–6 週完整療效；使用防曬與防護衣物；緩慢改變姿勢；避免酒精；濃縮液以果汁稀釋並避免接觸皮膚；尿液可能變粉紅至紅褐色；需逐步減藥；長效針劑可提升服藥順從性；監測血糖與尿量。
  • NMS (rare, life-threatening; usually with first-generation drugs, at start, dose increase or switch): hyperpyrexia, severe muscle rigidity, autonomic instability (labile BP, tachycardia, diaphoresis, drooling), altered LOC, dysphagia, elevated WBC, CPK and LFTs.NMS（罕見、危及生命；多見於第一代藥物，開始、增量或換藥時）：高熱、嚴重肌肉僵硬、自律神經不穩（血壓波動、心搏過速、冒汗、流口水）、意識改變、吞嚥困難、白血球、CPK 及肝功能升高。
  • NMS actions: stop the drug, notify provider, VS and LOC monitoring, seizure precautions, antipyretics and cooling blanket, IV fluids/electrolytes; watch for DVT and rhabdomyolysis.NMS 處置：停藥、通知醫師、監測生命徵象與意識、抽搐防範、退燒藥及冷卻毯、靜脈輸液與電解質；注意深部靜脈栓塞與橫紋肌溶解。

Alcohol Withdrawal and Alcohol Use Disorder Drugs酒精戒斷與酒精使用障礙用藥
  • Benzodiazepines (chlordiazepoxide, diazepam, lorazepam, oxazepam) are the most effective for withdrawal: stabilize vital signs and reduce seizure and delirium tremens risk; long half-life agents give best protection.苯二氮平類（chlordiazepoxide、diazepam、lorazepam、oxazepam）為戒斷首選：穩定生命徵象、降低抽搐與震顫譫妄風險；長半衰期藥物保護最佳。
  • Atenolol/propranolol and clonidine reduce autonomic symptoms but only as adjuncts to benzodiazepines.atenolol／propranolol 與 clonidine 可減少自律神經症狀，但只能作為苯二氮平的輔助。
  • Thiamine (B1) IM then oral to prevent Wernicke's encephalopathy; also folic acid, multivitamin, magnesium if low.先肌肉注射再口服 thiamine（B1）預防 Wernicke 腦病變；另補葉酸、綜合維他命，鎂低時補鎂。
  • Maintaining abstinence (only after withdrawal is over): naltrexone blocks the pleasurable effect of alcohol; acamprosate reduces discomfort of abstinence; disulfiram causes a severe reaction if alcohol is ingested.維持戒酒（須戒斷結束後才用）：naltrexone 阻斷飲酒愉悅感；acamprosate 減輕戒酒不適；disulfiram 一旦飲酒會引起嚴重反應。
  • Disulfiram reaction: flushing, throbbing headache, nausea/vomiting, sweating, hypotension, tachycardia; begins within minutes, lasts 30 min-2 hr. Abstain at least 12 hours before first dose; avoid alcohol in cough medicines, mouthwash, aftershave, vinegar, rubbing alcohol; effect can persist several days after stopping.disulfiram 反應：臉潮紅、搏動性頭痛、噁心嘔吐、冒汗、低血壓、心搏過速；數分鐘內出現，持續 30 分鐘至 2 小時。首次用藥前至少戒酒 12 小時；避免含酒精之咳嗽藥、漱口水、刮鬍水、醋、消毒酒精；停藥後數天仍可能反應。

Effects: tachycardia, high BP, anorexia and weight loss, insomnia, agitation.ADHD 中樞刺激劑（methylphenidate、dexmethylphenidate、安非他命鹽類、lisdexamfetamine；另有 atomoxetine、guanfacine）使兒童穩定並延長專注。副作用：心搏過速、高血壓、食慾不振與體重下降、失眠、躁動。
  • Nursing: baseline ECG, BP, height and weight; last dose at least 6 hours before bedtime (14 hours for extended release); avoid OTC drugs; drug holidays may be used; benefit takes weeks.護理：基礎心電圖、血壓、身高體重；最後一劑至少於睡前 6 小時（長效劑型 14 小時）；避免非處方藥；可安排藥物假期；療效需數週。
  • Cholinesterase inhibitors for mild to moderate Alzheimer disease: donepezil (bradycardia), galantamine (bronchoconstriction; caution in asthma/COPD), rivastigmine (give with food; caution in ulcer, bradycardia, sick sinus, urinary obstruction). Common: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, anorexia, weight loss.輕中度阿茲海默症之膽鹼酯酶抑制劑：donepezil（心搏過緩）、galantamine（支氣管收縮；氣喘／COPD 慎用）、rivastigmine（隨餐服用；潰瘍、心搏過緩、病竇症候群、尿路阻塞者慎用）。常見：噁心、嘔吐、腹瀉、食慾不振、體重下降。
  • Memantine: NMDA antagonist for moderate to severe disease; dizziness, headache, confusion, constipation; avoid with amantadine or ketamine; urine-alkalinizing drugs (sodium bicarbonate) and renal impairment raise levels.memantine：NMDA 受體拮抗劑，用於中重度；副作用頭暈、頭痛、混亂、便秘；避免與 amantadine 或 ketamine 併用；鹼化尿液藥物（碳酸氫鈉）及腎功能不全會使濃度上升。
