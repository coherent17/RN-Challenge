Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Fever發燒 📖 PDF p.465 (book p.416)

  • Fever is 38.0 C (100.4 F) or higher; normal about 36.4-37.0 C. Fever in an infant under 1 month is an emergency: call the provider immediately.發燒定義為 ≥38.0°C（100.4°F）；正常約 36.4–37.0°C。未滿 1 個月嬰兒發燒屬緊急，須立即聯絡醫師。
  • Signs: flushed warm skin, diaphoresis, chills, restlessness or lethargy.徵象：皮膚潮紅溫熱、出汗、寒顫、躁動或嗜睡。
  • Remove excess clothing/blankets, cool room, circulate air; acetaminophen, or ibuprofen if older than 6 months; no aspirin (Reye syndrome).脫去多餘衣被、降低室溫、增加通風；給予乙醯胺酚，或 6 個月以上給 ibuprofen；禁用阿斯匹靈（雷氏症候群）。
  • Recheck temperature 30-60 minutes after antipyretic; encourage fluids; avoid cold sponge baths (shivering).退燒藥後 30–60 分鐘再測體溫；鼓勵飲水；避免冷水擦浴（引發顫抖）。

Dehydration脫水 📖 PDF p.465 (book p.416), PDF p.466 (book p.417)

  • Infants/children are vulnerable: immature kidneys and more body water in extracellular fluid.嬰幼兒易脫水：腎臟未成熟且細胞外液比例較高。
  • Table 33.1: mild 3-5% (infant) weight loss; moderate 6-9%; severe 10% or more. Severe: very fast pulse, hyperpnea, lethargy, absent tears, sunken fontanel, capillary refill over 4 sec, oliguria/anuria.表 33.1：輕度體重減少 3–5%（嬰兒）；中度 6–9%；重度 ≥10%。重度：脈搏很快、深快呼吸、嗜睡、無淚、囪門凹陷、微血管再充填 >4 秒、少尿/無尿。
  • Monitor weight, I&O, urine specific gravity, LOC, skin turgor, mucous membranes.監測體重、輸出入量、尿比重、意識、皮膚彈性、黏膜。
  • Mild-moderate: oral rehydration (Pedialyte); avoid carbonated drinks and apple juice. Severe: NPO and IV fluids.輕中度：口服電解質液（如 Pedialyte）；避免碳酸飲料與蘋果汁。重度：禁食並靜脈輸液。
  • Before IV potassium confirm the child has voided and has adequate renal function.給靜脈鉀前確認已排尿且腎功能足夠。

Phenylketonuria (PKU)苯酮尿症 📖 PDF p.466 (book p.417), PDF p.467 (book p.418)

  • Autosomal recessive; phenylalanine toxic to CNS. PKU: phenylalanine above 20 mg/dL; normal 0-2 mg/dL.體染色體隱性；苯丙胺酸對中樞神經有毒性。PKU：苯丙胺酸 >20 mg/dL；正常 0–2 mg/dL。
  • Signs: vomiting, seizures, musty urine odor, intellectual disability; older: eczema, hypertonia, hypopigmentation, hyperactivity.徵象：嘔吐、癲癇、尿液霉味、智能障礙；較大：濕疹、肌張力高、毛髮皮膚虹膜色素減退、過動。
  • Newborn screening after feeding begun; rescreen by 14 days if first test before 24 hours old.新生兒篩檢須已開始餵奶；若 24 小時內採檢，14 天內再篩。
  • Restrict phenylalanine: meats, dairy, aspartame; use special formula; allowed cereals, pasta, rice, fruits, vegetables; follow-up and social support.限制苯丙胺酸：肉類、乳製品、阿斯巴甜；使用特殊配方奶；可吃穀類、麵、飯、蔬果；定期追蹤與社會資源。

Childhood obesity兒童肥胖 📖 PDF p.467 (book p.418), PDF p.468 (book p.419)

  • Use BMI percentile for ages 2-20: overweight 85th to under 95th; obese above 95th; severe obesity 120% of 95th percentile or BMI 35 or more.2–20 歲使用 BMI 百分位：過重 85th 至 <95th；肥胖 >95th；重度肥胖為 95th 的 120% 或 BMI ≥35。
  • Consequences: asthma, sleep apnea, joint problems, type 2 diabetes, hyperlipidemia, heart disease, adult obesity.後果：氣喘、睡眠呼吸中止、關節問題、第二型糖尿病、高血脂、心臟病、成年肥胖。
  • Box 33.1: water instead of sugary drinks, more fruits/vegetables, 60 minutes activity daily, screen time limited to 60 minutes.框 33.1：以水取代含糖飲料、多吃蔬果、每日活動 60 分鐘、螢幕時間限 60 分鐘。

Diabetes mellitus: basics, diet, exercise糖尿病：基礎、飲食、運動 📖 PDF p.467 (book p.418), PDF p.468 (book p.419), PDF p.469 (book p.420)

  • Type 1: beta-cell destruction and absolute insulin deficiency. Type 2: insulin resistance; overweight is the top childhood risk.第一型：胰島 beta 細胞破壞，絕對缺乏胰島素。第二型：胰島素阻抗；過重為最大危險因子。
  • Signs: polyuria, polydipsia, polyphagia, weight loss, fatigue, enuresis in toilet-trained child, vaginal candidiasis, fruity breath, blurred vision, slow healing. DKA can be first presentation.徵象：多尿、多渴、多食、體重減輕、疲倦、已訓練如廁者遺尿、陰道念珠菌感染、水果味呼吸、視力模糊、傷口難癒。DKA 可能是首次表現。
  • Diet: three meals plus midafternoon and bedtime snacks, consistent carbohydrate; fat 30% or less; carry glucose tablets.飲食：三餐加下午與睡前點心，碳水穩定；脂肪 ≤30%；隨身攜帶葡萄糖錠。
  • Exercise: 10-15 g extra carbohydrate per 30-45 minutes of activity; check glucose before and after.運動：每 30–45 分鐘活動加 10–15 g 碳水；運動前後測血糖。
  • Long-term: growth failure, neuropathy, retinal and renal microvascular disease, cardiovascular disease.長期併發症：生長遲緩、神經病變、視網膜與腎微血管病變、心血管疾病。

Insulin and monitoring胰島素與監測 📖 PDF p.469 (book p.420)

  • HbA1c every 3 months (reference under 6%). Never withhold insulin during illness; stress and infection raise insulin need.每 3 個月測 HbA1c（參考值 <6%）。生病時不可停胰島素；感染與壓力使需求增加。
  • Teach hypo/hyperglycemia recognition, glucagon IM/SC for unconscious child, spare insulin, MedicAlert.教導辨識低/高血糖、無法進食者施打 glucagon（IM/SC）、備用胰島素、醫療警示手環。
  • Blood glucose monitoring more accurate than urine; second-voided urine for ketones; test ketones when ill or glucose persistently over 200 mg/dL.血糖監測比尿糖準確；尿酮用第二次排尿；生病或血糖持續 >200 mg/dL 時驗尿酮。
  • Less painful finger sticks: warm finger, side of the pad, ring finger or thumb, light lancet pressure.減輕採血疼痛：先溫手、取指腹側邊、用無名指或拇指、輕壓採血筆。

Hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia低血糖與高血糖 📖 PDF p.469 (book p.420), PDF p.470 (book p.421)

  • Hypoglycemia: under 70 mg/dL; sweating, tremors, hunger, headache, confusion, slurred speech, perioral tingling.低血糖：<70 mg/dL；出汗、顫抖、飢餓、頭痛、意識混亂、口齒不清、口周麻刺。
  • Treat at once with fast sugar (1/2 cup juice, 2-3 glucose tablets, 1 tsp honey), then complex carbohydrate plus protein; if unconscious, glucose paste on gums, glucagon, IV dextrose in hospital.立即給快速糖（1/2 杯果汁、2–3 顆葡萄糖錠、1 茶匙蜂蜜），再給複合碳水加蛋白質；昏迷則牙齦塗糖膏、glucagon、院內靜脈葡萄糖。
  • Hyperglycemia: over 200 mg/dL; polyuria, polydipsia, blurred vision. Sick-day rules: always give insulin, test glucose every 4 hours, ketones each void, fluids, rest, call provider if vomiting, fruity breath, deep fast breathing, decreased LOC.高血糖：>200 mg/dL；多尿、多渴、視力模糊。生病日原則：照打胰島素、至少每 4 小時測血糖、每次排尿驗酮、多喝水、休息；嘔吐、水果味呼吸、深快呼吸、意識下降須通知醫師。

Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)糖尿病酮酸中毒 📖 PDF p.470 (book p.421), PDF p.471 (book p.422)

  • Severe insulin deficiency: glucose over 300 mg/dL, positive serum and urine ketones, metabolic acidosis; Kussmaul respirations, fruity breath, lethargy.嚴重胰島素缺乏：血糖 >300 mg/dL、血尿酮陽性、代謝性酸中毒；Kussmaul 呼吸、水果味呼吸、嗜睡。
  • First: restore volume with IV 0.9% or 0.45% saline; then IV regular insulin; dextrose added when glucose falls.首要：以靜脈 0.9% 或 0.45% 生理食鹽水補充血容量；再給靜脈常規胰島素；血糖降低後加葡萄糖。
  • Monitor potassium closely (falls with insulin); give potassium only if child is voiding; watch for fluid overload and cerebral edema; give oxygen as prescribed.密切監測鉀（胰島素使鉀下降）；有排尿才補鉀；留意體液過多與腦水腫；依醫囑給氧。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Fever 38.0 C (100.4 F) in an infant under 1 month: emergency.未滿 1 個月嬰兒體溫 38.0°C：緊急。 📖 PDF p.465 (book p.416)
  2. Never give aspirin to a febrile child (Reye syndrome).發燒孩童勿給阿斯匹靈（雷氏症候群）。 📖 PDF p.465 (book p.416), PDF p.473 (book p.424)
  3. PKU: phenylalanine over 20 mg/dL; normal 0-2; avoid aspartame and high-protein foods.PKU：苯丙胺酸 >20 mg/dL；正常 0–2；避免阿斯巴甜與高蛋白食物。 📖 PDF p.466 (book p.417), PDF p.467 (book p.418), PDF p.472 (book p.423)
  4. Hypoglycemia is under 70 mg/dL: give fast carbohydrate immediately, do not delay to recheck.低血糖 <70 mg/dL：立即給快速碳水，勿延遲。 📖 PDF p.469 (book p.420), PDF p.473 (book p.424)
  5. Do not withhold insulin during illness; test ketones; push fluids.生病時不可停胰島素；驗尿酮；多補水。 📖 PDF p.469 (book p.420), PDF p.470 (book p.421), PDF p.472 (book p.423)
  6. DKA: glucose over 300, ketones, Kussmaul breathing; initial treatment is IV normal saline, not dextrose or potassium.DKA：血糖 >300、酮體、Kussmaul 呼吸；初始治療為靜脈生理食鹽水，非葡萄糖或鉀。 📖 PDF p.470 (book p.421), PDF p.472 (book p.423)
  7. Before IV potassium check urine output (void, adequate renal function).靜脈給鉀前先確認尿量。 📖 PDF p.466 (book p.417), PDF p.472 (book p.423)
  8. Fluid deficit resolving: capillary refill under 2 sec, urine output at least 1 mL/kg/hr, adequate tears, specific gravity normal.脫水改善：微血管再充填 <2 秒、尿量 ≥1 mL/kg/hr、有淚、尿比重正常。 📖 PDF p.466 (book p.417), PDF p.473 (book p.424)
  9. Exercise: extra 10-15 g carbohydrate per 30-45 minutes; do not alter insulin amount or timing without provider.運動：每 30–45 分鐘多補 10–15 g 碳水；勿自行改胰島素劑量或時間。 📖 PDF p.469 (book p.420), PDF p.472 (book p.423)
  10. NPH insulin is never given IV; regular insulin is IV insulin.NPH 絕不可靜脈注射；靜脈用常規胰島素。 📖 PDF p.470 (book p.421), PDF p.472 (book p.423)
  11. BMI percentile: overweight 85th-95th, obese above 95th.BMI 百分位：過重 85–95th，肥胖 >95th。 📖 PDF p.467 (book p.418)
  12. Ketone testing when ill or glucose persistently over 200 mg/dL; second-voided urine is most accurate.生病或血糖持續 >200 mg/dL 時驗酮；第二次排尿最準確。 📖 PDF p.469 (book p.420)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Hypo = cold, clammy, shaky; Hyper = hot, dry, sweet-smelling (fruity breath).低血糖＝冷汗顫抖；高血糖＝皮膚熱乾、水果味呼吸。 📖 PDF p.470 (book p.421)

1. A child with type 1 diabetes has soccer practice. What should the nurse advise to prevent hypoglycemia?第一型糖尿病孩童要參加足球練習，護理師應如何指導以預防低血糖？

An extra 15-30 g carbohydrate snack before activity prevents hypoglycemia.活動前額外補充 15–30 g 碳水可預防低血糖。

Fluids clear ketones; do not change insulin doses without the provider.水分可幫助清除酮體；未經醫師勿調整胰島素。

3. What is the priority assessment before giving IV D5 0.45% saline with 40 mEq KCl?給予 D5 0.45% 生理食鹽水含 40 mEq KCl 前，優先評估為何？

Potassium is not given in oliguria or anuria.少尿或無尿時不可給鉀。

5. A 2-week-old rescreen shows phenylalanine 1 mg/dL. Interpretation?2 週大嬰兒再篩苯丙胺酸 1 mg/dL，如何判讀？

Normal is 0-2 mg/dL; PKU is above 20 mg/dL.正常 0–2 mg/dL；PKU >20 mg/dL。

6. Initial IV therapy for a child in DKA?DKA 孩童初始靜脈治療為何？

Rehydration first; insulin regular IV; dextrose added later.先補液；之後給常規胰島素；後期加葡萄糖。
