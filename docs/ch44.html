Dermatologic pharmacology: poison ivy and dermatitis treatments, topical glucocorticoids, drugs for actinic keratosis, sunscreens, psoriasis therapies (topical, systemic, biologic, phototherapy), acne products including isotretinoin and iPLEDGE, and burn products (silver sulfadiazine, mafenide).皮膚科藥理：毒藤與皮膚炎治療、外用糖皮質激素、光化性角化症用藥、防曬乳、乾癬療法（外用、全身、生物製劑、光療）、痤瘡用藥（含isotretinoin與iPLEDGE）、燒傷用藥（silver sulfadiazine、mafenide）。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Poison ivy and dermatitis treatment毒藤與皮膚炎治療

  • Poison ivy: calamine, astringent/antiseptic wet compresses, colloidal oatmeal baths; topical steroids work best before blisters form; oral steroids for severe cases; diphenhydramine may aid sleep.毒藤：calamine、收斂/抗菌濕敷、膠體燕麥浴；外用類固醇於水泡形成前效果最佳；嚴重者口服類固醇；diphenhydramine可助眠。
  • Dermatitis (atopic, contact, stasis) is epidermal inflammation; first-line moisturizer plus topical glucocorticoid; systemic immunosuppressants (azathioprine, cyclosporine, methotrexate) if refractory.皮膚炎（異位性、接觸性、鬱滯性）為表皮發炎；首選保濕劑加外用糖皮質激素；無效時用全身免疫抑制劑（azathioprine、cyclosporine、methotrexate）。
  • Topical tacrolimus/pimecrolimus: burning, itching, sun sensitivity (protect from sun); tacrolimus specifically is linked to a higher risk of skin cancer/lymphoma and, in children, varicella zoster infection.外用tacrolimus/pimecrolimus：灼熱、癢、光敏感（需防曬）；其中tacrolimus本身與皮膚癌/淋巴瘤風險上升有關，兒童使用則可能增加感染帶狀疱疹的風險。
  • Wear gloves for any topical medication or patch and wash hands before and after.使用任何外用藥或貼片時戴手套，前後洗手。

2Topical glucocorticoids外用糖皮質激素

  • Anti-inflammatory, antipruritic, vasoconstrictive; potency depends on concentration, vehicle and occlusion (occlusive dressings increase absorption).抗發炎、止癢、血管收縮；強度依濃度、劑型與是否密封而異（密封敷料增加吸收）。
  • Absorption is higher on scalp, axilla, face, neck, eyelids, perineum; lower on palms, soles, back.頭皮、腋下、臉、頸、眼瞼、會陰吸收較高；手掌、腳底、背部吸收較低。
  • Contraindicated with prior steroid sensitivity or current systemic fungal, viral or bacterial infection.曾對類固醇過敏或目前有全身性黴菌、病毒、細菌感染者禁用。
  • Local effects: burning, dryness, skin thinning/atrophy, striae, purpura, telangiectasia, acneiform eruptions, hypopigmentation, infection overgrowth.局部效應：灼熱、乾燥、皮膚變薄/萎縮、妊娠紋、紫斑、微血管擴張、痤瘡樣疹、色素減退、感染過度生長。
  • Systemic effects: growth retardation in children, adrenal suppression, Cushing syndrome, glaucoma, cataracts.全身效應：兒童生長遲緩、腎上腺抑制、庫欣症候群、青光眼、白內障。
  • Apply sparingly in a thin film on cleaned skin with gloves; no dry occlusive dressing unless prescribed; avoid open areas unless prescribed.戴手套、清潔皮膚後薄薄塗抹；除非醫囑勿用乾性密封敷料；非醫囑勿塗於開放傷口。

3Actinic keratosis medications光化性角化症用藥

  • Drugs: diclofenac 3% gel (NSAID, up to 3 months), fluorouracil (2-6 weeks, sun sensitivity), imiquimod 5% (up to 4 months, also genital warts), ingenol mebutate, aminolevulinic acid.藥物：diclofenac 3%凝膠（NSAID，最長3個月）、fluorouracil（2-6週、光敏感）、imiquimod 5%（最長4個月、亦用於尖濕疣）、ingenol mebutate、aminolevulinic acid。
  • Expected reactions: redness, burning, crusting, swelling; ingenol risk of severe allergy and herpes zoster.預期反應：紅、灼熱、結痂、腫；ingenol有嚴重過敏與帶狀疱疹風險。
  • Lesions are premalignant (squamous cell carcinoma); other treatments: cryotherapy, curettage, excision, laser.病灶為癌前病變（鱗狀細胞癌）；其他治療：冷凍、刮除、切除、雷射。

4Sunscreens防曬乳

  • Organic (chemical) products absorb UV; inorganic (physical) products reflect/scatter UV.有機（化學性）吸收紫外線；無機（物理性）反射/散射紫外線。
  • Use broad-spectrum (UVA and UVB) with SPF at least 15.使用廣譜（UVA與UVB）、SPF至少15。
  • Apply at least 30 minutes before exposure (PABA or padimate O products 2 hours before); reapply every 2-3 hours and after swimming or sweating.曝曬前至少30分鐘塗抹（含PABA或padimate O者需2小時前）；每2-3小時及游泳/流汗後補擦。
  • Avoid PABA if allergic to benzocaine, sulfonamides or thiazides; sunscreens can cause contact dermatitis/photosensitivity.對benzocaine、磺胺類或thiazide過敏者避免含PABA產品；防曬乳可能造成接觸性皮膚炎/光敏感。
  • UV peaks 10 a.m.-4 p.m.; add hat, sunglasses, protective clothing.紫外線於上午10點至下午4點最強；另戴帽、太陽眼鏡、穿防護衣物。

5Psoriasis: topical agents乾癬：外用藥

  • Topical glucocorticoids for mild disease; not on face, groin, axilla or genitalia (atrophy).外用糖皮質激素用於輕度；勿用於臉、鼠蹊、腋下、生殖器（萎縮）。
  • Tazarotene (vitamin A derivative): apply in the evening to dry skin; irritation and sun sensitivity.Tazarotene（維生素A衍生物）：晚上塗於乾燥皮膚；刺激與光敏感。
  • Calcipotriene (vitamin D analog): 1-3 weeks to work; local irritation; rarely hypercalcemia at high doses.Calcipotriene（維生素D類似物）：1-3週見效；局部刺激；高劑量罕見高血鈣。
  • Coal tar: odor, staining, irritation, sun sensitivity, cancer risk at high doses.煤焦油：氣味、染色、刺激、光敏感、高劑量致癌風險。
  • Keratolytics: salicylic acid can cause salicylism (dizziness, tinnitus, hyperpnea, psychological changes), so avoid large areas/open wounds; sulfur promotes peeling.角質溶解劑：salicylic acid可造成水楊酸中毒（頭暈、耳鳴、過度換氣、精神症狀），故避免大面積/開放傷口；硫磺促進脫屑。

6Psoriasis: systemic, biologic and phototherapy乾癬：全身、生物製劑與光療

  • Methotrexate: GI effects, stomatitis, bone marrow depression, hepatotoxic (monitor liver function), teratogenic (wait 3 months after stopping before pregnancy).Methotrexate：胃腸反應、口腔炎、骨髓抑制、肝毒性（監測肝功能）、致畸胎（停藥後3個月才可懷孕）。
  • Acitretin: severe psoriasis only; teratogenic (two contraceptive forms starting 1 month before, continuing 3 years after), hepatotoxic, raises triglycerides, lowers HDL; avoid alcohol, vitamin A, tetracycline.Acitretin：僅用於嚴重乾癬；致畸（治療前1個月起使用兩種避孕、停藥後持續3年）、肝毒性、升高三酸甘油酯、降低HDL；避免酒精、維生素A、四環黴素。
  • Cyclosporine: immunosuppressant, nephrotoxic, for severe disease.Cyclosporine：免疫抑制劑、腎毒性、用於嚴重乾癬。
  • Biologics (TNF-alpha blockers and interleukin inhibitors: adalimumab, etanercept, infliximab, ustekinumab, etc.): screen for TB first; risk of serious infection, hepatitis B reactivation, lymphoma; no live vaccines; no BCG 1 year before/after.生物製劑（TNF-alpha阻斷劑與介白素抑制劑：adalimumab、etanercept、infliximab、ustekinumab等）：先篩檢結核；嚴重感染、B肝再活化、淋巴瘤風險；不可接種活性疫苗；BCG前後1年不接種。
  • Phototherapy: coal tar plus UVB; psoralen with UVA (oral methoxsalen) raises skin cancer and aging risk.光療：煤焦油加UVB；psoralen合併UVA（口服methoxsalen）增加皮膚癌與老化風險。

7Acne: topical agents and oral antibiotics痤瘡：外用藥與口服抗生素

  • Stepwise: mild = gentle cleansing and topical antimicrobials/retinoids; moderate = oral antibiotics and comedolytics; severe = isotretinoin; hormonal options.階段性：輕度＝溫和清潔與外用抗菌/類視黃酮；中度＝口服抗生素與溶粉刺劑；重度＝isotretinoin；可加荷爾蒙療法。
  • Wash and dry skin before applying; wash hands after; keep away from eyes, nose, lips, mucosa, inflamed skin.塗抹前洗淨擦乾皮膚；之後洗手；避開眼、鼻、唇、黏膜、發炎皮膚。
  • Benzoyl peroxide: drying and peeling; reduce frequency if severe irritation. Clindamycin/erythromycin: combine with benzoyl peroxide to prevent resistance. Dapsone topical.Benzoyl peroxide：乾燥脫皮；刺激嚴重時減少頻率。Clindamycin/erythromycin：與benzoyl peroxide併用防抗藥性。外用dapsone。
  • Tretinoin, adapalene, tazarotene: vitamin A derivatives, burning, peeling, UV sensitivity (SPF 15+); stop vitamin A supplements; adapalene benefits at 8-12 weeks, may worsen initially. Azelaic acid: stinging, hypopigmentation in dark skin.Tretinoin、adapalene、tazarotene：維生素A衍生物，灼熱、脫皮、光敏感（SPF 15以上）；停用維生素A補充；adapalene 8-12週見效，初期可能惡化。Azelaic acid：刺痛、深膚色者色素減退。
  • Oral antibiotics (doxycycline, minocycline, tetracycline, erythromycin, etc.) take 3-6 months to improve.口服抗生素（doxycycline、minocycline、tetracycline、erythromycin等）需3-6個月見效。

8Isotretinoin, iPLEDGE and hormonal therapyIsotretinoin、iPLEDGE與荷爾蒙療法

  • Isotretinoin: severe cystic acne unresponsive to other therapy; highly teratogenic; stop vitamin A and tetracyclines.Isotretinoin：用於對其他療法無效的嚴重囊腫性痤瘡；高度致畸；停用維生素A與四環黴素。
  • Effects: dry lips/eyes/skin, nosebleeds, joint/muscle pain, reduced night vision, UV sensitivity, depression (stop if it occurs).副作用：唇/眼/皮膚乾燥、流鼻血、關節/肌肉痛、夜視力下降、光敏感、憂鬱（發生需停藥）。
  • Check triglycerides before and during therapy; avoid alcohol.治療前與治療中監測三酸甘油酯；避免酒精。
  • iPLEDGE: controlled central system ensuring no one starts therapy pregnant or becomes pregnant; strict rules for patient, prescriber, pharmacist, wholesaler.iPLEDGE：集中管控系統，確保開始治療時未懷孕且治療期間不懷孕；病人、處方者、藥師、批發商皆須遵守。
  • Spironolactone and oral contraceptives lower androgen activity; spironolactone is teratogenic and can cause hyperkalemia, breast tenderness, menstrual irregularity.Spironolactone與口服避孕藥降低雄性素活性；spironolactone致畸，可能高血鉀、乳房脹痛、月經不規則。

9Burn products燒傷用藥

  • Silver sulfadiazine: broad antimicrobial (including yeast) to prevent sepsis; does not speed healing; apply 1/16-inch film and keep covered; does not cause acidosis.Silver sulfadiazine：廣效抗菌（含酵母）預防敗血症；不促進癒合；塗1/16吋薄層並保持覆蓋；不會造成酸中毒。
  • Silver sulfadiazine adverse effects: rash, itching, blue-green/gray discoloration, leukopenia, interstitial nephritis; monitor CBC/WBC and report leukopenia.Silver sulfadiazine副作用：皮疹、癢、藍綠/灰色變色、白血球減少、間質性腎炎；監測CBC/WBC並通報白血球低下。
  • Mafenide acetate: penetrates avascular tissue; carbonic anhydrase inhibition causes metabolic acidosis with hyperventilation; painful, so premedicate; bone marrow depression, hemolytic anemia.Mafenide acetate：可穿透無血管組織；抑制碳酸酐酶造成代謝性酸中毒伴過度換氣；疼痛故需先給止痛；骨髓抑制、溶血性貧血。
  • If hyperventilation occurs, notify provider, check ABG/electrolytes; if acidosis, wash off and hold 1-2 days.出現過度換氣須通知醫師、檢查血液氣體/電解質；若酸中毒，洗去並停用1-2天。
  • Other products: bacitracin (first-degree burns only), povidone-iodine, chlorhexidine skin cleanser.其他：bacitracin（僅1度燒傷）、povidone-iodine、chlorhexidine皮膚清潔劑。

  • Cyclosporine: immunosuppressant, nephrotoxic, for severe disease.Cyclosporine：免疫抑制劑、腎毒性、用於嚴重乾癬。
  • Biologics (TNF-alpha blockers and interleukin inhibitors: adalimumab, etanercept, infliximab, ustekinumab, etc.): screen for TB first; risk of serious infection, hepatitis B reactivation, lymphoma; no live vaccines; no BCG 1 year before/after.生物製劑（TNF-alpha阻斷劑與介白素抑制劑：adalimumab、etanercept、infliximab、ustekinumab等）：先篩檢結核；嚴重感染、B肝再活化、淋巴瘤風險；不可接種活性疫苗；BCG前後1年不接種。
  • Phototherapy: coal tar plus UVB; psoralen with UVA (oral methoxsalen) raises skin cancer and aging risk.光療：煤焦油加UVB；psoralen合併UVA（口服methoxsalen）增加皮膚癌與老化風險。

Acne: topical agents and oral antibiotics痤瘡：外用藥與口服抗生素
  • Stepwise: mild = gentle cleansing and topical antimicrobials/retinoids; moderate = oral antibiotics and comedolytics; severe = isotretinoin; hormonal options.階段性：輕度＝溫和清潔與外用抗菌/類視黃酮；中度＝口服抗生素與溶粉刺劑；重度＝isotretinoin；可加荷爾蒙療法。
  • Wash and dry skin before applying; wash hands after; keep away from eyes, nose, lips, mucosa, inflamed skin.塗抹前洗淨擦乾皮膚；之後洗手；避開眼、鼻、唇、黏膜、發炎皮膚。
  • Benzoyl peroxide: drying and peeling; reduce frequency if severe irritation. Clindamycin/erythromycin: combine with benzoyl peroxide to prevent resistance. Dapsone topical.Benzoyl peroxide：乾燥脫皮；刺激嚴重時減少頻率。Clindamycin/erythromycin：與benzoyl peroxide併用防抗藥性。外用dapsone。
  • Tretinoin, adapalene, tazarotene: vitamin A derivatives, burning, peeling, UV sensitivity (SPF 15+); stop vitamin A supplements; adapalene benefits at 8-12 weeks, may worsen initially. Azelaic acid: stinging, hypopigmentation in dark skin.Tretinoin、adapalene、tazarotene：維生素A衍生物，灼熱、脫皮、光敏感（SPF 15以上）；停用維生素A補充；adapalene 8-12週見效，初期可能惡化。Azelaic acid：刺痛、深膚色者色素減退。
  • Oral antibiotics (doxycycline, minocycline, tetracycline, erythromycin, etc.) take 3-6 months to improve.口服抗生素（doxycycline、minocycline、tetracycline、erythromycin等）需3-6個月見效。

Isotretinoin, iPLEDGE and hormonal therapyIsotretinoin、iPLEDGE與荷爾蒙療法
  • Isotretinoin: severe cystic acne unresponsive to other therapy; highly teratogenic; stop vitamin A and tetracyclines.Isotretinoin：用於對其他療法無效的嚴重囊腫性痤瘡；高度致畸；停用維生素A與四環黴素。

  • Check triglycerides before and during therapy; avoid alcohol.治療前與治療中監測三酸甘油酯；避免酒精。
  • iPLEDGE: controlled central system ensuring no one starts therapy pregnant or becomes pregnant; strict rules for patient, prescriber, pharmacist, wholesaler.iPLEDGE：集中管控系統，確保開始治療時未懷孕且治療期間不懷孕；病人、處方者、藥師、批發商皆須遵守。

Burn products燒傷用藥
  • Silver sulfadiazine: broad antimicrobial (including yeast) to prevent sepsis; does not speed healing; apply 1/16-inch film and keep covered; does not cause acidosis.Silver sulfadiazine：廣效抗菌（含酵母）預防敗血症；不促進癒合；塗1/16吋薄層並保持覆蓋；不會造成酸中毒。
  • Silver sulfadiazine adverse effects: rash, itching, blue-green/gray discoloration, leukopenia, interstitial nephritis; monitor CBC/WBC and report leukopenia.Silver sulfadiazine副作用：皮疹、癢、藍綠/灰色變色、白血球減少、間質性腎炎；監測CBC/WBC並通報白血球低下。
  • Mafenide acetate: penetrates avascular tissue; carbonic anhydrase inhibition causes metabolic acidosis with hyperventilation; painful, so premedicate; bone marrow depression, hemolytic anemia.Mafenide acetate：可穿透無血管組織；抑制碳酸酐酶造成代謝性酸中毒伴過度換氣；疼痛故需先給止痛；骨髓抑制、溶血性貧血。

  • Other products: bacitracin (first-degree burns only), povidone-iodine, chlorhexidine skin cleanser.其他：bacitracin（僅1度燒傷）、povidone-iodine、chlorhexidine皮膚清潔劑。
