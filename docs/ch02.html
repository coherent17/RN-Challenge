Chapter 2: Self-Efficacy and Pathways to Success第 2 章：自我效能與成功途徑

📖 Source: PDF p.35–39 · printed book p.17–21來源：PDF 第 35–39 頁 · 書本印刷頁 17–21

This short chapter applies Bandura's self-efficacy theory to NCLEX preparation. It guides you through self-reflection, goal setting, a structured study plan, self-care and a calm approach to the final days and exam day.本章將班杜拉自我效能理論應用於 NCLEX 備考，引導自我反思、設定目標、結構化讀書計畫、自我照顧，以及最後階段與考試日的穩定心態。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Self-efficacy (Bandura)自我效能（班杜拉） 📖 PDF p.35 (book p.17)

  • Self-efficacy is a belief in your own ability to carry out particular behaviors; it shapes which behaviors you choose, how long you persevere and how well you perform.自我效能是相信自己有能力執行特定行為；它影響你選擇的行為、堅持程度與表現品質。
  • High self-efficacy expectations lead to hard work and persistence; low expectations lead to self-doubt about passing.高自我效能期望促使努力與堅持；低期望則導致對通過考試的自我懷疑。
  • Research (Silvestri, Clark and Moonie, 2013) found self-efficacy expectations were an important predictor of NCLEX success.研究（Silvestri 等，2013）顯示自我效能期望是 NCLEX 成功的重要預測因子。

Pathways to success: self-reflection成功途徑：自我反思 📖 PDF p.35 (book p.17), PDF p.36 (book p.18)

  • Self-reflection lets you review past experiences and build confidence about your capabilities.自我反思讓你回顧過往經驗並建立對能力的信心。
  • Journal your accomplishments and re-read them whenever doubt appears.記錄你的成就，疑慮出現時重新閱讀。
  • Ask yourself about goal setting: are goals realistic, do you reach them, how do you overcome obstacles?自問目標設定：目標是否務實、是否達成、遇到障礙如何克服？

The foundation and the List基礎與目標清單 📖 PDF p.36 (book p.18)

  • Foundation: strong positive attitude, sense of self-efficacy, self-control, and short- and long-term goals.基礎：積極態度、自我效能感、自我控制，以及短期與長期目標。
  • Calm yourself with slow breathing (inhale through the nose, hold for 4, exhale slowly through the mouth).以緩慢呼吸平靜自己（鼻吸、屏息數到 4、嘴慢慢吐氣）。
  • Write the List of goals (today, tomorrow, next month, future), set it aside 2-3 days, then review it.寫下目標清單（今天、明天、下個月、未來），擱置 2-3 天後再檢視。

Plan for preparation and study schedule準備計畫與讀書時程 📖 PDF p.36 (book p.18), PDF p.37 (book p.19)

  • Choose the study pattern that has worked for you before (alone vs group, time of day, place).選擇過去有效的讀書模式（獨自或小組、時段、地點）。
  • Schedule at least 2 hours of quality, uninterrupted study daily on a calendar; quality matters more than quantity.在行事曆排定每天至少 2 小時不被打擾的高品質讀書時間；重質不重量。
  • Share your plan with family and friends; protect study time when others pressure you to skip it.與家人朋友分享計畫；他人勸你跳過時要守住讀書時間。

Implementing the study plan執行讀書計畫 📖 PDF p.37 (book p.19), PDF p.38 (book p.20)

  • Self-assess strong and weak content areas, then start practice questions in weaker areas.自我評估強弱項目，從較弱領域開始做題。
  • Note unfamiliar content and review it in your review book and textbooks after each session.記下不熟的內容，每次讀完後翻閱複習書與教科書補強。
  • Practice at least 4000 questions; start in Study Mode, read every rationale and strategy, then move to Exam Mode to check readiness.至少練習 4000 題；先用學習模式、閱讀每題解析與策略，再用考試模式檢測準備度。

Positive pampering, final preparation and exam day正向照顧自己、最後準備與考試日 📖 PDF p.38 (book p.20), PDF p.39 (book p.21)

  • Include exercise, healthy eating, water, complex carbohydrates, limited caffeine and fun in your schedule.排入運動、健康飲食、足量飲水、複合碳水化合物、限制咖啡因及休閒活動。
  • End studying 2 days before the exam and rest the day before; avoid perfectionism.考前 2 天結束複習，前一天好好休息；避免完美主義。
  • Do a test drive to the testing center; post your name with RN and the exam date.事先試開到考場；貼出你的名字加上 RN 與考試日期。
  • Exam day: eat a nutritious breakfast, breathe, believe, stay in control and visualize RN after your name.考試日：吃營養早餐，呼吸、相信自己、保持掌控，想像名字後面有 RN。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Self-efficacy is a significant predictor of NCLEX success.自我效能是 NCLEX 成功的重要預測因子。 📖 PDF p.35 (book p.17)
  2. Plan at least 2 hours of quality study daily.每天規劃至少 2 小時高品質讀書時間。 📖 PDF p.36 (book p.18)
  3. Practice at least 4000 questions before the exam.考前至少練習 4000 題。 📖 PDF p.37 (book p.19), PDF p.38 (book p.20)
  4. Read rationales and test-taking strategies for every question, right or wrong.每題都要讀解析與策略，無論對錯。 📖 PDF p.38 (book p.20)
  5. Start with weaker content areas and keep notes on unfamiliar topics.從較弱領域開始並記錄不熟的主題。 📖 PDF p.37 (book p.19)
  6. Use Study Mode first, then Exam Mode to judge readiness.先用學習模式，再用考試模式評估準備度。 📖 PDF p.38 (book p.20)
  7. Stop studying 2 days before the exam; rest the day before.考前 2 天停止讀書；前一天休息。 📖 PDF p.38 (book p.20)
  8. Do a test drive to the testing center beforehand.事先試開前往考場。 📖 PDF p.38 (book p.20)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Day-of-exam cue: Breathe, Believe, Control, Believe, Visualize.考試日口訣：呼吸、相信、掌控、相信、想像（Breathe-Believe-Control-Believe-Visualize）。 📖 PDF p.39 (book p.21)

Self-reflection on achievements builds self-efficacy expectations and persistence.對成就的反思能建立自我效能期望與堅持。
