This chapter covers environmental safety (fire, electrical, radiation, sharps), fall prevention, restraints, poisoning, health care-associated infections, standard and transmission-based precautions, and bioterrorism/disaster response. Most questions ask what to do FIRST and which precaution or PPE fits the organism.本章涵蓋環境安全（火災、用電、輻射、尖銳物）、預防跌倒、約束、中毒、醫療照護相關感染、標準與傳播途徑預防措施，以及生物恐怖攻擊與災難應變。考題多半問「首先做什麼」，以及何種疾病應採用哪種隔離措施與個人防護裝備。

1Fire and Electrical Safety火災與用電安全

  • Know exits, alarms, extinguisher locations and the agency evacuation plan; never use elevators in a fire; turn off oxygen and nearby appliances.熟悉出口、警報器、滅火器位置及機構疏散計畫；火災時不可搭電梯；關閉附近氧氣與電器。
  • Clients on life support are bagged manually with an Ambu bag until moved away from the danger.使用維生設備的個案，在移離危險區前以甦醒球（Ambu bag）人工維持呼吸。
  • Evacuation order: ambulatory clients walk out first, then wheelchair, then bedridden clients by bed/stretcher.疏散順序：可自行行走者先走，其次輪椅，臥床者以床或擔架移送。
  • Extinguisher classes: A ordinary combustibles, B flammable liquids, C electrical, D metals, K cooking oils/fats.滅火器類別：A 一般可燃物、B 易燃液體、C 電器、D 可燃金屬、K 烹飪油脂。
  • Electrical: use grounded three-prong plugs (third longer prong is ground), pull the plug not the cord, avoid water, remove frayed or faulty equipment, and turn off power before touching a shocked client.用電：使用有接地的三孔插頭（較長的第三腳為接地），拔插頭時握插頭不可拉電線，遠離水源，移除破損設備，觸電個案須先關閉電源再接觸。
  • Client-owned electrical equipment must be inspected before use in the facility.個案自備的電器必須經檢查後才能在院內使用。

2Radiation Safety and Waste Disposal輻射安全與感染性廢棄物處理

  • Reduce exposure with time (limit), distance (maximize) and shielding (lead apron); wear a dosimeter badge.降低輻射暴露：縮短時間、拉大距離、使用鉛衣屏蔽，並配戴劑量計。
  • Client with a radiation implant: private room; never touch a dislodged implant with bare hands; keep linens in the room until the implant is removed.放射性植入物個案：住單人房；脫落的植入物不可徒手碰觸；植入物取出前，床單衣物留在房內。
  • Treat all infectious material as hazardous; use labeled, closed, puncture-resistant containers; never recap, bend or break needles.所有感染性物質視為危害物；使用有標示、密閉、防刺穿容器；針頭不可回套、折彎或折斷。

3Fall Prevention and Safe Ambulation預防跌倒與安全行走

  • Older adults have slower reflexes, reduced vision/hearing, weaker muscles and nocturia, all raising accident risk.老年人反射變慢、視聽力下降、肌力減弱、夜尿增加，都會提高意外風險。
  • Fall risk assessment is client-centered: validated scale, gait, balance, vision, medications, home hazards, prior falls, upcoming procedures.跌倒風險評估以個案為中心：使用量表，評估步態、平衡、視力、藥物、居家環境危險、過去跌倒史及即將進行的檢查處置。
  • Prevention: bed low and locked, call light and belongings within reach, bed/chair alarms, room near nurses' station, toileting schedule, clutter-free room, good lighting, sturdy non-skid footwear.預防措施：床降至最低並鎖定、呼叫鈴與物品放在可及處、使用床/椅警報器、安排靠近護理站、定時如廁、環境整潔、燈光充足、穿穩固防滑鞋。
  • Gait belt: apply before ambulating, snug enough for two fingers, hold both sides/back; return client to bed or chair if dizzy.使用步態帶（gait belt）：行走前繫上，鬆緊以可伸入兩指為度，握住兩側或後方；個案頭暈時立即回床或坐椅。
  • Nurse body mechanics: keep load close, bend knees, tighten abdominal muscles, keep trunk erect, use equipment.護理人員身體力學：負重靠近身體、彎曲膝蓋、收緊腹肌、保持軀幹直立、善用輔助設備。

4Restraints (Safety Devices)約束（安全裝置）

  • Physical restraints limit movement by a device; chemical restraints are drugs used to control behavior. Use alternatives first.身體約束以裝置限制活動；化學約束則以藥物抑制行為。須優先使用替代方式。
  • Requires a PHCP prescription naming type, behaviors and a limited time; never PRN; PHCP evaluates within 1 hour if not present; renew per policy; explain to and get permission from client/family.需醫囑，註明約束類型、適用行為及有限時間；不得開 PRN；醫師若不在場須於1小時內評估；依規定更新；向個案與家屬說明並取得同意。
  • Secure straps to the bed frame (not side rails or movable parts) with a quick-release knot/half-bow or buckle; leave slack, two fingers fit under strap.約束帶固定於床架（不可綁在床欄或可移動部位），使用快速解開結或快扣；保留鬆度，可伸入兩指。
  • Assess skin, circulation and neurovascular status frequently; release at least every 2 hours for ROM, toileting, fluids and ADLs.頻繁評估皮膚、循環及神經血管狀態；至少每2小時解除一次，進行關節活動、如廁、補水與日常照護。
  • Only the top two side rails are used and bed stays lowest; side rails to prevent a sedated client from falling are generally not a restraint per policy.床欄僅使用上方兩側，且床維持最低位置；依規定，為防鎮靜個案跌落的床欄通常不算約束。
  • Alternatives for confusion: reorientation, sitters/family, room near station, clocks/night-light, familiar objects, toileting routine, remove unneeded tubes, review medications, look for causes (hypoxia, electrolytes).意識混亂的替代措施：重新定向、陪伴者或家屬、靠近護理站、時鐘與夜燈、熟悉物品、定時如廁、盡早移除不必要管路、檢視藥物、尋找原因（缺氧、電解質異常）。
  • Document reason, method, time applied, duration, response, release/assessments and ongoing need.記錄使用原因、方法、開始時間、持續時間、反應、解除與評估情形及持續需要性。

5Poisoning中毒

  • Children (infants to young school-age) and older adults with poor vision or memory are most at risk of accidental poisoning.嬰幼兒至低年級學童，以及視力或記憶力差的老年人，是意外中毒的高風險族群。
  • Call Poison Control FIRST, before intervening; keep the number near the phone.先撥打毒物諮詢中心，再採取處置；電話號碼應放在電話旁。
  • Remove visible material from mouth/eyes/skin, identify substance and amount, save vomitus if asked, call an ambulance if directed to the ED.移除口、眼、皮膚上可見毒物，確認物質種類與量，依要求保留嘔吐物，若被指示送急診則叫救護車。
  • Never induce vomiting for lye, household cleaners, grease or petroleum products, or in an unconscious victim.吞食強鹼、家用清潔劑、油脂或石油產品，或個案昏迷時，絕不可催吐。

6Health Care-Associated Infections and Standard Precautions醫療照護相關感染與標準防護措施

  • HAIs are acquired in a facility and not incubating on admission; common resistant organisms: MRSA, VRE, CRE, multidrug-resistant TB, plus C. difficile.醫療照護相關感染為入院時未存在或潛伏、在醫療機構內獲得；常見抗藥菌：MRSA、VRE、CRE、多重抗藥結核，另有困難梭狀芽孢桿菌（C. difficile）。
  • C. difficile spreads by hand contact, mostly after prolonged multiple antibiotics; alcohol rubs do not kill spores, so wash with soap and water.C. difficile 經手接觸傳播，多見於長期使用多種抗生素者；酒精乾洗手無法殺死孢子，須以肥皂與水洗手。
  • Standard precautions apply to every client: blood, all body fluids, secretions, excretions, nonintact skin, mucous membranes.標準防護措施適用於所有個案：血液、所有體液、分泌物、排泄物、不完整皮膚及黏膜。
  • Hand hygiene between clients and after glove removal; alcohol rub if hands not visibly soiled; gloves, mask/eye protection, gown as splash/soiling risk dictates.個案間及脫手套後須手部衛生；手無明顯髒污可用酒精乾洗手；依噴濺或弄髒風險使用手套、口罩與護目、隔離衣。
  • Donning order: gown, mask/respirator, goggles/face shield, gloves. Removal: gloves first (outside is contaminated), then goggles, gown, mask; wash hands.穿戴順序：隔離衣、口罩/呼吸器、護目鏡/面罩、手套。脫除：先脫手套（外側受污染），再脫護目、隔離衣、口罩，最後洗手。
  • Clean blood spills with 1:10 bleach solution or approved disinfectant; contaminated linen in leak-proof bags; use safety needle devices.血液噴濺以 1:10 漂白水或核可消毒劑清潔；受污染被單置於防漏袋；使用安全針具。

7Transmission-Based Precautions傳播途徑別防護措施

  • Airborne: measles, varicella/disseminated zoster, pulmonary or laryngeal TB, COVID-19. Negative-pressure private room, door closed, 6-12 air exchanges/hr, N95 or higher; mask client for transport.空氣傳染：麻疹、水痘／散播性帶狀疱疹、肺或喉結核、COVID-19。負壓單人房、門關閉、每小時6-12次換氣、戴 N95 或更高等級；個案外出時戴外科口罩。
  • Droplet: influenza, pertussis, meningitis, mumps, rubella, epiglottitis, pharyngeal diphtheria, scarlet fever, strep pharyngitis, mycoplasma/meningococcal pneumonia, parvovirus B19, sepsis. Private room or cohort; mask within about 3 feet; mask on client for transport.飛沫傳染：流感、百日咳、腦膜炎、腮腺炎、德國麻疹、會厭炎、咽喉白喉、猩紅熱、鏈球菌咽炎、黴漿菌／腦膜炎雙球菌肺炎、細小病毒B19、敗血症。單人房或同病原體集中安置；約3英尺內戴口罩；外出時個案戴口罩。
  • Contact: multidrug-resistant organisms, C. difficile and enteric infections, RSV, wound and skin infections (impetigo, scabies, lice, herpes simplex, zoster), conjunctivitis. Private room/cohort; gloves and gown on entry; dedicated equipment.接觸傳染：多重抗藥菌、C. difficile 及腸道感染、RSV、傷口與皮膚感染（膿痂疹、疥瘡、頭蝨、單純疱疹、帶狀疱疹）、結膜炎。單人房或集中安置；進入時穿戴手套與隔離衣；專用器材。
  • For C. difficile: gloves and gown, soap and water on exit, no shared equipment, room cleaned with chlorine (bleach).C. difficile：穿手套與隔離衣，離開時用肥皂水洗手，不共用器材，以含氯（漂白水）清潔病室。
  • COVID-19 may need airborne plus droplet plus contact precautions with eye protection, per agency protocol.COVID-19 可能需同時採空氣、飛沫與接觸防護並加護目，依機構規定。

8Disasters and Biological Warfare Agents災難與生物戰劑

  • When notified of a disaster, activate the facility emergency response plan first; internal disasters occur in the facility, external ones (tornado, COVID surge) send victims to it.接獲災難通報時，首先啟動院內緊急應變計畫；內部災難發生在院內，外部災難（龍捲風、COVID 疫情高峰）則使傷病患湧入。
  • Anthrax (Bacillus anthracis): skin (painless black-centered ulcer), GI (undercooked meat), inhalation (flu-like then severe respiratory distress/shock); no person-to-person spread; ciprofloxacin, doxycycline or penicillin.炭疽（Bacillus anthracis）：皮膚型（無痛黑色焦痂潰瘍）、腸胃型（食用未煮熟肉類）、吸入型（先似流感，後出現嚴重呼吸窘迫與休克）；不會人傳人；用環丙沙星、多西環素或青黴素治療。
  • Smallpox: highly contagious by droplets/contaminated material; fever, back pain, then papules to pustules on face and limbs; vaccine available.天花：傳染力強，經飛沫或污染物傳播；發燒、背痛，之後出現丘疹進展為膿疱，先見於臉與四肢；有疫苗。
  • Botulism: toxin from Clostridium botulinum; descending paralysis, diplopia, ptosis, dysphagia; not person-to-person; antitoxin; may need ventilation; no vaccine.肉毒桿菌中毒：肉毒桿菌毒素；下行性麻痺、複視、眼瞼下垂、吞嚥困難；不會人傳人；用抗毒素；可能需呼吸器；無疫苗。
  • Plague (Yersinia pestis): bubonic most common, septicemic most deadly; pneumonic spreads person to person; streptomycin or gentamicin. Tularemia: ticks/deer flies, ulcer with lymphadenopathy.鼠疫（Yersinia pestis）：腺鼠疫最常見、敗血性最致命；肺鼠疫可人傳人；用鏈黴素或慶大黴素。兔熱病：蜱或鹿蠅傳播，皮膚潰瘍伴淋巴結腫大。
  • Viral hemorrhagic fevers (Ebola, Marburg, Lassa): fluids transmit; fever, headache, vomiting, unexplained bleeding; symptoms 2-21 days after exposure; supportive treatment only.病毒性出血熱（伊波拉、馬堡、拉薩）：經體液傳播；發燒、頭痛、嘔吐、不明出血；暴露後2-21天出現症狀；僅支持性治療。
  • Suspected Ebola (travel to endemic area within 21 days plus symptoms or temp 38 C / 100.4 F): isolate in private room with private bathroom, full PPE, limit entrants with a log, notify infection control and public health.疑似伊波拉（21天內曾至疫區並有症狀或體溫 38°C／100.4°F）：安置單人房與專用衛浴、完整 PPE、限制進入人員並記錄、通報感控與公共衛生單位。
  • Chemical agents: sarin (nerve gas, respiratory paralysis), phosgene (pulmonary edema), mustard gas (garlic odor, blisters). Ionizing radiation causes nausea, vomiting, leukopenia, hemorrhage.化學戰劑：沙林（神經毒氣，呼吸肌麻痺）、光氣（肺水腫）、芥子氣（蒜味、皮膚水疱）。游離輻射引起噁心、嘔吐、白血球減少與出血。

  • Only the top two side rails are used and bed stays lowest; side rails to prevent a sedated client from falling are generally not a restraint per policy.床欄僅使用上方兩側，且床維持最低位置；依規定，為防鎮靜個案跌落的床欄通常不算約束。
  • Alternatives for confusion: reorientation, sitters/family, room near station, clocks/night-light, familiar objects, toileting routine, remove unneeded tubes, review medications, look for causes (hypoxia, electrolytes).意識混亂的替代措施：重新定向、陪伴者或家屬、靠近護理站、時鐘與夜燈、熟悉物品、定時如廁、盡早移除不必要管路、檢視藥物、尋找原因（缺氧、電解質異常）。
  • Document reason, method, time applied, duration, response, release/assessments and ongoing need.記錄使用原因、方法、開始時間、持續時間、反應、解除與評估情形及持續需要性。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Poisoning中毒
  • Children (infants to young school-age) and older adults with poor vision or memory are most at risk of accidental poisoning.嬰幼兒至低年級學童，以及視力或記憶力差的老年人，是意外中毒的高風險族群。
  • Call Poison Control FIRST, before intervening; keep the number near the phone.先撥打毒物諮詢中心，再採取處置；電話號碼應放在電話旁。
  • Remove visible material from mouth/eyes/skin, identify substance and amount, save vomitus if asked, call an ambulance if directed to the ED.移除口、眼、皮膚上可見毒物，確認物質種類與量，依要求保留嘔吐物，若被指示送急診則叫救護車。
  • Never induce vomiting for lye, household cleaners, grease or petroleum products, or in an unconscious victim.吞食強鹼、家用清潔劑、油脂或石油產品，或個案昏迷時，絕不可催吐。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Health Care-Associated Infections and Standard Precautions醫療照護相關感染與標準防護措施
  • HAIs are acquired in a facility and not incubating on admission; common resistant organisms: MRSA, VRE, CRE, multidrug-resistant TB, plus C. difficile.醫療照護相關感染為入院時未存在或潛伏、在醫療機構內獲得；常見抗藥菌：MRSA、VRE、CRE、多重抗藥結核，另有困難梭狀芽孢桿菌（C. difficile）。
  • C. difficile spreads by hand contact, mostly after prolonged multiple antibiotics; alcohol rubs do not kill spores, so wash with soap and water.C. difficile 經手接觸傳播，多見於長期使用多種抗生素者；酒精乾洗手無法殺死孢子，須以肥皂與水洗手。
  • Standard precautions apply to every client: blood, all body fluids, secretions, excretions, nonintact skin, mucous membranes.標準防護措施適用於所有個案：血液、所有體液、分泌物、排泄物、不完整皮膚及黏膜。
  • Hand hygiene between clients and after glove removal; alcohol rub if hands not visibly soiled; gloves, mask/eye protection, gown as splash/soiling risk dictates.個案間及脫手套後須手部衛生；手無明顯髒污可用酒精乾洗手；依噴濺或弄髒風險使用手套、口罩與護目、隔離衣。
  • Donning order: gown, mask/respirator, goggles/face shield, gloves. Removal: gloves first (outside is contaminated), then goggles, gown, mask; wash hands.穿戴順序：隔離衣、口罩/呼吸器、護目鏡/面罩、手套。脫除：先脫手套（外側受污染），再脫護目、隔離衣、口罩，最後洗手。
  • Clean blood spills with 1:10 bleach solution or approved disinfectant; contaminated linen in leak-proof bags; use safety needle devices.血液噴濺以 1:10 漂白水或核可消毒劑清潔；受污染被單置於防漏袋；使用安全針具。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Transmission-Based Precautions傳播途徑別防護措施
  • Airborne: measles, varicella/disseminated zoster, pulmonary or laryngeal TB, COVID-19. Negative-pressure private room, door closed, 6-12 air exchanges/hr, N95 or higher; mask client for transport.空氣傳染：麻疹、水痘／散播性帶狀疱疹、肺或喉結核、COVID-19。負壓單人房、門關閉、每小時6-12次換氣、戴 N95 或更高等級；個案外出時戴外科口罩。
  • Droplet: influenza, pertussis, meningitis, mumps, rubella, epiglottitis, pharyngeal diphtheria, scarlet fever, strep pharyngitis, mycoplasma/meningococcal pneumonia, parvovirus B19, sepsis. Private room or cohort; mask within about 3 feet; mask on client for transport.飛沫傳染：流感、百日咳、腦膜炎、腮腺炎、德國麻疹、會厭炎、咽喉白喉、猩紅熱、鏈球菌咽炎、黴漿菌／腦膜炎雙球菌肺炎、細小病毒B19、敗血症。單人房或同病原體集中安置；約3英尺內戴口罩；外出時個案戴口罩。
  • Contact: multidrug-resistant organisms, C. difficile and enteric infections, RSV, wound and skin infections (impetigo, scabies, lice, herpes simplex, zoster), conjunctivitis. Private room/cohort; gloves and gown on entry; dedicated equipment.接觸傳染：多重抗藥菌、C. difficile 及腸道感染、RSV、傷口與皮膚感染（膿痂疹、疥瘡、頭蝨、單純疱疹、帶狀疱疹）、結膜炎。單人房或集中安置；進入時穿戴手套與隔離衣；專用器材。
  • For C. difficile: gloves and gown, soap and water on exit, no shared equipment, room cleaned with chlorine (bleach).C. difficile：穿手套與隔離衣，離開時用肥皂水洗手，不共用器材，以含氯（漂白水）清潔病室。
  • COVID-19 may need airborne plus droplet plus contact precautions with eye protection, per agency protocol.COVID-19 可能需同時採空氣、飛沫與接觸防護並加護目，依機構規定。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Disasters and Biological Warfare Agents災難與生物戰劑
  • When notified of a disaster, activate the facility emergency response plan first; internal disasters occur in the facility, external ones (tornado, COVID surge) send victims to it.接獲災難通報時，首先啟動院內緊急應變計畫；內部災難發生在院內，外部災難（龍捲風、COVID 疫情高峰）則使傷病患湧入。
  • Anthrax (Bacillus anthracis): skin (painless black-centered ulcer), GI (undercooked meat), inhalation (flu-like then severe respiratory distress/shock); no person-to-person spread; ciprofloxacin, doxycycline or penicillin.炭疽（Bacillus anthracis）：皮膚型（無痛黑色焦痂潰瘍）、腸胃型（食用未煮熟肉類）、吸入型（先似流感，後出現嚴重呼吸窘迫與休克）；不會人傳人；用環丙沙星、多西環素或青黴素治療。
  • Smallpox: highly contagious by droplets/contaminated material; fever, back pain, then papules to pustules on face and limbs; vaccine available.天花：傳染力強，經飛沫或污染物傳播；發燒、背痛，之後出現丘疹進展為膿疱，先見於臉與四肢；有疫苗。
  • Botulism: toxin from Clostridium botulinum; descending paralysis, diplopia, ptosis, dysphagia; not person-to-person; antitoxin; may need ventilation; no vaccine.肉毒桿菌中毒：肉毒桿菌毒素；下行性麻痺、複視、眼瞼下垂、吞嚥困難；不會人傳人；用抗毒素；可能需呼吸器；無疫苗。
  • Plague (Yersinia pestis): bubonic most common, septicemic most deadly; pneumonic spreads person to person; streptomycin or gentamicin. Tularemia: ticks/deer flies, ulcer with lymphadenopathy.鼠疫（Yersinia pestis）：腺鼠疫最常見、敗血性最致命；肺鼠疫可人傳人；用鏈黴素或慶大黴素。兔熱病：蜱或鹿蠅傳播，皮膚潰瘍伴淋巴結腫大。
  • Viral hemorrhagic fevers (Ebola, Marburg, Lassa): fluids transmit; fever, headache, vomiting, unexplained bleeding; symptoms 2-21 days after exposure; supportive treatment only.病毒性出血熱（伊波拉、馬堡、拉薩）：經體液傳播；發燒、頭痛、嘔吐、不明出血；暴露後2-21天出現症狀；僅支持性治療。
  • Suspected Ebola (travel to endemic area within 21 days plus symptoms or temp 38 C / 100.4 F): isolate in private room with private bathroom, full PPE, limit entrants with a log, notify infection control and public health.疑似伊波拉（21天內曾至疫區並有症狀或體溫 38°C／100.4°F）：安置單人房與專用衛浴、完整 PPE、限制進入人員並記錄、通報感控與公共衛生單位。
  • Chemical agents: sarin (nerve gas, respiratory paralysis), phosgene (pulmonary edema), mustard gas (garlic odor, blisters). Ionizing radiation causes nausea, vomiting, leukopenia, hemorrhage.化學戰劑：沙林（神經毒氣，呼吸肌麻痺）、光氣（肺水腫）、芥子氣（蒜味、皮膚水疱）。游離輻射引起噁心、嘔吐、白血球減少與出血。
