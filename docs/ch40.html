Chapter 40: Musculoskeletal Problems第 40 章：肌肉骨骼問題（兒科）

📖 Source: PDF p.554–563 · printed book p.498–506.e1來源：PDF 第 554–563 頁 · 書本印刷頁 498–506.e1

Pediatric musculoskeletal disorders: developmental dysplasia of the hip, clubfoot, scoliosis, juvenile idiopathic arthritis, Marfan syndrome, Legg-Calve-Perthes disease, fractures with traction and casts, and osteomyelitis.兒童肌肉骨骼疾病：發展性髖關節發育不良、內翻足、脊柱側彎、幼年特發性關節炎、馬凡氏症候群、Legg-Calve-Perthes病、骨折與牽引/石膏、骨髓炎。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Developmental dysplasia of the hip (DDH)發展性髖關節發育不良（DDH） 📖 PDF p.554 (book p.498), PDF p.555 (book p.499)

  • Femoral head seated improperly in the acetabulum; degrees: acetabular dysplasia (mildest), subluxation, dislocation.股骨頭未正確位於髖臼；程度：髖臼發育不良（最輕）、半脫位、脫位。
  • Neonate: ligament laxity; positive Ortolani (click as head relocates) and Barlow (head dislocates) tests.新生兒：韌帶鬆弛；Ortolani陽性（股骨頭復位時有彈響）、Barlow陽性（股骨頭脫出）。
  • Infant: shortened leg (Galeazzi/Allis), limited hip abduction, asymmetric gluteal folds.嬰兒：患側肢體縮短（Galeazzi/Allis徵象）、髖外展受限、臀紋不對稱。
  • Older child: shorter leg, positive Trendelenburg (pelvis drops on the normal side), prominent greater trochanter, waddling gait/lordosis if bilateral.較大兒童：患肢較短、Trendelenburg陽性（健側骨盆下垂）、大轉子突出、雙側者鴨步/腰椎前凸。
  • 0-6 months: Pavlik harness (flexion, abduction), worn 23 h/day, removed only for bathing, 6-12 weeks.0-6個月：Pavlik吊帶（屈曲、外展），每天配戴23小時，僅洗澡時取下，6-12週。
  • 6-18 months: traction then closed/open reduction and hip spica cast 6-12 weeks, then brace ~6 weeks; older child needs surgery.6-18個月：牽引後閉鎖/開放復位並打髖人字石膏6-12週，再用支架約6週；較大兒童需手術。

Congenital clubfoot先天性內翻足 📖 PDF p.555 (book p.499), PDF p.556 (book p.500)

  • Forefoot adduction, midfoot supination, hindfoot varus, ankle equinus; may be unilateral or bilateral.前足內收、中足旋後、後足內翻、踝馬蹄足；可單側或雙側。
  • Terms: varus = inward, valgus = outward, equinus = plantar flexion (toes lower), calcaneus = dorsiflexion.名詞：varus＝內翻、valgus＝外翻、equinus＝蹠屈（腳趾低於腳跟）、calcaneus＝背屈。
  • Goal: painless, stable plantigrade foot; follow-up to skeletal maturity.目標：無痛、穩定的蹠行足；追蹤至骨骼成熟。
  • Start treatment soon after birth: weekly manipulation and casting 8-12 weeks, then splint; surgery if alignment not achieved by 6-12 weeks of age.出生後盡早治療：每週手法矯正與石膏8-12週，之後夾板；6-12週齡未矯正則手術。
  • Monitor pain and toe neurovascular status; report impairment immediately.監測疼痛與腳趾神經血管狀態；有異常立即通報。

Idiopathic scoliosis特發性脊柱側彎 📖 PDF p.556 (book p.500)

  • Three-dimensional spine deformity with lateral curve and rotation; found in preadolescent growth spurt, so screen then.三度空間脊柱變形，含側彎與旋轉；於青春期前生長快速期發現，故需在此時篩檢。
  • Adam's forward-bend test shows rib/flank asymmetry; also uneven shoulders, hips, rib hump; X-ray confirms.Adam前彎測試可見肋骨/腰側不對稱；另有肩、髖不等高、肋骨隆起；X光確診。
  • Brace 16-23 h/day slows progression (not curative); check skin, keep clean/dry, no lotion/powder, soft clothing underneath, exercises, address body image.背架每日16-23小時，減緩進展（非治癒）；檢查皮膚、保持清潔乾燥、不擦乳液/粉、內穿柔軟衣物、運動、處理身體心像。
  • Spinal fusion postop: logroll, keep alignment, neurovascular checks, pulmonary hygiene, pain control, watch for incontinence/infection.脊椎融合術後：軸線翻身、維持排列、神經血管評估、肺部衛生、止痛、注意失禁/感染。
  • Superior mesenteric artery syndrome: vomiting and abdominal distention after fusion; notify the provider.腸繫膜上動脈症候群：融合術後嘔吐與腹脹；通知醫師。

Juvenile idiopathic arthritis (JIA)幼年特發性關節炎 📖 PDF p.556 (book p.500), PDF p.557 (book p.501)

  • Autoimmune joint inflammation; no cure; care preserves function and limits deformity via an interprofessional team.自體免疫性關節發炎；無法治癒；由跨專業團隊保存功能並減少變形。
  • Findings: stiff, swollen, warm, tender joints, morning stiffness and after inactivity; uveitis may cause blindness; no definitive test (ESR, WBC may be raised).表現：關節僵硬、腫脹、發熱、壓痛；晨僵與休息後僵硬；葡萄膜炎可致失明；無決定性檢驗（ESR、WBC可能升高）。
  • Meds: intra-articular steroids for few joints; short low-dose oral steroids; DMARDs and biologics (anti-TNF) second line.藥物：少數關節用關節內類固醇；短期低劑量口服類固醇；DMARDs與生物製劑（抗TNF）為第二線。
  • Care: ROM, heat/cold, splinting in neutral position, isometric exercises during flares, PT/OT, regular eye exams, support normal ADLs.照護：關節活動、冷熱敷、中立位夾板、發作期做等長運動、PT/OT、定期眼科檢查、鼓勵一般日常活動。

Marfan syndrome馬凡氏症候群 📖 PDF p.557 (book p.501)

  • Connective tissue disorder (fibrillin-1 gene) affecting skeletal, cardiovascular and ocular systems; no cure.結締組織疾病（fibrillin-1基因），影響骨骼、心血管與眼睛；無法治癒。
  • Tall thin build, long limbs and slender fingers, spinal curvature, vision and heart problems.高瘦體型、長肢細指、脊柱彎曲、視力與心臟問題。
  • Care: regular eye exams, monitor spine, heart-rate-slowing drugs to protect aorta, avoid competitive and contact sports, antibiotic prophylaxis before dental work, possible aortic root surgery.照護：定期眼科檢查、監測脊柱、減慢心率藥物保護主動脈、避免競技與接觸性運動、牙科處置前預防性抗生素、可能需主動脈根部手術。

Legg-Calve-Perthes diseaseLegg-Calve-Perthes病 📖 PDF p.557 (book p.501), PDF p.558 (book p.502)

  • Temporary loss of blood supply to the femoral head causes bone death and growth arrest.股骨頭血供暫時中斷，造成骨壞死與生長停止。
  • Signs: limp, hip/groin/thigh/knee pain or stiffness, limited ROM.徵象：跛行、髖/鼠蹊/大腿/膝痛或僵硬、關節活動受限。
  • Care: PT stretching, crutches to avoid weight bearing, bed rest/traction if severe, casting, night brace, hip surgery.照護：物理治療伸展、拐杖避免患髖承重、重症臥床/牽引、石膏、夜間支架、髖部手術。

Fractures, traction and casts骨折、牽引與石膏 📖 PDF p.558 (book p.502), PDF p.559 (book p.503)

  • Fractures in infancy are rare: investigate for child abuse and bone disease.嬰兒期骨折少見：須調查是否為兒虐或骨骼疾病。
  • Signs: pain, deformity, edema, bruising, spasm, loss of function, crepitus.徵象：疼痛、變形、水腫、瘀青、肌肉痙攣、功能喪失、骨摩擦音。
  • First aid: immobilize, check neurovascular status, cover open wound with sterile dressing, elevate, apply cold, transport.急救：固定、檢查神經血管、開放傷口以無菌敷料覆蓋、抬高、冰敷、送醫。
  • Reduction is closed (manual) or open (internal fixation); retention with traction or cast.復位分閉鎖（手法）與開放（內固定）；以牽引或石膏維持。
  • Traction: Russell, balanced suspension, 90-90; keep prescribed weights hanging freely, ropes in pulleys and unfrayed, neurovascular checks, pin care, prevent immobility complications, provide play.牽引：Russell、平衡懸吊、90-90牽引；依醫囑重量並使重物自由懸空、繩索在滑輪上且無磨損、神經血管評估、針道護理、預防不動併發症、提供遊戲。
  • Cast care: check for pressure/hot spots, petal rough edges, keep clean and dry, no objects inside, isometric exercise; signs of compartment syndrome (pain beyond expected, pallor, numbness, coolness, diminished pulse) = notify PHCP, bivalve cast, possible fasciotomy.石膏照護：檢查壓迫/熱點、邊緣修平、保持乾燥清潔、不塞異物、等長運動；骨筋膜室症候群徵象（疼痛過度、蒼白、麻木、冰冷、脈搏減弱）＝通知醫師、剖開石膏、可能需筋膜切開術。

Osteomyelitis骨髓炎 📖 PDF p.559 (book p.503)

  • Bone infection, usually Staphylococcus aureus.骨骼感染，常見金黃色葡萄球菌。
  • Signs: pain, fever, irritability, lethargy, warm tender limb, decreased ROM.徵象：疼痛、發燒、易怒、嗜睡、患肢溫熱壓痛、活動受限。
  • Care: comfort positioning, splint, non-weight bearing; start antibiotics after cultures; long-term IV (PICC); surgery if no response; pain control, PT/OT.照護：舒適擺位、夾板、免承重；採檢後開始抗生素；長期靜脈（PICC）；無效則手術；止痛、PT/OT。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Pavlik harness: worn 23 hours/day, removed only for bathing; maintains flexion and abduction.Pavlik吊帶：每天23小時，僅洗澡取下；維持屈曲與外展。 📖 PDF p.554 (book p.498)
  2. Ortolani = relocation click; Barlow = dislocation; asymmetrical gluteal folds and limited abduction in infants.Ortolani＝復位彈響；Barlow＝脫位；嬰兒臀紋不對稱與外展受限。 📖 PDF p.554 (book p.498)
  3. Positive Trendelenburg sign in older child with DDH.較大兒童DDH：Trendelenburg徵象陽性。 📖 PDF p.554 (book p.498)
  4. Clubfoot treatment begins immediately after birth with weekly casting for 8-12 weeks.內翻足出生後立即開始，每週石膏約8-12週。 📖 PDF p.556 (book p.500)
  5. Neurovascular compromise in a casted extremity: notify PHCP immediately; prepare for bivalving.石膏肢體神經血管受損：立即通報；準備剖開石膏。 📖 PDF p.556 (book p.500), PDF p.559 (book p.503)
  6. Scoliosis screening: Adam's forward-bend test during preadolescent growth spurt.脊柱側彎篩檢：青春期前生長期做Adam前彎測試。 📖 PDF p.556 (book p.500)
  7. Brace 16-23 h/day; no lotions or powders under brace; soft cloth underneath.背架每天16-23小時；架下不擦乳液/粉；內穿柔軟布料。 📖 PDF p.556 (book p.500)
  8. After spinal fusion: logroll; vomiting with abdominal distention = superior mesenteric artery syndrome; call provider.脊椎融合後：軸線翻身；嘔吐加腹脹＝腸繫膜上動脈症候群；通知醫師。 📖 PDF p.556 (book p.500)
  9. JIA: morning stiffness, uveitis risk (eye exams); no definitive test.JIA：晨僵、葡萄膜炎風險（需眼科檢查）；無決定性檢驗。 📖 PDF p.557 (book p.501)
  10. JIA flares: isometric exercises, neutral splinting, heat/cold.JIA發作期：等長運動、中立位夾板、冷熱敷。 📖 PDF p.557 (book p.501)
  11. Marfan: avoid contact/competitive sports; antibiotics before dental work; aortic and eye monitoring.馬凡氏症：避免接觸/競技運動；牙科前預防性抗生素；監測主動脈與眼睛。 📖 PDF p.557 (book p.501)
  12. Legg-Calve-Perthes: avascular necrosis of femoral head; crutches, avoid weight bearing.Legg-Calve-Perthes：股骨頭缺血性壞死；用拐杖、避免承重。 📖 PDF p.557 (book p.501), PDF p.558 (book p.502)
  13. Infant fracture: consider child abuse.嬰兒骨折：須考慮兒虐。 📖 PDF p.558 (book p.502)
  14. Traction: weights hang freely, ropes in pulleys; absent distal pulse = emergency, notify provider (do not release traction without order).牽引：重物自由懸空、繩在滑輪上；遠端脈搏消失＝緊急，通知醫師（無醫囑不可放鬆牽引）。 📖 PDF p.558 (book p.502)
  15. No lotion/powder, objects, or shoe polish on or in a cast; no heating pad for cold fingers.石膏上或內不使用乳液/粉、異物、鞋油；手指冰冷不可用熱敷墊。 📖 PDF p.559 (book p.503)
  16. Osteomyelitis: get cultures before antibiotics; S. aureus most common.骨髓炎：先採檢再給抗生素；以金黃色葡萄球菌最常見。 📖 PDF p.559 (book p.503)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Compartment syndrome '6 Ps': Pain, Pallor, Pulselessness, Paresthesia, Paralysis, Pressure (poikilothermia/coolness).骨筋膜室症候群「6P」：疼痛、蒼白、無脈、感覺異常、麻痺、壓力（及冰冷）。 📖 PDF p.559 (book p.503)
  • Ortolani = 'Out to In' (click as head moves into socket); Barlow = 'Out the back' (head pops out).Ortolani＝「入」（股骨頭回到髖臼有彈響）；Barlow＝「出」（股骨頭向後脫出）。 📖 PDF p.554 (book p.498)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. A child in temporary skin traction for a femur fracture has an absent dorsalis pedis pulse on the affected foot. What should the nurse do?股骨骨折暫時使用皮膚牽引的孩童，患足足背動脈消失。護理師應？

Absent pulse suggests compromised circulation/compartment syndrome, an emergency. Ice worsens perfusion; traction is not released without an order.脈搏消失提示循環受損/骨筋膜室症候群，為緊急狀況；冰敷會惡化灌流；無醫囑不可放鬆牽引。

📖 PDF p.558 (book p.502), PDF p.559 (book p.503)

2. Which finding in a 1-month-old suggests developmental dysplasia of the hip?1個月大嬰兒哪項發現提示DDH？

DDH shows limited abduction, asymmetric folds and apparent shortening (Galeazzi).DDH表現為外展受限、臀紋不對稱與肢體縮短（Galeazzi）。

3. Which statement by a parent of an infant in a Pavlik harness shows understanding?Pavlik吊帶嬰兒的家長哪項說法顯示理解？

The harness is worn nearly continuously for about 6-12 weeks.吊帶需幾乎持續配戴約6-12週。

4. A child who had spinal fusion for scoliosis develops vomiting and abdominal distention. What is the best action?脊椎融合術後孩童出現嘔吐與腹脹。最佳行動？

These signs suggest superior mesenteric artery syndrome mimicking obstruction/ileus.這些徵象提示腸繫膜上動脈症候群，類似腸阻塞/麻痺性腸阻塞。

6. During a painful flare of juvenile idiopathic arthritis, what exercise advice is best?JIA疼痛發作期，最佳運動建議？

Isometric exercise and neutral positioning maintain function during flares.發作期等長運動與中立位擺位維持功能。

7. Which statement shows a parent needs further teaching about plaster cast care?哪項家長說法顯示石膏照護需再衛教？

Powders/lotions near cast edges irritate skin and may fall inside.石膏邊緣用粉或乳液會刺激皮膚並可能掉入內部。

8. A 10-year-old with osteomyelitis is to start IV antibiotics. What must be done first?10歲骨髓炎孩童將開始靜脈抗生素。首先應完成？

Antibiotics are started after appropriate cultures are obtained.於取得適當培養後才開始抗生素。
