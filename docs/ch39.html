Pediatric neurological and cognitive disorders: cerebral palsy, head injury and increased ICP, hydrocephalus and shunts, meningitis, submersion injury, Reye's syndrome, seizures, neural tube defects, ADHD, autism spectrum disorder, and intellectual disability.兒童神經與認知疾病：腦性麻痺、頭部外傷與顱內壓上升、水腦與分流管、腦膜炎、溺水、Reye症候群、癲癇、神經管缺損、ADHD、自閉症類群與智能障礙。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Cerebral palsy腦性麻痺

  • Impaired movement, posture and muscle tone from a motor-system abnormality; spastic (upper motor neuron) is most common; athetoid, ataxic, mixed are less common.因運動系統異常導致動作、姿勢與肌張力受損；以痙攣型（上運動神經元）最常見；另有手足徐動型、運動失調型、混合型。
  • Signs: irritability, feeding problems, abnormal tone, delayed milestones, primitive reflexes (Moro, tonic neck) persisting past 6 months, opisthotonos, seizures.徵象：易怒、餵食困難、肌張力異常、發展遲緩、原始反射（Moro、頸張力）6個月後仍存在、角弓反張、癲癇。
  • Care: interprofessional team (PT, OT, speech), early intervention, assistive devices, safe environment/helmet, seizure precautions, upright after meals, muscle relaxants, parent support.照護：跨專業團隊（物理/職能/語言治療）、早期介入、輔具、安全環境/安全帽、癲癇防護、餐後保持直立、肌肉鬆弛劑、家長支持。
  • Communicate and play according to developmental, not chronological, age.溝通與遊戲依發展年齡而非實際年齡。

2Head injury and increased ICP頭部外傷與顱內壓上升

  • Closed head injury (blunt trauma, shaken baby) is more serious due to ICP rise in a closed vault; open injury involves fracture/penetration.閉鎖性頭傷（鈍傷、嬰兒搖晃症候群）因顱腔封閉易使ICP升高而更嚴重；開放性涉及骨折或穿透。
  • Level of consciousness is the earliest and most sensitive indicator of neurological change.意識程度是神經狀態變化最早、最敏感的指標。
  • Early ICP: irritability, high-pitched cry, bulging fontanel, Macewen sign, setting-sun eyes (infants); headache, vomiting, diplopia, seizures (children).早期ICP：嬰兒易怒、高頻哭聲、囟門膨出、Macewen徵象、日落眼；兒童頭痛、嘔吐、複視、癲癇。
  • Late ICP: marked drop in LOC, bradycardia, pupil changes, decorticate or decerebrate posturing, Cheyne-Stokes respirations, coma.晚期ICP：意識明顯下降、心搏過緩、瞳孔改變、去皮質/去大腦僵直、陳施氏呼吸、昏迷。
  • Immobilize neck if spinal injury suspected; once ruled out, HOB 15-30 degrees with head midline; minimize stimuli; withhold sedatives acutely.疑脊椎損傷先固定頸部；排除後床頭抬高15-30度、頭保持中線；減少刺激；急性期不給鎮靜劑。
  • Clear nose/ear drainage: test for glucose (positive = CSF leak, notify PHCP); no nasal suctioning with skull fracture.鼻/耳流出液測葡萄糖（陽性＝腦脊髓液漏，立即通報）；顱骨骨折者禁止經鼻抽吸。
  • Epidural hematoma: unequal, dilated nonreactive pupil is a neurosurgical emergency. Brainstem signs: irregular respirations, wide pulse/BP swings, sluggish or unequal pupils.硬膜外血腫：單側瞳孔放大且無反應為神經外科急症。腦幹受壓徵象：呼吸不規則、脈搏/血壓大幅波動、瞳孔遲鈍或不等大。
  • Osmotic diuretics or corticosteroids reduce edema; careful IV fluids avoid overhydration.渗透性利尿劑或類固醇減輕腦水腫；謹慎靜脈輸液避免過量。

3Hydrocephalus and shunts水腦與分流管

  • CSF absorption/production imbalance; communicating (absorption problem) vs noncommunicating (obstructive).腦脊髓液吸收/產生失衡；交通性（吸收問題）與非交通性（阻塞性）。
  • Infants: enlarging head, tense bulging non-pulsating fontanel, separated sutures, setting-sun eyes, frontal bossing, dilated scalp veins, irritability, poor feeding. Children: headache on waking, vomiting, ataxia, papilledema. Late: high shrill cry, seizures.嬰兒：頭圍增大、囟門緊繃膨出無搏動、縫合分離、日落眼、前額突出、頭皮靜脈擴張、易怒、拒食。兒童：晨起頭痛、嘔吐、運動失調、視乳突水腫。晚期：高尖哭聲、癲癇。
  • VP shunt drains CSF to peritoneum; VA shunt to right atrium; endoscopic third ventriculostomy is an alternative; revisions needed with growth.腦室腹腔分流（VP）將腦脊髓液引流至腹腔；腦室心房分流（VA）至右心房；內視鏡第三腦室造瘻為替代方式；成長後需修正。
  • Preop: I&O, small frequent feeds, reposition head often with egg-crate to prevent pressure sores.術前：I&O、少量多餐、經常變換頭部位置並用蛋盒型墊防壓瘡。
  • Postop: position on unoperated side, flat if prescribed to avoid rapid CSF drop, monitor head circumference, infection, ICP; teach shunt malfunction signs (infant: irritable, lethargic, poor feeding, shrill cry; toddler: headache, poor appetite; older: LOC change).術後：側臥於未手術側、依醫囑平躺以免腦脊髓液驟降、測頭圍、監測感染與ICP；教導分流管故障徵象（嬰兒：易怒、嗜睡、拒食、尖哭；幼兒：頭痛、食慾差；較大兒童：意識改變）。

4Meningitis腦膜炎

  • CNS infection (bacterial or viral); bacterial causes: H. influenzae b, S. pneumoniae, N. meningitidis (droplet spread, epidemic).中樞神經感染（細菌或病毒）；細菌性病原：b型流感嗜血桿菌、肺炎鏈球菌、腦膜炎雙球菌（飛沫傳播、可流行）。
  • Bacterial CSF: cloudy, high pressure, high WBC, high protein, low glucose (via lumbar puncture).細菌性腦脊髓液：混濁、壓力高、WBC高、蛋白高、葡萄糖低（腰椎穿刺）。
  • Signs: fever, headache, vomiting, nuchal rigidity, high shrill cry, bulging fontanel, positive Kernig and Brudzinski, petechial/purpuric rash (meningococcal).徵象：發燒、頭痛、嘔吐、頸部僵硬、高尖哭聲、囟門膨出、Kernig與Brudzinski陽性、瘀點/紫斑（腦膜炎雙球菌）。
  • Respiratory (droplet) isolation until 24 hours after antibiotics started; give antibiotics promptly after cultures/LP; watch for SIADH, seizures, hearing loss; prophylaxis for close contacts.呼吸（飛沫）隔離至抗生素開始後24小時；採檢/腰穿後立即給抗生素；注意SIADH、癲癇、聽力喪失；密切接觸者預防性投藥。
  • Pneumococcal conjugate vaccine from age 2 months helps prevent meningitis.自2個月起接種肺炎鏈球菌結合疫苗可預防腦膜炎。

5Submersion injury溺水

  • Hypoxia is the main problem; cerebral cells suffer irreversible damage after 4-6 minutes; aspiration and hypothermia add risk.缺氧為主要問題；腦細胞缺氧4-6分鐘後造成不可逆損傷；另有吸入與低體溫風險。
  • Prognosis good if submerged <5 min with responsiveness, reactive pupils, normal rhythm; poor if >10 min and no response to CPR within 25 min.浸水<5分鐘且有反應、瞳孔有反應、心律正常則預後佳；>10分鐘且CPR 25分鐘無反應預後極差。
  • Support ventilation/circulation; respiratory compromise and cerebral edema can appear 24 hours later; watch for aspiration pneumonia; teach water supervision.維持通氣與循環；24小時後仍可能出現呼吸惡化與腦水腫；注意吸入性肺炎；教導水域看護。

6Reye's syndromeReye症候群

  • Acute encephalopathy with cerebral edema and fatty liver after a viral illness (influenza, varicella) 4-7 days earlier.病毒感染（流感、水痘）後4-7天出現的急性腦病變，伴腦水腫與脂肪肝。
  • Avoid aspirin/salicylates in febrile or viral illness; use acetaminophen or ibuprofen.發燒或病毒感染時避免阿斯匹靈/水楊酸類；改用acetaminophen或ibuprofen。
  • Signs: fever, vomiting, lethargy, progressive neurological decline, elevated ammonia, coagulopathy.徵象：發燒、嘔吐、嗜睡、神經狀態進行性惡化、血氨升高、凝血異常。
  • Care: quiet dim environment, neuro checks, ICP monitoring, liver function, I&O, bleeding precautions.照護：安靜昏暗環境、神經評估、ICP監測、肝功能、I&O、出血監測。

7Seizure disorders癲癇

  • Excessive disorganized neuronal discharge; generalized (tonic-clonic, absence, myoclonic, atonic) or partial (simple, complex).神經元過度且無序放電；全身性（強直陣攣、失神、肌躍型、失張力）或局部性（單純、複雜）。
  • During seizure: protect airway, time it, ease to floor and side-lying, cushion head, loosen clothing, clear hazards, never restrain or put anything in the mouth, stay with child.發作時：保護呼吸道、計時、協助躺地並側臥、墊頭、鬆開衣物、清除危險物、勿約束、勿放任何物入口、陪伴在旁。
  • Bedside: suction and oxygen. Postictal: disoriented and sleepy.床邊備吸引與氧氣。發作後：定向力障礙、嗜睡。
  • Home: medical ID, swim with a companion, helmet for biking/skating, take medications on schedule.居家：配戴醫療識別、游泳需有同伴、騎車/溜冰戴安全帽、規律服藥。
p.547 · book 492

8Neural tube defects神經管缺損

  • Failure of neural tube closure; folic acid in childbearing years/pregnancy lowers risk; associated clubfoot, hip dislocation, hydrocephalus.神經管閉合不全；育齡期與孕期補充葉酸可降低風險；常合併內翻足、髖關節脫位、水腦。
  • Spina bifida occulta: vertebral arches fail to close, usually no deficits. Meningocele: meninges and CSF sac, cord not involved. Myelomeningocele: cord/nerve roots in sac, neurological deficits.隱性脊柱裂：椎弓未閉合，通常無神經缺損。脊髓膜膨出：腦膜與腦脊髓液囊，脊髓未受累。脊髓脊膜膨出：脊髓/神經根在囊內，有神經缺損。
  • Myelomeningocele findings: visible defect, flaccid leg paralysis, bladder/bowel dysfunction, hip deformity, hydrocephalus.脊髓脊膜膨出表現：可見缺損、下肢弛緩性麻痺、膀胱/腸道功能異常、髖關節變形、水腦。
  • Care: sterile moist nonadherent saline dressing on sac, aseptic technique, prone positioning (head turned for feeds), measure head circumference, antibiotics, watch for infection.照護：囊上覆蓋無菌濕潤不沾黏生理食鹽水敷料、無菌技術、俯臥（餵食時頭轉向一側）、測頭圍、抗生素、監測感染。
  • Long-term: bladder program/clean intermittent catheterization, antispasmodics, bowel program with fiber and fluids, latex allergy precautions.長期：膀胱計畫/間歇導尿、抗痙攣藥、高纖多水排便計畫、乳膠過敏防護。

9ADHD注意力不足過動症

  • Developmentally inappropriate inattention, overactivity and impulsivity; diagnosed from parent/teacher reports and tools.發展上不適當的注意力不足、過動與衝動；依家長/老師報告與評估工具診斷。
  • Signs: fidgeting, distractibility, not following instructions, shifting tasks, excessive talking, interrupting, risk taking.徵象：坐立不安、易分心、不遵從指示、常轉換任務、多話、打斷他人、冒險行為。
  • Care: behavioral therapy, consistent routine at home/school, safety, self-esteem; stimulants may cause appetite loss, weight loss, nervousness, tics, insomnia, raised BP.照護：行為治療、家庭/學校一致作息、安全、提升自尊；興奮劑可能造成食慾下降、體重減輕、緊張、抽動、失眠、血壓升高。
p.548 · book 493

10Autism spectrum and intellectual disability自閉症類群與智能障礙

  • ASD: impaired social interaction and communication with restricted, repetitive behaviors; usually noticed by age 3; M-CHAT-R screens toddlers.ASD：社交互動與溝通受損並有侷限重複行為；多於3歲前被察覺；M-CHAT-R用於幼兒篩檢。
  • Signs: little social play or comfort-seeking, echolalia/monotone, no gestures, fixation on objects, rigid routines, rocking, head banging, self-injury.徵象：少社交遊戲或尋求安慰、仿說/單調語調、缺乏手勢、執著物品、堅持常規、搖晃、撞頭、自傷。
  • Care: keep routines, use the child's communication method, avoid demands, prevent self-injury; safe environment is priority; refer and support parents.照護：維持常規、使用孩子的溝通方式、避免提出要求、預防自傷；安全環境為優先；轉介並支持家長。
  • Intellectual disability: subaverage intellect plus adaptive deficits; Down syndrome is trisomy 21. Care: multidisciplinary services, self-care skills, communication, play, safety, family support.智能障礙：智力低於平均並合併適應功能缺陷；唐氏症為第21對染色體三體。照護：多專業服務、生活自理訓練、溝通、遊戲、安全、家庭支持。

  • CNS infection (bacterial or viral); bacterial causes: H. influenzae b, S. pneumoniae, N. meningitidis (droplet spread, epidemic).中樞神經感染（細菌或病毒）；細菌性病原：b型流感嗜血桿菌、肺炎鏈球菌、腦膜炎雙球菌（飛沫傳播、可流行）。
  • Bacterial CSF: cloudy, high pressure, high WBC, high protein, low glucose (via lumbar puncture).細菌性腦脊髓液：混濁、壓力高、WBC高、蛋白高、葡萄糖低（腰椎穿刺）。
  • Signs: fever, headache, vomiting, nuchal rigidity, high shrill cry, bulging fontanel, positive Kernig and Brudzinski, petechial/purpuric rash (meningococcal).徵象：發燒、頭痛、嘔吐、頸部僵硬、高尖哭聲、囟門膨出、Kernig與Brudzinski陽性、瘀點/紫斑（腦膜炎雙球菌）。
  • Respiratory (droplet) isolation until 24 hours after antibiotics started; give antibiotics promptly after cultures/LP; watch for SIADH, seizures, hearing loss; prophylaxis for close contacts.呼吸（飛沫）隔離至抗生素開始後24小時；採檢/腰穿後立即給抗生素；注意SIADH、癲癇、聽力喪失；密切接觸者預防性投藥。
  • Pneumococcal conjugate vaccine from age 2 months helps prevent meningitis.自2個月起接種肺炎鏈球菌結合疫苗可預防腦膜炎。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Submersion injury溺水
  • Hypoxia is the main problem; cerebral cells suffer irreversible damage after 4-6 minutes; aspiration and hypothermia add risk.缺氧為主要問題；腦細胞缺氧4-6分鐘後造成不可逆損傷；另有吸入與低體溫風險。
  • Prognosis good if submerged <5 min with responsiveness, reactive pupils, normal rhythm; poor if >10 min and no response to CPR within 25 min.浸水<5分鐘且有反應、瞳孔有反應、心律正常則預後佳；>10分鐘且CPR 25分鐘無反應預後極差。
  • Support ventilation/circulation; respiratory compromise and cerebral edema can appear 24 hours later; watch for aspiration pneumonia; teach water supervision.維持通氣與循環；24小時後仍可能出現呼吸惡化與腦水腫；注意吸入性肺炎；教導水域看護。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Reye's syndromeReye症候群
  • Acute encephalopathy with cerebral edema and fatty liver after a viral illness (influenza, varicella) 4-7 days earlier.病毒感染（流感、水痘）後4-7天出現的急性腦病變，伴腦水腫與脂肪肝。
  • Avoid aspirin/salicylates in febrile or viral illness; use acetaminophen or ibuprofen.發燒或病毒感染時避免阿斯匹靈/水楊酸類；改用acetaminophen或ibuprofen。
  • Signs: fever, vomiting, lethargy, progressive neurological decline, elevated ammonia, coagulopathy.徵象：發燒、嘔吐、嗜睡、神經狀態進行性惡化、血氨升高、凝血異常。
  • Care: quiet dim environment, neuro checks, ICP monitoring, liver function, I&O, bleeding precautions.照護：安靜昏暗環境、神經評估、ICP監測、肝功能、I&O、出血監測。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Seizure disorders癲癇
  • Excessive disorganized neuronal discharge; generalized (tonic-clonic, absence, myoclonic, atonic) or partial (simple, complex).神經元過度且無序放電；全身性（強直陣攣、失神、肌躍型、失張力）或局部性（單純、複雜）。
  • During seizure: protect airway, time it, ease to floor and side-lying, cushion head, loosen clothing, clear hazards, never restrain or put anything in the mouth, stay with child.發作時：保護呼吸道、計時、協助躺地並側臥、墊頭、鬆開衣物、清除危險物、勿約束、勿放任何物入口、陪伴在旁。
  • Bedside: suction and oxygen. Postictal: disoriented and sleepy.床邊備吸引與氧氣。發作後：定向力障礙、嗜睡。
  • Home: medical ID, swim with a companion, helmet for biking/skating, take medications on schedule.居家：配戴醫療識別、游泳需有同伴、騎車/溜冰戴安全帽、規律服藥。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Neural tube defects神經管缺損
  • Failure of neural tube closure; folic acid in childbearing years/pregnancy lowers risk; associated clubfoot, hip dislocation, hydrocephalus.神經管閉合不全；育齡期與孕期補充葉酸可降低風險；常合併內翻足、髖關節脫位、水腦。
  • Spina bifida occulta: vertebral arches fail to close, usually no deficits. Meningocele: meninges and CSF sac, cord not involved. Myelomeningocele: cord/nerve roots in sac, neurological deficits.隱性脊柱裂：椎弓未閉合，通常無神經缺損。脊髓膜膨出：腦膜與腦脊髓液囊，脊髓未受累。脊髓脊膜膨出：脊髓/神經根在囊內，有神經缺損。
  • Myelomeningocele findings: visible defect, flaccid leg paralysis, bladder/bowel dysfunction, hip deformity, hydrocephalus.脊髓脊膜膨出表現：可見缺損、下肢弛緩性麻痺、膀胱/腸道功能異常、髖關節變形、水腦。
  • Care: sterile moist nonadherent saline dressing on sac, aseptic technique, prone positioning (head turned for feeds), measure head circumference, antibiotics, watch for infection.照護：囊上覆蓋無菌濕潤不沾黏生理食鹽水敷料、無菌技術、俯臥（餵食時頭轉向一側）、測頭圍、抗生素、監測感染。
  • Long-term: bladder program/clean intermittent catheterization, antispasmodics, bowel program with fiber and fluids, latex allergy precautions.長期：膀胱計畫/間歇導尿、抗痙攣藥、高纖多水排便計畫、乳膠過敏防護。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
ADHD注意力不足過動症
  • Developmentally inappropriate inattention, overactivity and impulsivity; diagnosed from parent/teacher reports and tools.發展上不適當的注意力不足、過動與衝動；依家長/老師報告與評估工具診斷。
  • Signs: fidgeting, distractibility, not following instructions, shifting tasks, excessive talking, interrupting, risk taking.徵象：坐立不安、易分心、不遵從指示、常轉換任務、多話、打斷他人、冒險行為。
  • Care: behavioral therapy, consistent routine at home/school, safety, self-esteem; stimulants may cause appetite loss, weight loss, nervousness, tics, insomnia, raised BP.照護：行為治療、家庭/學校一致作息、安全、提升自尊；興奮劑可能造成食慾下降、體重減輕、緊張、抽動、失眠、血壓升高。
Topic 10 · tap to flip主題 10 · 點擊翻面
Autism spectrum and intellectual disability自閉症類群與智能障礙
  • ASD: impaired social interaction and communication with restricted, repetitive behaviors; usually noticed by age 3; M-CHAT-R screens toddlers.ASD：社交互動與溝通受損並有侷限重複行為；多於3歲前被察覺；M-CHAT-R用於幼兒篩檢。
  • Signs: little social play or comfort-seeking, echolalia/monotone, no gestures, fixation on objects, rigid routines, rocking, head banging, self-injury.徵象：少社交遊戲或尋求安慰、仿說/單調語調、缺乏手勢、執著物品、堅持常規、搖晃、撞頭、自傷。
  • Care: keep routines, use the child's communication method, avoid demands, prevent self-injury; safe environment is priority; refer and support parents.照護：維持常規、使用孩子的溝通方式、避免提出要求、預防自傷；安全環境為優先；轉介並支持家長。
  • Intellectual disability: subaverage intellect plus adaptive deficits; Down syndrome is trisomy 21. Care: multidisciplinary services, self-care skills, communication, play, safety, family support.智能障礙：智力低於平均並合併適應功能缺陷；唐氏症為第21對染色體三體。照護：多專業服務、生活自理訓練、溝通、遊戲、安全、家庭支持。

Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Level of consciousness is the earliest indicator of neurological change.意識程度是神經變化最早的指標。
    p.543 · book 488
  2. Late ICP signs: bradycardia, decreased LOC, pupil changes, posturing, Cheyne-Stokes, coma.晚期ICP徵象：心搏過緩、意識下降、瞳孔改變、僵直姿勢、陳施氏呼吸、昏迷。
    p.543 · book 488
  3. Decorticate = flexion (arms flexed on chest); decerebrate = rigid extension and pronation (midbrain).去皮質＝屈曲（手臂彎向胸前）；去大腦＝僵硬伸直與旋前（中腦）。
    p.543 · book 488
  4. Head injury: HOB 15-30 degrees only after spinal injury ruled out; keep head midline; withhold sedatives.頭部外傷：排除脊椎損傷後才抬高床頭15-30度；頭保持中線；不給鎮靜劑。
    p.543 · book 488
  5. Basilar skull fracture: no nasal suctioning; glucose-positive nasal/ear drainage = CSF leak.顱底骨折：不可經鼻抽吸；鼻/耳液葡萄糖陽性＝腦脊髓液漏。
    p.544 · book 489
  6. One dilated nonreactive pupil may signal epidural hematoma, a neurosurgical emergency.單側瞳孔放大無反應可能為硬膜外血腫，屬神經外科急症。
    p.544 · book 489
  7. Shunt malfunction: infant irritable/lethargic/poor feeding/shrill cry; toddler headache and poor appetite; older child LOC change.分流管故障：嬰兒易怒/嗜睡/拒食/尖哭；幼兒頭痛與食慾差；較大兒童意識改變。
    p.545 · book 490
  8. Post-shunt: lie on unoperated side; raise HOB 15-30 degrees if ICP rises.分流術後：躺在未手術側；ICP上升時床頭抬高15-30度。
    p.545 · book 490
  9. Bacterial meningitis CSF: cloudy, high protein, low glucose, high WBC and pressure.細菌性腦膜炎腦脊髓液：混濁、蛋白高、葡萄糖低、WBC與壓力高。
    p.545 · book 490
  10. Kernig (cannot extend leg with thigh flexed) and Brudzinski signs indicate meningeal irritation.Kernig徵象（大腿屈曲時無法伸直小腿）與Brudzinski徵象代表腦膜刺激。
    p.546 · book 491
  11. Droplet isolation for meningitis until 24 hours after antibiotics; give antibiotics right after LP/cultures.腦膜炎飛沫隔離至抗生素後24小時；腰穿/採檢後立即給抗生素。
    p.546 · book 491
  12. No aspirin for children with viral illness, varicella or influenza (Reye's).病毒感染、水痘或流感的兒童不可使用阿斯匹靈（Reye症候群）。
    p.546 · book 491
  13. Seizure: never put anything in the mouth, never restrain; protect airway, side-lying, time it; bedside suction and oxygen.癲癇：口內不放任何物品、不約束；保護呼吸道、側臥、計時；床邊備吸引與氧氣。
    p.547 · book 492
  14. Myelomeningocele sac: sterile moist nonadherent saline dressing, prone position; high latex allergy risk.脊髓脊膜膨出囊：無菌濕潤不沾黏食鹽水敷料、俯臥；乳膠過敏風險高。
    p.548 · book 493
  15. Folic acid before and during pregnancy reduces neural tube defects.孕前與孕期補充葉酸可減少神經管缺損。
    p.547 · book 492
  16. Autism: maintain routine and safety; avoid placing demands; M-CHAT-R screening.自閉症：維持常規與安全；避免提出要求；使用M-CHAT-R篩檢。
    p.549 · book 494

1A 5-year-old fell from a bunk bed. Which is a LATE sign of increased intracranial pressure?5歲兒童從上下鋪跌落。何者為ICP上升的晚期徵象？
