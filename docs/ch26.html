Summary重點摘要Test-critical考試關鍵Mnemonics記憶口訣

This chapter covers normal physiologic recovery after birth (involution, lochia, breasts, urinary and GI changes, vital signs), routine postpartum assessment and teaching, common discomforts, emotional changes, and breast-feeding/chest-feeding. Know what is normal versus a red flag for hemorrhage or infection.本章說明產後正常生理恢復（子宮復舊、惡露、乳房、泌尿與腸胃變化、生命徵象）、產後評估與衛教、常見不適、情緒變化與哺乳。重點在分辨正常與出血或感染的警訊。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Postpartum period and involution產後期與子宮復舊

  • The postpartum period runs from birth to about 6 weeks.產後期從分娩後至約6週。
  • Uterus shrinks from about 2 lb (900 g) to 2 oz (57 g) in 6 weeks; fundus descends about 1 cm/day and is not palpable abdominally by day 10. Breast-/chest-feeding speeds involution through oxytocin.子宮於6週內由約2磅（900 g）縮至2盎司（57 g）；子宮底每日下降約1公分，第10天腹部已摸不到。哺乳透過催產素加速復舊。
  • Boggy (flaccid) fundus = atony, massage until firm; tender fundus suggests infection; a full bladder displaces the uterus upward and prevents contraction, so have client void before assessment.子宮軟而鬆弛＝子宮收縮不良，需按摩至變硬；子宮壓痛提示感染；膀胱脹滿會將子宮推高並妨礙收縮，評估前先請產婦排尿。
  • Afterpains are more common in multiparas, nursing parents, oxytocin use, and overdistended uterus.後陣痛較常見於經產婦、哺乳者、使用催產素及子宮過度膨脹者。

2Lochia惡露

  • Rubra: bright red, days 0-3. Serosa: brownish pink, days 4-10. Alba: white, days 11-14.紅惡露：鮮紅，第0至3天；漿液惡露：粉褐色，第4至10天；白惡露：白色，第11至14天。
  • Normal odor resembles menstrual flow; foul odor suggests infection. Large clots (>1 cm) or saturating a pad in 15 minutes (excessive) need provider notification.正常氣味似月經；惡臭提示感染。血塊大於1公分或15分鐘浸濕一片護墊（過量）需通知醫師。
  • Amount: scant <2.5 cm/hr, light <10 cm, moderate <15 cm, heavy saturated in 1 hr, excessive saturated in 15 min. Weigh pads for accuracy.量：極少（每小時<2.5 cm）、少（<10 cm）、中（<15 cm）、多（1小時浸濕）、過多（15分鐘浸濕）。可秤護墊重量以求準確。

3Other physiologic changes and vital signs其他生理變化與生命徵象

  • Menses return in 1-2 months if not nursing, 3-6 months if nursing; ovulation can precede menses, so lactation is not reliable contraception.未哺乳者1至2個月月經恢復，哺乳者約3至6個月；可能先排卵再來月經，因此哺乳不是可靠的避孕。
  • Colostrum 48-72 hr; milk comes in day 3; engorgement about day 4. Non-nursing: snug bra/binder, ice, avoid nipple stimulation; resolves in 24-36 hr.初乳持續48至72小時；第3天泌乳；約第4天脹奶。未哺乳者：穿緊身胸罩或束帶、冰敷、避免刺激乳頭；24至36小時緩解。
  • Diuresis begins within 12 hr; urinary retention is possible; bowel movement by day 2-3; hemorrhoids common.12小時內開始利尿；可能尿滯留；第2至3天排便；痔瘡常見。
  • Temp may reach 100.4 F (38 C) in first 24 hr from dehydration, higher suggests infection. Pulse may fall to 50 (normal); >100 suggests hemorrhage or infection. Falling BP suggests hypovolemia; rising respirations suggest PE, atony or hemorrhage.前24小時因脫水體溫可達100.4°F（38°C），更高提示感染。脈搏可降至50次/分（正常）；大於100提示出血或感染。血壓下降提示低血容量；呼吸明顯加快提示肺栓塞、子宮收縮不良或出血。
  • Orthostatic hypotension may occur in the first 8 hours: assist when getting up.產後前8小時可能有姿勢性低血壓：下床需有人協助。

4Postpartum assessment and teaching產後評估與衛教

  • Assess vital signs, pain, fundus (height, consistency, location after voiding), lochia, breasts, perineum (REEDA-type swelling/discoloration), incisions, bowel, I&O, calf for thrombophlebitis, bonding, emotions.評估生命徵象、疼痛、子宮底（排尿後測高度、硬度、位置）、惡露、乳房、會陰（腫脹或瘀青）、傷口、腸道、輸出入量、小腿血栓靜脈炎、親子依附與情緒。
  • Give Rho(D) immune globulin within 72 hours to Rh-negative clients with Rh-positive newborn; check rubella immunity and vaccinate if nonimmune, avoid conception 1-3 months.Rh陰性且新生兒為Rh陽性者，72小時內給予Rho(D)免疫球蛋白；確認德國麻疹免疫，無免疫者接種疫苗，之後1至3個月避免受孕。
  • Teach: no heavy lifting for 3 weeks, daily rest, delay intercourse until lochia stops, follow-up at 4-6 weeks, report chills, fever, increased lochia, or depressed mood.衛教：3週內避免提重物、每日休息、惡露停止後再行房、4至6週回診、有畏寒、發燒、惡露增多或情緒低落立即通報。

5Postpartum discomforts and emotional changes產後不適與情緒變化

  • Perineum/episiotomy: ice first 24 hours then sitz baths; perineal care after voiding; analgesic spray; avoid suppositories/enemas with lacerations.會陰／會陰切開：前24小時冰敷，之後坐浴；排尿後做會陰護理；使用止痛噴劑；有撕裂傷時避免塞劑與灌腸。
  • Constipation: 2000 mL fluid/day, fiber, ambulation, stool softener if prescribed.便秘：每日2000 mL水分、高纖飲食、走動、依醫囑用軟便劑。
  • Postpartum blues: tearfulness, mood swings, anxiety, fatigue, usually self-limited. Depression: guilt, loss of pleasure, less responsive to infant, suicidal thoughts. Psychosis: delusions, hallucinations, confusion, break with reality (emergency).產後憂鬱情緒：愛哭、情緒起伏、焦慮、疲倦，通常自行緩解。產後憂鬱症：罪惡感、失去興趣、對嬰兒反應少、自殺意念。產後精神病：妄想、幻覺、混亂、脫離現實（急症）。
  • All clients should be screened for depression in pregnancy and postpartum.所有孕產婦於孕期與產後均應篩檢憂鬱。

6Nutrition and breast-feeding/chest-feeding營養與哺乳

  • Nursing parents need 200-500 extra kcal/day, more fluids, and continued prenatal vitamins; avoid alcohol, unprescribed medications, gas-forming foods and caffeine.哺乳者每日增加200至500大卡、多喝水、持續服用孕期維生素；避免酒精、未經處方藥物、產氣食物與咖啡因。
  • Start feeding early; use LATCH (Latch, Audible swallowing, Type of nipple, Comfort, Hold).儘早開始哺乳；以LATCH評估（含乳、吞嚥聲、乳頭型態、舒適度、抱姿）。
  • Wash breasts with water only (no soap); supportive non-underwire bra day and night; air-dry cracked nipples 10-20 min, ensure areolar latch, rotate positions.乳房只用清水清潔（不用肥皂）；日夜穿支撐型無鋼圈胸罩；乳頭皸裂時風乾10至20分鐘、確保含住乳暈、輪換姿勢。
  • Engorgement: feed frequently, warm before, cold between feeds, massage. Estrogen-containing pills not recommended; progestin-only less likely to reduce milk.脹奶：頻繁哺乳、餵前熱敷、餵後冷敷、按摩。不建議含雌激素避孕藥；單純黃體素較不影響奶量。
  • Newborn stools are light yellow, seedy, watery, frequent.母乳寶寶糞便為淺黃色、顆粒狀、水樣、次數多。

1On palpation of a client 2 hours postpartum, the uterus is soft and boggy. What is the first action?產後2小時觸診發現子宮柔軟鬆弛。第一步處置為何？

Massage stimulates contraction; pushing on an atonic uterus risks inversion.按摩可促進子宮收縮；推壓鬆弛子宮有內翻風險。
