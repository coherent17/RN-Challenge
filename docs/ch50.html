Summary重點摘要Test-critical考試關鍵Mnemonics記憶口訣

This chapter covers drugs for acid-related disease (antacids, protectants, H2 blockers, PPIs, H. pylori regimens), motility (metoclopramide), liver, pancreas and bowel disease (lactulose, pancrelipase, IBD and IBS drugs), and symptom relief (antiemetics, laxatives, antidiarrheals). Know each class's action, signature adverse effect, timing relative to meals and interactions.本章涵蓋治療胃酸相關疾病的藥物（制酸劑、保護劑、H2 阻斷劑、質子幫浦抑制劑、H. pylori 療程）、腸胃蠕動藥（metoclopramide）、肝胰與腸道疾病用藥（lactulose、胰酵素、IBD 與 IBS 用藥），以及症狀緩解藥（止吐、瀉劑、止瀉）。需熟記各類別的作用、代表性不良反應、與餐的時間關係及交互作用。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Antacids制酸劑

  • Neutralize gastric acid, inactivate pepsin; do not coat the ulcer. Goal: gastric pH above 5. Used for PUD and GERD.中和胃酸、使胃蛋白酶失活；不會覆蓋潰瘍面。目標胃內 pH 高於 5。用於消化性潰瘍與胃食道逆流。
  • Chew tablets thoroughly then drink water or milk; shake liquids; take regularly (e.g., 1 and 3 hours after meals and at bedtime); separate from other drugs by 1 hour.錠劑嚼碎後搭配水或牛奶；液體先搖勻；規律服用（如餐後 1、3 小時與睡前）；與其他藥物間隔 1 小時。
  • Aluminum: constipation, can cause hypophosphatemia, contains sodium (caution in HTN/HF), reduces tetracycline, warfarin, digoxin.鋁製劑：便秘、可致低血磷、含鈉（高血壓/心衰竭慎用）、降低 tetracycline、warfarin、digoxin 吸收。
  • Magnesium: diarrhea; avoid in intestinal obstruction, appendicitis, undiagnosed abdominal pain; accumulates in renal impairment. Often combined with aluminum.鎂製劑：腹瀉；腸阻塞、闌尾炎、不明腹痛禁用；腎功能不全會蓄積。常與鋁製劑併用。
  • Calcium carbonate: acid rebound, belching, constipation, milk-alkali syndrome. Sodium bicarbonate: gas, systemic alkalosis in renal impairment, caution in HTN/HF.碳酸鈣：酸反彈、打嗝、便秘、乳鹼症候群。碳酸氫鈉：脹氣、腎功能不全時全身性鹼中毒、高血壓/心衰竭慎用。

2Gastric protectants (misoprostol, sucralfate)胃黏膜保護劑（misoprostol、sucralfate）

  • Misoprostol: prostaglandin that reduces acid and maintains mucosal blood flow; prevents NSAID/aspirin ulcers; take with meals; diarrhea and abdominal pain; contraindicated in pregnancy.Misoprostol：前列腺素類，降低胃酸並維持黏膜血流；預防 NSAID/阿斯匹靈潰瘍；隨餐服用；腹瀉與腹痛；懷孕禁用。
  • Sucralfate: forms a protective barrier on the ulcer; give on an empty stomach (1 hour before meals); constipation; separate at least 2 hours from warfarin, phenytoin, theophylline, digoxin and some antibiotics.Sucralfate：在潰瘍面形成保護屏障；空腹（餐前 1 小時）服用；便秘；與 warfarin、phenytoin、theophylline、digoxin 及部分抗生素至少間隔 2 小時。
p.744 · book 671

3H2-receptor antagonistsH2 受體拮抗劑

  • Cimetidine, famotidine, nizatidine suppress acid secretion; heal GERD, prevent stress ulcers and ulcer recurrence; use caution in renal/hepatic impairment and reduce dose in renal impairment.Cimetidine、famotidine、nizatidine 抑制胃酸分泌；治療胃食道逆流、預防壓力性潰瘍與潰瘍復發；腎/肝功能不全慎用，腎功能不全需減量。
  • Cimetidine: crosses blood-brain barrier (confusion, agitation, psychosis, especially elderly); inhibits liver enzymes, raising warfarin, phenytoin, theophylline, lidocaine levels (reduce doses).Cimetidine：可通過血腦障壁（意識混亂、躁動、精神症狀，尤其老年人）；抑制肝酵素使 warfarin、phenytoin、theophylline、lidocaine 濃度上升（需減量）。
  • IV cimetidine can cause hypotension and dysrhythmias. Antacids lower oral absorption: separate by at least 1 hour. Famotidine and nizatidine need not be taken with food.靜脈注射 cimetidine 可引起低血壓與心律不整。制酸劑降低口服吸收，間隔至少 1 小時。Famotidine 與 nizatidine 不需與食物併服。

4Proton pump inhibitors and H. pylori therapy質子幫浦抑制劑與 H. pylori 治療

  • PPIs (omeprazole, esomeprazole, lansoprazole, pantoprazole, rabeprazole, dexlansoprazole) strongly suppress acid; used for active ulcers, erosive esophagitis, hypersecretory states.PPI（omeprazole、esomeprazole、lansoprazole、pantoprazole、rabeprazole、dexlansoprazole）強力抑制胃酸；用於活動性潰瘍、糜爛性食道炎、胃酸過度分泌。
  • Common effects: headache, diarrhea, abdominal pain, nausea.常見副作用：頭痛、腹瀉、腹痛、噁心。
  • H. pylori: one antibiotic alone fails because of resistance; use triple therapy (PPI + amoxicillin + clarithromycin, or PPI + metronidazole + clarithromycin) or quadruple therapy (PPI + metronidazole + tetracycline + bismuth subsalicylate) if triple fails.H. pylori：單一抗生素易產生抗藥性而無效；使用三合一療法（PPI + amoxicillin + clarithromycin，或 PPI + metronidazole + clarithromycin），失敗時用四合一療法（PPI + metronidazole + tetracycline + 次水楊酸鉍）。

5Prokinetic agent (metoclopramide)促進腸胃蠕動藥（metoclopramide）

  • Increases upper GI motility and gastric emptying; used for GERD, paralytic ileus and as an antiemetic. Give 30 minutes before meals and at bedtime.增加上消化道蠕動與胃排空；用於胃食道逆流、麻痺性腸阻塞及止吐。餐前 30 分鐘與睡前服用。
  • Adverse effects: restlessness, drowsiness, dizziness, extrapyramidal/parkinsonian reactions (stop drug), insomnia, headache.不良反應：不安、嗜睡、頭暈、錐體外徑/類巴金森反應（需停藥）、失眠、頭痛。
  • Contraindicated in mechanical obstruction, perforation, GI hemorrhage, and pheochromocytoma (hypertensive crisis). Anticholinergics and opioids antagonize it; alcohol and sedatives add CNS depression.機械性阻塞、穿孔、消化道出血與嗜鉻細胞瘤（高血壓危象）禁用。抗膽鹼藥與鴉片類拮抗其作用；酒精與鎮靜劑增加中樞抑制。

6Bile acid sequestrants, lactulose and pancreatic enzymes膽酸結合劑、lactulose 與胰酵素

  • Cholestyramine, colestipol, colesevelam bind bile salts in the gut for hypercholesterolemia and itching from biliary obstruction; constipation, bloating, impaction; use caution in bowel obstruction; mix powders with juice, add fiber/stool softener.Cholestyramine、colestipol、colesevelam 在腸道結合膽鹽，用於高膽固醇與膽道阻塞搔癢；便秘、脹氣、糞便嵌塞；腸阻塞慎用；粉劑混果汁，並增加纖維/軟便劑。
  • Lactulose lowers ammonia in hepatic encephalopathy by promoting peristalsis and ammonia excretion; given orally or rectally; expect diarrhea (2-3 soft stools); monitor I&O and dehydration.Lactulose 促進腸蠕動與氨排出以降低肝腦病變的氨；可口服或灌腸；預期腹瀉（約 2-3 次軟便）；監測輸出入與脫水。
  • Pancrelipase replaces pancreatic enzymes (reduces fatty stools); take with all meals and snacks; calcium carbonate/magnesium hydroxide products interfere; cramps, nausea, diarrhea.Pancrelipase 補充胰酵素（減少脂肪便）；隨所有餐點與點心服用；含碳酸鈣/氫氧化鎂產品會干擾；絞痛、噁心、腹瀉。

7Inflammatory bowel disease and IBS drugs發炎性腸道疾病與腸躁症藥物

  • IBD (Crohn's, ulcerative colitis): 5-ASAs (sulfasalazine, mesalamine, balsalazide, olsalazine) - nausea, rash, arthralgia, blood disorders; corticosteroids (budesonide, prednisone); immunosuppressants (azathioprine, mercaptopurine, cyclosporine, tacrolimus); biologics (infliximab, adalimumab, certolizumab, natalizumab); antimicrobials (metronidazole, ciprofloxacin, rifaximin).IBD（克隆氏症、潰瘍性結腸炎）：5-ASA（sulfasalazine、mesalamine、balsalazide、olsalazine）-噁心、皮疹、關節痛、血液異常；類固醇（budesonide、prednisone）；免疫抑制劑（azathioprine、mercaptopurine、cyclosporine、tacrolimus）；生物製劑（infliximab、adalimumab、certolizumab、natalizumab）；抗微生物藥（metronidazole、ciprofloxacin、rifaximin）。
  • IBS-C: bulk-forming laxatives with full glass of water, lubiprostone (with food and water), linaclotide (30 minutes before breakfast).便秘型腸躁症：纖維性瀉劑搭配一大杯水、lubiprostone（與食物及水併服）、linaclotide（早餐前 30 分鐘）。
  • IBS-D: alosetron (serotonin antagonist) can cause constipation, impaction, obstruction, perforation, ischemic colitis; stop immediately if serious effects; strict risk program. Also antidiarrheals.腹瀉型腸躁症：alosetron（血清素拮抗劑）可致便秘、嵌塞、阻塞、穿孔、缺血性結腸炎；出現嚴重反應需立即停藥；需嚴格風險管控。亦可用止瀉藥。
p.747 · book 674p.748 · book 675

8Antiemetics止吐藥

  • Choice depends on cause. Classes: serotonin antagonists (ondansetron, granisetron, palonosetron), NK-1 antagonists (aprepitant, fosaprepitant, rolapitant), phenothiazines (prochlorperazine, promethazine, chlorpromazine), antihistamines (meclizine, dimenhydrinate, diphenhydramine, hydroxyzine), scopolamine patch, dexamethasone, lorazepam, dronabinol, metoclopramide, haloperidol.依原因選藥。類別：血清素拮抗劑（ondansetron、granisetron、palonosetron）、NK-1 拮抗劑（aprepitant、fosaprepitant、rolapitant）、吩噻嗪類（prochlorperazine、promethazine、chlorpromazine）、抗組織胺（meclizine、dimenhydrinate、diphenhydramine、hydroxyzine）、scopolamine 貼片、dexamethasone、lorazepam、dronabinol、metoclopramide、haloperidol。
  • Cause drowsiness: priority is protecting from injury. Monitor vital signs, I&O, dehydration and electrolytes; limit odors; clear liquids only while nauseated.會造成嗜睡：優先保護病人避免受傷。監測生命徵象、輸出入、脫水與電解質；減少氣味；噁心期間僅給清流質。

9Laxatives and antidiarrheals瀉劑與止瀉藥

  • Bulk-forming (psyllium, methylcellulose, polycarbophil): water into stool; need adequate fluids; contraindicated in obstruction. Stimulants (bisacodyl, senna). Emollient (docusate) softens stool and avoids straining. Osmotics (magnesium hydroxide/citrate, sodium phosphates, polyethylene glycol, lactulose).膨脹性（psyllium、methylcellulose、polycarbophil）：使糞便吸水；需足夠水分；阻塞時禁用。刺激性（bisacodyl、senna）。潤滑軟便劑（docusate）軟化糞便避免用力。滲透性（氫氧化鎂/檸檬酸鎂、磷酸鈉、聚乙二醇、lactulose）。
  • All laxatives: risk of dehydration and electrolyte imbalance; long-term use can cause dependency; increase fluids as directed.各類瀉劑：有脫水與電解質失衡風險；長期使用可能依賴；依醫囑增加水分。
  • Antidiarrheals: treat cause, replace fluids/electrolytes. Opioids (diphenoxylate with atropine, loperamide) slow motility but worsen diarrhea from poisons, infections or bacterial toxins. Also bismuth subsalicylate and anticholinergic antispasmodics (dicyclomine, glycopyrrolate).止瀉藥：治療原因並補充水分/電解質。鴉片類（diphenoxylate/atropine、loperamide）降低蠕動，但中毒、感染、細菌毒素引起的腹瀉會因此惡化。另有次水楊酸鉍與抗膽鹼解痙藥（dicyclomine、glycopyrrolate）。
p.748 · book 675

Inflammatory bowel disease and IBS drugs發炎性腸道疾病與腸躁症藥物
  • IBD (Crohn's, ulcerative colitis): 5-ASAs (sulfasalazine, mesalamine, balsalazide, olsalazine) - nausea, rash, arthralgia, blood disorders; corticosteroids (budesonide, prednisone); immunosuppressants (azathioprine, mercaptopurine, cyclosporine, tacrolimus); biologics (infliximab, adalimumab, certolizumab, natalizumab); antimicrobials (metronidazole, ciprofloxacin, rifaximin).IBD（克隆氏症、潰瘍性結腸炎）：5-ASA（sulfasalazine、mesalamine、balsalazide、olsalazine）-噁心、皮疹、關節痛、血液異常；類固醇（budesonide、prednisone）；免疫抑制劑（azathioprine、mercaptopurine、cyclosporine、tacrolimus）；生物製劑（infliximab、adalimumab、certolizumab、natalizumab）；抗微生物藥（metronidazole、ciprofloxacin、rifaximin）。
  • IBS-C: bulk-forming laxatives with full glass of water, lubiprostone (with food and water), linaclotide (30 minutes before breakfast).便秘型腸躁症：纖維性瀉劑搭配一大杯水、lubiprostone（與食物及水併服）、linaclotide（早餐前 30 分鐘）。
  • IBS-D: alosetron (serotonin antagonist) can cause constipation, impaction, obstruction, perforation, ischemic colitis; stop immediately if serious effects; strict risk program. Also antidiarrheals.腹瀉型腸躁症：alosetron（血清素拮抗劑）可致便秘、嵌塞、阻塞、穿孔、缺血性結腸炎；出現嚴重反應需立即停藥；需嚴格風險管控。亦可用止瀉藥。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Antiemetics止吐藥
  • Choice depends on cause. Classes: serotonin antagonists (ondansetron, granisetron, palonosetron), NK-1 antagonists (aprepitant, fosaprepitant, rolapitant), phenothiazines (prochlorperazine, promethazine, chlorpromazine), antihistamines (meclizine, dimenhydrinate, diphenhydramine, hydroxyzine), scopolamine patch, dexamethasone, lorazepam, dronabinol, metoclopramide, haloperidol.依原因選藥。類別：血清素拮抗劑（ondansetron、granisetron、palonosetron）、NK-1 拮抗劑（aprepitant、fosaprepitant、rolapitant）、吩噻嗪類（prochlorperazine、promethazine、chlorpromazine）、抗組織胺（meclizine、dimenhydrinate、diphenhydramine、hydroxyzine）、scopolamine 貼片、dexamethasone、lorazepam、dronabinol、metoclopramide、haloperidol。
  • Cause drowsiness: priority is protecting from injury. Monitor vital signs, I&O, dehydration and electrolytes; limit odors; clear liquids only while nauseated.會造成嗜睡：優先保護病人避免受傷。監測生命徵象、輸出入、脫水與電解質；減少氣味；噁心期間僅給清流質。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
Laxatives and antidiarrheals瀉劑與止瀉藥
  • Bulk-forming (psyllium, methylcellulose, polycarbophil): water into stool; need adequate fluids; contraindicated in obstruction. Stimulants (bisacodyl, senna). Emollient (docusate) softens stool and avoids straining. Osmotics (magnesium hydroxide/citrate, sodium phosphates, polyethylene glycol, lactulose).膨脹性（psyllium、methylcellulose、polycarbophil）：使糞便吸水；需足夠水分；阻塞時禁用。刺激性（bisacodyl、senna）。潤滑軟便劑（docusate）軟化糞便避免用力。滲透性（氫氧化鎂/檸檬酸鎂、磷酸鈉、聚乙二醇、lactulose）。
  • All laxatives: risk of dehydration and electrolyte imbalance; long-term use can cause dependency; increase fluids as directed.各類瀉劑：有脫水與電解質失衡風險；長期使用可能依賴；依醫囑增加水分。
  • Antidiarrheals: treat cause, replace fluids/electrolytes. Opioids (diphenoxylate with atropine, loperamide) slow motility but worsen diarrhea from poisons, infections or bacterial toxins. Also bismuth subsalicylate and anticholinergic antispasmodics (dicyclomine, glycopyrrolate).止瀉藥：治療原因並補充水分/電解質。鴉片類（diphenoxylate/atropine、loperamide）降低蠕動，但中毒、感染、細菌毒素引起的腹瀉會因此惡化。另有次水楊酸鉍與抗膽鹼解痙藥（dicyclomine、glycopyrrolate）。
