Adult integumentary content: skin anatomy and risk factors, wound healing, diagnostic tests, fungal/viral/bacterial skin infections (Candida, herpes zoster, MRSA, cellulitis), contact dermatitis, bites and stings, frostbite, skin cancer, psoriasis, acne, Stevens-Johnson syndrome and pressure injury.成人皮膚系統：皮膚解剖與危險因子、傷口癒合、診斷檢查、真菌/病毒/細菌性皮膚感染（念珠菌、帶狀疱疹、MRSA、蜂窩性組織炎）、接觸性皮膚炎、咬傷與螫傷、凍傷、皮膚癌、乾癬、痤瘡、史蒂文斯-強生症候群與壓力性損傷。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Skin anatomy, risks and psychosocial impact皮膚解剖、危險因子與心理社會影響

  • Skin is the largest organ: barrier against infection, sensation, thermoregulation, excretion, prevents water loss, vitamin D synthesis, nutrient storage.皮膚是最大器官：感染屏障、感覺、體溫調節、排泄、防止水分流失、合成維生素D、儲存養分。
  • Layers: epidermis, dermis, hypodermis; appendages: nails, hair, sebaceous and sweat glands; acidic pH 4.2-5.6 limits bacterial growth.層次：表皮、真皮、皮下組織；附屬物：指甲、毛髮、皮脂腺與汗腺；pH 4.2-5.6的酸性環境抑制細菌。
  • Risks: sun/tanning, radiation, pollutants, poor hygiene, harsh soaps, long-term glucocorticoids, nutritional deficiency, stress, infection, repeated injury, genetics, systemic illness.危險因子：日曬/日光浴、輻射、污染物、衛生不良、強效肥皂、長期糖皮質激素、營養不良、壓力、感染、反覆損傷、遺傳、全身性疾病。
  • Psychosocial effects: body-image change, low self-esteem, social isolation, pain, activity limits, cost.心理社會影響：身體心像改變、自尊低落、社交孤立、疼痛、活動受限、費用負擔。

2Wound healing傷口癒合

  • Inflammatory phase 3-5 days (edema, pain, redness, warmth); fibroblastic from day 4 for 2-4 weeks (granulation tissue, scar); maturation from ~3 weeks up to 1 year.發炎期3-5天（水腫、疼痛、紅、熱）；纖維母細胞期自第4天起2-4週（肉芽組織、疤痕）；成熟期約3週起可達1年。
  • First intention: edges approximated (sutures). Second: tissue loss fills in gradually. Third: delayed primary closure after being left open for cleaning.第一癒合：傷口緣對合（縫合）。第二癒合：組織缺損逐漸填補。第三癒合：先敞開清創後延遲縫合。
  • Drainage: serous (clear) and serosanguineous (pink) normal; sanguineous and hemorrhagic abnormal; purulent (yellow/gray/green) means infection.引流液：漿液性（清澈）與漿液血性（粉紅）屬正常；血性與出血為異常；膿性（黃/灰/綠）表示感染。

3Diagnostic tests診斷檢查

  • Skin biopsy (punch, excisional, shave): confirm consent, send specimen to pathology; keep dressing on at least 8 h; sutures out in 7-10 days; report drainage, redness.皮膚切片（打孔、切除、削切）：確認同意書、送病理；敷料至少保留8小時；7-10天拆線；通報滲液、紅腫。
  • Skin/wound culture: obtain before antibiotics; viral cultures go on ice; nasal swab screens exposure.皮膚/傷口培養：於抗生素前採檢；病毒培養置冰上；鼻腔拭子篩檢帶菌。
  • Wood's light (UV) identifies superficial infections: darken room, reassure that light is harmless, help adjust afterward.伍氏燈（紫外線）辨識表淺感染：暗室、說明無害、事後協助適應光線。
  • Diascopy: press glass slide on lesion to blanch erythema.玻片壓診：以玻片壓迫病灶使紅斑褪色以便觀察。

4Candida, herpes zoster and MRSA念珠菌、帶狀疱疹與MRSA

  • Candida: superficial yeast infection (skinfolds, perineum, axilla, mouth thrush); risk: immunosuppression, long antibiotics, diabetes, obesity; keep folds clean/dry, mouth care, antifungals.念珠菌：表淺酵母感染（皮膚皺褶、會陰、腋下、口腔鵝口瘡）；危險因子：免疫抑制、長期抗生素、糖尿病、肥胖；保持皺褶乾燥、口腔護理、抗黴菌藥。
  • Shingles: reactivated varicella-zoster in dorsal root ganglia; unilateral segmental vesicles with burning pain; Tzanck smear/viral culture; postherpetic neuralgia possible.帶狀疱疹：水痘帶狀疱疹病毒於背根神經節再活化；單側節段性水泡伴灼痛；Tzanck抹片/病毒培養；可能有疱疹後神經痛。
  • Shingles care: contact precautions while vesicles present; cool environment, air mattress/bed cradle, loose cotton clothes, astringent compresses; antivirals within 3 days; vaccine at age 50+.帶狀疱疹照護：水泡期接觸防護；環境涼爽、氣墊床/床架；寬鬆棉質衣物；收斂濕敷；3天內抗病毒藥；50歲以上接種疫苗。
  • Staff who never had chickenpox or varicella vaccine should not care for shingles clients; watch for Bell's palsy.未得過水痘或未接種水痘疫苗的工作人員不應照護帶狀疱疹病人；注意貝爾氏麻痺。
  • MRSA: folliculitis, furuncles; confirm with culture and sensitivity; standard + contact precautions, antibiotics, monitor for sepsis.MRSA：毛囊炎、癤；以培養與藥敏確認；標準+接觸防護、抗生素、監測敗血症。

5Cellulitis, erysipelas and plant dermatitis蜂窩性組織炎、丹毒與植物性皮膚炎

  • Erysipelas: acute superficial infection of upper skin layers (group A Strep). Cellulitis: dermis and hypodermis (Strep or S. aureus).丹毒：表層皮膚急性感染（A群鏈球菌）。蜂窩性組織炎：真皮與皮下組織（鏈球菌或金黃色葡萄球菌）。
  • Signs: pain, erythema, warmth, edema, fever; rest affected area, warm compresses, culture before antibiotics.徵象：疼痛、紅、熱、水腫、發燒；患部休息、溫敷、抗生素前先採培養。
  • Poison ivy/oak/sumac: urushiol causes papulovesicular itchy dermatitis; wash skin immediately (lather and rinse repeatedly), cool compresses, topical or oral steroids if severe.毒藤/毒橡樹/毒漆樹：urushiol引起丘疹水泡性癢疹；立即清洗皮膚（反覆起泡沖洗）、冷濕敷、嚴重者用外用或口服類固醇。

6Bites, stings and frostbite咬傷、螫傷與凍傷

  • Brown recluse: necrotic wound; ice early and intermittently up to 4 days. Black widow: neurotoxin; ice immediately. Tarantula: remove barbed hairs with sticky tape, irrigate eyes, elevate. Keep tetanus current; call Poison Control.棕色隱士蛛：壞死性傷口；早期間歇冰敷可達4天。黑寡婦蛛：神經毒；立即冰敷。狼蛛：用膠帶黏除倒刺毛、沖洗眼睛、抬高。維持破傷風疫苗；聯絡毒物中心。
  • Bark scorpion: neurotoxic, dangerous in children/elderly; go to ED, antivenom.樹皮蠍：神經毒，對兒童與老人危險；立即就醫，使用抗蛇毒血清。
  • Bee/wasp: scrape stinger off (no tweezers), ice; anaphylaxis needs epinephrine autoinjector IM, then ED; carry two injectors; watch for rebound anaphylaxis.蜜蜂/黃蜂：刮除螫針（勿用鑷子）、冰敷；過敏性休克用腎上腺素自動注射筆肌注後送急診；攜帶兩支；注意二次反應。
  • Snakebite: first move victim away and keep at rest, remove jewelry/constrictions, immobilize limb below heart, keep warm, no caffeine/alcohol, no cutting/sucking/ice, get to hospital for antivenom.蛇咬：先將傷者移離現場並休息、取下首飾/束縛物、肢體固定並低於心臟、保暖、不給咖啡因/酒精、不切開/吸毒/冰敷、儘速就醫給抗蛇毒血清。
  • Frostbite stages: 1st white plaque with hyperemia; 2nd clear blisters; 3rd hemorrhagic blisters/eschar; 4th full-thickness necrosis into muscle/bone.凍傷分級：1度白斑與周圍充血；2度透明水泡；3度出血性水泡/焦痂；4度全層壞死達肌肉/骨。
  • Frostbite care: rewarm rapidly in 40-42 C (104-107.6 F) water, handle gently, no rubbing or dry heat, analgesics, loose dressings, tetanus, monitor compartment syndrome.凍傷處理：以40-42 C（104-107.6 F）溫水快速復溫、動作輕柔、不可搓揉或乾熱、止痛、鬆散敷料、破傷風、監測骨筋膜室症候群。

7Actinic keratosis and skin cancer光化性角化症與皮膚癌

  • Actinic keratosis: rough scaly premalignant lesion from sun exposure, may progress to squamous cell carcinoma.光化性角化症：日曬造成的粗糙鱗屑性癌前病灶，可進展為鱗狀細胞癌。
  • Basal cell: pearly papule with central crater and waxy border, rarely metastasizes. Squamous cell: oozing, crusting lesion, can metastasize. Melanoma: irregular, multicolored, highly metastatic.基底細胞癌：珍珠狀丘疹、中央凹陷、蠟質邊緣，少轉移。鱗狀細胞癌：滲液結痂病灶，可轉移。黑色素瘤：不規則多色、高度轉移。
  • Risks: sun, tanning beds, light skin, age >60, outdoor work, radiation, chemicals; skin biopsy confirms.危險因子：日曬、日光浴床、淺膚色、>60歲、戶外工作、輻射、化學物質；以皮膚切片確診。
  • Teach monthly self-exam, sunscreen reapplication, protective clothing, avoid sun 10 a.m.-4 p.m.; treatment includes cryosurgery, curettage/electrodesiccation, excision.教導每月自我檢查、防曬乳補擦、防護衣物、避開上午10點至下午4點日曬；治療含冷凍、刮除電燒、切除。

8Psoriasis, acne, Stevens-Johnson, pressure injury乾癬、痤瘡、史蒂文斯-強生症候群、壓力性損傷

  • Psoriasis: chronic non-infectious, raised red plaques with silvery-white scales on scalp, knees, elbows, sacrum; nail pitting; Koebner phenomenon; psoriatic arthritis; improvement = fewer, thinner plaques and scales.乾癬：慢性非感染性，頭皮、膝、肘、薦部紅色隆起斑塊覆銀白色鱗屑；指甲點狀凹陷；Koebner現象；乾癬性關節炎；改善＝斑塊變少變薄、鱗屑減少。
  • Psoriasis teaching: don't scratch, keep skin lubricated, cotton clothing, avoid OTC drugs, stress reduction, watch for infection.乾癬衛教：勿抓、保持潤膚、棉質衣物、避免自行用成藥、減壓、注意感染。
  • Acne: comedones, papules, pustules, nodules; don't scrub or squeeze, use noncomedogenic water-based products, follow-up.痤瘡：粉刺、丘疹、膿皰、結節；勿用力搓洗或擠壓、使用不致粉刺的水性產品、定期追蹤。
  • Stevens-Johnson/TEN: drug-induced (sulfonamides, antiseizure drugs, NSAIDs); flu-like prodrome, mucosal/skin erythema, blisters; can involve eyes, airway; stop the causative drug immediately, supportive care.史蒂文斯-強生症候群/TEN：藥物誘發（磺胺類、抗癲癇藥、NSAIDs）；類流感前驅症狀、黏膜/皮膚紅斑水泡；可侵犯眼睛、呼吸道；立即停用致病藥物並支持性照護。
  • Stage 2 pressure injury: partial-thickness dermal loss, shallow open ulcer or blister; stage 1 intact, stage 3 full-thickness, stage 4 exposed bone/tendon/muscle.第2期壓力性損傷：真皮部分皮層缺損、淺開放潰瘍或水泡；第1期皮膚完整、第3期全層、第4期暴露骨/肌腱/肌肉。

  • Bee/wasp: scrape stinger off (no tweezers), ice; anaphylaxis needs epinephrine autoinjector IM, then ED; carry two injectors; watch for rebound anaphylaxis.蜜蜂/黃蜂：刮除螫針（勿用鑷子）、冰敷；過敏性休克用腎上腺素自動注射筆肌注後送急診；攜帶兩支；注意二次反應。
  • Snakebite: first move victim away and keep at rest, remove jewelry/constrictions, immobilize limb below heart, keep warm, no caffeine/alcohol, no cutting/sucking/ice, get to hospital for antivenom.蛇咬：先將傷者移離現場並休息、取下首飾/束縛物、肢體固定並低於心臟、保暖、不給咖啡因/酒精、不切開/吸毒/冰敷、儘速就醫給抗蛇毒血清。
  • Frostbite stages: 1st white plaque with hyperemia; 2nd clear blisters; 3rd hemorrhagic blisters/eschar; 4th full-thickness necrosis into muscle/bone.凍傷分級：1度白斑與周圍充血；2度透明水泡；3度出血性水泡/焦痂；4度全層壞死達肌肉/骨。
  • Frostbite care: rewarm rapidly in 40-42 C (104-107.6 F) water, handle gently, no rubbing or dry heat, analgesics, loose dressings, tetanus, monitor compartment syndrome.凍傷處理：以40-42 C（104-107.6 F）溫水快速復溫、動作輕柔、不可搓揉或乾熱、止痛、鬆散敷料、破傷風、監測骨筋膜室症候群。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Actinic keratosis and skin cancer光化性角化症與皮膚癌
  • Actinic keratosis: rough scaly premalignant lesion from sun exposure, may progress to squamous cell carcinoma.光化性角化症：日曬造成的粗糙鱗屑性癌前病灶，可進展為鱗狀細胞癌。
  • Basal cell: pearly papule with central crater and waxy border, rarely metastasizes. Squamous cell: oozing, crusting lesion, can metastasize. Melanoma: irregular, multicolored, highly metastatic.基底細胞癌：珍珠狀丘疹、中央凹陷、蠟質邊緣，少轉移。鱗狀細胞癌：滲液結痂病灶，可轉移。黑色素瘤：不規則多色、高度轉移。
  • Risks: sun, tanning beds, light skin, age >60, outdoor work, radiation, chemicals; skin biopsy confirms.危險因子：日曬、日光浴床、淺膚色、>60歲、戶外工作、輻射、化學物質；以皮膚切片確診。
  • Teach monthly self-exam, sunscreen reapplication, protective clothing, avoid sun 10 a.m.-4 p.m.; treatment includes cryosurgery, curettage/electrodesiccation, excision.教導每月自我檢查、防曬乳補擦、防護衣物、避開上午10點至下午4點日曬；治療含冷凍、刮除電燒、切除。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Psoriasis, acne, Stevens-Johnson, pressure injury乾癬、痤瘡、史蒂文斯-強生症候群、壓力性損傷
  • Psoriasis: chronic non-infectious, raised red plaques with silvery-white scales on scalp, knees, elbows, sacrum; nail pitting; Koebner phenomenon; psoriatic arthritis; improvement = fewer, thinner plaques and scales.乾癬：慢性非感染性，頭皮、膝、肘、薦部紅色隆起斑塊覆銀白色鱗屑；指甲點狀凹陷；Koebner現象；乾癬性關節炎；改善＝斑塊變少變薄、鱗屑減少。
  • Psoriasis teaching: don't scratch, keep skin lubricated, cotton clothing, avoid OTC drugs, stress reduction, watch for infection.乾癬衛教：勿抓、保持潤膚、棉質衣物、避免自行用成藥、減壓、注意感染。
  • Acne: comedones, papules, pustules, nodules; don't scrub or squeeze, use noncomedogenic water-based products, follow-up.痤瘡：粉刺、丘疹、膿皰、結節；勿用力搓洗或擠壓、使用不致粉刺的水性產品、定期追蹤。
  • Stevens-Johnson/TEN: drug-induced (sulfonamides, antiseizure drugs, NSAIDs); flu-like prodrome, mucosal/skin erythema, blisters; can involve eyes, airway; stop the causative drug immediately, supportive care.史蒂文斯-強生症候群/TEN：藥物誘發（磺胺類、抗癲癇藥、NSAIDs）；類流感前驅症狀、黏膜/皮膚紅斑水泡；可侵犯眼睛、呼吸道；立即停用致病藥物並支持性照護。
  • Stage 2 pressure injury: partial-thickness dermal loss, shallow open ulcer or blister; stage 1 intact, stage 3 full-thickness, stage 4 exposed bone/tendon/muscle.第2期壓力性損傷：真皮部分皮層缺損、淺開放潰瘍或水泡；第1期皮膚完整、第3期全層、第4期暴露骨/肌腱/肌肉。
