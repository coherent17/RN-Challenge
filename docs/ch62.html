Part XVI · Musculoskeletal Problems of the Adult Client成人肌肉骨骼問題

Chapter 62: Musculoskeletal Medications第 62 章：肌肉骨骼系統藥物

📖 Source: PDF p.1005–1013 · printed book p.910–917.e1來源：PDF 第 1005–1013 頁 · 書本印刷頁 910–917.e1

Drug classes for musculoskeletal conditions: skeletal muscle relaxants, antigout drugs, antiarthritic agents (DMARDs and biologics), osteoporosis drugs and analgesics for musculoskeletal pain. Know each class's action, signature toxicity, monitoring labs and patient teaching.肌肉骨骼疾病用藥類別：骨骼肌鬆弛劑、抗痛風藥、抗關節炎藥（DMARD與生物製劑）、骨質疏鬆藥物與肌肉骨骼疼痛止痛藥。需掌握各類別作用、特徵性毒性、監測檢驗與衛教。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Skeletal muscle relaxants骨骼肌鬆弛劑 📖 PDF p.1005 (book p.910), PDF p.1006 (book p.911)

  • Examples: baclofen, carisoprodol, chlorzoxazone, cyclobenzaprine, dantrolene, diazepam, metaxalone, methocarbamol, orphenadrine, tizanidine.例：baclofen、carisoprodol、chlorzoxazone、cyclobenzaprine、dantrolene、diazepam、metaxalone、methocarbamol、orphenadrine、tizanidine。
  • Action: central (depress spinal/brain neurons) or peripheral (dantrolene blocks calcium release in muscle); relieve spasm and spasticity.作用：中樞性（抑制脊髓/腦神經元）或周邊性（dantrolene阻斷肌肉鈣釋放）；緩解痙攣與痙直。
  • Adverse effects: drowsiness, dizziness, hypotension, weakness, dry mouth, GI upset, photosensitivity, hepatotoxicity. Avoid alcohol and other CNS depressants; no driving; take with food; monitor LFTs.不良反應：嗜睡、頭暈、低血壓、無力、口乾、腸胃不適、光敏感、肝毒性。避免酒精與其他中樞抑制劑；勿開車；隨餐服用；監測肝功能。
  • Baclofen: drowsiness, constipation, urinary retention; intrathecal pump - keep refill appointments, abrupt withdrawal can be life-threatening.Baclofen：嗜睡、便秘、尿滯留；脊髓腔幫浦－按時回診補藥，突然停藥可致命。
  • Cyclobenzaprine: contraindicated within 14 days of MAOIs and in cardiac disorders; anticholinergic (caution with urinary retention, angle-closure glaucoma); short-term use 2-3 weeks. Orphenadrine has similar anticholinergic effects.Cyclobenzaprine：MAO抑制劑14天內及心臟疾病者禁用；有抗膽鹼作用（尿滯留、閉角型青光眼慎用）；短期使用2-3週。Orphenadrine有類似抗膽鹼作用。
  • Dantrolene: most serious effect is liver damage (check LFTs before and during), GI bleeding, photosensitivity; report rash, tarry stools, jaundice.Dantrolene：最嚴重為肝損傷（治療前後檢查肝功能）、消化道出血、光敏感；皮疹、黑便、黃疸需通報。
  • Methocarbamol: IV form can cause hypotension, bradycardia, seizures if rapid, extravasation injury; urine may turn brown, black or green. Chlorzoxazone: urine orange/red, hepatotoxic. Diazepam: sedation. Tizanidine and metaxalone: liver damage.Methocarbamol：靜脈過快可致低血壓、心搏過緩、癲癇，注意外滲；尿液可能變棕、黑或綠。Chlorzoxazone：尿液橘/紅、肝毒性。Diazepam：鎮靜。Tizanidine與metaxalone：肝損傷。

Antigout medications抗痛風藥 📖 PDF p.1006 (book p.911), PDF p.1007 (book p.912)

  • Allopurinol and febuxostat reduce uric acid production; probenecid increases uric acid excretion (uricosuric); colchicine, NSAIDs and glucocorticoids treat acute attacks.Allopurinol與febuxostat減少尿酸生成；probenecid促進尿酸排泄（排尿酸藥）；colchicine、NSAID與類固醇治療急性發作。
  • Adverse effects: headache, GI upset, bone marrow depression, rash, flushing, uric acid stones, sore gums, metallic taste.不良反應：頭痛、腸胃不適、骨髓抑制、皮疹、潮紅、尿酸結石、牙齦痛、金屬味。
  • Teaching: fluids 2000-3000 mL/day, avoid alcohol, caffeine and high-purine foods (organ meats, sardines, salmon, scallops, gravy); take with food; no aspirin (raises uric acid).衛教：水分2000-3000 mL/日、避免酒精、咖啡因與高普林食物（內臟、沙丁魚、鮭魚、干貝、肉汁）；隨餐服用；避免aspirin（升高尿酸）。
  • Allopurinol: raises warfarin and oral hypoglycemic effect; avoid large vitamin C doses (stones); rare hypersensitivity (rash, fever, eosinophilia, liver/kidney changes) - withhold and report; sun protection and annual eye exam.Allopurinol：增強warfarin與口服降血糖藥效果；避免大量維生素C（結石）；罕見過敏症候群（皮疹、發燒、嗜酸性球增多、肝腎異常）－停藥並通報；防曬並每年眼科檢查。
  • Colchicine: caution in older, debilitated, cardiac, renal or GI disease; if nausea, vomiting, diarrhea or abdominal pain develop, withhold and notify PHCP.Colchicine：老年、體弱、心、腎或胃腸疾病者慎用；出現噁心、嘔吐、腹瀉或腹痛時停藥並通報。
  • Monitor serum uric acid, I&O, CBC, renal and liver function.監測血清尿酸、輸出入量、CBC、腎與肝功能。

Antiarthritic medications (DMARDs and biologics)抗關節炎藥（DMARD與生物製劑） 📖 PDF p.1007 (book p.912), PDF p.1008 (book p.913), PDF p.1009 (book p.914)

  • RA therapy: NSAIDs, glucocorticoids, conventional DMARDs and biologic DMARDs; early aggressive treatment preserves joints.類風濕性關節炎治療：NSAID、類固醇、傳統DMARD與生物性DMARD；早期積極治療可保護關節。
  • Conventional DMARDs: methotrexate, leflunomide, hydroxychloroquine, azathioprine, cyclosporine, sulfasalazine, penicillamine; risks: infection, neutropenia, low platelets, liver damage.傳統DMARD：methotrexate、leflunomide、hydroxychloroquine、azathioprine、cyclosporine、sulfasalazine、penicillamine；風險：感染、嗜中性球低下、血小板低下、肝損傷。
  • Biologics: abatacept, adalimumab, anakinra, etanercept, infliximab, rituximab (tofacitinib is a JAK inhibitor): injection-site reactions, serious infection/TB, malignancy risk.生物製劑：abatacept、adalimumab、anakinra、etanercept、infliximab、rituximab（tofacitinib為JAK抑制劑）：注射部位反應、嚴重感染/結核、惡性腫瘤風險。
  • Drug-specific: methotrexate - hepatic fibrosis, marrow suppression, GI ulcers, pneumonitis; hydroxychloroquine - retinal damage (report vision changes); cyclosporine - nephrotoxicity; azathioprine - hepatitis, blood dyscrasias; leflunomide - hepatotoxic, reversible hair loss; etanercept - heart failure risk, demyelination; adalimumab - neurological injury signs; infliximab - infusion reactions and hepatotoxicity.藥物特點：methotrexate－肝纖維化、骨髓抑制、胃腸潰瘍、肺炎；hydroxychloroquine－視網膜損傷（視覺改變需通報）；cyclosporine－腎毒性；azathioprine－肝炎、血液惡病質；leflunomide－肝毒性、可逆性落髮；etanercept－心衰竭風險、去髓鞘；adalimumab－神經損傷徵象；infliximab－輸注反應與肝毒性。
  • Gold salts: toxicity = pruritus, rash, metallic taste, stomatitis, diarrhea; antidote dimercaprol.金鹽：毒性＝搔癢、皮疹、金屬味、口腔炎、腹瀉；解毒劑dimercaprol。
  • Nursing: baseline and ongoing WBC, neutrophil, platelet counts; teach infection signs; no live vaccines without PHCP approval; tofacitinib contraindicated with active infection, recent live vaccine, hepatic impairment, lactation.護理：基礎與持續監測WBC、嗜中性球、血小板；教導感染徵象；未經醫師同意勿接種活性疫苗；tofacitinib於活動性感染、近期活性疫苗、肝功能不全、哺乳期禁用。

Osteoporosis medications骨質疏鬆藥物 📖 PDF p.1009 (book p.914), PDF p.1010 (book p.915)

  • Calcium and vitamin D support bone; antiresorptives: bisphosphonates, raloxifene, calcitonin, denosumab; anabolic: teriparatide, abaloparatide, romosozumab.鈣與維生素D支持骨骼；抗骨吸收：雙磷酸鹽、raloxifene、calcitonin、denosumab；促進骨形成：teriparatide、abaloparatide、romosozumab。
  • Bisphosphonates (alendronate, risedronate, ibandronate, pamidronate, zoledronic acid): morning on empty stomach with a full glass of water, stay upright 30 min (60 min ibandronate); risk esophagitis, muscle pain, ocular problems; contraindicated with esophageal disorders or inability to stay upright.雙磷酸鹽（alendronate、risedronate、ibandronate、pamidronate、zoledronic acid）：早晨空腹以一整杯水服用、保持直立30分鐘（ibandronate為60分鐘）；風險為食道炎、肌肉痛、眼部問題；食道疾病或無法直立者禁用。
  • Raloxifene: avoid with history of venous thromboembolism; stop 72 h before prolonged immobilization; avoid long periods of restricted activity such as travel.Raloxifene：有靜脈血栓病史者避免；長時間不動前72小時停藥；避免旅途等長期不動。
  • Calcitonin-salmon: inhibits osteoclasts; alternate nostrils for nasal form; monitor for hypocalcemia.Calcitonin-salmon：抑制蝕骨細胞；鼻噴劑輪流使用左右鼻孔；監測低血鈣。
  • Teriparatide: PTH analog stimulating osteoblasts, for high fracture risk, associated with bone cancer concern; abaloparatide: contraindicated with hypercalcemia/hyperparathyroidism, watch orthostatic hypotension within 4 h; romosozumab: MI and stroke risk; denosumab: monoclonal antibody.Teriparatide：PTH類似物刺激造骨細胞，用於高骨折風險者，與骨癌疑慮相關；abaloparatide：高血鈣/副甲狀腺機能亢進者禁用，4小時內注意姿位性低血壓；romosozumab：心肌梗塞與中風風險；denosumab：單株抗體。

Medications for musculoskeletal pain肌肉骨骼疼痛用藥 📖 PDF p.1010 (book p.915)

  • First-line: NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen, diclofenac, meloxicam, indomethacin, celecoxib) and acetaminophen.第一線：NSAID（ibuprofen、naproxen、diclofenac、meloxicam、indomethacin、celecoxib）與acetaminophen。
  • Adjuncts: skeletal muscle relaxants, antidepressants, opioids as needed (see chapters 60 and 69).輔助：骨骼肌鬆弛劑、抗憂鬱藥、必要時鴉片類（見第60、69章）。
  • NSAID cautions: GI bleeding, renal impairment, take with food; acetaminophen: liver toxicity above 4000 mg/day.NSAID注意：胃腸出血、腎功能受損、隨餐服用；acetaminophen：每日超過4000 mg有肝毒性。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Baclofen intrathecal pump: missed refill causes abrupt withdrawal that can be life-threatening.Baclofen脊髓腔幫浦：未回診補藥導致突然停藥，可能危及生命。 📖 PDF p.1005 (book p.910)
  2. Cyclobenzaprine: contraindicated with MAOIs (within 14 days) and in cardiac disease; anticholinergic - caution in glaucoma and urinary retention; use 2-3 weeks only.Cyclobenzaprine：禁與MAO抑制劑（14天內）併用、心臟疾病者禁用；抗膽鹼－青光眼與尿滯留慎用；僅用2-3週。 📖 PDF p.1006 (book p.911), PDF p.1012 (book p.917)
  3. Dantrolene: hepatotoxicity - monitor liver function tests before and during therapy.Dantrolene：肝毒性－治療前後監測肝功能。 📖 PDF p.1006 (book p.911), PDF p.1012 (book p.917)
  4. IV methocarbamol: hypotension and bradycardia; give slowly; watch for extravasation.靜脈methocarbamol：低血壓與心搏過緩；緩慢給予；注意外滲。 📖 PDF p.1006 (book p.911), PDF p.1012 (book p.917)
  5. All muscle relaxants: avoid alcohol/CNS depressants and driving; fall and safety risk.所有肌肉鬆弛劑：避免酒精/中樞抑制劑與開車；跌倒與安全風險。 📖 PDF p.1005 (book p.910), PDF p.1006 (book p.911)
  6. Gout drugs: fluids 2000-3000 mL/day, low-purine diet, no aspirin; allopurinol - stop and report rash or fever.抗痛風藥：水分2000-3000 mL/日、低普林飲食、避免aspirin；allopurinol－出現皮疹或發燒須停藥並通報。 📖 PDF p.1007 (book p.912)
  7. Colchicine: stop and notify PHCP for nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain; caution in renal, cardiac, GI disease.Colchicine：噁心、嘔吐、腹瀉、腹痛時停藥並通報；腎、心、胃腸疾病者慎用。 📖 PDF p.1007 (book p.912)
  8. Allopurinol: avoid large vitamin C doses; potentiates warfarin.Allopurinol：避免大量維生素C；增強warfarin效果。 📖 PDF p.1007 (book p.912)
  9. DMARDs/biologics: monitor CBC (neutrophils, WBC, platelets), infection risk, TB, no live vaccines.DMARD/生物製劑：監測CBC（嗜中性球、WBC、血小板）、感染風險、結核、避免活性疫苗。 📖 PDF p.1007 (book p.912), PDF p.1008 (book p.913)
  10. Hydroxychloroquine: retinal damage; methotrexate: hepatic fibrosis, marrow suppression; cyclosporine: nephrotoxicity; gold toxicity antidote dimercaprol.Hydroxychloroquine：視網膜損傷；methotrexate：肝纖維化、骨髓抑制；cyclosporine：腎毒性；金中毒解毒劑dimercaprol。 📖 PDF p.1007 (book p.912), PDF p.1008 (book p.913)
  11. Bisphosphonates: morning, empty stomach, full glass of water, upright at least 30 min (60 min ibandronate); contraindicated with esophageal disorders.雙磷酸鹽：早晨空腹、一整杯水、至少直立30分鐘（ibandronate為60分鐘）；食道疾病者禁用。 📖 PDF p.1009 (book p.914)
  12. Raloxifene: stop 72 h before prolonged immobilization; contraindicated with history of venous thrombosis.Raloxifene：長期不動前72小時停藥；有靜脈血栓病史者禁用。 📖 PDF p.1009 (book p.914)
  13. Teriparatide and abaloparatide: anabolic bone agents; abaloparatide - orthostatic hypotension within 4 h, contraindicated in hypercalcemia.Teriparatide與abaloparatide：促骨形成；abaloparatide－4小時內姿位性低血壓、高血鈣者禁用。 📖 PDF p.1009 (book p.914), PDF p.1010 (book p.915)
  14. Acetaminophen and NSAIDs are first-line for musculoskeletal pain.Acetaminophen與NSAID為肌肉骨骼疼痛第一線用藥。 📖 PDF p.1010 (book p.915)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Bisphosphonate rule: 'Up, Water, Empty' - upright 30 min, full glass of water, empty stomach in the morning.雙磷酸鹽口訣：直立、一整杯水、空腹（早晨）。 📖 PDF p.1009 (book p.914)
  • Allopurinol 'blocks production', probenecid 'pushes it out', colchicine 'calms the attack'.Allopurinol＝阻止生成；probenecid＝促進排出；colchicine＝緩解發作。 📖 PDF p.1006 (book p.911), PDF p.1007 (book p.912)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. A client is prescribed alendronate. Which instruction should the nurse give?病人被開立alendronate。護理師應給予何種指導？

Upright positioning and water reduce esophagitis and aid absorption.直立與足量水可降低食道炎並助吸收。

2. A client on allopurinol should be taught to:服用allopurinol的病人應被教導：

Fluids prevent uric acid stones; lip swelling signals hypersensitivity.水分可預防尿酸結石；口唇腫脹提示過敏。

3. A client with gout takes colchicine and develops nausea, vomiting and diarrhea. What should the nurse do?痛風病人服用colchicine後出現噁心、嘔吐、腹瀉。護理師應如何處置？

GI symptoms signal toxicity; hold and report.腸胃症狀提示毒性；停藥並通報。

4. Which laboratory test is most important for a client receiving dantrolene?接受dantrolene的病人最重要的檢驗為何？

Dose-related hepatotoxicity is the most serious adverse effect.劑量相關肝毒性為最嚴重不良反應。

5. The client prescribed cyclobenzaprine has which condition requiring PHCP notification?病人被開立cyclobenzaprine，下列何種疾病需通知醫師？

Cyclobenzaprine has anticholinergic effects that raise intraocular pressure.Cyclobenzaprine具抗膽鹼作用，可升高眼壓。

6. A client with an intrathecal baclofen pump misses refill visits. What is the risk?使用脊髓腔baclofen幫浦的病人未回診補藥。風險為何？

The pump can empty, causing severe withdrawal.幫浦可能排空而導致嚴重戒斷。

📖 PDF p.1005 (book p.910)

Fever and persistent site inflammation may signal infection.發燒與注射部位持續發炎可能為感染徵象。
