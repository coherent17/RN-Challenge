Part IX · Endocrine Problems of the Adult Client成人內分泌問題

Chapter 48: Endocrine Medications第 48 章：內分泌藥物

📖 Source: PDF p.694–710 · printed book p.626–640.e2來源：PDF 第 694–710 頁 · 書本印刷頁 626–640.e2

This chapter organizes endocrine drugs by class: pituitary hormones, desmopressin, thyroid and antithyroid agents, calcium/parathyroid agents, corticosteroids, sex hormones, and diabetes drugs including insulin. For each class know the action, the signature adverse effect, what to monitor, and the teaching that prevents harm (e.g., never stop steroids abruptly, draw up regular insulin before NPH).本章依藥物類別整理內分泌用藥：腦下垂體激素、去氨加壓素、甲狀腺與抗甲狀腺藥、鈣/副甲狀腺藥、皮質類固醇、性荷爾蒙，以及含胰島素在內的糖尿病藥物。每一類都要掌握作用、代表性不良反應、監測項目，以及能避免傷害的衛教（例如類固醇不可突然停藥、混合胰島素時先抽取速效/常規型）。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Pituitary hormones and antidiuretic hormone腦下垂體激素與抗利尿激素 📖 PDF p.694 (book p.626), PDF p.695 (book p.627)

  • Growth hormone (somatropin, mecasermin) treats GH deficiency; receptor antagonists/somatostatin analogs (octreotide, lanreotide, pasireotide, pegvisomant) treat acromegaly.生長激素（somatropin、mecasermin）用於生長激素缺乏；受體拮抗劑/體抑素類似物（octreotide、lanreotide、pasireotide、pegvisomant）用於肢端肥大症。
  • GH adverse effects: headache, mild hyperglycemia, hypertension, weight gain, injection-site pain, raised AST/ALT, antibody formation.生長激素不良反應：頭痛、輕度高血糖、高血壓、體重增加、注射部位疼痛、AST/ALT 上升、產生抗體。
  • Monitor children's growth against standards, yearly bone-age studies, vital signs, glucose, liver enzymes and thyroid tests.監測兒童生長曲線、每年骨齡檢查、生命徵象、血糖、肝酵素與甲狀腺功能。
  • Desmopressin (DDAVP) and vasopressin increase renal water reabsorption; used for diabetes insipidus (vasopressin is also used in shock).去氨加壓素（desmopressin）與血管加壓素增加腎臟水分再吸收，用於尿崩症（血管加壓素亦用於休克）。
  • Main risk is water intoxication (drowsiness, listlessness, headache, dyspnea, hypertension): decrease dose. Dehydration/polyuria means the dose may need to increase.主要風險為水中毒（嗜睡、倦怠、頭痛、呼吸喘、高血壓）：需減量；脫水或多尿代表可能需增量。
  • Monitor weight daily, I&O, urine osmolality, electrolytes and BP; teach correct intranasal technique.每日測體重、記錄輸出入量、尿滲透壓、電解質與血壓；教導鼻內給藥技巧。

Thyroid hormones (levothyroxine, liothyronine)甲狀腺素（levothyroxine、liothyronine） 📖 PDF p.695 (book p.627)

  • Replace deficient hormone in hypothyroidism and myxedema coma; raise metabolic rate, heat production and oxygen use.補充甲狀腺機能低下與黏液水腫昏迷時不足的荷爾蒙；提高代謝率、產熱與耗氧。
  • Overdose looks like hyperthyroidism: tachycardia, palpitations, chest pain, nervousness, tremor, insomnia, sweating, heat intolerance, weight loss, diarrhea.過量表現如甲亢：心搏過速、心悸、胸痛、緊張、顫抖、失眠、盜汗、不耐熱、體重減輕、腹瀉。
  • Take at the same time every morning on an empty stomach; separate by at least 4 hours from calcium, iron, antacids, multivitamins, bile acid sequestrants, sucralfate.每天同一時間晨起空腹服用；與鈣、鐵、制酸劑、綜合維他命、膽酸結合劑、sucralfate 至少間隔 4 小時。
  • Enhances warfarin, sympathomimetics and antidepressants; decreases effect of insulin, oral hypoglycemics and digoxin; phenytoin/carbamazepine reduce thyroid effect.增強 warfarin、擬交感神經藥與抗憂鬱藥作用；降低胰島素、口服降血糖藥、digoxin 的效果；phenytoin/carbamazepine 會降低甲狀腺素效果。
  • Monitor T3, T4, TSH, pulse, weight; teach client to take pulse and to avoid OTC drugs; wear a MedicAlert bracelet.監測 T3、T4、TSH、脈搏與體重；教導自測脈搏、避免自行使用成藥；佩戴醫療警示手環。

Antithyroid drugs and thyroid eye disease抗甲狀腺藥與甲狀腺眼病 📖 PDF p.695 (book p.627), PDF p.696 (book p.628)

  • Methimazole and propylthiouracil inhibit hormone synthesis (Graves' disease, hyperthyroidism); potassium iodide/strong iodine (Lugol's) are used about 2 weeks before thyroid surgery.Methimazole 與 propylthiouracil 抑制甲狀腺素合成（Graves 病、甲亢）；碘化鉀/強碘液（Lugol）用於甲狀腺手術前約 2 週。
  • Agranulocytosis (fever, sore throat) is the key adverse effect; also rash, GI upset, leukopenia, thrombocytopenia, alopecia. Overdose causes hypothyroidism.關鍵不良反應為顆粒球低下症（發燒、喉嚨痛）；另有皮疹、腸胃不適、白血球/血小板低下、落髮。過量導致甲狀腺機能低下。
  • Iodism: metallic/brassy taste, sore gums and salivary glands, vomiting, abdominal pain, rash.碘中毒：口中金屬味、牙齦與唾液腺疼痛、嘔吐、腹痛、皮疹。
  • Give with meals; never stop abruptly (risk of thyroid storm: fever, flushing, confusion, tachycardia, heart failure). Avoid aspirin and iodine-containing products.與餐同服；不可突然停藥（可能引發甲狀腺風暴：發燒、潮紅、意識混亂、心搏過速、心衰竭）。避免阿斯匹靈與含碘產品。
  • Teprotumumab reduces swelling, bulging and double vision in thyroid eye disease.Teprotumumab 可減輕甲狀腺眼病的腫脹、突眼與複視。

Calcium and parathyroid-related drugs鈣與副甲狀腺相關藥物 📖 PDF p.696 (book p.628)

  • Hypoparathyroidism (low Ca, tetany) is treated with calcium and vitamin D; hyperparathyroidism/hypercalcemia uses agents such as cinacalcet, calcitonin, bisphosphonates.副甲狀腺功能低下（低血鈣、手足抽搐）以鈣與維生素 D 治療；副甲狀腺功能亢進/高血鈣用 cinacalcet、calcitonin、雙磷酸鹽等。
  • Calcium with digoxin raises digoxin toxicity risk; oral calcium lowers tetracycline absorption.鈣合併 digoxin 會增加 digoxin 中毒風險；口服鈣降低 tetracycline 吸收。
  • Alendronate: swallow whole with plain water at least 30 minutes before breakfast and stay upright 30 minutes.Alendronate：早餐前至少 30 分鐘以白開水整顆吞服，並保持直立至少 30 分鐘。
  • Calcitonin nasal spray: alternate nostrils. Calcium supplements: adequate vitamin D, fluids and fiber (constipation); give other drugs 1 hour apart.Calcitonin 鼻噴劑：左右鼻孔交替。鈣補充劑：維生素 D 充足、多喝水與纖維（預防便秘）；其他藥物間隔 1 小時。
  • Hypercalcemia signs: renal stones, polyuria, polydipsia, anorexia, nausea, constipation; clients on antihypercalcemics limit calcium-rich foods.高血鈣徵象：腎結石、多尿、多渴、厭食、噁心、便秘；使用降鈣藥者需減少高鈣食物。

Corticosteroids: mineralocorticoids and glucocorticoids皮質類固醇：礦物皮質素與糖皮質素 📖 PDF p.697 (book p.629), PDF p.698 (book p.630)

  • Fludrocortisone (mineralocorticoid) retains Na and water and excretes K: replacement in Addison's disease; adverse effects edema, hypertension, hypokalemia, heart failure. Encourage high-potassium diet.Fludrocortisone（礦物皮質素）留鈉留水、排鉀：用於 Addison 氏病替代治療；不良反應水腫、高血壓、低血鉀、心衰竭。建議高鉀飲食。
  • Glucocorticoids (prednisone, hydrocortisone, dexamethasone, methylprednisolone, etc.) are anti-inflammatory/immunosuppressive.糖皮質素（prednisone、hydrocortisone、dexamethasone、methylprednisolone 等）具抗發炎與免疫抑制作用。
  • Adverse effects: hyperglycemia, hypokalemia, fluid retention, infection risk with masked signs, osteoporosis, moon face, buffalo hump, mood swings or psychosis, cataracts, GI ulcers, growth retardation in children.不良反應：高血糖、低血鉀、水分滯留、感染風險且徵象被掩蓋、骨質疏鬆、月亮臉、水牛肩、情緒波動或精神病、白內障、胃潰瘍、兒童生長遲緩。
  • Take in the early morning with food; never stop abruptly (adrenal insufficiency); extra dose may be needed during stress or surgery; no live vaccines; avoid infected people.清晨隨食物服用；不可突然停藥（腎上腺功能不全）；壓力或手術時可能需追加劑量；不可接種活病毒疫苗；避免接觸感染者。
  • Monitor glucose, electrolytes, I&O, weight, BP; NSAIDs/aspirin plus steroids raise GI bleeding risk; barbiturates, phenytoin, rifampin reduce prednisone effect.監測血糖、電解質、輸出入、體重、血壓；NSAIDs/阿斯匹靈合併類固醇增加消化道出血風險；巴比妥類、phenytoin、rifampin 降低 prednisone 效果。

Androgens, estrogens and progestins雄性激素、雌激素與黃體素 📖 PDF p.698 (book p.630), PDF p.699 (book p.631)

  • Androgens (testosterone products, methyltestosterone): virilization, priapism, edema, acne, hepatotoxicity, hypercalcemia; enhance warfarin; may lower insulin needs; avoid in prostate/breast cancer.雄性激素（睪固酮製劑、methyltestosterone）：男性化、陰莖異常勃起、水腫、痤瘡、肝毒性、高血鈣；增強 warfarin；可能降低胰島素需求；前列腺癌/乳癌避免使用。
  • Report priapism, fluid retention, jaundice/RUQ pain; give with meals; use nonhormonal contraception.出現陰莖異常勃起、水腫、黃疸/右上腹痛須通報；隨餐服用；使用非荷爾蒙避孕。
  • Estrogens: contraindicated in breast/endometrial cancer, thromboembolism, pregnancy, lactation; adverse effects thromboembolism, stroke, MI, hypertension, migraine, nausea, edema.雌激素：乳癌/子宮內膜癌、血栓栓塞、懷孕、哺乳為禁忌；不良反應血栓栓塞、中風、心肌梗塞、高血壓、偏頭痛、噁心、水腫。
  • Progestins: contraindicated with thromboembolic disorders; avoid in breast tumors and liver disease. Teach no smoking, routine breast/pelvic exams, BP and weight monitoring.黃體素：血栓栓塞疾病禁用；乳房腫瘤與肝病避免使用。衛教戒菸、定期乳房與骨盆檢查、監測血壓與體重。
  • Barbiturates, phenytoin and rifampin reduce estrogen effectiveness.巴比妥類、phenytoin、rifampin 降低雌激素效果。

Oral and injectable antidiabetic drugs (non-insulin)口服與非胰島素注射降血糖藥 📖 PDF p.699 (book p.631), PDF p.700 (book p.632), PDF p.703 (book p.635)

  • Metformin (biguanide): lowers hepatic glucose output; GI upset, lactic acidosis risk; little hypoglycemia. Hold before and 48 hours after IV contrast; check creatinine before resuming.Metformin（雙胍類）：減少肝臟葡萄糖輸出；腸胃不適、乳酸中毒風險；低血糖少見。靜脈注射顯影劑前及後 48 小時暫停，恢復前檢查肌酸酐。
  • Sulfonylureas (glimepiride, glipizide, glyburide) and meglitinides (repaglinide, nateglinide): increase insulin secretion; hypoglycemia, weight gain; sulfonylureas can cause a disulfiram-like reaction with alcohol; meglitinides taken right before/with the meal and skipped if meal skipped.磺醯脲類與格列奈類（repaglinide、nateglinide）：促進胰島素分泌；低血糖、體重增加；磺醯脲類與酒精可產生類 disulfiram 反應；格列奈類於餐前/餐時服用，不進食則不服。
  • Thiazolidinediones (pioglitazone): heart failure, fractures, bladder cancer, ovulation; alpha-glucosidase inhibitors (acarbose, miglitol): flatulence, give with first bite of meal.噻唑烷二酮（pioglitazone）：心衰竭、骨折、膀胱癌、恢復排卵；α-葡萄糖苷酶抑制劑（acarbose、miglitol）：脹氣，隨第一口飯服用。
  • DPP-4 inhibitors (-gliptins): pancreatitis, hypersensitivity. SGLT-2 inhibitors (-gliflozins): genital mycotic infection, orthostatic hypotension, weight loss.DPP-4 抑制劑（-gliptin）：胰臟炎、過敏。SGLT-2 抑制劑（-gliflozin）：生殖器黴菌感染、姿位性低血壓、體重下降。
  • GLP-1 agonists (semaglutide, dulaglutide, exenatide): slow gastric emptying, appetite suppression; nausea, pancreatitis, thyroid C-cell tumor warning (semaglutide); not for type 1; give other oral drugs 1 hour before.GLP-1 受體促效劑（semaglutide、dulaglutide、exenatide）：延緩胃排空、抑制食慾；噁心、胰臟炎、甲狀腺 C 細胞腫瘤警示（semaglutide）；不適用第一型；其他口服藥於注射前 1 小時服用。
  • Pramlintide (amylin mimetic) for insulin users: severe hypoglycemia risk, nausea; oral drugs 1 hour before or 2 hours after. Sympathomimetics, steroids, thiazides, phenytoin raise glucose; beta blockers mask hypoglycemia.Pramlintide（類澱粉素）用於使用胰島素者：嚴重低血糖風險、噁心；口服藥於注射前 1 小時或後 2 小時服用。擬交感藥、類固醇、thiazide、phenytoin 使血糖升高；β 阻斷劑掩蓋低血糖症狀。

Insulin and glucagon胰島素與升糖素 📖 PDF p.701 (book p.633), PDF p.702 (book p.634), PDF p.703 (book p.635)

  • Onset/peak/duration: lispro 15-30 min/0.5-2.5 h/3-6 h; aspart 10-20 min/1-3 h/3-5 h; regular 30-60 min/1-5 h/6-10 h; NPH 60-120 min/6-14 h/16-24 h; glargine no peak, 18-24 h; detemir 12-24 h peak; degludec no peak, >40 h.起效/高峰/持續：lispro 15-30 分/0.5-2.5 小時/3-6 小時；aspart 10-20 分/1-3 小時/3-5 小時；常規型 30-60 分/1-5 小時/6-10 小時；NPH 60-120 分/6-14 小時/16-24 小時；glargine 無高峰、18-24 小時；detemir 高峰 12-24 小時；degludec 無高峰、>40 小時。
  • Mixing: draw up clear (regular/short) first, then cloudy NPH ("RN"); only NPH is cloudy and must be rolled, not shaken; inject air into both vials; use mixed dose within 5-15 minutes; glargine is never mixed.混合：先抽透明（常規/速效），再抽混濁的 NPH（口訣 RN）；只有 NPH 混濁，需滾動不可劇烈搖晃；兩瓶都先注入空氣；混合後 5-15 分鐘內使用；glargine 不可混合。
  • Storage: unopened vials refrigerated, never frozen; vial in use may stay at room temperature if used within about 1 month; give at room temperature; avoid heat/sunlight.保存：未開封瓶冷藏、不可冷凍；使用中且約 1 個月內用完可置室溫；注射時為室溫；避免高溫與日照。
  • Sites: abdomen absorbs fastest/most even; rotate within one area, 1-1.5 inch apart, not same site for 2-3 weeks; heat, massage, exercise raise absorption (hypoglycemia); scar tissue delays it. Angle 45-90 degrees (45-60 if thin).部位：腹部吸收最快且均勻；同一區域輪替，間距 1-1.5 吋，2-3 週不重複同一點；熱敷、按摩、運動加速吸收（低血糖）；疤痕組織延遲吸收。角度 45-90 度（消瘦者 45-60 度）。
  • U-100 is standard; only regular insulin comes as U-500 (severe resistance, special syringe). Pens need a new needle each injection. Aspiration generally not needed.標準濃度 U-100；僅常規型有 U-500（嚴重胰島素阻抗，需專用針筒）。筆型注射每次換新針頭。一般不需回抽。
  • Glucagon raises glucose by liver glycogenolysis; SC/IM/IV for insulin hypoglycemia when client cannot swallow; effect within 5-20 minutes; also used for beta blocker overdose; teach family.升糖素經肝醣分解升高血糖；病人無法吞嚥時以皮下/肌肉/靜脈給予，5-20 分鐘起效；亦用於 β 阻斷劑過量；需教導家屬。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Never stop corticosteroids abruptly: risk of acute adrenal insufficiency. Dose taper schedules; extra dose may be needed under stress.皮質類固醇不可突然停藥：恐致急性腎上腺功能不全；需依遞減計畫，壓力時可能需追加劑量。 📖 PDF p.697 (book p.629), PDF p.698 (book p.630)
  2. Glucocorticoids mask infection and raise glucose; no live-virus vaccines.糖皮質素會掩蓋感染徵象並升高血糖；不可接種活病毒疫苗。 📖 PDF p.697 (book p.629), PDF p.698 (book p.630)
  3. Methimazole: agranulocytosis. Fever or sore throat means call the PHCP.Methimazole：顆粒球低下症；發燒或喉嚨痛須通知醫師。 📖 PDF p.696 (book p.628)
  4. Abrupt antithyroid withdrawal can trigger thyroid storm (fever, tachycardia, confusion, flushing, HF).抗甲狀腺藥突然停用可能引發甲狀腺風暴（發燒、心搏過速、意識混亂、潮紅、心衰竭）。 📖 PDF p.696 (book p.628)
  5. Levothyroxine: morning, empty stomach, same time daily; separate from calcium/iron/antacids by 4 hours; report tachycardia, chest pain, palpitations, sweating (toxicity).Levothyroxine：晨起空腹、每天同一時間；與鈣/鐵/制酸劑間隔 4 小時；出現心搏過速、胸痛、心悸、盜汗（中毒）須通報。 📖 PDF p.695 (book p.627)
  6. Desmopressin: water intoxication signs (headache, drowsiness, dyspnea) mean dose too high; dehydration means dose may be too low.去氨加壓素：水中毒徵象（頭痛、嗜睡、呼吸喘）代表劑量過高；脫水可能代表劑量不足。 📖 PDF p.694 (book p.626)
  7. Metformin: hold for IV contrast and 48 hours after; early lactic acidosis signs are unusual somnolence, hyperventilation, myalgia, malaise.Metformin：靜脈顯影劑前及後 48 小時暫停；乳酸中毒早期徵象為異常嗜睡、過度換氣、肌痛、倦怠。 📖 PDF p.700 (book p.632), PDF p.701 (book p.633), PDF p.705 (book p.637)
  8. Regular (clear) insulin is drawn up before NPH (cloudy); glargine is never mixed; do not freeze insulin.先抽常規型（透明）再抽 NPH（混濁）；glargine 不可混合；胰島素不可冷凍。 📖 PDF p.702 (book p.634)
  9. Heat, massage or exercise at the injection site speeds absorption and can cause hypoglycemia.注射部位熱敷、按摩或運動會加速吸收而導致低血糖。 📖 PDF p.701 (book p.633)
  10. Sulfonylurea + alcohol: disulfiram-like reaction (flushing, palpitations, nausea) and added hypoglycemia.磺醯脲類 + 酒精：類 disulfiram 反應（潮紅、心悸、噁心）並加重低血糖。 📖 PDF p.699 (book p.631), PDF p.705 (book p.637)
  11. Beta blockers mask hypoglycemia signs; steroids, thiazides, phenytoin, estrogen raise glucose.β 阻斷劑掩蓋低血糖徵象；類固醇、thiazide、phenytoin、雌激素使血糖升高。 📖 PDF p.699 (book p.631)
  12. GLP-1 agonists are not for type 1 diabetes and are not a substitute for insulin; query the order.GLP-1 促效劑不適用第一型糖尿病，也不可取代胰島素；應向醫師確認醫囑。 📖 PDF p.702 (book p.634), PDF p.703 (book p.635), PDF p.705 (book p.637)
  13. Pramlintide: oral meds 1 hour before or 2 hours after; risk of severe hypoglycemia with insulin.Pramlintide：口服藥於注射前 1 小時或後 2 小時；與胰島素併用有嚴重低血糖風險。 📖 PDF p.703 (book p.635)
  14. Glucagon is for the semiconscious/unconscious hypoglycemic client who cannot take liquids; works in 5-20 minutes.升糖素用於半昏迷/昏迷、無法飲水的低血糖病人；5-20 分鐘起效。 📖 PDF p.703 (book p.635)
  15. Alendronate: upright 30 minutes, plain water, 30 minutes before breakfast.Alendronate：餐前 30 分鐘以白開水服用並直立 30 分鐘。 📖 PDF p.696 (book p.628), PDF p.705 (book p.637)
  16. Estrogens: thromboembolism, stroke, MI; teach no smoking. Androgens: report priapism.雌激素：血栓栓塞、中風、心肌梗塞；教導戒菸。雄性激素：陰莖異常勃起須通報。 📖 PDF p.698 (book p.630), PDF p.699 (book p.631)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. A client is prescribed regular insulin and NPH insulin in one syringe. Which action indicates correct technique?病人須將常規型胰島素與 NPH 抽在同一支針筒。下列何者為正確技巧？

Draw up the short-acting (clear) insulin first to avoid contaminating its vial with NPH (RN). Roll NPH gently, inject air into both vials.先抽速效（透明）胰島素，避免其瓶被 NPH 污染（RN）。NPH 輕輕滾動，兩瓶皆先注入空氣。

2. The nurse reviews a hospitalized client taking metformin who is scheduled for a CT scan with IV contrast. What is the priority action?護理師查閱住院病人資料，病人服用 metformin 並預定做靜脈顯影劑電腦斷層。首要處置為何？

Metformin is withheld before and for 48 hours after IV contrast because of contrast nephropathy and lactic acidosis; encourage fluids and check creatinine before resuming.顯影劑前及後 48 小時需暫停 metformin（顯影劑腎病變與乳酸中毒風險）；鼓勵飲水並於恢復前檢查肌酸酐。
