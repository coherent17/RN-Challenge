Chapter 32: Oncological Problems第 32 章：腫瘤問題

📖 Source: PDF p.454–464 · printed book p.406–415.e1來源：PDF 第 454–464 頁 · 書本印刷頁 406–415.e1

Childhood cancers: leukemia, Hodgkin disease, Wilms tumor, neuroblastoma, osteosarcoma and brain tumors. Key nursing themes are infection and bleeding protection during chemotherapy, ICP monitoring, and tumor-specific precautions such as not palpating a Wilms tumor.兒童癌症：白血病、何杰金氏病、威爾姆氏瘤、神經母細胞瘤、骨肉瘤與腦瘤。護理重點為化療期間防感染與防出血、顱內壓監測，以及各腫瘤特殊注意事項（如威爾姆氏瘤不可觸診腹部）。

Leukemia: description and assessment白血病：說明與評估 📖 PDF p.454 (book p.406)

  • Malignant overproduction of immature WBCs (blasts) in bone marrow causing anemia, neutropenia (infection) and thrombocytopenia (bleeding). ALL is the most common childhood cancer.骨髓中未成熟白血球（blast）惡性增生，造成貧血、嗜中性球低下（感染）與血小板低下（出血）。ALL 為兒童最常見癌症。
  • Chemotherapy phases: induction (remission), intensification/consolidation, CNS prophylaxis, maintenance.化療階段：誘導（緩解）、強化/鞏固、中樞神經預防、維持。
  • Signs: fever, pallor, fatigue, anorexia, petechiae, bone/joint pain, pathological fractures, hepatosplenomegaly, lymphadenopathy.徵象：發燒、蒼白、疲倦、厭食、瘀點、骨關節痛、病理性骨折、肝脾腫大、淋巴結腫大。
  • WBC may be low, normal or high; Hgb/Hct and platelets decreased.白血球可低、正常或高；Hgb/Hct 與血小板下降。
  • Bone marrow biopsy with blasts confirms; lumbar puncture detects CNS disease (signs of increased ICP, cranial nerve VII).骨髓切片見 blast 確診；腰椎穿刺偵測中樞神經侵犯（顱內壓上升徵象、第 VII 對腦神經）。

Protecting from infection, bleeding; chemotherapy care防感染、防出血與化療照護 📖 PDF p.454 (book p.406), PDF p.455 (book p.407), PDF p.456 (book p.408)

  • Infection: protective isolation, private room (HEPA), strict asepsis, masks, no raw fruits/vegetables, no fresh flowers or standing water, daily antimicrobial bath, frequent oral care.感染防護：保護性隔離、單人房（HEPA）、嚴格無菌技術、戴口罩、不吃生鮮蔬果、禁鮮花與積水、每日抗菌洗澡、常口腔護理。
  • Avoid injections, rectal temperatures, catheterization; give G-CSF; no live-virus vaccines or varicella vaccine; inactivated polio ok.避免注射、肛溫、導尿；給予 G-CSF；不可打活性病毒疫苗與水痘疫苗；可打不活化小兒麻痺疫苗。
  • Neutropenic fever: obtain cultures and CBC with differential, then start antibiotics.嗜中性球低下發燒：先採檢體培養與 CBC 分類，再給抗生素。
  • Bleeding: platelet transfusion for active bleeding unresponsive to local care; soft toothbrush, no floss, no NSAIDs/aspirin, no rectal meds, firm pressure 10 minutes after needlestick, measure abdominal girth.出血防護：活動性出血且局部處理無效才輸血小板；軟毛牙刷、不用牙線、禁 NSAID/阿斯匹靈、禁肛門給藥、針刺後加壓至少 10 分鐘、量腹圍。
  • Chemo: watch nadir, nausea/vomiting (antiemetics before, during, after), constipation (stool softeners), mucositis, hemorrhagic cystitis, peripheral neuropathy; hair regrows in 3-6 months.化療：留意最低點（nadir）、噁心嘔吐（化療前中後給止吐藥）、便祕（軟便劑）、黏膜炎、出血性膀胱炎、周邊神經病變；毛髮 3–6 個月再生。
  • Small, soft, non-irritating meals; cluster care to conserve energy.少量、軟、無刺激飲食；集中護理節省體力。

Increased ICP and Hodgkin disease顱內壓上升與何杰金氏病 📖 PDF p.455 (book p.407), PDF p.456 (book p.408), PDF p.457 (book p.409), PDF p.458 (book p.410)

  • Infant ICP signs: bulging fontanel, split sutures, Macewen sign, high-pitched cry, setting-sun eyes; older child: headache, forceful vomiting, diplopia, seizures.嬰兒顱內壓徵象：囪門膨出、縫線分離、Macewen 徵象、高頻哭聲、落日眼；較大兒童：頭痛、噴射嘔吐、複視、癲癇。
  • Late ICP signs: bradycardia, pupil changes, posturing, Cheyne-Stokes, papilledema, coma.顱內壓晚期徵象：心搏過緩、瞳孔改變、去大腦/去皮質姿勢、Cheyne-Stokes 呼吸、視乳突水腫、昏迷。
  • Hodgkin: lymph node malignancy with Reed-Sternberg cells; peak mid-adolescence; excellent prognosis.何杰金氏病：淋巴結惡性腫瘤，可見 Reed-Sternberg 細胞；高峰在青春中期；預後佳。
  • Painless firm movable cervical/supraclavicular nodes, nonproductive cough, fever, night sweats, weight loss, pruritus; biopsy confirms.無痛、硬、可移動的頸部/鎖骨上淋巴結、乾咳、發燒、盜汗、體重減輕、搔癢；切片確診。
  • Treat with chemo +/- radiation; Table 32.1 lists radiation effects (anorexia, mucositis, alopecia, skin desquamation, cystitis, myelosuppression).以化療加/不加放療；表 32.1 列放療副作用（厭食、黏膜炎、掉髮、皮膚脫屑、膀胱炎、骨髓抑制）。

Wilms tumor and neuroblastoma威爾姆氏瘤與神經母細胞瘤 📖 PDF p.457 (book p.409), PDF p.458 (book p.410), PDF p.459 (book p.411)

  • Wilms: most common childhood kidney/intra-abdominal tumor, peak age 3; firm, nontender, one-sided flank mass, hematuria, hypertension (renin), anemia, fever.威爾姆氏瘤：兒童最常見腎臟/腹內腫瘤，高峰 3 歲；單側側腹硬、無痛腫塊、血尿、高血壓（腎素）、貧血、發燒。
  • Do NOT palpate the abdomen (risk of tumor rupture and spread); post sign; measure girth daily; handle gently. Treatment: nephrectomy plus chemo +/- radiation.勿觸診腹部（腫瘤破裂擴散風險）；床邊放警示牌；每日量腹圍；輕柔搬動。治療：腎切除加化療 ± 放療。
  • Post-op Wilms: monitor BP, temperature, hemorrhage, infection, I&O, bowel sounds/obstruction.威爾姆氏瘤術後：監測血壓、體溫、出血、感染、輸出入量、腸音/腸阻塞。
  • Neuroblastoma: neural crest tumor of adrenal medulla/sympathetic chain; diagnosed late with metastasis; urine VMA, HVA, dopamine, norepinephrine elevated.神經母細胞瘤：源自腎上腺髓質/交感神經鏈的神經嵴腫瘤；多診斷時已轉移；尿 VMA、HVA、多巴胺、正腎上腺素升高。
  • Firm irregular abdominal mass crossing midline, bone pain, periorbital ecchymosis/exophthalmos, cord compression paralysis; younger age gives better survival.堅硬不規則腹部腫塊且越過中線、骨痛、眼眶周圍瘀斑/突眼、脊髓壓迫麻痺；診斷年齡越小存活越好。

Osteosarcoma骨肉瘤 📖 PDF p.459 (book p.411)

  • Most common bone cancer in children, peak age 13-16, metaphysis of long bones, often femur.兒童最常見骨癌，高峰 13–16 歲，長骨幹骺端，多在股骨。
  • Localized pain (often relieved by flexion), mass, limp, limited ROM, pathological fracture; early mistaken for growing pains.局部疼痛（屈曲時緩解）、腫塊、跛行、活動受限、病理性骨折；早期常誤為生長痛。
  • Treatment: chemo plus limb-salvage surgery or amputation; prosthesis fitting; teach phantom limb pain (tingling, itching, pain).治療：化療加保肢手術或截肢；義肢配置；教導幻肢痛（刺麻、癢、痛）。
  • Support body image; communicate honestly.支持身體心像；誠實溝通。

Brain tumors and craniotomy care腦瘤與開顱術照護 📖 PDF p.459 (book p.411), PDF p.460 (book p.412)

  • Infratentorial (posterior, cerebellum/brainstem) tumors are most common in children; supratentorial in cerebrum; MRI is the main diagnostic.幕下（後方，小腦/腦幹）腫瘤在兒童最常見；幕上位於大腦；MRI 為主要診斷。
  • Signs: morning headache improving through the day, vomiting unrelated to feeding, ataxia, seizures, behavior change, diplopia, facial weakness.徵象：晨起頭痛白天緩解、與進食無關的嘔吐、共濟失調、癲癇、行為改變、複視、顏面無力。
  • Preop: neuro checks at least every 4 hours, seizure precautions, nutrition assessment, prepare child for large head dressing.術前：至少每 4 小時神經評估、癲癇防護、營養評估、告知術後會有大頭部敷料。
  • Postop: neuro and pupil checks, temperature (cooling blanket), meningitis signs, ICP signs, hemorrhage (check back of dressing), prevent vomiting, quiet environment.術後：神經與瞳孔檢查、體溫（備降溫毯）、腦膜炎徵象、顱內壓徵象、出血（檢查敷料後方）、預防嘔吐、安靜環境。
  • Clear/colorless drainage = CSF (glucose positive on dipstick): report immediately.無色引流液＝腦脊髓液（試紙測葡萄糖陽性）：立即通報。
  • Positioning: never Trendelenburg; supratentorial: head elevated; infratentorial: flat or on side; after large tumor removal avoid operative side.擺位：絕不採頭低腳高；幕上手術抬高頭部；幕下手術平躺或側臥；大型腫瘤切除後避免壓術側。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Colorless drainage on a craniotomy dressing is CSF: notify the surgeon immediately.開顱術後敷料無色滲液＝腦脊髓液：立即通知外科醫師。 📖 PDF p.460 (book p.412), PDF p.462 (book p.414)
  2. Do not palpate the abdomen in suspected Wilms tumor.疑似威爾姆氏瘤不可觸診腹部。 📖 PDF p.458 (book p.410), PDF p.460 (book p.412), PDF p.462 (book p.414)
  3. Never place a post-craniotomy child in Trendelenburg or supine; it raises ICP.開顱術後絕不採頭低腳高或平躺；會增加顱內壓。 📖 PDF p.460 (book p.412), PDF p.462 (book p.414)
  4. Bone marrow biopsy with blasts confirms leukemia; Reed-Sternberg cells confirm Hodgkin.骨髓見 blast 確診白血病；Reed-Sternberg 細胞確診何杰金氏病。 📖 PDF p.454 (book p.406), PDF p.457 (book p.409), PDF p.463 (book p.415)
  5. Platelets below 50,000 call for bleeding precautions (example 19,500 mm3).血小板低於 50,000 需出血防護（例：19,500 mm3）。 📖 PDF p.461 (book p.413), PDF p.462 (book p.414)
  6. No fresh flowers, plants or standing water for neutropenic children (Aspergillus, Pseudomonas).嗜中性球低下兒童禁鮮花、植物與積水（麴菌、綠膿桿菌）。 📖 PDF p.455 (book p.407), PDF p.463 (book p.415)
  7. Neutropenic fever: cultures and CBC with differential first, then antibiotics.嗜中性球低下發燒：先採培養與 CBC 分類，再給抗生素。 📖 PDF p.455 (book p.407)
  8. No live virus vaccines for immunosuppressed child; inactivated polio acceptable.免疫抑制兒童禁打活性病毒疫苗；不活化小兒麻痺可。 📖 PDF p.455 (book p.407)
  9. Vomiting is an early ICP sign in a child whose fontanels are closed; infants show bulging fontanel.囪門已閉的兒童嘔吐是顱內壓上升早期徵象；嬰兒則見囪門膨出。 📖 PDF p.455 (book p.407), PDF p.462 (book p.414)
  10. Urine VMA elevation suggests neuroblastoma.尿 VMA 升高提示神經母細胞瘤。 📖 PDF p.458 (book p.410), PDF p.463 (book p.415)
  11. Avoid aspirin/NSAIDs, rectal temperatures, enemas, suppositories, injections in thrombocytopenic child.血小板低下兒童避免阿斯匹靈/NSAID、肛溫、灌腸、塞劑、注射。 📖 PDF p.456 (book p.408)
  12. Osteosarcoma pain often dismissed as growing pains; femur most common.骨肉瘤疼痛常被誤認為生長痛；股骨最常見。 📖 PDF p.459 (book p.411)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Wilms = Won't palpate.Wilms 腫瘤＝「不要摸」（Won't palpate）。 📖 PDF p.458 (book p.410)
  • ICP early vs late: vomiting, headache early; bradycardia, posturing late (Cushing-type).顱內壓早期：嘔吐、頭痛；晚期：心搏過緩、姿勢異常。 📖 PDF p.455 (book p.407)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. After a craniotomy the nurse notes colorless drainage on the back of the head dressing. What is the first action?開顱術後護理師發現頭部敷料後側有無色滲液，首要行動為何？

Colorless drainage suggests CSF leak; report at once.無色滲液提示腦脊髓液漏，須立即通報。

2. A 4-year-old has a swollen abdomen, eats well, normal activity. Wilms tumor is suspected. What does the nurse avoid?4 歲孩童腹脹、食慾與活動正常，疑威爾姆氏瘤，護理師應避免什麼？

Palpation may rupture the encapsulated tumor and spread cells.觸診可能使包膜腫瘤破裂並擴散。

3. A child with leukemia has a platelet count of 19,500/mm3. Which intervention is included?白血病孩童血小板 19,500/mm3，應納入何項措施？

Thrombocytopenia increases bleeding risk.血小板低下增加出血風險。

7. A 3-year-old post-craniotomy: which is an early sign of increased ICP?3 歲孩童開顱術後，何為顱內壓上升的早期徵象？

Vomiting is early; bradycardia and posturing are late; toddler cannot reliably report headache.嘔吐為早期；心搏過緩與姿勢為晚期；幼兒難以描述頭痛。
