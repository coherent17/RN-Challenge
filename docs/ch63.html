Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Immune function, response and immunity免疫功能、反應與免疫類型 📖 PDF p.1015 (book p.919)

  • The immune system defends against outside microbes and removes dead or damaged cells.免疫系統抵禦外來微生物並清除死亡或受損細胞。
  • T and B lymphocytes arise in bone marrow; T cells include helper, suppressor and cytotoxic types.T與B淋巴球源自骨髓；T細胞包括輔助型、抑制型與細胞毒性型。
  • Humoral (B cell, antibody) response is immediate and protects against acute bacterial and viral infection.體液性（B細胞、抗體）反應迅速，防禦急性細菌與病毒感染。
  • Cellular response is delayed (delayed hypersensitivity); involved in slow bacterial infections, autoimmunity, some allergies and graft rejection.細胞性反應延遲（遲發型過敏）；與緩慢細菌感染、自體免疫、部分過敏及移植排斥有關。
  • Innate immunity is present at birth (barriers, inflammation); acquired immunity is passive (maternal antibodies, serum) or active (disease, immunization).先天免疫出生即有（屏障、發炎）；後天免疫分被動（母體抗體、血清）與主動（疾病、疫苗）。

Laboratory studies and skin testing檢驗與皮膚試驗 📖 PDF p.1015 (book p.919), PDF p.1016 (book p.920), PDF p.1017 (book p.921)

  • ANA: negative at 1:40 dilution; positive in most SLE (also scleroderma, RA) but not diagnostic alone; false positives occur.ANA：1:40稀釋時為陰性；多數SLE為陽性（硬皮症、RA亦可）但不能單獨診斷；可有偽陽性。
  • Anti-dsDNA: specific to SLE, tracks disease activity; normal under 70 U/mL by ELISA.Anti-dsDNA：對SLE具特異性、可追蹤疾病活性；ELISA正常小於70 U/mL。
  • CD4+ count: normal 500-1600 cells/uL; 200-499 immune problems; below 200 severe (AIDS criteria). CD4:CD8 ratio normally about 2:1.CD4+計數：正常500-1600 cells/µL；200-499免疫出現問題；低於200為嚴重（AIDS標準）。CD4:CD8比值正常約2:1。
  • HIV testing: maintain confidentiality; reactive screening test needs confirmation; viral load measures HIV RNA; p24 antigen assay; oral test uses oral transmucosal fluid and needs blood confirmation.HIV檢測：保密；篩檢陽性需確認；病毒量測HIV RNA；p24抗原檢驗；口腔試驗用口腔黏膜滲出液，陽性需血液確認。
  • Skin testing: stop systemic steroids/antihistamines 5 days prior as prescribed; consent; observe 30 min; anaphylaxis equipment ready; measure wheal and erythema (1+ about 0.5 cm to 4+ about 2.0 cm); record site, date, time.皮膚試驗：依醫囑前5天停用全身性類固醇/抗組織胺；同意書；觀察30分鐘；備妥過敏性休克急救設備；測量風團與紅暈（1+約0.5 cm至4+約2.0 cm）；記錄部位、日期、時間。

Hypersensitivity, anaphylaxis and latex allergy過敏、過敏性休克與乳膠過敏 📖 PDF p.1017 (book p.921), PDF p.1018 (book p.922), PDF p.1019 (book p.923)

  • Allergy: abnormal response to normally harmless substances, often on second exposure; manage with allergen identification, antihistamines, anti-inflammatories, corticosteroids, desensitization.過敏：對一般無害物質的異常反應，常於再次接觸時出現；處置包括辨識過敏原、抗組織胺、抗發炎藥、類固醇、減敏治療。
  • Anaphylaxis (type I, IgE-mediated): airway compromise, stridor, wheeze, hypotension, urticaria, angioedema, GI cramps, sense of doom.過敏性休克（第一型、IgE媒介）：呼吸道受阻、喘鳴、哮鳴、低血壓、蕁麻疹、血管性水腫、腹絞痛、瀕死感。
  • Anaphylaxis actions: assess airway/breathing, stay with client, call PHCP and rapid response, stop the suspect infusion, start IV normal saline, oxygen, raise HOB if BP normal, prepare epinephrine and diphenhydramine, intubation equipment ready.過敏性休克處置：評估呼吸道/呼吸、不離開病人、呼叫醫師與快速反應小組、停止可疑輸注、建立靜脈輸注生理食鹽水、給氧、血壓正常時抬高床頭、準備epinephrine與diphenhydramine、備妥插管設備。
  • Latex allergy: type I (anaphylaxis) or type IV delayed contact dermatitis (6-48 h); risk groups: health care workers, rubber industry, multiple surgeries, spina bifida, frequent glove users; cross-reactive foods: banana, kiwi, avocado, chestnut, tropical fruits.乳膠過敏：第一型（過敏性休克）或第四型遲發接觸性皮膚炎（6-48小時）；高風險：醫療人員、橡膠工業、多次手術、脊柱裂、常戴手套者；交叉反應食物：香蕉、奇異果、酪梨、栗子、熱帶水果。
  • Latex precautions: ask about allergy, nonlatex gloves, latex-safe cart, glass ampules/avoid rubber-stopper vials, cloth barrier under BP cuff, MedicAlert bracelet, epinephrine pen.乳膠防護：詢問過敏史、使用無乳膠手套、備乳膠安全物品車、使用玻璃安瓿/避免橡膠瓶塞、血壓計袖套下墊布、醫療警示手環、攜帶epinephrine筆。

Immunodeficiency and autoimmune disease免疫不全與自體免疫疾病 📖 PDF p.1019 (book p.923), PDF p.1020 (book p.924), PDF p.1021 (book p.925)

  • Immunodeficiency (primary or secondary): infection is the priority concern; strict asepsis, balanced diet, infection prevention teaching, MedicAlert.免疫不全（原發或續發）：感染為首要顧慮；嚴格無菌技術、均衡飲食、感染預防衛教、醫療警示。
  • SLE: malar (butterfly) rash, discoid rash, photosensitivity, oral ulcers, joint pain, fatigue, anemia; positive ANA, anti-dsDNA, high ESR/CRP; lupus nephritis early (proteinuria, red cell casts, monitor BUN/creatinine).SLE：蝶形紅斑、盤狀疹、光敏感、口腔潰瘍、關節痛、疲倦、貧血；ANA與anti-dsDNA陽性、ESR/CRP升高；狼瘡腎炎早期（蛋白尿、紅血球圓柱、監測BUN/肌酸酐）。
  • SLE care: avoid sun/UV, mild soap, pace activity, avoid hot baths and long inactivity, corticosteroids, NSAIDs, hydroxychloroquine, plasmapheresis, emotional support.SLE照護：避免日曬/紫外線、溫和肥皂、活動節奏、避免熱水澡與久不活動、類固醇、NSAID、hydroxychloroquine、血漿置換、情緒支持。
  • Scleroderma: taut shiny skin, pitting hand edema, dysphagia, contractures; small frequent meals, sit up 1-2 h after meals, constant room temperature, avoid fingersticks and cold extremes, no smoking, corticosteroids.硬皮症：皮膚緊繃光亮、手部凹陷性水腫、吞嚥困難、攣縮；少量多餐、餐後直坐1-2小時、室溫恆定、避免指尖採血與極端低溫、戒菸、類固醇。
  • Polyarteritis nodosa: systemic vasculitis; fever, severe abdominal pain, bloody diarrhea, high ESR; renal/cardiac involvement; treated like SLE.結節性多動脈炎：全身性血管炎；發燒、劇烈腹痛、血便、ESR升高；腎臟/心臟受累；治療類似SLE。
  • Pemphigus: autoimmune flaccid bullae, Nikolsky's sign, oral lesions, infection risk; corticosteroids, cytotoxics, antibiotics for secondary infection, oral hygiene.天疱瘡：自體免疫鬆弛性水泡、Nikolsky氏徵象、口腔病灶、感染風險；類固醇、細胞毒性藥物、續發感染用抗生素、口腔衛生。
  • Goodpasture's: antibodies against glomerular and alveolar basement membranes; hemoptysis, hematuria, dyspnea, edema, HTN; corticosteroids, plasmapheresis, azathioprine, stop smoking.Goodpasture氏症候群：針對腎絲球與肺泡基底膜的自體抗體；咳血、血尿、呼吸困難、水腫、高血壓；類固醇、血漿置換、azathioprine、戒菸。

Lyme disease萊姆病 📖 PDF p.1022 (book p.926)

  • Caused by Borrelia burgdorferi from tick bite; the ring-shaped rash (erythema migrans) does not always occur and may appear away from the bite.由蜱（壁蝨）叮咬傳播的Borrelia burgdorferi所致；環狀紅斑（移行性紅斑）不一定出現，可出現在非叮咬處。
  • Stage 1: red papule/ring rash, flu-like symptoms; stage 2 (weeks): joint pain, meningitis, facial palsy, cardiac dysrhythmias; stage 3: large-joint arthritis.第一期：紅丘疹/環狀紅斑、類流感症狀；第二期（數週）：關節痛、腦膜炎、顏面麻痺、心律不整；第三期：大關節關節炎。
  • Remove tick with tweezers close to the skin, pull steadily upward, clean with antiseptic; do not burn it. Blood test reliable 4-6 weeks after bite.以鑷子貼近皮膚夾住蜱並穩定向上拔除、以消毒劑清潔；勿燒灼。血液檢驗於叮咬後4-6週較可靠。
  • Treatment: doxycycline, cefuroxime or amoxicillin; erythromycin if penicillin-allergic; start promptly.治療：doxycycline、cefuroxime或amoxicillin；青黴素過敏者用erythromycin；及早開始。
  • Prevention: DEET repellent, long sleeves/pants, light-colored clothing, tuck pants into socks, permethrin on gear, tick checks, stay in trail center.預防：DEET驅蟲劑、長袖長褲、淺色衣物、褲管塞入襪內、裝備噴permethrin、檢查蜱蟲、走在步道中央。

HIV/AIDSHIV/愛滋病 📖 PDF p.1023 (book p.927), PDF p.1024 (book p.928)

  • HIV destroys CD4 T cells; AIDS is diagnosed with CD4 <200 or an AIDS-defining opportunistic infection/cancer; long incubation period; now a chronic illness.HIV破壞CD4 T細胞；CD4<200或出現AIDS定義之伺機性感染/癌症即診斷AIDS；潛伏期長；現為慢性疾病。
  • Findings: fever, malaise, weight loss, night sweats, lymphadenopathy >3 months, diarrhea, leukopenia, opportunistic infections.表現：發燒、倦怠、體重減輕、盜汗、淋巴結腫大>3個月、腹瀉、白血球低下、伺機性感染。
  • Opportunistic: Pneumocystis jiroveci pneumonia (major cause of death), candidiasis, CMV, herpes, TB, toxoplasmosis, PML; Kaposi's sarcoma, lymphomas, invasive cervical cancer.伺機性：肺囊蟲肺炎（主要死因）、念珠菌、CMV、疱疹、結核、弓漿蟲、進行性多灶性白質腦病；卡波西氏肉瘤、淋巴瘤、侵襲性子宮頸癌。
  • Labs: decreased CD4, reduced CD4:CD8 ratio, lymphopenia, thrombocytopenia, increased IgG/IgA, anergy; viral load measures therapy effectiveness.檢驗：CD4下降、CD4:CD8比值降低、淋巴球減少、血小板低下、IgG/IgA上升、無反應性；病毒量評估治療效果。
  • Nursing: respiratory support, standard precautions, protective measures when immunosuppressed, skin care, nutrition, fluid balance, psychosocial support, confidentiality.護理：呼吸支持、標準防護、免疫低下時保護措施、皮膚護理、營養、體液平衡、心理社會支持、保密。
  • Kaposi's sarcoma: raised purplish/red-brown lesions on skin and organs; radiation, chemotherapy, antivirals, keep lesions clean; use gown and gloves for bathing/linen if draining.卡波西氏肉瘤：皮膚與內臟紫紅/紅褐色隆起病灶；放射、化療、抗病毒藥、保持病灶清潔；有滲液時沐浴/換床單穿隔離衣與戴手套。

Posttransplantation immunodeficiency移植後免疫不全 📖 PDF p.1024 (book p.928), PDF p.1025 (book p.929)

  • Lifelong immunosuppressants prevent organ rejection but create secondary immunodeficiency.終生免疫抑制劑可預防器官排斥，但造成續發性免疫不全。
  • Monitor renal and hepatic function, CBC with differential, body secretions for blood; assess for opportunistic infection and rejection.監測腎肝功能、CBC與分類、體液是否有血；評估伺機性感染與排斥。
  • High-risk: history of malignancy, recent TB, herpes zoster or varicella exposure.高風險：惡性腫瘤病史、近期結核、帶狀疱疹或水痘暴露。
  • Use strict asepsis, protective isolation as needed, and teach infection and rejection signs plus medication adherence.嚴格無菌技術、必要時保護性隔離，並教導感染與排斥徵象及規則服藥。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Anaphylaxis: airway first, stay with client, stop the causative infusion, call rapid response, IV NS, oxygen, epinephrine, diphenhydramine; HOB up if BP normal.過敏性休克：先顧呼吸道、不離開病人、停止致敏輸注、呼叫快速反應、靜脈生理食鹽水、給氧、epinephrine、diphenhydramine；血壓正常時抬高床頭。 📖 PDF p.1018 (book p.922)
  2. Skin testing: observe 30 min with anaphylaxis equipment; hold steroids/antihistamines 5 days prior; measure (not estimate) wheal.皮膚試驗：觀察30分鐘並備急救設備；前5天停類固醇/抗組織胺；測量（非估計）風團。 📖 PDF p.1017 (book p.921)
  3. Latex allergy: nonlatex gloves, latex-safe cart, avoid rubber-stoppered vials; cross-reaction with banana, kiwi, avocado, chestnut.乳膠過敏：無乳膠手套、乳膠安全車、避免橡膠瓶塞藥瓶；與香蕉、奇異果、酪梨、栗子交叉反應。 📖 PDF p.1018 (book p.922), PDF p.1019 (book p.923)
  4. Immunodeficiency priority: protect from infection (strict asepsis).免疫不全首要：預防感染（嚴格無菌技術）。 📖 PDF p.1019 (book p.923)
  5. SLE: butterfly rash, photosensitivity, positive ANA and anti-dsDNA; monitor for lupus nephritis (BUN, creatinine, proteinuria).SLE：蝶形紅斑、光敏感、ANA與anti-dsDNA陽性；監測狼瘡腎炎（BUN、肌酸酐、蛋白尿）。 📖 PDF p.1020 (book p.924)
  6. SLE fatigue teaching: avoid hot baths and long inactivity, schedule exercise when not fatigued, conserve energy, avoid sun.SLE疲倦衛教：避免熱水澡與久不活動、不疲倦時運動、節省體力、避免日曬。 📖 PDF p.1020 (book p.924), PDF p.1026 (book p.930)
  7. Scleroderma: sit upright 1-2 h after meals, avoid fingersticks and cold, constant room temperature, corticosteroids.硬皮症：餐後直坐1-2小時、避免指尖採血與寒冷、室溫恆定、類固醇。 📖 PDF p.1021 (book p.925)
  8. Pemphigus: flaccid blisters, positive Nikolsky's sign; risk of infection; corticosteroids.天疱瘡：鬆弛性水泡、Nikolsky氏徵象陽性；感染風險；類固醇。 📖 PDF p.1021 (book p.925)
  9. Goodpasture's: lung plus kidney involvement (hemoptysis, hematuria); plasmapheresis; stop smoking.Goodpasture氏：肺與腎受累（咳血、血尿）；血漿置換；戒菸。 📖 PDF p.1021 (book p.925), PDF p.1022 (book p.926)
  10. Lyme disease: remove tick with tweezers, do not burn; test 4-6 weeks after bite; treat with doxycycline/cefuroxime/amoxicillin early.萊姆病：以鑷子拔蜱、勿燒灼；叮咬後4-6週檢驗；及早以doxycycline/cefuroxime/amoxicillin治療。 📖 PDF p.1022 (book p.926)
  11. CD4 normal 500-1600 cells/uL; <200 = AIDS criterion; viral load assesses treatment effectiveness.CD4正常500-1600 cells/µL；<200＝AIDS標準；病毒量評估治療效果。 📖 PDF p.1016 (book p.920), PDF p.1024 (book p.928)
  12. PCP (Pneumocystis jiroveci pneumonia) is a major cause of death in AIDS; Kaposi's sarcoma = purplish skin lesions, confirmed by punch biopsy.肺囊蟲肺炎為AIDS主要死因之一；卡波西氏肉瘤＝紫紅皮膚病灶、以穿刺切片確診。 📖 PDF p.1023 (book p.927), PDF p.1028 (book p.932)
  13. HIV confidentiality: positive tests need confirmation and state reporting per protocol.HIV保密：陽性結果需確認並依規定通報。 📖 PDF p.1016 (book p.920), PDF p.1017 (book p.921)
  14. Posttransplant: lifelong immunosuppression, strict asepsis, teach rejection and infection signs.移植後：終生免疫抑制、嚴格無菌、教導排斥與感染徵象。 📖 PDF p.1024 (book p.928), PDF p.1025 (book p.929)
  15. Draining Kaposi's lesions: gown and gloves for contact with drainage.卡波西氏病灶有滲液：接觸滲液時穿隔離衣並戴手套。 📖 PDF p.1025 (book p.929), PDF p.1027 (book p.931)

  • Type I hypersensitivity = IgE on mast cells, immediate histamine release; type IV = delayed T-cell (6-48 h), e.g., latex contact dermatitis.第一型過敏＝肥大細胞上IgE、立即釋放組織胺；第四型＝延遲T細胞反應（6-48小時），如乳膠接觸性皮膚炎。 📖 PDF p.1019 (book p.923)
  • CD4 ladder: >500 healthy, 200-499 problems, <200 AIDS.CD4階梯：>500健康、200-499出現問題、<200為AIDS。 📖 PDF p.1016 (book p.920)

8. What is the priority nursing intervention for a client with immunodeficiency?免疫不全病人的首要護理措施為何？
