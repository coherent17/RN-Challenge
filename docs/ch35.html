Pediatric strabismus, conjunctivitis, otitis media/externa and myringotomy, tonsillitis with tonsillectomy care, epistaxis and allergic rhinitis. Post-tonsillectomy bleeding recognition and ear/eye medication technique are frequent test points.小兒斜視、結膜炎、中耳炎/外耳炎與鼓膜切開術、扁桃腺炎與扁桃腺切除術照護、流鼻血與過敏性鼻炎。術後出血辨識與耳/眼藥使用技巧為常考重點。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Strabismus斜視

  • Eyes misaligned from extraocular muscle imbalance, congenital defect or poor vision; normal in young infants but not after about 4 months.眼外肌失衡、先天缺陷或視力不佳造成眼位不正；嬰兒早期屬正常，約 4 個月後不應再有。
  • Untreated causes amblyopia (reduced acuity) and permanent vision loss because the brain suppresses the misaligned eye.未治療會造成弱視（視力下降）與永久視力喪失，因大腦抑制偏斜眼。
  • Assess with six cardinal gaze positions, corneal light reflex, cover-uncover test.以六個基本注視方向、角膜光反射、遮蓋-移開測試評估。
  • Signs: crossed eyes, head tilting or closing one eye, impaired depth perception, headaches, diplopia.徵象：眼睛交叉、歪頭或閉一眼、深度知覺受損、頭痛、複視。
  • Treatment: glasses, patching the good eye, surgery if needed, follow-up visits.治療：配戴眼鏡、遮蓋好眼、必要時手術、定期追蹤。
p.495 · book 444

2Conjunctivitis結膜炎

  • Viral, bacterial or allergic; viral and bacterial forms are very contagious. Signs: itching, burning, redness, edema, discharge.病毒、細菌或過敏性；病毒與細菌性傳染力強。徵象：癢、灼熱、紅、水腫、分泌物。
  • Viral resolves in 7-14 days (antivirals only for herpes simplex or varicella zoster); antibiotics do not treat viruses.病毒性 7–14 天自癒（僅單純疱疹或水痘帶狀疱疹用抗病毒藥）；抗生素對病毒無效。
  • Bacterial: topical antibiotic drops/ointment shorten course and spread; allergic: remove allergen, antihistamine drops.細菌性：外用抗生素眼藥水/藥膏縮短病程與傳播；過敏性：去除過敏原、抗組織胺眼藥水。
  • Do not touch dropper tip to eye or lashes; handwashing; no sharing towels; no eye rubbing; discard contact lenses and eye makeup.滴管尖端勿碰眼睛或睫毛；勤洗手；不共用毛巾；不揉眼；丟棄隱形眼鏡與眼妝。
  • Chlamydial conjunctivitis in a child who is not sexually active requires evaluation for sexual abuse.未有性行為孩童出現披衣菌結膜炎，須評估是否遭性侵。
p.495 · book 444p.496 · book 445

3Otitis media中耳炎

  • Blocked eustachian tube; children's tubes are shorter, wider, straighter. Often follows RSV, influenza or group A strep infections.耳咽管阻塞；兒童耳咽管較短、寬、直。常繼發於 RSV、流感或 A 群鏈球菌感染。
  • Prevention: upright feeding, immunizations, breastfeeding for at least 6 months, avoid smoke and allergens.預防：直立餵食、按時接種、至少哺乳 6 個月、避免菸害與過敏原。
  • Signs: fever, ear pain, irritability, pulling the ear, head rolling, purulent drainage; red, opaque, bulging, immobile tympanic membrane.徵象：發燒、耳痛、易怒、拉耳朵、搖頭、膿性耳漏；鼓膜紅、混濁、膨出、不動。
  • Care: fluids, limit chewing, lie with affected ear down, heat or cold as prescribed, acetaminophen/ibuprofen, complete antibiotics; watchful waiting up to 72 hours may be used in healthy older infants.照護：補充水分、少咀嚼、患耳朝下側臥、依醫囑冷熱敷、乙醯胺酚/ibuprofen、完成抗生素；健康大嬰兒可觀察至 72 小時。
  • Screen for hearing loss; otitis externa (swimmer's ear): pain on pinna/tragus manipulation, topical antibiotics.須篩檢聽力；外耳炎（游泳者耳）：拉動耳廓/耳屏疼痛、外用抗生素。
  • Ear drops: under 3 years pull earlobe down and back; over 3 years pull pinna up and back.滴耳藥：3 歲以下耳垂向下向後拉；3 歲以上耳廓向上向後拉。
p.496 · book 445p.497 · book 446

4Myringotomy and tympanostomy tubes鼓膜切開與通氣管

  • Incision in tympanic membrane drains fluid; tubes equalize pressure and ventilate.鼓膜切開引流中耳積液；通氣管平衡壓力並通氣。
  • Keep ears dry; earplugs for bathing, shampooing, swimming; no diving or submerging.保持耳朵乾燥；洗澡洗頭游泳戴耳塞；不可潛水或浸入水中。
  • No nose blowing for 7-10 days; analgesics for discomfort.7–10 天內不可擤鼻；疼痛給止痛藥。
  • Tubes fall out in 6-12 months (small white spool-shaped); not an emergency but notify provider.通氣管 6–12 個月自行脫落（白色小捲軸狀）；非緊急但須通知醫師。
p.497 · book 446

5Tonsillitis and adenoiditis扁桃腺炎與腺樣體炎

  • Viral, bacterial (group A strep) or fungal; enlarged tonsils/adenoids cause snoring, mouth breathing, nasal speech, obstructive sleep apnea.病毒、細菌（A 群鏈球菌）或黴菌；扁桃腺/腺樣體腫大造成打鼾、口呼吸、鼻音、阻塞性睡眠呼吸中止。
  • Signs: sore throat, bright red tonsils with exudate, dysphagia, fever, bad breath, cough.徵象：喉嚨痛、扁桃腺鮮紅有滲出物、吞嚥困難、發燒、口臭、咳嗽。
  • Preop: check for active infection, bleeding/clotting studies (PT), loose teeth, prepare for sore throat.術前：檢查有無感染、出凝血檢查（PT）、鬆動牙齒、告知術後喉痛。
  • Surgery depends on infections per year and obstructive disturbance.手術依每年感染次數與呼吸阻塞程度決定。
p.497 · book 446

6Post-tonsillectomy care扁桃腺切除術後照護

  • Position prone or side-lying; suction equipment ready but do not suction unless obstruction.採俯臥或側臥；備抽吸設備但除非阻塞勿抽吸。
  • Bleeding signs: frequent swallowing or throat clearing; bright red vomit; turn child to side and notify provider.出血徵象：頻繁吞嚥或清喉嚨；嘔吐鮮紅血；側臥並通知醫師。
  • Avoid coughing, throat clearing, nose blowing; ice collar and analgesics; antiemetics.避免咳嗽、清喉嚨、擤鼻；冰領與止痛；止吐藥。
  • Clear, cool, noncitrus, noncarbonated fluids; avoid red/purple/brown liquids and milk products initially; no straws or sharp objects.給清澈、冰涼、非柑橘、非碳酸飲料；避免紅/紫/棕色液體與初期乳製品；不用吸管或尖銳物。
  • Mild fever, bad breath, ear pain for a few days; report bleeding, persistent earache or fever; normal activity in 1-2 weeks.數日內可有輕微發燒、口臭、耳痛；出血、持續耳痛或發燒須通報；1–2 週恢復活動。
p.497 · book 446p.498 · book 447

7Epistaxis and allergic rhinitis流鼻血與過敏性鼻炎

  • Epistaxis from trauma, foreign body, nose picking; recurrent/severe bleeding suggests underlying disease.鼻出血來自外傷、異物、挖鼻；反覆或嚴重出血提示潛在疾病。
  • First aid: sit up, lean forward, continuous pressure with thumb and finger at least 10 minutes, ice to bridge, ED if uncontrolled.急救：坐起前傾、拇指食指持續加壓至少 10 分鐘、鼻梁冰敷、無法止血送急診。
  • Allergic rhinitis: itchy watery eyes, clear drainage, dark circles, pale nasal mucosa, cobblestoning; test for allergies, eczema, asthma.過敏性鼻炎：眼癢流淚、清水鼻涕、黑眼圈、鼻黏膜蒼白、鵝卵石樣咽部；評估過敏、濕疹、氣喘。
  • Treat with trigger avoidance, antihistamines, nasal corticosteroids.治療：避開誘發物、抗組織胺、鼻用類固醇。
p.498 · book 447

Protect the airway first, then notify the provider.先保護呼吸道，再通知醫師。
