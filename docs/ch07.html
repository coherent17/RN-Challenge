This chapter covers health care delivery and insurance, nursing delivery models, leadership and management, quality improvement, change and conflict, the health care team, delegation and assignment, prioritizing care, and disaster response and triage. It is the core reference for prioritization and delegation questions.本章涵蓋醫療照護體系與保險、護理提供模式、領導與管理、品質改善、變革與衝突、醫療團隊、委派與指派、照護優先順序，以及災難應變與檢傷，是優先順序與委派題的核心參考。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Care delivery systems, insurance and critical pathways照護提供系統、保險與臨床路徑

  • Managed care controls costs through outcome-driven care, health promotion, early detection and efficient resource use; case management supports it with an interprofessional approach.管理式照護以成果導向、健康促進、早期發現與有效利用資源控制成本；個案管理以跨專業方式支持之。
  • Case manager (RN): assesses, plans, coordinates referrals and services, facilitates discharge, advocates for cost-effective care and knows insurance options.個案管理師（RN）：評估、計畫、協調轉介與服務、促進出院、倡導具成本效益的照護並熟悉保險。
  • Insurance types: HMO, PPO, EPO, POS, HDHP, HSA. Medicare: age 65+, some disabled, ESRD (Part A hospital/SNF/hospice, Part B outpatient 80%, Part C private plan, Part D drugs). Medicaid: joint federal-state for low income.保險類型：HMO、PPO、EPO、POS、HDHP、HSA。Medicare：65 歲以上、部分身障者、末期腎病（A 部分住院/SNF/安寧、B 部分門診 80%、C 部分私人計畫、D 部分藥物）。Medicaid：聯邦與州合辦的低收入保險。
  • Critical pathway: evidence-based, time-framed care plan; variation analysis compares actual to expected outcomes to spot negative variance early.臨床路徑：循證、設定時間的照護計畫；變異分析比較實際與預期結果，及早發現負向變異。
  • Care planning tools: nursing care plan, ADPIE, nursing process, SBAR, SOAP; NCSBN Clinical Judgment Measurement Model (NCJMM) cognitive skills: recognize cues, analyze cues, prioritize hypotheses, generate solutions, take action, evaluate outcomes.照護規劃工具：護理計畫、ADPIE、護理過程、SBAR、SOAP；NCSBN 臨床判斷測量模式（NCJMM）認知技能：辨識線索、分析線索、排列假設優先順序、產生解決方案、採取行動、評估結果。

2Nursing delivery systems護理提供模式

  • Functional nursing: task-oriented, efficient for many clients but fragmented with low accountability.功能制護理：以工作為導向，可照顧較多病人但照護片段且責任分散。
  • Team nursing: an RN team leader assesses, plans and evaluates; staff work within their scope (e.g., RN leading 2 LPNs and 3 APs for 12 clients).小組護理：RN 組長負責評估、計畫與評值；成員在各自範圍內工作（例：RN 帶 2 位 LPN 與 3 位 AP 照顧 12 位病人）。
  • Relationship-based (primary) nursing: one primary nurse manages care from admission to discharge, with an associate when off duty.關係導向（責任制）護理：由一位責任護理師從入院到出院負責，輪休時由輔助護理師接手。
  • Client-focused (total care/case method): the RN gives all needed care to assigned clients for the shift.以病人為中心（全責/個案制）：RN 在該班次提供指派病人所有所需照護。

3Accountability, leadership styles and managers責任、領導風格與管理者

  • Accountability: answerable for choices and actions; assume only responsibilities within competence; admit mistakes.責任：對選擇與行動負責；只承擔能力範圍內的職責；承認錯誤。
  • Leadership influences others to achieve goals; management accomplishes tasks through oneself or others.領導是影響他人達成目標；管理是自己或透過他人完成任務。
  • Styles: authoritarian (leader decides), democratic (participative), laissez-faire (passive), situational, bureaucratic (policies), transformational (shared vision), servant, transactional (reward-and-punishment, social exchange theory).風格：權威式（領導者決定）、民主式（參與）、放任式（被動）、情境式、官僚式（依規定）、轉型式（共同願景）、服務式、交易式（獎懲、社會交換理論）。
  • Effective leaders treat followers as individuals, communicate a vision and build trust, and are credible, persuasive critical thinkers who take initiative and appropriate risks.有效的領導者把追隨者視為獨立個體，傳達願景並建立信任，且具備可信度、說服力、批判思考、主動性與適度冒險的特質。
  • Management functions: planning, organizing, directing, controlling. Problem-solving steps parallel the nursing process and NCJMM.管理功能：計畫、組織、指導、控制。問題解決步驟與護理過程及 NCJMM 平行。
  • Managers: front-line (charge nurse, team leader), middle (unit manager, supervisor), nurse executive.管理者：第一線（組長、領導護理師）、中階（單位主任、督導）、護理主管。
  • Power types: reward, coercive, referent, expert, legitimate, personal, informational; empowerment enables others through open communication and shared decisions.權力類型：獎賞、強制、參照、專家、法定、個人、資訊；賦權透過開放溝通與共同決策使他人有能力。

4Organizations, evidence-based practice and quality improvement組織、實證護理與品質改善

  • Mission, goals, organizational chart; policies guide, procedures describe methods, protocols prescribe care for a specific problem. Centralized vs decentralized decision-making.使命、目標、組織圖；政策提供方向、程序說明方法、準則規範特定問題的照護。集權與分權決策。
  • Evidence-based practice integrates best research, clinical expertise and client preferences; nurses question practice and formulate researchable questions. Level I evidence (reviews of RCTs) is strongest.實證護理整合最佳研究、臨床專業與病人偏好；護理師質疑現行作法並提出可研究的問題。第 I 級證據（多個 RCT 回顧）最強。
  • Quality improvement monitors outcomes against standards; retrospective audits review charts after discharge, concurrent audits evaluate during care; peer review evaluates colleagues' care.品質改善依標準監測成果；回溯稽核在出院後查閱病歷，同步稽核於照護期間評估；同儕審查評估同事的護理品質。

5Change process and conflict變革歷程與衝突

  • Lewin's change model: unfreezing (identify problem, gather evidence), moving/changing (plan and implement), refreezing (stabilize).勒溫變革模式：解凍（辨識問題、蒐集證據）、移動/改變（規劃與執行）、再凍結（穩定）。
  • Resistance reasons: conformity, habit, fear, secondary gains, threats to basic needs, differing values. Overcome by involving everyone, stressing benefits, evaluating progress and giving feedback; confront resistant staff to elicit feelings.抗拒原因：從眾、習慣、恐懼、次級獲益、基本需求受威脅、價值差異。克服方法：全員參與、強調益處、持續評值與回饋；面質抗拒者以引導說出感受。
  • Conflict types: intrapersonal, interpersonal, organizational. Modes: avoidance, accommodation, competition, compromise (assertive and cooperative).衝突類型：個人內在、人際、組織。處理方式：迴避、順應、競爭、妥協（堅定且合作）。
  • Change can be planned (a deliberate effort to improve a situation) or unplanned (a reactive response to a disruption).變革可分為計畫性變革（有意改善現況的努力）與非計畫性變革（因突發狀況而產生的被動回應）。

6Health care team and interprofessional collaboration醫療團隊與跨專業合作

  • Team roles: PHCP, PA, NP/APRN, PT, OT, respiratory therapist, speech therapist (swallowing), dietitian, continuing care nurse, pharmacist, social worker, chaplain, AP.團隊角色：醫師、PA、NP/APRN、物理治療師、職能治療師、呼吸治療師、語言治療師（吞嚥）、營養師、延續照護護理師、藥師、社工、牧靈人員、助理。
  • Reports: factual, accurate, complete, organized; change-of-shift reports may be at the bedside; transfer reports include name, diagnosis, status, needs, allergies, resuscitation status.報告：事實、準確、完整、有條理；交班可在床邊；轉送報告含姓名、診斷、狀況、需求、過敏與急救狀態。
  • SBAR: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation. Telephone reports should be documented (when, who, what).SBAR：情況、背景、評估、建議。電話報告須記錄（時間、對象、內容）。
  • Consultation is sought when the problem exceeds nursing resources; rapid-response teams respond to early deterioration on non-ICU units to prevent arrest.問題超出護理資源時尋求會診；快速反應小組處理非加護單位早期惡化以預防心肺停止。
  • Medication reconciliation: compare current and new medication lists at transitions, resolve discrepancies, communicate the final list.藥物整合：在轉換照護時比對現用與新藥單、解決差異並傳達最終清單。
  • Discharge planning begins at admission; teach with teach-back about medications, diet, activity, complications, equipment, resources and follow-up.出院準備自入院即開始；以回述教導藥物、飲食、活動、併發症、設備、資源與追蹤。

7Delegation, assignments and time management委派、指派與時間管理

  • Delegation transfers a task to a competent person; the nurse keeps accountability. Five rights: task, circumstances, person, direction/communication, supervision/evaluation.委派是將任務交給有能力者；護理師仍負責任。五項正確：任務、情境、人員、指示/溝通、督導/評值。
  • Cannot delegate nursing judgment or critical decision making (assessment, teaching initiation, planning, evaluation).不可委派護理判斷或關鍵決策（評估、起始衛教、計畫、評值）。
  • AP: noninvasive tasks for stable clients. LPN/VN: stable clients, dressings, suctioning, catheterization, oral/SC/IM meds. RN: stable and unstable clients, IV meds, initial assessment and teaching.AP：穩定病人的非侵入性工作。LPN/VN：穩定病人、換藥、抽吸、導尿、口服/皮下/肌肉藥。RN：穩定與不穩定病人、靜脈給藥、初始評估與衛教。
  • Give clear directions, validate understanding, give feedback; match task to the nurse practice act and job description.指示清楚、確認理解、給予回饋；依護理執業法與職務說明配對任務。
  • LPN/VN may collect data and reinforce teaching plans the RN already initiated, but only the RN analyzes data (assessment) and initiates teaching.LPN/VN 可收集資料並複習 RN 已起始的衛教計畫，但僅 RN 可分析資料（評估）與起始衛教。
  • Time management: list tasks, prioritize, begin rounds early, delegate, document concurrently, evaluate the day.時間管理：列出任務、排序、早班巡視、委派、同步記錄、評估當日。

8Prioritizing care排定照護優先順序

  • Priorities are high (life-threatening), intermediate (nonemergency) or low (unrelated to illness); the client's own priorities count.優先順序分高（危及生命）、中（非緊急）、低（與疾病無直接關係）；病人自己的優先事項也要考量。
  • Use ABCs, Maslow and nursing process (assessment first); CPR uses CAB; actual problems before potential ones; act on critically abnormal values.使用 ABC、馬斯洛、護理過程（評估優先）；CPR 用 CAB；現存問題先於潛在問題；對嚴重異常值立即處理。
  • Group of clients: heart failure with 4-lb gain and dyspnea first, then fresh chest tube post-op, then absent bowel sounds, then pre-op teaching.多位病人：心衰竭體重增加 4 磅且呼吸喘者優先，其次術後胸管，其次腸音消失，最後術前衛教。
  • Teaching priorities: immediate needs, client's perceived needs, anxiety level, time available.衛教優先：立即需求、病人認為重要的事、焦慮程度、可用時間。

9Disasters and emergency response災難與緊急應變

  • Internal disasters occur within the facility; external outside it; a mass casualty event exceeds hospital resources. Activate the emergency response plan immediately.內部災難發生於機構內；外部災難發生於機構外；大規模傷亡超出醫院資源。立即啟動緊急應變計畫。
  • FEMA phases: mitigation, preparedness, response, recovery; three disaster levels (I massive to III minor). ARC provides free relief, shelters and crisis counseling; HAZMAT teams handle exposures.FEMA 階段：減災、整備、應變、復原；災難三級（I 級重大至 III 級輕微）。紅十字會提供免費救援、庇護與危機諮商；HAZMAT 處理暴露事件。
  • Triage: START tags in 30-60 seconds; ED triage emergent (red, priority 1), urgent (yellow, priority 2), nonurgent (green, priority 3); black for expected deaths. Think survivability.檢傷：START 於 30-60 秒內分類；急診緊急（紅、第 1 優先）、次緊急（黃、第 2 優先）、非緊急（綠、第 3 優先）；黑色為預期死亡。思考可存活性。
  • Primary assessment (ABCs, check central pulse 5-10 seconds, agonal breathing needs ventilation) precedes secondary assessment (history, vital signs, neuro, pain, head-to-toe).初級評估（ABC、中心脈搏檢查 5-10 秒、瀕死喘息需通氣）先於次級評估（病史、生命徵象、神經、疼痛、頭到腳）。
  • Personal preparedness: 3-day supply of water (1 gallon per person per day) and food, medications, radio, flashlight, documents; replace water every 3 months and food every 6 months.個人整備：三天份飲水（每人每日 1 加侖）與食物、藥物、收音機、手電筒、證件；水每 3 個月、食物每 6 個月更換。

Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Change process and conflict變革歷程與衝突
  • Lewin's change model: unfreezing (identify problem, gather evidence), moving/changing (plan and implement), refreezing (stabilize).勒溫變革模式：解凍（辨識問題、蒐集證據）、移動/改變（規劃與執行）、再凍結（穩定）。
  • Resistance reasons: conformity, habit, fear, secondary gains, threats to basic needs, differing values. Overcome by involving everyone, stressing benefits, evaluating progress and giving feedback; confront resistant staff to elicit feelings.抗拒原因：從眾、習慣、恐懼、次級獲益、基本需求受威脅、價值差異。克服方法：全員參與、強調益處、持續評值與回饋；面質抗拒者以引導說出感受。
  • Conflict types: intrapersonal, interpersonal, organizational. Modes: avoidance, accommodation, competition, compromise (assertive and cooperative).衝突類型：個人內在、人際、組織。處理方式：迴避、順應、競爭、妥協（堅定且合作）。
  • Change can be planned (a deliberate effort to improve a situation) or unplanned (a reactive response to a disruption).變革可分為計畫性變革（有意改善現況的努力）與非計畫性變革（因突發狀況而產生的被動回應）。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Health care team and interprofessional collaboration醫療團隊與跨專業合作
  • Team roles: PHCP, PA, NP/APRN, PT, OT, respiratory therapist, speech therapist (swallowing), dietitian, continuing care nurse, pharmacist, social worker, chaplain, AP.團隊角色：醫師、PA、NP/APRN、物理治療師、職能治療師、呼吸治療師、語言治療師（吞嚥）、營養師、延續照護護理師、藥師、社工、牧靈人員、助理。
  • Reports: factual, accurate, complete, organized; change-of-shift reports may be at the bedside; transfer reports include name, diagnosis, status, needs, allergies, resuscitation status.報告：事實、準確、完整、有條理；交班可在床邊；轉送報告含姓名、診斷、狀況、需求、過敏與急救狀態。
  • SBAR: Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation. Telephone reports should be documented (when, who, what).SBAR：情況、背景、評估、建議。電話報告須記錄（時間、對象、內容）。
  • Consultation is sought when the problem exceeds nursing resources; rapid-response teams respond to early deterioration on non-ICU units to prevent arrest.問題超出護理資源時尋求會診；快速反應小組處理非加護單位早期惡化以預防心肺停止。
  • Medication reconciliation: compare current and new medication lists at transitions, resolve discrepancies, communicate the final list.藥物整合：在轉換照護時比對現用與新藥單、解決差異並傳達最終清單。
  • Discharge planning begins at admission; teach with teach-back about medications, diet, activity, complications, equipment, resources and follow-up.出院準備自入院即開始；以回述教導藥物、飲食、活動、併發症、設備、資源與追蹤。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Delegation, assignments and time management委派、指派與時間管理
  • Delegation transfers a task to a competent person; the nurse keeps accountability. Five rights: task, circumstances, person, direction/communication, supervision/evaluation.委派是將任務交給有能力者；護理師仍負責任。五項正確：任務、情境、人員、指示/溝通、督導/評值。
  • Cannot delegate nursing judgment or critical decision making (assessment, teaching initiation, planning, evaluation).不可委派護理判斷或關鍵決策（評估、起始衛教、計畫、評值）。
  • AP: noninvasive tasks for stable clients. LPN/VN: stable clients, dressings, suctioning, catheterization, oral/SC/IM meds. RN: stable and unstable clients, IV meds, initial assessment and teaching.AP：穩定病人的非侵入性工作。LPN/VN：穩定病人、換藥、抽吸、導尿、口服/皮下/肌肉藥。RN：穩定與不穩定病人、靜脈給藥、初始評估與衛教。
  • Give clear directions, validate understanding, give feedback; match task to the nurse practice act and job description.指示清楚、確認理解、給予回饋；依護理執業法與職務說明配對任務。
  • LPN/VN may collect data and reinforce teaching plans the RN already initiated, but only the RN analyzes data (assessment) and initiates teaching.LPN/VN 可收集資料並複習 RN 已起始的衛教計畫，但僅 RN 可分析資料（評估）與起始衛教。
  • Time management: list tasks, prioritize, begin rounds early, delegate, document concurrently, evaluate the day.時間管理：列出任務、排序、早班巡視、委派、同步記錄、評估當日。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Prioritizing care排定照護優先順序
  • Priorities are high (life-threatening), intermediate (nonemergency) or low (unrelated to illness); the client's own priorities count.優先順序分高（危及生命）、中（非緊急）、低（與疾病無直接關係）；病人自己的優先事項也要考量。
  • Use ABCs, Maslow and nursing process (assessment first); CPR uses CAB; actual problems before potential ones; act on critically abnormal values.使用 ABC、馬斯洛、護理過程（評估優先）；CPR 用 CAB；現存問題先於潛在問題；對嚴重異常值立即處理。
  • Group of clients: heart failure with 4-lb gain and dyspnea first, then fresh chest tube post-op, then absent bowel sounds, then pre-op teaching.多位病人：心衰竭體重增加 4 磅且呼吸喘者優先，其次術後胸管，其次腸音消失，最後術前衛教。
  • Teaching priorities: immediate needs, client's perceived needs, anxiety level, time available.衛教優先：立即需求、病人認為重要的事、焦慮程度、可用時間。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
Disasters and emergency response災難與緊急應變
  • Internal disasters occur within the facility; external outside it; a mass casualty event exceeds hospital resources. Activate the emergency response plan immediately.內部災難發生於機構內；外部災難發生於機構外；大規模傷亡超出醫院資源。立即啟動緊急應變計畫。
  • FEMA phases: mitigation, preparedness, response, recovery; three disaster levels (I massive to III minor). ARC provides free relief, shelters and crisis counseling; HAZMAT teams handle exposures.FEMA 階段：減災、整備、應變、復原；災難三級（I 級重大至 III 級輕微）。紅十字會提供免費救援、庇護與危機諮商；HAZMAT 處理暴露事件。
  • Triage: START tags in 30-60 seconds; ED triage emergent (red, priority 1), urgent (yellow, priority 2), nonurgent (green, priority 3); black for expected deaths. Think survivability.檢傷：START 於 30-60 秒內分類；急診緊急（紅、第 1 優先）、次緊急（黃、第 2 優先）、非緊急（綠、第 3 優先）；黑色為預期死亡。思考可存活性。
  • Primary assessment (ABCs, check central pulse 5-10 seconds, agonal breathing needs ventilation) precedes secondary assessment (history, vital signs, neuro, pain, head-to-toe).初級評估（ABC、中心脈搏檢查 5-10 秒、瀕死喘息需通氣）先於次級評估（病史、生命徵象、神經、疼痛、頭到腳）。
  • Personal preparedness: 3-day supply of water (1 gallon per person per day) and food, medications, radio, flashlight, documents; replace water every 3 months and food every 6 months.個人整備：三天份飲水（每人每日 1 加侖）與食物、藥物、收音機、手電筒、證件；水每 3 個月、食物每 6 個月更換。
