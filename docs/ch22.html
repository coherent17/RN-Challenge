Summary重點摘要Test-critical考試關鍵Mnemonics記憶口訣

This chapter covers dating the pregnancy (Naegele's rule, GTPAL), signs of pregnancy, fundal height, physiological and psychological changes, common discomforts and their remedies, risk factors, and antepartum tests such as Rh screening, GCT, ultrasound, amniocentesis, NST and CST, plus prenatal nutrition.本章涵蓋妊娠日期推算（Naegele 法則、GTPAL）、懷孕徵象、子宮底高度、生理與心理變化、常見不適與緩解方式、風險因子、產前檢查（Rh 篩檢、GCT、超音波、羊膜穿刺、NST、CST），以及孕期營養。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Gestation, Naegele's Rule, Gravidity and Parity (GTPAL)妊娠、Naegele 法則、懷孕與生產史（GTPAL）

  • Gestation lasts about 280 days.妊娠期約 280 天。
  • Naegele's rule (regular 28-day cycle): first day of LMP minus 3 months plus 7 days (plus 1 year if needed). LMP Oct 19, 2023 gives July 26, 2024.Naegele 法則（月經週期規則 28 天）：末次月經第一天減 3 個月加 7 天（必要時加 1 年）。LMP 2023/10/19 → 2024/7/26。
  • Gravidity counts all pregnancies including the current one; parity counts births after 20 weeks, not fetuses.Gravidity 為包含本次的懷孕次數；parity 為 20 週後的生產次數，不論胎數。
  • GTPAL: Gravida, Term (over 37 wk), Preterm, Abortions (before 20 wk), Living children. Twins count once for G, T or P but twice for L.GTPAL：懷孕次數、足月產（超過 37 週）、早產、流產（20 週前）、現存子女數。雙胞胎在 G、T、P 算 1，在 L 算 2。

2Signs of Pregnancy懷孕徵象

  • Presumptive: amenorrhea, nausea/vomiting, breast changes, urinary frequency, quickening (16-20 wk), fatigue, linea nigra, melasma.推定徵象：停經、噁心嘔吐、乳房變化、頻尿、胎動感（16-20 週）、疲倦、黑線、黃褐斑。
  • Probable: uterine enlargement, Hegar's, Goodell's and Chadwick's signs, ballottement, Braxton Hicks contractions, positive hCG test.可能徵象：子宮變大、Hegar、Goodell、Chadwick 徵象、浮球感、Braxton Hicks 收縮、hCG 陽性。
  • Positive (diagnostic): FHR by Doppler at 10-12 wk or fetoscope at 20 wk, palpable active fetal movement, fetal outline on ultrasound or radiograph.確定徵象：10-12 週都卜勒或 20 週胎心聽診器聽到胎心、檢查者觸及胎動、超音波或 X 光見胎兒輪廓。

3Fundal Height and Physiological Changes子宮底高度與生理變化

  • Weeks 18-30: fundal height in cm approximately equals weeks of gestation, plus or minus 2 cm. 16 wk halfway between symphysis and umbilicus; 20-22 wk at umbilicus; 36 wk at xiphoid. Have client void first.18-30 週：子宮底高度（公分）約等於週數 ±2。16 週在恥骨聯合與肚臍中間；20-22 週在肚臍；36 週在劍突。測量前先排尿。
  • Cardiovascular: blood volume up 40-50%, physiological anemia, higher iron need, pulse up 10-15 bpm, BP dips in second trimester.心血管：血容積增加 40-50%、生理性貧血、鐵需求增加、脈搏增加 10-15 次/分、第二孕期血壓略降。
  • Respiratory: oxygen consumption up 15-20%, diaphragm elevated, dyspnea possible.呼吸：耗氧量增加 15-20%、橫膈上升、可能呼吸困難。
  • GI: nausea from hCG, constipation, heartburn from progesterone slowing motility, hemorrhoids, gum swelling and ptyalism from estrogen.消化：hCG 造成噁心；黃體素使蠕動變慢造成便秘、火燒心；痔瘡；雌激素造成牙齦腫脹與流涎。
  • Renal: urinary frequency in 1st and 3rd trimesters; reduced renal glucose threshold so glycosuria may appear.腎臟：第一與第三孕期頻尿；腎糖閾下降故可能出現糖尿。
  • Endocrine: higher BMR, prolactin and oxytocin, more insulin production. Uterus grows from about 60 g to 1000 g.內分泌：基礎代謝率上升、泌乳素與催產素、胰島素分泌增加。子宮由約 60 公克增至 1000 公克。
  • Cervix softens (Chadwick sign, violet) and forms a mucous plug; breasts enlarge, colostrum may leak; skin shows linea nigra, chloasma, striae.子宮頸變軟（Chadwick 徵象、紫藍色）並形成黏液栓；乳房變大、可能漏初乳；皮膚出現黑線、黃褐斑、妊娠紋。
  • Musculoskeletal: relaxin and progesterone loosen joints, lordosis, waddling gait, fall risk; eyes may have corneal edema.肌肉骨骼：relaxin 與黃體素使關節鬆弛、腰椎前凸、鴨步、易跌倒；眼睛可能角膜水腫。

4Psychological Changes心理變化

  • Early ambivalence is normal even in planned pregnancy; partner may be ambivalent too.即使是計畫中的懷孕，早期矛盾感也屬正常；伴侶亦可能如此。
  • Tasks: accept pregnancy, identify with parent role, solidify partner relationship, bond with the fetus, prepare for birth.任務：接受懷孕、認同父母角色、鞏固伴侶關係、與胎兒建立連結、為生產做準備。
  • Emotional lability and body image changes are common; bonding progresses from accepting pregnancy, to accepting the fetus as separate, to preparing for birth.情緒不穩與身體形象改變很常見；連結歷程為接受懷孕、接受胎兒為獨立個體、實際準備生產。

5Discomforts of Pregnancy and Interventions孕期不適與處置

  • Nausea: dry crackers before rising, small frequent low-fat meals, fluids between meals, avoid fried/spicy food, protein bedtime snack.噁心：起床前吃蘇打餅乾、少量多餐低脂、兩餐間喝水、避免油炸辛辣、睡前吃蛋白質點心。
  • Supine hypotension: avoid lying flat in 2nd/3rd trimester (uterus compresses inferior vena cava); side-lying, change positions slowly.仰臥低血壓：第二、三孕期避免平躺（子宮壓迫下腔靜脈）；採側臥並緩慢變換姿勢。
  • Urinary frequency: at least 2000 mL fluid by day, limit evening fluids and diuretic foods, Kegels.頻尿：白天至少飲水 2000 mL，傍晚減少並避免利尿食物，做凱格爾運動。
  • Heartburn: small meals, upright 30 min after eating, avoid fatty/spicy food; ask provider about antacids.火燒心：少量多餐、飯後坐直 30 分鐘、避免油膩辛辣；詢問醫師可否用制酸劑。
  • Ankle edema and varicose veins: elevate legs, side-lying, support hose, avoid prolonged standing or crossing legs.腳踝水腫與靜脈曲張：抬高腿、側臥、穿彈性襪、避免久站久坐或蹺腳。
  • Constipation and hemorrhoids: high fiber, 2000 mL fluids, walking, sitz bath; consult provider for stool softeners.便秘與痔瘡：高纖、2000 mL 水分、散步、溫水坐浴；用軟便劑前諮詢醫師。
  • Backache: pelvic tilt, good posture, squat to lift, low-heeled shoes. Leg cramps: dorsiflex the foot, increase calcium, walk.背痛：骨盆傾斜運動、良好姿勢、蹲下拿物、穿低跟鞋。腿抽筋：足背屈、增加鈣質、散步。
  • Increased thin vaginal discharge (leukorrhea) is normal; use cotton underwear, no douching or tampons. Nasal stuffiness: humidifier, avoid decongestant sprays unless approved.陰道分泌物增加（白帶）正常；穿棉質內褲，不灌洗、不用衛生棉條。鼻塞：用加濕器，未經許可避免鼻噴劑。
  • Headaches are usually benign in first trimester but need evaluation later; acetaminophen only if prescribed.頭痛在第一孕期多屬良性，後期需進一步評估；僅在處方下使用乙醯胺酚。

6Pregnancy Risk Factors妊娠風險因子

  • Age under 20 or over 35 raises perinatal risk; adolescent risks include poor nutrition, hypertension, anemia, CPD, preterm and low-birth-weight infants, and lack of support.年齡小於 20 或大於 35 歲增加風險；青少年懷孕風險包括營養不良、高血壓、貧血、頭骨盆不對稱、早產與低出生體重、缺乏支持。
  • Folic acid for all people of childbearing age prevents neural tube defects and orofacial clefts.所有育齡者應補充葉酸，預防神經管缺陷與唇顎裂。
  • Rubella in the first 8 weeks has highest fetal infection and anomaly rate.前 8 週感染德國麻疹胎兒感染與畸形率最高。
  • Abuse raises risk of abruptio placentae and preterm birth; interview the client alone and examine breasts, abdomen, genitals.暴力增加胎盤早期剝離與早產風險；應單獨訪談個案，並檢查乳房、腹部與生殖器。
  • HIV: perinatal zidovudine lowers transmission. Substance use: growth restriction, abruptio, fetal bradycardia; smoking causes low birth weight and stillbirth; alcohol causes fetal alcohol syndrome.HIV：周產期給予 zidovudine 可降低傳染。物質濫用：胎兒生長受限、胎盤早剝、胎心過慢；吸菸造成低出生體重與死產；酒精造成胎兒酒精症候群。

7Antepartum Laboratory Tests產前實驗室檢查

  • Visit schedule: every 4 weeks to 28-32 wk, every 2 weeks 32-36 wk, weekly 36-40 wk.產檢頻率：28-32 週前每 4 週、32-36 週每 2 週、36-40 週每週。
  • Rh-negative with negative antibody screen: RhoGAM at 28 weeks and within 72 hr after birth of an Rh-positive infant; also after miscarriage/termination and CVS.Rh 陰性且抗體篩檢陰性：28 週給 RhoGAM，Rh 陽性新生兒出生後 72 小時內再給；流產、人工流產及 CVS 後亦給。
  • Rubella titer under 1:8: give live vaccine subcutaneously postpartum; avoid pregnancy 1-3 months and contact with immunocompromised people; ask about egg allergy; never give during pregnancy.德國麻疹抗體價低於 1:8：產後皮下注射活性疫苗；1-3 個月內避免懷孕並避免接觸免疫低下者；詢問蛋過敏；孕期不可施打。
  • Anemia in pregnancy: Hgb under 10 g/dL or Hct under 30%. WBC mildly elevated is normal.孕期貧血：Hgb 低於 10 g/dL 或 Hct 低於 30%。白血球輕度上升屬正常。
  • GCT at 24-28 weeks: 50 g glucose, 1-hour level over 140 mg/dL is positive and needs the 3-hour test.GCT 於 24-28 週：口服 50 g 葡萄糖，1 小時血糖超過 140 mg/dL 為陽性，需做 3 小時試驗。
  • Urine at each visit for glucose and protein; 2+ to 4+ protein suggests infection or preeclampsia.每次產檢驗尿糖與尿蛋白；蛋白 2+ 至 4+ 提示感染或子癇前症。
  • Hepatitis B antigen testing for all; hepatitis B vaccine is not contraindicated in pregnancy. TB: positive skin test needs chest X-ray with shield only after 20 weeks.所有孕婦篩檢 B 肝表面抗原；B 肝疫苗孕期不禁忌。結核菌素陽性者需於 20 週後以鉛屏護腹拍胸部 X 光。
  • GBS vaginal/rectal swab at about 36 weeks (20-25% colonized).約 36 週採陰道/直腸 GBS 培養（20-25% 帶菌）。

8Antepartum Fetal Assessment and Procedures產前胎兒評估與侵入性檢查

  • Ultrasound: no known risk; abdominal scan needs a full bladder; transvaginal in first trimester checks cervix length.超音波：無已知風險；腹部超音波需憋尿；第一孕期經陰道超音波可測子宮頸長度。
  • Biophysical profile: breathing, movement, tone, amniotic fluid index, NST. Doppler studies placental and fetal blood flow.生物物理評估：呼吸、動作、張力、羊水指數、NST。都卜勒評估胎盤與胎兒血流。
  • Quad screen (AFP at 16-18 wk) screens for Down syndrome and neural tube defects; cell-free DNA blood test from 7 weeks screens trisomy 21, 18, 13.四指標篩檢（16-18 週 AFP）篩檢唐氏症與神經管缺陷；7 週起可做游離 DNA 抽血篩檢 21、18、13 三染色體。
  • CVS at 10-13 wk (genetic diagnosis, give RhoGAM if Rh-negative, rest 24 hr). Amniocentesis at 15-20 wk (full bladder before 20 wk, empty after; consent; FHR monitoring; left side after).CVS 於 10-13 週（基因診斷，Rh 陰性給 RhoGAM，休息 24 小時）。羊膜穿刺於 15-20 週（20 週前憋尿、之後排空；需同意書；監測胎心；術後左側臥）。
  • After CVS or amniocentesis report fever over 100.4 F (38 C), chills, bleeding, fluid leak, decreased fetal movement, contractions or cramping.CVS 或羊膜穿刺後，若發燒超過 38 度（100.4 F）、寒顫、出血、漏液、胎動減少、宮縮或痙攣應通報。
  • Kick counts from 28 weeks: sitting or side-lying, at least 10 movements in 2 hours.28 週起數胎動：坐或側臥，2 小時至少 10 次。
  • Ruptured membranes: fern test shows ferning; nitrazine paper turns blue (pH 7.0-7.5) versus vaginal pH 4.5-5.5. Fetal fibronectin at 22-34 wk predicts preterm labor (negative is reassuring).胎膜破裂：羊齒葉試驗呈羊齒狀；硝氮試紙變藍（pH 7.0-7.5，陰道 pH 4.5-5.5）。22-34 週胎兒纖維連接蛋白預測早產（陰性較安心）。

9NST, CST and Prenatal NutritionNST、CST 與孕期營養

  • Reactive NST (normal): 2 or more accelerations of at least 15 bpm lasting at least 15 sec within 20 min with movement. Nonreactive: none in 40 min.反應型 NST（正常）：20 分鐘內有 2 次以上、至少上升 15 bpm 且持續 15 秒以上的加速。無反應型：40 分鐘內無。
  • Position side-lying for NST/CST to avoid vena cava compression.NST/CST 採側臥避免壓迫下腔靜脈。
  • CST: negative (normal) = no late decelerations; positive (abnormal) = late decelerations with 50% or more of contractions; 3 contractions of 40 sec in 10 min required.CST：陰性（正常）＝無晚期減速；陽性（異常）＝50% 以上宮縮出現晚期減速；需 10 分鐘內 3 次、每次 40 秒以上宮縮。
  • Weight gain 25-35 lb (11-16 kg) for normal prepregnancy weight; +300 kcal/day in pregnancy, +500 during lactation; fluids 8-10 glasses; sodium not restricted.體重增加：孕前體重正常者 11-16 公斤（25-35 磅）；孕期每日加 300 大卡、哺乳加 500 大卡；水分 8-10 杯；不限鈉。
  • Vegetarian risks: B12, iron, zinc, calcium, vitamin D, omega-3; pair iron with vitamin C. Lactose intolerance: nondairy calcium, lactase.素食風險：B12、鐵、鋅、鈣、維生素 D、omega-3；鐵搭配維生素 C。乳糖不耐：非乳製鈣來源、乳糖酶。
  • Pica (eating dirt, clay, starch) can cause iron-deficiency anemia.異食癖（吃泥土、黏土、澱粉）可造成缺鐵性貧血。

  • Emotional lability and body image changes are common; bonding progresses from accepting pregnancy, to accepting the fetus as separate, to preparing for birth.情緒不穩與身體形象改變很常見；連結歷程為接受懷孕、接受胎兒為獨立個體、實際準備生產。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Discomforts of Pregnancy and Interventions孕期不適與處置
  • Nausea: dry crackers before rising, small frequent low-fat meals, fluids between meals, avoid fried/spicy food, protein bedtime snack.噁心：起床前吃蘇打餅乾、少量多餐低脂、兩餐間喝水、避免油炸辛辣、睡前吃蛋白質點心。
  • Supine hypotension: avoid lying flat in 2nd/3rd trimester (uterus compresses inferior vena cava); side-lying, change positions slowly.仰臥低血壓：第二、三孕期避免平躺（子宮壓迫下腔靜脈）；採側臥並緩慢變換姿勢。
  • Urinary frequency: at least 2000 mL fluid by day, limit evening fluids and diuretic foods, Kegels.頻尿：白天至少飲水 2000 mL，傍晚減少並避免利尿食物，做凱格爾運動。
  • Heartburn: small meals, upright 30 min after eating, avoid fatty/spicy food; ask provider about antacids.火燒心：少量多餐、飯後坐直 30 分鐘、避免油膩辛辣；詢問醫師可否用制酸劑。
  • Ankle edema and varicose veins: elevate legs, side-lying, support hose, avoid prolonged standing or crossing legs.腳踝水腫與靜脈曲張：抬高腿、側臥、穿彈性襪、避免久站久坐或蹺腳。
  • Constipation and hemorrhoids: high fiber, 2000 mL fluids, walking, sitz bath; consult provider for stool softeners.便秘與痔瘡：高纖、2000 mL 水分、散步、溫水坐浴；用軟便劑前諮詢醫師。
  • Backache: pelvic tilt, good posture, squat to lift, low-heeled shoes. Leg cramps: dorsiflex the foot, increase calcium, walk.背痛：骨盆傾斜運動、良好姿勢、蹲下拿物、穿低跟鞋。腿抽筋：足背屈、增加鈣質、散步。
  • Increased thin vaginal discharge (leukorrhea) is normal; use cotton underwear, no douching or tampons. Nasal stuffiness: humidifier, avoid decongestant sprays unless approved.陰道分泌物增加（白帶）正常；穿棉質內褲，不灌洗、不用衛生棉條。鼻塞：用加濕器，未經許可避免鼻噴劑。
  • Headaches are usually benign in first trimester but need evaluation later; acetaminophen only if prescribed.頭痛在第一孕期多屬良性，後期需進一步評估；僅在處方下使用乙醯胺酚。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Pregnancy Risk Factors妊娠風險因子
  • Age under 20 or over 35 raises perinatal risk; adolescent risks include poor nutrition, hypertension, anemia, CPD, preterm and low-birth-weight infants, and lack of support.年齡小於 20 或大於 35 歲增加風險；青少年懷孕風險包括營養不良、高血壓、貧血、頭骨盆不對稱、早產與低出生體重、缺乏支持。
  • Folic acid for all people of childbearing age prevents neural tube defects and orofacial clefts.所有育齡者應補充葉酸，預防神經管缺陷與唇顎裂。
  • Rubella in the first 8 weeks has highest fetal infection and anomaly rate.前 8 週感染德國麻疹胎兒感染與畸形率最高。
  • Abuse raises risk of abruptio placentae and preterm birth; interview the client alone and examine breasts, abdomen, genitals.暴力增加胎盤早期剝離與早產風險；應單獨訪談個案，並檢查乳房、腹部與生殖器。
  • HIV: perinatal zidovudine lowers transmission. Substance use: growth restriction, abruptio, fetal bradycardia; smoking causes low birth weight and stillbirth; alcohol causes fetal alcohol syndrome.HIV：周產期給予 zidovudine 可降低傳染。物質濫用：胎兒生長受限、胎盤早剝、胎心過慢；吸菸造成低出生體重與死產；酒精造成胎兒酒精症候群。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Antepartum Laboratory Tests產前實驗室檢查
  • Visit schedule: every 4 weeks to 28-32 wk, every 2 weeks 32-36 wk, weekly 36-40 wk.產檢頻率：28-32 週前每 4 週、32-36 週每 2 週、36-40 週每週。
  • Rh-negative with negative antibody screen: RhoGAM at 28 weeks and within 72 hr after birth of an Rh-positive infant; also after miscarriage/termination and CVS.Rh 陰性且抗體篩檢陰性：28 週給 RhoGAM，Rh 陽性新生兒出生後 72 小時內再給；流產、人工流產及 CVS 後亦給。
  • Rubella titer under 1:8: give live vaccine subcutaneously postpartum; avoid pregnancy 1-3 months and contact with immunocompromised people; ask about egg allergy; never give during pregnancy.德國麻疹抗體價低於 1:8：產後皮下注射活性疫苗；1-3 個月內避免懷孕並避免接觸免疫低下者；詢問蛋過敏；孕期不可施打。
  • Anemia in pregnancy: Hgb under 10 g/dL or Hct under 30%. WBC mildly elevated is normal.孕期貧血：Hgb 低於 10 g/dL 或 Hct 低於 30%。白血球輕度上升屬正常。
  • GCT at 24-28 weeks: 50 g glucose, 1-hour level over 140 mg/dL is positive and needs the 3-hour test.GCT 於 24-28 週：口服 50 g 葡萄糖，1 小時血糖超過 140 mg/dL 為陽性，需做 3 小時試驗。
  • Urine at each visit for glucose and protein; 2+ to 4+ protein suggests infection or preeclampsia.每次產檢驗尿糖與尿蛋白；蛋白 2+ 至 4+ 提示感染或子癇前症。
  • Hepatitis B antigen testing for all; hepatitis B vaccine is not contraindicated in pregnancy. TB: positive skin test needs chest X-ray with shield only after 20 weeks.所有孕婦篩檢 B 肝表面抗原；B 肝疫苗孕期不禁忌。結核菌素陽性者需於 20 週後以鉛屏護腹拍胸部 X 光。
  • GBS vaginal/rectal swab at about 36 weeks (20-25% colonized).約 36 週採陰道/直腸 GBS 培養（20-25% 帶菌）。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Antepartum Fetal Assessment and Procedures產前胎兒評估與侵入性檢查
  • Ultrasound: no known risk; abdominal scan needs a full bladder; transvaginal in first trimester checks cervix length.超音波：無已知風險；腹部超音波需憋尿；第一孕期經陰道超音波可測子宮頸長度。
  • Biophysical profile: breathing, movement, tone, amniotic fluid index, NST. Doppler studies placental and fetal blood flow.生物物理評估：呼吸、動作、張力、羊水指數、NST。都卜勒評估胎盤與胎兒血流。
  • Quad screen (AFP at 16-18 wk) screens for Down syndrome and neural tube defects; cell-free DNA blood test from 7 weeks screens trisomy 21, 18, 13.四指標篩檢（16-18 週 AFP）篩檢唐氏症與神經管缺陷；7 週起可做游離 DNA 抽血篩檢 21、18、13 三染色體。
  • CVS at 10-13 wk (genetic diagnosis, give RhoGAM if Rh-negative, rest 24 hr). Amniocentesis at 15-20 wk (full bladder before 20 wk, empty after; consent; FHR monitoring; left side after).CVS 於 10-13 週（基因診斷，Rh 陰性給 RhoGAM，休息 24 小時）。羊膜穿刺於 15-20 週（20 週前憋尿、之後排空；需同意書；監測胎心；術後左側臥）。
  • After CVS or amniocentesis report fever over 100.4 F (38 C), chills, bleeding, fluid leak, decreased fetal movement, contractions or cramping.CVS 或羊膜穿刺後，若發燒超過 38 度（100.4 F）、寒顫、出血、漏液、胎動減少、宮縮或痙攣應通報。
  • Kick counts from 28 weeks: sitting or side-lying, at least 10 movements in 2 hours.28 週起數胎動：坐或側臥，2 小時至少 10 次。
  • Ruptured membranes: fern test shows ferning; nitrazine paper turns blue (pH 7.0-7.5) versus vaginal pH 4.5-5.5. Fetal fibronectin at 22-34 wk predicts preterm labor (negative is reassuring).胎膜破裂：羊齒葉試驗呈羊齒狀；硝氮試紙變藍（pH 7.0-7.5，陰道 pH 4.5-5.5）。22-34 週胎兒纖維連接蛋白預測早產（陰性較安心）。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
NST, CST and Prenatal NutritionNST、CST 與孕期營養
  • Reactive NST (normal): 2 or more accelerations of at least 15 bpm lasting at least 15 sec within 20 min with movement. Nonreactive: none in 40 min.反應型 NST（正常）：20 分鐘內有 2 次以上、至少上升 15 bpm 且持續 15 秒以上的加速。無反應型：40 分鐘內無。
  • Position side-lying for NST/CST to avoid vena cava compression.NST/CST 採側臥避免壓迫下腔靜脈。
  • CST: negative (normal) = no late decelerations; positive (abnormal) = late decelerations with 50% or more of contractions; 3 contractions of 40 sec in 10 min required.CST：陰性（正常）＝無晚期減速；陽性（異常）＝50% 以上宮縮出現晚期減速；需 10 分鐘內 3 次、每次 40 秒以上宮縮。
  • Weight gain 25-35 lb (11-16 kg) for normal prepregnancy weight; +300 kcal/day in pregnancy, +500 during lactation; fluids 8-10 glasses; sodium not restricted.體重增加：孕前體重正常者 11-16 公斤（25-35 磅）；孕期每日加 300 大卡、哺乳加 500 大卡；水分 8-10 杯；不限鈉。
  • Vegetarian risks: B12, iron, zinc, calcium, vitamin D, omega-3; pair iron with vitamin C. Lactose intolerance: nondairy calcium, lactase.素食風險：B12、鐵、鋅、鈣、維生素 D、omega-3；鐵搭配維生素 C。乳糖不耐：非乳製鈣來源、乳糖酶。
  • Pica (eating dirt, clay, starch) can cause iron-deficiency anemia.異食癖（吃泥土、黏土、澱粉）可造成缺鐵性貧血。
