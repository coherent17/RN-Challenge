This chapter covers crisis phases and types, grief and loss, COVID-19 coping, suicide risk, anger and violence (including restraint and seclusion rules), bullying, family violence, child and elder abuse, child abduction, and rape/sexual assault. Safety, direct assessment, mandatory reporting, and non-judgmental support are the recurring priorities.本章涵蓋危機的階段與類型、悲傷與失落、COVID-19 因應、自殺風險、憤怒與暴力（含約束與隔離規範）、霸凌、家庭暴力、兒童與老人虐待、兒童誘拐，以及強暴與性侵害。反覆出現的優先事項為安全、直接評估、強制通報與不批判的支持。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Crisis: phases, types and intervention危機：階段、類型與介入

  • Crisis is a temporary state of severe emotional disorganization after a threatening event; problem-solving ability is inadequate; it is not itself a mental illness.危機是遭遇威脅事件後暫時性的嚴重情緒失序；解決問題能力不足；本身並非精神疾病。
  • Phases: 1 precipitating event; 2 perceived threat and rising anxiety; 3 failed coping, disorganization, physical symptoms; 4 mobilizing resources, goal is return to at least precrisis functioning.階段：1 誘發事件；2 感知威脅、焦慮上升；3 因應失敗、失序、出現身體症狀；4 動員資源，目標是回到至少危機前的功能。
  • Types (Box 68.1): maturational (developmental, e.g., retirement), situational (external, e.g., job loss, death, divorce), adventitious (disaster, war, violent crime).類型（方框 68.1）：成熟性（發展，如退休）、情境性（外在，如失業、死亡、離婚）、意外性（災難、戰爭、暴力犯罪）。
  • Intervention is immediate, supportive, goal-directed: acknowledge feelings, validate, link event meaning to crisis, explore and try new coping.介入是立即、支持性且目標導向：承認感受、確認、連結事件意義與危機、探索並嘗試新的因應。
  • Initial assessment: ask what led the client to seek help now (precipitating event) before asking about supports and coping.初步評估：先詢問促使客戶此時求助的原因（誘發事件），再詢問支持系統與因應方式。

2Grief, loss and the nurse's role悲傷、失落與護理角色

  • Grief is the natural response to loss. Three-stage response (Box 68.2): shock/disbelief; experiencing the loss (anger, guilt, bargaining, depression); reintegration.悲傷是對失落的自然反應。三階段反應（方框 68.2）：震驚與難以置信；經歷失落（憤怒、罪惡感、討價還價、憂鬱）；重新整合。
  • Types: normal, anticipatory, disenfranchised (cannot be openly acknowledged), dysfunctional (prolonged, no progress), and child grief by developmental level.類型：正常、預期性、被剝奪的（無法公開承認）、失功能（持續且無進展），以及依發展階段的兒童悲傷。
  • Loss: actual, perceived (only felt by the person), anticipatory. Mourning = outward social expression, shaped by culture/religion. Bereavement = grief plus mourning.失落：實際、感知（僅當事人感受）、預期。哀悼＝外在的社會表達，受文化/宗教影響。喪親＝悲傷加哀悼。
  • Children's grief (Box 68.3): infants react to caregiver loss; toddlers/preschoolers see death as reversible and may regress; 5-9 years begin to see it as permanent and may feel responsible; adolescents see it as permanent with strong reactions.兒童悲傷（方框 68.3）：嬰兒對照顧者失去有反應；幼兒/學齡前視死亡為可逆且可能退化；5–9 歲開始理解永久性並可能覺得自己有責任；青少年理解永久性且情緒強烈。
  • Nurse's role: trusting, nonjudgmental environment; listen, silence, touch if appropriate; be honest, do not abandon; respect culture; avoid personal opinions; acknowledge own feelings (Box 68.4).護理角色：建立信任、不批判的環境；傾聽、沉默、適當時給予碰觸；誠實並不離棄；尊重文化；避免個人意見；承認自己的感受（方框 68.4）。

3COVID-19 copingCOVID-19 因應

  • The pandemic is a real threat to physical and emotional well-being; expect anxiety, fear of the unknown, isolation stress, grief, survival guilt, depression, suicide risk.疫情是對身心健康的真實威脅；預期焦慮、對未知的恐懼、隔離壓力、悲傷、倖存者罪惡感、憂鬱與自殺風險。
  • Moral injury is a concern for health care workers forced into impossible choices and for families unable to be with loved ones.道德傷害是醫療人員被迫做艱難抉擇、以及家屬無法陪伴親人時的重要議題。
  • Interventions: assess for stress, depression and suicidality; foster hope and resilience; cognitive reappraisal; list what can be controlled; safety behaviors (distancing, handwashing, masks); limit media; stay connected; eat, sleep, exercise.處置：評估壓力、憂鬱與自殺風險；培養希望與復原力；認知重新評價；列出可控制的事；安全行為（保持距離、洗手、戴口罩）；限制媒體接觸；保持聯繫；規律飲食、睡眠與運動。

4Suicidal behavior自殺行為

  • Always consider suicide in a depressed client. Risk groups: prior attempts, family history, adolescents, older adults, disabled/terminally ill, personality disorders, dementia, depression/psychosis, substance abuse, bullied, abuse history, past hospitalizations.憂鬱客戶一律要考慮自殺。高風險族群：曾嘗試自殺、家族史、青少年、老年人、身心障礙/末期病人、人格疾患、失智、憂鬱/精神病、物質濫用、被霸凌、受虐史、曾住院。
  • Cues (Box 68.5): giving away possessions, will/insurance changes, canceling plans, statements of intent, asking about lethal means, sudden calm or improvement.警訊（方框 68.5）：送出財物、修改遺囑/保險、取消約定、表達意圖、詢問致命工具、突然平靜或好轉。
  • Assess plan, means and timing, prior attempts and increasing lethality, hallucinations (command), substance use, recent losses, isolation (Box 68.6).評估計畫、方法與時間、過去嘗試與致命性增加、幻覺（命令式）、物質使用、近期失落、孤立（方框 68.6）。
  • Interventions: ask directly; suicide precautions; document every 15 min; remove harmful objects; never leave alone (one-to-one for those at risk); nonjudgmental; support systems; staff accompany off unit; check visitors' items.處置：直接詢問；自殺防範；每 15 分鐘記錄；移除危險物；絕不單獨留置（高風險者一對一）；不批判；找出支持系統；離開病房需工作人員陪同；檢查訪客物品。

5Anger, aggression, restraints and seclusion憤怒、攻擊、約束與隔離

  • Warning signs of violence: history of violence, poor impulse control, raised voice, threats, pacing, muscle rigidity, flushed face, glaring.暴力警訊：暴力史、衝動控制差、提高音量、威脅、踱步、肌肉僵硬、臉紅、怒視。
  • De-escalation: low-stimuli safe setting, calm clear voice, large personal space, nonaggressive posture, listen, acknowledge anger, clear options and limits, problem-solve. Use least restrictive measures first.降級：低刺激安全環境、平穩清楚語氣、保持較大個人空間、非攻擊性姿勢、傾聽、承認憤怒、給予清楚選項與限制、一起解決問題。先使用最少限制措施。
  • Restraints/seclusion: never for punishment or staff convenience; only when behavior is physically harmful and less restrictive measures fail; document the behavior.約束/隔離：絕不用於懲罰或方便工作人員；僅在行為造成身體傷害且較少限制措施無效時使用；須記錄行為。
  • PHCP prescription is normally required (specifying type, duration, release criteria); in an emergency the nurse may act and then obtain the order promptly; face-to-face evaluation within 1 hour; one-to-one observation; assess needs every 15-30 min.通常須醫囑（註明種類、時間長度與解除條件）；緊急時護理師可先處置再儘快取得醫囑；1 小時內須面對面評估；一對一觀察；每 15–30 分鐘評估需求。

6Bullying霸凌

  • Bullying is repeated aggressive acts by someone using power over another (physical, relational, verbal, cyberbullying).霸凌是以權力壓制他人的重複攻擊行為（身體、關係、言語、網路霸凌）。
  • Victims can develop depression, low self-esteem, isolation, self-harm, suicide.受害者可能出現憂鬱、低自尊、孤立、自傷與自殺。
  • Nurse's role: observe for signs and educate teachers, administrators and parents.護理角色：觀察徵象並教育老師、行政人員與家長。

7Family violence家庭暴力

  • Cycle of violence (Fig. 68.1): tension building, acute battering, honeymoon/calm with undoing behavior; victim blames self, isolation is common.暴力循環（圖 68.1）：張力累積、急性毆打、蜜月/平靜期（含補償行為）；受害者自責，常見孤立。
  • Types (Box 68.7): physical, sexual, emotional violence, physical/developmental/educational neglect, economic exploitation. Children and older adults are most vulnerable.類型（方框 68.7）：身體、性、情緒暴力，身體/發展/教育忽視，經濟剝削。兒童與老人最脆弱。
  • Care: private, confidential, nonjudgmental assessment; assess injuries, suicide and homicide potential, substance use; help make a specific safety plan and give hotline/shelter information; report suspected child/elder abuse; accurate records.照護：在隱密、保密、不批判的情況下評估；評估傷勢、自殺與他殺風險、物質使用；協助訂定具體安全計畫並提供熱線/庇護所資訊；通報疑似兒童/老人虐待；正確記錄。
  • Abuser traits: low self-esteem, dependency, history of childhood abuse, sees victim as property. Offer therapy to both victim and abuser.施虐者特徵：自尊低、依賴性強、童年受虐史、視受害者為財產。對受害者與施虐者都提供治療。

8Child abduction, child abuse, latchkey children兒童誘拐、兒童虐待與鑰匙兒童

  • Child abduction: by strangers or a non-custodial parent; teach children safety rules (don't go alone, tell an adult, say no, no secrets, don't accept gifts, go to a clerk if lost, know name/address/phone). Watch for PTSD after abduction.兒童誘拐：陌生人或無監護權的父母所為；教導兒童安全守則（不單獨外出、告知大人、說不、不保守秘密、不收禮物、走失找店員、記得姓名/地址/電話）。誘拐後留意 PTSD。
  • Child abuse includes neglect and physical, sexual, emotional maltreatment. Shaken baby: subdural hemorrhage, retinal hemorrhages, bulging fontanel, decreased consciousness (rising ICP), usually no external signs.兒童虐待包含忽視與身體、性、情緒傷害。嬰兒搖晃症候群：硬腦膜下出血、視網膜出血、囟門膨出、意識下降（顱內壓上升），常無外傷。
  • Physical abuse cues (Box 68.9): unexplained bruises, burns, fractures, spiral fractures, bald spots, apprehensive child, lack of crying with strangers. Neglect: poor hygiene, hunger, inadequate weight gain.身體虐待線索（方框 68.9）：不明瘀青、燒燙傷、骨折、螺旋骨折、禿斑、畏縮、對陌生人不哭。忽視：衛生差、飢餓、體重增加不足。
  • Nurses are legally required to report suspected child abuse; protect the child, document objectively, support the family and refer to resources.護理師依法必須通報疑似兒童虐待；保護兒童、客觀記錄、支持家庭並轉介資源。
  • Latchkey children lack adult supervision; teach self-care skills and refer to after-school programs.鑰匙兒童缺乏成人監督；教導自我照顧技能並轉介課後方案。

9Elder abuse老人虐待

  • Forms: physical, emotional, sexual abuse, neglect, economic exploitation; risk is highest for dependent elders with illness, immobility or confusion; caregiver stress and family violence history contribute.形式：身體、情緒、性虐待、忽視、經濟剝削；依賴的老人（疾病、行動不便、意識混亂）風險最高；照顧者壓力與家庭暴力史為促成因素。
  • Signs: fractures, bruises, pressure sores, burns, torn underclothing, dehydration, malnutrition, missing glasses/dentures, medication overdose, unpaid bills.徵象：骨折、瘀青、壓瘡、燒傷、內衣破損、脫水、營養不良、缺少眼鏡/假牙、藥物過量、帳單未付。
  • Care: treat injuries, ask if someone is harming them, report to authorities, separate from abuser if possible and contact adult protective services, least restrictive placement, caregiver respite/counseling.照護：治療傷勢、詢問是否有人傷害、通報主管機關、可能時與施虐者分開並聯絡成人保護服務、採最少限制的安置、為照顧者提供喘息或諮商。

10Rape and sexual assault強暴與性侵害

  • Rape = sexual act through force/coercion without consent. Includes acquaintance, statutory, same-gender and marital rape. The victim is not legally required to report.強暴＝以強迫或脅迫未經同意進行性行為。包含熟人、法定、同性與婚內強暴。受害者無法律上報案義務。
  • Responses: shame, humiliation, anger, fear of not being believed, self-blame. Rape trauma syndrome: sleep disturbance, nightmares, fear, somatic complaints, relationship disruption; see PTSD.反應：羞愧、屈辱、憤怒、害怕不被相信、自責。強暴創傷症候群：睡眠障礙、夢魘、恐懼、身體不適、關係受損；參見 PTSD。
  • ED care: private quiet area, stay with the victim, treat injuries, written consent, explain why not to shower/douche/change clothes before exam, preserve evidence, offer shower afterward, refer to SADV nurse and support groups.急診照護：安靜隱私區域、陪伴受害者、治療傷勢、書面同意、解釋檢查前不可淋浴/沖洗/換衣、保存證據、檢查後提供淋浴、轉介 SADV 護理師與支持團體。
  • Reinforce that surviving was what mattered: the victim did what was necessary to stay alive. Respond to feelings with open-ended therapeutic communication.強調存活最重要：受害者做了讓自己活下來所需的事。以開放式治療性溝通回應感受。

  • Always consider suicide in a depressed client. Risk groups: prior attempts, family history, adolescents, older adults, disabled/terminally ill, personality disorders, dementia, depression/psychosis, substance abuse, bullied, abuse history, past hospitalizations.憂鬱客戶一律要考慮自殺。高風險族群：曾嘗試自殺、家族史、青少年、老年人、身心障礙/末期病人、人格疾患、失智、憂鬱/精神病、物質濫用、被霸凌、受虐史、曾住院。
  • Cues (Box 68.5): giving away possessions, will/insurance changes, canceling plans, statements of intent, asking about lethal means, sudden calm or improvement.警訊（方框 68.5）：送出財物、修改遺囑/保險、取消約定、表達意圖、詢問致命工具、突然平靜或好轉。
  • Assess plan, means and timing, prior attempts and increasing lethality, hallucinations (command), substance use, recent losses, isolation (Box 68.6).評估計畫、方法與時間、過去嘗試與致命性增加、幻覺（命令式）、物質使用、近期失落、孤立（方框 68.6）。
  • Interventions: ask directly; suicide precautions; document every 15 min; remove harmful objects; never leave alone (one-to-one for those at risk); nonjudgmental; support systems; staff accompany off unit; check visitors' items.處置：直接詢問；自殺防範；每 15 分鐘記錄；移除危險物；絕不單獨留置（高風險者一對一）；不批判；找出支持系統；離開病房需工作人員陪同；檢查訪客物品。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Anger, aggression, restraints and seclusion憤怒、攻擊、約束與隔離
  • Warning signs of violence: history of violence, poor impulse control, raised voice, threats, pacing, muscle rigidity, flushed face, glaring.暴力警訊：暴力史、衝動控制差、提高音量、威脅、踱步、肌肉僵硬、臉紅、怒視。
  • De-escalation: low-stimuli safe setting, calm clear voice, large personal space, nonaggressive posture, listen, acknowledge anger, clear options and limits, problem-solve. Use least restrictive measures first.降級：低刺激安全環境、平穩清楚語氣、保持較大個人空間、非攻擊性姿勢、傾聽、承認憤怒、給予清楚選項與限制、一起解決問題。先使用最少限制措施。
  • Restraints/seclusion: never for punishment or staff convenience; only when behavior is physically harmful and less restrictive measures fail; document the behavior.約束/隔離：絕不用於懲罰或方便工作人員；僅在行為造成身體傷害且較少限制措施無效時使用；須記錄行為。
  • PHCP prescription is normally required (specifying type, duration, release criteria); in an emergency the nurse may act and then obtain the order promptly; face-to-face evaluation within 1 hour; one-to-one observation; assess needs every 15-30 min.通常須醫囑（註明種類、時間長度與解除條件）；緊急時護理師可先處置再儘快取得醫囑；1 小時內須面對面評估；一對一觀察；每 15–30 分鐘評估需求。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Bullying霸凌
  • Bullying is repeated aggressive acts by someone using power over another (physical, relational, verbal, cyberbullying).霸凌是以權力壓制他人的重複攻擊行為（身體、關係、言語、網路霸凌）。
  • Victims can develop depression, low self-esteem, isolation, self-harm, suicide.受害者可能出現憂鬱、低自尊、孤立、自傷與自殺。
  • Nurse's role: observe for signs and educate teachers, administrators and parents.護理角色：觀察徵象並教育老師、行政人員與家長。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Family violence家庭暴力
  • Cycle of violence (Fig. 68.1): tension building, acute battering, honeymoon/calm with undoing behavior; victim blames self, isolation is common.暴力循環（圖 68.1）：張力累積、急性毆打、蜜月/平靜期（含補償行為）；受害者自責，常見孤立。
  • Types (Box 68.7): physical, sexual, emotional violence, physical/developmental/educational neglect, economic exploitation. Children and older adults are most vulnerable.類型（方框 68.7）：身體、性、情緒暴力，身體/發展/教育忽視，經濟剝削。兒童與老人最脆弱。
  • Care: private, confidential, nonjudgmental assessment; assess injuries, suicide and homicide potential, substance use; help make a specific safety plan and give hotline/shelter information; report suspected child/elder abuse; accurate records.照護：在隱密、保密、不批判的情況下評估；評估傷勢、自殺與他殺風險、物質使用；協助訂定具體安全計畫並提供熱線/庇護所資訊；通報疑似兒童/老人虐待；正確記錄。
  • Abuser traits: low self-esteem, dependency, history of childhood abuse, sees victim as property. Offer therapy to both victim and abuser.施虐者特徵：自尊低、依賴性強、童年受虐史、視受害者為財產。對受害者與施虐者都提供治療。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Child abduction, child abuse, latchkey children兒童誘拐、兒童虐待與鑰匙兒童
  • Child abduction: by strangers or a non-custodial parent; teach children safety rules (don't go alone, tell an adult, say no, no secrets, don't accept gifts, go to a clerk if lost, know name/address/phone). Watch for PTSD after abduction.兒童誘拐：陌生人或無監護權的父母所為；教導兒童安全守則（不單獨外出、告知大人、說不、不保守秘密、不收禮物、走失找店員、記得姓名/地址/電話）。誘拐後留意 PTSD。
  • Child abuse includes neglect and physical, sexual, emotional maltreatment. Shaken baby: subdural hemorrhage, retinal hemorrhages, bulging fontanel, decreased consciousness (rising ICP), usually no external signs.兒童虐待包含忽視與身體、性、情緒傷害。嬰兒搖晃症候群：硬腦膜下出血、視網膜出血、囟門膨出、意識下降（顱內壓上升），常無外傷。
  • Physical abuse cues (Box 68.9): unexplained bruises, burns, fractures, spiral fractures, bald spots, apprehensive child, lack of crying with strangers. Neglect: poor hygiene, hunger, inadequate weight gain.身體虐待線索（方框 68.9）：不明瘀青、燒燙傷、骨折、螺旋骨折、禿斑、畏縮、對陌生人不哭。忽視：衛生差、飢餓、體重增加不足。
  • Nurses are legally required to report suspected child abuse; protect the child, document objectively, support the family and refer to resources.護理師依法必須通報疑似兒童虐待；保護兒童、客觀記錄、支持家庭並轉介資源。
  • Latchkey children lack adult supervision; teach self-care skills and refer to after-school programs.鑰匙兒童缺乏成人監督；教導自我照顧技能並轉介課後方案。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
Elder abuse老人虐待
  • Forms: physical, emotional, sexual abuse, neglect, economic exploitation; risk is highest for dependent elders with illness, immobility or confusion; caregiver stress and family violence history contribute.形式：身體、情緒、性虐待、忽視、經濟剝削；依賴的老人（疾病、行動不便、意識混亂）風險最高；照顧者壓力與家庭暴力史為促成因素。
  • Signs: fractures, bruises, pressure sores, burns, torn underclothing, dehydration, malnutrition, missing glasses/dentures, medication overdose, unpaid bills.徵象：骨折、瘀青、壓瘡、燒傷、內衣破損、脫水、營養不良、缺少眼鏡/假牙、藥物過量、帳單未付。
  • Care: treat injuries, ask if someone is harming them, report to authorities, separate from abuser if possible and contact adult protective services, least restrictive placement, caregiver respite/counseling.照護：治療傷勢、詢問是否有人傷害、通報主管機關、可能時與施虐者分開並聯絡成人保護服務、採最少限制的安置、為照顧者提供喘息或諮商。
Topic 10 · tap to flip主題 10 · 點擊翻面
Rape and sexual assault強暴與性侵害
  • Rape = sexual act through force/coercion without consent. Includes acquaintance, statutory, same-gender and marital rape. The victim is not legally required to report.強暴＝以強迫或脅迫未經同意進行性行為。包含熟人、法定、同性與婚內強暴。受害者無法律上報案義務。
  • Responses: shame, humiliation, anger, fear of not being believed, self-blame. Rape trauma syndrome: sleep disturbance, nightmares, fear, somatic complaints, relationship disruption; see PTSD.反應：羞愧、屈辱、憤怒、害怕不被相信、自責。強暴創傷症候群：睡眠障礙、夢魘、恐懼、身體不適、關係受損；參見 PTSD。
  • ED care: private quiet area, stay with the victim, treat injuries, written consent, explain why not to shower/douche/change clothes before exam, preserve evidence, offer shower afterward, refer to SADV nurse and support groups.急診照護：安靜隱私區域、陪伴受害者、治療傷勢、書面同意、解釋檢查前不可淋浴/沖洗/換衣、保存證據、檢查後提供淋浴、轉介 SADV 護理師與支持團體。
  • Reinforce that surviving was what mattered: the victim did what was necessary to stay alive. Respond to feelings with open-ended therapeutic communication.強調存活最重要：受害者做了讓自己活下來所需的事。以開放式治療性溝通回應感受。

Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. A client in crisis is not necessarily mentally ill; responses are individual. Goal: return to at least precrisis functioning.處於危機的客戶不一定有精神疾病；反應因人而異。目標：回到至少危機前的功能。
    p.1090 · book 987p.1101 · book 998
  2. Situational crisis = external life event (death, job loss, divorce); adventitious crisis = disaster, violence, rape, war.情境性危機＝外在生活事件（死亡、失業、離婚）；意外性危機＝災難、暴力、強暴、戰爭。
    p.1091 · book 988p.1101 · book 998
  3. In crisis assessment, first identify the precipitating event ('What led you to seek help now?').危機評估先找出誘發事件（「是什麼讓你現在來求助？」）。
    p.1101 · book 998
  4. Ask directly about suicide: 'Are you thinking of hurting yourself?'; assess plan, means, timing.直接詢問自殺：「你有想傷害自己嗎？」；評估計畫、方法與時間。
    p.1093 · book 990p.1100 · book 997
  5. Sudden calm or improvement in a depressed client may signal a decision to attempt suicide - increase precautions.憂鬱客戶突然平靜或好轉，可能代表已決定自殺——應加強防範。
    p.1093 · book 990p.1102 · book 999
  6. Giving away possessions is a suicide cue.送出財物是自殺警訊。
    p.1093 · book 990p.1102 · book 999
  7. After a suicide attempt: one-to-one constant supervision; treat physical injury first (airway/bleeding) in the ED.自殺未遂後：一對一持續監視；急診先處理生理傷害（呼吸道/出血）。
    p.1093 · book 990p.1102 · book 999
  8. Escalating aggression: acknowledge behavior, quiet area, safe distance, least restrictive first; confinement only in the crisis period.攻擊行為升高：承認行為、移至安靜區域、保持安全距離、先用最少限制措施；僅於危機期才使用隔離。
    p.1094 · book 991p.1101 · book 998
  9. Restraints/seclusion: never as punishment; PHCP order; face-to-face evaluation within 1 hour; one-to-one observation; check needs every 15-30 min.約束/隔離：不得作為懲罰；需醫囑；1 小時內面對面評估；一對一觀察；每 15–30 分鐘評估需求。
    p.1094 · book 991
  10. Mandatory reporting of suspected child abuse and elder abuse; document objectively.疑似兒童與老人虐待須強制通報；客觀記錄。
    p.1096 · book 993p.1097 · book 994p.1098 · book 995
  11. Shaken baby syndrome: retinal hemorrhage, bulging fontanel, decreasing LOC (increased ICP), few external signs.嬰兒搖晃症候群：視網膜出血、囟門膨出、意識下降（顱內壓上升）、外傷少。
    p.1096 · book 993p.1097 · book 994
  12. Family violence discharge: give specific safety plan and shelter/hotline information, not just 'leave' or 'call the police'.家暴出院指導：提供具體安全計畫與庇護所/熱線資訊，而非僅叫對方離開或報警。
    p.1095 · book 992p.1102 · book 999
  13. Cycle of violence: tension-building, acute battering, honeymoon (loving, contrite).暴力循環：張力累積、急性毆打、蜜月期（討好、悔過）。
    p.1095 · book 992
  14. Rape victim: stay with the victim, private area, do not bathe/douche/change clothes before exam, preserve evidence, written consent.強暴受害者：陪伴、隱私空間、檢查前不可洗澡/沖洗/更衣、保存證據、書面同意。
    p.1099 · book 996
  15. Grief stages: shock/disbelief, experiencing the loss, reintegration; respond to feelings and do not give false reassurance.悲傷階段：震驚與難以置信、經歷失落、重新整合；回應感受，不給錯誤安慰。
    p.1091 · book 988p.1102 · book 999
  16. Children under about 5 may view death as reversible; 5-9 begin to see it as permanent and may feel responsible.約 5 歲以下兒童可能視死亡為可逆；5–9 歲開始理解永久性並可能覺得自己有責任。
    p.1091 · book 988

Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Suicide warning: 'SAD PERSONS'-style recall - Sex (male), Age, Depression, Previous attempt, Ethanol, Rational thinking loss, Social support lacking, Organized plan, No spouse, Sickness (external mnemonic, not from the chapter).自殺風險記憶法「SAD PERSONS」：性別（男）、年齡、憂鬱、曾嘗試、酒精、理性喪失、缺乏支持、有計畫、無配偶、疾病（外部口訣，非本章內容）。
    p.1093 · book 990
  • Crisis priority: Safety first, then feelings, then problem-solving. For violence: least restrictive first (talk, space, quiet area, medicate) before seclusion/restraint.危機優先順序：先安全，再感受，後解決問題。暴力處理：先最少限制（談話、空間、安靜區、用藥），再隔離/約束。
    p.1094 · book 991
