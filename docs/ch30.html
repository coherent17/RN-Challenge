Pediatric skin disorders: eczema, impetigo, head lice, scabies and burns. Focus on infection control, correct topical medication use, home decontamination teaching and the special features of burns in children.小兒皮膚疾病：濕疹、膿痂疹、頭蝨、疥瘡與燒傷。重點為感染控制、外用藥正確使用、居家環境處理衛教，以及兒童燒傷的特殊性。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Eczema (dermatitis)濕疹（皮膚炎）

  • Superficial inflammation of the epidermis; linked to family history, allergies, asthma, allergic rhinitis.表皮淺層發炎；與家族史、過敏、氣喘、過敏性鼻炎有關。
  • Signs: itching, redness, scaling, tiny papules and vesicles, weeping/oozing and crusting; typical on scalp, face, elbow/knee creases, neck, wrists, ankles; axilla, buttocks, groin uncommon.症狀：搔癢、紅斑、脫屑、小丘疹與水泡、滲液結痂；好發頭皮、臉、肘膝彎處、頸、腕、踝；腋下、臀、鼠蹊少見。
  • Infantile form starts at 2-6 months and may remit by 3 years; childhood form 2-3 years; adolescent form from about 12 years onward.嬰兒型 2–6 個月開始，3 歲前可能緩解；兒童型 2–3 歲；青少年型約 12 歲起。
  • Goals: relieve itch, hydrate skin, reduce inflammation, prevent/control secondary infection.目標：止癢、保濕、消炎、預防或控制續發感染。
p.438 · book 392

2Eczema care濕疹照護

  • Short (5-10 min) lukewarm baths, oatmeal soothing; moisturize immediately with thick cream/ointment.短時間（5–10 分鐘）微溫水浴，可用燕麥浴；浴後立即塗厚層乳霜或軟膏。
  • Topical medication goes on within 3 minutes after the bath, before the moisturizer; corticosteroids in a thin layer rubbed in well.外用藥於浴後 3 分鐘內先塗，再塗保濕劑；類固醇薄塗並充分推開。
  • Avoid irritants: harsh soaps, detergents, fabric softeners, wipes, powder, wool, latex, heat, wet diapers.避免刺激物：刺激性肥皂、洗衣精、柔軟精、濕紙巾、爽身粉、羊毛、乳膠、高溫、濕尿布。
  • Keep nails short, cover hands with gloves/cotton socks; cool wet compresses for itch.指甲剪短，手戴手套或棉襪；冷濕敷止癢。
  • Honey-colored crusts with surrounding redness means secondary infection: seek care promptly.蜜黃色痂皮伴周圍紅斑代表續發感染，應立即就醫。

3Impetigo膿痂疹

  • Contagious skin infection by group A strep and Staphylococcus aureus; more common in hot, humid months, often at insect bites or damaged skin.由 A 群鏈球菌與金黃色葡萄球菌引起的傳染性皮膚感染；炎熱潮濕季節多見，常發生於蚊蟲叮咬或受損皮膚。
  • Most common on face around mouth/nose, then hands, neck, extremities; vesicles/pustules rupture leaving honey-colored crusts.好發於口鼻周圍，其次手、頸、四肢；水泡/膿疱破裂後留下蜜黃色痂皮。
  • Contact isolation plus standard precautions; contagious until 24 hours after antibiotics started.接觸隔離加標準防護；開始抗生素後 24 小時內仍具傳染性。
  • Cover lesions, warm compresses 2-3 times daily to soften crusts, topical or oral antibiotics; finish the full course (risk of glomerulonephritis with strep).覆蓋傷口，每日 2–3 次溫敷軟化痂皮，外用或口服抗生素；須完成療程（鏈球菌可併發腎絲球腎炎）。
  • Separate towels, linens, utensils; hot-water wash of linens; bleach the tub; short nails; careful handwashing.毛巾、床單、餐具分開；寢具以熱水清洗；浴缸以漂白水清潔；指甲剪短；勤洗手。
p.439 · book 393

4Pediculosis capitis (lice)頭蝨

  • Lice infest hair/scalp, mostly occipital area and behind ears; nits are glued to the hair shaft near the scalp and do not flake off like dandruff.蝨子寄生頭髮與頭皮，多在枕部與耳後；蟲卵（蝨卵）牢固黏於近頭皮髮幹，不像頭皮屑易掉落。
  • Spread by direct/indirect contact (brushes, hats, towels, bedding); lice live about 48 hours off the host; nits hatch in 7-10 days.經直接或間接接觸（梳子、帽子、毛巾、寢具）傳播；離開宿主約存活 48 小時；卵 7–10 天孵化。
  • Use pediculicide as prescribed; most not for infants under 6 months; lindane not for under 2 years (neurotoxicity, seizures).依醫囑使用殺蝨劑；多數不適用 6 個月以下；林丹（lindane）不可用於 2 歲以下（神經毒性、癲癇）。
  • Daily nit removal with fine metal comb (wear gloves); examine and treat contacts as needed.每日以細齒金屬梳除卵（戴手套）；檢查並視需要治療接觸者。
  • Launder clothing/bedding from previous 2 days in hot water and hot dryer 20 min; seal nonwashables in plastic bag 2 weeks; vacuum and discard bag; soak combs in boiling water 10 min.洗滌前 2 天衣物寢具用熱水並熱烘 20 分鐘；不可洗物品密封塑膠袋 2 週；吸塵並丟棄集塵袋；梳子以沸水浸泡 10 分鐘。
p.439 · book 393p.440 · book 394

5Scabies疥瘡

  • Caused by the itch mite Sarcoptes scabiei; spread by close contact; infectious throughout the infestation.由疥蟲引起；經密切接觸傳染；整個感染期皆具傳染性。
  • Findings: pruritic papular rash and burrows (fine grayish-red threadlike lines).表現：搔癢丘疹與皮膚隧道（細灰紅色線狀）。
  • Permethrin: apply to cool, dry skin at least 30 min after bath, over all skin surfaces, head to soles per this chapter (scabicides in general: neck down, not face; follow product label), leave 8-14 hours, then wash off; repeat if needed.Permethrin：於沐浴後至少 30 分鐘、皮膚涼且乾時塗抹，全身皮膚（非僅皮疹處；本章述及頭至腳底，一般殺疥劑為頸部以下、避開臉，依產品說明）塗抹，停留 8–14 小時後洗去；必要時重複。
  • Treat all household members and contacts simultaneously.所有同住者與接觸者須同時治療。
  • Change and hot-wash linens daily for at least 1 week; seal nonwashables in bags at least 4 days.每日更換並熱水清洗寢具至少 1 週；不可洗物品密封至少 4 天。

6Burn injuries in children兒童燒傷

  • Thinner skin: lower temperature and shorter exposure cause deeper burns; higher mortality in very young children.皮膚較薄：較低溫度與較短時間即可造成深度燒傷；幼童死亡率較高。
  • Greater risk of fluid and heat loss, dehydration, metabolic acidosis, protein-calorie deficiency, infection, severe scarring, body image problems and growth delay.較易發生水分與熱量散失、脫水、代謝性酸中毒、蛋白質熱量不足、感染、嚴重疤痕、身體心像問題與生長遲緩。
  • Do not use the rule of nines; use age-related charts (Lund-Browder type, Fig 30.3) for %TBSA.勿用九分法；以依年齡之圖表（圖 30.3）估算燒傷面積百分比。
  • Burns >10% TBSA need fluid resuscitation during the first 24 hours; crystalloids first, colloids to maintain volume.燒傷 >10% TBSA 需於前 24 小時液體復甦；先給晶體液，膠體液維持血容量。
  • Adequacy of resuscitation: heart rate, urine output, capillary refill, sensorium.復甦是否足夠：心跳、尿量、微血管再充填、意識狀態。
  • First aid: stop burning, check airway, CAB if needed, remove burned clothing not stuck, cool (not cold) running water, cover clean, keep warm, call EMS.急救：停止燒灼、確認呼吸道、必要時 CAB、移除未黏著衣物、以涼（非冰）流水沖、乾淨布覆蓋、保暖、呼叫 EMS。
