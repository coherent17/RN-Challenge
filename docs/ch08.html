This chapter covers how water and electrolytes are distributed, moved and regulated, then walks through fluid volume deficit/excess and each major electrolyte imbalance (K+, Na+, Ca2+, Mg2+, phosphorus). For each one, know the normal range, common causes, assessment findings, ECG changes and the priority nursing interventions.本章說明水分與電解質在體內的分布、移動與調節,接著依序介紹體液容積不足與過多,以及主要電解質(鉀、鈉、鈣、鎂、磷)失衡。每一項都要熟記正常值、常見原因、評估發現、心電圖變化與優先護理措施。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Concepts of fluid and electrolyte balance體液與電解質平衡的基本概念

  • Electrolytes ionize in solution; cations are positive and anions negative, and they must balance. Activity is measured in milliequivalents (mEq).電解質在溶液中會解離成離子;陽離子帶正電、陰離子帶負電,兩者必須平衡,以毫當量(mEq)表示活性。
  • Fluid compartments: intracellular (about 70% of body water) and extracellular (about 30%), the latter split into interstitial (22%), intravascular (6%) and transcellular (2%).體液分區:細胞內液約佔 70%,細胞外液約佔 30%,其中組織間液 22%、血管內液 6%、跨細胞液 2%。
  • Third-spacing traps extracellular fluid in a body space (pleural, peritoneal, bowel, burned tissue); it is a real intravascular loss that may not show in weight or intake/output.第三間隙積液是指細胞外液被困在體腔(胸腔、腹腔、腸道、燒傷組織)中,實際造成血管內容積流失,但體重與輸出入量不一定反映。
  • Edema is excess interstitial fluid from altered oncotic or hydrostatic pressure, capillary permeability or lymphatic obstruction; generalized edema (anasarca) suggests cardiac, renal or liver disease.水腫是組織間液過多,源於膠體滲透壓、靜水壓、微血管通透性或淋巴阻塞改變;全身性水腫(anasarca)常見於心、腎或肝臟疾病。
  • Total body water is about 60% of weight in adults, 55% in older adults and 80% in infants, so infants and older adults are at greater risk for fluid imbalance.成人體水約佔體重 60%,老年人 55%,嬰兒 80%,因此嬰兒與老年人較易發生體液失衡。

2Fluid transport and tonicity體液運輸與張力

  • Diffusion moves solute from high to low concentration; osmosis moves water toward the side with more solute; filtration moves fluid from higher to lower hydrostatic pressure.擴散是溶質由高濃度往低濃度移動;滲透是水往溶質較多的一側移動;過濾則是液體由高靜水壓往低靜水壓移動。
  • At the arterial end of a capillary hydrostatic pressure pushes fluid out; at the venous end osmotic pull draws fluid back; lymph returns the remainder.微血管動脈端靜水壓使液體流出,靜脈端滲透壓將液體拉回,剩餘部分由淋巴回收。
  • Normal plasma osmolality is 275-295 mOsm/kg.血漿滲透壓正常值為 275-295 mOsm/kg。
  • Isotonic solutions match body fluid osmolality (little osmosis); hypotonic solutions have lower and hypertonic solutions higher osmolality than body fluids.等張溶液與體液滲透壓相同(幾乎不產生滲透);低張溶液滲透壓較低,高張溶液較高。
  • Active transport moves ions (Na+, K+, Ca2+, H+) against a gradient and needs cellular energy.主動運輸需消耗細胞能量,使離子(Na+、K+、Ca2+、H+)逆濃度梯度移動。

3Fluid intake/output and regulation水分攝入、排出與調節

  • Intake is about 2300-2900 mL/day (drinks, food, metabolic water); output is about 2600-3000 mL/day, mostly kidney (about 1500 mL).每日攝入約 2300-2900 mL(飲水、食物、代謝水),排出約 2600-3000 mL,以腎臟最多(約 1500 mL)。
  • Insensible loss occurs through skin (600-800 mL) and lungs (400-600 mL) and cannot be measured exactly.不感性流失來自皮膚(600-800 mL)與肺部(400-600 mL),無法精確測量。
  • Kidneys are the major regulators; aldosterone retains sodium (and water), and ADH controls water reabsorption.腎臟是主要調節器官;醛固酮促進鈉(及水)再吸收,抗利尿激素(ADH)調節水分再吸收。
  • Severe diarrhea causes large fluid and electrolyte losses. With any imbalance, monitor cardiovascular, respiratory, neurological, musculoskeletal, renal, skin and GI status.嚴重腹瀉會造成大量體液與電解質流失。發生任何失衡時,須監測心血管、呼吸、神經、肌肉骨骼、腎臟、皮膚與腸胃狀態。
p.112 · book 91

4Fluid volume deficit and excess體液容積不足與過多

  • Isotonic dehydration (hypovolemia) is the most common deficit: water and electrolytes are lost equally. Hypertonic: water loss exceeds electrolytes (cells shrink). Hypotonic: electrolyte loss exceeds water (cells swell).等張性脫水(低血容)最常見,水與電解質等比例流失;高張性脫水為水分流失多於電解質(細胞萎縮);低張性脫水為電解質流失多於水分(細胞腫脹)。
  • Deficit findings: tachycardia with thready pulse, hypotension, flat neck veins, low CVP, dry mucosa, poor turgor, weight loss, low urine output, high hematocrit/BUN/osmolality/urine specific gravity.容積不足表現:脈搏快而細弱、低血壓、頸靜脈扁塌、CVP 低、黏膜乾燥、皮膚彈性差、體重下降、尿量少,血比容、BUN、滲透壓與尿比重上升。
  • Excess findings: bounding pulse, high BP, distended neck veins, high CVP, crackles, dyspnea, edema, weight gain, ascites; low hematocrit/BUN/osmolality/urine specific gravity.容積過多表現:脈搏洪大、高血壓、頸靜脈怒張、CVP 高、囉音、呼吸困難、水腫、體重增加、腹水;血比容、BUN、滲透壓與尿比重下降。
  • Treat deficit with oral or IV fluids and treat the cause (isotonic loss with isotonic fluid, hypertonic with hypotonic, hypotonic with hypertonic fluid); monitor I&O.不足時給予口服或靜脈輸液並處理原因(等張流失用等張液、高張性用低張液、低張性用高張液),並監測輸出入量。
  • Treat excess with diuretics, fluid and sodium restriction, daily weight and I&O. Highest risk: AKI, CKD, heart failure, long-term corticosteroids.過多時給予利尿劑、限制水分與鈉攝取,監測每日體重與輸出入量。高風險族群:急性腎損傷、慢性腎病、心衰竭、長期使用類固醇者。
  • Hypotonic overhydration (water intoxication) occurs with SIADH, heart failure, early kidney disease, or hypotonic IV/irrigation fluids.低張性水過多(水中毒)見於 SIADH、心衰竭、早期腎病,或使用低張靜脈輸液/沖洗液。

5Potassium imbalances鉀離子失衡

  • Normal K+ is 3.5-5.0 mEq/L. Hypokalemia causes: diuretics, corticosteroids, albuterol, vomiting, diarrhea, NG suction, Cushing's, alkalosis, hyperinsulinism, NPO status.鉀正常值 3.5-5.0 mEq/L。低血鉀原因:利尿劑、類固醇、albuterol、嘔吐、腹瀉、鼻胃管抽吸、庫欣氏症、鹼中毒、高胰島素、禁食。
  • Hypokalemia findings: weak thready irregular pulse, shallow respirations, weakness and cramps, hypoactive bowel/paralytic ileus; ECG shows ST depression, flat/inverted T waves, prominent U waves.低血鉀表現:脈搏細弱不規則、呼吸淺、肌無力與抽筋、腸蠕動減弱/麻痺性腸阻塞;心電圖 ST 段下降、T 波平坦或倒置、U 波明顯。
  • Oral potassium: give with food/juice; liquid KCl tastes bad. IV potassium: always diluted, via infusion pump, never IV push, IM or subcutaneous.口服鉀劑須隨餐或加果汁服用;靜脈鉀劑一定要稀釋並使用輸液幫浦,絕不可靜脈推注、肌肉或皮下注射。
  • Hyperkalemia causes: K+ supplements or salt substitutes, K+-sparing diuretics, kidney disease, Addison's, tissue damage/burns, acidosis, stored blood.高血鉀原因:鉀補充劑或代鹽、保鉀利尿劑、腎病、愛迪生氏症、組織損傷/燒傷、酸中毒、庫存血。
  • Hyperkalemia findings: slow weak irregular pulse, hypotension, muscle weakness to ascending flaccid paralysis, hyperactive bowel/diarrhea; ECG shows tall peaked T, flat P, wide QRS, prolonged PR.高血鉀表現:心跳慢而弱且不規則、低血壓、肌無力甚至上行性弛緩性麻痺、腸蠕動亢進/腹瀉;心電圖 T 波高尖、P 波平坦、QRS 變寬、PR 間期延長。
  • Treat hyperkalemia: stop K+ intake, IV calcium (protect heart), insulin with glucose, sodium polystyrene sulfonate, K+-excreting diuretics, dialysis if critical.高血鉀處置:停止鉀攝入、靜脈鈣劑(保護心臟)、胰島素加葡萄糖、sodium polystyrene sulfonate、排鉀利尿劑,嚴重時透析。

6Sodium imbalances鈉離子失衡

  • Normal Na+ is 135-145 mEq/L; sodium problems usually travel with fluid volume problems.鈉正常值 135-145 mEq/L;鈉失衡通常伴隨體液容積問題。
  • Hyponatremia causes: diaphoresis, diuretics, vomiting, diarrhea, SIADH, hypotonic fluids, heart failure, hyperglycemia, freshwater drowning.低血鈉原因:大量流汗、利尿劑、嘔吐、腹瀉、SIADH、低張液體、心衰竭、高血糖、淡水溺水。
  • Hyponatremia findings: headache, confusion, seizures, weakness, hyperactive bowel sounds, abdominal cramping, increased urine output, low urine specific gravity.低血鈉表現:頭痛、意識混亂、癲癇、無力、腸蠕動亢進、腹絞痛、尿量增加、尿比重降低。
  • Correct hyponatremia slowly; rapid correction risks osmotic demyelination. Hyponatremia can raise lithium levels toward toxicity.低血鈉須緩慢矯正,過快有滲透性去髓鞘病變風險;低血鈉亦可導致鋰中毒。
  • Hypernatremia causes: corticosteroids, Cushing's, hyperaldosteronism, excess sodium intake, low water intake, fever, watery diarrhea, diabetes insipidus.高血鈉原因:類固醇、庫欣氏症、高醛固酮症、鈉攝取過多、水分攝取不足、發燒、水瀉、尿崩症。
  • Hypernatremia findings: extreme thirst, dry sticky mucosa, flushed skin, agitation, confusion, seizures; high urine specific gravity. Altered cerebral function is the most common sign.高血鈉表現:極度口渴、黏膜乾而黏、皮膚潮紅、躁動、意識混亂、癲癇;尿比重上升。腦功能改變為最常見症狀。

7Calcium imbalances鈣離子失衡

  • Normal calcium is 9-10.5 mg/dL. Corrected Ca = serum Ca + 0.8 x (4 - albumin). Ionized calcium normal is 4.64-5.28.鈣正常值 9-10.5 mg/dL。校正鈣 = 血清鈣 + 0.8 x (4 - 白蛋白)。離子鈣正常值 4.64-5.28。
  • Hypocalcemia causes: low intake or vitamin D, lactose intolerance, malabsorption (Crohn's, celiac), diarrhea, alkalosis, acute pancreatitis, hyperphosphatemia, hypoparathyroidism.低血鈣原因:攝取或維生素 D 不足、乳糖不耐、吸收不良(克隆氏症、乳糜瀉)、腹瀉、鹼中毒、急性胰臟炎、高血磷、副甲狀腺功能低下。
  • Hypocalcemia findings: tingling/numbness, twitching, cramps, tetany, seizures, hyperactive reflexes, positive Chvostek's and Trousseau's signs, increased GI motility; ECG prolonged ST and QT.低血鈣表現:麻刺感、抽搐、痙攣、手足僵直、癲癇、反射亢進、Chvostek 與 Trousseau 徵象陽性、腸蠕動增加;心電圖 ST 與 QT 延長。
  • Treat with oral calcium and vitamin D or slow warmed IV calcium (keep 10% calcium gluconate available), seizure precautions, quiet environment, careful handling (fracture risk).治療:口服鈣劑與維生素 D,或緩慢給予已加溫的靜脈鈣劑(備妥 10% 葡萄糖酸鈣)、癲癇防護、安靜環境、謹慎搬動(骨折風險)。
  • Hypercalcemia causes: hyperparathyroidism, hyperthyroidism, malignancy, immobility, thiazides, lithium, dehydration, excess calcium/vitamin D.高血鈣原因:副甲狀腺或甲狀腺功能亢進、惡性腫瘤、制動、thiazide 利尿劑、鋰、脫水、鈣/維生素 D 過量。
  • Hypercalcemia findings: muscle weakness, absent reflexes, constipation, anorexia, bradycardia/heart block; ECG shortened ST, widened T. Give isotonic saline, calcitonin, bisphosphonates; strain urine for stones.高血鈣表現:肌無力、反射消失、便秘、食慾差、心搏過緩/心臟傳導阻滯;心電圖 ST 縮短、T 波變寬。治療:等張生理食鹽水、calcitonin、雙磷酸鹽,並過濾尿液檢查結石。

8Magnesium imbalances鎂離子失衡

  • Normal magnesium is 1.8-2.6 mEq/L. Hypomagnesemia causes: malnutrition, alcoholism, diuretics, malabsorption, vomiting/diarrhea, insulin, sepsis.鎂正常值 1.8-2.6 mEq/L。低血鎂原因:營養不良、酒精中毒、利尿劑、吸收不良、嘔吐/腹瀉、胰島素、敗血症。
  • Hypomagnesemia findings: tachycardia, hypertension, hyperreflexia, tremors/twitching, tetany, seizures, positive Chvostek's/Trousseau's; often coexists with hypocalcemia.低血鎂表現:心跳快、高血壓、反射亢進、顫抖/抽搐、手足僵直、癲癇、Chvostek/Trousseau 陽性;常合併低血鈣。
  • IV magnesium sulfate is preferred in ill clients (IM is painful); monitor reflexes for loss, which signals toxicity.重症者以靜脈硫酸鎂補充(肌肉注射疼痛且傷組織);須監測深部肌腱反射,反射消失表示鎂過量。
  • Hypermagnesemia causes: Mg-containing antacids/laxatives, excess IV magnesium, renal insufficiency. Findings: bradycardia, hypotension, drowsiness, absent reflexes, respiratory depression; ECG prolonged PR, wide QRS.高血鎂原因:含鎂制酸劑/瀉劑、靜脈鎂過量、腎功能不全。表現:心跳慢、低血壓、嗜睡、反射消失、呼吸抑制;心電圖 PR 延長、QRS 變寬。
  • Antidote for magnesium overdose is IV calcium gluconate (or calcium chloride); diuretics increase excretion; avoid Mg-containing laxatives/antacids.鎂中毒的解毒劑為靜脈葡萄糖酸鈣(或氯化鈣);利尿劑促進排出;避免使用含鎂瀉劑與制酸劑。

9Phosphorus imbalances磷離子失衡

  • Normal phosphorus is 3.0-4.5 mg/dL. Phosphorus and calcium have a reciprocal relationship: when one rises, the other falls.磷正常值 3.0-4.5 mg/dL。磷與鈣呈相反關係:一方上升,另一方下降。
  • Hypophosphatemia causes: malnutrition/starvation, hyperparathyroidism, malignancy, Mg- or aluminum-based antacids, hyperglycemia, respiratory alkalosis. Mild cases may be symptom-free.低血磷原因:營養不良/飢餓、副甲狀腺亢進、惡性腫瘤、含鎂或鋁的制酸劑、高血糖、呼吸性鹼中毒。輕度可能無症狀。
  • Treat hypophosphatemia with oral phosphorus plus vitamin D; IV phosphorus only when below 1 mg/dL with critical signs, given slowly after assessing renal function.低血磷以口服磷加維生素 D 治療;僅在低於 1 mg/dL 並有嚴重症狀時才靜脈給磷,須先評估腎功能並緩慢輸注。
  • Hyperphosphatemia causes: renal insufficiency, tumor lysis syndrome, hypoparathyroidism, phosphate laxatives/enemas. Its problems come from the resulting hypocalcemia.高血磷原因:腎功能不全、腫瘤溶解症候群、副甲狀腺功能低下、含磷瀉劑/灌腸劑。其問題主要來自隨之發生的低血鈣。
  • Give phosphate binders with or right after meals; limit high-phosphorus foods; avoid phosphate laxatives and enemas.磷結合劑須隨餐或餐後立即服用;減少高磷食物;避免含磷瀉劑與灌腸劑。

  • Kidneys are the major regulators; aldosterone retains sodium (and water), and ADH controls water reabsorption.腎臟是主要調節器官;醛固酮促進鈉(及水)再吸收,抗利尿激素(ADH)調節水分再吸收。
  • Severe diarrhea causes large fluid and electrolyte losses. With any imbalance, monitor cardiovascular, respiratory, neurological, musculoskeletal, renal, skin and GI status.嚴重腹瀉會造成大量體液與電解質流失。發生任何失衡時,須監測心血管、呼吸、神經、肌肉骨骼、腎臟、皮膚與腸胃狀態。
Topic 4 · tap to flip主題 4 · 點擊翻面
Fluid volume deficit and excess體液容積不足與過多
  • Isotonic dehydration (hypovolemia) is the most common deficit: water and electrolytes are lost equally. Hypertonic: water loss exceeds electrolytes (cells shrink). Hypotonic: electrolyte loss exceeds water (cells swell).等張性脫水(低血容)最常見,水與電解質等比例流失;高張性脫水為水分流失多於電解質(細胞萎縮);低張性脫水為電解質流失多於水分(細胞腫脹)。
  • Deficit findings: tachycardia with thready pulse, hypotension, flat neck veins, low CVP, dry mucosa, poor turgor, weight loss, low urine output, high hematocrit/BUN/osmolality/urine specific gravity.容積不足表現:脈搏快而細弱、低血壓、頸靜脈扁塌、CVP 低、黏膜乾燥、皮膚彈性差、體重下降、尿量少,血比容、BUN、滲透壓與尿比重上升。
  • Excess findings: bounding pulse, high BP, distended neck veins, high CVP, crackles, dyspnea, edema, weight gain, ascites; low hematocrit/BUN/osmolality/urine specific gravity.容積過多表現:脈搏洪大、高血壓、頸靜脈怒張、CVP 高、囉音、呼吸困難、水腫、體重增加、腹水;血比容、BUN、滲透壓與尿比重下降。
  • Treat deficit with oral or IV fluids and treat the cause (isotonic loss with isotonic fluid, hypertonic with hypotonic, hypotonic with hypertonic fluid); monitor I&O.不足時給予口服或靜脈輸液並處理原因(等張流失用等張液、高張性用低張液、低張性用高張液),並監測輸出入量。
  • Treat excess with diuretics, fluid and sodium restriction, daily weight and I&O. Highest risk: AKI, CKD, heart failure, long-term corticosteroids.過多時給予利尿劑、限制水分與鈉攝取,監測每日體重與輸出入量。高風險族群:急性腎損傷、慢性腎病、心衰竭、長期使用類固醇者。
  • Hypotonic overhydration (water intoxication) occurs with SIADH, heart failure, early kidney disease, or hypotonic IV/irrigation fluids.低張性水過多(水中毒)見於 SIADH、心衰竭、早期腎病,或使用低張靜脈輸液/沖洗液。
Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Potassium imbalances鉀離子失衡
  • Normal K+ is 3.5-5.0 mEq/L. Hypokalemia causes: diuretics, corticosteroids, albuterol, vomiting, diarrhea, NG suction, Cushing's, alkalosis, hyperinsulinism, NPO status.鉀正常值 3.5-5.0 mEq/L。低血鉀原因:利尿劑、類固醇、albuterol、嘔吐、腹瀉、鼻胃管抽吸、庫欣氏症、鹼中毒、高胰島素、禁食。
  • Hypokalemia findings: weak thready irregular pulse, shallow respirations, weakness and cramps, hypoactive bowel/paralytic ileus; ECG shows ST depression, flat/inverted T waves, prominent U waves.低血鉀表現:脈搏細弱不規則、呼吸淺、肌無力與抽筋、腸蠕動減弱/麻痺性腸阻塞;心電圖 ST 段下降、T 波平坦或倒置、U 波明顯。
  • Oral potassium: give with food/juice; liquid KCl tastes bad. IV potassium: always diluted, via infusion pump, never IV push, IM or subcutaneous.口服鉀劑須隨餐或加果汁服用;靜脈鉀劑一定要稀釋並使用輸液幫浦,絕不可靜脈推注、肌肉或皮下注射。
  • Hyperkalemia causes: K+ supplements or salt substitutes, K+-sparing diuretics, kidney disease, Addison's, tissue damage/burns, acidosis, stored blood.高血鉀原因:鉀補充劑或代鹽、保鉀利尿劑、腎病、愛迪生氏症、組織損傷/燒傷、酸中毒、庫存血。
  • Hyperkalemia findings: slow weak irregular pulse, hypotension, muscle weakness to ascending flaccid paralysis, hyperactive bowel/diarrhea; ECG shows tall peaked T, flat P, wide QRS, prolonged PR.高血鉀表現:心跳慢而弱且不規則、低血壓、肌無力甚至上行性弛緩性麻痺、腸蠕動亢進/腹瀉;心電圖 T 波高尖、P 波平坦、QRS 變寬、PR 間期延長。
  • Treat hyperkalemia: stop K+ intake, IV calcium (protect heart), insulin with glucose, sodium polystyrene sulfonate, K+-excreting diuretics, dialysis if critical.高血鉀處置:停止鉀攝入、靜脈鈣劑(保護心臟)、胰島素加葡萄糖、sodium polystyrene sulfonate、排鉀利尿劑,嚴重時透析。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
Sodium imbalances鈉離子失衡
  • Normal Na+ is 135-145 mEq/L; sodium problems usually travel with fluid volume problems.鈉正常值 135-145 mEq/L;鈉失衡通常伴隨體液容積問題。
  • Hyponatremia causes: diaphoresis, diuretics, vomiting, diarrhea, SIADH, hypotonic fluids, heart failure, hyperglycemia, freshwater drowning.低血鈉原因:大量流汗、利尿劑、嘔吐、腹瀉、SIADH、低張液體、心衰竭、高血糖、淡水溺水。
  • Hyponatremia findings: headache, confusion, seizures, weakness, hyperactive bowel sounds, abdominal cramping, increased urine output, low urine specific gravity.低血鈉表現:頭痛、意識混亂、癲癇、無力、腸蠕動亢進、腹絞痛、尿量增加、尿比重降低。
  • Correct hyponatremia slowly; rapid correction risks osmotic demyelination. Hyponatremia can raise lithium levels toward toxicity.低血鈉須緩慢矯正,過快有滲透性去髓鞘病變風險;低血鈉亦可導致鋰中毒。
  • Hypernatremia causes: corticosteroids, Cushing's, hyperaldosteronism, excess sodium intake, low water intake, fever, watery diarrhea, diabetes insipidus.高血鈉原因:類固醇、庫欣氏症、高醛固酮症、鈉攝取過多、水分攝取不足、發燒、水瀉、尿崩症。
  • Hypernatremia findings: extreme thirst, dry sticky mucosa, flushed skin, agitation, confusion, seizures; high urine specific gravity. Altered cerebral function is the most common sign.高血鈉表現:極度口渴、黏膜乾而黏、皮膚潮紅、躁動、意識混亂、癲癇;尿比重上升。腦功能改變為最常見症狀。
Topic 7 · tap to flip主題 7 · 點擊翻面
Calcium imbalances鈣離子失衡
  • Normal calcium is 9-10.5 mg/dL. Corrected Ca = serum Ca + 0.8 x (4 - albumin). Ionized calcium normal is 4.64-5.28.鈣正常值 9-10.5 mg/dL。校正鈣 = 血清鈣 + 0.8 x (4 - 白蛋白)。離子鈣正常值 4.64-5.28。
  • Hypocalcemia causes: low intake or vitamin D, lactose intolerance, malabsorption (Crohn's, celiac), diarrhea, alkalosis, acute pancreatitis, hyperphosphatemia, hypoparathyroidism.低血鈣原因:攝取或維生素 D 不足、乳糖不耐、吸收不良(克隆氏症、乳糜瀉)、腹瀉、鹼中毒、急性胰臟炎、高血磷、副甲狀腺功能低下。
  • Hypocalcemia findings: tingling/numbness, twitching, cramps, tetany, seizures, hyperactive reflexes, positive Chvostek's and Trousseau's signs, increased GI motility; ECG prolonged ST and QT.低血鈣表現:麻刺感、抽搐、痙攣、手足僵直、癲癇、反射亢進、Chvostek 與 Trousseau 徵象陽性、腸蠕動增加;心電圖 ST 與 QT 延長。
  • Treat with oral calcium and vitamin D or slow warmed IV calcium (keep 10% calcium gluconate available), seizure precautions, quiet environment, careful handling (fracture risk).治療:口服鈣劑與維生素 D,或緩慢給予已加溫的靜脈鈣劑(備妥 10% 葡萄糖酸鈣)、癲癇防護、安靜環境、謹慎搬動(骨折風險)。
  • Hypercalcemia causes: hyperparathyroidism, hyperthyroidism, malignancy, immobility, thiazides, lithium, dehydration, excess calcium/vitamin D.高血鈣原因:副甲狀腺或甲狀腺功能亢進、惡性腫瘤、制動、thiazide 利尿劑、鋰、脫水、鈣/維生素 D 過量。
  • Hypercalcemia findings: muscle weakness, absent reflexes, constipation, anorexia, bradycardia/heart block; ECG shortened ST, widened T. Give isotonic saline, calcitonin, bisphosphonates; strain urine for stones.高血鈣表現:肌無力、反射消失、便秘、食慾差、心搏過緩/心臟傳導阻滯;心電圖 ST 縮短、T 波變寬。治療:等張生理食鹽水、calcitonin、雙磷酸鹽,並過濾尿液檢查結石。
Topic 8 · tap to flip主題 8 · 點擊翻面
Magnesium imbalances鎂離子失衡
  • Normal magnesium is 1.8-2.6 mEq/L. Hypomagnesemia causes: malnutrition, alcoholism, diuretics, malabsorption, vomiting/diarrhea, insulin, sepsis.鎂正常值 1.8-2.6 mEq/L。低血鎂原因:營養不良、酒精中毒、利尿劑、吸收不良、嘔吐/腹瀉、胰島素、敗血症。
  • Hypomagnesemia findings: tachycardia, hypertension, hyperreflexia, tremors/twitching, tetany, seizures, positive Chvostek's/Trousseau's; often coexists with hypocalcemia.低血鎂表現:心跳快、高血壓、反射亢進、顫抖/抽搐、手足僵直、癲癇、Chvostek/Trousseau 陽性;常合併低血鈣。
  • IV magnesium sulfate is preferred in ill clients (IM is painful); monitor reflexes for loss, which signals toxicity.重症者以靜脈硫酸鎂補充(肌肉注射疼痛且傷組織);須監測深部肌腱反射,反射消失表示鎂過量。
  • Hypermagnesemia causes: Mg-containing antacids/laxatives, excess IV magnesium, renal insufficiency. Findings: bradycardia, hypotension, drowsiness, absent reflexes, respiratory depression; ECG prolonged PR, wide QRS.高血鎂原因:含鎂制酸劑/瀉劑、靜脈鎂過量、腎功能不全。表現:心跳慢、低血壓、嗜睡、反射消失、呼吸抑制;心電圖 PR 延長、QRS 變寬。
  • Antidote for magnesium overdose is IV calcium gluconate (or calcium chloride); diuretics increase excretion; avoid Mg-containing laxatives/antacids.鎂中毒的解毒劑為靜脈葡萄糖酸鈣(或氯化鈣);利尿劑促進排出;避免使用含鎂瀉劑與制酸劑。
Topic 9 · tap to flip主題 9 · 點擊翻面
Phosphorus imbalances磷離子失衡
  • Normal phosphorus is 3.0-4.5 mg/dL. Phosphorus and calcium have a reciprocal relationship: when one rises, the other falls.磷正常值 3.0-4.5 mg/dL。磷與鈣呈相反關係:一方上升,另一方下降。
  • Hypophosphatemia causes: malnutrition/starvation, hyperparathyroidism, malignancy, Mg- or aluminum-based antacids, hyperglycemia, respiratory alkalosis. Mild cases may be symptom-free.低血磷原因:營養不良/飢餓、副甲狀腺亢進、惡性腫瘤、含鎂或鋁的制酸劑、高血糖、呼吸性鹼中毒。輕度可能無症狀。
  • Treat hypophosphatemia with oral phosphorus plus vitamin D; IV phosphorus only when below 1 mg/dL with critical signs, given slowly after assessing renal function.低血磷以口服磷加維生素 D 治療;僅在低於 1 mg/dL 並有嚴重症狀時才靜脈給磷,須先評估腎功能並緩慢輸注。
  • Hyperphosphatemia causes: renal insufficiency, tumor lysis syndrome, hypoparathyroidism, phosphate laxatives/enemas. Its problems come from the resulting hypocalcemia.高血磷原因:腎功能不全、腫瘤溶解症候群、副甲狀腺功能低下、含磷瀉劑/灌腸劑。其問題主要來自隨之發生的低血鈣。
  • Give phosphate binders with or right after meals; limit high-phosphorus foods; avoid phosphate laxatives and enemas.磷結合劑須隨餐或餐後立即服用;減少高磷食物;避免含磷瀉劑與灌腸劑。
