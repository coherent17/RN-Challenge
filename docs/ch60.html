Part XV · Neurological Problems of the Adult Client成人神經問題

Chapter 60: Neurological Medications第 60 章：神經系統藥物

📖 Source: PDF p.969–980 · printed book p.877–887.e1來源：PDF 第 969–980 頁 · 書本印刷頁 877–887.e1

Drug classes used in neurological care: antimyasthenics, multiple sclerosis agents, antiparkinsonians, antiseizure drugs, CNS stimulants, NSAIDs and acetaminophen, opioids and antagonists, and osmotic diuretics. Focus on actions, toxicity signs, serum levels, antidotes and teaching.涵蓋神經照護常用藥物類別：抗肌無力藥、多發性硬化症用藥、抗帕金森藥、抗癲癇藥、中樞神經興奮劑、NSAID與acetaminophen、鴉片類與拮抗劑、滲透性利尿劑。重點為作用、毒性徵象、血中濃度、解毒劑與衛教。

Antimyasthenic (anticholinesterase) drugs抗肌無力藥（抗膽鹼酯酶） 📖 PDF p.969 (book p.877)

  • Class/examples: neostigmine, pyridostigmine (treatment); edrophonium (diagnosis, distinguishes myasthenic from cholinergic crisis).類別/例：neostigmine、pyridostigmine（治療）；edrophonium（診斷，區分肌無力與膽鹼性危象）。
  • Action: block acetylcholine breakdown at the neuromuscular junction, improving strength.作用：阻斷神經肌肉接合處乙醯膽鹼分解，增進肌力。
  • Adverse effects (overdose = cholinergic crisis): abdominal cramps, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, miosis, blurred vision, bradycardia, bronchospasm, increased secretions, sweating, hypotension, facial twitching.不良反應（過量＝膽鹼性危象）：腹絞痛、噁心嘔吐、腹瀉、瞳孔縮小、視力模糊、心搏過緩、支氣管痙攣、分泌物增加、出汗、低血壓、臉部抽動。
  • Antidote: atropine sulfate; keep atropine and resuscitation equipment ready during edrophonium use.解毒劑：atropine sulfate；使用edrophonium時備妥atropine與急救設備。
  • Nursing: give on time (lifelong), with a little food; meal 45-60 min after dose to reduce aspiration risk; assess strength, reflexes, gait; MedicAlert bracelet.護理：準時服用（終生）、搭配少量食物；服藥後45-60分鐘再進餐以降低吸入風險；評估肌力、反射、步態；配戴醫療警示手環。

Multiple sclerosis medications多發性硬化症藥物 📖 PDF p.969 (book p.877), PDF p.970 (book p.878)

  • Disease-modifying immunomodulators: interferon beta, glatiramer, dimethyl fumarate, fingolimod, teriflunomide, cladribine; reduce relapses and lesions.疾病修飾免疫調節劑：interferon beta、glatiramer、dimethyl fumarate、fingolimod、teriflunomide、cladribine；減少復發與病灶。
  • Immunomodulator adverse effects: flu-like reactions, hepatotoxicity, myelosuppression, injection-site reactions, depression.免疫調節劑不良反應：類流感反應、肝毒性、骨髓抑制、注射部位反應、憂鬱。
  • Immunosuppressants (mitoxantrone) and monoclonal antibodies (alemtuzumab, natalizumab, ocrelizumab, ofatumumab): myelosuppression, cardiotoxicity, fetal harm, alopecia, GI injury.免疫抑制劑（mitoxantrone）與單株抗體（alemtuzumab、natalizumab、ocrelizumab、ofatumumab）：骨髓抑制、心毒性、胎兒傷害、落髮、胃腸黏膜損傷。
  • Acute relapse: high-dose IV glucocorticoid (methylprednisolone) or IV immune globulin.急性復發：高劑量靜脈類固醇（methylprednisolone）或靜脈免疫球蛋白。
  • Symptom drugs: psyllium/docusate (bowel), memantine (cognition), fluoxetine/sertraline (depression), amantadine/modafinil (fatigue), gabapentin/carbamazepine (neuropathic pain), dalfampridine (walking speed).症狀用藥：psyllium/docusate（腸道）、memantine（認知）、fluoxetine/sertraline（憂鬱）、amantadine/modafinil（疲倦）、gabapentin/carbamazepine（神經痛）、dalfampridine（行走速度）。

Antiparkinsonian drugs抗帕金森藥 📖 PDF p.970 (book p.878), PDF p.971 (book p.879)

  • Dopaminergics (carbidopa-levodopa, pramipexole, ropinirole, bromocriptine, amantadine, apomorphine, selegiline, rasagiline): increase dopamine activity; contraindicated in significant cardiac, renal or psychiatric disease.多巴胺類（carbidopa-levodopa、pramipexole、ropinirole、bromocriptine、amantadine、apomorphine、selegiline、rasagiline）：增加多巴胺作用；重度心、腎或精神疾病者禁用。
  • Adverse effects: dyskinesia, orthostatic hypotension, nausea/vomiting, hallucinations, confusion, depression, urinary retention, constipation, insomnia.不良反應：異動症、姿位性低血壓、噁心嘔吐、幻覺、意識混亂、憂鬱、尿滯留、便秘、失眠。
  • Carbidopa-levodopa with MAO inhibitor antidepressant can cause hypertensive crisis; avoid excess vitamin B6; spread protein across meals; urine and sweat may darken (harmless); do not stop abruptly.carbidopa-levodopa與MAO抑制劑類抗憂鬱藥併用可致高血壓危象；避免過量維生素B6；蛋白質平均分配於各餐；尿與汗可能變色（無害）；勿突然停藥。
  • Diabetic clients should not rely on urine glucose testing with levodopa.糖尿病病人使用levodopa時勿以尿糖檢測。
  • Anticholinergics (benztropine, trihexyphenidyl): reduce tremor and drooling; little effect on rigidity/bradykinesia; contraindicated in glaucoma.抗膽鹼藥（benztropine、trihexyphenidyl）：減少顫抖與流口水；對僵硬與動作遲緩效果小；青光眼禁用。
  • Anticholinergic effects: dry mouth, blurred vision, tachycardia, constipation, urinary retention, confusion, photophobia; use ice chips/sugarless candy, sunglasses, fluids and fiber; abrupt stop worsens parkinsonism.抗膽鹼副作用：口乾、視力模糊、心搏過速、便秘、尿滯留、意識混亂、畏光；以冰塊/無糖糖果、太陽眼鏡、水分與纖維緩解；突然停藥使症狀加重。
  • COMT inhibitors (entacapone, opicapone, tolcapone) block peripheral dopamine metabolism and extend levodopa effect.COMT抑制劑（entacapone、opicapone、tolcapone）阻斷周邊多巴胺代謝，延長levodopa效果。

Antiseizure drugs抗癲癇藥 📖 PDF p.971 (book p.879), PDF p.972 (book p.880), PDF p.973 (book p.881)

  • Action: depress abnormal neuronal discharge and prevent spread. Monitor serum levels, liver and renal function, signs of toxicity (CNS depression, ataxia, nystagmus, visual disturbance). Seizure precautions.作用：抑制異常神經放電並防止擴散。監測血中濃度、肝腎功能與毒性徵象（中樞抑制、運動失調、眼震、視覺障礙）。癲癇預防措施。
  • Therapeutic ranges: phenytoin 10-20 mcg/mL, carbamazepine 3-14, divalproex 50-100, phenobarbital 15-40, ethosuximide 40-100 mcg/mL; clonazepam 20-80 and lorazepam 50-240 ng/mL.治療濃度：phenytoin 10-20 mcg/mL、carbamazepine 3-14、divalproex 50-100、phenobarbital 15-40、ethosuximide 40-100 mcg/mL；clonazepam 20-80、lorazepam 50-240 ng/mL。
  • Hydantoins (phenytoin, fosphenytoin): gingival hyperplasia, nystagmus, ataxia, hirsutism, blood dyscrasias, hyperglycemia, teratogenic, may reduce birth control effectiveness. IV: normal saline only (dextrose precipitates), inline filter, no faster than 25-50 mg/min (hypotension, dysrhythmias); separate tube feedings.乙內醯脲類（phenytoin、fosphenytoin）：牙齦增生、眼震、運動失調、多毛、血液惡病質、高血糖、致畸胎、可能降低避孕藥效果。靜脈：僅用生理食鹽水（葡萄糖會沈澱）、使用過濾器、速率不超過25-50 mg/分（低血壓、心律不整）；與管灌飲食分開給藥。
  • Barbiturates (phenobarbital): tonic-clonic and status epilepticus; sedation, respiratory depression, hypotension, tolerance.巴比妥類（phenobarbital）：強直-陣攣與癲癇重積；鎮靜、呼吸抑制、低血壓、耐受性。
  • Benzodiazepines (diazepam, lorazepam, clonazepam, clorazepate): status epilepticus, absence; sedation, dependence, hepatotoxicity, blood dyscrasias; IV slowly; antidote flumazenil but avoid in increased ICP or status epilepticus treated with benzodiazepines.苯二氮平類（diazepam、lorazepam、clonazepam、clorazepate）：癲癇重積、失神發作；鎮靜、依賴、肝毒性、血液惡病質；靜脈緩慢給予；解毒劑flumazenil，但顱內壓升高或以苯二氮平治療的癲癇重積者避免使用。
  • Succinimides (ethosuximide): absence seizures; GI upset, blood dyscrasias.Succinimide類（ethosuximide）：失神發作；腸胃不適、血液惡病質。
  • Valproates (valproic acid, divalproex): pancreatitis, hepatotoxicity, thrombocytopenia, teratogenic (neural tube defects).Valproate類（valproic acid、divalproex）：胰臟炎、肝毒性、血小板低下、致畸胎（神經管缺損）。
  • Carbamazepine (iminostilbene): also trigeminal neuralgia; drowsiness, rash, agranulocytosis/aplastic anemia - report sore throat, bruising, fever; check CBC.Carbamazepine（亞胺芪類）：亦用於三叉神經痛；嗜睡、皮疹、顆粒球缺乏/再生不良性貧血－喉嚨痛、瘀青、發燒須通報；監測CBC。
  • Education: never stop abruptly, avoid alcohol, take with food but not milk/antacids, soft toothbrush and dental care, medical ID.衛教：勿突然停藥、避免酒精、隨食物服用但避免牛奶/制酸劑、軟毛牙刷與牙科檢查、配戴醫療警示。

CNS stimulants中樞神經興奮劑 📖 PDF p.973 (book p.881), PDF p.974 (book p.882)

  • Examples: amphetamines, methylphenidate, dexmethylphenidate, lisdexamfetamine, atomoxetine, caffeine; anorexiants (phentermine). Used for narcolepsy, ADHD, adjunct for obesity.例：安非他命類、methylphenidate、dexmethylphenidate、lisdexamfetamine、atomoxetine、咖啡因；食慾抑制劑（phentermine）。用於猝睡症、ADHD、肥胖輔助。
  • Adverse effects: irritability, insomnia, tachycardia, hypertension, dysrhythmias, anorexia/weight loss, psychosis, dependence.不良反應：易怒、失眠、心搏過速、高血壓、心律不整、厭食/體重減輕、精神病症狀、依賴。
  • Nursing: monitor vital signs, CBC, growth in children; give before meals, last dose at least 6 h before bedtime; do not crush long-acting forms; avoid caffeine; do not stop abruptly; effect in ADHD in 3-4 weeks.護理：監測生命徵象、CBC、兒童生長；餐前服用、最後一劑在睡前至少6小時；長效劑型勿壓碎；避免咖啡因；勿突然停藥；ADHD約3-4週見效。

NSAIDs, aspirin and acetaminophenNSAID、阿斯匹靈與acetaminophen 📖 PDF p.974 (book p.882), PDF p.975 (book p.883)

  • NSAIDs/aspirin inhibit prostaglandin synthesis: analgesic, antipyretic, anti-inflammatory, antiplatelet. Examples: ibuprofen, naproxen, ketorolac, diclofenac, indomethacin, meloxicam; celecoxib (COX-2).NSAID/aspirin抑制前列腺素合成：止痛、退燒、抗發炎、抗血小板。例：ibuprofen、naproxen、ketorolac、diclofenac、indomethacin、meloxicam；celecoxib（COX-2）。
  • Adverse effects: GI irritation/bleeding, renal impairment, fluid retention, tinnitus (salicylism), cardiovascular thrombotic events, blood dyscrasias. Monitor salicylate level with high doses.不良反應：胃腸刺激/出血、腎功能下降、水腫、耳鳴（水楊酸中毒）、心血管血栓事件、血液惡病質。大劑量時監測水楊酸濃度。
  • Avoid in anticoagulant therapy and liver/renal disease; do not combine aspirin with another NSAID; children with flu/varicella: no aspirin (Reye's syndrome); stop aspirin 3-7 days before surgery as prescribed.抗凝血治療與肝腎疾病者避免；aspirin不與其他NSAID併用；兒童流感/水痘勿用aspirin（雷氏症候群）；依醫囑術前3-7天停aspirin。
  • Teach: take with food/milk, sit upright 20-30 min after aspirin, do not crush enteric-coated tablets, avoid alcohol, watch for tarry stools and bruising.衛教：隨食物/牛奶服用、服aspirin後坐直20-30分鐘、腸溶錠勿壓碎、避免酒精、注意黑便與瘀青。
  • Acetaminophen: analgesic and antipyretic; hepatotoxicity risk above 4000 mg/day; antidote acetylcysteine; avoid in liver disease and alcoholism; self-use no longer than 10 days (adult) / 5 days (child); monitor liver enzymes.Acetaminophen：止痛退燒；每日超過4000 mg有肝毒性風險；解毒劑acetylcysteine；肝病與酗酒者避免；自行使用成人不超過10天、兒童5天；監測肝酵素。

Opioids and opioid antagonists鴉片類與鴉片拮抗劑 📖 PDF p.975 (book p.883), PDF p.976 (book p.884)

  • Examples: morphine, hydromorphone, fentanyl, methadone, oxycodone, codeine, tapentadol. Act on opioid receptors; cause sedation, euphoria, dependence.例：morphine、hydromorphone、fentanyl、methadone、oxycodone、codeine、tapentadol。作用於鴉片受體；造成鎮靜、欣快、依賴。
  • Adverse effects: respiratory depression, orthostatic hypotension, urinary retention, nausea/vomiting, constipation, sedation, confusion, cough suppression, miosis.不良反應：呼吸抑制、姿位性低血壓、尿滯留、噁心嘔吐、便秘、鎮靜、意識混亂、咳嗽抑制、瞳孔縮小。
  • Hold dose if adult respiratory rate <12 (unless ventilated or terminal as prescribed) or bradycardia; give 30-60 min before painful activity; encourage turning, deep breathing; manage constipation.成人呼吸<12次/分（除非有呼吸支持或依醫囑末期）或心搏過緩時暫停給藥；疼痛活動前30-60分鐘給藥；鼓勵翻身深呼吸；處理便秘。
  • Opioid antagonists (naloxone, naltrexone, methylnaltrexone, naloxegol, alvimopan): reverse opioid respiratory depression.鴉片拮抗劑（naloxone、naltrexone、methylnaltrexone、naloxegol、alvimopan）：逆轉鴉片所致呼吸抑制。
  • After naloxone: check BP, pulse, respirations every 5 min then taper, cardiac monitor, stay with client; its short half-life means overdose signs can return.給予naloxone後：每5分鐘測血壓、脈搏、呼吸後逐漸延長、心電監測、不離開病人；因半衰期短，過量症狀可能再發。

Osmotic diuretics (mannitol)滲透性利尿劑（mannitol） 📖 PDF p.976 (book p.884), PDF p.977 (book p.885)

  • Action: raises osmotic pressure of filtrate; used to lower ICP and intraocular pressure and for oliguria.作用：提高濾液滲透壓；用於降低顱內壓與眼壓、少尿。
  • Adverse effects: fluid and electrolyte imbalance, pulmonary edema, hyponatremia, dehydration, headache, tachycardia.不良反應：體液電解質失衡、肺水腫、低血鈉、脫水、頭痛、心搏過速。
  • Monitor weight, urine output, electrolytes, lungs, neuro status; do not use mannitol vials with crystals; change position slowly.監測體重、尿量、電解質、肺部、神經狀態；有結晶之mannitol瓶勿使用；緩慢改變姿勢。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Cholinergic crisis (anticholinesterase overdose): cramps, diarrhea, miosis, bradycardia, secretions; withhold drug and give atropine.膽鹼性危象（抗膽鹼酯酶過量）：絞痛、腹瀉、瞳孔縮小、心搏過緩、分泌物多；停藥並給atropine。 📖 PDF p.969 (book p.877), PDF p.970 (book p.878)
  2. Edrophonium: improvement = myasthenic crisis (needs more drug); worsening = cholinergic crisis. Have atropine and resuscitation equipment ready.Edrophonium：改善＝肌無力危象（需加藥）；惡化＝膽鹼性危象。備妥atropine與急救設備。 📖 PDF p.969 (book p.877), PDF p.978 (book p.886)
  3. Phenytoin therapeutic level 10-20 mcg/mL; nystagmus above 20, ataxia and slurred speech above 30.Phenytoin治療濃度10-20 mcg/mL；超過20出現眼震、超過30出現運動失調與口齒不清。 📖 PDF p.972 (book p.880), PDF p.978 (book p.886)
  4. IV phenytoin: normal saline only, inline filter, max 25-50 mg/min; fast infusion causes hypotension and dysrhythmias.靜脈phenytoin：僅用生理食鹽水、過濾器、最快25-50 mg/分；過快造成低血壓與心律不整。 📖 PDF p.972 (book p.880)
  5. Phenytoin: gingival hyperplasia - good oral hygiene; may decrease birth control effectiveness; teratogenic.Phenytoin：牙齦增生－保持口腔衛生；可能降低避孕藥效果；致畸胎。 📖 PDF p.972 (book p.880)
  6. Carbamazepine: agranulocytosis/aplastic anemia; low WBC is the adverse-effect lab finding.Carbamazepine：顆粒球缺乏/再生不良性貧血；白血球過低為不良反應檢驗發現。 📖 PDF p.973 (book p.881), PDF p.979 (book p.887)
  7. Valproate: hepatotoxicity, pancreatitis, thrombocytopenia, neural tube defects.Valproate：肝毒性、胰臟炎、血小板低下、神經管缺損。 📖 PDF p.973 (book p.881)
  8. Flumazenil reverses benzodiazepines but avoid with increased ICP or seizure disorders treated with benzodiazepines.Flumazenil可逆轉苯二氮平，但顱內壓升高或以苯二氮平控制癲癇者避免。 📖 PDF p.973 (book p.881)
  9. Carbidopa-levodopa: avoid MAOIs (hypertensive crisis), limit vitamin B6, spread protein; dyskinesia is a key adverse effect; never stop abruptly.Carbidopa-levodopa：避免MAO抑制劑（高血壓危象）、限制維生素B6、蛋白質分散；異動症為重要副作用；勿突然停藥。 📖 PDF p.970 (book p.878), PDF p.971 (book p.879)
  10. Anticholinergics are contraindicated in glaucoma; cause dry mouth, constipation, urinary retention.抗膽鹼藥青光眼禁用；造成口乾、便秘、尿滯留。 📖 PDF p.971 (book p.879)
  11. Acetaminophen: max 4000 mg/day, liver toxicity (elevated bilirubin/enzymes), antidote acetylcysteine.Acetaminophen：每日上限4000 mg、肝毒性（膽紅素/酵素上升）、解毒劑acetylcysteine。 📖 PDF p.975 (book p.883), PDF p.979 (book p.887)
  12. Aspirin toxicity (salicylism): tinnitus is the classic early sign; no aspirin in children with viral illness (Reye's).Aspirin中毒（水楊酸中毒）：耳鳴為典型早期徵象；病毒感染兒童勿用aspirin（雷氏症候群）。 📖 PDF p.974 (book p.882), PDF p.975 (book p.883), PDF p.979 (book p.887)
  13. Opioids: hold and notify if respirations <12; keep naloxone available; constipation is expected; naloxone half-life is short.鴉片類：呼吸<12次/分暫停給藥並通報；備妥naloxone；便秘為預期副作用；naloxone半衰期短。 📖 PDF p.976 (book p.884)
  14. Mannitol: do not use if crystals in vial; monitor for pulmonary edema, electrolytes and urine output.Mannitol：瓶內有結晶勿使用；監測肺水腫、電解質與尿量。 📖 PDF p.977 (book p.885)
  15. Stimulants: give last dose 6 h before bed, no caffeine, monitor growth in children.興奮劑：最後一劑睡前至少6小時、避免咖啡因、監測兒童生長。 📖 PDF p.974 (book p.882)

  • Phenytoin toxicity ladder: 10-20 therapeutic, >20 nystagmus, >30 ataxia/slurred speech.Phenytoin中毒階梯：10-20治療範圍、>20眼震、>30運動失調/口齒不清。 📖 PDF p.972 (book p.880), PDF p.978 (book p.886)
  • Anticholinergic effects: dry as a bone (mouth), blind as a bat (blurred vision), red-flushed, constipated, can't pee.抗膽鹼副作用：口乾、視力模糊、皮膚潮紅、便秘、尿滯留。 📖 PDF p.971 (book p.879)

1. A client taking carbidopa-levodopa reports the urine and sweat are dark. What should the nurse say?服用carbidopa-levodopa的病人表示尿液與汗水顏色變深。護理師應如何回應？

Discoloration of urine and sweat is harmless. Do not stop abruptly, and spread protein over the day.尿與汗變色無害。勿突然停藥，蛋白質應分散於全日。

2. A client receiving phenytoin has a level of 35 mcg/mL. Which finding is expected?接受phenytoin治療的病人血中濃度35 mcg/mL。預期出現何種表現？

Therapeutic is 10-20; levels above 30 cause ataxia and slurred speech.治療範圍10-20；超過30會出現運動失調與口齒不清。

4. A nurse prepares IV phenytoin. Which action is correct?護理師準備靜脈phenytoin。何者正確？

Dextrose causes precipitation; rapid infusion causes hypotension and dysrhythmias.葡萄糖造成沈澱；過快輸注造成低血壓與心律不整。

5. A client given edrophonium for suspected crisis becomes temporarily weaker. What does this indicate and what is prepared?疑似危象病人注射edrophonium後暫時更無力。此代表何意？應準備什麼？

Worsening after edrophonium means overmedication; atropine is the antidote.注射後惡化代表藥物過量；解毒劑為atropine。

6. A client taking oxycodone has a respiratory rate of 10 breaths/min and is drowsy. What is the priority action?服用oxycodone的病人呼吸10次/分且嗜睡。首要行動為何？

Respiratory depression is life-threatening; naloxone has a short half-life so monitor closely afterward.呼吸抑制危及生命；naloxone半衰期短，之後需密切監測。

7. Which finding suggests acetaminophen toxicity?下列何者提示acetaminophen毒性？

Acetaminophen overdose injures the liver; tinnitus is a salicylate sign. Antidote is acetylcysteine.Acetaminophen過量傷肝；耳鳴為水楊酸徵象。解毒劑為acetylcysteine。
