This broad chapter covers high-acuity nursing skills and emergencies: IV therapy and central lines, parenteral nutrition, blood transfusion, GI/airway/chest tubes, burns, oncologic and endocrine emergencies, respiratory and cardiovascular crises, hemodynamic monitoring, neurological injury, sedation, anaphylaxis, shock and sepsis. NCLEX prioritizes airway, perfusion, early recognition of complications, and knowing when to stop an infusion or call the provider.本章涵蓋高急性度照護技能與急症：靜脈治療與中心靜脈導管、全靜脈營養、輸血、胃腸／氣道／胸管、燒傷、腫瘤與內分泌急症、呼吸與心血管危象、血行動力學監測、神經損傷、鎮靜、過敏性休克、休克與敗血症。NCLEX 重視呼吸道、灌流、及早辨識併發症，以及何時停止輸注並通知醫師。

1IV Therapy, Devices and Central Lines靜脈輸液、裝置與中心靜脈導管

  • Isotonic fluids (NS, LR) expand extracellular volume; hypotonic fluids move water into cells (give slowly); hypertonic fluids pull water out of cells; colloids pull fluid into the vascular space (second line). LR is avoided in kidney injury (contains potassium) and caution in severe liver disease.等張液（NS、LR）擴增細胞外液；低張液使水進入細胞（需緩慢）；高張液將水拉出細胞；膠體液將水拉入血管內（第二線）。LR 含鉀，腎損傷者避免，嚴重肝病慎用。
  • Gauge: smaller number = larger lumen. 16-18 for rapid fluids, blood, surgery; 20-22 for lipids; 22-24 standard; 24-26 fragile veins. Macrodrip 10-20 gtt/mL; microdrip 60 gtt/mL for slow rates (<50 mL/hr) or potent drugs.管徑：號數越小管腔越大。16–18 G 用於快速輸液、輸血、手術；20–22 G 用於脂肪乳劑；22–24 G 為一般；24–26 G 用於脆弱血管。大滴管 10–20 gtt/mL；微滴管 60 gtt/mL 用於慢速（<50 mL/hr）或強效藥。
  • Site and care: use forearm veins, start distally, avoid legs in adults, edematous, paralyzed, mastectomy-side or dialysis-fistula arms. Change site every 72-96 hours, tubing every 96 hours, hang no solution longer than 24 hours; label with date and time, not marker on plastic bags.部位與照護：優先前臂靜脈、由遠端開始；成人避免下肢、水腫、癱瘓、乳房切除側、透析瘻管手臂。每 72–96 小時更換部位，管路每 96 小時更換，溶液掛置不超過 24 小時；貼標籤註明日期時間，勿在塑膠袋上直接用麥克筆書寫。
  • Complications: air embolism (clamp, left side-lying Trendelenburg, notify); circulatory overload (slow infusion, raise head, oxygen, notify); infiltration (stop, remove, elevate, compress; do not rub); phlebitis (remove, warm compress, restart in other arm); hematoma; infection; catheter embolism (tourniquet proximal).併發症：空氣栓塞（夾管、左側臥頭低、通報）；循環超載（減慢輸注、抬高床頭、給氧、通報）；滲漏（停止、拔除、抬高、冷／熱敷，勿搓揉）；靜脈炎（拔除、溫濕敷、改對側手臂）；血腫；感染；導管斷裂栓塞（近端綁止血帶）。
  • Central lines (PICC, tunneled Hickman/Broviac/Groshong, implanted port with Huber needle) allow hyperosmolar solutions and long-term therapy; confirm position by x-ray. For tubing changes use Trendelenburg and Valsalva to prevent air embolism.中心靜脈導管（PICC、隧道式 Hickman／Broviac／Groshong、植入式port須用 Huber 針）可輸注高滲液與長期治療；以 X 光確認位置。換管時採頭低位並做 Valsalva 動作以防空氣栓塞。
  • Epidural catheter: assess VS, LOC, lower-extremity motor/sensory function; if aspiration returns more than 1 mL clear fluid or blood do not inject and notify the anesthesia provider. PCA is locked with a lockout interval and verified by two RNs.硬膜外導管：評估生命徵象、意識及下肢運動感覺；若回抽超過 1 mL 澄清液或血液，勿注藥並通知麻醉人員。PCA 上鎖、設鎖定間隔並由兩位 RN 核對。

2Parenteral Nutrition (PN)靜脈營養（PN）

  • PN supplies dextrose, amino acids, lipids, vitamins, minerals, electrolytes; hypertonic. PPN goes through peripheral line or midline; TPN needs a central vein. Use only when enteral feeding is not possible.PN 提供葡萄糖、胺基酸、脂肪、維生素、礦物質、電解質，屬高張液。PPN 經周邊或中線導管；TPN 須經中心靜脈。僅在無法腸道營養時使用。
  • Lipids are isotonic, contain egg yolk (check egg allergy), need a 1.2-micron filter, start at 1 mL/min, check VS every 10 minutes for 30 minutes; stop for chest/back pain, dyspnea, fever, flushing. Do not use if emulsion has separated.脂肪乳劑為等張、含蛋黃（確認蛋過敏）、需 1.2 微米濾器，起始 1 mL/min，前 30 分鐘每 10 分鐘測生命徵象；若胸背痛、呼吸困難、發燒、潮紅則停止。乳劑分層則不可使用。
  • If the PN bag runs out, infuse D10W at the same rate to prevent hypoglycemia. Taper gradually over 1-2 hours when stopping; never stop abruptly. Do not give blood or IV drugs through the PN line.PN 袋用完時，以相同速率輸注 10% 葡萄糖預防低血糖。停止時 1–2 小時逐步減量，不可驟停。勿由 PN 管路輸血或給藥。
  • Monitor glucose about every 4 hours (hyperglycemia > 200 mg/dL), daily weight, I&O, electrolytes, liver and renal function. Refeeding syndrome in malnourished clients: low K, Mg, phosphate. Solutions refrigerated, used within 24 hours; discard if cloudy.約每 4 小時監測血糖（高血糖 > 200 mg/dL）、每日體重、出入量、電解質、肝腎功能。營養不良者留意再餵食症候群：低鉀、低鎂、低磷。溶液冷藏、24 小時內使用，混濁則丟棄。
  • Complications: infection (sterile technique, culture catheter tip), air embolism, pneumothorax (confirm placement by x-ray before starting), hypervolemia, hyper/hypoglycemia. Home teaching: report weight gain > 3 lb/week, arm swelling, catheter leaking.併發症：感染（無菌技術、導管尖端培養）、空氣栓塞、氣胸（開始前以 X 光確認位置）、容積過多、高／低血糖。居家衛教：每週體重增加 > 3 磅、手臂腫脹、導管滲漏須通報。

3Blood Products and Transfusion Reactions血液製品與輸血反應

  • PRBCs (1 unit raises Hgb about 1 g/dL, Hct 3%, infuse 2-4 hours); platelets (give rapidly 15-30 min, +5000-10,000/mm3 per unit); FFP (clotting factors, within 2 hours of thaw); cryoprecipitate (factor VIII, fibrinogen); granulocytes for refractory infection.濃縮紅血球（1 單位約升 Hgb 1 g/dL、Hct 3%，輸注 2–4 小時）；血小板（15–30 分鐘快速輸注，每單位升 5000–10,000/mm3）；新鮮冷凍血漿（凝血因子，解凍後 2 小時內）；冷凍沉澱品（第 VIII 因子、纖維蛋白原）；顆粒球用於難治感染。
  • Compatibility: O negative = universal RBC donor, AB positive = universal recipient. Two licensed nurses verify the client with two identifiers, order, blood tag and ABO/Rh at the bedside. Use 18-19 G, normal saline only, blood tubing with filter, no meds added.相容性：O 型陰性為萬能供血者，AB 型陽性為萬能受血者。兩位合格護理師於床邊以兩種辨識資料核對個案、醫囑、血袋標籤及 ABO／Rh。使用 18–19 G、僅用生理食鹽水、含濾器血液管路，不加藥。
  • Start slowly and stay with the client for the first 15 minutes (most critical); check VS before, at 15 minutes, then every 30-60 minutes. Start within 20-30 minutes of blood bank release; each unit within 4 hours. Fever before transfusion: hold and notify provider.緩慢開始並於前 15 分鐘陪伴個案（最關鍵）；輸血前、15 分鐘、之後每 30–60 分鐘測生命徵象。血庫領出後 20–30 分鐘內開始；每單位 4 小時內輸完。輸血前發燒：暫緩並通知醫師。
  • Suspected reaction (chills, back pain, hives, dyspnea, hypotension): STOP the transfusion, change tubing down to IV site, keep line open with saline, check VS, notify provider and blood bank, stay with client, collect urine, return bag and tubing to blood bank.疑似反應（寒顫、背痛、蕁麻疹、呼吸困難、低血壓）：立即停止輸血，更換自針頭以下管路並以生理食鹽水維持通路，測生命徵象，通知醫師與血庫，陪伴個案，留尿檢體，血袋與管路送回血庫。
  • Other complications: circulatory overload (slow, upright, O2, diuretic); septicemia (cultures, antibiotics); hypocalcemia/citrate toxicity with massive transfusion; hyperkalemia with old blood; iron overload (deferoxamine); hepatitis C most common transmitted disease.其他併發症：循環超載（減速、坐起、給氧、利尿劑）；敗血症（培養、抗生素）；大量輸血之低血鈣／檸檬酸中毒；舊血之高血鉀；鐵過載（deferoxamine）；最常傳播之疾病為 C 型肝炎。

4Gastrointestinal Tubes and Tube Feeding胃腸管路與管灌飲食

  • NG tubes decompress, feed, medicate, or lavage. Levin is single lumen; Salem sump is double lumen with an air vent that must not be clamped and stays above stomach level. Measure NEX (nose-earlobe-xiphoid); confirm by x-ray before first use.鼻胃管用於減壓、灌食、給藥或灌洗。Levin 為單腔；Salem sump 為雙腔，空氣通氣孔不可夾閉並維持高於胃的位置。以 NEX（鼻–耳垂–劍突）測量長度；首次使用前以 X 光確認。
  • Before every feeding or medication check placement (aspirate, pH of 3.5 or lower) and residual; hold feeding if residual > 100 mL (agency policy) or no bowel sounds. High-Fowler's for feeding; semi-Fowler's at all times for continuous feeds; flush 30-50 mL.每次灌食或給藥前確認位置（抽吸、pH 3.5 以下）與殘留量；殘留 > 100 mL（依院規）或無腸蠕動音則暫停。灌食採高坐臥位；持續灌食全程至少半坐臥；沖洗 30–50 mL。
  • Complications: aspiration (verify placement, HOB up), diarrhea (room-temperature, slow, fiber formula), clogged tube (liquid meds, flush), vomiting (stop feeding, side-lying, suction).併發症：吸入（確認位置、抬高床頭）、腹瀉（室溫、慢速、含纖維配方）、管路阻塞（液態藥物、沖洗）、嘔吐（停灌、側臥、抽吸）。
  • Intestinal tubes (Cantor single lumen, Miller-Abbott double lumen) have a tungsten bag; position on the right side, do not tape until final position. Esophageal balloon tubes (Sengstaken-Blakemore, Minnesota) tamponade varices: keep scissors at bedside and cut tubes if respiratory distress, watch for esophageal rupture.腸管（單腔 Cantor、雙腔 Miller-Abbott）末端含鎢袋；右側臥以利通過幽門，到達最終位置前不固定膠帶。食道氣囊管（Sengstaken-Blakemore、Minnesota）壓迫靜脈曲張：床邊備剪刀，呼吸窘迫時剪斷管路，留意食道破裂。
  • Lavage tubes (Lavacuator, Ewald) remove toxic substances from the stomach. Gastrostomy/jejunostomy sites: clean skin, dressing changes, check for leakage.灌洗管（Lavacuator、Ewald）用於移除胃內毒物。胃造口／空腸造口：清潔皮膚、更換敷料、檢查滲漏。

5Endotracheal and Tracheostomy Tubes氣管內管與氣切管

  • Confirm ET placement with end-tidal CO2 detector, bilateral breath sounds and gastric auscultation, then chest x-ray (tip 1-2 cm above carina). Absent left breath sounds suggest right main-stem intubation. Cuff pressure should not exceed 20 mm Hg per text (minimal leak or occlusive technique).以呼氣末 CO2 偵測、雙側呼吸音與胃部聽診確認氣管內管位置，再以 X 光確認（尖端距隆突 1–2 公分）。左側呼吸音消失提示管入右主支氣管。依本書氣囊壓不超過 20 mm Hg（最小漏氣或封閉技術）。
  • Oral tube: move to opposite side of mouth daily with two providers; secure, suction only when needed; keep Ambu bag at bedside. Extubation: hyperoxygenate and suction, deflate cuff, remove at peak inspiration, assess voice and stridor (laryngeal edema).經口管：每日由兩人換至口腔另一側；固定、必要時才抽吸；床邊備 Ambu 袋。拔管：給高濃度氧並抽吸，放氣囊、於吸氣高峰拔除，評估發聲與喘鳴（喉頭水腫）。
  • Tracheostomy: keep obturator, spare same-size tube and Ambu bag at bedside; humidified oxygen; cuff inflated for meals and 1 hour after if eating; never cap a tube until cuff deflated and inner cannula removed; cuffed fenestrated tube is not used for weaning.氣切：床邊備閉塞器、同尺寸備用管及 Ambu 袋；給予加濕氧；進食時及餐後 1 小時氣囊充氣；氣囊未放氣且內管未移除前不可蓋上塞子；有氣囊開窗管不用於脫離。
  • Complications: tracheomalacia, tracheal stenosis, tracheoesophageal fistula, tracheoinnominate artery fistula (pulsating tube/bleeding: direct pressure, emergency), obstruction, dislodgement within 72 hours of surgery (ventilate with Ambu and call rapid response).併發症：氣管軟化、氣管狹窄、氣管食道瘻管、氣管無名動脈瘻管（管子搏動／出血：直接加壓、緊急處理）、阻塞、術後 72 小時內脫出（以 Ambu 通氣並呼叫快速反應團隊）。

6Chest Tube Drainage Systems胸管引流系統

  • Three chambers: collection (notify if > 70-100 mL/hr or bright red/sudden increase), water seal (fluctuates with breathing; continuous bubbling = air leak), suction control (gentle bubbling expected in wet system; dry system uses orange float).三個腔室：收集腔（> 70–100 mL/hr 或鮮紅色／驟增須通報）、水封腔（隨呼吸擺動；持續冒泡＝漏氣）、抽吸控制腔（濕式系統應有輕柔氣泡；乾式用橘色浮標）。
  • Keep system below chest level, tubing free of kinks and dependent loops, occlusive dressing, all connections secure. Do not strip or milk tubes without order; never clamp without a prescription.系統保持低於胸部、管路無扭曲與下垂圈、密閉敷料、接頭牢固。無醫囑不可擠壓管路；無處方不可夾管。
  • Absent fluctuation means kinked/obstructed tube or lung re-expansion. If system cracks, put tube end in sterile water and replace. If tube pulled out: pinch skin opening, apply an occlusive sterile dressing secured with overlapping tape, and call the provider immediately. Keep clamp and sterile occlusive dressing at bedside.水封腔不再擺動表示管路扭曲／阻塞或肺已復張。系統破裂時將管端置入無菌水再更換系統。若管路被拉出：捏緊皮膚開口、貼密閉無菌敷料並以重疊膠帶固定，立即通知醫師。床邊備夾子與無菌密閉敷料。

7Burn Injuries燒傷

  • Major burn: 25% or more TBSA in adults (10% in children). Rule of nines estimates extent. Depth: superficial (red, painful, no blisters), superficial partial (blisters, wet, painful), deep partial (red/dry/white areas), full thickness (leathery, painless, eschar).重度燒傷：成人 TBSA 25% 以上（兒童 10%）。以九分法估計範圍。深度：淺層（紅、痛、無水泡）、淺部分層（水泡、濕、痛）、深部分層（紅／乾／白色區）、全層（皮革樣、無痛、焦痂）。
  • Airway first: inhalation injury signs include facial burns, singed nasal hair, hoarseness, sooty sputum, stridor. Give 100% oxygen by nonrebreather; carbon monoxide has 200 times the affinity for hemoglobin; continue until carboxyhemoglobin < 15%.先顧呼吸道：吸入性損傷徵象包括臉部燒傷、鼻毛燒焦、聲音嘶啞、含煙灰痰、喘鳴。以非再吸入面罩給 100% 氧；一氧化碳與血紅素親和力為氧的 200 倍；持續至碳氧血紅素 < 15%。
  • Emergent phase: fluid shift with hypovolemia, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, hemoconcentration. Parkland formula 4 mL x kg x %TBSA (half in first 8 hours, from time of injury). Titrate to urine output 30-50 mL/hr, the best indicator of perfusion. IV opioids, not IM/oral; NPO, NG tube, tetanus prophylaxis.急性期：液體移位造成低血容、低鈉、高鉀、血液濃縮。Parkland 公式 4 mL x 公斤 x %TBSA（前 8 小時給一半，自受傷時間起算）。依尿量 30–50 mL/hr 調整，為最佳灌流指標。使用靜脈鴉片類止痛，非肌肉／口服；禁食、置鼻胃管、破傷風預防。
  • Circumferential burns: elevate extremities, check pulses and capillary refill; escharotomy (bedside) or fasciotomy (OR) for compartment syndrome. Diuretics generally avoided. Protective isolation, stress ulcer prophylaxis, expect 15-20 lb weight gain in 72 hours.環狀燒傷：抬高肢體、檢查脈搏與微血管充填；因腔室症候群行焦痂切開術（床邊）或筋膜切開術（開刀房）。一般避免利尿劑。保護性隔離、預防壓力性潰瘍；72 小時內預期增重 15–20 磅。
  • Acute phase: wound care, debridement, hydrotherapy under 30 minutes, high protein/calorie diet (> 5000 calories for major burns), grafts. Autograft immobilized 3-7 days, watch for bleeding beneath graft; donor site heals in 7-14 days. Splinting and pressure garments 23 hours/day for 18-24 months in rehabilitation.急性後期：傷口護理、清創、水療 30 分鐘內、高蛋白高熱量飲食（重度燒傷 > 5000 大卡）、植皮。自體植皮固定 3–7 天，注意皮下出血；供皮區 7–14 天癒合。復健期夾板與壓力衣每日穿 23 小時，持續 18–24 個月。

8Oncologic Emergencies and DKA/HHS腫瘤急症與 DKA／HHS

  • Sepsis/DIC in cancer: strict asepsis, IV antibiotics, anticoagulants early, cryoprecipitate when bleeding dominates. SIADH: water retention, Na 115-120 mild, near 110 seizures; fluid restriction and ADH antagonist.癌症敗血症／DIC：嚴格無菌、靜脈抗生素、早期抗凝劑、出血為主時給冷凍沉澱品。SIADH：水滯留，鈉 115–120 為輕度，近 110 會抽搐；限水並給 ADH 拮抗劑。
  • Spinal cord compression: back pain precedes deficits; high-dose corticosteroids, radiation. Hypercalcemia: fatigue, constipation, polyuria, ECG change; fluids, calcium-lowering drugs, mobility.   • PRBCs (1 unit raises Hgb about 1 g/dL, Hct 3%, infuse 2-4 hours); platelets (give rapidly 15-30 min, +5000-10,000/mm3 per unit); FFP (clotting factors, within 2 hours of thaw); cryoprecipitate (factor VIII, fibrinogen); granulocytes for refractory infection.濃縮紅血球（1 單位約升 Hgb 1 g/dL、Hct 3%，輸注 2–4 小時）；血小板（15–30 分鐘快速輸注，每單位升 5000–10,000/mm3）；新鮮冷凍血漿（凝血因子，解凍後 2 小時內）；冷凍沉澱品（第 VIII 因子、纖維蛋白原）；顆粒球用於難治感染。
  • Compatibility: O negative = universal RBC donor, AB positive = universal recipient. Two licensed nurses verify the client with two identifiers, order, blood tag and ABO/Rh at the bedside. Use 18-19 G, normal saline only, blood tubing with filter, no meds added.相容性：O 型陰性為萬能供血者，AB 型陽性為萬能受血者。兩位合格護理師於床邊以兩種辨識資料核對個案、醫囑、血袋標籤及 ABO／Rh。使用 18–19 G、僅用生理食鹽水、含濾器血液管路，不加藥。
  • Start slowly and stay with the client for the first 15 minutes (most critical); check VS before, at 15 minutes, then every 30-60 minutes. Start within 20-30 minutes of blood bank release; each unit within 4 hours. Fever before transfusion: hold and notify provider.緩慢開始並於前 15 分鐘陪伴個案（最關鍵）；輸血前、15 分鐘、之後每 30–60 分鐘測生命徵象。血庫領出後 20–30 分鐘內開始；每單位 4 小時內輸完。輸血前發燒：暫緩並通知醫師。
  • Suspected reaction (chills, back pain, hives, dyspnea, hypotension): STOP the transfusion, change tubing down to IV site, keep line open with saline, check VS, notify provider and blood bank, stay with client, collect urine, return bag and tubing to blood bank.疑似反應（寒顫、背痛、蕁麻疹、呼吸困難、低血壓）：立即停止輸血，更換自針頭以下管路並以生理食鹽水維持通路，測生命徵象，通知醫師與血庫，陪伴個案，留尿檢體，血袋與管路送回血庫。
  • Other complications: circulatory overload (slow, upright, O2, diuretic); septicemia (cultures, antibiotics); hypocalcemia/citrate toxicity with massive transfusion; hyperkalemia with old blood; iron overload (deferoxamine); hepatitis C most common transmitted disease.其他併發症：循環超載（減速、坐起、給氧、利尿劑）；敗血症（培養、抗生素）；大量輸血之低血鈣／檸檬酸中毒；舊血之高血鉀；鐵過載（deferoxamine）；最常傳播之疾病為 C 型肝炎。

Gastrointestinal Tubes and Tube Feeding胃腸管路與管灌飲食
  • NG tubes decompress, feed, medicate, or lavage. Levin is single lumen; Salem sump is double lumen with an air vent that must not be clamped and stays above stomach level. Measure NEX (nose-earlobe-xiphoid); confirm by x-ray before first use.鼻胃管用於減壓、灌食、給藥或灌洗。Levin 為單腔；Salem sump 為雙腔，空氣通氣孔不可夾閉並維持高於胃的位置。以 NEX（鼻–耳垂–劍突）測量長度；首次使用前以 X 光確認。
  • Before every feeding or medication check placement (aspirate, pH of 3.5 or lower) and residual; hold feeding if residual > 100 mL (agency policy) or no bowel sounds. High-Fowler's for feeding; semi-Fowler's at all times for continuous feeds; flush 30-50 mL.每次灌食或給藥前確認位置（抽吸、pH 3.5 以下）與殘留量；殘留 > 100 mL（依院規）或無腸蠕動音則暫停。灌食採高坐臥位；持續灌食全程至少半坐臥；沖洗 30–50 mL。
  • Complications: aspiration (verify placement, HOB up), diarrhea (room-temperature, slow, fiber formula), clogged tube (liquid meds, flush), vomiting (stop feeding, side-lying, suction).併發症：吸入（確認位置、抬高床頭）、腹瀉（室溫、慢速、含纖維配方）、管路阻塞（液態藥物、沖洗）、嘔吐（停灌、側臥、抽吸）。
  • Intestinal tubes (Cantor single lumen, Miller-Abbott double lumen) have a tungsten bag; position on the right side, do not tape until final position.   • Lavage tubes (Lavacuator, Ewald) remove toxic substances from the stomach. Gastrostomy/jejunostomy sites: clean skin, dressing changes, check for leakage.灌洗管（Lavacuator、Ewald）用於移除胃內毒物。胃造口／空腸造口：清潔皮膚、更換敷料、檢查滲漏。

Endotracheal and Tracheostomy Tubes氣管內管與氣切管
  • Confirm ET placement with end-tidal CO2 detector, bilateral breath sounds and gastric auscultation, then chest x-ray (tip 1-2 cm above carina). Burn Injuries燒傷
  • Major burn: 25% or more TBSA in adults (10% in children). Rule of nines estimates extent. Depth: superficial (red, painful, no blisters), superficial partial (blisters, wet, painful), deep partial (red/dry/white areas), full thickness (leathery, painless, eschar).重度燒傷：成人 TBSA 25% 以上（兒童 10%）。以九分法估計範圍。深度：淺層（紅、痛、無水泡）、淺部分層（水泡、濕、痛）、深部分層（紅／乾／白色區）、全層（皮革樣、無痛、焦痂）。
  • Airway first: inhalation injury signs include facial burns, singed nasal hair, hoarseness, sooty sputum, stridor. Give 100% oxygen by nonrebreather; carbon monoxide has 200 times the affinity for hemoglobin; continue until carboxyhemoglobin < 15%.先顧呼吸道：吸入性損傷徵象包括臉部燒傷、鼻毛燒焦、聲音嘶啞、含煙灰痰、喘鳴。以非再吸入面罩給 100% 氧；一氧化碳與血紅素親和力為氧的 200 倍；持續至碳氧血紅素 < 15%。
  • Emergent phase: fluid shift with hypovolemia, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, hemoconcentration. Parkland formula 4 mL x kg x %TBSA (half in first 8 hours, from time of injury). Titrate to urine output 30-50 mL/hr, the best indicator of perfusion. IV opioids, not IM/oral; NPO, NG tube, tetanus prophylaxis.急性期：液體移位造成低血容、低鈉、高鉀、血液濃縮。Parkland 公式 4 mL x 公斤 x %TBSA（前 8 小時給一半，自受傷時間起算）。依尿量 30–50 mL/hr 調整，為最佳灌流指標。使用靜脈鴉片類止痛，非肌肉／口服；禁食、置鼻胃管、破傷風預防。
  • Circumferential burns: elevate extremities, check pulses and capillary refill; escharotomy (bedside) or fasciotomy (OR) for compartment syndrome. Diuretics generally avoided. Protective isolation, stress ulcer prophylaxis, expect 15-20 lb weight gain in 72 hours.環狀燒傷：抬高肢體、檢查脈搏與微血管充填；因腔室症候群行焦痂切開術（床邊）或筋膜切開術（開刀房）。一般避免利尿劑。保護性隔離、預防壓力性潰瘍；72 小時內預期增重 15–20 磅。
  • Acute phase: wound care, debridement, hydrotherapy under 30 minutes, high protein/calorie diet (> 5000 calories for major burns), grafts. Autograft immobilized 3-7 days, watch for bleeding beneath graft; donor site heals in 7-14 days. Splinting and pressure garments 23 hours/day for 18-24 months in rehabilitation.急性後期：傷口護理、清創、水療 30 分鐘內、高蛋白高熱量飲食（重度燒傷 > 5000 大卡）、植皮。自體植皮固定 3–7 天，注意皮下出血；供皮區 7–14 天癒合。復健期夾板與壓力衣每日穿 23 小時，持續 18–24 個月。

  • Anaphylaxis: assess airway and breathing, call for help but stay with the client, oxygen, IV line with normal saline, epinephrine, diphenhydramine, corticosteroids; document.過敏性休克：評估呼吸道與呼吸、求援但不離開個案、給氧、建立靜脈通路輸注生理食鹽水、epinephrine、diphenhydramine、皮質類固醇；記錄。
  • Shock types: hypovolemic (most common), cardiogenic, distributive/vasogenic (septic, anaphylactic, neurogenic), obstructive (tamponade, tension pneumothorax, PE). CPP = MAP - ICP, normal 70-100; MAP > 60 needed for organ perfusion.休克類型：低血容性（最常見）、心因性、分佈性／血管性（敗血性、過敏性、神經性）、阻塞性（填塞、張力性氣胸、肺栓塞）。CPP＝MAP－ICP，正常 70–100；器官灌流需 MAP > 60。
  • Stages: initial (restless, tachycardia, cool pale skin); compensatory (CO low, SBP < 100, oliguria, confusion); progressive (edema, dysrhythmias, thready pulse); refractory (unresponsive to pressors). Early intervention is the priority.
