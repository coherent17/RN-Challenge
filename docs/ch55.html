Part XIII · Renal and Urinary Problems of the Adult Client成人腎臟與泌尿問題

Chapter 55: Renal and Urinary Problems第 55 章：腎臟與泌尿問題

📖 Source: PDF p.862–895 · printed book p.779–810.e2來源：PDF 第 862–895 頁 · 書本印刷頁 779–810.e2

This chapter reviews kidney anatomy and physiology, renal diagnostic tests, acute kidney injury (AKI), chronic kidney disease (CKD), dialysis, kidney transplantation, and common urinary and genitourinary disorders such as UTI, calculi, BPH and trauma. Priority themes are fluid and electrolyte balance (especially potassium), protecting dialysis access, and recognizing complications early.本章涵蓋腎臟解剖生理、腎功能檢查、急性腎損傷(AKI)、慢性腎臟病(CKD)、透析、腎臟移植，以及泌尿道感染、結石、攝護腺肥大與外傷等常見泌尿生殖疾病。重點在於體液與電解質平衡(尤其是鉀)、保護透析通路，以及及早辨識併發症。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Kidney anatomy and physiology腎臟解剖與生理 📖 PDF p.862 (book p.779), PDF p.863 (book p.780)

  • Nephron (glomerulus + tubules) is the functional unit; the glomerulus filters, the PCT reabsorbs most water/electrolytes, the loop of Henle concentrates urine, the DCT fine-tunes sodium/water.腎元(腎絲球+腎小管)為功能單位；腎絲球過濾，近曲小管再吸收大部分水與電解質，亨利氏環濃縮尿液，遠曲小管調節鈉與水。
  • Kidney functions: acid-base balance, waste excretion, fluid/electrolyte control, drug excretion, renin (BP), erythropoietin (RBC), activation of vitamin D.腎臟功能：酸鹼平衡、排除廢物、體液電解質調控、藥物排泄、分泌腎素(血壓)與紅血球生成素(RBC)、活化維生素D。
  • ADH from the posterior pituitary makes the distal tubule/collecting duct permeable to water; lack of ADH causes diabetes insipidus.ADH由腦下垂體後葉釋放，使遠端小管與集尿管對水通透；缺乏ADH則造成尿崩症。
  • Renin-angiotensin-aldosterone: low BP/flow releases renin, then angiotensin II (vasoconstriction) and aldosterone (retain Na+/water, excrete K+).腎素-血管張力素-醛固酮系統：低血壓或低流量釋放腎素，生成血管張力素II(血管收縮)與醛固酮(保鈉保水、排鉀)。
  • Kidneys control bicarbonate and lungs control carbonic acid; normal pH 7.35-7.45 with a 20:1 bicarbonate:carbonic acid ratio.腎臟調控碳酸氫根，肺調控碳酸；正常pH 7.35-7.45，碳酸氫根與碳酸比為20:1。
  • Bladder capacity about 1 L; normal adult urine output about 1500 mL/day. The prostate surrounds the urethra.膀胱容量約1公升；成人正常尿量約1500 mL/天。攝護腺環繞尿道。

Diagnostic tests診斷檢查 📖 PDF p.863 (book p.780), PDF p.864 (book p.781), PDF p.865 (book p.782), PDF p.866 (book p.783), PDF p.867 (book p.784)

  • Serum creatinine 0.6-1.2 mg/dL (M), 0.5-1.1 (F) reflects GFR; it rises only after about 50% of function is lost. BUN 10-20 mg/dL can rise with dehydration, high protein, GI bleeding, steroids.血清肌酸酐0.6-1.2 mg/dL(男)、0.5-1.1(女)反映GFR，約喪失50%功能後才上升。BUN 10-20 mg/dL，脫水、高蛋白、消化道出血、類固醇亦可升高。
  • BUN/creatinine ratio (general clinical reference, not stated in this chapter): typically about 10-20:1; rises with dehydration/prerenal causes, and falls with fluid excess or intrinsic kidney damage.BUN/肌酸酐比值（一般臨床參考，本章未載明）：通常約10-20:1；脫水或腎前性原因時上升，體液過多或腎實質受損時下降。
  • Urinalysis: specific gravity 1.003-1.030, pH 4-8; glucose, ketones, protein, blood, casts normally negative. Use first morning void, 10-15 mL.尿液分析：比重1.003-1.030、pH 4-8；糖、酮體、蛋白、血、圓柱體正常為陰性。留取晨間第一次尿10-15 mL。
  • 24-hour urine: discard the first void at start, collect all urine for 24 h, include the final void, keep on ice/refrigerated.24小時尿液：起始時排尿並丟棄，之後收集全部尿液，結束時最後一次排尿也加入，檢體需冰存。
  • Creatinine clearance best estimates GFR (about 125 mL/min young adult; falls about 10% per decade). Urine culture: clean-catch midstream sample.肌酸酐清除率最能估算GFR(年輕成人約125 mL/分，每十年約降10%)。尿液培養：中段尿清潔收集。
  • IV urography/contrast: check iodine/shellfish allergy and pregnancy, encourage fluids, watch urine output because dye is nephrotoxic.靜脈腎盂攝影/顯影劑：確認碘/海鮮過敏與懷孕，鼓勵飲水並監測尿量，顯影劑具腎毒性。
  • Cystoscopy with biopsy: expect pink-tinged urine and burning; report bright red urine, clots, fever. Renal biopsy: prone positioning, check coagulation, bed rest supine 2-6 h, watch for hypotension/tachycardia, hematuria.膀胱鏡切片：術後可有粉紅尿與灼熱感；出現鮮紅尿、血塊、發燒需通報。腎臟切片：俯臥、檢查凝血、術後仰臥臥床2-6小時，監測低血壓/心搏過速與血尿。

Acute kidney injury (AKI)急性腎損傷 📖 PDF p.867 (book p.784), PDF p.868 (book p.785), PDF p.869 (book p.786)

  • AKI is abrupt, potentially reversible loss of kidney function. Causes: prerenal (hypoperfusion), intrarenal (ATN, nephrotoxins), postrenal (obstruction).AKI為突發且可能可逆的腎功能喪失。病因：腎前性(灌流不足)、腎內性(急性腎小管壞死、腎毒物)、腎後性(阻塞)。
  • Phases: onset, oliguric (<400 mL/day, 8-15 days), diuretic (4-5 L/day), recovery (may take 1-2 years).分期：起始期、少尿期(<400 mL/天，8-15天)、利尿期(4-5 L/天)、恢復期(可達1-2年)。
  • Oliguric phase: fluid overload, hyperkalemia, hyperphosphatemia, hypocalcemia, metabolic acidosis (Kussmaul), uremia, pericarditis; restrict fluids to measured output plus about 400-1000 mL.少尿期：體液過多、高血鉀、高血磷、低血鈣、代謝性酸中毒(庫斯毛氏呼吸)、尿毒症、心包炎；液體限制為前日尿量加約400-1000 mL。
  • Diuretic phase: risk of hypovolemia, hypotension, hypokalemia, hyponatremia; replace fluids/electrolytes.利尿期：易低血容量、低血壓、低血鉀、低血鈉；需補充液體與電解質。
  • Monitor hourly I&O, daily weight (gain 0.25-0.5 kg/day = retention), labs, lung sounds; low-moderate protein, high carbohydrate diet; avoid nephrotoxins (aminoglycosides, NSAIDs, contrast).監測每小時輸出入量、每日體重(增加0.25-0.5公斤/天代表水分滯留)、檢驗值、肺音；低至中蛋白高醣飲食；避免腎毒性藥物(胺基糖苷類、NSAIDs、顯影劑)。
  • Dialysis or CRRT may be needed for fluid overload, azotemia, or severe acidosis.體液過量、氮血症或嚴重酸中毒時可能需透析或連續性腎臟替代療法(CRRT)。

Chronic kidney disease and uremic syndrome慢性腎臟病與尿毒症候群 📖 PDF p.869 (book p.786), PDF p.870 (book p.787), PDF p.871 (book p.788), PDF p.872 (book p.789), PDF p.873 (book p.790)

  • CKD: progressive irreversible loss, GFR <=60 mL/min for 3 months or more. Stages: >90 at risk, 60-89 mild, 30-59 moderate, 15-29 severe, <15 ESRD.CKD：漸進不可逆，GFR≦60 mL/分且持續3個月以上。分期：>90高風險、60-89輕度、30-59中度、15-29重度、<15末期(ESRD)。
  • Main causes: diabetes, hypertension, chronic obstruction, recurrent infection, autoimmune disease, following AKI.主要原因：糖尿病、高血壓、慢性阻塞、反覆感染、自體免疫疾病、AKI後續。
  • Anemia (low erythropoietin): give epoetin/darbepoetin, folic acid, iron; transfuse only when necessary.貧血(紅血球生成素不足)：給予epoetin/darbepoetin、葉酸、鐵劑；僅在必要時輸血。
  • Hyperkalemia: telemetry; low-K diet; sodium polystyrene sulfonate; IV calcium gluconate (protect heart), regular insulin + 50% dextrose, sodium bicarbonate; avoid spironolactone/triamterene.高血鉀：連續心電圖監測；低鉀飲食；聚苯乙烯磺酸鈉；靜脈葡萄糖酸鈣(保護心肌)、常規胰島素+50%葡萄糖、碳酸氫鈉；避免spironolactone/triamterene。
  • Hyperphosphatemia/hypocalcemia: phosphate binders with meals, activated vitamin D, calcium; hypermagnesemia: avoid Mg antacids/laxatives.高血磷/低血鈣：隨餐服用磷結合劑、活化維生素D、鈣；高血鎂：避免含鎂制酸劑與瀉劑。
  • Other problems: hypertension, hypervolemia (restrict Na/fluid), GI bleeding, pruritus (uremic frost late), neuropathy, bone disease/fractures, infection risk, psychosocial stress.其他問題：高血壓、體液過多(限鈉限水)、消化道出血、搔癢(尿霜為晚期)、神經病變、骨病與骨折、感染風險、心理社會壓力。
  • Uremic syndrome: nitrogenous waste buildup; limit protein (high quality), sodium, potassium, phosphate; monitor for altered LOC, stomatitis, N/V.尿毒症候群：含氮廢物蓄積；限制蛋白質(高品質)、鈉、鉀、磷；監測意識改變、口腔炎、噁心嘔吐。

Hemodialysis and vascular access血液透析與血管通路 📖 PDF p.873 (book p.790), PDF p.874 (book p.791), PDF p.875 (book p.792), PDF p.876 (book p.793)

  • Hemodialysis uses diffusion, osmosis and ultrafiltration across a semipermeable membrane; removes urea, creatinine, excess fluid and corrects electrolytes.血液透析利用擴散、滲透與超過濾，透過半透膜清除尿素、肌酸酐、多餘水分並矯正電解質。
  • Before dialysis: weigh, check vitals/labs; hold antihypertensives and dialyzable drugs (water-soluble vitamins, some antibiotics, digoxin) until after treatment. After: watch for hypotension, bleeding (heparin), hypovolemia.透析前：量體重、生命徵象與檢驗值；降血壓藥及可被透析的藥物(水溶性維生素、部分抗生素、digoxin)延至透析後給予。透析後：留意低血壓、出血(肝素)與低血容量。
  • AV fistula is the access of choice (matures 4-6 weeks); AV graft usable after about 2 weeks; temporary subclavian/femoral catheters; external shunt usable immediately.動靜脈瘻管為首選通路(成熟需4-6週)；人工血管約2週後可用；暫時性鎖骨下/股靜脈導管；外部分流管可立即使用。
  • Access arm: no BP, venipuncture, IV or injections; check thrill and bruit; teach hand exercises (fistula).通路手臂：不量血壓、不抽血、不打點滴或注射；檢查震顫與血管雜音；教導手部運動(瘻管)。
  • Femoral catheter: client should not sit up more than 45 degrees. Arterial steal: ischemia of the hand (pain, coolness, pallor).股靜脈導管：病人坐起不超過45度。動脈竊血：手部缺血(疼痛、冰冷、蒼白)。
  • Complications: air embolus, disequilibrium syndrome, hypotension, hemorrhage, sepsis, hepatitis, electrolyte shifts.併發症：空氣栓塞、失衡症候群、低血壓、出血、敗血症、肝炎、電解質變化。

Peritoneal dialysis腹膜透析 📖 PDF p.876 (book p.793), PDF p.877 (book p.794), PDF p.878 (book p.795), PDF p.879 (book p.796)

  • The peritoneum is the membrane; Tenckhoff catheter placed 3-5 cm below the umbilicus; one exchange = fill (10-20 min, 1-2 L), dwell, drain.以腹膜為透析膜；Tenckhoff導管置於臍下3-5 cm；一次交換=灌入(10-20分鐘、1-2 L)、留置、引流。
  • Contraindications: peritonitis, recent abdominal surgery, adhesions, other GI problems such as diverticulosis.禁忌：腹膜炎、近期腹部手術、腹腔沾黏、憩室症等腸胃問題。
  • Higher dextrose concentration pulls more fluid; do not extend dwell beyond order (hyperglycemia). Warm the dialysate to reduce cramping.葡萄糖濃度越高脫水越多；留置時間不可超過醫囑(高血糖風險)。將透析液加溫以減少腹部絞痛。
  • Outflow less than inflow: retained fluid counts as intake. Cloudy outflow = peritonitis; bloody (after first exchanges) = vascular complication; brown = bowel perforation; urine-colored = bladder perforation.引流量少於灌入量：滯留量算為攝入。混濁=腹膜炎；(初期後)血性=血管併發症；棕色=腸穿孔；尿色=膀胱穿孔。
  • Poor outflow: usually constipation, then catheter migration, kinks, fibrin; reposition, check tubing, keep bag below abdomen.引流不良：最常因便秘，其次導管移位、管路扭折、纖維蛋白塊；調整姿勢、檢查管路、引流袋置於腹部下方。
  • Types: CAPD (manual, 4 exchanges/day), automated (CCPD, IPD, NPD) with a cycler. Peritonitis prevention: strict sterile technique, keep dressing dry.種類：連續性可攜式(CAPD，手動、每日4次)、自動化(CCPD、IPD、NPD)使用循環機。預防腹膜炎：嚴守無菌技術、敷料保持乾燥。

Kidney transplantation腎臟移植 📖 PDF p.879 (book p.796), PDF p.880 (book p.797), PDF p.881 (book p.798)

  • Living related donor is the most desirable; recipient takes immunosuppressants for life. Dialysis completed within 24 h before surgery; strict asepsis.活體親屬捐贈最理想；受贈者需終生服用免疫抑制劑。手術前24小時內完成透析；嚴格無菌。
  • Kidney placed in the iliac fossa; own kidneys usually left in place. Monitor hourly urine output; abrupt drop must be reported; IV replacement based on urine output for 12-24 h.新腎置於髂窩；原腎通常保留。每小時監測尿量，突然減少須通報；前12-24小時依尿量補充輸液。
  • Urinary catheter 3-5 days; initial urine may be pink/bloody; report gross hematuria with clots.導尿管留置3-5天；初期尿可呈粉紅或血尿；肉眼血尿合併血塊須通報。
  • Rejection signs: fever >100 F (37.8 C), pain/tenderness over graft, weight gain 2-3 lb (0.9-1.4 kg) in 24 h, edema, hypertension, rising BUN/creatinine.排斥徵象：體溫>100°F(37.8°C)、移植腎處疼痛壓痛、24小時增重2-3磅(0.9-1.4公斤)、水腫、高血壓、BUN/肌酸酐上升。
  • Hyperacute rejection within 48 h (remove kidney); acute within first week or later (increase immunosuppression); chronic over months-years.超急性排斥於48小時內(切除移植腎)；急性排斥多於一週內或其後(加強免疫抑制)；慢性排斥於數月至數年。
  • Teach: never miss immunosuppressants, avoid infection exposure and contact sports, avoid prolonged sitting.衛教：不可漏服免疫抑制劑、避免接觸感染源與接觸性運動、避免久坐。

Urinary tract infections and inflammation泌尿道感染與發炎 📖 PDF p.881 (book p.798), PDF p.882 (book p.799), PDF p.883 (book p.800)

  • Cystitis: E. coli most common; frequency, urgency, dysuria, cloudy foul urine, hematuria. In older adults altered mentation may be the only sign.膀胱炎：最常見為大腸桿菌；頻尿、急尿、解尿灼痛、混濁惡臭尿、血尿。老年人可能僅表現意識混亂。
  • Collect culture before antibiotics; fluids up to 3000 mL/day; complete full antibiotic course; keep urine acidic; avoid caffeine/alcohol.使用抗生素前先送尿培養；每日飲水可達3000 mL；完成整個療程；維持酸性尿；避免咖啡因與酒精。
  • Prevention: wipe front to back, void every 2-3 h, void after intercourse, avoid bubble baths, cotton underwear.預防：由前往後擦拭、每2-3小時排尿、性行為後排尿、避免泡泡浴、穿棉質內褲。
  • Urethritis: commonly STI (gonorrhea, chlamydia); treat partners, abstinence until treated; chlamydia can cause sterility.尿道炎：常與性傳染病(淋病、披衣菌)相關；伴侶需治療，治療完成前禁止性行為；披衣菌可致不孕。
  • Acute pyelonephritis: fever, chills, flank pain, CVA tenderness, pyuria; fluids to 3000 mL/day, antibiotics, warm compresses. Chronic: scarring, can lead to CKD.急性腎盂腎炎：發燒寒顫、腰痛、肋脊角叩痛、膿尿；每日飲水達3000 mL、抗生素、溫敷。慢性者造成腎臟疤痕，可進展為CKD。

Polycystic kidney, hydronephrosis, calculi, tumors多囊腎、水腎、結石與腫瘤 📖 PDF p.883 (book p.800), PDF p.884 (book p.801), PDF p.885 (book p.802), PDF p.886 (book p.803)

  • Adult polycystic kidney disease: autosomal dominant, symptoms at 30-40 y; flank pain, hematuria, hypertension, recurrent UTI; avoid NSAIDs/aspirin; genetic counseling.成人多囊腎：體染色體顯性，30-40歲發病；腰痛、血尿、高血壓、反覆泌尿道感染；避免NSAIDs/阿斯匹靈；遺傳諮詢。
  • Hydronephrosis: obstruction with distended renal pelvis; after relief watch for diuresis and dehydration.水腎：阻塞造成腎盂擴張；阻塞解除後留意利尿與脫水。
  • Renal calculi: severe colicky flank pain radiating to groin, hematuria, N/V; strain all urine, send stones for analysis, fluids up to 3000 mL/day, scheduled analgesics, ambulation.腎結石：劇烈絞痛由腰部放射至鼠蹊、血尿、噁心嘔吐；過濾所有尿液並送結石分析、每日飲水至3000 mL、定時給予止痛、鼓勵走動。
  • Diet by stone type: limit purines, calcium or oxalate as indicated.依結石種類調整飲食：限制普林、鈣或草酸。
  • Treatment: ESWL (watch for obstruction, bleeding, hematoma), percutaneous lithotripsy, ureterolithotomy, nephrectomy. Do not irrigate nephrostomy tube unless prescribed.治療：體外震波碎石(留意阻塞、出血、血腫)、經皮碎石、輸尿管切開取石、腎切除。除非醫囑否則不沖洗腎造口管。
  • Renal tumor: painless gross hematuria, flank pain, mass; radical nephrectomy care: monitor bleeding, adrenal insufficiency, urine 30-50 mL/h.腎腫瘤：無痛性肉眼血尿、腰痛、腫塊；根治性腎切除術後：監測出血、腎上腺功能不足、尿量30-50 mL/小時。

Epididymitis, prostatitis, BPH and bladder trauma副睪炎、攝護腺炎、BPH與膀胱外傷 📖 PDF p.886 (book p.803), PDF p.887 (book p.804), PDF p.888 (book p.805)

  • Epididymitis: scrotal pain/swelling, fever; bed rest with scrotal elevation, cold compresses, antibiotics (partner if STI).副睪炎：陰囊疼痛腫脹、發燒；臥床並抬高陰囊、冷敷、抗生素(性傳染病時伴侶亦需治療)。
  • Bacterial prostatitis: acutely ill, fever, perineal/low back pain, tender warm prostate; antibiotics, sitz baths, stool softeners; avoid spicy food, caffeine, alcohol.細菌性攝護腺炎：急性病容、發燒、會陰與下背痛、攝護腺壓痛發熱；抗生素、坐浴、軟便劑；避免辛辣、咖啡因、酒精。
  • BPH: weak stream (early), hesitancy, nocturia, retention, overflow dribbling; avoid anticholinergics, antihistamines, decongestants; fluids 2000-3000 mL/day.BPH：尿流細弱(早期)、遲疑、夜尿、尿滯留、溢流性滴尿；避免抗膽鹼藥、抗組織胺、解充血劑；每日飲水2000-3000 mL。
  • Bladder trauma (often pelvic fracture): hematuria, suprapubic pain, anuria; if blood at meatus, do NOT catheterize until retrograde urethrogram/ureterogram.膀胱外傷(常因骨盆骨折)：血尿、恥骨上疼痛、無尿；若尿道口有血，在逆行尿道/輸尿管攝影前勿導尿。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Serum creatinine rises only after about 50% of kidney function is lost; creatinine clearance is the best GFR estimate (normal ~125 mL/min, ~65 at age 65).血清肌酸酐需喪失約50%腎功能才上升；肌酸酐清除率最能估算GFR(正常約125 mL/分，65歲約65)。 📖 PDF p.864 (book p.781), PDF p.865 (book p.782)
  2. AKI oliguric phase: urine <400 mL/day, hyperkalemia, fluid overload, acidosis; diuretic phase: urine 4-5 L/day, risk of dehydration and hypokalemia.AKI少尿期：尿量<400 mL/天、高血鉀、體液過多、酸中毒；利尿期：尿量4-5 L/天，注意脫水與低血鉀。 📖 PDF p.867 (book p.784), PDF p.868 (book p.785)
  3. Hyperkalemia in kidney disease: place on telemetry; calcium gluconate protects the heart, insulin + dextrose shifts K+ into cells, sodium polystyrene sulfonate and dialysis remove it.腎病高血鉀：給予心電圖監測；葡萄糖酸鈣保護心臟、胰島素+葡萄糖使鉀進入細胞、聚苯乙烯磺酸鈉與透析排除鉀。 📖 PDF p.870 (book p.787), PDF p.871 (book p.788)
  4. Daily weight gain of 0.5-1 lb/day (0.25-0.5 kg) indicates fluid retention; weigh same scale, same clothes, same time.每日增重0.5-1磅(0.25-0.5公斤)代表水分滯留；固定體重計、衣著與時間。 📖 PDF p.868 (book p.785)
  5. Contrast dye and aminoglycosides/NSAIDs are nephrotoxic; hydrate and monitor urine output and creatinine.顯影劑、胺基糖苷類與NSAIDs具腎毒性；需補充水分並監測尿量與肌酸酐。 📖 PDF p.866 (book p.783), PDF p.867 (book p.784)
  6. Never take BP, draw blood, start IVs or give injections in the arm with a fistula/graft; check for thrill and bruit; absent thrill/bruit = notify PHCP immediately.有瘻管/人工血管的手臂不可量血壓、抽血、打點滴或注射；檢查震顫與雜音；無震顫/雜音需立即通報。 📖 PDF p.875 (book p.792), PDF p.876 (book p.793)
  7. Air embolism during hemodialysis: stop dialysis (do not reinfuse), turn client left side with head down, give oxygen, call PHCP/rapid response.血液透析中空氣栓塞：停止透析(不回輸血液)、左側臥頭低足高、給氧、通知醫師與快速反應小組。 📖 PDF p.876 (book p.793)
  8. Hold antihypertensives and dialyzable drugs (digoxin, water-soluble vitamins, some antibiotics) until after hemodialysis.降血壓藥與可被透析的藥物(digoxin、水溶性維生素、部分抗生素)延至血液透析後給予。 📖 PDF p.873 (book p.790)
  9. Peritoneal dialysis outflow: cloudy = peritonitis (early sign); brown = bowel perforation; urine-colored = bladder perforation; constipation is the main cause of poor outflow.腹膜透析引流液：混濁=腹膜炎(早期徵象)；棕色=腸穿孔；尿色=膀胱穿孔；便秘是引流不良主因。 📖 PDF p.878 (book p.795), PDF p.879 (book p.796)
  10. Kidney transplant: report abrupt fall in urine output, fever, graft tenderness, 2-3 lb gain in 24 h (rejection); immunosuppressants for life.腎移植：尿量突然減少、發燒、移植腎壓痛、24小時增重2-3磅(排斥)須通報；終生服用免疫抑制劑。 📖 PDF p.880 (book p.797), PDF p.881 (book p.798)
  11. Strain all urine in renal calculi; fluids up to 3000 mL/day; severe sudden flank pain radiating to groin = renal colic.腎結石須過濾所有尿液；每日飲水至3000 mL；突發劇烈腰痛放射至鼠蹊為腎絞痛。 📖 PDF p.884 (book p.801), PDF p.885 (book p.802)
  12. Altered mental status may be the only sign of UTI in older adults; obtain culture before antibiotics.老年人泌尿道感染可能僅表現意識改變；使用抗生素前先採尿培養。 📖 PDF p.881 (book p.798)
  13. Blood at the urethral meatus after trauma: do not insert a urinary catheter until urethral injury is ruled out.外傷後尿道口見血：排除尿道損傷前不可導尿。 📖 PDF p.888 (book p.805)
  14. After renal biopsy: supine bed rest 2-6 h, watch for hypotension, tachycardia, falling Hgb/Hct, hematuria; avoid heavy lifting 1-2 weeks.腎臟切片後：仰臥臥床2-6小時，監測低血壓、心搏過速、Hgb/Hct下降與血尿；1-2週避免提重物。 📖 PDF p.867 (book p.784)
  15. Avoid anticholinergics, antihistamines and decongestants in BPH because they cause urinary retention.BPH避免使用抗膽鹼藥、抗組織胺與解充血劑，因可造成尿滯留。 📖 PDF p.887 (book p.804)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Kidney stones: Strain urine, push Fluids, scheduled Analgesics, Ambulate.腎結石口訣：過濾尿液、補充水分、定時止痛、鼓勵走動。 📖 PDF p.884 (book p.801), PDF p.885 (book p.802)
  • AKI phases: O-O-D-R = Onset, Oliguric (K+ high), Diuretic (K+ low, dehydration), Recovery.AKI分期口訣O-O-D-R：起始、少尿(鉀高)、利尿(鉀低、脫水)、恢復。 📖 PDF p.867 (book p.784), PDF p.868 (book p.785)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. A client with acute kidney injury has a serum potassium of 7.0 mEq/L. Which actions are priorities? Select all that apply.急性腎損傷病人血鉀7.0 mEq/L，下列哪些為優先措施？(多選) (Select all that apply)（複選）

Severe hyperkalemia risks lethal dysrhythmias: monitor the heart, notify the provider, and remove sources of potassium. Extra fluid risks overload in oliguric AKI.嚴重高血鉀可能造成致命心律不整：需監測心臟、通知醫師並排除鉀來源。少尿型AKI額外輸液會造成液體過量。

2. During hemodialysis a client suddenly has chest pain, dyspnea, cyanosis and SpO2 88%. What is the nurse's first action?血液透析中病人突然胸痛、呼吸困難、發紺、SpO2 88%，護理師首要處置為何？

Suspect air embolism: stop dialysis without reinfusing, left side-lying Trendelenburg traps air in the right heart, then oxygen and call for help.懷疑空氣栓塞：停止透析且不回輸，左側臥頭低使空氣滯留右心，再給氧並求援。
