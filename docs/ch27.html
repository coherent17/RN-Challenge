Chapter 27: Postpartum Complications第 27 章：產後併發症

📖 Source: PDF p.393–401 · printed book p.350–357.e1來源：PDF 第 393–401 頁 · 書本印刷頁 350–357.e1

This chapter covers postpartum complications: cystitis, hematoma, uterine atony, hemorrhage and shock, infection, mastitis, endometritis, pulmonary embolism, subinvolution, thrombophlebitis, and perinatal loss. The key skill is recognizing early hemorrhage and shock and acting in the right order.本章涵蓋產後併發症：膀胱炎、血腫、子宮收縮不良、出血與休克、感染、乳腺炎、子宮內膜炎、肺栓塞、子宮復舊不全、血栓靜脈炎與周產期喪失。重點是及早辨識出血與休克，並依正確順序處置。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Cystitis and hematoma膀胱炎與血腫 📖 PDF p.393 (book p.350)

  • Cystitis: encourage fluids and frequent voiding; get urine culture (voided or straight cath) before antibiotics.膀胱炎：鼓勵多喝水與頻繁排尿；使用抗生素前先留尿液培養（自解或單次導尿）。
  • Hematoma (vulvar most common): linked to forceps and vessel injury. Signs: severe unrelieved pain, perineal pressure, bulging discolored mass, inability to void, falling Hgb/Hct, tachycardia/hypotension.血腫（以外陰最常見）：與產鉗及血管損傷有關。徵象：無法緩解的劇痛、會陰壓迫感、隆起變色腫塊、無法排尿、血紅素與血容比下降、心跳快、低血壓。
  • Care: ice, analgesics, catheter if cannot void, IV fluids, blood products, antibiotics, possible incision and evacuation; small ones reabsorb.處置：冰敷、止痛、無法排尿則導尿、靜脈輸液、輸血、抗生素，必要時切開引流；小血腫可自行吸收。
  • With epidural anesthesia the client may not feel pain, so changes in vital signs are the best clue.接受硬膜外麻醉者可能無痛感，因此生命徵象改變是最佳線索。

Uterine atony and postpartum hemorrhage子宮收縮不良與產後出血 📖 PDF p.393 (book p.350), PDF p.394 (book p.351), PDF p.395 (book p.352)

  • Atony: boggy uterus; massage until firm, empty the bladder, notify provider if not resolved.收縮不良：子宮鬆軟；按摩至變硬、排空膀胱，無改善則通知醫師。
  • Hemorrhage: >500 mL vaginal birth, >1000 mL cesarean, or 10% Hgb/Hct drop with symptoms. Highest risk in first 4 hours, then first 24 hours; late is beyond 24 hours.出血定義：陰道生產大於500 mL、剖腹產大於1000 mL，或血紅素／血容比下降10%並有症狀。風險最高為產後4小時內，其次24小時內；24小時後為晚期出血。
  • Causes: atony, lacerations, hematoma, retained placenta fragments. Risk factors: prior PPH, coagulopathy, chorioamnionitis, previa/abruption, overdistention, multiparity, prolonged labor, operative delivery.原因：收縮不良、裂傷、血腫、胎盤殘留。危險因子：曾有產後出血、凝血異常、絨毛膜羊膜炎、前置胎盤／胎盤早剝、子宮過度膨脹、多產、產程延長、手術生產。
  • Assess: pad soaked in 15 min; weigh pads (1 g = 1 mL). Early signs restlessness and rising pulse; later low BP, cool clammy ashen skin, low urine output, altered consciousness.評估：15分鐘浸濕護墊；秤護墊重量（1 g = 1 mL）。早期徵象為躁動與脈搏上升；晚期為低血壓、皮膚冰冷濕黏灰白、尿量減少、意識改變。
  • Take-action sequence: airway, oxygen 8-10 L/min nonrebreather, call OB, legs up about 30 degrees, massage if atonic, IV (two lines, 16-18 gauge), uterotonics (oxytocin, prostaglandins), fluids (LR/NS), Foley, blood products, prepare for surgery.處置順序：確保呼吸道、非再吸入面罩給氧8至10 L/分、呼叫產科醫師、抬腿約30度、鬆軟則按摩、建立兩條16至18號靜脈、子宮收縮劑（催產素、前列腺素）、輸液（LR／NS）、導尿、輸血、準備手術。

Infection, mastitis and endometritis感染、乳腺炎與子宮內膜炎 📖 PDF p.394 (book p.351), PDF p.395 (book p.352), PDF p.396 (book p.353)

  • Postpartum infection occurs within 28 days; fever at or above 100.4 F after the first 24 hours is infection until proven otherwise. Malodorous discharge, chills, malaise, pelvic pain, high WBC.產後感染發生於28天內；24小時後體溫達100.4°F以上視為感染。惡臭分泌物、畏寒、倦怠、骨盆痛、白血球升高。
  • Care: VS q2-4h, position for drainage, fluids 3000-4000 mL/day, high-calorie high-protein diet, hand hygiene, antibiotics per culture, antipyretics.處置：每2至4小時測生命徵象、引流姿勢、每日水分3000至4000 mL、高熱量高蛋白飲食、洗手、依培養結果用抗生素、退燒藥。
  • Mastitis: usually 2-3 weeks postpartum in nursing parents; localized heat, swelling, pain, axillary nodes, fever, flu-like symptoms. Continue emptying the breast (nurse or pump every 3-4 h), fluids 2500-3000 mL, supportive non-underwire bra, antibiotics.乳腺炎：多見於產後2至3週的哺乳者；局部紅熱腫痛、腋下淋巴結痛、發燒、類流感症狀。持續排空乳房（哺乳或每3至4小時擠奶）、水分2500至3000 mL、穿無鋼圈支撐胸罩、抗生素。
  • Endometritis: fever, chills, tachycardia, headache, backache, severe prolonged afterpains, large tender uterus, foul or reddish-brown lochia, ileus. Fowler's position, private room, IV antibiotics, no need to isolate newborn.子宮內膜炎：發燒、畏寒、心跳快、頭痛、背痛、嚴重持續後陣痛、子宮大而壓痛、惡露惡臭或紅褐色、腸阻塞。採半坐臥、單人房、靜脈抗生素，不需隔離新生兒。

Pulmonary embolism, subinvolution and thrombophlebitis肺栓塞、子宮復舊不全與血栓靜脈炎 📖 PDF p.396 (book p.353), PDF p.397 (book p.354)

  • PE: sudden dyspnea, chest pain, tachypnea, tachycardia, cough, crackles, hemoptysis, impending doom. Oxygen first, elevate head, monitor, IV fluids, anticoagulants, possible thrombolytics.肺栓塞：突發呼吸困難、胸痛、呼吸心跳快、咳嗽、囉音、咳血、瀕死感。先給氧、抬高床頭、監測、輸液、抗凝血劑，必要時溶栓。
  • Subinvolution: painful, larger than expected uterus, heavy bleeding, prolonged lochia rubra; treat with methylergonovine as prescribed, frequent voiding, monitor Hgb/Hct.子宮復舊不全：子宮疼痛、比預期大、出血多、紅惡露延長；依醫囑給予methylergonovine、常排尿、監測血紅素／血容比。
  • Thrombophlebitis: hard bumpy tender vessel, warm red area, fever, diminished pulses, swelling. Bed rest, elevate leg, warm moist heat, stockings, anticoagulation; NEVER massage the leg; avoid aspirin-containing drugs with anticoagulants; early ambulation after cesarean prevents it.血栓靜脈炎：血管硬、凹凸、壓痛，局部紅熱、發燒、脈搏減弱、腫脹。臥床休息、抬高患肢、溫濕敷、彈性襪、抗凝血；絕不可按摩小腿；使用抗凝血劑時避免含阿斯匹靈藥物；剖腹產後早期下床可預防。
  • Teach: avoid leg crossing and prolonged sitting, avoid constrictive clothing, stop smoking, adhere to anticoagulants.衛教：避免蹺腳與久坐、避免緊身衣物、戒菸、確實服用抗凝血劑。

Perinatal loss周產期喪失 📖 PDF p.397 (book p.354)

  • Includes miscarriage, stillbirth, neonatal death, abortion, and loss from preterm/complicated births or adoption.包括流產、死產、新生兒死亡、人工流產，以及早產／合併症新生兒或送養所致的失落。
  • Listen actively and allow time to grieve; offer chaplain; offer seeing/holding/bathing the infant and rituals; memory box (footprints, photos); private room with a discreet loss sign; support group referral.積極傾聽並給予哀悼時間；通知院牧；提供看、抱、沐浴嬰兒及儀式的選項；製作紀念盒（腳印、照片）；單人房並於門口放置隱諱標示；轉介支持團體。
  • Respect cultural, spiritual and religious practices; not every intervention fits every family.尊重文化、靈性與宗教習慣；並非每項措施都適用每個家庭。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Earliest sign of postpartum hemorrhage: rising pulse and restlessness; blood pressure drops late.產後出血最早徵象：脈搏上升與躁動；血壓下降較晚。 📖 PDF p.394 (book p.351), PDF p.398 (book p.355)
  2. Firm fundus but excessive bleeding suggests laceration: notify the OB; do not keep massaging.子宮底堅硬但出血過多提示裂傷：通知產科醫師，不要一直按摩。 📖 PDF p.399 (book p.356)
  3. Boggy uterus: massage until firm; never push on a boggy uterus (inversion).子宮鬆軟：按摩至變硬；切勿推壓鬆軟子宮（內翻）。 📖 PDF p.393 (book p.350), PDF p.400 (book p.357)
  4. PPH: >500 mL vaginal, >1000 mL cesarean; weigh pads 1 g = 1 mL.產後出血：陰道生產大於500 mL、剖腹產大於1000 mL；護墊秤重1 g = 1 mL。 📖 PDF p.393 (book p.350), PDF p.394 (book p.351)
  5. Shock bundle: airway, O2 8-10 L/min, legs up 30 degrees (avoid Trendelenburg), call OB, fluids, uterotonics, 2 large IVs, Foley, blood.休克處置：呼吸道、給氧8至10 L/分、抬腿30度（避免頭低腳高）、呼叫醫師、輸液、子宮收縮劑、兩條大管徑靜脈、導尿、輸血。 📖 PDF p.395 (book p.352), PDF p.399 (book p.356)
  6. Temperature 100.4 F or higher after 24 hours is infection.24小時後體溫達100.4°F以上為感染。 📖 PDF p.394 (book p.351)
  7. Mastitis: keep emptying the breast, finish antibiotics, fluids, supportive bra.乳腺炎：持續排空乳房、完成抗生素療程、多喝水、穿支撐胸罩。 📖 PDF p.395 (book p.352), PDF p.399 (book p.356)
  8. Endometritis: tender boggy uterus, foul lochia, fever; Fowler's position; do not isolate baby.子宮內膜炎：子宮壓痛、惡露惡臭、發燒；半坐臥；不隔離嬰兒。 📖 PDF p.396 (book p.353)
  9. Suspected PE: oxygen first, then IV, vitals, anticoagulant.疑似肺栓塞：先給氧，再建立靜脈、監測生命徵象、抗凝血劑。 📖 PDF p.396 (book p.353), PDF p.399 (book p.356)
  10. Never massage a leg with thrombophlebitis; avoid aspirin with anticoagulants; monitor for PE.血栓靜脈炎的腿不可按摩；抗凝血劑期間避免阿斯匹靈；監測肺栓塞。 📖 PDF p.396 (book p.353), PDF p.397 (book p.354)
  11. Vulvar hematoma: severe perineal pain/pressure; ice in first 24 hours; with epidural, vital-sign changes are the clue.外陰血腫：會陰劇痛與壓迫；初期冰敷；硬膜外麻醉者以生命徵象改變為線索。 📖 PDF p.393 (book p.350), PDF p.400 (book p.357)
  12. Subinvolution: prolonged lochia rubra, large tender uterus; methylergonovine.子宮復舊不全：紅惡露延長、子宮大而壓痛；methylergonovine。 📖 PDF p.396 (book p.353)
  13. Obtain urine culture before starting antibiotics for cystitis; push fluids.膀胱炎於抗生素前先留尿培養；多補充水分。 📖 PDF p.393 (book p.350), PDF p.399 (book p.356)
  14. Perinatal loss: individualize to culture and religion; offer memories and time with infant.周產期喪失：依文化與宗教個別化照護；提供紀念與與嬰兒相處的時間。 📖 PDF p.397 (book p.354)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • The 4 Ts of postpartum hemorrhage: Tone (atony), Trauma (lacerations/hematoma), Tissue (retained placenta), Thrombin (coagulopathy).產後出血4T：張力（收縮不良）、創傷（裂傷／血腫）、組織（胎盤殘留）、凝血（凝血異常）。 📖 PDF p.394 (book p.351)
  • Early shock: restless and fast pulse; late shock: low BP and cool, clammy skin.休克早期：躁動與脈搏快；晚期：低血壓與冰冷濕黏的皮膚。 📖 PDF p.394 (book p.351)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. Which is the earliest sign of excessive blood loss in the immediate postpartum period?產後立即期，下列何者為失血過多最早徵象？

Compensatory tachycardia comes first; hypotension and cool skin are late.代償性心搏過速最先出現；低血壓與冰冷皮膚較晚。

A contracted uterus with bleeding suggests laceration needing provider repair.子宮收縮良好仍出血，提示裂傷，需醫師修補。

4. A postpartum client has sudden chest pain and dyspnea, tachycardia and tachypnea. What is the initial nursing action?產婦突然胸痛、呼吸困難、心搏過速與呼吸過速。首要護理行動為何？

Suspected PE: correct hypoxemia first (ABCs).疑似肺栓塞：依ABC先矯正缺氧。

5. Which finding best indicates a vulvar hematoma in a client who received epidural anesthesia?接受硬膜外麻醉的產婦，哪項最能提示外陰血腫？

The epidural blunts pain; hypovolemic vital sign changes reveal the bleed.硬膜外麻醉使疼痛不明顯；低血容量的生命徵象改變可揭露出血。

7. Which client has the greatest risk for postpartum hemorrhage?哪位產婦產後出血風險最高？

Multiparity, overdistention from a large fetus and induction are risk factors.多產、大胎兒造成子宮過度膨脹與引產均為危險因子。
