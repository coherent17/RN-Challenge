This chapter reviews GI anatomy, diagnostic tests with pre/post-procedure care, and the major GI, liver, biliary and pancreatic disorders. Focus on red flags (bleeding, perforation/peritonitis, encephalopathy, shock after paracentesis), diet teaching, and priority nursing actions.本章複習消化道解剖、各項檢查的檢查前後護理，以及主要的胃腸、肝膽與胰臟疾病。重點為危險徵象（出血、穿孔/腹膜炎、肝腦病變、腹腔穿刺後休克）、飲食衛教與優先護理處置。

1GI anatomy and physiology胃腸道解剖與生理

  • The GI tract digests food, absorbs nutrients, excretes waste, and hosts bacteria that make vitamin K and some B vitamins.消化道負責消化食物、吸收養分、排泄廢物，腸道菌群合成維生素 K 與部分 B 群維生素。
  • Stomach: HCl, pepsin, intrinsic factor (from parietal cells, needed for B12), gastrin controls acidity; lower esophageal sphincter prevents reflux, pylorus controls emptying.胃：鹽酸、胃蛋白酶、內在因子（壁細胞分泌，吸收 B12 所需）、胃泌素調節酸度；下食道括約肌防逆流，幽門控制排空。
  • Small intestine: duodenum, jejunum, ileum; large intestine absorbs water; ileocecal valve prevents backflow into the ileum.小腸：十二指腸、空腸、迴腸；大腸吸收水分；迴盲瓣防止內容物回流至迴腸。
  • Liver: Kupffer cells, bile production (500-1000 mL/day), stores vitamins A, D, B and iron. Gallbladder stores bile; cholecystokinin contracts it after fatty meals.肝臟：庫佛氏細胞、產膽汁（每日 500-1000 mL）、儲存維生素 A、D、B 與鐵。膽囊儲存膽汁；高脂食物刺激 cholecystokinin 使膽囊收縮。
  • Pancreas: exocrine enzymes and bicarbonate; endocrine insulin, glucagon, somatostatin.胰臟：外分泌酵素與碳酸氫鈉；內分泌胰島素、升糖素、體抑素。

2Diagnostic procedures: endoscopy and imaging診斷檢查：內視鏡與影像

  • Barium swallow: NPO 4-8 h; afterward fluids, possible laxative, chalky white stools 24-72 h; barium can cause obstruction.上消化道鋇劑檢查：禁食 4-8 小時；檢查後多喝水、可能給瀉劑，糞便呈灰白色 24-72 小時；鋇劑可能造成阻塞。
  • EGD: consent, NPO 6-8 h, left side position; afterward NPO until gag reflex returns; watch for perforation (pain, bleeding, fever, difficulty swallowing).上消化道內視鏡：需同意書、禁食 6-8 小時、左側臥；檢查後禁食至嘔吐反射恢復；監測穿孔（疼痛、出血、發燒、吞嚥困難）。
  • Colonoscopy: bowel prep and clear liquids the day before (avoid red/orange/purple), NPO 4-6 h; afterward left lateral, expect flatus; watch for perforation/peritonitis; bowel prep causes fluid/electrolyte shifts.大腸鏡：前一天腸道準備與清流質（避免紅、橙、紫色），禁食 4-6 小時；檢查後左側臥、排氣屬正常；監測穿孔/腹膜炎；腸道準備可致水分電解質失衡。
  • ERCP: NPO 6-8 h, consent, avoid anticoagulants/NSAIDs, ask about contrast allergy; afterward check gag reflex, perforation/peritonitis, liver and pancreatic enzymes. MRCP is the noninvasive alternative.ERCP：禁食 6-8 小時、同意書、避免抗凝血劑/NSAIDs、詢問顯影劑過敏；檢查後確認嘔吐反射、監測穿孔/腹膜炎、肝與胰臟酵素。MRCP 為非侵入性替代。
  • Capsule endoscopy: bowel prep, clear liquids evening before, brief NPO; gastric analysis requires fasting 8-12 h and no tobacco/gum 24 h.膠囊內視鏡：腸道準備、前一晚清流質、短暫禁食；胃液分析需禁食 8-12 小時，24 小時內不可抽菸/嚼口香糖。
  • CT: NPO at least 4 h, ask about contrast allergy, push fluids afterward to protect kidneys.電腦斷層：至少禁食 4 小時、詢問顯影劑過敏、檢查後多喝水保護腎臟。

3Paracentesis, liver biopsy, stool and laboratory tests腹腔穿刺、肝臟切片、糞便與實驗室檢查

  • Paracentesis prep: consent, vitals, weight, void, girth, upright position. After: monitor BP/pulse and mental status for shock from rapid vasodilation, bleeding, hematuria, weight and girth loss.腹腔穿刺前：同意書、生命徵象、體重、排尿、腹圍、直坐姿。檢查後：監測血壓/脈搏與意識（快速血管擴張致休克）、出血、血尿、體重與腹圍減少。
  • Liver biopsy: NPO 8-12 h, check PT/PTT/platelets, hold breath after expiration during needle; afterward lie on RIGHT side with pillow under costal margin for 2 h, bed rest 12-14 h; dyspnea/cyanosis signals pneumothorax.肝臟切片：禁食 8-12 小時、檢查 PT/PTT/血小板、穿刺時吐氣後閉氣；術後右側臥並於肋緣下墊枕 2 小時、臥床 12-14 小時；呼吸困難/發紺提示氣胸。
  • Urea breath test detects H. pylori (hold antibiotics/bismuth 1 month, PPIs/sucralfate 1 week, H2RAs 24 h).尿素呼氣試驗偵測幽門螺旋桿菌（抗生素/鉍劑停 1 個月、PPI/sucralfate 停 1 週、H2 阻斷劑停 24 小時）。
  • Liver tests: ALP 38-126 U/L, AST 0-35, ALT 4-36, PT 11-12.5 s, PTT 30-40 s, INR 0.8-1.2, ammonia 10-80 mcg/dL, total bilirubin 0.3-1.0 mg/dL, amylase 60-120 Somogyi units/dL, lipase 0-160 U/L.肝功能：ALP 38-126 U/L、AST 0-35、ALT 4-36、PT 11-12.5 秒、PTT 30-40 秒、INR 0.8-1.2、氨 10-80 mcg/dL、總膽紅素 0.3-1.0 mg/dL、澱粉酶 60-120 Somogyi 單位/dL、脂肪酶 0-160 U/L。
  • Prolonged PT with low platelets before biopsy: notify PHCP and start bleeding precautions.切片前 PT 延長且血小板低：通知醫師並採取出血防護。
  • Signs of perforation/peritonitis: guarding, rigid distended abdomen, rebound tenderness, absent bowel sounds, fever, tachycardia, hypotension.穿孔/腹膜炎徵象：腹部防衛、僵硬脹大、反彈痛、腸音消失、發燒、心搏過速、低血壓。

4GERD, gastritis, hiatal hernia胃食道逆流、胃炎、食道裂孔疝氣

  • GERD: incompetent LES; heartburn, regurgitation, chronic night cough, hoarseness, dysphagia. Avoid peppermint, chocolate, caffeine, fatty food, alcohol, citrus, tomato, smoking.胃食道逆流：下食道括約肌鬆弛；燒心、逆流、夜間慢性咳嗽、聲音沙啞、吞嚥困難。避免薄荷、巧克力、咖啡因、油膩、酒精、柑橘、番茄與抽菸。
  • GERD teaching: small meals, no food 2 h before bed, stay upright 2-3 h after eating, raise head of bed 6-8 inches; avoid anticholinergics, NSAIDs, calcium channel blockers. Fundoplication if refractory.衛教：少量多餐、睡前 2 小時不進食、餐後直立 2-3 小時、床頭抬高 6-8 吋；避免抗膽鹼藥、NSAIDs、鈣離子阻斷劑。難治時做胃底折疊術。
  • Acute gastritis: NSAIDs, alcohol, H. pylori; watch for hemorrhagic gastritis (hematemesis, tachycardia, hypotension). Chronic gastritis can cause B12 deficiency.急性胃炎：NSAIDs、酒精、H. pylori；注意出血性胃炎（吐血、心搏過速、低血壓）。慢性胃炎可致 B12 缺乏。
  • Hiatal hernia: stomach herniates through diaphragm; symptoms worse lying down; small frequent meals, do not recline for 1 hour after eating; complications include strangulation.食道裂孔疝氣：胃經橫膈膜突入胸腔；平躺時症狀加重；少量多餐、餐後 1 小時內勿平躺；併發症含絞扼。

5Peptic ulcer disease, gastric surgery and dumping syndrome消化性潰瘍、胃部手術與傾倒症候群

  • Gastric ulcer: pain 1-2 h after meals, worsened by food, hematemesis more common. Duodenal ulcer: pain 2-5 h after meals/night, relieved by food, melena more common.胃潰瘍：餐後 1-2 小時痛、進食加重、較常吐血。十二指腸潰瘍：餐後 2-5 小時或夜間痛、進食緩解、較常黑便。
  • Risks: H. pylori, NSAIDs, smoking, alcohol, steroids, stress. Avoid caffeinated and decaffeinated coffee, alcohol, aspirin/NSAIDs. Complications: hemorrhage, perforation, obstruction.危險因子：H. pylori、NSAIDs、抽菸、酒精、類固醇、壓力。避免含咖啡因與去咖啡因咖啡、酒精、阿斯匹靈/NSAIDs。併發症：出血、穿孔、阻塞。
  • Active bleeding: stabilize airway/circulation, two large-bore IVs, NPO, monitor Hgb/Hct and coagulation, type and cross, transfusion, vasoconstrictive drugs.活動性出血：穩定呼吸道與循環、兩條大管徑靜脈管路、禁食、監測 Hgb/Hct 與凝血、血型交叉、輸血、血管收縮藥。
  • Surgeries: vagotomy, antrectomy, Billroth I (gastroduodenostomy), Billroth II (gastrojejunostomy), pyloroplasty. Post-op: Fowler's, NG suction, NPO 1-3 days; do not irrigate or reposition the NG tube without order.手術：迷走神經切斷、胃竇切除、Billroth I（胃十二指腸吻合）、Billroth II（胃空腸吻合）、幽門成形。術後：半坐臥、鼻胃管抽吸、禁食 1-3 天；無醫囑不可沖洗或移動鼻胃管。
  • Post-op complications: hemorrhage, dumping syndrome, hypoglycemia, bile reflux gastritis, B12 deficiency.術後併發症：出血、傾倒症候群、低血糖、膽汁逆流性胃炎、B12 缺乏。
  • Dumping syndrome (about 30 min after eating): nausea, fullness, cramping, diarrhea, palpitations, sweating, dizziness. Prevent: 5-6 small meals, high-protein/complex carbs, limit sugar, no fluids with meals, lie down 20-30 min after.傾倒症候群（進食後約 30 分鐘）：噁心、飽脹、絞痛、腹瀉、心悸、冒汗、頭暈。預防：一天 5-6 小餐、高蛋白與複合碳水、限制糖、餐中不喝水、餐後平躺 20-30 分鐘。

6Bariatric surgery and biliary disease減重手術與膽道疾病

  • Bariatric surgery: psychological screening, lifelong lifestyle change; anastomotic leak (abdominal pain, restlessness, unexplained tachycardia, oliguria) is the most serious complication.減重手術：心理評估、需終身改變生活型態；吻合口漏（腹痛、躁動、不明原因心搏過速、少尿）為最嚴重併發症。
  • Post-bariatric diet: 1-ounce clear liquids, then puree, liquids/puree for about 6 weeks; eat slowly; supplements calcium, iron, multivitamin, B12; avoid high sugar/fat.術後飲食：先 30 mL 清流質，再進展至泥狀食物，流質/泥狀約 6 週；細嚼慢嚥；補充鈣、鐵、綜合維他命、B12；避免高糖高脂。
  • Cholecystitis: RUQ pain radiating to right shoulder after fatty meal, Murphy's sign, fever, nausea; biliary obstruction causes jaundice, dark foamy urine, clay stools, pruritus.膽囊炎：高脂餐後右上腹痛並放射至右肩、Murphy 氏徵象、發燒、噁心；膽道阻塞出現黃疸、深色泡沫尿、陶土色便、搔癢。
  • Treatment: NPO, NG decompression, antiemetics, analgesics, antispasmodics; cholecystectomy usually laparoscopic. Post-op: cough/deep breathe, splint, early ambulation.治療：禁食、鼻胃管減壓、止吐、止痛、解痙；膽囊切除常為腹腔鏡。術後：咳嗽深呼吸、按壓傷口、早期下床。
  • T-tube: drainage bag below gallbladder level, semi-Fowler's, report sudden increase in bile or foul purulent drainage; no irrigation/clamping without order.T 型管：引流袋低於膽囊高度、半坐臥、膽汁突然增加或惡臭膿性引流須通報；無醫囑不可沖洗/夾閉。

7Cirrhosis and esophageal varices肝硬化與食道靜脈曲張

  • Cirrhosis: chronic hepatocyte destruction and scarring (hepatitis C, alcohol, NAFLD/NASH). Complications: portal hypertension, ascites, varices, coagulopathy, jaundice, encephalopathy, hepatorenal syndrome.肝硬化：肝細胞慢性破壞與纖維化（C 肝、酒精、非酒精性脂肪肝）。併發症：門脈高壓、腹水、靜脈曲張、凝血障礙、黃疸、肝腦病變、肝腎症候群。
  • Encephalopathy: ammonia not detoxified; asterixis, altered LOC, fetor hepaticus. Give lactulose (lowers bowel pH, ammonia excretion) and antibiotics; avoid opioids, sedatives, barbiturates, hepatotoxins; alcohol abstinence.肝腦病變：氨無法代謝；撲翼樣顫抖、意識改變、肝臭。給予 lactulose（降低腸道 pH、排出氨）與抗生素；避免鴉片類、鎮靜劑、巴比妥與肝毒性物質；戒酒。
  • Care: HOB elevated, Na and fluid restriction, diuretics, daily weight and abdominal girth, vitamin K/B complex, monitor coagulation and electrolytes, paracentesis if needed.照護：抬高床頭、限鈉限水、利尿劑、每日體重與腹圍、維生素 K/B 群、監測凝血與電解質、必要時腹腔穿刺。
  • Varices: dilated esophageal veins from portal hypertension; rupture = life-threatening hematemesis/melena/shock. NPO, oxygen, IV fluids, blood, monitor Hgb/Hct, vasoconstrictors, endoscopic sclerotherapy or band ligation.靜脈曲張：門脈高壓使食道靜脈擴張；破裂會危及生命（吐血、黑便、休克）。禁食、氧氣、靜脈輸液、輸血、監測 Hgb/Hct、血管收縮藥、內視鏡硬化或套扎。
  • Balloon tamponade is uncomfortable and rarely used; shunts include portacaval, distal splenorenal, mesocaval, and TIPS.氣囊填塞不舒適且少用；分流手術包含門腔、遠端脾腎、腸繫膜腔靜脈分流與 TIPS。

8Viral hepatitis A to E病毒性肝炎 A 至 E

  • Stages: preicteric (flu-like, anorexia, most transmissible), icteric (jaundice, dark urine, clay stools, pruritus), posticteric (recovery, 6-8 weeks).分期：黃疸前期（類流感、厭食、傳染力最強）、黃疸期（黃疸、深色尿、陶土便、搔癢）、黃疸後期（恢復，約 6-8 週）。
  • Hepatitis A: fecal-oral, incubation 15-50 days, anti-HAV IgM = acute; vaccine 2 doses 6 months apart; immune globulin within 2 weeks of exposure.A 肝：糞口傳染，潛伏期 15-50 天，anti-HAV IgM 代表急性感染；疫苗兩劑間隔 6 個月；暴露後 2 週內給免疫球蛋白。
  • Hepatitis B: blood/body fluids, incubation 60-180 days; HBsAg diagnostic, persisting beyond 6 months = chronic/carrier; anti-HBs = recovery/immunity; vaccine and HBIG.B 肝：血液/體液傳染，潛伏期 60-180 天；HBsAg 為診斷指標，6 個月後仍存在=慢性/帶原；anti-HBs=康復/免疫；疫苗與 HBIG。
  • Hepatitis C: blood, IV drug use, transfusions, born 1945-1965; anti-HCV; leads to chronic liver disease and carcinoma. Hepatitis D needs HBV to replicate. Hepatitis E: waterborne, high mortality in third-trimester pregnancy.C 肝：血液、靜脈藥癮、輸血、1945-1965 年出生者；anti-HCV；可致慢性肝病與肝癌。D 肝需有 B 肝才可複製。E 肝：水源傳染，孕晚期死亡率高。
  • Home care: strict handwashing, personal items not shared, no food preparation for others, avoid alcohol and hepatotoxic OTC drugs (acetaminophen, sedatives), small high-carbohydrate low-fat meals, no blood donation.居家：嚴格洗手、個人用品不共用、不為他人備餐、避免酒精與肝毒性成藥（acetaminophen、鎮靜劑）、少量多餐高碳水低脂、不可捐血。

9Pancreatitis胰臟炎

  • Causes: alcohol, gallstones, trauma, hyperlipidemia, hypercalcemia. Acute: sudden epigastric/LUQ pain radiating to back, worse after fatty meal/lying flat, N/V, low-grade fever.病因：酒精、膽結石、外傷、高血脂、高血鈣。急性：突發上腹/左上腹痛放射至背部，油膩餐後或平躺加重、噁心嘔吐、低燒。
  • Labs: elevated serum lipase and amylase, WBC, glucose, bilirubin. Cullen's sign (periumbilical) and Turner's sign (flank bruising).檢驗：血清脂肪酶與澱粉酶、WBC、血糖、膽紅素升高。Cullen 徵象（臍周瘀斑）與 Turner 徵象（側腹瘀斑）。
  • Acute care: NPO, IV fluids, NG for vomiting/ileus, opioids for pain, H2RA/PPI, parenteral or nasojejunal feeding, watch for shock, avoid alcohol.急性期：禁食、靜脈輸液、嘔吐/腸阻塞時鼻胃管、止痛用鴉片類、H2 阻斷劑/PPI、腸外或鼻空腸餵食、注意休克、戒酒。
  • Chronic: steatorrhea, weight loss, diabetes; pancreatic enzymes with meals, small high-calorie low-fat meals, no alcohol/caffeine, insulin if needed.慢性：脂肪瀉、體重減輕、糖尿病；餐時給胰酵素、少量高熱量低脂餐、不喝酒/咖啡因、必要時用胰島素。

10IBS, ulcerative colitis, Crohn's disease腸躁症、潰瘍性結腸炎、克隆氏症

  • IBS: functional; increase fiber, 8-10 cups fluid, probiotics; lubiprostone/linaclotide for constipation-predominant, alosetron for diarrhea-predominant.腸躁症：功能性；增加纖維、每日 8-10 杯水、益生菌；便秘型用 lubiprostone/linaclotide，腹瀉型用 alosetron。
  • Ulcerative colitis: starts in rectum and moves proximally; severe bloody mucus diarrhea, anemia, dehydration; acute phase NPO/IV or TPN, then low-fiber high-protein diet; avoid dairy, raw produce, nuts, caffeine, alcohol; salicylates, steroids, immunosuppressants.潰瘍性結腸炎：由直腸向上蔓延；嚴重血便黏液便、貧血、脫水；急性期禁食/靜脈或全靜脈營養，之後低纖高蛋白飲食；避免乳製品、生鮮蔬果、堅果、咖啡因、酒精；水楊酸製劑、類固醇、免疫抑制劑。
  • Surgery: restorative proctocolectomy with ileal pouch-anal anastomosis (two-stage, temporary ileostomy) or total proctocolectomy with permanent ileostomy.手術：迴腸袋肛門吻合重建性大腸直腸切除（兩階段、暫時性迴腸造口）或全大腸直腸切除合併永久性迴腸造口。

  • Chronic: steatorrhea, weight loss, diabetes; pancreatic enzymes with meals, small high-calorie low-fat meals, no alcohol/caffeine, insulin if needed.慢性：脂肪瀉、體重減輕、糖尿病；餐時給胰酵素、少量高熱量低脂餐、不喝酒/咖啡因、必要時用胰島素。
Topic 10 · tap to flip主題 10 · 點擊翻面
IBS, ulcerative colitis, Crohn's disease腸躁症、潰瘍性結腸炎、克隆氏症
  • IBS: functional; increase fiber, 8-10 cups fluid, probiotics; lubiprostone/linaclotide for constipation-predominant, alosetron for diarrhea-predominant.腸躁症：功能性；增加纖維、每日 8-10 杯水、益生菌；便秘型用 lubiprostone/linaclotide，腹瀉型用 alosetron。
  • Ulcerative colitis: starts in rectum and moves proximally; severe bloody mucus diarrhea, anemia, dehydration; acute phase NPO/IV or TPN, then low-fiber high-protein diet; avoid dairy, raw produce, nuts, caffeine, alcohol; salicylates, steroids, immunosuppressants.潰瘍性結腸炎：由直腸向上蔓延；嚴重血便黏液便、貧血、脫水；急性期禁食/靜脈或全靜脈營養，之後低纖高蛋白飲食；避免乳製品、生鮮蔬果、堅果、咖啡因、酒精；水楊酸製劑、類固醇、免疫抑制劑。
  • Surgery: restorative proctocolectomy with ileal pouch-anal anastomosis (two-stage, temporary ileostomy) or total proctocolectomy with permanent ileostomy.手術：迴腸袋肛門吻合重建性大腸直腸切除（兩階段、暫時性迴腸造口）或全大腸直腸切除合併永久性迴腸造口。
  • Stoma: pink to bright red and shiny is normal; pale pink = low Hgb/Hct; purple-black = compromised circulation, notify PHCP immediately. Empty pouch when one-third full.造口：粉紅至鮮紅濕潤為正常；蒼白代表 Hgb/Hct 低；紫黑代表血流受損，須立即通知醫師。袋子三分之一滿即排空。
  • Crohn's: any GI segment, usually terminal ileum, fistulas, strictures, cramping after meals, worse malnutrition; surgery avoided because recurrence is common.克隆氏症：可發生於任何消化道、多在末端迴腸，有瘻管、狹窄、餐後絞痛、營養不良更嚴重；因復發率高盡量避免手術。

Appendicitis, diverticular disease, hemorrhoids闌尾炎、憩室疾病、痔瘡
  • Appendicitis: periumbilical pain moving to RLQ, maximal at McBurney's point, rebound tenderness, low-grade fever, high WBC. No heat, laxatives or enemas; pain that suddenly stops may mean rupture.闌尾炎：臍周痛轉移至右下腹、McBurney 點最痛、反彈痛、低燒、WBC 升高。不可熱敷、不給瀉劑或灌腸。
  • Pre-op: NPO, IV fluids, antibiotics, right side-lying or semi-Fowler's, ice pack if prescribed. Post-op: if ruptured, drain/open wound, semi-Fowler's with legs flexed, antibiotics, early ambulation.術前：禁食、靜脈輸液、抗生素、右側臥或半坐臥、必要時冰敷。術後：若破裂有引流/開放傷口、半坐臥雙腿屈曲、抗生素、早期下床。
  • Diverticulitis: LLQ pain, fever, N/V, blood in stool; bed rest, NPO or clear liquids, low-fiber during flare then high-fiber; fluids 2500-3000 mL; avoid straining, seeds/nuts/popcorn; watch for perforation, abscess, fistula.憩室炎：左下腹痛、發燒、噁心嘔吐、血便；臥床休息、禁食或清流質、發炎期低纖維，緩解後高纖維；水分 2500-3000 mL；避免用力、種子/堅果/爆米花；注意穿孔、膿瘍、瘻管。
  • Hemorrhoids: bright red bleeding with defecation; high fiber, fluids, stool softeners, sitz baths, witch hazel.痔瘡：排便時鮮紅色出血；高纖維、多喝水、軟便劑、坐浴、金縷梅。
  • Post-hemorrhoidectomy: prone/side-lying, ice packs, watch urinary retention, first bowel movement painful (vasovagal risk, stay nearby), short sitting periods, sitz baths 3-4 times daily.痔瘡切除術後：俯臥/側臥、冰敷、注意尿滯留、第一次排便會痛（可能迷走神經反應，需有人在旁）、縮短久坐、每日坐浴 3-4 次。
