Chapter 24: Labor and Birth第 24 章：分娩與生產

📖 Source: PDF p.363–376 · printed book p.323–335.e1來源：PDF 第 363–376 頁 · 書本印刷頁 323–335.e1

This chapter covers the normal process of labor: the 4 Ps, fetal lie/presentation/position/station, signs of true vs false labor, fetal heart rate (FHR) monitoring, the four stages of labor, anesthesia options, and common obstetric procedures. Reading fetal monitor patterns and knowing the first-priority action for nonreassuring patterns is the most heavily tested skill.本章說明正常分娩過程：4P（產力、產道、胎兒、心理）、胎位相關名詞（胎勢、胎向、先露、胎位、站位）、真假陣痛、胎心率（FHR）監測、產程四階段、麻醉方式與常見產科處置。能判讀胎心監測圖形並知道不安心圖形的首要處置，是最常考的能力。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Process of Labor: the 4 Ps and fetal terms分娩過程：4P與胎兒相關名詞 📖 PDF p.363 (book p.323)

  • The 4 Ps are Powers (contractions, effacement, dilation, pushing), Passageway (bony pelvis and soft tissue), Passenger (fetus, membranes, placenta) and Psyche (emotional state).4P為產力（宮縮、子宮頸變薄與擴張、用力）、產道（骨盆與軟組織）、胎兒及附屬物（胎兒、胎膜、胎盤）與心理狀態。
  • Normal attitude is flexion (chin to chest, limbs folded); extension presents larger diameters.正常胎勢為屈曲（下巴貼胸、四肢蜷曲）；伸展會使胎頭通過的徑線變大。
  • Lie: longitudinal (spines parallel; cephalic or breech) vs transverse (right angle, shoulder presenting, cesarean needed).胎向：縱向（脊柱平行，頭位或臀位）；橫向（垂直，肩先露，需剖腹產）。
  • Presentation: cephalic (most common: vertex, military, brow, face), breech (frank, full, footling), shoulder.先露：頭位（最常見：頂位、軍姿位、額位、顏面位）、臀位（單臀、完全、足先露）、肩位。
  • Station measures descent relative to the ischial spines: 0 = at spines (engaged), minus = above, plus = below.站位以坐骨棘為基準測量下降：0為在坐骨棘（已入盆）、負值在上方、正值在下方。

Signs of labor and mechanisms of labor產兆與分娩機轉 📖 PDF p.364 (book p.324), PDF p.365 (book p.325), PDF p.366 (book p.326)

  • Pre-labor signs: lightening (about 2 weeks before in first pregnancies), more Braxton Hicks, bloody show, cervical ripening, nesting burst of energy, 1-3 lb weight loss, rupture of membranes.產前徵兆：胎兒下降（初產婦約提前2週）、假性宮縮增加、見紅、子宮頸成熟、築巢本能、體重減輕1至3磅、破水。
  • True labor: contractions regular, stronger, longer, closer; progressive dilation/effacement and descent. False labor: irregular, no cervical change, often relieved by walking.真陣痛：規律、越來越強、越長、間隔越短，子宮頸持續變化與下降；假陣痛：不規則、無頸部變化，走動常可緩解。
  • Cardinal movements in order: engagement, descent, flexion, internal rotation, extension, restitution, external rotation, expulsion.分娩機轉順序：入盆、下降、屈曲、內旋轉、伸展、復位、外旋轉、娩出。
  • Leopold maneuvers locate the fetal back (smooth, hard) and small parts (knobs) and help place the Doppler.李氏四步觸診可判斷胎背（光滑堅硬）與小肢體（不規則突起），並協助放置胎心探頭。
  • Breathing: slow-paced, modified (shallow fast), pattern-paced (pant-blow); blow in short puffs to avoid pushing early; limit breath-holding to under 6-8 seconds when pushing.呼吸技巧：慢式、改良式（淺快）、模式式（喘吹）；不想用力時短促吹氣；用力時憋氣少於6至8秒。

Fetal monitoring and FHR patterns胎兒監測與胎心率圖形 📖 PDF p.366 (book p.326), PDF p.367 (book p.327), PDF p.368 (book p.328)

  • Normal baseline FHR at term is 110-160 bpm, measured between contractions. Bradycardia <110, tachycardia >160 for 10 minutes or more.足月正常基準胎心率為110至160次/分，於宮縮間測量；持續10分鐘以上低於110為心搏過緩，高於160為過速。
  • External monitoring: ultrasound transducer over the fetal back, tocotransducer over the fundus; side-lying to avoid vena cava compression. Internal monitoring (scalp electrode, IUPC) needs ruptured membranes and 2-3 cm dilation.外部監測：超音波探頭置於胎背處、宮縮壓力計置於子宮底；採側臥避免壓迫下腔靜脈。內部監測（胎頭電極、子宮內壓導管）需破水且擴張2至3公分。
  • Variability: absent (undetected), minimal (<=5), moderate (6-25), marked (>25 bpm). Absent/minimal can mean hypoxemia, acidosis, drugs, or fetal sleep (up to 30 min).變異性：無（測不到）、輕微（5以下）、中度（6至25）、顯著（大於25次/分）。缺乏或輕微可能代表缺氧、酸中毒、藥物或胎兒睡眠（最長約30分鐘）。
  • Accelerations (>=15 bpm for >=15 sec) are reassuring.加速（上升至少15次/分且持續至少15秒）為安心圖形。
  • Early decelerations: mirror the contraction, head compression, benign. Late: start after contraction onset, uteroplacental insufficiency, nonreassuring. Variable: abrupt, variable shape, cord compression.早期減速：與宮縮呈鏡像、胎頭受壓、良性；晚期減速：宮縮開始後才出現、子宮胎盤功能不全、不安心；變異減速：突然、形狀不一、臍帶受壓。
  • Significant variables fall below 70 bpm and last 60 seconds or longer. Uterine resting tone averages 5-15 mm Hg; contractions 50-75 mm Hg; uterus should relax 60 seconds or more between contractions.重要的變異減速為降至70次/分以下且持續60秒以上。子宮靜止張力平均5至15 mmHg，宮縮強度50至75 mmHg，宮縮間子宮應放鬆至少60秒。

Stages of labor產程階段 📖 PDF p.368 (book p.328), PDF p.369 (book p.329), PDF p.370 (book p.330)

  • Stage 1 latent: 1-4 cm, contractions q15-30 min, 15-30 sec, mild; ambulate, fluids, void every 1-2 hr.第一產程潛伏期：1至4公分，每15至30分鐘一次、持續15至30秒、輕度；鼓勵走動、補充水分、每1至2小時排尿。
  • Stage 1 active: 4-7 cm, q3-5 min, 30-60 sec, moderate; breathing support, back rubs, sacral pressure.活躍期：4至7公分，每3至5分鐘、持續30至60秒、中度；協助呼吸、背部按摩、薦骨加壓。
  • Transition: 8-10 cm, q2-3 min, 45-90 sec, strong; rest between contractions, privacy, wake client to start breathing.過渡期：8至10公分，每2至3分鐘、持續45至90秒、強烈；宮縮間休息、維護隱私、宮縮開始時喚醒產婦開始呼吸。
  • Stage 2: complete dilation to birth; Ferguson reflex urge to push, bloody show increases; assess every 5 minutes.第二產程：全開至胎兒娩出；出現Ferguson反射的用力衝動、血性分泌物增加；每5分鐘評估一次。
  • Stage 3: placenta expelled 5-30 min after birth. Schultze: shiny fetal side first; Duncan: dull maternal side first. Inspect placenta for completeness; fundus firm, 2 fingerbreadths below umbilicus.第三產程：胎兒娩出後5至30分鐘胎盤娩出。Schultze為光亮胎兒面先出；Duncan為粗糙母體面先出。檢查胎盤是否完整；子宮底堅硬、位於肚臍下兩指幅。
  • Stage 4: 1-4 hours after birth; assess every 15 min x1 hr, 30 min x1 hr, then hourly x2; warm blankets, perineal ice, fundal massage, lochia moderate and red.第四產程：產後1至4小時；每15分鐘評估1小時、每30分鐘1小時，之後每小時2小時；保暖、會陰冰敷、子宮按摩、惡露中量紅色。

Anesthesia and analgesia麻醉與止痛 📖 PDF p.370 (book p.330)

  • Local (episiotomy): no fetal effect.局部麻醉（會陰切開）：對胎兒無影響。
  • Epidural (L3-L4): hypotension, bladder distention, longer second stage; keep side-lying or wedge under right hip, give IV fluids, check bladder; watch opioid effects (nausea, itching, respiratory depression).硬膜外麻醉（L3至L4）：可能低血壓、膀胱脹、第二產程延長；側臥或右臀下墊枕、給予靜脈輸液、檢查膀胱；注意鴉片類副作用（噁心、搔癢、呼吸抑制）。
  • Spinal (L3-L5): hypotension and postdural headache; lie flat 8-12 hours.脊髓麻醉（L3至L5）：低血壓與脊髓穿刺後頭痛；需平躺8至12小時。
  • General: risk of respiratory depression, vomiting, aspiration.全身麻醉：有呼吸抑制、嘔吐與吸入性肺炎風險。

Obstetric procedures產科處置 📖 PDF p.370 (book p.330), PDF p.371 (book p.331), PDF p.372 (book p.332)

  • Bishop score (dilation, effacement, consistency, position, station) of 8 or more means a favorable cervix for induction.Bishop評分（擴張、變薄、質地、位置、站位）達8分以上代表子宮頸有利於引產。
  • Oxytocin induction: baseline tracing first; do not increase once contractions reach every 2-3 min lasting 60 sec; stop if contractions closer than 2 min, longer than 90 sec, or fetal distress.催產素引產：先取得基準圖形；宮縮達每2至3分鐘、持續60秒即不再加量；宮縮間隔小於2分鐘、持續超過90秒或胎兒窘迫時停藥。
  • Amniotomy at 0 or plus station: risks cord prolapse and infection; check FHR before and after, note fluid color (meconium = distress, bloody = abruption, foul = infection).人工破水於站位0或正值時進行：有臍帶脫垂與感染風險；破水前後檢查胎心，記錄羊水顏色（胎便＝窘迫、血性＝胎盤早剝、臭味＝感染）。
  • External version after 34 weeks: give RhoGAM if Rh-negative, nonstress test afterward, Kleihauer-Betke test.外轉胎位術於34週後進行：Rh陰性者確認已給予RhoGAM，術後做無壓力試驗與Kleihauer-Betke試驗。
  • Episiotomy: ice 24 hours then sitz baths; front-to-back, blot dry. Forceps/vacuum: check infant for injury; vacuum no longer than 25 minutes; watch for cephalhematoma (caput resolves in 24 hr).會陰切開：24小時冰敷後改坐浴；由前往後、輕拍擦乾。產鉗與真空吸引：檢查新生兒有無傷害；真空吸引不超過25分鐘；注意頭血腫（產瘤24小時內消退）。
  • Cesarean: consent, IV, Foley, Rh status; postop watch for UTI, endometritis (tender uterus, foul lochia), pneumonia, thrombophlebitis.剖腹產：同意書、靜脈留置、導尿、確認Rh；術後注意泌尿道感染、子宮內膜炎（子宮壓痛、惡露惡臭）、肺炎、血栓靜脈炎。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Normal FHR 110-160 bpm; a baseline of 100 between contractions or 180 needs provider notification.正常胎心率110至160次/分；宮縮間為100或達180需通知醫師。 📖 PDF p.366 (book p.326), PDF p.374 (book p.334), PDF p.375 (book p.335)
  2. Late decelerations = uteroplacental insufficiency: turn side-lying, oxygen 8-10 L/min by face mask, IV fluids, stop oxytocin, notify provider.晚期減速＝子宮胎盤功能不全：側臥、面罩給氧8至10 L/分、靜脈輸液、停催產素、通知醫師。 📖 PDF p.367 (book p.327), PDF p.374 (book p.334)
  3. Variable decelerations = cord compression: stop oxytocin, reposition, oxygen, assist with amnioinfusion if prescribed.變異減速＝臍帶受壓：停催產素、變換姿勢、給氧、依醫囑協助羊水灌注。 📖 PDF p.367 (book p.327), PDF p.368 (book p.328)
  4. Early decelerations and accelerations are reassuring; early decelerations need no intervention.早期減速與加速為安心圖形；早期減速不需處置。 📖 PDF p.366 (book p.326), PDF p.367 (book p.327)
  5. Nonreassuring patterns: bradycardia, tachycardia, late or prolonged decelerations, hypertonic uterus, absent variability, severe variables (<70 bpm for >60 sec).不安心圖形：心搏過緩、過速、晚期或延長減速、子宮張力過高、變異性消失、嚴重變異減速（低於70次/分超過60秒）。 📖 PDF p.367 (book p.327)
  6. If oxytocin is running and fetal distress or hyperstimulation appears, the priority is to stop the oxytocin infusion.使用催產素時若出現胎兒窘迫或過度刺激，首要處置為停止催產素。 📖 PDF p.371 (book p.331), PDF p.375 (book p.335)
  7. After rupture of membranes (spontaneous or amniotomy) check FHR first because of cord prolapse risk.破水後（自然或人工）首先確認胎心率，以排除臍帶脫垂。 📖 PDF p.369 (book p.329), PDF p.371 (book p.331), PDF p.375 (book p.335)
  8. Avoid supine position: use side-lying or a wedge under the right hip to prevent vena cava compression, including on the cesarean table.避免仰臥：採側臥或右臀下墊楔形枕，以防下腔靜脈受壓，剖腹產手術台亦然。 📖 PDF p.366 (book p.326), PDF p.370 (book p.330), PDF p.374 (book p.334)
  9. Spinal anesthesia: lie flat 8-12 hours, risk of hypotension and headache. Epidural: hypotension, check bladder.脊髓麻醉：平躺8至12小時，有低血壓與頭痛風險；硬膜外：低血壓、檢查膀胱。 📖 PDF p.370 (book p.330)
  10. True labor shows progressive cervical change; contractions that last longer and stronger. Walking relieves false labor.真陣痛使子宮頸持續變化、宮縮增強延長；假陣痛走動可緩解。 📖 PDF p.365 (book p.325), PDF p.375 (book p.335)
  11. Bishop score 8 or higher = favorable for induction. Amniotomy requires fetus at 0 or plus station.Bishop評分8分以上有利引產；人工破水需胎頭在站位0或正值。 📖 PDF p.371 (book p.331)
  12. Meconium-stained fluid suggests fetal distress; bloody fluid suggests abruption; foul-smelling fluid suggests infection.胎便污染羊水提示胎兒窘迫；血性提示胎盤早剝；惡臭提示感染。 📖 PDF p.371 (book p.331)
  13. Never leave a laboring client in transition alone; in second stage prioritize rest between contractions and effective pushing.過渡期產婦不可獨留；第二產程重點為宮縮間休息與有效用力。 📖 PDF p.369 (book p.329), PDF p.375 (book p.335)
  14. Postpartum complications after cesarean: pain/burning urination (UTI), tender uterus plus foul lochia (endometritis), productive cough/chills (pneumonia), calf pain/redness (thrombophlebitis).剖腹產術後併發症：排尿灼痛（泌尿道感染）、子宮壓痛合併惡露惡臭（子宮內膜炎）、有痰咳嗽或畏寒（肺炎）、小腿疼痛紅腫（血栓靜脈炎）。 📖 PDF p.372 (book p.332)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • VEAL CHOP: Variable decels - Cord compression; Early - Head compression; Accelerations - OK; Late - Placental insufficiency.VEAL CHOP：變異減速－臍帶受壓；早期－胎頭受壓；加速－正常；晚期－胎盤功能不全。 📖 PDF p.367 (book p.327)
  • Cardinal movements: "EDFIREE" - Engagement, Descent, Flexion, Internal rotation, Extension, Restitution/External rotation, Expulsion.分娩機轉口訣：入盆、下降、屈曲、內旋轉、伸展、復位與外旋轉、娩出。 📖 PDF p.365 (book p.325)
  • 4 Ps of labor: Powers, Passageway, Passenger, Psyche.分娩4P：產力、產道、胎兒、心理。 📖 PDF p.363 (book p.323)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. A client in labor with an oxytocin infusion has contractions every 90 seconds lasting 100 seconds, and the FHR shows late decelerations. What is the priority action?一位使用催產素的產婦，宮縮每90秒一次、持續100秒，胎心監測出現晚期減速。首要處置為何？

Uterine hyperstimulation is causing uteroplacental insufficiency; removing the cause (oxytocin) is first. Then reposition, give oxygen and fluids, and notify the provider.子宮過度刺激造成子宮胎盤灌流不足，先移除原因（停催產素），之後變換姿勢、給氧、輸液並通知醫師。

📖 PDF p.367 (book p.327), PDF p.371 (book p.331), PDF p.375 (book p.335)

Stage 2 starts at complete dilation; Ferguson reflex and increased bloody show accompany it. 4 cm is active phase of stage 1; placenta delivery is stage 3.第二產程自全開開始，伴隨Ferguson反射及血性分泌物增加；4公分為第一產程活躍期；胎盤娩出為第三產程。

3. The monitor shows decelerations that begin as the contraction begins, mirror it, and return to baseline by the end of the contraction; the lowest FHR is 120. What should the nurse do?監視器顯示減速在宮縮開始時出現、與宮縮呈鏡像，並於宮縮結束時回到基準；最低胎心為120。護理師應如何處理？

These are early decelerations from head compression, a benign pattern.此為胎頭受壓引起的早期減速，屬良性，無需處置。

📖 PDF p.367 (book p.327)

4. After a spinal anesthetic for cesarean birth, which instruction is most important for the client?剖腹產接受脊髓麻醉後，下列哪項指導最重要？

Lying flat decreases the risk of postdural puncture headache.平躺可降低脊髓穿刺後頭痛的風險。

📖 PDF p.370 (book p.330)

5. A client at 0 station is to have an amniotomy. Which actions does the nurse plan? Select all that apply.站位0的產婦將接受人工破水。護理師應計畫哪些行動？（複選） (Select all that apply)（複選）

Amniotomy raises risk of cord prolapse and infection (so limit vaginal exams) and more variable decelerations are expected.人工破水增加臍帶脫垂與感染風險（故應減少陰道檢查），且預期變異減速增多。

6. Which Bishop score indicates the cervix is favorable for induction of labor?下列哪個Bishop評分代表子宮頸有利於引產？

A score of 8 or greater predicts successful induction.評分達8分以上預測引產成功。

7. A client in labor lying supine becomes pale, dizzy and tachycardic with a BP drop. What is the best action?仰臥待產的產婦臉色蒼白、頭暈、心搏過速且血壓下降。最佳處置為何？

This is supine hypotension; turning the client to the side relieves vena cava compression.此為仰臥低血壓症候群，側臥可解除下腔靜脈壓迫。
