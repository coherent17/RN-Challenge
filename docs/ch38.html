Pediatric renal and genitourinary disorders: UTI, glomerulonephritis, nephrotic syndrome, hemolytic-uremic syndrome, vesicoureteral reflux, enuresis, and congenital genital/urinary anomalies (cryptorchidism, hypospadias/epispadias, bladder exstrophy).兒童腎臟與泌尿生殖疾病：泌尿道感染、腎絲球腎炎、腎病症候群、溶血性尿毒症候群、膀胱輸尿管逆流、遺尿，以及先天性泌尿生殖異常（隱睪症、尿道下裂/上裂、膀胱外翻）。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Urinary tract infection泌尿道感染

  • Bacteria from skin or gut flora invade the urinary tract.皮膚或腸道菌叢細菌侵入泌尿道。
  • Uncircumcised infants have higher UTI risk than circumcised infants.未包皮環切的嬰兒UTI風險較高。
  • Teach front-to-back wiping; asymptomatic bacteriuria can occur, so screen and treat when infection is suspected.教導由前往後擦拭；可能有無症狀菌尿，懷疑感染時須篩檢並治療。

2Glomerulonephritis腎絲球腎炎

  • Immune (antigen-antibody) inflammatory injury of glomeruli, typically after group A beta-hemolytic strep: pharyngitis 1-2 weeks before, or skin infection 3-6 weeks before.腎絲球之免疫（抗原抗體）發炎損傷，多見於A群乙型溶血性鏈球菌感染後：咽炎後1-2週，或皮膚感染後3-6週。
  • Findings: periorbital/facial edema worse in the morning, decreased urine, cola-colored urine (hematuria), hypertension, proteinuria, elevated BUN/creatinine, elevated ASO titer.表現：晨間較明顯的眼周/臉部水腫、尿量減少、可樂色尿（血尿）、高血壓、蛋白尿、BUN/肌酸酐上升、ASO升高。
  • Complications: kidney failure, hypertensive encephalopathy, pulmonary edema, heart failure, seizures.併發症：腎衰竭、高血壓腦病變、肺水腫、心衰竭、癲癇。
  • Care: VS, I&O, urine characteristics, daily weight (same time, scale, clothing), limited activity, sodium (and potassium if oliguric) restriction, diuretics, antihypertensives, antibiotics for persistent strep, seizure precautions.照護：生命徵象、I&O、尿液性狀、每日體重（同時間、同磅秤、同衣著）、限制活動、限鈉（少尿時限鉀）、利尿劑、降壓藥、持續鏈球菌感染給抗生素、癲癇防護。
  • Daily weight is the most useful measure of fluid balance; teach parents to treat sore throat, URI and skin infections promptly.每日體重是評估體液平衡最有用的方法；教導家長及早處理喉嚨痛、上呼吸道與皮膚感染。

3Nephrotic syndrome腎病症候群

  • Classic tetrad: massive proteinuria, hypoalbuminemia, hyperlipidemia, edema.典型四聯：大量蛋白尿、低白蛋白血症、高血脂、水腫。
  • Findings: weight gain, periorbital edema worse in morning, leg/labial/scrotal edema, ascites, dark frothy urine, normal or low BP, lethargy, pallor.表現：體重增加、晨間眼周水腫、腿/陰唇/陰囊水腫、腹水、深色泡沫尿、血壓正常或偏低、倦怠、蒼白。
  • Goals: reduce urinary protein loss, reduce edema, prevent infection.目標：減少尿蛋白流失、減輕水腫、預防感染。
  • Treatment: corticosteroids started at diagnosis, immunosuppressants for relapse/non-response, diuretics, salt-poor albumin for severe edema; sodium restriction during massive edema.治療：確診即開始類固醇；復發/無反應加免疫抑制劑；利尿劑；嚴重水腫用低鹽白蛋白；大量水腫時限鈉。
  • Monitor urine specific gravity and protein; teach home urine protein testing, infection signs, avoiding infectious contacts.監測尿比重與蛋白；教導居家尿蛋白檢測、感染徵象、避免接觸感染者。

4Hemolytic-uremic syndrome (HUS)溶血性尿毒症候群

  • Acute kidney injury in infants and children 6 months-5 years, linked to bacterial toxins, chemicals and viruses.6個月至5歲嬰幼兒的急性腎損傷，與細菌毒素、化學物質、病毒有關。
  • Triad: hemolytic anemia, thrombocytopenia, kidney failure; also CNS signs, hemorrhagic findings, oliguria/anuria.三聯徵：溶血性貧血、血小板低下、腎衰竭；並有中樞神經症狀、出血表現、少尿/無尿。
  • Labs: high BUN/creatinine, low Hgb/Hct, high reticulocytes, proteinuria, hematuria, casts.檢驗：BUN/肌酸酐高、Hgb/Hct低、網狀紅血球高、蛋白尿、血尿、尿圓柱。
  • Care: strict fluid balance, fluid restriction if anuric, dialysis if worsening, infection prevention, nutrition, blood transfusion cautiously to avoid fluid overload.照護：嚴格體液平衡、無尿時限水、惡化時透析、預防感染、營養、謹慎輸血避免液體過量。
p.535 · book 481

5Vesicoureteral reflux膀胱輸尿管逆流

  • Urine flows backward from bladder to ureters/kidneys; primary type from faulty ureter-bladder valve, secondary from bladder outlet blockage.尿液由膀胱逆流至輸尿管/腎臟；原發性為輸尿管膀胱瓣膜缺陷，續發性為膀胱出口阻塞。
  • Risk: bladder exstrophy, family history, neurogenic bladder (e.g., spina bifida).危險因子：膀胱外翻、家族史、神經性膀胱（如脊柱裂）。
  • Diagnosis: ultrasound, voiding cystourethrogram; recurrent UTI is the common presentation.診斷：超音波、排尿性膀胱尿道攝影；常見表現為反覆UTI。
  • Treatment: prophylactic antibiotics, Deflux injection, or surgical repair; teach fluids, avoid juice/soda, warm towel for discomfort.治療：預防性抗生素、Deflux注射或手術修補；教導多喝水、避免果汁與汽水、溫毛巾緩解不適。

6Enuresis遺尿症

  • Diagnosed in children older than 5 who wet at least twice weekly for 3 months; the child cannot control it.5歲以上、每週至少2次且持續3個月的尿濕；孩子無法控制。
  • Primary: never dry; nocturnal enuresis is common and usually outgrown. Secondary: wetting after established continence; check for UTI, diabetes.原發性：從未乾爽；夜間遺尿常見，多會自行好轉。續發性：已控制後又發生；需排除UTI、糖尿病。
  • Care: urinalysis/culture, limit fluids at night, void before bed, reward systems, conditioning (alarms), desmopressin or anticholinergics.照護：尿液分析/培養、夜間限水、睡前排尿、獎勵制度、行為制約（警報器）、desmopressin或抗膽鹼藥。
p.536 · book 482

7Cryptorchidism隱睪症

  • One or both testes not descended into the scrotum; not palpable or easily guided down.單側或雙側睪丸未下降至陰囊；摸不到或無法輕易推入。
  • Observe for spontaneous descent in the first 6 months; surgical correction (orchiopexy) usually at 6-12 months.前6個月觀察是否自然下降；睪丸固定術通常於6-12個月進行。
  • Postop: monitor bleeding, infection, pain; teach home care and activity limits; offer counseling about fertility.術後：監測出血、感染、疼痛；教導居家照護與活動限制；提供生育力諮詢。
p.536 · book 482p.537 · book 483

8Epispadias and hypospadias尿道上裂與下裂

  • Epispadias: urethral opening on dorsal penis (often with bladder exstrophy). Hypospadias: opening on ventral side below glans.尿道上裂：開口在陰莖背側（常伴膀胱外翻）。尿道下裂：開口在龜頭下腹側。
  • Do not circumcise newborns; foreskin may be needed for repair. Surgery at 6-12 months, before toilet training.新生兒勿行包皮環切，包皮可能用於重建。手術於6-12個月，在如廁訓練前進行。
  • Postop: pressure dressing, stent/diversion, encourage fluids, monitor I&O, report no urine for 1 hour (kinked stent or sediment), anticholinergics for bladder spasms, antibiotics, no tub bath until stent removed.術後：加壓敷料、導管/支架、鼓勵飲水、監測I&O、1小時無尿須通報（支架扭曲或沉積物）、抗膽鹼藥治膀胱痙攣、抗生素、支架拔除前不盆浴。
p.537 · book 483

9Bladder exstrophy and STIs膀胱外翻與性傳染病

  • Bladder protrudes through a lower abdominal wall defect; epispadias and vesicoureteral reflux are associated; closure in first days of life.膀胱經下腹壁缺損外露；伴尿道上裂與膀胱輸尿管逆流；出生後數日內閉合。
  • Protect exposed mucosa with sterile nonadherent covering (e.g., plastic wrap) to keep it moist; never petroleum jelly or dry gauze.以無菌不沾黏敷料（如塑膠膜）覆蓋保持濕潤；不可用凡士林或乾紗布。
  • Monitor urine output, infection, renal labs; give antibiotics; support parents.監測尿量、感染、腎功能檢驗；給抗生素；支持家長。
  • STI screening for selected children/adolescents follows CDC guidelines.特定兒童與青少年依CDC指引進行STI篩檢。

5A newborn with bladder exstrophy is admitted. Which intervention protects the exposed bladder?膀胱外翻新生兒入院。何者可保護外露膀胱？
