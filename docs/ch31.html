Chapter 31: Hematological Problems第 31 章：血液問題

📖 Source: PDF p.446–453 · printed book p.399–405.e1來源：PDF 第 446–453 頁 · 書本印刷頁 399–405.e1

Pediatric blood disorders: sickle cell anemia, hemophilia, von Willebrand disease, beta-thalassemia major, iron-deficiency anemia and aplastic anemia. Key themes are perfusion, bleeding safety, hydration and transfusion complications.小兒血液疾病：鐮狀細胞貧血、血友病、類血友病（von Willebrand 病）、重型 β 型地中海型貧血、缺鐵性貧血與再生不良性貧血。重點為組織灌流、出血安全、水分補充與輸血併發症。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

Sickle cell anemia: pathophysiology鐮狀細胞貧血：病理 📖 PDF p.446 (book p.399)

  • Hemoglobinopathy: hemoglobin S replaces HbA; autosomal inheritance, higher risk with heterozygous parents or African American descent.血紅素病：HbS 取代 HbA；父母為異型合子或非裔美國人風險較高。
  • Screening Sickledex (finger stick, 3 minutes); a positive result needs Hgb electrophoresis to tell trait from disease.篩檢用 Sickledex（指尖血、3 分鐘）；陽性需血紅素電泳區分帶因者與疾病。
  • Low oxygen makes cells rigid and sickled, clumping and blocking capillaries; sickling is reversible with oxygen and hydration at first.缺氧使紅血球僵硬呈鐮狀並聚集阻塞微血管；早期補氧與補水可逆。
  • Triggers: fever, dehydration, hypoxia, infection, trauma, emotional or physical stress.誘因：發燒、脫水、缺氧、感染、外傷、情緒或身體壓力。

Sickle cell crises鐮狀細胞危象 📖 PDF p.447 (book p.400)

  • Vaso-occlusive: ischemia and infarction; fever, painful swelling of hands/feet/joints, abdominal pain.血管阻塞型：缺血梗塞；發燒、手足關節疼痛腫脹、腹痛。
  • Splenic sequestration: blood pools in spleen; profound anemia, hypovolemia, shock.脾臟滯留型：血液積聚於脾臟；嚴重貧血、低血容、休克。
  • Hyperhemolytic: rapid RBC destruction; anemia, jaundice, reticulocytosis.溶血型：紅血球快速破壞；貧血、黃疸、網狀紅血球增多。
  • Aplastic: decreased production (viral infection, folic acid depletion); profound anemia and pallor.再生不良型：造血減少（病毒感染、葉酸缺乏）；嚴重貧血蒼白。
  • Complications seen in Fig 31.1: dactylitis, stroke, acute chest syndrome, priapism, gallstones, avascular necrosis, autosplenectomy.圖 31.1 併發症：指趾炎、中風、急性胸症候群、陰莖異常勃起、膽結石、缺血性壞死、自體脾切除。

Sickle cell care鐮狀細胞照護 📖 PDF p.446 (book p.399), PDF p.447 (book p.400)

  • Hydration orally and IV (1.5-2 times maintenance); without it pain is not controlled.口服與靜脈補液（維持量 1.5–2 倍）；水分不足疼痛無法控制。
  • Oxygen, transfusions/exchange transfusion, around-the-clock analgesics; avoid meperidine (normeperidine seizures).氧氣、輸血/換血、定時止痛；避免 meperidine（代謝物 normeperidine 致癲癇）。
  • Position extremities extended, HOB no more than 30 degrees, do not raise knee gatch, avoid joint strain; bed rest.四肢伸直擺位，床頭不超過 30 度，勿抬膝部，避免關節受壓；臥床休息。
  • High-calorie, high-protein diet with folic acid; hydroxyurea reduces sickling and vaso-occlusive events.高熱量高蛋白飲食加葉酸；hydroxyurea 減少鐮狀化與血管阻塞事件。
  • Antibiotics for infection; pneumococcal, meningococcal and annual influenza vaccines (functional asplenia); splenectomy for recurrent sequestration.抗生素治療感染；接種肺炎鏈球菌、腦膜炎雙球菌與每年流感疫苗（功能性無脾）；反覆脾臟滯留可能切脾。
  • Monitor for shock, worsening anemia, poor perfusion; teach early crisis signs and genetic counseling.監測休克、貧血惡化、灌流不良；教導早期危象徵象與遺傳諮詢。

Hemophilia血友病 📖 PDF p.447 (book p.400), PDF p.448 (book p.401)

  • Missing clotting factor: factor VIII (hemophilia A) or IX (B, Christmas disease); X-linked recessive, carrier mothers pass to sons.凝血因子缺乏：第 VIII 因子（A 型）或第 IX 因子（B 型）；X 染色體隱性，帶因母親傳給兒子。
  • Signs: bleeding after trauma/surgery/circumcision, epistaxis, easy bruising, hemarthrosis (joint pain, swelling, limited motion).表現：外傷/手術/割包皮後出血、流鼻血、易瘀青、關節積血（疼痛腫脹活動受限）。
  • Platelet function tests normal; PTT prolonged.血小板功能檢查正常；PTT 延長。
  • Treat with replacement factor (recombinant or pooled plasma); DDAVP raises factor VIII in mild disease.以替代凝血因子治療（重組或血漿來源）；DDAVP 可提高輕度患者因子 VIII。
  • Joint bleed: immobilize, elevate, ice; superficial bleed: pressure 15 minutes; watch neuro status for intracranial hemorrhage and hematuria.關節出血：固定、抬高、冰敷；表淺出血：加壓 15 分鐘；監測神經狀態（顱內出血）與血尿。
  • Avoid contact sports; swimming is safe; helmets and pads; MedicAlert bracelet.避免接觸性運動；游泳安全；戴頭盔護具；配戴醫療警示手環。

Von Willebrand disease類血友病（von Willebrand 病） 📖 PDF p.448 (book p.401)

  • Hereditary deficiency/defect of von Willebrand factor, which helps platelets adhere and carries factor VIII.遺傳性 von Willebrand 因子缺乏或缺陷；此因子協助血小板黏附並攜帶因子 VIII。
  • Mucous membrane bleeding: epistaxis, gum bleeding, easy bruising, heavy menses.黏膜出血：流鼻血、牙齦出血、易瘀青、經血過多。
  • Management similar to hemophilia, including clotting factors; emotional support; MedicAlert.處置類似血友病，包括補充凝血因子；情緒支持；醫療警示手環。

Beta-thalassemia major重型 β 型地中海型貧血 📖 PDF p.448 (book p.401), PDF p.449 (book p.402)

  • Autosomal recessive, both parents carriers; common in Mediterranean descent; also called Cooley anemia.體染色體隱性，雙親皆為帶因者；地中海後裔多見；又稱 Cooley 貧血。
  • Findings: frontal bossing, maxillary prominence, wide-set eyes, greenish-yellow skin, hepatosplenomegaly, severe microcytic hypochromic anemia.表現：額頭突出、上顎突出、眼距寬、膚色黃綠、肝脾腫大、嚴重小球低色素貧血。
  • Lifelong transfusions; bone marrow transplant possible; splenectomy for severe splenomegaly.終身輸血；可考慮骨髓移植；嚴重脾腫大可切脾。
  • Iron overload (hemosiderosis): chelation with deferasirox or deferoxamine.鐵過量（含鐵血黃素沉積）：以 deferasirox 或 deferoxamine 排鐵。
  • After splenectomy report infection (sepsis risk); give pneumococcal, meningococcal, annual flu vaccines; genetic counseling.切脾後有感染應回報（敗血症風險）；接種肺炎鏈球菌、腦膜炎雙球菌與每年流感疫苗；遺傳諮詢。

Iron-deficiency and aplastic anemia缺鐵性與再生不良性貧血 📖 PDF p.449 (book p.402)

  • Iron stores depleted; RBCs microcytic and hypochromic; Hgb low, RBC count low.鐵儲存耗盡；紅血球小球低色素；血紅素與紅血球數降低。
  • Risk: cow's milk over 24 oz/day and poor iron-rich diet.風險：每日牛奶超過 24 盎司與富鐵食物攝取不足。
  • Give iron between meals, not with milk; liquid iron through a straw or dropper at back of mouth; brush teeth afterward (stains).鐵劑於兩餐之間服用，不與牛奶併服；液態鐵用吸管或滴管置於口腔後方；服後刷牙（會染齒）。
  • Aplastic anemia: marrow failure with pancytopenia (low RBC, WBC, platelets).再生不良性貧血：骨髓衰竭致全血球減少（紅血球、白血球、血小板皆低）。

⭐ Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Sickle crisis: hydrate, oxygenate, treat pain; never restrict fluids; avoid meperidine.鐮狀危象：補水、給氧、止痛；勿限水；避免 meperidine。 📖 PDF p.446 (book p.399), PDF p.447 (book p.400), PDF p.451 (book p.404)
  2. Hemophilia: PTT prolonged, platelets/Hgb/Hct normal.血友病：PTT 延長，血小板、Hgb、Hct 正常。 📖 PDF p.448 (book p.401), PDF p.450 (book p.403)
  3. Hemophilia A joint/bleed after injury: give IV factor VIII; ice, elevate, immobilize.血友病 A 受傷：靜脈給予第 VIII 因子；冰敷、抬高、固定。 📖 PDF p.448 (book p.401), PDF p.451 (book p.404)
  4. Pressure for at least 15 minutes on superficial bleeding in hemophilia.血友病表淺出血至少加壓 15 分鐘。 📖 PDF p.448 (book p.401)
  5. Safe sport for hemophilia: swimming; avoid contact sports like soccer, football, hockey.血友病安全運動：游泳；避免足球等接觸運動。 📖 PDF p.448 (book p.401), PDF p.450 (book p.403)
  6. Sickle crisis position: extremities extended, HOB max 30 degrees, no knee gatch elevation.鐮狀危象擺位：四肢伸直，床頭最多 30 度，勿抬膝。 📖 PDF p.446 (book p.399)
  7. Deferoxamine (defer- = remove iron) treats iron overload from thalassemia transfusions.Deferoxamine（defer＝移除鐵）用於地中海型貧血輸血後鐵過量。 📖 PDF p.449 (book p.402), PDF p.451 (book p.404)
  8. Splenic sequestration = profound anemia, hypovolemia, shock: emergency.脾臟滯留＝嚴重貧血、低血容、休克：緊急。 📖 PDF p.447 (book p.400)
  9. Vaccinate asplenic or splenectomized children: pneumococcal, meningococcal, annual flu.無脾或切脾兒童須接種肺炎鏈球菌、腦膜炎雙球菌、年度流感疫苗。 📖 PDF p.447 (book p.400), PDF p.449 (book p.402)
  10. Oral liquid iron: straw, between meals, no milk, brush teeth.口服液態鐵：吸管、兩餐間、不配牛奶、服後刷牙。 📖 PDF p.449 (book p.402), PDF p.451 (book p.404)
  11. Sickling triggers: fever, dehydration, hypoxia, stress, infection. Fluid overload is not a trigger.鐮狀化誘因：發燒、脫水、缺氧、壓力、感染；液體過量不是誘因。 📖 PDF p.446 (book p.399), PDF p.451 (book p.404)
  12. Von Willebrand: mucosal bleeding, heavy menses; treat like hemophilia.von Willebrand 病：黏膜出血、月經過多；處置如血友病。 📖 PDF p.448 (book p.401)

🧠 Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Sickle crisis types (V-S-H-A): Vaso-occlusive, Sequestration, Hyperhemolytic, Aplastic.鐮狀危象四型（V-S-H-A）：血管阻塞、脾滯留、溶血、再生不良。 📖 PDF p.447 (book p.400)
  • Defer- drugs remove iron (deferoxamine, deferasirox).Defer- 開頭藥物移除鐵（deferoxamine、deferasirox）。 📖 PDF p.449 (book p.402)

📝 Practice questions練習題

1. A child with hemophilia A has fallen and bumped a knee. What should the nurse prepare to give?血友病 A 孩童跌倒撞到膝蓋，護理師應準備何者？

Hemophilia A is factor VIII deficiency; replace the factor and apply ice, elevation, immobilization.血友病 A 缺乏第 VIII 因子，須補充並冰敷、抬高、固定。

2. Which lab result is most likely abnormal in hemophilia?血友病最可能異常的檢驗為何？

Platelet function is normal; PTT is prolonged.血小板功能正常；PTT 延長。

3. Which prescriptions would the nurse question for a child in vaso-occlusive crisis? Select all that apply.血管阻塞危象孩童，護理師應質疑哪些醫囑？（多選） (Select all that apply)（複選）

Fluids are essential; meperidine causes normeperidine-induced seizures.補水是治療關鍵；meperidine 的代謝物會誘發癲癇。

📖 PDF p.447 (book p.400), PDF p.452 (book p.405)

4. Which sport would the nurse recommend to a 10-year-old with hemophilia?護理師會建議 10 歲血友病孩童從事何種運動？

Avoid contact and collision sports; swimming is low risk.避免接觸碰撞運動；游泳風險低。

5. A child with beta-thalassemia major receives frequent transfusions. Which drug is anticipated for iron overload?重型 β 地中海型貧血孩童常輸血，預期使用何藥處理鐵過量？

Chelation therapy with deferoxamine or deferasirox removes iron.以 deferoxamine 或 deferasirox 進行排鐵治療。

6. Which teaching about liquid oral iron is correct?關於口服液態鐵劑，何項指導正確？

Iron stains teeth and absorbs better without food or milk.鐵劑會染齒，且空腹無乳製品吸收較佳。

7. Select all that apply: characteristics of von Willebrand disease.von Willebrand 病的特徵（多選）。 (Select all that apply)（複選）

It is hereditary mucosal bleeding disorder; creatinine is not involved.為遺傳性黏膜出血疾病，與肌酸酐無關。

8. Parents of a child with sickle cell disease list crisis triggers. Which indicates need for more teaching?鐮狀細胞病孩童家長列舉危象誘因，何項顯示需再指導？

Dehydration, not fluid overload, triggers crises.脫水而非液體過量才會誘發危象。
