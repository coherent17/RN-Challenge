1Anatomy and physiology解剖與生理

  • Each cerebral hemisphere senses and controls the opposite side of the body.每側大腦半球感覺並控制身體對側。
  • Lobes: frontal (Broca's speech, judgment, memory), parietal (touch, pain, spatial perception), temporal (hearing, Wernicke's comprehension), occipital (vision); limbic system governs emotion and memory.額葉（Broca語言區、判斷、記憶）、頂葉（觸痛覺、空間感）、顳葉（聽覺、Wernicke理解區）、枕葉（視覺）；邊緣系統負責情緒與記憶。
  • Hypothalamus regulates temperature, appetite, sleep, fluid balance; pons holds respiratory centers; medulla controls heart rate, breathing, vomiting, swallowing; cerebellum coordinates movement, posture and balance.下視丘調節體溫、食慾、睡眠、體液；橋腦有呼吸中樞；延腦控制心跳、呼吸、嘔吐、吞嚥；小腦協調動作、姿勢與平衡。
  • CSF: pressure 6-25 cm H2O, volume 125-150 mL, about 500 mL produced daily; circle of Willis supplies the brain via carotid and vertebral arteries.腦脊髓液：壓力6-25 cm H2O、總量125-150 mL、每日約產生500 mL；威利氏環由頸動脈與椎動脈供血。
  • Sympathetic (adrenergic, norepinephrine) raises HR/BP and dilates pupils; parasympathetic (cholinergic, acetylcholine) slows HR and increases secretions.交感（腎上腺素性、正腎上腺素）使心跳血壓上升、瞳孔放大；副交感（膽鹼性、乙醯膽鹼）使心跳減慢、分泌增加。
  • Neurons do not regenerate after the neonatal period; peripheral axon repair is slow and needs an intact neurolemma.神經元出生後不再再生；周邊軸突修復緩慢且需神經鞘完整。

2Diagnostic tests診斷檢查

  • CT: check iodine/shellfish allergy and renal function; consider holding metformin with iodinated contrast; encourage fluids afterward.電腦斷層：評估碘/貝類過敏與腎功能；使用含碘顯影劑時考慮暫停metformin；檢查後鼓勵補充水分。
  • MRI: remove all metal; no pacemaker, implanted defibrillator or certain implants; saline lock instead of IV pump; watch for pulse oximeter burns.核磁共振：移除所有金屬；有心律調節器、植入式去顫器等者不可做；用生理食鹽水留置針取代點滴幫浦；注意血氧夾灼傷。
  • Lumbar puncture (L3-L4): contraindicated with increased ICP (herniation risk); lateral knee-chest position; afterward lie flat as prescribed, increase fluids, monitor for headache and CSF leak.腰椎穿刺（L3-L4）：顱內壓上升者禁忌（腦疝風險）；側臥屈膝抱胸；術後依醫囑平躺、增加水分、監測頭痛與腦脊髓液滲漏。
  • Cerebral angiography: assess allergies and renal function, NPO 4-6 h, mark pulses; afterward bed rest ~12 h, check neurovascular status of the limb, and watch for neck swelling or trouble swallowing.腦血管攝影：評估過敏與腎功能、禁食4-6小時、標記脈搏；術後臥床約12小時、監測肢體神經血管狀態，注意頸部腫脹或吞嚥困難。
  • EEG: wash hair, withhold caffeine (and possibly antiseizure drugs) 24-48 h as prescribed; wash hair afterward.腦電圖：先洗髮，依醫囑24-48小時前避免咖啡因（可能含抗癲癇藥）；檢查後洗髮。
  • Caloric testing tests CN VIII/brainstem; normal response is vertigo and nystagmus within 20-30 seconds; absent or disconjugate movement suggests brainstem damage.冷熱水試驗評估第八對腦神經/腦幹；正常為20-30秒內出現眩暈與眼震；無反應或雙眼不協調提示腦幹損傷。

3Neurological assessment and GCS神經評估與昏迷指數

  • Level of consciousness is the earliest and most sensitive indicator of neurological change.意識程度是神經狀態最早也最敏感的指標。
  • Pupils: unilateral dilation = CN III compression; midposition fixed = midbrain; pinpoint = pons or opioids; bilateral fixed dilated = ominous.瞳孔：單側放大＝第三對腦神經受壓；中位固定＝中腦；針尖樣＝橋腦或鴉片類；雙側放大固定＝嚴重不良徵兆。
  • Decorticate (flexor) posturing = cortex dysfunction; decerebrate (extensor) = brainstem lesion; flaccid = no response.去皮質（屈曲）姿勢＝大腦皮質功能不良；去大腦（伸直）姿勢＝腦幹病灶；鬆弛＝無反應。
  • Respiratory patterns: Cheyne-Stokes (hemispheres/metabolic), neurogenic hyperventilation (midbrain/pons), apneustic (pons), cluster, ataxic (medulla).呼吸型態：陳施氏（大腦/代謝）、神經性過度換氣（中腦/橋腦）、長吸式（橋腦）、群集式、運動失調式（延腦）。
  • Babinski is abnormal after age 2; loss of corneal reflex = CN V; loss of gag = CN IX and X.兩歲以後巴賓斯基反射陽性為異常；角膜反射消失＝第五對腦神經；嘔吐反射消失＝第九、十對。
  • Meningeal irritation: nuchal rigidity, Kernig's and Brudzinski's signs, photophobia, severe headache, fever.腦膜刺激：頸項僵硬、Kernig氏與Brudzinski氏徵象、畏光、劇烈頭痛、發燒。
  • GCS ranges 3-15 (eye 1-4, verbal 1-5, motor 1-6); below 8 indicates coma.GCS範圍3-15分（睜眼1-4、語言1-5、運動1-6）；低於8分表示昏迷。

4Unconscious client and increased ICP昏迷病人與顱內壓上升

  • Care of the unconscious client: airway and ventilation first, semi-Fowler's, turn every 2 h, NPO until swallow/gag returns, eye care, ROM, footboard, assume the client can hear.昏迷照護：首重呼吸道與通氣、半坐臥、每2小時翻身、吞嚥/嘔吐反射恢復前禁食、眼部護理、關節活動、腳板防垂足、假設病人聽得見。
  • Early sign of rising ICP: decreased LOC; later: headache, vomiting, pupil changes, rising systolic BP with widening pulse pressure, bradycardia, irregular respirations (Cushing's triad), fever, posturing, seizures.ICP上升早期：意識下降；後期：頭痛、嘔吐、瞳孔變化、收縮壓升高與脈搏壓增寬、心搏過緩、呼吸不規則（庫欣三徵）、發燒、姿勢異常、癲癇。
  • Interventions: HOB 30-40 degrees, head midline, avoid hip/neck flexion, Trendelenburg and Valsalva; limit fluids (~1200 mL/day), quiet environment, prevent shivering and hypoxia; avoid morphine.處置：床頭抬高30-40度、頭部正中、避免髖/頸屈曲、頭低腳高位與Valsalva；限水（約1200 mL/日）、安靜環境、預防寒顫與缺氧；避免嗎啡。
  • Mechanical ventilation with PaCO2 30-35 mm Hg causes cerebral vasoconstriction and lowers ICP.機械通氣維持PaCO2 30-35 mm Hg可使腦血管收縮、降低顱內壓。
  • Medications: antiseizure, antipyretics/muscle relaxants, BP control (notify if SBP <100 or >150), corticosteroids (taper slowly), IV fluids by pump, osmotic diuretics (monitor renal function).藥物：抗癲癇藥、退燒藥/肌肉鬆弛劑、血壓控制（收縮壓<100或>150通知醫師）、類固醇（需緩慢減量）、點滴使用幫浦、滲透性利尿劑（監測腎功能）。
  • Hyperthermia (>105 F / 40.6 C) raises cerebral metabolism; cool with fluids, baths, fans or cooling blanket and prevent shivering. VP shunt diverts CSF to the peritoneum; watch for shunt failure and infection.高熱（>105°F/40.6°C）增加腦代謝；以輸液、冷水浴、風扇或降溫毯降溫並防寒顫。腦室腹腔分流將腦脊髓液導入腹腔；注意分流失效與感染。

5Head injury and craniotomy頭部外傷與開顱術

  • Epidural hematoma: arterial, rapid, lucid interval then coma; surgical emergency. Subdural: venous, slower. Concussion, contusion and skull fractures are other injury types.硬腦膜外血腫：動脈出血、進展快、有清醒期後昏迷，為外科急症。硬腦膜下血腫：靜脈出血、較慢。另有腦震盪、腦挫傷與顱骨骨折。
  • CSF leak from nose/ear: halo (ring) sign and glucose-positive; do not suction the nose, clean the ear or let the client blow the nose; apply loose dry sterile dressing and notify the PHCP.鼻/耳漏出腦脊髓液：出現暈環徵並葡萄糖陽性；勿經鼻抽吸、清潔耳道或擤鼻；以鬆的乾燥無菌敷料覆蓋並通知醫師。
  • Head injury care: patent airway, HOB elevated, avoid neck flexion and coughing, seizure precautions, monitor neuro status and for infection; give opioids cautiously.頭部外傷照護：維持呼吸道、抬高床頭、避免頸屈曲與咳嗽、癲癇預防、監測神經狀態與感染；鴉片類藥物須謹慎。
  • Craniotomy complications: increased ICP, hemorrhage, CSF obstruction, DI, SIADH, infection. Monitor neuro status every 30-60 min, drain output (report >30-50 mL per shift), fluid restriction ~1500 mL/day.開顱術併發症：ICP上升、出血、腦脊髓液阻塞、尿崩症、抗利尿激素分泌不當症候群、感染。每30-60分鐘監測神經狀態、引流量（每班>30-50 mL需通報）、限水約1500 mL/日。
  • Positioning after surgery follows prescription: bone flap removal - turn to the nonoperative side; posterior fossa - side-lying; infratentorial - flat or HOB 30-45 per order; supratentorial - HOB 30 degrees.術後擺位依醫囑：骨瓣移除－翻向非手術側；後顱窩－側臥；幕下手術－平躺或依醫囑抬高30-45度；幕上手術－床頭抬高30度。
  • Periorbital edema and ecchymosis may be expected after craniotomy; use ice packs as prescribed.開顱術後可預期眼周水腫與瘀青；依醫囑冰敷。

6Cerebral aneurysm and seizures腦動脈瘤與癲癇

  • Aneurysm precautions: bed rest, semi-Fowler's or side-lying, dark quiet room, limited visitors, avoid Valsalva (stool softeners), no rectal temperatures, prevent hypertension, DVT prophylaxis, antiseizure drugs.動脈瘤預防措施：臥床休息、半坐臥或側臥、昏暗安靜、限制訪客、避免Valsalva（給軟便劑）、勿測肛溫、預防高血壓、預防深部靜脈栓塞、抗癲癇藥。
  • Seizure types: tonic-clonic (tonic 10-20 s then clonic ~30 s), absence (brief staring, children), myoclonic, atonic, simple partial (conscious), complex partial (temporal lobe, altered awareness).癲癇類型：強直-陣攣（強直10-20秒後陣攣約30秒）、失神（短暫凝視、常見於兒童）、肌躁、失張力、單純局部（意識清楚）、複雜局部（顳葉、意識改變）。
  • During a seizure: note time, protect the head, lower to the floor, side-lying, loosen clothing, suction/oxygen ready, stay with client; do NOT restrain or place anything in the mouth.發作時：記錄時間、保護頭部、移至地板、側臥、鬆開衣物、備好抽吸/氧氣、陪伴；勿約束或放入口中物品。
  • Status epilepticus: rapid succession of seizures without recovery of consciousness; an emergency with risk of brain damage.癲癇重積狀態：連續發作而意識未恢復；屬急症，可致腦損傷。
  • Teaching: lifelong medication with level monitoring, avoid alcohol, stress, fatigue and strobe lights, wear a medical ID bracelet.衛教：終生服藥並監測血中濃度、避免酒精、壓力、疲勞與閃光、配戴醫療警示手環。

7Stroke (brain attack)中風（腦中風）

  • Ischemic (thrombotic, embolic) vs hemorrhagic; type must be confirmed by CT because symptoms overlap and treatment differs. TIA is a warning sign.缺血性（血栓、栓塞）與出血性；症狀相似但治療不同，需以CT確認類型。短暫性腦缺血發作為警訊。
  • Check fingerstick glucose to rule out hypoglycemia; airway is always the priority; deficits appear on the side opposite the lesion.測指尖血糖排除低血糖；呼吸道永遠優先；缺損出現在病灶對側。
  • Left-brain stroke: right hemiplegia, aphasia, slow cautious, aware of deficits. Right-brain stroke: left hemiplegia, neglect, impulsive, poor judgment, denies problems.左腦中風：右側偏癱、失語、緩慢謹慎、知道自己缺損。右腦中風：左側偏癱、忽略症、衝動、判斷力差、否認問題。
  • Expressive aphasia (Broca's) - understands but cannot speak; receptive (Wernicke's) - cannot understand. Use one-step directions, picture boards, allow time.表達性失語（Broca）－聽得懂但說不出；接受性失語（Wernicke）－聽不懂。使用單一步驟指令、圖卡、給予時間。
  • Acute phase: keep BP around 150/100 for perfusion, HOB 15-30 degrees, side-lying, suction orally and not longer than 10 seconds, ICP risk highest in first 72 hours.急性期：血壓維持約150/100以保持腦灌流、床頭15-30度、側臥、經口抽吸且不超過10秒、前72小時為ICP高風險期。
  • Post-acute: position 2 h on unaffected side and 20 min on affected side, passive ROM, swallow evaluation, thickened fluids, chin-down, food on the unaffected side.急性期後：健側臥2小時、患側臥20分鐘、被動關節活動、吞嚥評估、增稠液體、下巴微收、食物放健側。
  • Homonymous hemianopsia and neglect: approach from the unaffected side, place items in the intact visual field, teach head turning to scan.同側偏盲與忽略症：從健側接近、物品放在可視野、教導轉頭掃視。

8MS, myasthenia gravis, Parkinson's多發性硬化症、重症肌無力、帕金森氏症

  • MS: demyelination, remissions and exacerbations; fatigue, ataxia, spasticity, diplopia, bladder/bowel problems; avoid heat, fatigue, stress and infection; IV glucocorticoids for flare-ups.多發性硬化症：去髓鞘、緩解與惡化交替；疲倦、運動失調、痙攣、複視、膀胱/腸道問題；避免熱、疲勞、壓力與感染；急性發作用靜脈類固醇。
  • Myasthenia gravis: weakness worse with activity, ptosis, diplopia, dysphagia; respiratory failure risk; plan activity at peak strength; anticholinesterase drugs on time.重症肌無力：活動後無力加重、眼瞼下垂、複視、吞嚥困難；有呼吸衰竭風險；在肌力高峰安排活動；按時給予抗膽鹼酯酶藥。
  • Myasthenic crisis = too little medication (strength improves with edrophonium); cholinergic crisis = too much (cramps, diarrhea, miosis, bradycardia, secretions; edrophonium worsens weakness); antidote atropine.肌無力危象＝藥量不足（給edrophonium會改善）；膽鹼性危象＝藥量過多（腹絞痛、腹瀉、瞳孔縮小、心搏過緩、分泌物多；edrophonium使無力加重）；解毒劑為atropine。
  • Edrophonium test: keep atropine and emergency equipment available (risk of ventricular fibrillation/arrest).Edrophonium試驗：備妥atropine與急救設備（有心室顫動/心跳停止風險）。
  • Parkinson's: dopamine depletion; bradykinesia, resting pill-rolling tremor, rigidity, masklike face, shuffling gait, drooling.帕金森氏症：多巴胺缺乏；動作遲緩、靜止性搓丸樣顫抖、僵硬、面具臉、拖曳步態、流口水。
  • Parkinson's care: high-calorie, high-protein, high-fiber soft diet, 2000 mL fluids, low-heeled shoes, lift feet, avoid rushing; avoid vitamin B6-rich foods with levodopa and avoid MAO inhibitors.帕金森照護：高熱量高蛋白高纖軟食、水分2000 mL、低跟鞋、抬腳走路、勿催促；使用levodopa時避免高維生素B6食物，避免MAO抑制劑。

9Cranial nerve and neuromuscular disorders腦神經與神經肌肉疾病

  • Trigeminal neuralgia (CN V): severe sharp facial pain triggered by cold, washing, chewing; avoid temperature extremes, chew on unaffected side; surgical options include microvascular decompression and rhizotomy.三叉神經痛（第五對）：因冷、洗臉、咀嚼誘發的劇烈刺痛；避免冷熱、用健側咀嚼；手術包括微血管減壓與神經根切斷術。
  • Bell's palsy (CN VII): one-sided facial paralysis, loss of taste, cannot close eyelid; protect the eye from dryness, facial exercises, chew on the unaffected side; usually recovers in weeks.貝爾氏麻痺（第七對）：單側顏面麻痺、味覺喪失、眼瞼無法閉合；保護眼睛防乾燥、臉部運動、用健側咀嚼；通常數週內恢復。
  • Guillain-Barre: follows infection by days to weeks; ascending symmetric weakness, paresthesias, CSF protein elevated; main danger is respiratory failure and dysrhythmias; recovery is slow.格林-巴利症候群：感染後數日至數週發生；上行性對稱無力、感覺異常、腦脊髓液蛋白升高；主要危險為呼吸衰竭與心律不整；恢復緩慢。
  • ALS: progressive motor neuron disease, sensory, autonomic and mental status spared; weakness, atrophy, fasciculations, dysphagia, dysarthria; supportive care and advance directives.ALS：進行性運動神經元疾病，感覺、自律神經與心智不受影響；無力、萎縮、肌束顫動、吞嚥與構音困難；支持性照護與預立醫囑。

10CNS infections中樞神經感染

  • Encephalitis: brain inflammation, usually viral (HSV-1, arboviruses); fever, altered LOC, signs of ICP; acyclovir for herpes encephalitis; HOB 30-45 degrees.腦炎：腦實質發炎，多為病毒（HSV-1、蟲媒病毒）；發燒、意識改變、ICP徵象；疱疹性腦炎用acyclovir；床頭抬高30-45度。
  • West Nile virus: mosquito-borne, symptoms 3-14 days after bite, many asymptomatic; care is supportive; prevent with DEET, long sleeves, eliminating standing water.西尼羅病毒：蚊媒傳播、被叮咬後3-14天發病、許多人無症狀；以支持性照護；以DEET、長袖與清除積水預防。
  • Meningitis: fever, nuchal rigidity, photophobia, positive Kernig and Brudzinski; CSF cloudy with high protein and WBC and low glucose; meningococcal rash is red macular.腦膜炎：發燒、頸僵硬、畏光、Kernig與Brudzinski陽性；腦脊髓液混濁、蛋白與白血球升高、葡萄糖降低；腦膜炎雙球菌有紅色斑疹。
  • Meningitis care: droplet/isolation precautions for bacterial types, seizure precautions, quiet dim room, HOB 30 degrees, avoid neck/hip flexion, antibiotics and analgesics.腦膜炎照護：細菌性採飛沫/隔離預防、癲癇預防、安靜昏暗環境、床頭30度、避免頸/髖過度屈曲、給予抗生素與止痛藥。

Anatomy and physiology解剖與生理
  • Each cerebral hemisphere senses and controls the opposite side of the body.每側大腦半球感覺並控制身體對側。
  • Lobes: frontal (Broca's speech, judgment, memory), parietal (touch, pain, spatial perception), temporal (hearing, Wernicke's comprehension), occipital (vision); limbic system governs emotion and memory.額葉（Broca語言區、判斷、記憶）、頂葉（觸痛覺、空間感）、顳葉（聽覺、Wernicke理解區）、枕葉（視覺）；邊緣系統負責情緒與記憶。
  • Hypothalamus regulates temperature, appetite, sleep, fluid balance; pons holds respiratory centers; medulla controls heart rate, breathing, vomiting, swallowing; cerebellum coordinates movement, posture and balance.下視丘調節體溫、食慾、睡眠、體液；橋腦有呼吸中樞；延腦控制心跳、呼吸、嘔吐、吞嚥；小腦協調動作、姿勢與平衡。
  • CSF: pressure 6-25 cm H2O, volume 125-150 mL, about 500 mL produced daily; circle of Willis supplies the brain via carotid and vertebral arteries.腦脊髓液：壓力6-25 cm H2O、總量125-150 mL、每日約產生500 mL；威利氏環由頸動脈與椎動脈供血。
  • Sympathetic (adrenergic, norepinephrine) raises HR/BP and dilates pupils; parasympathetic (cholinergic, acetylcholine) slows HR and increases secretions.交感（腎上腺素性、正腎上腺素）使心跳血壓上升、瞳孔放大；副交感（膽鹼性、乙醯膽鹼）使心跳減慢、分泌增加。
  • Neurons do not regenerate after the neonatal period; peripheral axon repair is slow and needs an intact neurolemma.神經元出生後不再再生；周邊軸突修復緩慢且需神經鞘完整。

Diagnostic tests診斷檢查
  • CT: check iodine/shellfish allergy and renal function; consider holding metformin with iodinated contrast; encourage fluids afterward.電腦斷層：評估碘/貝類過敏與腎功能；使用含碘顯影劑時考慮暫停metformin；檢查後鼓勵補充水分。
  • MRI: remove all metal; no pacemaker, implanted defibrillator or certain implants; saline lock instead of IV pump; watch for pulse oximeter burns.核磁共振：移除所有金屬；有心律調節器、植入式去顫器等者不可做；用生理食鹽水留置針取代點滴幫浦；注意血氧夾灼傷。

Neurological assessment and GCS神經評估與昏迷指數
  • Level of consciousness is the earliest and most sensitive indicator of neurological change.意識程度是神經狀態最早也最敏感的指標。
  • Pupils: unilateral dilation = CN III compression; midposition fixed = midbrain; pinpoint = pons or opioids; bilateral fixed dilated = ominous.瞳孔：單側放大＝第三對腦神經受壓；中位固定＝中腦；針尖樣＝橋腦或鴉片類；雙側放大固定＝嚴重不良徵兆。
  • Decorticate (flexor) posturing = cortex dysfunction; decerebrate (extensor) = brainstem lesion; flaccid = no response.去皮質（屈曲）姿勢＝大腦皮質功能不良；去大腦（伸直）姿勢＝腦幹病灶；鬆弛＝無反應。
  • Respiratory patterns: Cheyne-Stokes (hemispheres/metabolic), neurogenic hyperventilation (midbrain/pons), apneustic (pons), cluster, ataxic (medulla).呼吸型態：陳施氏（大腦/代謝）、神經性過度換氣（中腦/橋腦）、長吸式（橋腦）、群集式、運動失調式（延腦）。
  • Babinski is abnormal after age 2; loss of corneal reflex = CN V; loss of gag = CN IX and X.兩歲以後巴賓斯基反射陽性為異常；角膜反射消失＝第五對腦神經；嘔吐反射消失＝第九、十對。
  • Meningeal irritation: nuchal rigidity, Kernig's and Brudzinski's signs, photophobia, severe headache, fever.腦膜刺激：頸項僵硬、Kernig氏與Brudzinski氏徵象、畏光、劇烈頭痛、發燒。
  • GCS ranges 3-15 (eye 1-4, verbal 1-5, motor 1-6); below 8 indicates coma.GCS範圍3-15分（睜眼1-4、語言1-5、運動1-6）；低於8分表示昏迷。

Unconscious client and increased ICP昏迷病人與顱內壓上升
  • Care of the unconscious client: airway and ventilation first, semi-Fowler's, turn every 2 h, NPO until swallow/gag returns, eye care, ROM, footboard, assume the client can hear.昏迷照護：首重呼吸道與通氣、半坐臥、每2小時翻身、吞嚥/嘔吐反射恢復前禁食、眼部護理、關節活動、腳板防垂足、假設病人聽得見。
  • Early sign of rising ICP: decreased LOC; later: headache, vomiting, pupil changes, rising systolic BP with widening pulse pressure, bradycardia, irregular respirations (Cushing's triad), fever, posturing, seizures.ICP上升早期：意識下降；後期：頭痛、嘔吐、瞳孔變化、收縮壓升高與脈搏壓增寬、心搏過緩、呼吸不規則（庫欣三徵）、發燒、姿勢異常、癲癇。
  • Interventions: HOB 30-40 degrees, head midline, avoid hip/neck flexion, Trendelenburg and Valsalva; limit fluids (~1200 mL/day), quiet environment, prevent shivering and hypoxia; avoid morphine.處置：床頭抬高30-40度、頭部正中、避免髖/頸屈曲、頭低腳高位與Valsalva；限水（約1200 mL/日）、安靜環境、預防寒顫與缺氧；避免嗎啡。
  • Mechanical ventilation with PaCO2 30-35 mm Hg causes cerebral vasoconstriction and lowers ICP.機械通氣維持PaCO2 30-35 mm Hg可使腦血管收縮、降低顱內壓。
  • Medications: antiseizure, antipyretics/muscle relaxants, BP control (notify if SBP <100 or >150), corticosteroids (taper slowly), IV fluids by pump, osmotic diuretics (monitor renal function).藥物：抗癲癇藥、退燒藥/肌肉鬆弛劑、血壓控制（收縮壓<100或>150通知醫師）、類固醇（需緩慢減量）、點滴使用幫浦、滲透性利尿劑（監測腎功能）。
  • Hyperthermia (>105 F / 40.6 C) raises cerebral metabolism; cool with fluids, baths, fans or cooling blanket and prevent shivering. VP shunt diverts CSF to the peritoneum; watch for shunt failure and infection.高熱（>105°F/40.6°C）增加腦代謝；以輸液、冷水浴、風扇或降溫毯降溫並防寒顫。腦室腹腔分流將腦脊髓液導入腹腔；注意分流失效與感染。

Head injury and craniotomy頭部外傷與開顱術
  • Epidural hematoma: arterial, rapid, lucid interval then coma; surgical emergency. Subdural: venous, slower. Concussion, contusion and skull fractures are other injury types.硬腦膜外血腫：動脈出血、進展快、有清醒期後昏迷，為外科急症。硬腦膜下血腫：靜脈出血、較慢。另有腦震盪、腦挫傷與顱骨骨折。
  • CSF leak from nose/ear: halo (ring) sign and glucose-positive; do not suction the nose, clean the ear or let the client blow the nose; apply loose dry sterile dressing and notify the PHCP.鼻/耳漏出腦脊髓液：出現暈環徵並葡萄糖陽性；勿經鼻抽吸、清潔耳道或擤鼻；以鬆的乾燥無菌敷料覆蓋並通知醫師。
  • Head injury care: patent airway, HOB elevated, avoid neck flexion and coughing, seizure precautions, monitor neuro status and for infection; give opioids cautiously.頭部外傷照護：維持呼吸道、抬高床頭、避免頸屈曲與咳嗽、癲癇預防、監測神經狀態與感染；鴉片類藥物須謹慎。
  • Craniotomy complications: increased ICP, hemorrhage, CSF obstruction, DI, SIADH, infection. Monitor neuro status every 30-60 min, drain output (report >30-50 mL per shift), fluid restriction ~1500 mL/day.開顱術併發症：ICP上升、出血、腦脊髓液阻塞、尿崩症、抗利尿激素分泌不當症候群、感染。每30-60分鐘監測神經狀態、引流量（每班>30-50 mL需通報）、限水約1500 mL/日。
  • Positioning after surgery follows prescription: bone flap removal - turn to the nonoperative side; posterior fossa - side-lying; infratentorial - flat or HOB 30-45 per order; supratentorial - HOB 30 degrees.術後擺位依醫囑：骨瓣移除－翻向非手術側；後顱窩－側臥；幕下手術－平躺或依醫囑抬高30-45度；幕上手術－床頭抬高30度。
  • Periorbital edema and ecchymosis may be expected after craniotomy; use ice packs as prescribed.開顱術後可預期眼周水腫與瘀青；依醫囑冰敷。

Cerebral aneurysm and seizures腦動脈瘤與癲癇
  • Aneurysm precautions: bed rest, semi-Fowler's or side-lying, dark quiet room, limited visitors, avoid Valsalva (stool softeners), no rectal temperatures, prevent hypertension, DVT prophylaxis, antiseizure drugs.動脈瘤預防措施：臥床休息、半坐臥或側臥、昏暗安靜、限制訪客、避免Valsalva（給軟便劑）、勿測肛溫、預防高血壓、預防深部靜脈栓塞、抗癲癇藥。
  • Seizure types: tonic-clonic (tonic 10-20 s then clonic ~30 s), absence (brief staring, children), myoclonic, atonic, simple partial (conscious), complex partial (temporal lobe, altered awareness).癲癇類型：強直-陣攣（強直10-20秒後陣攣約30秒）、失神（短暫凝視、常見於兒童）、肌躁、失張力、單純局部（意識清楚）、複雜局部（顳葉、意識改變）。
  • During a seizure: note time, protect the head, lower to the floor, side-lying, loosen clothing, suction/oxygen ready, stay with client; do NOT restrain or place anything in the mouth.發作時：記錄時間、保護頭部、移至地板、側臥、鬆開衣物、備好抽吸/氧氣、陪伴；勿約束或放入口中物品。
  • Status epilepticus: rapid succession of seizures without recovery of consciousness; an emergency with risk of brain damage.癲癇重積狀態：連續發作而意識未恢復；屬急症，可致腦損傷。
  • Teaching: lifelong medication with level monitoring, avoid alcohol, stress, fatigue and strobe lights, wear a medical ID bracelet.衛教：終生服藥並監測血中濃度、避免酒精、壓力、疲勞與閃光、配戴醫療警示手環。

Stroke (brain attack)中風（腦中風）
  • Ischemic (thrombotic, embolic) vs hemorrhagic; type must be confirmed by CT because symptoms overlap and treatment differs. TIA is a warning sign.缺血性（血栓、栓塞）與出血性；症狀相似但治療不同，需以CT確認類型。短暫性腦缺血發作為警訊。
  • Check fingerstick glucose to rule out hypoglycemia; airway is always the priority; deficits appear on the side opposite the lesion.測指尖血糖排除低血糖；呼吸道永遠優先；缺損出現在病灶對側。
  • Left-brain stroke: right hemiplegia, aphasia, slow cautious, aware of deficits. Right-brain stroke: left hemiplegia, neglect, impulsive, poor judgment, denies problems.左腦中風：右側偏癱、失語、緩慢謹慎、知道自己缺損。右腦中風：左側偏癱、忽略症、衝動、判斷力差、否認問題。
  • Expressive aphasia (Broca's) - understands but cannot speak; receptive (Wernicke's) - cannot understand. Use one-step directions, picture boards, allow time.表達性失語（Broca）－聽得懂但說不出；接受性失語（Wernicke）－聽不懂。使用單一步驟指令、圖卡、給予時間。
  • Acute phase: keep BP around 150/100 for perfusion, HOB 15-30 degrees, side-lying, suction orally and not longer than 10 seconds, ICP risk highest in first 72 hours.急性期：血壓維持約150/100以保持腦灌流、床頭15-30度、側臥、經口抽吸且不超過10秒、前72小時為ICP高風險期。
  • Post-acute: position 2 h on unaffected side and 20 min on affected side, passive ROM, swallow evaluation, thickened fluids, chin-down, food on the unaffected side.急性期後：健側臥2小時、患側臥20分鐘、被動關節活動、吞嚥評估、增稠液體、下巴微收、食物放健側。
  • Homonymous hemianopsia and neglect: approach from the unaffected side, place items in the intact visual field, teach head turning to scan.同側偏盲與忽略症：從健側接近、物品放在可視野、教導轉頭掃視。

MS, myasthenia gravis, Parkinson's多發性硬化症、重症肌無力、帕金森氏症
  • MS: demyelination, remissions and exacerbations; fatigue, ataxia, spasticity, diplopia, bladder/bowel problems; avoid heat, fatigue, stress and infection; IV glucocorticoids for flare-ups.多發性硬化症：去髓鞘、緩解與惡化交替；疲倦、運動失調、痙攣、複視、膀胱/腸道問題；避免熱、疲勞、壓力與感染；急性發作用靜脈類固醇。
  • Myasthenia gravis: weakness worse with activity, ptosis, diplopia, dysphagia; respiratory failure risk; plan activity at peak strength; anticholinesterase drugs on time.重症肌無力：活動後無力加重、眼瞼下垂、複視、吞嚥困難；有呼吸衰竭風險；在肌力高峰安排活動；按時給予抗膽鹼酯酶藥。
  • Myasthenic crisis = too little medication (strength improves with edrophonium); cholinergic crisis = too much (cramps, diarrhea, miosis, bradycardia, secretions; edrophonium worsens weakness); antidote atropine.肌無力危象＝藥量不足（給edrophonium會改善）；膽鹼性危象＝藥量過多（腹絞痛、腹瀉、瞳孔縮小、心搏過緩、分泌物多；edrophonium使無力加重）；解毒劑為atropine。
  • Edrophonium test: keep atropine and emergency equipment available (risk of ventricular fibrillation/arrest).Edrophonium試驗：備妥atropine與急救設備（有心室顫動/心跳停止風險）。
  • Parkinson's: dopamine depletion; bradykinesia, resting pill-rolling tremor, rigidity, masklike face, shuffling gait, drooling.帕金森氏症：多巴胺缺乏；動作遲緩、靜止性搓丸樣顫抖、僵硬、面具臉、拖曳步態、流口水。
  • Parkinson's care: high-calorie, high-protein, high-fiber soft diet, 2000 mL fluids, low-heeled shoes, lift feet, avoid rushing; avoid vitamin B6-rich foods with levodopa and avoid MAO inhibitors.帕金森照護：高熱量高蛋白高纖軟食、水分2000 mL、低跟鞋、抬腳走路、勿催促；使用levodopa時避免高維生素B6食物，避免MAO抑制劑。

Cranial nerve and neuromuscular disorders腦神經與神經肌肉疾病
  • Trigeminal neuralgia (CN V): severe sharp facial pain triggered by cold, washing, chewing; avoid temperature extremes, chew on unaffected side; surgical options include microvascular decompression and rhizotomy.三叉神經痛（第五對）：因冷、洗臉、咀嚼誘發的劇烈刺痛；避免冷熱、用健側咀嚼；手術包括微血管減壓與神經根切斷術。
  • Bell's palsy (CN VII): one-sided facial paralysis, loss of taste, cannot close eyelid; protect the eye from dryness, facial exercises, chew on the unaffected side; usually recovers in weeks.貝爾氏麻痺（第七對）：單側顏面麻痺、味覺喪失、眼瞼無法閉合；保護眼睛防乾燥、臉部運動、用健側咀嚼；通常數週內恢復。
  • Guillain-Barre: follows infection by days to weeks; ascending symmetric weakness, paresthesias, CSF protein elevated; main danger is respiratory failure and dysrhythmias; recovery is slow.格林-巴利症候群：感染後數日至數週發生；上行性對稱無力、感覺異常、腦脊髓液蛋白升高；主要危險為呼吸衰竭與心律不整；恢復緩慢。
  • ALS: progressive motor neuron disease, sensory, autonomic and mental status spared; weakness, atrophy, fasciculations, dysphagia, dysarthria; supportive care and advance directives.ALS：進行性運動神經元疾病，感覺、自律神經與心智不受影響；無力、萎縮、肌束顫動、吞嚥與構音困難；支持性照護與預立醫囑。

CNS infections中樞神經感染
  • Encephalitis: brain inflammation, usually viral (HSV-1, arboviruses); fever, altered LOC, signs of ICP; acyclovir for herpes encephalitis; HOB 30-45 degrees.腦炎：腦實質發炎，多為病毒（HSV-1、蟲媒病毒）；發燒、意識改變、ICP徵象；疱疹性腦炎用acyclovir；床頭抬高30-45度。
  • West Nile virus: mosquito-borne, symptoms 3-14 days after bite, many asymptomatic; care is supportive; prevent with DEET, long sleeves, eliminating standing water.西尼羅病毒：蚊媒傳播、被叮咬後3-14天發病、許多人無症狀；以支持性照護；以DEET、長袖與清除積水預防。
  • Meningitis: fever, nuchal rigidity, photophobia, positive Kernig and Brudzinski; CSF cloudy with high protein and WBC and low glucose; meningococcal rash is red macular.腦膜炎：發燒、頸僵硬、畏光、Kernig與Brudzinski陽性；腦脊髓液混濁、蛋白與白血球升高、葡萄糖降低；腦膜炎雙球菌有紅色斑疹。
  • Meningitis care: droplet/isolation precautions for bacterial types, seizure precautions, quiet dim room, HOB 30 degrees, avoid neck/hip flexion, antibiotics and analgesics.腦膜炎照護：細菌性採飛沫/隔離預防、癲癇預防、安靜昏暗環境、床頭30度、避免頸/髖過度屈曲、給予抗生素與止痛藥。

Test-critical points考試關鍵點

  1. Change in level of consciousness is the earliest, most sensitive sign of increased ICP.意識程度改變是ICP上升最早且最敏感的徵象。
    p.947 · book 857p.948 · book 858
  2. Late ICP signs (Cushing's triad): rising systolic BP with widened pulse pressure, bradycardia, irregular respirations.ICP晚期徵象（庫欣三徵）：收縮壓升高且脈搏壓增寬、心搏過緩、呼吸不規則。
    p.948 · book 858p.949 · book 859
  3. Increased ICP: HOB 30-40 degrees, avoid Trendelenburg, neck/hip flexion, Valsalva, coughing; avoid morphine; PaCO2 30-35 mm Hg.ICP上升：床頭30-40度，避免頭低位、頸/髖屈曲、Valsalva、咳嗽；避免嗎啡；PaCO2 30-35 mm Hg。
    p.949 · book 859
  4. Lumbar puncture is contraindicated with increased ICP (herniation).顱內壓上升者禁做腰椎穿刺（腦疝）。
    p.945 · book 855
  5. GCS below 8 = coma; range 3-15.GCS低於8分＝昏迷；範圍3-15。
    p.948 · book 858
  6. Epidural hematoma: arterial bleed, lucid interval, then rapid deterioration; surgical emergency.硬腦膜外血腫：動脈出血、清醒期後急速惡化；為外科急症。
    p.951 · book 861
  7. CSF leak: halo sign and glucose-positive; no nasal suction, no nose blowing; loose sterile dry dressing.腦脊髓液漏：暈環徵與葡萄糖陽性；勿經鼻抽吸、擤鼻；鬆敷乾燥無菌敷料。
    p.951 · book 861
  8. Seizure: protect head, side-lying, never restrain or put objects in the mouth, record time and characteristics.癲癇發作：保護頭部、側臥、絕不約束或放物入口、記錄時間與特徵。
    p.953 · book 863
  9. Stroke: airway first, check glucose, confirm type by CT; BP about 150/100 in acute stroke; ICP risk highest in the first 72 hours.中風：先顧呼吸道、測血糖、CT確認類型；急性期血壓約150/100；前72小時ICP風險最高。
    p.955 · book 865p.956 · book 866
  10. Stroke feeding: sit upright, chin slightly flexed, food in the back of the mouth on the unaffected side, thickened fluids; never suction nasally or over 10 seconds.中風進食：坐直、下巴微收、食物置口腔後方健側、增稠液體；勿經鼻抽吸且不超過10秒。
    p.956 · book 866
  11. Myasthenic crisis (undermedicated) vs cholinergic crisis (overmedicated): withhold anticholinesterase and give atropine in cholinergic crisis; edrophonium differentiates.肌無力危象（藥量不足）與膽鹼性危象（藥量過多）：膽鹼性危象停用抗膽鹼酯酶並給atropine；edrophonium可區分。
    p.958 · book 868
  12. Guillain-Barre: respiratory failure is the major concern; keep intubation equipment and ECG monitoring available.格林-巴利：主要顧慮為呼吸衰竭；備妥插管設備與心電圖監測。
    p.960 · book 870
  13. Parkinson's: avoid vitamin B6-rich foods with levodopa and avoid MAO inhibitors (hypertensive crisis).帕金森：使用levodopa時避免高維生素B6食物，避免MAO抑制劑（高血壓危象）。
    p.959 · book 869
  14. Aneurysm precautions: quiet, dim room, no straining, no rectal temperatures, prevent hypertension.動脈瘤預防措施：安靜昏暗、勿用力解便、勿測肛溫、預防高血壓。
    p.952 · book 862p.953 · book 863
  15. Meningitis: CSF cloudy, high protein/WBC, low glucose; positive Kernig/Brudzinski; isolation for bacterial types.腦膜炎：腦脊髓液混濁、蛋白/白血球高、葡萄糖低；Kernig/Brudzinski陽性；細菌性需隔離。
    p.961 · book 871
  16. Pupils: unilateral dilated = CN III compression; bilateral fixed dilated = ominous; pinpoint = pons or opioids.瞳孔：單側放大＝第三對受壓；雙側固定放大＝嚴重；針尖＝橋腦或鴉片類。
    p.947 · book 857

Mnemonics記憶口訣

  • Right brain = Risky (impulsive, neglect, left-sided deficits); Left brain = Language (aphasia, slow and cautious, right-sided deficits).右腦＝冒險（衝動、忽略症、左側缺損）；左腦＝語言（失語、緩慢謹慎、右側缺損）。
    p.955 · book 865
  • Cholinergic crisis = SLUDGE/DUMBELS signs (salivation, lacrimation, urination, defecation, GI cramps, emesis) - too much drug; myasthenic crisis = too little.膽鹼性危象＝SLUDGE症狀（流涎、流淚、排尿、排便、腸胃絞痛、嘔吐）－藥過量；肌無力危象＝藥不足。
    p.958 · book 868
  • Cushing's triad: high BP (wide pulse pressure), low HR, irregular respirations.庫欣三徵：高血壓（脈搏壓增寬）、心搏過慢、呼吸不規則。

1A client with a head injury has a GCS that falls from 15 to 11, with new restlessness and slowed pupil response. What is the nurse's priority action?頭部外傷病人GCS由15降至11，出現躁動且瞳孔反應變慢。護理師的首要行動為何？

A falling LOC is the earliest sign of rising ICP and needs immediate reporting. Morphine can depress respiration and raise ICP; lowering the HOB increases ICP.意識程度下降是ICP上升最早徵象，需立即通報。嗎啡會抑制呼吸、增加ICP；降低床頭會增加ICP。
