This chapter covers respiratory anatomy, diagnostic tests (bronchoscopy, thoracentesis, V/Q scan, CT angiography, TB skin test), chest trauma, asthma, COPD, pneumonia and other infections, pleural disorders, pulmonary embolism, and tuberculosis. Gas exchange and perfusion are the priority concepts: position upright, give oxygen, and recognize life-threatening changes.本章涵蓋呼吸系統解剖、診斷檢查（支氣管鏡、胸腔穿刺、V/Q 掃描、CT 肺動脈攝影、結核菌素試驗）、胸部外傷、氣喘、COPD、肺炎與其他感染、胸膜疾病、肺栓塞與結核病。優先概念為氣體交換與灌流：採直立姿勢、給氧並辨識危及生命的變化。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Respiratory anatomy and physiology呼吸系統解剖與生理

  • Functions: supply oxygen, remove CO2, plus acid-base balance, speech, smell, water and heat balance.功能：供氧、排出二氧化碳，另維持酸鹼平衡、發聲、嗅覺、水分與熱平衡。
  • Right mainstem bronchus is wider, shorter, more vertical; right lung has 3 lobes, left lung 2. Trachea branches at the carina.右主支氣管較寬、短且垂直；右肺 3 葉、左肺 2 葉。氣管於隆凸分叉。
  • Alveoli are the gas exchange units; type 2 alveolar cells make surfactant, which lowers surface tension and prevents collapse. Terminal bronchioles lack cartilage and cilia.肺泡為氣體交換單位；第二型肺泡細胞分泌表面張力素，降低表面張力防止塌陷。終末細支氣管無軟骨與纖毛。
  • Diaphragm descends on inspiration creating negative pressure; expiration is passive recoil. Gas exchange needs matched ventilation and perfusion.吸氣時橫膈下降形成負壓；呼氣為被動回彈。氣體交換需要通氣與灌流相配。
  • Parietal pleura lines the chest wall; visceral pleura covers the lung; pleural fluid lubricates.壁層胸膜覆蓋胸壁；臟層胸膜覆蓋肺；胸膜液具潤滑作用。

2Diagnostic tests: imaging, sputum, scopes診斷檢查：影像、痰液、內視鏡

  • Chest x-ray: remove metal objects, ask about pregnancy. Sputum: early morning, rinse mouth, deep cough, collect before antibiotics (about 15 mL); saliva is not sputum.胸部 X 光：移除金屬物，詢問懷孕。痰液：清晨、先漱口、深咳、於使用抗生素前採集（約 15 mL）；唾液不是痰。
  • Bronchoscopy: consent, NPO, check coagulation, local spray; afterward semi-Fowler's, NPO until gag reflex returns, watch for bloody sputum, bronchospasm, perforation (facial/neck crepitus), hypoxemia, pneumothorax.支氣管鏡：同意書、禁食、檢查凝血、局部噴劑；術後半坐臥、嘔吐反射恢復前禁食、監測血痰、支氣管痙攣、穿孔（臉頸皮下氣腫）、低血氧、氣胸。
  • Pulmonary angiography: assess iodine/seafood allergy; afterward no BP in injection arm for 24 h, check neurovascular status and site bleeding.肺動脈攝影：評估碘/海鮮過敏；術後該側肢體 24 小時不量血壓、檢查神經血管狀態與穿刺處出血。
  • Spiral CT and CT pulmonary angiography are preferred for PE (contrast, renal function); V/Q scan used with renal impairment or contrast allergy; encourage fluids.螺旋 CT 與 CT 肺動脈攝影為肺栓塞首選（顯影劑、腎功能）；腎功能不全或顯影劑過敏者做 V/Q 掃描；鼓勵多喝水。
  • D-dimer: high sensitivity, low specificity; normal < 50 ng/mL. Pulmonary function tests: hold bronchodilators if prescribed, no heavy meal or smoking 4-6 h.D-dimer：高敏感性、低特異性；正常 < 50 ng/mL。肺功能檢查：依醫囑停用支氣管擴張劑，檢查前 4-6 小時不吃大餐或抽菸。
  • Do not suction before drawing an ABG (it depletes oxygen and distorts results).抽動脈血氣前不可抽吸（會消耗氧氣、影響結果）。

3Thoracentesis and lung biopsy胸腔穿刺與肺活檢

  • Thoracentesis removes fluid or air from the pleural space: check coagulation, sit upright leaning on a bedside table (or lie toward the unaffected side), do not cough or move.胸腔穿刺移除胸腔積液或空氣：檢查凝血、坐直倚靠床邊桌（或向健側臥），不可咳嗽或移動。
  • After: monitor respiratory status, pressure dressing, bleeding/crepitus; watch for pneumothorax, air embolism, pulmonary edema; review chest x-ray.術後：監測呼吸、壓迫敷料、出血/皮下氣腫；觀察氣胸、空氣栓塞、肺水腫；檢視胸部 X 光。
  • Lung biopsy: NPO, sedation, remain still; afterward dressing, watch for respiratory distress, pneumothorax, air emboli.肺活檢：禁食、鎮靜、保持不動；術後敷料、觀察呼吸窘迫、氣胸、空氣栓塞。

4Chest injuries and pneumothorax胸部外傷與氣胸

  • Rib fracture: pain, shallow breathing, high pneumonia risk; Fowler's, analgesia, self-splint with pillow (no external binders), nerve block if severe.肋骨骨折：疼痛、淺呼吸、肺炎風險高；半坐臥、止痛、以枕頭自行按壓（不用外部束縛）、嚴重時神經阻斷。
  • Flail chest: paradoxical movement of a chest segment, severe pain, dyspnea, cyanosis, tachycardia, hypotension; oxygen, analgesia, Fowler's, intubation with PEEP if respiratory failure.連枷胸：胸壁一段反常運動、劇痛、呼吸困難、發紺、心搏過速、低血壓；給氧、止痛、半坐臥、呼吸衰竭時插管並用 PEEP。
  • Pulmonary contusion: interstitial/alveolar hemorrhage, hypoxemia, hemoptysis; ARDS is the major complication.肺挫傷：間質/肺泡出血、低血氧、咳血；主要併發症為急性呼吸窘迫症候群。
  • Pneumothorax: absent/diminished breath sounds on affected side, unilateral decreased chest expansion, sharp pain, crepitus; open pneumothorax has sucking sound; tension pneumothorax shows tracheal deviation to the unaffected side, distended neck veins, hypotension.氣胸：患側呼吸音消失/減弱、單側胸廓擴張減少、尖銳疼痛、皮下氣腫；開放性氣胸有吸吮聲；張力性氣胸氣管偏向健側、頸靜脈怒張、低血壓。
  • Interventions: nonporous dressing for open wound, oxygen, Fowler's, chest tube until lung re-expands, monitor drainage and subcutaneous emphysema.處置：開放傷口用不透氣敷料、給氧、半坐臥、置放胸管至肺完全擴張、監測引流與皮下氣腫。

5Asthma氣喘

  • Chronic airway inflammation with hyperresponsiveness and reversible obstruction; triggers include allergens, exercise, infection, irritants, cold air, aspirin, NSAIDs, beta blockers, sulfites, GERD, stress.慢性氣道發炎伴隨高反應性與可逆性阻塞；誘因包含過敏原、運動、感染、刺激物、冷空氣、阿斯匹靈、NSAIDs、β 阻斷劑、亞硫酸鹽、胃食道逆流、壓力。
  • Findings: wheeze, prolonged expiration, accessory muscle use, tachypnea, tachycardia, pulsus paradoxus, diaphoresis, decreased SpO2; silent chest means impending respiratory failure.表現：喘鳴、呼氣延長、使用輔助肌、呼吸急促、心搏過速、奇脈、盜汗、血氧下降；呼吸音消失（靜默胸）代表即將呼吸衰竭。
  • Acute episode: high-Fowler's/sitting, oxygen, stay with client, bronchodilators, corticosteroids, magnesium sulfate, IV fluids, auscultate before/during/after.急性發作：高坐臥/坐姿、給氧、陪伴病人、支氣管擴張劑、類固醇、硫酸鎂、靜脈輸液、治療前中後聽診。
  • Status asthmaticus is life-threatening and refractory to treatment. Teach peak flow use, spacer, trigger avoidance, action plan, MedicAlert.氣喘重積狀態危及生命且對治療反應不佳。衛教尖峰流速計、吸入輔助器、避開誘因、行動計畫、醫療警示手環。

6COPD (chronic bronchitis and emphysema)慢性阻塞性肺病（慢性支氣管炎與肺氣腫）

  • Progressive, not fully reversible airflow limitation; complications include pulmonary hypertension and cor pulmonale. Findings: cough, sputum, exertional dyspnea, barrel chest, prolonged expiration, clubbing, orthopnea, weight loss.進行性、不完全可逆的氣流受限；併發症含肺高壓與肺心症。表現：咳嗽、痰、運動時呼吸困難、桶狀胸、呼氣延長、杵狀指、端坐呼吸、體重下降。
  • ABGs show chronic respiratory acidosis with hypoxemia; uncompensated respiratory acidosis suggests acute exacerbation.動脈血氣顯示慢性呼吸性酸中毒伴低血氧；未代償的呼吸性酸中毒提示急性惡化。
  • Give oxygen at the lowest concentration per ABG/SpO2; pursed-lip and diaphragmatic breathing, tripod position, chest physiotherapy, fluids up to 3000 mL/day, high-calorie high-protein small meals.依血氣與血氧給最低有效濃度氧氣；噘嘴與橫膈式呼吸、三腳架姿勢、胸部物理治療、每日水分可達 3000 mL、高熱量高蛋白少量多餐。
  • Meds: bronchodilators, corticosteroids for exacerbations, mucolytics, antibiotics for infection. Teach stopping smoking, avoiding infection/crowds, immunizations, wet dusting.藥物：支氣管擴張劑、惡化時用類固醇、黏液溶解劑、感染時抗生素。衛教戒菸、避免感染/人群、疫苗、濕布擦拭灰塵。

7Pneumonia, influenza, COVID-19, SARS, Legionnaire's肺炎、流感、COVID-19、SARS、退伍軍人症

  • Pneumonia: fever, chills, pleuritic pain, productive cough, crackles/rhonchi, hypoxia, mental status change in older adults; sputum culture, high WBC; CXR consolidation.肺炎：發燒、畏寒、胸膜性疼痛、有痰咳嗽、囉音/鼾音、低血氧、老年人意識改變；痰培養、WBC 升高；胸片實變。
  • Care: oxygen, semi-Fowler's, cough/deep breathing/incentive spirometer, chest physiotherapy, fluids up to 3 L/day, high-calorie diet, antibiotics, hand hygiene; pneumococcal vaccine teaching.照護：給氧、半坐臥、咳嗽/深呼吸/誘發性肺量計、胸部物理治療、每日水分可達 3 L、高熱量飲食、抗生素、手部衛生；肺炎鏈球菌疫苗衛教。
  • Influenza: abrupt fever, chills, myalgia, headache, cough; annual vaccine (contraindicated with egg allergy per book), rest, fluids, antivirals, droplet hygiene.流感：突發發燒、畏寒、肌痛、頭痛、咳嗽；每年接種疫苗（書中指出蛋過敏者禁用）、休息、補水、抗病毒藥、飛沫衛生。
  • COVID-19: spread by droplets even without symptoms; symptoms 2-14 days after exposure incl. new loss of taste/smell; droplet (+/- contact) precautions; emergency signs: trouble breathing, persistent chest pain, confusion, cyanosis.COVID-19：即使無症狀也可經飛沫傳染；暴露後 2-14 天出現症狀，含新發味嗅覺喪失；飛沫（必要時接觸）隔離；急症徵象：呼吸困難、持續胸痛、意識混亂、發紺。
  • SARS: coronavirus, fever, aches, then dry cough/dyspnea after 2-7 days. Legionnaire's: Legionella from contaminated water sources, flu-like with high fever, dry cough, diarrhea; not person-to-person.SARS：冠狀病毒，發燒、痠痛，2-7 天後出現乾咳與呼吸困難。退伍軍人症：Legionella 來自受汙染水源，類流感高燒、乾咳、腹瀉；不會人傳人。

8Pleural effusion, empyema, pleurisy胸腔積液、膿胸、胸膜炎

  • Pleural effusion: pleuritic pain, dyspnea, decreased chest wall movement, dry cough, decreased breath sounds/friction rub, mediastinal shift away from fluid if > 250 mL; Fowler's, thoracentesis; pleurodesis/pleurectomy if recurrent.胸腔積液：胸膜性疼痛、呼吸困難、胸壁活動減少、乾咳、呼吸音減弱/摩擦音、積液 >250 mL 時縱膈偏離積液側；半坐臥、胸腔穿刺；反覆發生則做胸膜沾黏術/胸膜切除術。
  • Empyema: pus in pleural space; fever, night sweats, chest pain, weight loss; antibiotics, thoracentesis/chest tube drainage, decortication if thickened.膿胸：胸腔內膿液；發燒、盜汗、胸痛、體重下降；抗生素、胸腔穿刺/胸管引流、胸膜增厚時行剝離術。
  • Pleurisy: knife-like pain worse on deep breath, friction rub; analgesics, hot/cold applications, lie on the affected side to splint.胸膜炎：深呼吸時刀割樣痛、胸膜摩擦音；止痛藥、冷熱敷、患側臥以支撐固定。

9Pulmonary embolism and other lung diseases肺栓塞與其他肺部疾病

  • PE: thrombus (usually deep vein) travels to a pulmonary artery. Risks: immobility, surgery, obesity, pregnancy, HF, estrogen, long-bone fracture (fat emboli).肺栓塞：血栓（多來自深部靜脈）流至肺動脈。危險因子：不動、手術、肥胖、懷孕、心衰竭、雌激素、長骨骨折（脂肪栓塞）。
  • Signs: sudden dyspnea, pleuritic/chest pain, tachypnea, tachycardia, hypotension, cyanosis, anxiety/impending doom, hemoptysis, distended neck veins, petechiae over chest/axilla.徵象：突發呼吸困難、胸膜性/胸痛、呼吸急促、心搏過速、低血壓、發紺、焦慮/瀕死感、咳血、頸靜脈怒張、胸腋瘀點。
  • Action: elevate HOB, oxygen, call rapid response and PHCP, prepare ABG, labs, scan, heparin; monitor vitals and lung sounds; support client.處置：抬高床頭、給氧、呼叫快速反應小組與醫師、準備血氣、檢驗、影像、heparin；監測生命徵象與呼吸音；提供支持。
  • Histoplasmosis: inhaled spores from soil/bird droppings; mask and wet floor before sweeping barns; watch nephrotoxicity of antifungals. Sarcoidosis: granulomas, steroids. Occupational lung disease: prevention with respiratory protection.組織漿菌症：吸入土壤/鳥糞孢子；清掃穀倉前戴口罩並先噴濕地面；注意抗黴菌藥腎毒性。類肉瘤病：肉芽腫、使用類固醇。職業性肺病：以呼吸防護預防。

10Tuberculosis結核病

  • Mycobacterium tuberculosis, acid-fast, airborne droplet nuclei; mainly upper lobes; noncompliance leads to MDR-TB; risk after 2-3 weeks of treatment falls greatly.結核分枝桿菌，抗酸性、經空氣飛沫核傳染；多侵犯上肺葉；不遵從治療導致多重抗藥結核；治療 2-3 週後傳染力大幅下降。
  • Signs: fatigue, anorexia, weight loss, low-grade fever, night sweats, persistent cough with mucoid/bloody sputum; upper-lobe infiltrates and calcification on x-ray.徵象：疲倦、厭食、體重減輕、低燒、盜汗、持續咳嗽伴黏液/血痰；X 光上肺葉浸潤與鈣化。
  • Tests: TST (read at 48-72 h, induration in mm; positive means exposure, not active disease; BCG gives positive; confirm with chest x-ray), QuantiFERON-TB Gold, sputum acid-fast smear and culture (up to 6 weeks).檢查：結核菌素試驗（48-72 小時判讀硬結 mm；陽性代表曾暴露，非活動性疾病；BCG 可呈陽性；需胸部 X 光確認）、QuantiFERON-TB Gold、痰液抗酸染色與培養（最多 6 週）。
  • Hospital: airborne isolation, negative-pressure room with door closed, at least 6 air exchanges per hour, N95/particulate respirator for staff, surgical mask on client when transported.住院：空氣隔離、負壓病室門須關閉、每小時至少 6 次換氣、工作人員戴 N95/呼吸防護具、病人外出需戴外科口罩。
  • Teaching: take medication exactly as prescribed, up to 12 months; sputum cultures every 2-4 weeks, three negative cultures = not infectious; cover cough, handwashing, nutrition (iron, protein, vitamin C), notify public health department.衛教：嚴格按時服藥，最長達 12 個月；每 2-4 週痰培養，三次陰性即無傳染力；掩口咳嗽、洗手、營養（鐵、蛋白質、維生素 C）、通報公共衛生單位。

  • D-dimer: high sensitivity, low specificity; normal < 50 ng/mL. Pulmonary function tests: hold bronchodilators if prescribed, no heavy meal or smoking 4-6 h.D-dimer：高敏感性、低特異性；正常 < 50 ng/mL。肺功能檢查：依醫囑停用支氣管擴張劑，檢查前 4-6 小時不吃大餐或抽菸。
  • Do not suction before drawing an ABG (it depletes oxygen and distorts results).抽動脈血氣前不可抽吸（會消耗氧氣、影響結果）。

Thoracentesis and lung biopsy胸腔穿刺與肺活檢
  • Thoracentesis removes fluid or air from the pleural space: check coagulation, sit upright leaning on a bedside table (or lie toward the unaffected side), do not cough or move.胸腔穿刺移除胸腔積液或空氣：檢查凝血、坐直倚靠床邊桌（或向健側臥），不可咳嗽或移動。

Topic 4 · tap to flip主題 4 · 點擊翻面
Chest injuries and pneumothorax胸部外傷與氣胸
  • Rib fracture: pain, shallow breathing, high pneumonia risk; Fowler's, analgesia, self-splint with pillow (no external binders), nerve block if severe.肋骨骨折：疼痛、淺呼吸、肺炎風險高；半坐臥、止痛、以枕頭自行按壓（不用外部束縛）、嚴重時神經阻斷。
  • Flail chest: paradoxical movement of a chest segment, severe pain, dyspnea, cyanosis, tachycardia, hypotension; oxygen, analgesia, Fowler's, intubation with PEEP if respiratory failure.連枷胸：胸壁一段反常運動、劇痛、呼吸困難、發紺、心搏過速、低血壓；給氧、止痛、半坐臥、呼吸衰竭時插管並用 PEEP。
  • Pulmonary contusion: interstitial/alveolar hemorrhage, hypoxemia, hemoptysis; ARDS is the major complication.肺挫傷：間質/肺泡出血、低血氧、咳血；主要併發症為急性呼吸窘迫症候群。
  • Pneumothorax: absent/diminished breath sounds on affected side, unilateral decreased chest expansion, sharp pain, crepitus; open pneumothorax has sucking sound; tension pneumothorax shows tracheal deviation to the unaffected side, distended neck veins, hypotension.氣胸：患側呼吸音消失/減弱、單側胸廓擴張減少、尖銳疼痛、皮下氣腫；開放性氣胸有吸吮聲；張力性氣胸氣管偏向健側、頸靜脈怒張、低血壓。

Topic 5 · tap to flip主題 5 · 點擊翻面
Asthma氣喘
  • Chronic airway inflammation with hyperresponsiveness and reversible obstruction; triggers include allergens, exercise, infection, irritants, cold air, aspirin, NSAIDs, beta blockers, sulfites, GERD, stress.慢性氣道發炎伴隨高反應性與可逆性阻塞；誘因包含過敏原、運動、感染、刺激物、冷空氣、阿斯匹靈、NSAIDs、β 阻斷劑、亞硫酸鹽、胃食道逆流、壓力。
  • Findings: wheeze, prolonged expiration, accessory muscle use, tachypnea, tachycardia, pulsus paradoxus, diaphoresis, decreased SpO2; silent chest means impending respiratory failure.表現：喘鳴、呼氣延長、使用輔助肌、呼吸急促、心搏過速、奇脈、盜汗、血氧下降；呼吸音消失（靜默胸）代表即將呼吸衰竭。
  • Acute episode: high-Fowler's/sitting, oxygen, stay with client, bronchodilators, corticosteroids, magnesium sulfate, IV fluids, auscultate before/during/after.急性發作：高坐臥/坐姿、給氧、陪伴病人、支氣管擴張劑、類固醇、硫酸鎂、靜脈輸液、治療前中後聽診。
  • Status asthmaticus is life-threatening and refractory to treatment. Teach peak flow use, spacer, trigger avoidance, action plan, MedicAlert.氣喘重積狀態危及生命且對治療反應不佳。衛教尖峰流速計、吸入輔助器、避開誘因、行動計畫、醫療警示手環。
Topic 6 · tap to flip主題 6 · 點擊翻面
COPD (chronic bronchitis and emphysema)慢性阻塞性肺病（慢性支氣管炎與肺氣腫）
  • Progressive, not fully reversible airflow limitation; complications include pulmonary hypertension and cor pulmonale. Findings: cough, sputum, exertional dyspnea, barrel chest, prolonged expiration, clubbing, orthopnea, weight loss.進行性、不完全可逆的氣流受限；併發症含肺高壓與肺心症。表現：咳嗽、痰、運動時呼吸困難、桶狀胸、呼氣延長、杵狀指、端坐呼吸、體重下降。
  • ABGs show chronic respiratory acidosis with hypoxemia; uncompensated respiratory acidosis suggests acute exacerbation.動脈血氣顯示慢性呼吸性酸中毒伴低血氧；未代償的呼吸性酸中毒提示急性惡化。
  • Give oxygen at the lowest concentration per ABG/SpO2; pursed-lip and diaphragmatic breathing, tripod position, chest physiotherapy, fluids up to 3000 mL/day, high-calorie high-protein small meals.依血氣與血氧給最低有效濃度氧氣；噘嘴與橫膈式呼吸、三腳架姿勢、胸部物理治療、每日水分可達 3000 mL、高熱量高蛋白少量多餐。

Pneumonia, influenza, COVID-19, SARS, Legionnaire's肺炎、流感、COVID-19、SARS、退伍軍人症
  • Pneumonia: fever, chills, pleuritic pain, productive cough, crackles/rhonchi, hypoxia, mental status change in older adults; sputum culture, high WBC; CXR consolidation.肺炎：發燒、畏寒、胸膜性疼痛、有痰咳嗽、囉音/鼾音、低血氧、老年人意識改變；痰培養、WBC 升高；胸片實變。
  • Care: oxygen, semi-Fowler's, cough/deep breathing/incentive spirometer, chest physiotherapy, fluids up to 3 L/day, high-calorie diet, antibiotics, hand hygiene; pneumococcal vaccine teaching.照護：給氧、半坐臥、咳嗽/深呼吸/誘發性肺量計、胸部物理治療、每日水分可達 3 L、高熱量飲食、抗生素、手部衛生；肺炎鏈球菌疫苗衛教。
  • Influenza: abrupt fever, chills, myalgia, headache, cough; annual vaccine (contraindicated with egg allergy per book), rest, fluids, antivirals, droplet hygiene.流感：突發發燒、畏寒、肌痛、頭痛、咳嗽；每年接種疫苗（書中指出蛋過敏者禁用）、休息、補水、抗病毒藥、飛沫衛生。

Pulmonary embolism and other lung diseases肺栓塞與其他肺部疾病
  • PE: thrombus (usually deep vein) travels to a pulmonary artery. Risks: immobility, surgery, obesity, pregnancy, HF, estrogen, long-bone fracture (fat emboli).肺栓塞：血栓（多來自深部靜脈）流至肺動脈。危險因子：不動、手術、肥胖、懷孕、心衰竭、雌激素、長骨骨折（脂肪栓塞）。
  • Signs: sudden dyspnea, pleuritic/chest pain, tachypnea, tachycardia, hypotension, cyanosis, anxiety/impending doom, hemoptysis, distended neck veins, petechiae over chest/axilla.徵象：突發呼吸困難、胸膜性/胸痛、呼吸急促、心搏過速、低血壓、發紺、焦慮/瀕死感、咳血、頸靜脈怒張、胸腋瘀點。
  • Action: elevate HOB, oxygen, call rapid response and PHCP, prepare ABG, labs, scan, heparin; monitor vitals and lung sounds; support client.處置：抬高床頭、給氧、呼叫快速反應小組與醫師、準備血氣、檢驗、影像、heparin；監測生命徵象與呼吸音；提供支持。
  • Histoplasmosis: inhaled spores from soil/bird droppings; mask and wet floor before sweeping barns; watch nephrotoxicity of antifungals. Sarcoidosis: granulomas, steroids. Occupational lung disease: prevention with respiratory protection.組織漿菌症：吸入土壤/鳥糞孢子；清掃穀倉前戴口罩並先噴濕地面；注意抗黴菌藥腎毒性。類肉瘤病：肉芽腫、使用類固醇。職業性肺病：以呼吸防護預防。
Topic 10 · tap to flip主題 10 · 點擊翻面
Tuberculosis結核病
  • Mycobacterium tuberculosis, acid-fast, airborne droplet nuclei; mainly upper lobes; noncompliance leads to MDR-TB; risk after 2-3 weeks of treatment falls greatly.結核分枝桿菌，抗酸性、經空氣飛沫核傳染；多侵犯上肺葉；不遵從治療導致多重抗藥結核；治療 2-3 週後傳染力大幅下降。
  • Signs: fatigue, anorexia, weight loss, low-grade fever, night sweats, persistent cough with mucoid/bloody sputum; upper-lobe infiltrates and calcification on x-ray.徵象：疲倦、厭食、體重減輕、低燒、盜汗、持續咳嗽伴黏液/血痰；X 光上肺葉浸潤與鈣化。
  • Tests: TST (read at 48-72 h, induration in mm; positive means exposure, not active disease; BCG gives positive; confirm with chest x-ray), QuantiFERON-TB Gold, sputum acid-fast smear and culture (up to 6 weeks).檢查：結核菌素試驗（48-72 小時判讀硬結 mm；陽性代表曾暴露，非活動性疾病；BCG 可呈陽性；需胸部 X 光確認）、QuantiFERON-TB Gold、痰液抗酸染色與培養（最多 6 週）。
  • Hospital: airborne isolation, negative-pressure room with door closed, at least 6 air exchanges per hour, N95/particulate respirator for staff, surgical mask on client when transported.住院：空氣隔離、負壓病室門須關閉、每小時至少 6 次換氣、工作人員戴 N95/呼吸防護具、病人外出需戴外科口罩。
  • Teaching: take medication exactly as prescribed, up to 12 months; sputum cultures every 2-4 weeks, three negative cultures = not infectious; cover cough, handwashing, nutrition (iron, protein, vitamin C), notify public health department.衛教：嚴格按時服藥，最長達 12 個月；每 2-4 週痰培養，三次陰性即無傳染力；掩口咳嗽、洗手、營養（鐵、蛋白質、維生素 C）、通報公共衛生單位。
