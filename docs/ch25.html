This chapter covers intrapartum emergencies and complications: premature rupture of membranes, cord prolapse, supine hypotension, preterm labor, precipitous birth, dystocia, amniotic fluid embolism (anaphylactoid syndrome of pregnancy), fetal distress and demise, uterine rupture and inversion. Most items test the first priority action: relieve cord compression, improve oxygenation and perfusion, and notify the provider.本章涵蓋產程中的急症與併發症：胎膜早破、臍帶脫垂、仰臥低血壓、早產、急產、難產、羊水栓塞（妊娠類過敏症候群）、胎兒窘迫與胎死腹中、子宮破裂與子宮內翻。考題多測驗首要處置：解除臍帶壓迫、改善氧合與灌流並通知醫師。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Premature rupture of membranes胎膜早破

  • Spontaneous rupture before labor onset; gestational age guides management; preterm rupture with delayed birth raises infection risk.於陣痛開始前自然破水；週數決定處置；早產且延遲分娩時感染風險增加。
  • Assess pooled vaginal fluid, positive nitrazine test, fluid amount/color/odor, temperature, and FHR (fetal tachycardia can signal maternal infection).評估陰道積液、石蕊試紙（nitrazine）陽性、羊水量／顏色／氣味、體溫與胎心率（胎兒心搏過速可能代表母體感染）。
  • Avoid vaginal examinations; give antibiotics as prescribed; monitor for infection or fetal compromise.避免陰道檢查；依醫囑給予抗生素；監測感染與胎兒狀況。
p.377 · book 336

2Prolapsed umbilical cord臍帶脫垂

  • Cord slips ahead of or beside the presenting part, compressing and cutting off fetal circulation. Suspect it with a sensation of something in the vagina, visible or palpable cord, bradycardia or variable decelerations after rupture.臍帶滑到先露部前方或旁邊而受壓，阻斷胎兒血流。若產婦感覺陰道有東西、見到或摸到臍帶、破水後胎心過慢或變異減速，應懷疑。
  • Immediately relieve pressure: sterile gloved hand lifts the presenting part off the cord and stays there.立即解除壓力：以無菌手套的手將先露部上推離開臍帶並維持不動。
  • Position: extreme Trendelenburg, modified left lateral or knee-chest. Call for help, oxygen 8-10 L/min, wrap exposed cord in warm sterile saline-soaked towel, IV fluids, prepare for emergency cesarean.姿勢：極度頭低腳高、改良左側臥或膝胸臥位。呼叫協助、給氧8至10 L/分、以溫無菌生理食鹽水濕巾包覆外露臍帶、靜脈輸液、準備緊急剖腹產。
  • Never push the cord back into the uterus.絕不可將臍帶推回子宮。

3Supine hypotension and preterm labor仰臥低血壓與早產

  • Supine hypotension: uterus compresses vena cava and aorta; pallor, dizziness, tachycardia, hypotension, clammy skin, fetal distress. Turn to the side or place a wedge under the hip.仰臥低血壓：子宮壓迫下腔靜脈與主動脈；蒼白、頭暈、心跳快、低血壓、冒冷汗、胎兒窘迫。處置為側臥或臀下墊楔形枕。
  • Preterm labor: after 20 weeks and before 37 weeks. Risks include multiples, infection, anemia, substance abuse, age under 18, or a first pregnancy after age 40.早產：20週後至未滿37週。危險因子包括多胞胎、感染、貧血、物質濫用、年齡小於18歲，或大於40歲的初產婦。
  • Signs: contractions, cramping or diarrhea, low back pain, pelvic pressure, change in discharge, ruptured membranes, positive fetal fibronectin, shortened cervix.徵象：宮縮、腹部絞痛或腹瀉、下背痛、骨盆壓迫感、分泌物改變、破水、胎兒纖維連接蛋白陽性、子宮頸縮短。
  • Care: treat infection, restrict activity, hydrate, lateral bed rest, monitor fetus, tocolytics as prescribed, 17P injections to lower risk of preterm birth.處置：治療感染、限制活動、補充水分、側臥休息、監測胎兒、依醫囑給予安胎藥、使用17P注射以降低早產風險。
p.378 · book 337

4Precipitous labor and dystocia急產與難產

  • Precipitous labor lasts under 3 hours: stay with client, keep her calm, pant between contractions, do not try to stop delivery.急產指產程少於3小時：陪伴產婦、使其鎮定、宮縮間喘息呼吸，不可阻止胎兒娩出。
  • Emergency birth: support perineum and apply gentle pressure on head (Ritgen), check for cord around neck, suction mouth then nose, dry and warm the infant, allow placenta to separate naturally, skin-to-skin to promote contractions.緊急接生：支托會陰並輕壓胎頭（Ritgen手法）、檢查臍帶繞頸、先吸口再吸鼻、擦乾保暖新生兒、讓胎盤自然剝離、肌膚接觸促進子宮收縮。
  • Dystocia: prolonged or painful labor from contractions, fetus (large, malpositioned) or pelvis. Hypotonic contractions are weak and irregular (oxytocin or amniotomy may help); hypertonic are painful, frequent, uncoordinated (pain relief and rest; no oxytocin).難產：因宮縮、胎兒（過大、胎位不正）或骨盆問題導致產程延長或疼痛。低張性宮縮微弱不規則（可用催產素或人工破水）；高張性宮縮疼痛、頻繁、不協調（止痛與休息；不用催產素）。
  • Dystocia care: monitor FHR first, contractions, maternal temperature/pulse, hydration, I&O, position changes, watch for cord prolapse after rupture.難產處置：首要監測胎心率，並監測宮縮、母體體溫與脈搏、水分、輸出入量、變換姿勢，破水後留意臍帶脫垂。

5Amniotic fluid embolism and fetal distress/demise羊水栓塞與胎兒窘迫／胎死腹中

  • Anaphylactoid syndrome of pregnancy (amniotic fluid embolism): sudden respiratory distress, chest pain, cyanosis, coagulation failure; often fatal. Give 100% oxygen/ventilation, side position, fluids, blood products, prepare for emergency delivery.妊娠類過敏症候群（羊水栓塞）：突發呼吸窘迫、胸痛、發紺、凝血功能障礙，常致命。給予高濃度氧與通氣、側臥、輸液、血液製品，準備緊急分娩。
  • Fetal distress: FHR <110 or >160, meconium, late or severe variable decelerations, decreasing variability, abnormal activity. Stop oxytocin, lateral position, oxygen 8-10 L/min, IV fluid bolus, prepare for cesarean.胎兒窘迫：胎心<110或>160、胎便、晚期或嚴重變異減速、變異性下降、胎動異常。停催產素、側臥、給氧8至10 L/分、靜脈輸液快速注入、準備剖腹產。
  • Fetal demise (after 20 weeks): no fetal movement or heart tones; retained 3-4 weeks risks DIC (low platelets, bleeding from puncture sites). Support grieving, respect culture and religion, accept sadness/anger, refer to support groups.胎死腹中（20週後）：無胎動與胎心音；滯留3至4週有DIC風險（血小板低、穿刺處出血）。支持哀傷、尊重文化與宗教、接納悲傷與憤怒、轉介支持團體。
p.379 · book 338p.380 · book 339

6Uterine rupture and inversion子宮破裂與子宮內翻

  • Rupture risks: labor after prior cesarean, overdistended uterus, trauma. Signs: abdominal pain/tenderness, chest pain, contractions stop, rigid abdomen, absent FHR, shock.破裂危險因子：剖腹產後試產、子宮過度膨脹、外傷。徵象：腹痛壓痛、胸痛、宮縮停止、腹部僵硬、胎心消失、休克。
  • Rupture care: treat shock (oxygen, fluids, blood), prepare for cesarean, possible hysterectomy.破裂處置：治療休克（氧氣、輸液、輸血）、準備剖腹產，可能需子宮切除。
  • Inversion: uterus turns inside out, often with fundal placenta, cord traction, or atony. Signs: fundal depression, visible uterine tissue, severe pain, hemorrhage, shock.內翻：子宮翻出，常見於底部著床、強拉臍帶或子宮收縮不良。徵象：子宮底凹陷、可見子宮組織、劇痛、大出血、休克。
  • Inversion care: treat shock, prepare for vaginal replacement or laparotomy.內翻處置：治療休克、準備經陰道復位，失敗則剖腹復位。

  1. Cord prolapse: relieve pressure with a gloved hand holding the presenting part up; Trendelenburg or knee-chest; oxygen; never push the cord back.臍帶脫垂：戴手套的手上推先露部以解除壓迫；頭低腳高或膝胸臥位；給氧；絕不將臍帶推回。

Practice questions練習題

1A client in labor reports a feeling of something in the vagina and the nurse sees the cord protruding. What is the first action?待產婦覺得陰道有東西，護理師看到臍帶外露。第一個處置為何？

Relieve cord compression immediately; never push the cord back; call for help without leaving the client.必須立即解除臍帶壓迫；不可推回；呼叫協助但不離開產婦。

4A client with hypertonic uterine contractions is in severe pain. What is the priority intervention?高張性宮縮的產婦極度疼痛。首要處置為何？

Hypertonic dystocia is managed with analgesia and rest; oxytocin and amniotomy are for hypotonic patterns.高張性難產以止痛與休息處理；催產素與破水用於低張性。

6A client receiving oxytocin has a recurrent late deceleration pattern. What is the most important nursing action while preparing for cesarean?使用催產素的產婦反覆出現晚期減速。準備剖腹產時，最重要的護理行動為何？

Oxygen improves fetal oxygenation; oxytocin is stopped, fluids increased, client placed lateral.給氧可改善胎兒氧合；同時停催產素、增加輸液並側臥。
