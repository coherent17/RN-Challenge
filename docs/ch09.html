This chapter explains how the body holds pH at 7.35-7.45 using buffers, lungs, kidneys and potassium shifts, then covers the four primary acid-base disorders, their causes, signs and nursing care. It ends with ABG collection and the step-by-step method for interpreting ABGs, a staple of NCLEX questions.本章說明身體如何以緩衝系統、肺、腎與鉀離子移動將 pH 維持在 7.35-7.45,並介紹四種原發性酸鹼失衡的原因、徵象與護理處置,最後說明動脈血氣(ABG)採檢與逐步判讀法,這是 NCLEX 的常考重點。

Chapter summary章節重點摘要

1Hydrogen ions, acids and bases氫離子、酸與鹼

  • Blood pH must stay between 7.35 and 7.45; below is acidosis, above is alkalosis.血液 pH 須維持在 7.35-7.45;低於此為酸中毒,高於此為鹼中毒。
  • Acids donate hydrogen ions and are end products of metabolism; bases accept hydrogen ions.酸是氫離子的提供者,為代謝終產物;鹼是氫離子的接受者。
  • Normal serum bicarbonate (HCO3-) is 21-28 mEq/L.血清碳酸氫根(HCO3-)正常值 21-28 mEq/L。
p.128 · book 106

2Buffer systems緩衝系統

  • Buffers act fastest, giving immediate protection against pH change; once used up the body has less reserve.緩衝系統反應最快,能立即防止 pH 改變;用盡後身體的儲備就減少。
  • Extracellular buffers: hemoglobin (chloride shift), plasma proteins, carbonic acid-bicarbonate (the main buffer) and phosphate.細胞外緩衝系統包括血紅素(氯離子轉移)、血漿蛋白、碳酸-碳酸氫鹽(主要緩衝系統)與磷酸鹽。
  • Normal pH of 7.4 reflects a bicarbonate : carbonic acid ratio of 20:1; lungs control carbonic acid (CO2) and kidneys control bicarbonate.pH 7.4 代表碳酸氫根與碳酸比為 20:1;肺控制碳酸(CO2),腎控制碳酸氫根。
  • The underlying cause of any imbalance must be identified and treated.必須找出並治療酸鹼失衡的根本原因。
p.128 · book 106p.129 · book 107

3Lungs, kidneys and potassium肺、腎與鉀離子的調節

  • Lungs are the second defense and respond in 10-30 seconds: in acidosis rate and depth rise to blow off CO2; in alkalosis they fall to retain CO2.肺是第二道防線,10-30 秒內反應:酸中毒時呼吸加快加深以排出 CO2;鹼中毒時呼吸變慢變淺以留住 CO2。
  • Kidneys are slower (hours to days) but more thorough: they excrete H+ and retain HCO3- in acidosis, and excrete HCO3- in alkalosis.腎臟反應較慢(數小時至數天)但更完整:酸中毒時排 H+、留 HCO3-;鹼中毒時排 HCO3-。
  • Potassium shifts: in acidosis H+ enters cells and K+ leaves, causing hyperkalemia; in alkalosis K+ enters cells, causing hypokalemia.鉀離子移動:酸中毒時 H+ 進入細胞、K+ 移出,造成高血鉀;鹼中毒時 K+ 進入細胞,造成低血鉀。
  • Monitor potassium closely in any acid-base imbalance because of dysrhythmia risk.任何酸鹼失衡都須密切監測血鉀,因有心律不整風險。
p.129 · book 107p.130 · book 108

4Respiratory acidosis呼吸性酸中毒

  • Caused by hypoventilation with CO2 retention: COPD/emphysema, asthma, pneumonia, atelectasis, pulmonary edema or emboli, brain trauma, and CNS depressants (opioids, sedatives, anesthetics).因換氣不足導致 CO2 滯留:COPD/肺氣腫、氣喘、肺炎、肺不張、肺水腫或肺栓塞、腦外傷、中樞神經抑制劑(鴉片類、鎮靜劑、麻醉劑)。
  • Findings: lethargy, confusion, headache, dizziness, coma, hypotension, dysrhythmias (from hyperkalemia), warm flushed skin, seizures.表現:嗜睡、意識混亂、頭痛、頭暈、昏迷、低血壓、心律不整(高血鉀所致)、皮膚溫熱潮紅、癲癇。
  • Care: O2 as prescribed, semi-Fowler's position, turn/cough/deep-breathe, suction, respiratory treatments; avoid sedatives; prepare for intubation and ventilation if CO2 is above 50 mm Hg with acute distress.處置:依醫囑給氧、半坐臥、翻身咳嗽深呼吸、抽吸、呼吸治療;避免鎮靜劑;若 CO2 超過 50 mm Hg 且急性呼吸窘迫,準備插管與機械通氣。
p.130 · book 108p.131 · book 109

5Respiratory alkalosis呼吸性鹼中毒

  • Caused by overstimulated breathing: hyperventilation, anxiety, pain, fever, hypoxia, and over-ventilation by a mechanical ventilator.因呼吸過度刺激:換氣過度、焦慮、疼痛、發燒、缺氧,以及呼吸器過度換氣。
  • Findings: light-headedness, dizziness, confusion, tachycardia, dysrhythmias, nausea/vomiting, numbness and tingling of extremities, tetany, hyperreflexia, seizures.表現:輕飄感、頭暈、意識混亂、心跳快、心律不整、噁心嘔吐、四肢麻刺、手足僵直、反射亢進、癲癇。
  • Care: reassure, coach slow breathing, breath holding or rebreathing (paper bag/rebreather mask), adjust ventilator, calcium gluconate for tetany as prescribed.處置:安撫、指導緩慢呼吸、閉氣或再吸入(紙袋/再呼吸面罩)、調整呼吸器,依醫囑給葡萄糖酸鈣治療手足僵直。
p.131 · book 109

6Metabolic acidosis代謝性酸中毒

  • Causes: diabetic ketoacidosis, aspirin overdose, lactic acidosis, malnutrition, high-fat diet, renal failure, severe diarrhea (loss of alkaline fluid).原因:糖尿病酮酸中毒、阿斯匹靈過量、乳酸中毒、營養不良、高脂飲食、腎衰竭、嚴重腹瀉(流失鹼性液體)。
  • Findings: lethargy, confusion, headache, hypotension, dysrhythmias, cold clammy skin, nausea/vomiting/diarrhea, and Kussmaul's respirations (deep, rapid).表現:嗜睡、意識混亂、頭痛、低血壓、心律不整、皮膚冰冷濕黏、噁心嘔吐腹瀉,以及 Kussmaul 呼吸(深快)。
  • Care: IV fluids and buffer base, monitor I&O and electrolytes; in DKA give insulin and fluid resuscitation; in kidney disease dialysis and a low-protein, high-calorie diet.處置:靜脈輸液與補充緩衝鹼、監測輸出入量與電解質;DKA 給予胰島素與輸液復甦;腎病則透析並採低蛋白高熱量飲食。
p.131 · book 109p.132 · book 110

7Metabolic alkalosis代謝性鹼中毒

  • Causes: excessive vomiting or NG suction (loss of HCl), diuretics, hyperaldosteronism, excess sodium bicarbonate, massive whole-blood transfusion (citrate becomes bicarbonate).原因:大量嘔吐或鼻胃管抽吸(流失鹽酸)、利尿劑、高醛固酮症、碳酸氫鈉過量、大量輸入全血(檸檬酸轉為碳酸氫鹽)。
  • Findings: dizziness, irritability, confusion, tachycardia, dysrhythmias, nausea/vomiting, tetany, tremors, tingling, muscle cramps, seizures; breathing becomes slow and shallow.表現:頭暈、易怒、意識混亂、心跳快、心律不整、噁心嘔吐、手足僵直、顫抖、麻刺、肌肉痙攣、癲癇;呼吸變慢變淺。
  • Care: IV fluids and medications to promote bicarbonate excretion, replace potassium as prescribed, monitor respiratory status.處置:依醫囑給予靜脈輸液與藥物促進碳酸氫根排出、補充鉀,並監測呼吸狀態。
p.132 · book 110p.133 · book 111

8ABG collection動脈血氣(ABG)採檢

  • Normal ABG: pH 7.35-7.45; PaCO2 35-45 mm Hg; HCO3- 21-28 mEq/L; PaO2 80-100 mm Hg (venous pH 7.31-7.41, PvO2 40-50).ABG 正常值:pH 7.35-7.45;PaCO2 35-45 mm Hg;HCO3- 21-28 mEq/L;PaO2 80-100 mm Hg(靜脈 pH 7.31-7.41、PvO2 40-50)。
  • Perform the Allen's test before radial puncture to confirm ulnar collateral flow; positive means immediate flushing after releasing the ulnar artery.橈動脈穿刺前先做 Allen 測試確認尺動脈側支循環;放開尺動脈後手掌立即變紅即為陽性。
  • Use a heparinized syringe, remove any air, press the site 5 minutes (10 if on anticoagulants), recheck the radial pulse, send on ice.使用含肝素針筒、排除空氣,穿刺後壓迫 5 分鐘(使用抗凝劑者 10 分鐘),再確認橈動脈搏動,冰浴送檢。
  • Record temperature and O2 delivery on the lab form; note recent suctioning (within 20 minutes) or O2 changes.檢驗單上註明體溫與給氧方式;留意 20 分鐘內是否抽痰或調整氧氣。
p.133 · book 111

9Interpreting ABGs and compensationABG 判讀與代償

  • Step 1: pH low = acidosis, high = alkalosis. Step 2: if PaCO2 moves in the OPPOSITE direction to pH, it is respiratory. Step 3: if HCO3- moves in the SAME direction as pH, it is metabolic.步驟 1:pH 低為酸中毒、高為鹼中毒。步驟 2:PaCO2 與 pH 方向「相反」則為呼吸性。步驟 3:HCO3- 與 pH 方向「相同」則為代謝性。
  • Step 4: a normal pH (7.35-7.45) means full compensation; if pH is abnormal, check the other indicator: abnormal value = partial compensation, normal value = uncompensated.步驟 4:pH 回到 7.35-7.45 表示完全代償;pH 仍異常時,看另一指標:已異常為部分代償,仍正常為未代償。
  • Mixed disorders combine two problems (e.g., hyperventilation from pain plus gastric suction gives mixed alkalosis).混合性失衡為同時存在兩種問題(如疼痛致換氣過度加胃管抽吸造成混合性鹼中毒)。
  • Potassium is usually increased in acidosis and decreased in alkalosis.酸中毒時鉀通常上升,鹼中毒時鉀通常下降。

  3. Respiratory imbalance: pH and PaCO2 move in opposite directions. Metabolic imbalance: pH and HCO3- move together.呼吸性失衡:pH 與 PaCO2 方向相反。代謝性失衡:pH 與 HCO3- 方向一致。

  4. Full compensation = pH back within 7.35-7.45.完全代償 = pH 回到 7.35-7.45。

  5. Respiratory acidosis: pH low, PaCO2 high. Respiratory alkalosis: pH high, PaCO2 low.呼吸性酸中毒:pH 低、PaCO2 高。呼吸性鹼中毒:pH 高、PaCO2 低。
    p.133 · book 111
  6. Metabolic acidosis: pH low, HCO3- low. Metabolic alkalosis: pH high, HCO3- high.代謝性酸中毒:pH 低、HCO3- 低。代謝性鹼中毒:pH 高、HCO3- 高。
    p.134 · book 112
  7. Vomiting and NG suction cause metabolic alkalosis (HCl loss); severe diarrhea and DKA cause metabolic acidosis.嘔吐與鼻胃管抽吸造成代謝性鹼中毒(流失鹽酸);嚴重腹瀉與 DKA 造成代謝性酸中毒。

  8. COPD, hypoventilation and opioid/sedative overdose cause respiratory acidosis; avoid sedatives and opioids in these clients.COPD、換氣不足與鴉片類/鎮靜劑過量造成呼吸性酸中毒;此類患者應避免鎮靜劑與鴉片類。

  9. Kussmaul's respirations (deep and rapid) are the lungs' compensation for metabolic acidosis such as DKA.Kussmaul 呼吸(深而快)是肺對代謝性酸中毒(如 DKA)的代償。

  10. Acidosis raises serum K+ (hyperkalemia); alkalosis lowers it (hypokalemia). Watch for dysrhythmias.酸中毒使血鉀上升(高血鉀);鹼中毒使血鉀下降(低血鉀)。須留意心律不整。
    p.129 · book 107p.130 · book 108
  11. Lungs compensate in 10-30 seconds; kidneys take hours to days but are more complete.肺代償需 10-30 秒;腎臟需數小時至數天,但更完整。
    p.129 · book 107
  12. Intubation/ventilation needed in respiratory acidosis when CO2 exceeds 50 mm Hg with acute respiratory distress.呼吸性酸中毒若 CO2 超過 50 mm Hg 並有急性呼吸窘迫,需準備插管與通氣。

    p.128 · book 106p.130 · book 108
  15. Hyperventilation from anxiety, pain or fever causes respiratory alkalosis; rebreathing (paper bag) and calm coaching help.焦慮、疼痛或發燒造成的換氣過度導致呼吸性鹼中毒;可用再吸入(紙袋)與安撫指導呼吸。

  • Tic-Tac-Toe ABG: place pH, PaCO2, HCO3- in a row and check which one matches the pH direction.ABG 判讀三格法:把 pH、PaCO2、HCO3- 排成一列,看哪一個與 pH 的方向吻合(酸中毒:pH 低,PaCO2 高 = 呼吸;HCO3- 低 = 代謝)。

  • Vomiting = alkalosis (lose acid); diarrhea = acidosis (lose base).嘔吐=鹼中毒(失酸);腹瀉=酸中毒(失鹼)。

Reading an ABG in 4 steps判讀ABG的四個步驟

1Step 1步驟1Check pH看pH

Below 7.35 = acidosis. Above 7.45 = alkalosis.低於7.35＝酸中毒；高於7.45＝鹼中毒。

2Step 2步驟2Check PaCO2看PaCO2

Moves opposite to pH? It's respiratory.與pH方向相反＝呼吸性。

3Step 3步驟3Check HCO3-看HCO3-

Moves same direction as pH? It's metabolic.與pH方向相同＝代謝性。

4Step 4步驟4Check compensation看代償

pH normal = fully compensated. Other value abnormal = partial. Other value normal = uncompensated.pH正常＝完全代償；另一數值異常＝部分代償；另一數值正常＝未代償。

1ABG: pH 7.45, PaCO2 30 mm Hg, HCO3- 20 mEq/L. Which interpretation is correct?ABG:pH 7.45、PaCO2 30 mm Hg、HCO3- 20 mEq/L。下列判讀何者正確?

pH is at the high-normal end with low PaCO2 (opposite direction = respiratory alkalosis). The low HCO3- shows renal compensation, and pH is normal so compensation is full.pH 在正常偏高,PaCO2 低(與 pH 相反 = 呼吸性鹼中毒);HCO3- 偏低顯示腎臟代償,pH 已正常故為完全代償。

6Which client is at greatest risk for respiratory acidosis?下列哪位患者最容易發生呼吸性酸中毒?
